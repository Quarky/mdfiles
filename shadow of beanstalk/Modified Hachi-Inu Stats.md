## Modified Hachi-Inu Stats

| Characteristic | Value |
|----------------|-------|
| **Brawn** | 3 |
| **Agility** | 3 |
| **Intellect** | 1 |
| **Cunning** | 3 |
| **Willpower** | 2 |
| **Presence** | 1 |
| **Soak** | 3 |
| **Wound Threshold** | 12 |
| **Strain Threshold** | 10 |
| **M/R Defense** | 1/0 |

**Skills:** Athletics (Br) 2, Brawl (Br) 3, Coordination (Ag) 2, Perception (Cun) 3, Stealth (Ag) 2, Survival (Cun) 2, Vigilance (Will) 2

**Talents:**
- **Keen Scent:** Add a Boost die to all Perception checks involving scent.
- **Pursuit Predator:** When making an opposed check to track a target, add a Boost die.
- **Hand-like Paws:** Can open doors, use simple tools, and interface with computer terminals.

**Weapons:**
- **Bite (Brawl):** Damage 4, Critical 3, Range Engaged, **Pierce 2, Vicious 2**
- **Claw (Brawl):** Damage 3, Critical 4, Range Engaged, Knockdown

**Special Abilities:**
- **Armor-Piercing Bite:** The modified metal teeth grant **Pierce 2** on Bite attacks.
- **Vicious Jaws:** The reinforced jaw strength grants **Vicious 2** on Bite attacks.
- **Silent Hunter:** Add a Boost die to Stealth checks when moving silently.

**Handling and Risks:**
- Requires a trained handler to interpret its tail communications.
- Can interface with computer terminals using its hand-like paws.
- The modifications may have made the unit more aggressive; it may be harder to control.
- If the unit's owner is killed or incapacitated, the Hachi-Inu may become unpredictable.
