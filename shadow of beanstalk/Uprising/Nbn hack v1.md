## WHAT DANTE IS LOOKING FOR — AND WHY IT'S HERE

The theory: **NBN caused the Blackout.**

Vertex was broken up by anti-monopoly legislation. The Blackout destroyed the old Internet and created the vacuum that SYNC filled. NBN reconstituted the empire the law broke apart — this time with governments dependent on it rather than regulating it.

The question: is there evidence? And if so, where is it kept?

**Not on Earth.** The servers that hold evidence of what happened before the Blackout are too close to the people who have the most to lose. Earth-side NBN infrastructure is too well-monitored, too lawyered, too surrounded by the very governments whose dependency on NBN protects NBN from investigation.

**But the Moon is different.** The Heinlein facility is newer. It was built after NBN established its lunar presence — which means it was built after Vertex was reconstituted as NBN, after the Blackout, after the consolidation. It houses the infrastructure for the **StarReach Pathway** project — the deep-space broadcast satellite network that NBN is building to extend its reach beyond the Moon.

And critically: the StarReach project requires **historical data modeling**. To project how a system-wide broadcast network will function, you need historical records of how the old network functioned. Including the old Internet. Including the period immediately before and during the Blackout.

**Those records are here.** Not because NBN is careless. Because they need them. For the project. They are buried under layers of archival ICE that most runners would never have a reason to dig through.

**Dante has a reason.**

### Physical Approach Options

#### Option A — Legitimate Entry (social)

The broadcast facility runs tours for Heinlein residents as a PR exercise. Three times a week, small groups. A runner with good social skills and a plausible cover can get to Level 3 (production) legitimately.

This doesn't get close to Level 5 or 6. But Level 3 has a maintenance access panel that connects to the building's environmental system crawl — which connects to Level 5's server room via an air handling shaft.

NBN's security knows about this route. They monitor it with a camera that has been in "maintenance pending" status for **four months** because the facilities manager and the security manager have been disputing whose budget covers the repair. The camera is not working.

#### Option B — External Splice (technical)

The building has a comms array on its roof — part of the SYNC lunar relay network. A runner with EVA capability and the right equipment can splice into the external relay hardware directly, bypassing the building's physical security entirely.

This provides wireless network access without entering the building. The EWS does not cover this relay — it is civilian SYNC infrastructure, not military. But SYNC's own network ICE is formidable. And this approach triggers SYNC's anomaly detection **faster** because relay traffic is heavily monitored for signal integrity.

#### Option C — The Maintenance Contract (long-play)

Yusuf has a contact — a woman named **Adaeze** who runs a legitimate HVAC maintenance firm that holds the environmental systems contract for the NBN building. Adaeze doesn't know what Dante needs. She knows Yusuf. She can arrange for Dante to be on the crew for the quarterly filter inspection.

This gets him to Level 5's air handling units, which are adjacent to the server room, which has a **hardwired maintenance terminal** that is not on the network map NBN gave to Heinlein Authority but absolutely is on the building's actual infrastructure.

This is the cleanest physical approach. It takes 72 hours to arrange. Dante has whatever time the session allows.

## THE EMOTIONAL WEIGHT — WHAT DANTE FINDS

The information he finds, in the order it lands:

### Sigma-7 (System 1 Editorial Archive)

NBN knew Kaguya was coming **three days before it happened.** Someone told them. The coverage protocol was pre-written. "Do not mention clone casualties." The Insurrection was covered as planned, not as it happened.

### "Subject 14" (System 4 Counter-Runner Profiles)

Yusuf has been watched for **8 months.** Everything Dante and Yusuf did together — every meeting, every transaction, every safe house — was potentially visible. The network may have been compromised before it began.

### Petra's Suppression Order (System 4 Legal Archive)

Filed **before she published anything.** Before Kaguya. Someone told NBN she was going to be a problem. That means the same person who told NBN about Kaguya may have told them about Petra. There is a leak. There has always been a leak.

### "Pre-Event Behavioral Corpus" (System 3 StarReach Archive)

Someone called the Blackout an event **before it was one.** The data was preserved on purpose. The SYNC protocol was ready before the Blackout created the need for it.

### Project Static (System 5)

Keith Randolph Kane Sr. planned the Blackout. Eight months before it happened. To take back what legislation was taking from him.

### "Collateral modeled at acceptable parameters."

**That line.**

Dante sits with that line for however long he sits with it. The GM should not rush this moment. Dante has just read something that reframes everything — the Insurrection, the Kaguya broadcast, the whole architecture of the world he lives in — as downstream of a decision made by one man who decided that the people who would die when the old Internet collapsed were acceptable losses.

The Moon's workers who Zane and the Loonies died for. The rights they were trying to claim. The system that denied those rights. All of it built on a foundation that was laid in blood that someone labeled "acceptable" in a planning document that was then filed and kept and never destroyed because the Kane family kept everything.

**What Dante does with this information is the campaign's final question.**

## WHAT DANTE CAN DO WITH WHAT HE FINDS

### Option 1 — Give It to Petra

She has the journalistic context. She has the sources. She has spent her career building toward something she didn't know was this big. She is currently alive and writing somewhere.

This story — Project Static, the Kane archive, the full proof — is the story that ends careers, collapses governments, and forces NBN to spend the next decade in legal combat. It does not bring anyone back. It is the most powerful thing Dante could do with this information if the goal is changing the world through legitimate channels.

Petra would be in danger the moment she published. She knows this. She would publish anyway.

### Option 2 — Broadcast It Directly (System 1 Golden Objective)

Push it to every NBN channel simultaneously. Project Static. The Kane archive. All of it. To every screen in Heinlein. To every PAD. For as long as the Master Broadcast Override holds.

NBN takes it back in minutes. Maybe longer. But the Blackout files on a thousand PADs in the outpost transit hubs where SYNC can't reach — that is the second draft of history, written in real time.

### Option 3 — Send It to the Architects' Rivals

Three of the eleven architects are alive and in government. Their rivals — in those same governments, in competing corps — would pay a great deal for this information. Not to expose it. To use it.

This is the most cynical option. It is also the most likely to produce lasting structural change in how the corps relate to each other, because it creates leverage that doesn't go away when an article is buried. It is using a weapon the same way the weapon was used. Dante knows this if he thinks about it.

### Option 4 — Destroy It and Walk Away

The Kane archive is irreplaceable. If Dante deletes it cleanly from System 5 — and deletes the logs of the deletion, and deletes the logs of the logs, and does it before the DEAD MAN notification reaches whoever it reaches — the evidence is gone.

NBN loses the record they kept of their own crime. That's not justice. It's not nothing either. It is a decision about what the truth is worth when the truth belongs to the people who committed the crime.

### Option 5 — Keep It and Wait

The information exists on whatever storage medium Dante carries it on. It is the most dangerous thing he has ever held. It is also leverage. Against NBN. Against the surviving architects. Against anyone who would benefit from it staying buried.

This is how the Kane family operated. Dante now knows that.
