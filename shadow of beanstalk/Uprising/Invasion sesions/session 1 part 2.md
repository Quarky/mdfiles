## THE WARNING

Dante is in the maintenance chamber with the miner team. The tunnel shakes. Dust falls from the ceiling. It's not an explosion—it's something heavier. Something bigger.

**The Sound:** A deep, resonant thud. Then another. Then a third. The sound of heavy equipment moving through the facility.

> RIKER: (looking up from his panel) "That's not artillery. That's breaching equipment. They're breaking through the upper-level seals."

> FLETCHER: (already moving) "They're not waiting. They're pushing now. We have minutes, not hours."

> MAEKO: "Where?"

> FLETCHER: "Everywhere. They're breaching the inner dome seals simultaneously. They want to overwhelm us."

**Time:** Session start + 15-30 minutes

**The Tactics:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Breaching Teams** | 6 troopers per team. Heavy breaching equipment. |
| **Drone Screen** | EWS drones ahead of every team. Full sensor sweep. |
| **Controlled Pair Advance** | Teams move in pairs. Cover each other. |
| **No Civilian Stops** | If civilians are in the way, they are collateral. |

**What This Looks Like:**

- The breaching teams hit the upper-level seals simultaneously
- The seals break

- The Loonies fall back

### Discovery 11 — The Prosperity Connection

**Location:** Maintenance tunnel, section 28

Dante finds a wall covered in graffiti. Some of it is fresh—hours old. Some of it is old—years old. The old graffiti is faded, partially covered, but still visible.

**The Old Graffiti:**
> "REMEMBER PROSPERITY."
> "THEY KILLED US."
> "WE WILL NOT FORGET."
> "PROSPERITY WAS MURDER."

**The New Graffiti:**

**The Situation:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Environment** | Tunnel sealed. Oxygen dropping. Pressure dropping. |
| **Cause** | Military decompression event above |
| **Time** | 5-10 minutes before oxygen becomes dangerous |
| **Options** | Find a way out, override the seals, or wait |

**The Options:**

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|

| **Find Alternative** | Find another route out | Average | Avoids the decompression. Takes time. |
| **Cut Through** | Cut through a wall to escape | Hard | Creates a new route. Takes time. |
| **Wait** | Wait for the decompression to stop | Easy | 5-10 minutes. Might not be enough. |
