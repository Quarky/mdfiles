## THE LOONY TOOLKIT

### Improvised Technology

| Technology | Capability | Counter-Tactic |
|------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Plasma Cutters** | Cutting through metal, walls, doors, transplas | Creates new routes; also a melee weapon |
| **Thermal Blankets** | Blocks heat signatures | Foils thermal imaging |
| **Overtensioned Power Cells** | Improvised grenade (Blast 6, Burn 2) | Area denial, distraction, close-quarters combat |
| **Conveyor Chains** | Entangling, reaching, melee | Ensnare 1, Unwieldy 2 |
| **Rebar Clubs** | Crushing, breaking, melee | Cumbersome 2, Knockdown |
| **Crowbars** | Prying, breaking, melee | Sunder, Pierce 2 |
| **Signal Spoofers** | Simulating military signals | Confuses EWS, creates false targets |
| **Acoustic Baffles** | Sound dampening | Foils acoustic sensors |
| **Magnetic Boots** | Walking on walls/ceilings | Alternative movement routes; flanking positions |
| **Smoke Grenades (Improvised)** | Obscuring vision, cooling thermal signatures | Foils visual and thermal tracking |
| **Emergency Seals** | Creating pressure differentials | Traps, decompression events |
