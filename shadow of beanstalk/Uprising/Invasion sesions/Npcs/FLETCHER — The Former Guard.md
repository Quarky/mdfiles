### Tactical Role — Security Specialist

Fletcher knows the enemy. He was a guard. He knows how they think. He can infiltrate, navigate security, and manipulate guard behavior.

| Action | Skill | Difficulty | Effect |
|--------|-------|------------|--------|
| Identify Guard Pattern | Perception | Average | Identify where the guards will be and when |
| Infiltrate Security Area | Skulduggery | Hard | Gain access to a guarded area |
| Manipulate Guard Behavior | Deception | Average | Convince a guard to move away from a position |

**Motivation:**
- **Redemption:** He was a guard. He wants to make up for it.
- **Knowledge:** He knows how the system works. He uses it.

**Tactics:**
- Move through the facility, avoiding security.
- Identify guard patterns and exploit them.
- Use infiltration to get into secure areas.

**Player Interaction:**
- Fletcher is the person to call when you need to get past security.
- He can identify guard patterns and manipulate guard behavior.
- He is direct and pragmatic. He doesn't waste time.

**Combat Example:**
*The squad needs to get past a guarded checkpoint. Fletcher approaches the checkpoint, wearing his old uniform. He talks to the guard, convincing him that there's a different threat on the other side of the facility. The guard leaves the checkpoint. The squad moves through.*
