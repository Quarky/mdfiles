### Tactical Role — Extraction Specialist

Maeko is the only one who can reliably get the team off the Moon. She knows the surface. She knows the hoppers. She can drive through anything.

| Action | Skill | Difficulty | Effect |
|--------|-------|------------|--------|
| Hot Extract | Piloting | Daunting | Extract a team from a hostile location |
| Surface Navigation | Survival | Average | Navigate the lunar surface to a safe location |
| Vehicle Maintenance | Mechanics | Average | Repair a damaged hopper |

**Motivation:**
- **Escape:** She wants to leave the Moon. This is the only way.
- **Skill:** She's good at what she does. She trusts her abilities.

**Tactics:**
- Wait at the extraction point. Watch for the team.
- If the team is delayed, she waits.
- When they arrive, she gets them out.

**Player Interaction:**
- Maeko is the person to call when you need an extraction.
- She can also handle vehicle maintenance and surface navigation.
- She is fast-talking and confident. She doesn't hesitate.

**Combat Example:**
*The team is trapped on the surface. The military is closing in. Maeko arrives in a hopper. She lands, covers the approach, and waits for the team to board. She has 90 seconds before the exosuits on the rim get a clear shot. The team needs to board in 90 seconds.*
