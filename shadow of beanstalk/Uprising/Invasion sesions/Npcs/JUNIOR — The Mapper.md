### Tactical Role — Navigator

Junior knows the schematics. He knows where everything is supposed to be. He can find routes that nobody else knows about.

| Action | Skill | Difficulty | Effect |
|--------|-------|------------|--------|
| Find Alternative Route | Knowledge (Facility) | Average | Find a route that isn't on the tactical map |
| Navigate Maintenance Network | Survival | Hard | Navigate the maintenance tunnels to a specific location |
| Identify Facility Anomaly | Perception | Average | Identify a structural weakness or environmental hazard |

**Motivation:**
- **Proving Himself:** He wants to be useful. He wants to belong.
- **Knowledge:** He knows the schematics better than anyone. He uses them.

**Tactics:**
- Study the schematics. Find the routes nobody else knows about.
- Guide the team through the facility.
- Identify environmental hazards.

**Player Interaction:**
- Junior is the person to call when you need to find a route.
- He can identify structural weaknesses and environmental hazards.
- He is anxious and talkative. He needs reassurance.

**Combat Example:**
*The squad is trapped in a corridor. The military is advancing. Junior studies the schematics. He finds a maintenance hatch that isn't on any tactical map. It leads to a lower level. The squad escapes.*
