### Tactical Role — Heavy Breaker

Mac is the team's muscle. He can break anything. He can hold a position. He can intimidate.

| Action | Skill | Difficulty | Effect |
|--------|-------|------------|--------|
| Break Obstacle | Athletics | Average | Break through a door, transplas panel, or light wall |
| Hold Position | Resilience | Hard | Stand your ground against incoming fire |
| Intimidate | Coercion | Average | Scare a guard into backing down |
| Move Heavy Object | Athletics | Easy | Move cargo, debris, or anything that needs moving |

**Motivation:**
- **Protection:** He's here because his team is here. He won't leave them.
- **Duty:** He does what needs to be done.

**Tactics:**
- Move to the front. Break the obstacle. Hold the line.
- Engage in combat only when necessary.

**Player Interaction:**
- Mac is the person to call when you need something broken.
- He also helps with moving heavy objects, carrying wounded, and clearing debris.
- He doesn't talk much. He acts.

**Combat Example:**
*The military is advancing down a corridor. Mac is at the junction, holding the line with his rebar club. A trooper rounds the corner. Mac swings. The trooper is knocked prone. Mac steps forward, continuing to hold the line.*
