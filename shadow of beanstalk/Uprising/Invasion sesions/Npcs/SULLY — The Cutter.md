### Tactical Role — Precision Breaker

Sully is the person you call when you need something cut. He can create extraction routes, cut through obstacles, and perform precision strikes.

| Action | Skill | Difficulty | Effect |
|--------|-------|------------|--------|
| Cut Through Obstacle | Mechanics | Average | Cut through metal, concrete, or transplas |
| Create Extraction Route | Mechanics | Hard | Create a new route through a sealed section |
| Precision Strike | Melee | Hard | Attack a specific target with precision |

**Motivation:**
- **Survival:** He's here because he doesn't want to go back.
- **Skill:** He's good at what he does. He trusts his abilities.

**Tactics:**
- Move to the obstacle. Cut through it. Create the route.
- Use precision strikes when necessary.

**Player Interaction:**
- Sully is the person to call when you need to get through something.
- He can also create new routes through sealed areas.
- He is quiet and focused. He doesn't talk much.

**Combat Example:**
*The squad is trapped in a corridor. The military is advancing from both ends. Sully is at the wall, cutting through the transplas. He needs 90 seconds to create a large enough hole for the squad to escape. The squad needs to hold the corridor for 90 seconds.*
