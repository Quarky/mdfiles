# LUNAR MINERS — REBELLION CELL (Revised)
## Everyday Miners — Ad Hoc Soldiers

**Core Concept:** These are not professionals. They are miners, mechanics, and laborers who agreed to do something dangerous because they saw no other path. They have **makeshift weapons**, **no formal combat training**, and **limited knowledge** of the facility. They are learning as they go. They are scared. They are determined.

**Key Note:** They have **never been inside Outpost 14**. They know the **surface**, the **harvester routes**, and the **general layout from external observation**. They do **not** know internal corridors, guard rotations, or system quirks. They are **following Dante's lead**.
