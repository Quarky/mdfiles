## THE MILITARY MINDSET

The SXC troopers at Kaguya are not sadists. They are professionals with a job to do. They are doing it in an environment they trained for but have never fought in. They are following orders. They are not thinking about politics. They are thinking about the next corridor.

### Vasora's Command Philosophy

Vasora is the commander on the ground. She has been in command for 12 hours. She has not slept. She has not eaten. She has been moving through the facility, seeing the situation with her own eyes, adjusting tactics as she goes.

**Her Motives:**
1. **Secure the Beanstalk.** This is the primary objective. Everything else is secondary.
2. **Minimize military casualties.** Her troopers are professionals. She will not waste them.
3. **Minimize civilian casualties.** She is not a monster. But civilians are not the primary objective.
4. **Finish the job.** The longer this goes on, the more complex it becomes. She wants to end it.

**Her Decision-Making:**
- She will not sacrifice her troopers to save civilians.
- She will not sacrifice the Beanstalk to save civilians.
- She will not stop the advance for civilians.
- But she will not deliberately target civilians.
- She will detain Loonies when possible. She will kill Loonies when necessary.

**The Human Element:**
Vasora knows that every decision she makes will be scrutinized. She knows that there will be investigations. She knows that she will be blamed for everything that goes wrong. She is doing the job anyway. She believes in the mission.

### The Trooper Mindset

The average SXC trooper at Kaguya is 25-30 years old. They have trained for lunar combat. They have never used it. They are afraid. They are professional. They are following orders.

**What They Believe:**
- They are the good guys.
- They are protecting the Beanstalk.
- The Loonies are terrorists.
- Civilians are collateral.
- They want to go home.

**What They Do in Combat:**
- They follow doctrine.
- They cover each other.
- They advance methodically.
- They do not hesitate.
- They do not think about politics.

**What They Are Not:**
- They are not sadists.
- They are not murderers.
- They are not heroes.
- They are soldiers doing their job.
