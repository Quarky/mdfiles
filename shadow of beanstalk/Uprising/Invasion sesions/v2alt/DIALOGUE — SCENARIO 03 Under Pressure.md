## DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 03: Under Pressure

> SULLY: (cutting through a wall, plasma torch in hand)
> "You know, when I signed up for this, I thought it was going to be a lot more talking and a lot less getting shot at. I'm a technician. I fix things. I don't—"
>
> *He pauses. The torch sputters.*
>
> "I don't actually know how to fight. I've never been in a fight. I mean, I've been in fights, but not—not this kind of fight. Not with people who are actually trying to kill me."
>
> *He looks at Dante, a moment of genuine fear on his face.*
>
> "This is fine. This is fine. I've got this."
