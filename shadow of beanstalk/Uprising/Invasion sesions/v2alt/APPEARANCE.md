## APPEARANCE

Lean, quick-moving, always in motion. Dark hair perpetually messy from running his hands through it. Refinery jumpsuit that's too large for him—he's been meaning to get it tailored for two years. Wears a tool belt that's slightly too heavy for his frame but he refuses to take anything off because "you never know when you'll need it."

His hands are clean—he works the systems, not the physical labor. A scar on his left forearm from a conveyor belt incident he tells everyone was "way cooler than it actually was." He has an infectious grin that he deploys in almost every situation, even when things are going badly.
