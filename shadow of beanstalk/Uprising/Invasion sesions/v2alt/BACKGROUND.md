## BACKGROUND

Sully came to Luna four years ago on a Melange contract. He was twenty-three, fresh out of a technical training program in Rio, and he thought he was going to change the world. The Moon was supposed to be his adventure.

The adventure turned out to be long hours, low pay, and watching his friends get replaced by clones. He stayed because he didn't have anywhere else to go. He stayed because he got good at the work. He stayed because he made friends.

He joined the rebellion because Yusuf told him it was the right thing to do. He believed it. He still believes it. He's not sure what happens if it doesn't work.
