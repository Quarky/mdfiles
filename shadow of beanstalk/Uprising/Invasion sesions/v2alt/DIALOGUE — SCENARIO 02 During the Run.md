## DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 02: During the Run

> SULLY: (at the terminal, fingers flying)
> "The schematics say there's a junction here. There's not. There's a wall. They changed it. They didn't update the plans."
>
> *A pause. His voice wavers.*
>
> "They changed it. I didn't plan for this."
>
> *But then he's moving again, talking again, covering the moment.*
>
> "It's fine. It's fine. I can work around this. I just need—I just need a minute. Give me a minute. I've got this."
