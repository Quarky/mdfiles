## GM NOTES — SULLY'S ARC

**Session 1 (Outpost 14 Heist):**
- Sully is excited, confident, and a little naive
- He makes mistakes but recovers
- He proves himself to the team

**Session 2 (Post-Heist):**
- The weight of what happened starts to sink in
- He's more serious, less talkative
- He's still optimistic, but with more awareness of the cost

**Session 3 (Kaguya):**
- The stakes are higher
- He's scared but still moving
- He might freeze at a critical moment, or he might surprise everyone

**Session 4 (Aftermath):**
- He's changed. The naivety is gone. The optimism is tempered.
- He's still fast-talking, but now it's to fill the silence that reminds him of what he lost.
- The overconfidence is gone. He's more careful. More deliberate. He's learning.
