## DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 04: Facing the Military

> SULLY: (seeing the military patrol ahead)
> "Okay. Okay. That's—that's a lot of soldiers. That's more soldiers than I thought there would be."
>
> *He looks at Dante.*
>
> "I'm going to tell you something. I'm really good at schematics. I can read a building like other people read a book. But I'm not good at—"
>
> *He gestures vaguely at the soldiers.*
>
> "This. I'm not good at this."
>
> *A pause.*
>
> "But I'm going to do it anyway. Because I don't know what else to do."
