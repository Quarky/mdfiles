## SCENE 1F — THE STREET KID (Insert during the search for the teenager, or after)

If Dante searches deeper into StarScape Level 4, he finds a child. She's maybe 10 years old. She's alone. She's hiding behind a collapsed display—a toppled clothing rack.

**The Encounter:**

> CHILD: "Are you a soldier?"

She's not afraid. She's decided that being afraid doesn't help.

> DANTE: "No. I'm not a soldier."

> CHILD: "Are you one of them? The ones who sat down?"

> DANTE: "Yes. I'm with them."

> CHILD: "I saw my mother sit down. She sat down in the atrium. She told me to hide. She told me to stay here. She said she'd come back."

The child is quiet for a moment. She's too young to pretend she's okay. She's not pretending.

> CHILD: "She hasn't come back."

**What It Means:**
- The child's mother is one of the workers
- She's missing
- She might be in custody
- She might be dead
- The child is alone
