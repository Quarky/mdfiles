## SCENE 1P — THE CLONE'S PRAYER (Insert near the end of the session)

If Dante finds Kai again, it is sitting in a maintenance alcove. It is not doing anything. It is just sitting, looking at nothing.

**The Moment:**

> KAI: "I was praying."

A pause.

> KAI: "I do not know if it makes a difference. I do not know if anyone is listening. But I have been told that prayer is what you do when you have nothing else."

> "I have nothing else."

A long pause.

> KAI: "I have been praying for the ones who sat down. For the ones who did not get up. I have been praying for the clones. For the ones who are still waiting."

> "I have been praying for someone to find me."

**What It Means:**
- Kai is afraid
- It is reaching out
- It wants to be seen
- It is praying
