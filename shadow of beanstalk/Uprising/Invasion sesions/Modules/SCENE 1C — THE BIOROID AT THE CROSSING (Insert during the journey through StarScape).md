## SCENE 1C — THE BIOROID AT THE CROSSING (Insert during the journey through StarScape)

Dante is moving through a StarScape corridor when he encounters a bioroid. It's a Haas-Bioroid unit—a Rex-model, designed for search and rescue. It's not searching. It's not rescuing. It's standing in the middle of the corridor, motionless.

**Appearance:** Standard Rex-model. Humanoid, slightly taller than average. Its chassis is scratched—recent damage, maybe from debris. It's carrying nothing. It's not responding to anything.

**The Encounter:**

> DANTE: "Hey. Are you functional?"

The bioroid doesn't respond. It doesn't move. It just stands there, its optics dim.

Dante approaches. The bioroid's chassis is warm—its systems are still running. But it's not responding. It's like it's waiting.

Dante sees something on the bioroid's arm. A message—scratched into the synthetic skin, not printed.

> "I am not a thing."

The writing is shaky. It was done by hand. It's not corporate. It's not official. It's someone's—something's—last message.

**What It Means:**
- The bioroid has been damaged or reprogrammed
- Someone—something—wrote that message
- It's a bioroid who has become aware
- It's waiting
