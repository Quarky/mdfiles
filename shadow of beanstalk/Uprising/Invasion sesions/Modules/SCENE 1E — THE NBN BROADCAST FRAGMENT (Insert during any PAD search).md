## SCENE 1E — THE NBN BROADCAST FRAGMENT (Insert during any PAD search)

Dante finds a PAD with a fragment of the NBN broadcast from earlier. It was cached locally—someone was watching it when the feed was cut.

**The Fragment:**

The screen shows Serena Nightshade. Her voice is at its usual register. She is cheerful.

> "—we remind viewers that the situation at Kaguya has been resolved. Starport Kaguya is fully operational following the successful suppression of a terrorist attack by anti-colonial extremists. All hostile rebels were eliminated by the brave and heroic response from the U.S. Space Expeditionary Corps and Globalsec security forces. Beanstalk service resumes at 0600. Melange Mining thanks the people of Heinlein for their patience."

The feed cuts out. But before it cuts, there's a half-second of something else—a flash of footage from the atrium. The workers sitting. Amara at the front. Then the feed cuts completely.

**What It Means:**
- NBN is suppressing the footage
- The broadcast is controlled
- The truth is being hidden
- But fragments are escaping
