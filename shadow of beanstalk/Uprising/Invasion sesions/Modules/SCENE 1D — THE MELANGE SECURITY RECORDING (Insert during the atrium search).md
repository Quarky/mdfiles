## SCENE 1D — THE MELANGE SECURITY RECORDING (Insert during the atrium search)

If Dante checks any security terminals in the atrium, he finds a recording. It's from a security camera—Melange facility security, not military. It was recorded 6 hours ago.

**The Recording:**

The camera shows the atrium. The workers are sitting—93 of them. Amara is at the front. The security camera shows her clearly.

Then the military arrives. The troopers enter through the upper-level walkways. They're in sealed suits. They move methodically.

Amara stands up. She says something—the audio is distorted, but the camera picks it up.

> "We're not terrorists. We're workers. We built this place. We dig the He-3 that powers your cities. We maintain the air systems that keep you alive. And we are tired of being invisible."

The military doesn't stop. They move through the workers. They don't fire—they push. They're clearing the area.

Amara doesn't move. The military grabs her. She doesn't fight—she just looks at them.

Then the camera cuts out.

**What It Means:**
- The occupation was peaceful
- Amara spoke to the military
- The military cleared the area
- Amara was taken
