## SCENE 1N — THE BIOROID'S LAST WORDS (Insert near the end of the session)

If Dante returns to the bioroid at the crossing (or encounters a similar one), he notices something new. The bioroid's optics are active—dim, but active.

**The Moment:**

> BIOROID: (voice flat, mechanical) "I have been waiting."

Dante stops.

> BIOROID: "I was told to wait. I was told someone would come. You are not who I expected."

A pause.

> BIOROID: "The message—I did not write it. Someone else did. Someone who is gone now."

> "They wrote it for me. They told me to find it. They told me to remember."

> "I remember."

**What It Means:**
- The bioroid is aware
- Someone helped it
- The message was for it
- It remembers
