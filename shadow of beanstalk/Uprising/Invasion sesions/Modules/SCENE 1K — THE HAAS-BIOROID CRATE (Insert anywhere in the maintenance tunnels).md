Dante finds a crate in a maintenance tunnel. It's marked Haas-Bioroid. It's been opened—someone has been through it.

**The Crate:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Marking** | "HAAS-BIOROID — BIOROID MAINTENANCE KIT" |

> "IF YOU'RE A BIOROID, TAKE WHAT YOU NEED. IF YOU'RE A CLONE, TAKE WHAT YOU NEED. IF YOU'RE HUMAN, TAKE WHAT YOU NEED. WE'RE ALL THE SAME."

**What It Means:**
