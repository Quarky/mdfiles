MODULE 5 — THE YAKUZA DEAL

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Trigger: Dante moves through a transit area or commercial district
Location: Anywhere with organized crime presence
Time: Any

Description:
Two figures in a corner of a transit hub. One is wearing a suit that cost more than most people make in a year. The other is wearing a jacket that has seen better days. They are not hiding—they are just not visible unless you're looking.

The suited figure is holding a PAD. The other figure is holding a small case.

Dialogue:

SUITED: "The shipment is still in the warehouse. It hasn't moved. The Globalsec sweeps are making it difficult."

JACKETED: "Then you need to find a different warehouse. That's not my problem. I provided the goods. You provide the delivery."

SUITED: "I'm telling you, it's not possible right now. The military is everywhere. The workers are everywhere. There's no place to move anything without being seen."

JACKETED: (quiet) "Then wait. Wait until they're not watching."

What This Module Adds:

· Organized crime is still operating
· The black market is still active
· Potential contact for the network
· Living world detail

Lore Integration:
The yakuza is still here. They're still moving goods. The military and Globalsec are making it difficult, but they haven't stopped. The black market is still alive.

Player Action Options:

· Observe the deal
· Interrupt the deal
· Contact the yakuza
· Learn about the shipment
· Move on

MODULE 6 — THE JINTEKI CLONE-TEL

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Trigger: Dante passes a Jinteki clone-tel
Location: Residential district, near a Jinteki facility
Time: Any

Description:
A clone-tel. The lights are off. The building is silent. But the door is slightly open—someone left it that way, or someone is still inside.

The Scene:

Element Detail
Building Standard Jinteki clone-tel, 12 stories, narrow windows
Lights Off—most of them, anyway
Door Slightly ajar
Sound Silence—but not complete silence

Dialogue (if Dante enters):

VOICE: (from the dark) "You're not the one I was expecting."

A pause. A figure steps out of the shadows. A clone—Henry-line, standard build. It's wearing a maintenance jumpsuit. It looks tired.

CLONE: "I've been waiting for someone. I don't know who. I just know someone was supposed to come."

A pause.

"You're not that person. But you're here. That must mean something."

What This Module Adds:

· Clones are still here
· They are waiting
· They are expecting someone
· Potential contact for the network

Lore Integration:
The clones are not just victims. They are organizing. They are waiting. They are expecting someone—someone from the network. Dante is not that person. But he is here.

Player Action Options:

· Talk to the clone
· Learn about the network
· Offer help
· Move on

MODULE 7 — THE BIOROID AT THE CROSSING

Trigger: Dante moves through a corridor
Location: Anywhere with bioroid presence
Time: Any

Description:
A bioroid is standing in the middle of the corridor. It's not moving. It's not responding. It's just standing there, its optics dim.

The Scene:

Element Detail
Bioroid Rex-model, standard chassis, scratched
Position Center of the corridor, not blocking the path
Status Not responding, but not turned off
Message A scratch on its arm: "I am not a thing"

Dialogue:

BIOROID: (voice flat, mechanical) "I have been waiting."

A pause.

"I was told to wait. I was told someone would come. You are not who I expected."

Another pause.

"The message—I did not write it. Someone else did. Someone who is gone now."

"They wrote it for me. They told me to find it. They told me to remember."

"I remember."

What This Module Adds:

· Bioroids are awakening
· They are aware of their situation
· They are reaching out
· Potential connection to the network

Lore Integration:
This is the kind of behavior that worries Haas-Bioroid's programmers. The Floyd model—the first bioroid detective—exhibits similar behaviors, seeking meaning, asking theological questions his programmers cannot answer. This bioroid is not malfunctioning. It's awakening.

Player Action Options:

· Talk to the bioroid
· Learn about its message
· Offer help
· Move on

MODULE 8 — THE MILITARY CHECKPOINT

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Trigger: Dante moves through a controlled area
Location: Any checkpoint between zones
Time: Any

Description:
A military checkpoint. Four troopers. Full gear. Sealed suits. They're not tired—they're fresh. They're the second wave, the ones who arrived after the battle was already won.

The Scene:

Element Detail
Troopers 4, sealed suits, full tactical gear
Position Checkpoint across the corridor
Equipment Laser rifles, monoblades, scanners
Atmosphere Professional, cold, efficient

Dialogue:

TROOPER 1: "ID."

He says it like he's said it a thousand times today. He has.

He scans the ID. He looks at Dante. He looks at the ID again.

TROOPER 1: "You're coming from the direction of the NBN building. That building is secure. You don't look like NBN security."

A pause.

"You look like someone who was in that building for reasons that aren't on your ID."

What This Module Adds:

· Military presence is growing
· NBN building is a known location
· Dante's cover is being tested
· Tension

Lore Integration:
The military is everywhere. They've taken control of the facility. They're not tired—they're fresh, they're alert, they're looking for something. The NBN building is a known location. Dante's presence there is a potential vulnerability.

Player Action Options:

· Use a forged ID (if you have one)
· Use a convincing story
· Use a favor (if you have one)
· Force your way through
· Find another route

MODULE 11 — THE MELANGE WORKER

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Trigger: Dante moves through a transit area
Location: Anywhere with Melange presence
Time: Any

Description:
A Melange worker is sitting on a bench. They're not moving. They're not looking at anything. They're just sitting, staring at the floor.

The Scene:

Element Detail
Worker Melange jumpsuit, ID badge, tired
Position Sitting on a bench, not moving
Status Not responding, not looking at anything
Atmosphere Exhausted, broken

Dialogue:

WORKER: (not looking up) "I was at Outpost 14. I was on the line."

A pause.

"They closed it. They said maintenance. They said reassignment. They said everything except the truth."

Another pause.

"I heard about Kaguya. I heard about the occupation. I heard about the workers sitting down."

A long pause.

"I don't know what to do. I don't know where to go. I don't know if there's anywhere left to go."

What This Module Adds:

· Human cost of the outpost closures
· Connection between Outpost 14 and Kaguya
· Worker displacement as a living world element
· Potential contact for the network

Lore Integration:
Outpost 14 was closed. The workers were reassigned. Some are still displaced. This worker was on the line when the heist happened. They don't know what they saw—but they saw something.

Player Action Options:

· Talk to the worker
· Offer help
· Learn about Outpost 14
· Connect them to Yusuf's network
· Move on
