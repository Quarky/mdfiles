## SCENE 1H — THE ORDER OF SOL PAMPHLET (Insert anywhere in StarScape)

Dante finds a pamphlet on the floor. It's from the Order of Sol. It's been stepped on, crumpled, but still readable.

> **ORDER OF SOL — SOLACE IN THE LIGHT**
>
> "In the darkness of this world, the Light of Sol guides us. We do not turn away from the suffering. We do not ignore the pain. We stand with those who are broken, those who are lost, those who are forgotten. For the Light is not a shield—it is a beacon. And we will shine until the darkness is gone."

**The Note on the Back:**

Written in the margin, in pencil, someone has added:

> "THE LIGHT DOESN'T STOP THE BULLETS. BUT IT SHOWS US WHERE TO STAND."

**What It Means:**
- The Order of Sol is present
- Someone—the person who wrote the note—is with them
- They are showing up
- They are standing in the path of the bullets
