## SCENE 1I — THE PROSPERITY DEPOT MURAL (Insert during any StarScape exploration)

Dante finds a mural in a StarScape corridor. It's not official—it was painted by someone, recently. The paint is still wet in places.

**The Mural:**

A worker—a miner—stands in front of the Moon. The miner is looking up. Behind them, the Moon is cracked, bleeding. The words are at the bottom:

> "THEY TOOK THE MOON. WE WILL NOT FORGET."

Underneath, someone has written:

> "PROSPERITY 43 — MURDERED BY MELANGE."

**What It Means:**
- The Prosperity Depot disaster is still remembered
- The Loonies have not forgotten
- Melange is responsible
- The Insurrection is connected to Prosperity
