## SCENE 1L — THE MESSAGE IN THE VENT (Insert during the maintenance tunnel journey)

Dante finds a message scratched into the wall of a ventilation shaft. It's fresh—the metal is still bright.

> "WE ARE NOT MACHINES."
> "WE ARE NOT PROPERTY."
> "WE ARE PEOPLE."
> "—KAI"

**What It Means:**
- Kai has been here
- It left this message
- It's reaching out
- It wants to be seen
