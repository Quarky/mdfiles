## THE ENCOUNTER — THE BREACHING TEAM

**Location:** Service corridor, between the maintenance tunnels and Dome Gamma
**Time:** Session start + 30-45 minutes

Dante and the miner team are moving through the service corridor when they encounter a breaching team. The military is ahead of them—they have already breached the seal. They are advancing into Dome Gamma.

**The Situation:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Military** | Breaching team. 6 troopers. Heavy equipment. |
| **Location** | Service corridor junction, between tunnels and Dome Gamma |
| **Objectives** | Advance into Dome Gamma. Secure the cargo AI. |
| **Dante's Position** | Behind them. They haven't seen him yet. |

**The Options:**

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Ambush** | Hit them from behind | Hard | High risk. Could eliminate the team. |
| **Bypass** | Find another route | Average | Avoid the encounter. |
| **Observe** | Watch and learn | Easy | Information about military tactics. |
| **Sabotage** | Disable their equipment | Hard | Slow their advance. |

**The Dialogue — Observing the Military:**

> FLETCHER: (whispering) "They're using the drone screen. Drone first, then troopers. They're disciplined. They're not making mistakes."

> RIKER: "The drone is scanning for heat signatures. If we move, it'll see us."

> SULLY: "What if we don't move? What if we wait?"

> MAEKO: "Then we're trapped."
