## THE ENCOUNTER — THE MILITARY PATROL

**Location:** Maintenance tunnel, section 35
**Time:** Session start + 45-60 minutes

Dante and the miner team encounter a military patrol in the maintenance tunnels. The military has begun using the tunnels—they are not just on the upper levels anymore.

**The Situation:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Military** | Patrol. 4 troopers. Scanning the tunnels. |
| **Location** | Maintenance tunnel junction, section 35 |
| **Objective** | Search for Loonies in the maintenance network |
| **Dante's Position** | Ahead of them. They haven't seen him yet. |

**The Options:**

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Hide** | Find cover in the tunnels | Average | Avoid the patrol. |
| **Ambush** | Hit them from cover | Hard | Eliminate the patrol. Risks casualties. |
| **Bypass** | Find another route | Average | Avoid the encounter. |
| **Distraction** | Create a noise elsewhere | Average | Draw the patrol away. |

**The Dialogue — The Patrol:**

> PATROL LEADER: (to the team) "Sweep the tunnels. They're using them to move between domes. We need to cut them off."

> TROOPER 1: "What if we find civilians?"

> PATROL LEADER: "Identify and report. Do not engage unless they engage first. But if they're in the way, clear them."

> TROOPER 2: "And the Loonies?"

> PATROL LEADER: "Neutralize."
