## THE MINER TEAM — REUNITED

**Location:** Maintenance tunnel, section 22
**Time:** Session start + 60-75 minutes

Dante finds the miner team. They are in a maintenance chamber—4x6 meters, cargo containers stacked against the walls. They are tired. They are wounded. They are still fighting.

**The Team:**

| Name | State | Action |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Riker** | Working on a panel | Overriding a system |
| **Mac** | Standing guard | Watching the tunnel |
| **Sully** | Talking | Fast. Nervous. |
| **Fletcher** | Looking at a PAD | Information. |
| **Maeko** | Pacing | Impatient. |

**DIALOGUE — THE REUNION:**

> SULLY: (talking before Dante is fully in the room) "I'm telling you, if we can get to the maintenance hub, I can override the atmospheric controls. We can create a decompression event that will slow them down for—"
>
> *He sees Dante. Stops.*
>
> "Dante! You're alive! I knew you were alive. I told them you were alive. I said, 'Dante's fine, he's too smart to—'"
>
> RIKER: (not looking up from his panel) "You're alive. Good. I was starting to think you weren't."
