## THE JOURNEY INTO THE TUNNELS

Dante moves into the maintenance tunnels. The transition is immediate: light, sound, and smell all change. The tunnels are amber-lit, emergency lighting. The air is recycled, stale, but not as bad as the outer ring. The structure is older, rougher—the bones of the facility, not the tourist infrastructure.

**The Sights:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **The hatches** | Some sealed. Some forced. Some open. |
| **The graffiti** | Arrows. Directions. Messages. |
| **The vents** | Open. People have been moving through them. |
| **The pipes** | Leaking. Hydraulic fluid. Water. Coolant. |
| **The power conduits** | Tapped. Someone has been powering equipment from them. |

**The Sounds:**

- The hum of the ventilation system
- The drip of leaking pipes
- The distant sound of footsteps
- The occasional voice—quiet, urgent
