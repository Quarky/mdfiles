## THE JOURNEY THROUGH STARSCAPE

Dante moves through the ruins of the tourist paradise. The retail levels are empty—the civilians have fled, been evacuated, or are hiding. The only movement is the automated systems—walkways still running, ads still playing, fountains still off.

**The Sights:**

| Location | What Dante Sees | What It Means |
|----------|-----------------|---------------|
| **The burned retail level** | Fire damage. Smoke still rising. | The occupation didn't end peacefully |
| **The abandoned food court** | Tables overturned. Food still on plates. | People fled in a hurry |
| **The broken fountain** | The one Amara turned off. Still off. | The symbol of the occupation |
| **The damaged transplas** | Cracks. Emergency seals holding. | The facility is wounded |
| **The graffiti** | More of it. Fresh. Angry. | The rebellion is here |

**The Sounds:**

- The creak of damaged structure
- The hum of emergency systems
- The distant sound of military operations
- The silence of the spaces that should be full of people
