## THE ARRIVAL — WHAT DANTE FINDS

### Discovery 1 — The Abandoned PAD

Dante finds it on the floor of a StarScape corridor. It's cracked. The screen is flickering. It still has power.

**The File:** A partial broadcast fragment. Four minutes of video. The Section F file. NBN cut the feed, but the fragment is still here.

**The Footage:**
- Amara sitting in the atrium
- The workers around her
- The military arriving
- The compression zone
- The decompression event
- The casualties

**The Last Frame:** A teenager's face—the one from São Paulo. She is crying. She is holding a PAD. She is filming. The frame freezes. The file is corrupted. The video stops.

**What This Means:**
- The broadcast was cut but not erased
- The fragment is cached
- Someone—the teenager—is still alive
- She has the full recording

### Discovery 2 — The NBN Suppression Order (Network)

If Dante can access the NBN facility's systems from his PAD (or if he took data with him):

**The File:** A single line in a classified NBN communication log.

> "Outpost 14 incident — suppress all coverage. Weyland SEA request. No acknowledgement to be made."

**The Log Details:**
- Date: 6 weeks ago
- Time: 72 hours post-Outpost 14 incident
- Ordering Party: Weyland Consortium, Space Elevator Authority liaison
- Status: Confirmed. Suppression active.

**What This Means:**
- Weyland knew about Section F
- NBN was ordered to suppress it
- The order went through the Space Elevator Authority
- There is a chain of command for this

### Discovery 3 — The Klaxon Log

If Dante finds a StarScape security terminal (still powered, still active):

**The Log:**
> "Emergency seal activation — Corridor B-7 — Time: 08:47 — Cause: Pressure differential collapse — Status: Contained. Casualties pending."

**The Note:**
> "EWS network interference flagged as cause. No further action."

**What This Means:**
- The decompression event was caused by EWS interference
- The military didn't do it deliberately—but they didn't prevent it
- The casualties are "pending"—the military doesn't know how many civilians died

---

### Discovery 4 — The Clone Handprint

On a wall in the service corridor, Dante finds a handprint. Not human—slightly smaller. Clone. A Henry-line clone.

**Near the Handprint:** A message, scratched into the wall with a tool.

> "WE ARE NOT MACHINES."

**What This Means:**
- The clones are here
- They are fighting
- They are fighting for themselves
