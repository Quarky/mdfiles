## THE WARNING

Dante is in the maintenance chamber with the miner team. The tunnel shakes. Dust falls from the ceiling. It's not an explosion—it's something heavier. Something bigger.

**The Sound:** A deep, resonant thud. Then another. Then a third. The sound of heavy equipment moving through the facility.

> RIKER: (looking up from his panel) "That's not artillery. That's breaching equipment. They're breaking through the upper-level seals."

> FLETCHER: (already moving) "They're not waiting. They're pushing now. We have minutes, not hours."

> MAEKO: "Where?"

> FLETCHER: "Everywhere. They're breaching the inner dome seals simultaneously. They want to overwhelm us."
