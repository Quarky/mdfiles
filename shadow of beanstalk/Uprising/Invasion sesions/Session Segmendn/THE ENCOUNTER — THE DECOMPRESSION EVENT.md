## THE ENCOUNTER — THE DECOMPRESSION EVENT

**Location:** Maintenance tunnel, section 38
**Time:** Session start + 60-75 minutes

The tunnel ahead of them seals. Emergency doors slide down. The pressure changes. The air thins.

**The Cause:** The military has created a decompression event in the section above them. The tunnels are connected to that section. The decompression is affecting the tunnels.

**The Situation:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Environment** | Tunnel sealed. Oxygen dropping. Pressure dropping. |
| **Cause** | Military decompression event above |
| **Time** | 5-10 minutes before oxygen becomes dangerous |
| **Options** | Find a way out, override the seals, or wait |

**The Options:**

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Override Seals** | Use Riker's skills to open the seals | Hard | Buys time. Opens a route. |
| **Find Alternative** | Find another route out | Average | Avoids the decompression. Takes time. |
| **Cut Through** | Cut through a wall to escape | Hard | Creates a new route. Takes time. |
| **Wait** | Wait for the decompression to stop | Easy | 5-10 minutes. Might not be enough. |

**The Dialogue — The Decompression:**

> RIKER: (looking at his PAD) "Oxygen is dropping. 80%... 75%... They're not stopping. They're holding the decompression."

> SULLY: "I can find another route. The schematics show a maintenance hatch 50 meters back."

> FLETCHER: "That's 50 meters of tunnel with dropping oxygen."

> MAEKO: "Then we move fast. Or we stay here and suffocate."

> RIKER: "I can override the seals. But I need 90 seconds. And I need cover."

> SULLY: "I can give you cover. I can—I can talk. I'm good at talking."
