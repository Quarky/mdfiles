## THE JOURNEY DEEPER

Dante and the miner team move deeper into the maintenance tunnels. The military is above them—the sounds of the advance are audible through the walls. The tunnels are becoming more complex, more degraded.

**The Sights:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **The structure** | Older. Rougher. The bones of the facility. |
| **The damage** | Cracks. Leaks. Degraded seals. |
| **The graffiti** | Older. Messages from people who were here before. |
| **The equipment** | Abandoned. Broken. Forgotten. |
| **The paths** | Some sealed. Some forced. Some open. |

**The Sounds:**

- The military above them—footsteps, commands, the hum of equipment
- The drip of water from a leaking pipe
- The groan of the structure under stress
- The silence of the spaces that are empty
