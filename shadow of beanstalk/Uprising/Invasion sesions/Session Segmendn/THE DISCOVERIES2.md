## THE DISCOVERIES

### Discovery 8 — The Miner Team's Gear

**Location:** Maintenance tunnel, section 14

Dante finds a maintenance alcove with equipment stacked against the wall. It's the miner team's gear—tool bags, spare PADs, a plasma torch, a cutting torch. They left it here when they went to fight.

**The Gear:**

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Riker's tool bag** | Heavy. Full of tools. A note on top: "Be right back." |
| **Mac's work jacket** | Heavy. Pockets full of tools. |
| **Sully's PAD** | Cracked. Still has the schematics. |
| **Fletcher's old uniform** | Folded. Waiting. |
| **Maeko's surface suit** | Neatly folded. Ready. |

**What This Means:**
- The miner team is here
- They left their gear
- They left in a hurry
- They are still fighting

### Discovery 9 — The Clone Network Node

**Location:** Maintenance tunnel, section 18

Dante finds a maintenance terminal. It's being used by someone—a clone. The terminal is logged in. The logs are recent.

**The Log:**
> "Handshake frequency — active. Node 14 — confirmed. Node 22 — pending. Node 31 — lost."

**The Note:**
> "We are not machines. We are not property. We are people."

**What This Means:**
- The clones are communicating
- They are using the maintenance network
- They are organizing
- Kai is not alone

### Discovery 10 — The Clone (Kai)

**Location:** Maintenance tunnel, section 18, around the corner from the terminal

Kai is there. Watching. Waiting.

**APPEARANCE:** Henry-line clone. Same height, same features, same work-worn hands. The scratch on its left wrist is the only distinguishing feature. It has been in the tunnels for twelve hours. It has not slept.

**DIALOGUE:**

> KAI: "You are the one. The one who ran. I saw you."
>
> *It is not afraid. It is not surprised.*
>
> "I have been in the tunnels for twelve hours. The military does not see me. They do not see us."
>
> *A pause.*
>
> "I have been told not to speak. I have been told that speaking is dangerous. I am speaking to you because I do not know who else to speak to."

**What Kai Knows:**

| Information | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **The clone network** | It is active. Clones are communicating. |
| **Zane's location** | In the tunnels. Wounded. |
| **The miner team** | Scattered. Some in the tunnels. Some in the domes. |
| **The military** | Advancing. Methodically. |
| **The EWS** | At the network. Breaking through. |

**What Kai Asks:**

> KAI: "Please don't let the rebellion move without knowing what it's moving for."

**What Kai Offers:**

| Offer | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Information** | The clone network. The maintenance handshake frequency. |
| **Access** | The maintenance network. The routes the military doesn't know. |
| **Connection** | The clones who are watching. Waiting. |
