## THE FIRST ENCOUNTER — REYES

**Location:** StarScape Level 3, near a collapsed retail display
**Time:** Session start + 5 minutes

Reyes is sitting against the wall. He is processing people—the queue is small, the same people he's been processing for hours. He looks hollow. He looks like someone who has not made a decision yet and doesn't know if he ever will.

He looks up when Dante approaches. Recognition flickers—the tube-lev. The checkpoint. The person who walked past without checking the departure board.

**DIALOGUE:**

> REYES: "You're alive. I didn't think you would be."
>
> *He gestures vaguely at the facility.*
>
> "I've been processing people for twelve hours. The tube-lev hasn't moved. They keep coming, but there's nowhere to send them."
>
> *He looks at Dante.*
>
> "I asked a question. To a woman. She was sitting in the atrium. I asked her what she was protecting. She said 'from who.' I didn't have an answer."
>
> *A pause.*
>
> "I still don't have an answer."
