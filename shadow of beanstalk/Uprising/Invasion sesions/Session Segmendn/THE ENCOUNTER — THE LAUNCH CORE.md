## THE ENCOUNTER — THE LAUNCH CORE

**Location:** Launch Core, Berth 3
**Time:** Session start + 75-90 minutes

Dante and the team reach the Launch Core. The military is already here. They are securing the core. The beanpods are still docked, but the military is holding them.

**The Situation:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Military** | 8 troopers. Securing the Launch Core. |
| **Location** | Launch Core, Berth 3 |
| **Objective** | Secure the beanpods. Prevent escape. |
| **Dante's Position** | In the maintenance tunnels, observing the core. |

**The Options:**

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Find Another Route** | Use the maintenance tunnels to reach a different pod | Average | Bypasses the military. Takes time. |
| **Wait** | Wait for the military to leave | Very Hard | They aren't leaving. |
| **Distraction** | Create a distraction to draw the military away | Hard | Buys time. Risks detection. |
| **Fight** | Engage the military | Very Hard | High risk. High reward. |

**The Dialogue — The Launch Core:**

> FLETCHER: (watching the military) "Eight troopers. They're securing the core. They're not leaving."

> MAEKO: "I can get us to the other side. There's a maintenance hatch on the far side of the core."

> RIKER: "If we get to the pod, I can override the departure sequence. It'll take 60 seconds."

> SULLY: "I can create a distraction. I can—I don't know, I can do something."

> MAC: (quietly) "I can hold them off. If it comes to that."
