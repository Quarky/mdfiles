## THE SURVIVORS — THE ATRIUM

**Location:** StarScape Atrium, various hiding spots

**Who Dante Can Find:**

| Person | State | Information |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **An older man** | Hiding behind a column | "I was there. I saw it all." |
| **A young woman** | Crying in a corner | "They took my sister. I don't know where." |
| **A child** | Lost, scared, alone | "I'm looking for my mother." |

**The Dialogue — The Man Behind the Column:**

> MAN: "I was there. In the atrium. I sat down with them. I sat down with Amara."
>
> *He looks at Dante.*
>
> "When the soldiers came, she stood up. She told them we were workers. She told them to stop."
>
> *He is shaking.*
>
> "They didn't stop. They just walked through us."
>
> *A pause.*
>
> "I saw them take her. I saw them take Amara. I don't know where. I don't know if she's alive."
>
> *He looks at Dante.*
>
> "Are you going to do something? Or are you just going to watch?"
