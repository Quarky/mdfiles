## THE TEENAGER — STARSCAPE LEVEL 5

**Location:** StarScape Level 5, a storage room behind a vending machine
**Time:** Session start + 45-60 minutes

The teenager from São Paulo is hiding in the storage room. She has been here for hours. She has not stopped recording. She has a PAD, cracked but functional. She has the footage.

**APPEARANCE:** Eighteen, first trip off-Earth. She is wearing Earth clothing—bright colors, practical but touristy. She has not changed her clothes in twelve hours. She is holding a PAD like it is the only thing keeping her alive.

**DIALOGUE:**

> TEENAGER: "You're the one. The one from the tube-lev. I saw you."
>
> *She is shaking. She is holding the PAD.*
>
> "I recorded everything. The occupation. The military. The—" *she chokes* "—the people. The people who died."
>
> *She looks at Dante.*
>
> "I tried to upload it. NBN cut the feed. But I have it. I have all of it."
>
> *She holds out the PAD.*
>
> "What do I do with it?"

**What She Has:**

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| A PAD | Cracked. Fully recorded footage. Full Section F file. |
| The Footage | Four minutes of the occupation, the military, the compression zone, the casualties. |
| Her Experience | First trip off-Earth. She's not going to forget it. |

**What She Wants:**
- To get the footage out
- To get off the Moon
- To survive

**What She Asks:**

> TEENAGER: "I don't know what to do. I'm just a tourist. I was supposed to be here for a week. I was supposed to see the Beanstalk. I was supposed to go home."
>
> *She is crying now.*
>
> "I want to go home. But I can't. Not yet. I have to get this out first."
