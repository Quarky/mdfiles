## THE DISCOVERIES

### Discovery 5 — The Refugee

**Location:** StarScape Level 3, a storage closet behind a collapsed retail display

A woman is hiding in the closet. She is in her 40s, worker's clothing, frightened. She has been here for hours. She saw what happened in the atrium.

**DIALOGUE:**

> WOMAN: "I was there. In the atrium. I sat down. I sat next to Amara."
>
> *She shakes. She is not holding together well.*
>
> "I didn't know. I didn't know they would come. The soldiers. They walked through like we were nothing."
>
> *She looks at Dante.*
>
> "Amara stood up. She told them we were workers. She told them to stop. They didn't stop. They just walked past her."
>
> *She starts to cry.*
>
> "They took her. I don't know where. I don't know if she's alive."

**What She Knows:**

| Information | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **The occupation** | 93 people sat down. Not all of them are accounted for. |
| **The military arrival** | They came from the rim. They didn't stop for civilians. |
| **The compression zone** | There are people trapped between the military and the Loonies. |
| **The teenager** | "She was recording. The one from Earth. She's still here. Level 5." |

**What She Has:**

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| A PAD | Dead battery |
| A small bag | Personal items |
| Information | The location of other survivors |
| Hope | Barely |

### Discovery 6 — The Loonie Fighter

**Location:** StarScape Level 4, a maintenance alcove

A Loony fighter is sitting against the wall. She is wounded—a leg injury, not fatal but bad. She has been here for hours. She is running out of options.

**DIALOGUE:**

> FIGHTER: "You're not military. You're not one of us either. What are you?"
>
> *She sizes Dante up.*
>
> "I've been here since the occupation. I was on the rim when the dropships came. We fell back. We've been falling back since."
>
> *She gestures at her leg.*
>
> "I'm not going to make it to the tunnels. I need you to tell Zane. Tell him—"
>
> *She pauses.*
>
> "Tell him the rim is lost. We lost the rim. We lost the outer ring. We only have the inner domes. And we won't hold those forever."

**What She Knows:**

| Information | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Military position** | The rim is fully controlled. The outer ring is falling. |
| **Loony position** | The inner domes are held. The tunnels are contested. |
| **Zane's location** | In the maintenance tunnels beneath Dome Gamma. |
| **The casualties** | She doesn't know. She doesn't want to know. |

**What She Has:**

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| A pistol | Standard. Low ammo. |
| A PAD | Cracked. Still functional. |
| Information | The military is still advancing. |

### Discovery 7 — The Clone Maintenance Log

**Location:** StarScape Level 4, a Jinteki maintenance terminal

Dante finds a maintenance terminal. It's still powered. It's still logged in—someone has been using it recently.

**The Log:**
> "Handshake frequency — Maintenance network — active. Node 14 — contact confirmed. Node 22 — contact pending. Node 31 — contact lost."

**The Notes:**
> "We are not machines. We are not property. We are people."

**What This Means:**
- The clones are communicating
- They are using the maintenance network
- They are organizing
- Kai is not alone

### Discovery 11 — The Prosperity Connection

**Location:** Maintenance tunnel, section 28

Dante finds a wall covered in graffiti. Some of it is fresh—hours old. Some of it is old—years old. The old graffiti is faded, partially covered, but still visible.

**The Old Graffiti:**
> "REMEMBER PROSPERITY."
> "THEY KILLED US."
> "WE WILL NOT FORGET."
> "PROSPERITY WAS MURDER."

**The New Graffiti:**
> "WE REMEMBER."
> "THEY TOOK THE MOON."
> "PROSPERITY WAS JUST THE BEGINNING."

**What This Means:**
- The Prosperity Depot disaster is still remembered
- It is the origin of the Insurrection
- The Loonies have not forgotten
- The new graffiti is fresh—the Insurrection is connected to Prosperity

**The Communication:**

**What This Means:**

- Project Nisei is the neural experimentation program
- Outpost 14 is Section F
