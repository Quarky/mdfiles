## THE ATRIUM — THE SITE OF THE OCCUPATION

**Location:** StarScape Atrium
**Time:** Session start + 30-45 minutes

The atrium is empty. The fountain is off. The workers who sat here are gone. The tourists who filmed here are gone. The soldiers who cleared them are gone.

What remains:

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **The fountain** | Off. Dry. Amara turned it off. It hasn't been turned back on. |
| **The chairs** | Some are overturned. Some are arranged in rows. |
| **The PADs** | Abandoned. Some still recording. Some dead. |
| **The blood** | Not a lot. But some. |
| **The silence** | The atrium was built to echo. It doesn't anymore. |

**What Dante Can Do:**

| Action | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|------------|---------|
| **Search the PADs** | Average | Find fragments of the recording |
| **Search for survivors** | Hard | Some might still be here |
| **Check the fountain** | Easy | Amara left something? |
| **Look at the blood** | Average | Identify patterns, maybe evidence |
