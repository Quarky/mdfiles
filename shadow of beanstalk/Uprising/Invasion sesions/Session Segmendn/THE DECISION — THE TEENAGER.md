## THE DECISION — THE TEENAGER

**The Teenager Looks at Dante:**

> TEENAGER: "What do I do? I'm just a tourist. I don't know what to do."

**Dante's Choices:**

| Option | What It Does | Outcome |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| **Take the PAD** | Take the footage | He has the evidence |
| **Send her to the tunnels** | Guide her to safety | She survives. The footage survives. |
| **Send her to the Launch Core** | Get her on a pod | She gets off the Moon. The footage gets out. |
| **Keep her with you** | She stays with Dante | He protects her. He uses her footage. |
