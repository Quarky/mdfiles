## NPC ENCOUNTER — THE REFUGEE
**StarScape Level 3, storage closet behind a collapsed retail display**

A woman in her mid-forties. Worker's clothing — He-3 processing division patches.
She has been in this closet for hours. She was in the atrium. She saw everything.

She looks at Dante with the specific terror of someone who has already been
through something and is trying to assess whether this is more of that.

> **WOMAN:** "I was there. In the atrium. I sat down.
> I sat next to Amara. I didn't know—"
>
> She stops herself. Starts again.
>
> "I knew. I knew what we were doing. I just didn't know they would come
> like that. The soldiers. They walked through us like we were furniture.
> Like we weren't people, like we were just things in the way."
>
> She looks at her hands.
>
> "Amara stood up. She told them we were workers. She held out her hands.
> She said 'we have demands.' They didn't even look at her.
> They just walked past. And then they came back for her.
> And they took her."
>
> She starts to cry — not dramatically, the controlled Moonborn way,
> the way people cry in a place where there is no spare emotional infrastructure
> for other people's grief.
>
> "There was a girl. From Earth — Brazil, I think. She was filming.
> She had her PAD up the whole time. She's still here.
> Level 5. She wouldn't leave."

**What she has:** A dead PAD. The last thing on it before the battery died was
the beginning of a message to her daughter in the lower ring.
The message was never sent. If Dante can charge the PAD, the message
is still in draft form — a mother trying to explain to her daughter
what she did today and why.
