## THE ATRIUM — THE AFTERMATH

The atrium where the sit-down occupation happened. The center of StarScape.
Usually the most beautiful space in Kaguya — high ceiling, the Earth visible
through the dome overhead, the fountain at the center, the retail levels
rising around it in tiers.

Now: evidence of what happened here.

**READ ALOUD:**

> *The fountain is dry. The chairs that were arranged in concentric circles
> during the occupation are scattered — some knocked over, some pushed back,
> one against the far wall as if it was thrown there. The floor has marks
> from pressure suit boots — the specific pattern of military-grade soles,
> precise and numerous, moving in organized formation through a space
> that was full of seated people.*
>
> *There are personal effects on the floor. A work badge. A PAD with
> a cracked screen. A child's drawing — someone brought a child here,
> or a child followed someone here. The drawing is in crayon.*
>
> *The drawing shows a tall building reaching up to a planet.*
> *The building is the Beanstalk.*
> *The planet is Earth.*
> *There are small figures at the top and bottom.*
> *The figures have names written beside them.*
> *You cannot read the names from here.*
>
> *You could read them if you crouched down.*
> *The GM pauses.*
> *You do not have to crouched down.*
> *But you could.*

**The Klaxon Log** — if Dante finds a StarScape security terminal:

```
Emergency seal activation — Corridor B-7
Time: 08:47
Cause: Pressure differential collapse
Status: CONTAINED
Casualties: PENDING

EWS network interference flagged as contributing cause.
No further action recommended.
```

"EWS network interference." The military used the electronic warfare suite
to compress the corridor. The decompression event was not targeted at civilians.
The civilians were in the atrium. The atrium was in the corridor.
The military's model did not classify them as a variable worth adjusting for.
This is "collateral modeled at acceptable parameters" in real time.
