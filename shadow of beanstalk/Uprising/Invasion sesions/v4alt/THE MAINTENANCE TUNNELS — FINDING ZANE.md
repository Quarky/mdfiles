## THE MAINTENANCE TUNNELS — FINDING ZANE

The maintenance tunnels run beneath StarScape and the dome clusters —
a network of service corridors and infrastructure passages built for
Haas-Bioroid maintenance crews and engineering staff.

The workers know these tunnels. They have worked them for years.
The military does not know them. This is one of the few advantages
the workers still hold.

Zane's position: a maintenance chamber in the tunnel system beneath
Dome Delta, approximately 200 meters from the Beanstalk terminus junction.

**Getting there:** Navigation check or guidance from someone who knows the tunnels.
Riker (encountered later) knows the schematics. Sully knows the tunnels by
instinct — he has been in maintenance his whole working life.
