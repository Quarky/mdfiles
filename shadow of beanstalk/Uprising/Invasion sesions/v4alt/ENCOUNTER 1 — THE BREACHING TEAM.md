## ENCOUNTER 1 — THE BREACHING TEAM
**Service corridor between maintenance tunnels and Dome Gamma**
**Time: ~30-45 minutes into Part 2**

Dante and the miner team are moving through the service corridor when they
encounter a military breaching team coming the other way. Six troopers.
Heavy equipment. They have already breached the seal — they are advancing
into Dome Gamma to secure the cargo AI systems.

They haven't seen Dante yet. The team is behind them.

**TACTICAL SITUATION:**

```
MILITARY: 6 troopers, EWS support drone, breaching equipment
LOCATION: Service corridor junction, between tunnels and Dome Gamma
OBJECTIVE: Secure cargo AI systems — "Nisei Project" secondary objective
DANTE'S POSITION: Behind them, in the shadow of a collapsed wall section
VISIBILITY: The drone is scanning for heat signatures on a 15-second cycle
```

**OBSERVE (Easy — Dante watches without acting):**

> **FLETCHER:** *(whispering)* "They're using the drone screen. Drone first, troopers follow.
> They're disciplined. They're not making mistakes."
>
> **RIKER:** "The drone is on a 15-second scan cycle. Eight-second gap between passes.
> Not enough to move the whole team but enough to move one person."
>
> **SULLY:** "What if we wait? What if we just—"
>
> **MAEKO:** "Then we're trapped. The military is pushing from the east as well.
> If we don't move now, they seal the corridor and we're in a pocket."

**OPTIONS:**

| Option | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Ambush** — hit from behind | Hard | High risk. Could eliminate team. Loud. Draws response. |
| **Bypass** — Sully finds alternate route | Average | Avoids encounter. 15-minute delay. |
| **Sabotage** — disable breaching equipment | Hard | Slows their advance 30+ minutes. |
| **Observe and follow** — gather intel | Easy | Learn their objective. Information. No immediate impact. |

**If the team observes long enough:**

The breaching team finds a terminal in Dome Gamma. Riker can intercept
their data stream if Dante can create a 30-second distraction.

The intercepted data: **"Priority Objective: Secure Beanstalk infrastructure.
Secondary Objective: Neutralize hostile elements. Tertiary: Locate and recover
data labeled 'Nisei-Project.' Jinteki liaison insists on data recovery.
Authorization Level: BLACK. No civilian interference."**

No civilian interference — meaning they are not trying to harm civilians.
Meaning they are also not required to protect them.
Meaning the atrium was not a massacre. It was an absence of protection.
The distinction is meaningful. It is not consoling.

**EWS BREACH DATA** (if Riker can access military comms):
```
EWS breach progress: 82%
Departure freeze compromised.
Estimated time to full breach: 43 minutes.
Recommend immediate push to secure Launch Core.
```
