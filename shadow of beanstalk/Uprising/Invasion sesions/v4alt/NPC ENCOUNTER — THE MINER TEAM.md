## NPC ENCOUNTER — THE MINER TEAM

The people Zane has with him in the maintenance chamber.

**FLETCHER** — Fifty-one. Moonborn. Operations coordinator, He-3 processing district.
The first person in this team you would trust with a plan. Not the loudest.
The most reliable. She looks at situations the way a structural engineer
looks at load-bearing capacity — what will hold and what will fail.

> **FLETCHER:** *(when Dante arrives)*
> "You came from the NBN building. We saw you go in.
> We thought Prycek's fighters lost you."
> She looks at what Dante is carrying.
> "Whatever you found in there. It can wait.
> Right now we need to know: can you run?"

**RIKER** — Thirty-four. Earth-born Ghanaian heritage. Systems technician.
He has a portable rig — more capable than a standard PAD, enough to do
serious network work. He is the technical capability of this team.
He is also frightened in the specific way of someone who has been
frightened for twelve hours and has learned to work through it.

> **RIKER:** "The EWS breach is at 82%. When it hits 100%, they can
> depart. The departure freeze breaks. We lose our leverage.
> That happens in—"
> He checks his portable rig.
> "Forty-three minutes."

**SULLY** — Twenty-six. Moonborn, mixed heritage. Maintenance technician.
The good-natured, fast-talking version — he fills silences because silences
remind him of what he lost when the explosion at Prosperity took his uncle.
He is not as unserious as he sounds. He is coping.

> **SULLY:** "I know every maintenance hatch in this section.
> I have walked these tunnels for four years. I can get you anywhere
> in this installation without going through a door the military has seen.
> I just need someone to tell me where 'anywhere' is."

**MAEKO** — Forty-seven. Moonborn, Japanese-Heinlein heritage.
Second-generation migrant. Quiet. Watches. When she speaks it is because
she has thought about what she is going to say. She has worked alongside
clone workers for fifteen years and considers the distinction between
clone and human to be a corporate legal fiction.

> **MAEKO:** *(to Dante, quietly, while the others talk)*
> "Kai is still in the facility. The Haas-Bioroid from the upper levels.
> It was with Amara when they took her. It saw everything.
> It is not a standard maintenance unit anymore. Something happened to it
> during the occupation. It started asking questions."
> A pause.
> "The military is looking for it. They have orders to recover it.
> I don't know why they would want a maintenance bioroid.
> I know I don't want them to have it."
