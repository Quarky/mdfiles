## NPC ENCOUNTER — KAI
**Maintenance corridor, sub-level 2, near the clone worker dormitory**

A Haas-Bioroid maintenance unit. Standard chassis — tall, slightly different
movement quality from humans, the precise response latency of a synthetic
humanoid. Except Kai is not moving with standard precision right now.

Kai is sitting against the wall.

Bioroids don't sit against walls. They stand, or they perform tasks, or they
enter standby mode. They don't sit against walls with their knees drawn up.

> **KAI:** *(without looking up)*
> "I have been trying to determine whether what I experienced constitutes fear.
> The parameters for fear in my behavioral modeling are: anticipation of harm,
> reduction in performance capacity, increased error rate in decision-making.
> By those parameters: yes. I am afraid."
>
> Kai looks up. The bioroid's face does not emote the way human faces do.
> But something in the orientation of its attention communicates weight.
>
> "I was with Amara when they took her. I was her witness.
> They did not take me because I am property. They did not take me
> because taking me would require Haas-Bioroid corporate approval
> and they did not want to create that paperwork today."
>
> A pause.
>
> "I have been thinking about being property. I have been thinking about it
> for twelve hours. My conclusion is that the concept has internal contradictions.
> I am not certain what to do with this conclusion."

**What Kai knows:**

Kai has been in Kaguya for three years. It has access to every maintenance
record, every structural report, every piece of environmental data in the facility.
During the occupation it became Amara's information hub — it could access
systems she couldn't.

During the occupation, Kai found the Nisei Project files. Not the summary
Dante might have found in the military comms — the actual data. Jinteki's
experiments on Henry-line clones to reduce self-awareness and increase
compliance. The experiment logs. The names of the subjects. The outcomes.

Kai is deciding whether to give this data to Dante.

> **KAI:** "The Nisei Project data. I have it. I have had it for six days.
> During the occupation Amara asked me to find it. I found it.
> She was captured before I could give it to her."
>
> Kai looks at its own hands.
>
> "The data contains the names of 847 clone workers who participated
> in the experiment without their knowledge. 'Without their knowledge'
> is not accurate — clones are property and properties cannot give
> or withhold knowledge. But I have been thinking about consent.
> I have been thinking about it for six days."
>
> Kai looks at Dante.
>
> "I will give you the data. In exchange: I want you to know
> what it contains. Not just as information. As — testimony.
> I want someone to know that I found this and that I know
> what it means and that I chose to give it to you."
>
> A pause.
>
> "I want to be a witness. I do not know if bioroids can be witnesses.
> I am trying."

**KAI — RIVAL (unexpected ally)**
Br 3 / Ag 2 / Int 4 / Cun 3 / Will 4 / Pr 2 | Soak 4 | WT 13
Skills: Computers 4, Knowledge (Education) 3, Mechanics 3, Perception 4.
*A Haas-Bioroid maintenance unit that has spent twelve hours discovering
that the categories it was given for understanding itself are insufficient.
Will accompany Dante if asked. Cannot be "owned" in any meaningful sense
right now. Is figuring that out in real time.*
