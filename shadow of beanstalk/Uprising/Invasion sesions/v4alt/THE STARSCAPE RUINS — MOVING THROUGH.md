## THE STARSCAPE RUINS — MOVING THROUGH

As Dante moves from the NBN exit toward the maintenance tunnels or the atrium,
the environment tells the story of the occupation.

**WHAT HE PASSES:**

| Location | What Dante Sees | What It Means |
|----------|-----------------|---------------|
| The burned retail level | Fire damage. Smoke still rising. Automated suppression systems fighting it. | The occupation didn't end peacefully |
| The abandoned food court | Tables overturned. Food still on plates. Chairs pushed back in hurry. | People fled in minutes |
| The broken fountain | Dry. The fountain Amara turned off as her first act. Still off. | The symbol of the occupation, still present |
| The damaged transplas dome | Cracks spider-webbing from a central impact point. Emergency sealant holding. | The facility is wounded |
| The moving walkways | Still running. Carrying nothing. Maintaining their schedule for absent passengers. | The infrastructure does not know the humans are gone |

**THE SOUNDS:**

The creak of a structure under unusual stress.
The hum of atmospheric processors running harder than they should.
Distant, irregular gunfire — moving methodically, room to room.
And underneath it all: a sound Dante has to stop to identify.

Crying. Somewhere in the ruins. A person, trying to be quiet about it
and not quite succeeding.
