## NPC ENCOUNTER — LIEUTENANT VASORA

She is the SXC field commander. The first SXC deployment on the Moon.
No playbook. No precedent. She is making this up as she goes and she
is very good at making things up as she goes, which is why she is here.

She sees Dante before he expects her to. She has been watching that
maintenance tunnel for twenty minutes.

> **VASORA:** "I don't know how you got past the cordon. I don't know
> who you are. I know you've been in this facility for six hours
> and you've been in places that you should not have been able to reach."
>
> She does not raise her weapon. She has not lowered it either.
>
> "I'm going to ask you one question. You can answer it or not.
> But I want you to understand that I am asking it genuinely."
>
> A beat.
>
> "What did you find in the NBN building?"

**If Dante answers:**

Vasora listens completely. She does not interrupt. When he finishes:

> **VASORA:** "I have been given orders. My orders are to secure this facility,
> neutralize hostile elements, and protect Beanstalk infrastructure.
> My orders do not include 'recover Jinteki proprietary data labeled Nisei-Project'
> but that order came in two hours ago through a different channel."
>
> She looks at the beanpods.
>
> "I secured the atrium. I secured it correctly according to my orders.
> I have been thinking about the atrium for four hours."
>
> She looks at Dante.
>
> "There is a beanpod leaving in nine minutes. My orders are to secure
> beanpods against unauthorized departure. I will spend the next nine
> minutes securing the south corridor junction."
>
> She walks toward the south corridor junction.
>
> "If unauthorized personnel use Pod 3 in the next nine minutes,
> I will address that in my after-action report."
>
> She does not look back.

**If Dante doesn't answer or tries to bypass her:**

Vasora assesses the situation. She has been in the field for twenty-four hours
and has not been wrong yet. She is also genuinely uncertain about what she
is supposed to be doing at this specific moment.

> **VASORA:** "I'm going to give you thirty seconds to make a decision.
> After that, the situation changes in ways that are not reversible.
> Use them."
