## CLOSING IMAGE

The pod detaches. The magnetic connection releases.
The Beanstalk terminal is behind them — or behind some of them.

Through the pod's small viewport, if anyone looks:
Kaguya. The dome cluster. The outer ring where the fighting was.
The atrium dome with its spiderweb crack, the emergency sealant holding.
The NBN building where Dante spent hours in the basement of the conspiracy.

The pod begins its descent toward Earth. Or remains at Kaguya.
Depending on who went and who stayed.

On the PAD-to-PAD network in the outpost transit corridors of Heinlein —
the dead zones where SYNC can't reach — a video is spreading.
Or it isn't. Depending on what Dante decided.

And on Dante's PAD: the AI's message. Still there. Still waiting.
"Down the rabbit hole, Dante? Or do you prefer the blue pill."
"If the rabbit hole, chop chop."

The session ends here.
