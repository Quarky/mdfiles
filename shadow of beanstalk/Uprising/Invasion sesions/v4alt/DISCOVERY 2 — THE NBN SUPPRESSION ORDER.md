## DISCOVERY 2 — THE NBN SUPPRESSION ORDER

If Dante can access the NBN facility's systems from outside through his PAD,
or if he took data with him from the building:

A single line in a classified NBN communication log, dated six weeks ago,
72 hours after the Outpost 14 incident:

```
"Outpost 14 incident — suppress all coverage.
Weyland SEA request. No acknowledgement to be made."

Ordering party: Weyland Consortium, Space Elevator Authority liaison.
Status: Confirmed. Suppression active.
```

Weyland knew about Section F. NBN was ordered to suppress it.
The order went through the Space Elevator Authority.
The Beanstalk is Weyland's. The Beanstalk authority ordered the suppression
of seventeen clone workers being killed in an unlicensed Jinteki experiment
at a Melange facility. Three corporations. One order. One silence.
