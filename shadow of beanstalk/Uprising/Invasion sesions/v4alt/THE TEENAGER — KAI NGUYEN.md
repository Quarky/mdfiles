## THE TEENAGER — KAI NGUYEN
**StarScape Level 5, hiding in a service junction behind a collapsed food vendor**

Seventeen. Brazilian. Came to Heinlein with her mother six months ago
on a work visa. Her mother is in the He-3 processing district.
She came to StarScape because she thought it was safe.

She has a PAD. The PAD has the full Section F footage — the uncut version,
four minutes and thirty-two seconds, including the faces of the workers,
the arrival of the military, the compression zone, the aftermath.

She has been hiding for six hours because she knows that what is on her PAD
matters and she does not know what to do with it and she is seventeen
and she is alone.

> **KAI NGUYEN:** *(when Dante finds her)*
> "Are you military?"
>
> She asks it carefully, the way someone asks a question when
> the answer determines everything.
>
> When Dante says no:
>
> "I have it. The whole thing. I didn't stop recording.
> I didn't stop even when they — I kept recording."
>
> She holds up her PAD.
>
> "I don't know what to do with it. I know it matters.
> I know if the right person has it, it matters.
> I don't know who the right person is."

**If Dante tells her about Petra Vasquez:**

> "The journalist? The one who did the Outpost 14 story?
> NBN suppressed it. I know. My mother was following it on the
> PAD-to-PAD network."
>
> She looks at her PAD.
>
> "If I send it to her through the PAD-to-PAD network,
> NBN can't suppress it before it's cached. It's already on
> a hundred PADs before they can run the Sigma protocol."
>
> She looks at Dante.
>
> "Show me how to send it and I'll send it.
> But I'm not getting on that pod. My mother is in the lower ring.
> She doesn't know I'm here. I need to get back to her."
