## DISCOVERY 3 — THE CLONE HANDPRINT

On the wall of a service corridor near StarScape's lower maintenance level.
A handprint. Not human — slightly smaller. Clone proportions. Henry-line.
Beside it, scratched into the wall with a tool:

> *WE ARE NOT MACHINES.*

Below that, in a different hand — shakier, more urgent:

> *KAI SAW IT. KAI KNOWS. FIND KAI.*

"Kai" is a Haas-Bioroid maintenance unit that has been at Kaguya for three years.
Something happened to Kai during the occupation. Something that changed
what kind of entity Kai currently is.
