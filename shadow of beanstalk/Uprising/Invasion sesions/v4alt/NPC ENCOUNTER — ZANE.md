## NPC ENCOUNTER — ZANE

Zane is in the maintenance chamber's inner section — a smaller space
separated from the main chamber by a pressure door. He is wounded.
Not critically — a shrapnel fragment from an EWS burst, sealed and
treated, but the kind of wound that reminds the body of its limits.

He is sitting against the wall with a tactical map on a physical printout —
the digital version was compromised when they lost the secure channel.
He looks up when Dante enters.

> **ZANE:** "You're alive. Good. I was starting to wonder."
> He looks at Dante with the assessment of someone who has been
> making personnel decisions for twelve hours and has learned to read
> quickly.
> "Tell me what you found. All of it. We have—"
> He checks his watch.
> "Forty-three minutes before the EWS breaks the departure freeze.
> Talk fast."

**If Dante tells him about the NBN archive:**

> **ZANE:** *(quiet for a long moment)*
> "The Blackout was planned. Before we existed as a movement,
> before Prosperity, before any of this —"
> He looks at the tactical map.
> "The system we've been fighting is the aftermath of a decision
> made by one man who decided that the people who would die
> were acceptable losses."
> He looks at Dante.
> "That doesn't change what we're doing here. If anything—"
> He stops.
> "It means we were right to fight. The system is not an accident.
> It was built. It can be unbuilt."

**If Dante tells him about the AI's message:**

> **ZANE:** "The AI contacted you."
> Not a question.
> "It contacted runners during the Vertex years. That's documented.
> The whole psychographic engine — the thing that knows everyone's
> behavior — it has been working with specific runners for decades.
> Making them better. Using them."
> He looks at Dante.
> "The question is whether you can use it back."
> A beat.
> "The question after that is whether you'd know the difference."

**On his current plan:**

> **ZANE:** "They knew. They knew about Section F before we made our move.
> They had Sigma-7 ready before the first junction was taken.
> We were visible in the behavioral data weeks before we acted.
> The AI predicted us."
>
> He looks at the map.
>
> "Amara is in Dome Epsilon. Military command. They're questioning her.
> She's not saying anything — she hasn't said anything for twelve hours.
> She won't. But they'll run the psychographic profile on her eventually.
> The model will predict what she knows and what she'll do with it."
>
> He looks at Dante.
>
> "The broadcast. The Section F footage. The teenager has the full recording.
> If that footage gets to Petra Vasquez — to the wire services —
> before Sigma-7 can suppress it, it's already cached on too many PADs
> to pull back. That's what matters now. Not the military. The broadcast."

**ZANE — NEMESIS**
Br 3 / Ag 3 / Int 2 / Cu 3 / Wi 4 / Pr 3 | Soak 5 | WT 17 | Def 1/1
Skills: Athletics 2, Brawl 2, Cool 3, Coercion 3, Leadership 3,
  Melee 2, Ranged (Light) 2, Streetwise 3, Vigilance 3.
Talents: Adversary 1, Inspiring Rhetoric, Natural Leader.
Equipment: Monoblade (Melee; Dam +3; Crit 1; Breach 1, Vicious 2),
  custom modded pistol, light armor.
*Wounded but functional. Has been making decisions for twelve hours
that people will study for decades. Knows this. Is not performing heroism.
Is doing a job that needed doing.*
