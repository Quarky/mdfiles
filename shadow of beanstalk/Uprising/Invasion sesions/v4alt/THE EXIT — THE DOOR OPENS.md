## THE EXIT — THE DOOR OPENS

Dante has been inside the NBN facility for hours.
The air in the facility was recycled, climate-controlled, corporate-sterile.
The air that hits him when the door opens is different.

**Read aloud:**

> *Smell first. Smoke. Ozone. Hydraulic fluid. Something metallic and sharp
> that you recognize before you can name it. Blood. You've smelled blood before.
> This is different. This is a lot of blood.*
>
> *Sound second. The Beanstalk hum is gone. The constant vibration that you
> didn't notice until it stopped — the vibration that has been in the walls and
> floors of this installation since before you got here — is absent. What remains:
> distant gunfire, irregular. Emergency sirens on automated cycles. The groan of
> a structure under stress. Voices. Shouted, urgent, far away.*
>
> *Sight third. StarScape is burning. Not the whole thing — small fires,
> scattered, persistent. The tourist paradise you walked through hours ago
> is a ruin. The retail levels are empty. The moving walkways are still running,
> carrying no one. The holographic advertisements still cycle, still bright,
> still promising luxury and escape, to a space that is completely empty.*

**THE GRAFFITI:**

The walls have writing that was not there when Dante entered the building.
Some of it is fresh — hours old, the marker still slightly wet in the cold air.
Some of it looks days old. Some looks older than today should allow.

> *WE REMEMBER.*
> *THEY TOOK THE MOON.*
> *THE PROSPERITY DEPOT WAS MURDER.*
> *SECTION F. SEVENTEEN DEAD. JINTEKI LIED.*
> *THEY WILL NEVER TAKE HEINLEIN.*

The Insurrection has been building for a long time.
Dante is just seeing the surface of it now.
