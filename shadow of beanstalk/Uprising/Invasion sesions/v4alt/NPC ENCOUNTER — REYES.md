## NPC ENCOUNTER — REYES
**StarScape Level 3, near a collapsed retail display**
**Time: Session start + 5-10 minutes**

Reyes is sitting against the wall beside a collapsed display case.
He is processing people — or was, until there were no more people to process.
Now he is just sitting. The queue tablet is in his lap. He is staring at nothing.

He is the checkpoint officer from the tube-lev. He recognizes Dante.

> **REYES:** "You're alive. I didn't think you would be."
>
> He gestures vaguely at the space around them.
>
> "I've been processing people for twelve hours. The tube-lev hasn't moved.
> They keep coming, but there's nowhere to send them."
>
> He looks at Dante.
>
> "I asked a question. To a woman. She was sitting in the atrium.
> I asked her what she was protecting. She said 'from who.'
> I didn't have an answer."
>
> A pause.
>
> "I still don't have an answer."

**What Reyes knows:**

| Information | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Military position | Holding the rim. Waiting at inner dome entrances. |
| Amara | Captured. Holding in Dome Epsilon. Alive, as of two hours ago. |
| Zane | In the maintenance tunnels. Alive. Wounded. |
| The broadcast | Cut, but fragments are spreading on PAD-to-PAD. |
| The clones | Some are fighting. Some are helping civilians. Some are gone. |
| The teenager | Still here. StarScape Level 5. Has the full recording. |

> **REYES:** "I'm going to tell you something and then I'm not going to stop you.
> I'm also not going to help you. I just — can't stop you."
>
> He looks at the empty queue tablet.
>
> "I don't know if that makes me a good person or a bad person.
> I've been sitting here for twelve hours trying to figure that out."

**REYES — MINION (non-combatant, informant)**
Br 2 / Ag 2 / Int 2 / Cun 2 / Will 3 / Pr 2 | Soak 2 | WT 8
*Will not interfere. Will answer questions. Has been processing the same
moral equation for twelve hours and has not arrived at an answer.*
