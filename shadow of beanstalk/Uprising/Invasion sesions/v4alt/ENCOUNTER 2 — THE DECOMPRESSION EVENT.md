## ENCOUNTER 2 — THE DECOMPRESSION EVENT
**Maintenance tunnel, section 38**
**Time: ~60-75 minutes into Part 2**

The tunnel ahead seals. Emergency doors slide down. The pressure changes.
The air thins. The team has entered a section adjacent to a military
decompression zone.

**TACTICAL SITUATION:**

```
ENVIRONMENT: Tunnel sealed. Oxygen dropping. Pressure dropping.
CAUSE: Military decompression event in adjacent section above
TIME: 8-10 minutes before oxygen becomes dangerous
HEAT SIGNATURES: Military drone will detect them if they stay still too long
```

**RIKER:** *(looking at his PAD)* "Oxygen at 80%... 75%...
They're holding the decompression. They know someone is in the tunnels.
They're flushing us out."

**SULLY:** "I can find another route. There's a maintenance hatch
50 meters back. But 50 meters of tunnel with dropping oxygen—"

**MAEKO:** "Then we move fast."

**FLETCHER:** "Riker. Can you override the emergency seals from here?"

**RIKER:** "90 seconds. I need to get to the junction panel and I need
someone between me and whatever's on the other side of that door."

**SULLY:** *(quietly)* "I can talk. If anyone comes through that door,
I can talk to them. I'm very good at talking. I'm —"
He looks at the oxygen indicator.
"I'm also terrified. But I can still talk."

**OPTIONS:**

| Option | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Riker overrides seals** | Hard | 90 seconds to reach panel. Team must provide cover. Opens escape route. |
| **Sully finds alternate route** | Average | 50 meters at reduced oxygen. Sully leads. Fast movement required. |
| **Cut through wall** | Hard | Sully uses his maintenance cutter. Creates new route. Takes time. |
| **Wait for decompression to stop** | Very Hard | It doesn't stop. This is not an option. |

**AFTER THE ESCAPE:**

The team comes through either the override or the alternate route into
a section that has not been reached by the military yet. On the wall:

More graffiti. This piece is different. It is older — weeks old.
Not from the occupation. From before.

> *"My name is Priya. I was at Prosperity. I survived.*
> *I am telling you this because someone should know.*
> *The depot was not an accident.*
> *The safety system was disabled four hours before the explosion.*
> *Someone knew. Someone chose.*
> *The name in the maintenance log is Supervisor ID: SF-472-K.*
> *Find that name."*

SF-472-K. The same supervisor ID from the Section F files.
The same ID that appears in the Dome Gamma cargo incident logs.
The same person who signed off on the Outpost 14 incident.
One person, three incidents, three corporations, three suppressions.
