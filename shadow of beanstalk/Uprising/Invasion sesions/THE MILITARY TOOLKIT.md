## THE MILITARY TOOLKIT

### Available Technology

| Technology | Capability | Counter- Tactic |
|------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Wall-Penetrating Sensors** | Detects heat signatures, movement, and electronics through up to 1m of material | Thermal shielding (blankets, suit layers); moving in sync with facility machinery to mask signatures |
| **Mass Drivers (Gauss Rifles)** | High-velocity projectiles; can penetrate light walls | Heavy cover (plasteel, concrete); moving unpredictably; not staying in one position |
| **EWS Drones** | Full sensor suite (heat, motion, acoustic, visual) | Moving after the scan, before the re-scan; using maintenance hatches; thermal shielding |
| **Sealed Environmental Suits** | Immune to atmo tricks, decompression, gas, smoke | Decompression doesn't stop them; need alternative tactics |
| **Non-Lethal Suppression** | Sonic disruptors (stun, disorient), flashbangs, tear gas | Ear protection, sealed suits, moving through gas |
| **Laser Designators** | Target painting for orbital weapons | Cannot be jammed; need to move before orbital strike arrives |
| **Breaching Charges** | Controlled explosives for doors and walls | Can be heard from far away; gives warning |
| **Stun Grenades** | Blast 8, Disorient 3, Stun Damage | Ear protection, sealed suits |
| **Wall-Penetrating Lasers** | Low-power laser for cutting through panels, silent | Can be heard if close; leaves a small hole |
| **Drone Swarms** | 3-5 small recon drones, hard to spot | Area-effect weapons, EM interference, hitting them |

### Military Personnel Types

| Type | Role | Numbers | Motivation |
|------|------|---------|------------|
| **Fire Team (4 troopers)** | Standard tactical unit | 12-16 at Kaguya | Follow orders, complete mission, survive, get home |
| **Breach Team (6 troopers)** | Door and wall breaching specialists | 6 at Kaguya | Open paths, keep advance moving, first through the breach |
| **EWS Support (4 operators)** | Network warfare, drone control | 4 at Armstrong Base | Win the cyberwar, degrade enemy comms, track enemy movements |
| **Sniper Element (2 troopers)** | Long-range precision fire | 2 at Kaguya | Eliminate high-value targets, provide overwatch, suppress enemy movements |
| **Exosuit Pilots (2 troopers)** | Heavy weapons and mobility | 2 at Kaguya | Control the crater rim, prevent escape, dominate open areas |
| **Support Element (2 troopers)** | Ammo, medical, coordination | 2 at Kaguya | Keep the team operational, coordinate with command, provide medical care |
| **Vasora (Commander)** | Overall command | 1 at Kaguya | Complete the mission, minimize casualties, lead by example |
