### Tactical Role — System Breaker

Riker is not a front-line fighter. He is a system breaker. He can bypass security, create distractions, and manipulate the environment.

| Action | Skill | Difficulty | Effect |
|--------|-------|------------|--------|
| Bypass Security | Mechanics | Average | Disable a security system, open a locked door, or reset a sensor |
| Create Distraction | Mechanics | Hard | Trigger a false alarm, short out a power conduit, or cause a small explosion |
| Environmental Manipulation | Mechanics | Daunting | Change atmospheric pressure, trigger a fire suppression system, or redirect coolant |
| Cut Through Obstacle | Mechanics (w/ Plasma Torch) | Average | Cut through metal, concrete, or transplas |

**Motivation:**
- **Obsession:** He can't stop taking things apart. He's here to understand the system.
- **Loyalty:** He's committed to the team. He won't leave them.

**Tactics:**
- Move to the mechanical systems. Bypass the security. Create confusion.
- Don't engage in direct combat. You are more valuable as a saboteur.

**Player Interaction:**
- Riker is useful for getting through obstacles. If a door is locked, he can open it.
- He is also useful for creating distractions—he can make a lot of noise in a different location.
- He needs someone to watch his back. He loses himself when he's working.

**Combat Example:**
*The squad is pinned down in a corridor. The military is advancing. Riker is in a maintenance closet, working on the power conduit. He can cause a localized power surge—blinding the military's thermal imaging for 10 seconds. That's enough time for the squad to move. He needs 60 seconds of cover to complete the bypass.*
