# ACT 1 — EXIT THE NBN FACILITY (15-20 min)
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

## THE DOOR OPENS

Dante has been inside the NBN facility for hours. The air in the facility was recycled, climate-controlled, corporate-sterile. The air that hits him when the door opens is different.

**Smell First:** Smoke. Ozone. Hydraulic fluid. Something else—something metallic and sharp. Blood. He's smelled blood before. This is different. This is a lot of blood.

**Sound Second:** The Beanstalk hum is gone. The constant vibration he didn't notice until it stopped is the first thing he notices. What remains: distant gunfire. Emergency sirens. The groan of a wounded structure. Voices—shouted, urgent. And underneath it all, the ventilation system working harder than it should.

**Sight Third:** StarScape is burning.

Not the whole thing—small fires, scattered, persistent. The tourist paradise is a ruin. The retail levels are empty. The moving walkways are still running, carrying no one. The holographic ads still cycle, still bright, still promising luxury and escape to an empty space.

**The Graffiti:** "WE REMEMBER." "THEY TOOK THE MOON." "THE PROSPERITY DEPOT WAS MURDER." "THEY WILL NEVER TAKE HEINLEIN."

The walls are covered in it. Some of it is fresh—hours old. Some of it is days old. Some of it looks like it has been there for weeks. The Insurrection has been building for a long time. Dante is just seeing it now.
