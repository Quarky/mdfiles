# THE ENDING — THE BROADCAST
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Regardless of what Dante does or doesn't do, the broadcast comes. The version below is Serena Nightshade's version—what runs on every public screen in Heinlein, on every tourist PAD in Kaguya, on the feeds that Earth receives:

> "Starport Kaguya is fully operational following the successful suppression of a terrorist attack by anti-colonial extremists. All hostile rebels were eliminated by the brave and heroic response from the U.S. Space Expeditionary Corps and Globalsec security forces. Beanstalk service resumes at 0600. Melange Mining thanks the people of Heinlein for their patience."

The screen shows the crater from orbit. It looks like a spaceport.

**What the camera doesn't show:**
- The compression zone's aftermath
- The clone casualty count that won't be filed
- Okonkwo somewhere in the outpost network, opening a maintenance corridor
- Kai—wherever Kai is
- Petra Vasquez, if she got out, already writing
- Zane—whether he made it or didn't
- The teenager from São Paulo, with the recording

The War that follows the Insurrection is not shown. The Treaty of Heinlein that ends the War is not shown. The megacorps tightening their grip on the colonies is not shown.

The screen shows the crater.

Dante is wherever he is.

From the pod viewport, if Dante looks:

The Moon is grey. No atmosphere to give it color. The tiny cluster of lights at Heinlein visible for about four minutes before the trajectory carries it out of sightline.

The lights don't go out. They diminish. There is a difference.

Somewhere in those lights:
- Yusuf, already building the next network
- Okonkwo, who opened a maintenance corridor
- The Authority officer, back to her PAD
- Reyes, still processing people, not yet deciding
- Amara, sitting in a processing queue with her contractor badge face-up
- The teenager from São Paulo, not yet knowing what to do with her recording
- Kai, in the maintenance tunnels, watching, waiting to speak

The War hasn't started yet.

It started today.
