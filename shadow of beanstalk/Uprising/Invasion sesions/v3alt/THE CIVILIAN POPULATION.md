## THE CIVILIAN POPULATION

There are civilians in Kaguya. Some are tourists. Some are workers. Some are corporate staff. Some are families. Some are alone.

**Where They Are:**
- Some are in the inner domes, in shelters
- Some are in the outer ring, hiding
- Some are in the compression zone, in the middle
- Some are dead

**What They Want:**
- To leave
- To survive
- To be seen
