## THE AGE OF MEGACORPORATIONS

In the not-so-distant future, humanity has spread across the solar system, unlocked the frontiers of cyberspace, and created millions of intelligent androids in its own image. At the heart of this progress stands a ladder leading to the riches of the stars—the massive space elevator called the Beanstalk.

Four monolithic megacorporations control the vast majority of human interests:

**Haas-Bioroid** leads the world in cybernetics and high-tech research, holding the monopoly on bioroid manufacture and providing everything from cheap labor and pleasure bots to units specialized for network security. What Haas-Bioroid accomplishes digitally, **Jinteki** accomplishes biologically—by producing genetically engineered clones and researching the psychic potential of the human genome. Jinteki aims to push human evolution forward, at a profit.

**NBN** are the undisputed masters of information, supplying the global network's insatiable hunger for news and entertainment. **Weyland Consortium** uses brute economic force and dirty tactics to dominate heavy industry, construction, and space technologies.
