## ACT 3 — THE MAINTENANCE TUNNELS

### SCENE 1L — THE MESSAGE IN THE VENT

Dante finds a message scratched into the wall of a ventilation shaft. It's fresh—the metal is still bright.

> "WE ARE NOT MACHINES."
> "WE ARE NOT PROPERTY."
> "WE ARE PEOPLE."
> "—KAI"

**The Lore Connection:** Kai has been here. It's reaching out. It wants to be seen. The clone network is active and growing.

---

### SCENE 1M — THE SMUGGLER'S CACHE

Dante finds a cache in a maintenance tunnel—hidden behind a loose panel. It's not military. It's not Loony. It's something else.

**The Cache:**

| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Credit chips | Small stack |
| Forged IDs | 3 sets |
| Medical supplies | Partial kit |
| Monofilament knife | 1 |
| PAD | Encrypted, cracked |

**The Note:**

A small piece of paper, folded. It says:

> "FOR THE WORKERS. FOR THE ONES WHO SAT DOWN. FOR THE ONES WHO DIDN'T GET UP."

**The Lore Connection:** Someone is helping the workers. They were preparing for this. They had resources. They were ready.

---

### SCENE 1N — THE BIOROID'S LAST WORDS

If Dante returns to the bioroid at the crossing (or encounters a similar one), he notices something new. The bioroid's optics are active—dim, but active.

**The Moment:**

> BIOROID: (voice flat, mechanical) "I have been waiting."

Dante stops.

> BIOROID: "I was told to wait. I was told someone would come. You are not who I expected."

A pause.

> BIOROID: "The message—I did not write it. Someone else did. Someone who is gone now."

> "They wrote it for me. They told me to find it. They told me to remember."

> "I remember."

**The Lore Connection:** The bioroid is aware. Someone helped it. The message was for it. It remembers.

---

### SCENE 1O — THE NBN CREW

If Dante finds the NBN broadcast crew, they are in a StarScape level, tucked away behind a collapsed retail display. They are scared. They are hiding.

**The Crew:**

| Person | Role | State |
|--------|------|-------|
| **Correspondent** | NBN reporter | Shaking, but still holding a PAD |
| **Camera Operator** | NBN tech | Shaking, but still has the camera |
| **Audio Tech** | NBN tech | Hiding |

**The Dialogue:**

> CORRESPONDENT: "We were told to broadcast from here. We were told it was safe."
>
> "They cut the feed. They told us to stop. They told us to hide."
>
> "I don't know what to do. I don't know what to do."

**The Lore Connection:** NBN is suppressing the story. The crew is scared. They have footage. They don't know what to do with it.

---

### SCENE 1P — THE CLONE'S PRAYER

If Dante finds Kai again, it is sitting in a maintenance alcove. It is not doing anything. It is just sitting, looking at nothing.

**The Moment:**

> KAI: "I was praying."

A pause.

> KAI: "I do not know if it makes a difference. I do not know if anyone is listening. But I have been told that prayer is what you do when you have nothing else."

> "I have nothing else."

A long pause.

> KAI: "I have been praying for the ones who sat down. For the ones who did not get up. I have been praying for the clones. For the ones who are still waiting."

> "I have been praying for someone to find me."

**The Lore Connection:** Kai is afraid. It is reaching out. It wants to be seen. It is praying.
