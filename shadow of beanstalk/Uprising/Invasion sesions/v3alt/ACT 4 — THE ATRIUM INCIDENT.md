## ACT 4 — THE ATRIUM INCIDENT

### SCENE 1Q — THE BLACKOUT MEMORIAL

Dante finds a small memorial tucked away in a StarScape corner. It's not official—it was put here by someone, recently. A candle burns at its base. A small PAD displays a looping message.

> **THE BLACKOUT — 2031**
>
> "We were blind. We were helpless. We lost everything in a moment."
>
> "Never again."

**The PAD Message:**

Beneath the main text, someone has added:

> "They blamed the virus. They blamed the hackers. They blamed anyone but themselves."
>
> "But we remember. We remember what they did. We remember what they took."
>
> "And we will not forget."

**The Lore Connection:** The Blackout is still remembered. The conspiracy theories are still alive. Someone believes the Blackout was deliberate. They're connecting it to the present.

---

### SCENE 1R — THE CLONE-TEL

Dante passes through a section of the facility that was once a clone-tel—a housing facility for Jinteki clones. It's been converted, repurposed, abandoned.

**The Scene:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Bunks** | Stacked three high. Clothes still folded. |
| **Personal Items** | A few. Left behind. |
| **The Walls** | Covered in graffiti. Clones wrote messages to each other. |
| **The Atmosphere** | Empty. Quiet. Abandoned. |

**The Messages:**

> "WE ARE NOT MACHINES."
> "I AM A PERSON."
> "I HAVE A NAME."
> "MY NAME IS [BLANK]."

**The Lore Connection:** The clones lived here. They left messages. They wanted to be seen. They wanted to be known.

---

### SCENE 1S — THE HAAS-BIOROID AD

Dante passes a Haas-Bioroid advertising hologram. It's still active—it hasn't been shut off. It shows a smiling family with a bioroid servant.

> **HAAS-BIOROID — THE PERFECT COMPANION**
>
> "Bioroids make life easier. They work tirelessly. They never complain. They're the perfect addition to any home."

The ad is playing to an empty StarScape. It's cheerful. It's bright. It's obscene.

**The Graffiti:**

Someone has defaced the ad. Written across the hologram, in large letters:

> "THEY ARE NOT PERFECT. THEY ARE PEOPLE."

**The Lore Connection:** The bioroids are being seen. Someone is speaking for them. The line between machine and person is blurring.

---

### SCENE 1T — THE NBN SUBSUMPTION LOG

If Dante accesses an NBN terminal, he finds a log. It's the subsumption log for the broadcast—the moment NBN cut the feed.

**The Log:**

> "Subsumption Request — Emergency Broadcast Suppression."
> "Origin: Weyland Consortium, Space Elevator Authority."
> "Authorization: BLACK."
> "Status: Confirmed. Suppression active."

**The Note:**

At the bottom of the log, someone has added—in red text, overriding the file:

> "THEY KNEW. THEY KNEW ABOUT SECTION F. THEY KNEW ABOUT THE NISEI PROJECT. THEY KNEW ABOUT THE PROSPERITY DEPOT."
>
> "AND THEY SUPPRESSED IT ALL."

**The Lore Connection:** Weyland suppressed the broadcast. They knew about Section F. They knew about Nisei. They knew about Prosperity. They have been hiding it all.

---

### SCENE 1U — THE MARTIAN COLONIAL AUTHORITY FEED

If Dante checks any data feeds, he finds a fragment from the Martian Colonial Authority. It's a public statement, but it's been flagged as "incoming."

> **MARTIAN COLONIAL AUTHORITY — OFFICIAL STATEMENT**
>
> "The Martian Colonial Authority is monitoring the situation on Luna with concern. We urge all parties to seek a peaceful resolution."
>
> "We have authorized no military intervention in the Luna situation. Mars is not involved in this conflict."

**The Lore Connection:** Mars is watching. They're distancing themselves from the conflict. They're not involved—yet. That could change.

---

### SCENE 1V — THE NISEI PROJECT — FULL FILE

If Dante has found multiple fragments, he can access the full Nisei Project file. It's encrypted—but he has enough fragments to decrypt it.

**The File:**

> **PROJECT NISEI — COMPLETE OVERVIEW**
>
> Project Lead: Dr. Katashi Hara, Jinteki Senior Director of Neural Research
> Supervisor: MEL-221-REG-HAAS, Melange Regional Operations Manager
> Locations: Outpost 14, Outpost 22, Outpost 37, Heinlein Docklands Facility 7
> Subjects: Henry-line clones. Estimated count: 247.
>
> **Objective:**
> Create workforce with reduced self-awareness, increased compliance.
> Neural parameter adjustment through direct surgical intervention.
>
> **Status:**
> Active. Continuing.

**The Lore Connection:** The Nisei Project is real. 247 clones are subjects. Someone is working to save them.

---

### SCENE 1W — THE ORDER OF SOL'S HIDDEN CHAPEL

Dante finds a hidden chapel in the maintenance tunnels. It's not official—it was created by someone, recently. It has a small altar, a few candles, and a sign.

> **ORDER OF SOL — SOLACE IN THE LIGHT**
>
> "All are welcome here. Human. Clone. Bioroid. We are all children of the Light."
>
> "In the darkness, we shine. In the silence, we speak. In the fear, we stand."

**The Note:**

On the altar, a small piece of paper:

> "WE STAND WITH YOU. WE WILL NOT FORGET. WE WILL NOT FORGIVE."

**The Lore Connection:** The Order of Sol is present. They are supporting the Insurrection. They are helping the clones. They are standing with the workers.

---

### SCENE 1X — THE CLONE'S PRAYER (CONTINUED)

If Dante finds Kai again, it is still praying. It hasn't moved.

> **KAI:** "I have been praying for the ones who did not get up. I have been praying for the ones who are still waiting. I have been praying for the ones who are afraid."

> "I have been praying for the ones who will come after us."

A long pause.

> **KAI:** "I do not know if it will make a difference. I do not know if anyone is listening."

> "But I cannot stop."

**The Lore Connection:** Kai is afraid. It is reaching out. It wants to be seen. It is praying.

---

### SCENE 1Y — THE NBN EMPLOYEE

Dante finds an NBN employee hiding in a StarScape corridor. She's not a reporter—she's a technician. She's terrified.

> **TECHNICIAN:** "I was just doing my job. I was just—I was just doing my job."

> "They told me to cut the feed. They told me to suppress the broadcast. I didn't—I didn't know what it was."

> "I saw it. I saw the footage. I saw the atrium. I saw the workers. I saw—"

She stops. She's shaking.

> **TECHNICIAN:** "I helped them suppress it. I helped them hide the truth."

> "I'm a part of this. I'm part of what they did."

> "What do I do?"

**The Lore Connection:** NBN employees are complicit. Some of them are conflicted. Some of them want to help. They don't know how.

---

### SCENE 1Z — THE JINTEKI-CLONE CONNECTION

If Dante has been tracking the clone network, he finds a connection: Jinteki clones are not just victims—they are also organizers.

**The Data:**

> "Clone Network — Nodes: 14. Status: Active."
> "Node 7 — Kai. Node 7 has been communicating with other clones."
> "Messages sent: 247. Messages received: 198."
> "Network is growing."

**The Lore Connection:** The clones are organizing. Kai is a central node. The network is growing. The clones are becoming a force.

---

### SCENE 1AA — THE WAR

If Dante can access the network one last time, he finds a fragment. It's from a distant source—Mars, maybe. It's a news feed.

> **MARTIAN COLONIAL AUTHORITY — EMERGENCY BROADCAST**
>
> "The Martian Colonial Authority has authorized increased security measures in response to the Luna situation. All travelers from Luna to Mars will be subject to enhanced screening."
>
> "We urge all Martian citizens to remain calm. The situation is under control."

**The Lore Connection:** The War is spreading. Mars is locking down. The Insurrection is having effects. The world is changing.
