## ACT 1 — THE LAUNCH CORE STANDOFF

### SCENE 3A — THE AUTHORITY OFFICER

Dante can see the Authority officer at the boarding gate. She is the one who processed him earlier. She is still at her post. She looks tired. She looks like she has made a decision.

> OFFICER: "You're still here. I thought you would have left by now."
>
> *She looks at him. She knows who he is.*
>
> "The military is securing the core. They'll be here in 5 minutes. If you're going to leave, you need to do it now."
>
> *A pause.*
>
> "I can't help you. I can't stop them. I can just... not see you."

**The Lore Connection:** The Authority officer is Lunar-born. She has been on the Moon her whole life. She knows what the Insurrection is about. She's choosing not to see. That's the most she can do.
