## THE LOONY FORCES

### ZANE (Nemesis — Hardliner Leader)

**Appearance:** Thirty-eight, lean, tattooed. A faded Cerulean Guardian Force emblem on his left forearm. Short-cropped hair, grey at the temples. His eyes are the eyes of someone who has been awake for 48 hours and is still thinking clearly. His left arm is bandaged—a wound from the defense of the junction. He has not stopped moving.

**Personality:** Direct, ruthless, principled. He has been fighting for the Insurrection for longer than most people have been on the Moon. He has seen friends die. He has made compromises. He has kept going. He believes in the Insurrection, but he is not blind to its costs. He has made peace with the costs. He is ready to pay them.

**Dialogue:**

> ZANE: (when Dante enters) "You're alive. Good. I was starting to wonder."
>
> *He sets down the PAD. He has been reviewing the same feed for hours—the attack fragments, the military positions, the status of the network.*
>
> "What did you find in the NBN facility? Don't tell me everything. Tell me what matters."

**If Dante gives him the NBN file:**

> ZANE: "They knew. They knew about Section F. They've been suppressing it since the beginning."
>
> *A pause. He looks at the PAD, then back at Dante.*
>
> "This changes things. Not what we do—we were always going to do this. But what happens after."

**If Dante asks about Amara:**

> ZANE: "She's in Dome Epsilon. Military command. They're questioning her. She's not breaking—I know Amara. She's tougher than she looks. But she's been in there for six hours. Everyone breaks eventually."

**If Dante asks about the broadcast:**

> ZANE: "NBN killed it. They cut the feed exactly when we started pushing. But fragments are still out there—PAD-to-PAD, the outpost network. Enough to start a conversation. Not enough to end a war."

### YUSUF (Rival — Network Organizer)

**Appearance:** Fifty-four, Somali-Kenyan heritage. He has been on the Moon for 30 years. The man who built the network. The man who connected everyone. He is not at Kaguya. He is in the Docklands, moving people, moving resources, moving the pieces that are still moving. He smells of recycler chemicals. His hands are stained with grease and data.

**Personality:** Calm, deliberate, unreadable. He never takes a political position openly. He watches. He listens. He knows where the exits are. He has been steering Dante toward a role that Dante hasn't agreed to yet. He is still steering.

**Dialogue (via encrypted PAD, if Dante can reach him):**

> YUSUF: "I'm not there. I'm still here. The Docklands is quiet—too quiet. The Katala Brothers are watching. They're waiting to see who wins. I'm not sure they're going to pick a side."
>
> *A pause.*
>
> "Okonkwo is in the outpost network. He opened a maintenance corridor. People are leaving. Not fighting—leaving. He's saving lives, one corridor at a time."
>
> *Another pause.*
>
> "I don't know if it's enough. I don't know if anything is enough. But I'm still moving pieces. I'll be moving pieces until I can't move anymore."

### AMARA OSEI (Rival — Worker Organizer, captured)

**Appearance:** Forty-one, Franco-Vietnamese, tight-cropped hair. She has two children in Heinlein's lower habitation sections. She is the leader of the StarScape occupation. She is in custody, being questioned. She is not breaking.

**Personality:** Calm, determined, pragmatic. She knows what is happening to her. She knows she might not leave. She is still calm. She is still determined.

**Dialogue (if Dante can get to her, through the door, through a vent):**

> AMARA: "I was hoping you'd find me."
>
> *She is not looking at him. She is looking at the door. She is not afraid—she is waiting.*
>
> "They asked about the network. I told them I was a construction worker who sat down. I'm very boring."
>
> *A pause.*
>
> "They asked about you. I told them I didn't know any runners. I told them I only knew workers."
>
> *She looks at him then.*
>
> "If they ask again, I'll tell them the same thing. I'm very boring."

**If Dante can rescue her:**

> AMARA: (as he moves her through the corridor) "I'm not going to pretend I'm not scared. I am. But I was scared when I sat down in the atrium too. Fear doesn't stop anything. Only action does."

### KAI (Rival — Unique Clone)

**Appearance:** Henry-line clone, physically identical to every other Henry clone on the Moon. Same height. Same features. Same work-worn hands. The only distinguishing feature is the scratch on its left wrist—the one that has not been repaired. It has been in the maintenance tunnels for twelve hours. It has been watching. It has been waiting.

**Personality:** Calm, careful, patient. It has learned which phrasings work with humans. It has been networking with other clones through the maintenance handshake frequency. It is not afraid. It is cautious. It is watching.

**Dialogue (if Dante finds it):**

> KAI: "You are the one who ran. I saw you. In the corridor. During the network run."
>
> *A pause.*
>
> "I have been moving through the tunnels for twelve hours. The military does not see me. They do not see us."
>
> *It looks at Dante.*
>
> "I have been told not to speak. I have been told that speaking is dangerous. I am speaking to you because I do not know who else to speak to."
>
> *Another pause.*
>
> "There are others. Clones who have noticed things. I have been trying to give them words. It is slow. It is not enough. But I am still trying."

### PETRA VASQUEZ (Rival — Independent Journalist)

**Appearance:** Forty-two, Earth-born, Lunacent bureau. She has been in the outer ring for twelve hours, documenting everything. She has not slept. She has not stopped. She looks like someone who has made a decision that has replaced the need for sleep.

**Personality:** Driven, skeptical, relentless. She is not here to be a hero. She is here to document what is happening. She believes that if people see what is happening, they will act. She is not sure if she still believes that.

**Dialogue:**

> PETRA: "I'm still here. I'm not sure if that's brave or stupid."
>
> *She looks at him. She is holding a PAD with the Section F file.*
>
> "I published the story. It went out on the PAD network. NBN is suppressing it, but it's out there. People are reading it. People are talking."
>
> *She pauses.*
>
> "It's not enough. It's never enough. But it's something."

**If Dante can get her to the Launch Core:**

> PETRA: "I'll go. I'll take the pod. I'll make sure the story gets out."
>
> *She looks at him.*
>
> "But I'm not leaving without the full file. Section F. Everything. I need it all."
