## TACTICAL ROLE

**System Breaker:** Sully can bypass security, create distractions, and manipulate the environment. He knows the schematics and can find routes nobody else knows about.

**Schematics Expert:** He can identify facility anomalies, structural weaknesses, and environmental hazards. He can find alternative routes and maintenance hatches that aren't on any tactical map.

**Flaw — Overconfidence:** He sometimes takes risks that are unnecessary. He sometimes promises things he can't deliver. He is always surprised when things go wrong.
