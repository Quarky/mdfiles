## THE MILITARY FORCES

### LIEUTENANT VASORA (Rival — SXC Commander)

**Appearance:** Thirty-one, sharp, disciplined. She has been awake for thirty-six hours. She has not stopped. She is at the front of her element, not behind it. This is either bravery or doctrine. She is not sure which it is.

**Personality:** Professional, driven, exhausted. She believes in the mission. She believes in the Beanstalk. She believes in her troopers. She is doing the job. She is tired. She is still doing the job.

**Dialogue:**

> VASORA: (when Dante encounters her) "I don't know how you got past the cordon. I don't care. If you have a valid departure slot, use it now. If you don't, I can't help you."
>
> *She looks at him. She knows him—or she knows what he looks like from a file. She doesn't say anything about it.*
>
> "The Beanstalk is down. That's my priority. Everything else is secondary."

### EXOSUIT PILOT (Rival — Crater Rim)

**Appearance:** Mid-30s, sealed suit. Has been in the suit for twelve hours. Has not come out. Has not needed to. He is the one who keeps the cordon tight. He is the one who fires if anyone tries to leave.

**Personality:** Focused, disciplined, deadly. He has been in the suit for twelve hours. He has not come out. He is not coming out until the job is done.

**Dialogue:**

> EXOSUIT PILOT: (over comms, to the cordon) "I have visual on the rim. Nothing moving. If something moves, I'll take it out."
>
> *No dialogue with Dante. He doesn't see Dante as a person. He sees Dante as a target.*

---

### CHEN (Rival — Globalsec Supervisor)

**Appearance:** Forty-three, Lunar-born. He has been on the Moon for eleven years. He has been a Globalsec supervisor for eight. He has been in Kaguya for twelve hours. He has not slept. He is not sure if he is going to.

**Personality:** Pragmatic, conflicted, tired. He is not enjoying this. He is doing it anyway.

**Dialogue:**

> CHEN: (when Dante finds him) "You're still alive. I'm not sure if that's luck or planning. Either way, you need to go."
>
> *He gestures toward the inner domes.*
>
> "If you're one of Zane's, stay in the tunnels. The military is pushing through the upper levels. They won't stop for civilians. They won't stop for anyone."
>
> *He looks away.*
>
> "I can't help you. I can't stop them. I can just... not see you."

### REYES (Minion — Individual)

**Appearance:** Twenty-four, Earth-born, still learning the gravity. He has been at Kaguya for twelve hours. He has been processing people for twelve hours. He looks hollow. He hasn't made his decision yet. He's not sure he ever will.

**Personality:** Young, unsure, not yet decided. He joined Globalsec for structure. The structure has turned out to be this.

**Dialogue:**

> REYES: "I've been processing people for twelve hours. The tube-lev hasn't moved. They keep coming, but there's nowhere to send them."
>
> *He looks at Dante. He recognizes him. He doesn't say anything.*
>
> "She asked me what I was protecting. I didn't have an answer."
>
> *He looks away.*
>
> "I don't have an answer."
