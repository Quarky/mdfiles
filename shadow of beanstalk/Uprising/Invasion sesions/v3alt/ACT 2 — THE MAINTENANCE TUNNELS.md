## ACT 2 — THE MAINTENANCE TUNNELS

### SCENE 2C — THE PROSPERITY CONNECTION

Dante finds a wall covered in graffiti. Some of it is fresh—hours old. Some of it is old—years old. The old graffiti is faded, partially covered, but still visible.

**The Old Graffiti:**
> "REMEMBER PROSPERITY."
> "THEY KILLED US."
> "WE WILL NOT FORGET."
> "PROSPERITY WAS MURDER."

**The New Graffiti:**
> "WE REMEMBER."
> "THEY TOOK THE MOON."
> "PROSPERITY WAS JUST THE BEGINNING."

**The Lore Connection:** The Prosperity Depot disaster is still remembered. It is the origin of the Insurrection. The Loonies have not forgotten. The new graffiti is fresh—the Insurrection is connected to Prosperity.

---

### SCENE 2D — THE PROSPERITY FILES

If Dante searches the area, he finds a data terminal—old, abandoned, still partially powered.

**The Files:**
> "Prosperity Mining Depot — Incident Report — Suppressed."
> "Casualties: 147 confirmed."
> "Cause: Unknown. Suspected sabotage."
> "No responsibility claimed."

**The Note:**
> "The same supervisor ID appears in the Prosperity files and the Section F files. MEL-221-REG-HAAS."

**The Lore Connection:** Prosperity was not an accident. The same supervisor ID appears in both. The connection is real.

---

### SCENE 2E — THE KI ANSHO CONNECTION

Dante finds a hidden terminal—wired into the Jinteki network, not the main facility network. It contains internal Jinteki communications.

**The Communication:**
> "Project Nisei — Phase 2 — approved. Authorization: Ki Ansho."
> "Workforce optimization through direct neural parameter adjustment."
> "Continue operations at Outpost 14 and Outpost 22."

**The Lore Connection:** Ki Ansho is a Jinteki project director. Project Nisei is the neural experimentation program. Outpost 14 is Section F. There are other outposts.
