# THE HAAS-BIOROID AD — FULL DESCRIPTION

**Location:** StarScape Level 3, a corridor near a collapsed retail display
**Time:** Session 1, Part 1 — Dante's exit from NBN building

---

## THE SCENE

The ad is still running. It's been running since before the occupation. The power hasn't failed. The system hasn't been shut off. It's playing to an empty StarScape—to the smoke and damage and absence.

**The ad shows:**
A family of four. Mother, father, daughter, son. They are standing in a bright, clean, carefully curated home. The lighting is warm. The walls are white. There are plants. There is a view of Earth through a window.

The mother is smiling. The father is smiling. The children are smiling.

Standing behind them, slightly to the side, is a bioroid. It is a Rex-model, standard chassis, clean. It is not smiling. It is not frowning. It is just standing there, waiting.

**The voiceover:**

> *"Haas-Bioroid — The Perfect Companion."*

The father puts his hand on the bioroid's shoulder. The bioroid does not react. The family keeps smiling.

> *"Bioroids make life easier. They work tirelessly. They never complain."*

A shot of the bioroid cleaning. A shot of the bioroid cooking. A shot of the bioroid helping the daughter with her homework—she is smiling at it like it's her favorite teacher.

> *"They're the perfect addition to any home."*

The family gathers together, the bioroid behind them. The mother ruffles the son's hair. The daughter hugs the bioroid.

The bioroid does not hug her back. It stands perfectly still, its hands at its sides.

> *"Haas-Bioroid. The future of the family."*

The ad loops. It plays again. It will keep playing until the power fails.

---

## THE DEFACEMENT

Someone has defaced the ad. Written across the hologram, in large letters:

> **"THEY ARE NOT PERFECT. THEY ARE PEOPLE."**

The letters are still wet. The paint is fresh. Whoever did it is still in the facility. The ad is still playing—the hologram is still visible through the paint, the smiling family still smiling, the bioroid still standing.
