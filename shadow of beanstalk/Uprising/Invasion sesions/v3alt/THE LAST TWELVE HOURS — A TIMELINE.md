## THE LAST TWELVE HOURS — A TIMELINE

### Hour 0 — The Assault
- The Loonies seize Kaguya at 06:30
- Zane's force moves through the outer ring
- Amara turns off the fountain
- 93 workers sit down in the atrium
- Dante begins his network run at SC-7

### Hour 1 — The Seizure
- Dome Gamma is taken. The cargo AI is locked.
- Dome Delta is taken. The departure freeze is active.
- The broadcast is prepared. The Section F file is ready.
- The military is notified. Operation Falling Star begins.

### Hour 2 — The Military Arrives
- Four dropships enter the crater
- SXC troopers deploy on the crater rim
- EWS goes live on the network
- Dante feels the building change

### Hour 3 — The Compression Zone
- The military pushes inward from the rim
- Globalsec pushes outward from the inner domes
- Loonies are compressed into the middle
- Partial decompression in the connecting corridor
- Casualties mount on all sides

### Hour 4 — The Broadcast
- NBN cuts the feed. The Section F file is suppressed.
- The broadcast goes out on the PAD network only
- Sigg's statement is heard, but not seen
- The military announces "successful neutralization"

### Hour 5-12 — The Siege
- The military secures the outer ring
- Globalsec is fractured
- Loonies hold the inner domes
- EWS continues its siege
- Dante exits the NBN facility
