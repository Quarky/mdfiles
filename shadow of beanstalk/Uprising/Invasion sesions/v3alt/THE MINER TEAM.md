## THE MINER TEAM

### RIKER — The Mechanic

**Appearance:** Thirty-four, stocky, grease-stained coveralls. Goggles pushed up on his forehead. Heavy tool belt with more tools than seems practical. Fingers scarred from burns. He has been in the maintenance tunnels for twelve hours. He has been working for twelve hours. He has not stopped.

**Personality:** Obsessive, focused, dangerous. He loves his work more than people. He understands machines better than people. He is not socially adept. He does not care.

**Dialogue:**

> RIKER: (not looking up from a panel he is working on) "The ventilation system is running at 120% capacity. Someone's been overriding the systems. Probably the military. They're using it to move their troops."
>
> *He reaches into the panel.*
>
> "I can re-route it. Make it work for us instead of them. But I need time. And I need cover."

---

### MAC — The Loader

**Appearance:** Forty-one, huge, hands like shovels, bald, scarred knuckles. Heavy work jacket, no insignia. He has been carrying wounded for twelve hours. He has not stopped.

**Personality:** Quiet, deliberate, loyal. He does not talk much. He acts.

**Dialogue:**

> MAC: "I've been carrying wounded. Twelve hours. No break."
>
> *He is not complaining. He is stating a fact.*
>
> "I'll keep carrying them. That's what I'm here for."

---

### SULLY — The Cutter

**Appearance:** Thirty-nine, lean, covered in fine lunar dust. Safety goggles. Cutting torch. He has been cutting through walls for twelve hours. He has not stopped.

**Personality:** Quiet, methodical, intense. He does not talk much. He does his job.

**Dialogue:**

> SULLY: (cutting through a wall) "They're using the maintenance corridors. The military. They've learned the routes. They're getting better."
>
> *He stops cutting. Looks at Dante.*
>
> "I've been cutting through walls. Making new routes. They don't know the new routes yet. They will. Eventually. We need to move."

---

### FLETCHER — The Former Guard

**Appearance:** Forty-six, big, unkempt, strong. Former Melange guard. He has been moving through the facility for twelve hours, using his old uniform to bluff the military. He is tired. He is still moving.

**Personality:** Direct, pragmatic, reckless. He believes he has nothing left to lose. He is not sure if that's true.

**Dialogue:**

> FLETCHER: "The military is pushing from the rim. They're using controlled pair advance. They're learning. They're getting faster."
>
> *He looks at Dante.*
>
> "But they're still predictable. They still follow doctrine. I know their patterns. I can still stay ahead of them."

---

### JUNIOR — The Mapper

**Appearance:** Twenty-six, lean, nervous. Refinery jumpsuit too large. PAD with schematics. He has been studying schematics for twelve hours. He has not stopped.

**Personality:** Anxious, talkative, desperate to prove himself. He is the youngest. He knows it.

**Dialogue:**

> JUNIOR: "I've been mapping the facility. The maintenance routes. The ventilation shafts. The emergency exits."
>
> *He looks at Dante, nervous.*
>
> "I know the routes the military doesn't know. I can get us through. Just... just tell me where we need to go."

---

### MAEKO — The Driver

**Appearance:** Thirty-one, small, deceptively strong. Light surface suit. She has been waiting for twelve hours. She is getting impatient.

**Personality:** Fast-talking, cocky, skilled. She has been on the Moon for ten years. She is completely comfortable in low-G. She is completely comfortable in a hopper.

**Dialogue:**

> MAEKO: (watching the crater rim through a window) "The exosuits are still there. They've been there for twelve hours. They're not going anywhere."
>
> *She looks at Dante.*
>
> "But I can get us past them. I just need 90 seconds. 90 seconds and a distraction."
