## THE PROSPERITY DEPOT DISASTER

The Prosperity Mining Depot disaster was the event that triggered the original Insurrection. What would later spiral out of control into the Worlds War started when an alliance of mining workers broadcast their grievances to the Earth as a whole, stating that they would no longer work if the corporations continued to oppress and underpay their workforce. In response, an unmarked shuttle bombed the Prosperity Mining Depot during a protest, breaching the facility and suffocating hundreds of miners.

The workers rose up in all-out revolt across Luna, overwhelming prisec security forces in a brutal assault that saw heavy casualties on both sides. The rebels went on to seize the shipyards and mag-lev launch tracks in Heinlein, Xiangong City, and other major lunar settlements, blockading all shipments of He-3 in a single stroke.
