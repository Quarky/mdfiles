## ACT 2 — THE JOURNEY THROUGH STARSCAPE

### SCENE 1G — THE JINTEKI BIOMETRIC LOCK

If Dante finds a Jinteki maintenance terminal, he notices something: the biometric lock is still active. It hasn't been overridden. Someone with Jinteki credentials has been using this terminal.

**The Terminal:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Status** | Active. Logged in. |
| **User** | Clone maintenance personnel—authorized. |
| **Access** | Full system access. |
| **Recent Activity** | Maintenance handshake frequency access. |

**The Lore Connection:** Someone with Jinteki access is using the terminal to access the maintenance handshake frequency—the same frequency Kai uses to communicate with other clones. This is how the clones are organizing. This is how they're building their network.

### SCENE 1H — THE ORDER OF SOL PAMPHLET

Dante finds a pamphlet on the floor. It's from the Order of Sol. It's been stepped on, crumpled, but still readable.

> **ORDER OF SOL — SOLACE IN THE LIGHT**
>
> "In the darkness of this world, the Light of Sol guides us. We do not turn away from the suffering. We do not ignore the pain. We stand with those who are broken, those who are lost, those who are forgotten. For the Light is not a shield—it is a beacon. And we will shine until the darkness is gone."

**The Note on the Back:**

Written in the margin, in pencil, someone has added:

> "THE LIGHT DOESN'T STOP THE BULLETS. BUT IT SHOWS US WHERE TO STAND."

**The Lore Connection:** The Order of Sol is present—they're showing up, they're standing in the path of the bullets. They're helping the clones, the workers, the dispossessed.

---

### SCENE 1I — THE PROSPERITY DEPOT MURAL

Dante finds a mural in a StarScape corridor. It's not official—it was painted by someone, recently. The paint is still wet in places.

**The Mural:**

A worker—a miner—stands in front of the Moon. The miner is looking up. Behind them, the Moon is cracked, bleeding. The words are at the bottom:

> "THEY TOOK THE MOON. WE WILL NOT FORGET."

Underneath, someone has written:

> "PROSPERITY 43 — MURDERED BY MELANGE."

**The Lore Connection:** The Prosperity Depot disaster is still remembered. It's the origin of the Insurrection. The Loonies have not forgotten. Melange is responsible. The Insurrection is connected to Prosperity.

---

### SCENE 1J — THE HEINLEIN AUTHORITY HUB

If Dante passes near the Heinlein Authority operations hub (Dome Delta), he can hear the Authority staff inside. They're still sheltering in place. They're still following orders.

**The Sounds:**

> VOICE 1 (panicked): "They're not responding to emergency frequencies. The military is handling everything. We're just—we're just supposed to wait."

> VOICE 2 (calmer): "We wait. That's what we do. We wait. We trust that the system works. We trust that we'll be safe."

> VOICE 1: "What about the people out there? The ones who—the ones who sat down?"

> VOICE 2: "They're not our concern. We're just supposed to keep the Authority running."

**The Lore Connection:** The Authority is still operational, but the staff are scared. They're following orders. They're not fighting back. They're just waiting.

### SCENE 1K — THE HAAS-BIOROID CRATE

Dante finds a crate in a maintenance tunnel. It's marked Haas-Bioroid. It's been opened—someone has been through it.

**The Crate:**

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Marking** | "HAAS-BIOROID — BIOROID MAINTENANCE KIT" |
| **Status** | Open. Partially empty. |
| **Contents** | Bioroid parts, diagnostic tools, spare chassis components. |

**The Note:**

Inside the crate, someone has left a message—written on a scrap of paper:

> "IF YOU'RE A BIOROID, TAKE WHAT YOU NEED. IF YOU'RE A CLONE, TAKE WHAT YOU NEED. IF YOU'RE HUMAN, TAKE WHAT YOU NEED. WE'RE ALL THE SAME."

**The Lore Connection:** The line between human and bioroid is being broken. Someone is helping them. The resistance is growing.
