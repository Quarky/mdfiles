**Time:** 12 hours after the initial assault
**Location:** Starport Kaguya, Hypatia-C Crater, Luna
**Status:** The Insurrection is in full swing. The military has arrived. The network is contested. The broadcast is waiting.

**The World Tonight:**

The Moon has been fighting for twelve hours. Not the clean, surgical operation the military briefings promised. Not the quick victory the corps expected. Something else.

Something that has spread beyond Kaguya.

Heinlein is quiet in a way it hasn't been in decades. The tube-lev is still running—Metzger's orders—but the trains are empty. The workers who would normally be riding to Kaguya are not riding. They are watching. They are waiting. They are reading the fragments of the broadcast on their PADs.

The Docklands is not quiet. The Docklands is waiting. The Katala Brothers have not made a move—they are watching, calculating, deciding which side to back. The Loony network is fractured but alive. Yusuf has been moving people for twelve hours. He has not slept. He has not stopped.

Lunacent is a fortress. The arcologies have sealed their lower levels. The corporate towers are lit but empty—the executives who could not evacuate are watching the same PAD feeds, reading the same fragments of the broadcast, wondering if their contracts cover insurrection.

On Earth, New Angeles is watching. The NBN feed is controlled but the fragments are spreading. The Sol has published the Outpost 14 discrepancy story. Melange has issued a denial. Jinteki has issued a statement of "concern." Haas-Bioroid has said nothing.

The Beanstalk is dark. The pods that would normally be running are idle. The Root in New Angeles is quiet—the travelers who would normally be boarding are not boarding. They are watching. They are waiting.

The War hasn't started yet.

It started today.

## THE SOUNDSCAPE

Dante exits the NBN facility. The first thing he notices is the sound.

The Beanstalk hum is gone. It has been twelve hours since a pod departed. The absence of that constant vibration is almost physical—the facility feels lighter, looser, like a structure that has lost its purpose.

What remains:

| Sound | Source | Meaning |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **Distant gunfire** | Outer ring, StarScape, military clearing operations | The advance is still happening |
| **Emergency sirens** | Multiple sectors, automated systems | Damage control is still active |
| **Ventilation noise** | The facility's lungs, working harder than normal | Atmo systems are overloading |
| **The structure groaning** | Damage to dome integrity, pressure differentials | The facility is wounded |
| **Voices** | Shouted orders, occasional screaming, sometimes singing | People are still in here |
| **Silence** | Where there should be noise | The places that are empty |

**The Silence in StarScape:**

StarScape has been empty for eight hours. The retail levels that were once a constant hum of commerce are silent. The moving walkways are still running—automated systems don't stop—but there is no one on them. The holographic ads still cycle, still bright, still promising luxury and escape to no one. The fountains are still off. The lights are still on.

The silence in StarScape is the sound of a place that has been abandoned by its purpose. It is the sound of a tourist attraction in a war zone.

**The Noise in the Domes:**

The inner domes are not silent. They are filled with the noise of people holding a position—the murmur of conversation, the clatter of equipment, the occasional shouted order. The Loonies who hold the inner domes are not soldiers. They are workers. They talk. They laugh. They are scared. They keep going.

**The Sound of the Military:**

The military is not loud. They move in controlled silence—the soft hiss of sealed suits, the quiet commands over comms, the precise footfalls of disciplined advance. Their noise is the noise of efficiency. It is the noise of people doing a job.

**The Sound of the Wounded:**

Somewhere in the inner domes, a woman is crying. Not loud—a quiet, hopeless sound that carries through the ventilation system. It is the sound of someone who has lost someone. It is the sound of the Insurrection.

## THE SMELLSCAPE

Dante exits the NBN facility. The second thing he notices is the smell.

| Smell | Source | Meaning |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **Smoke** | StarScape fires, still burning | The facility is still damaged |
| **Ozone** | Weapons fire, electrical systems | There has been combat |
| **Hydraulic fluid** | Damaged systems, leaking | The facility is bleeding |
| **Blood** | The wounded, the dead | People have died |
| **Fear** | Not physical, but present | Everyone is afraid |
| **The clean air of the Launch Core** | Where the beanpods dock | The one place that still works |

**The Smoke of StarScape:**

StarScape has been burning for eight hours. The fires are not large—they are the small, persistent fires of retail spaces burning themselves out. The smoke is thin, acrid, the smell of synthetics and plastics and the things that made the tourist paradise comfortable. It is the smell of a place that is no longer what it was.

**The Ozone of Combat:**

The weapons used in Kaguya are mostly lasers. Lasers leave behind ozone—the sharp, clean smell of ionized air. It is the smell of combat without gunpowder. It is the smell of the future killing itself.

**The Hydraulic Fluid of the Wounded:**

Hydraulic fluid is the blood of the facility. It is leaking from damaged systems, pooling in corridors, dripping from ceiling panels. It is the smell of a place that is wounded but not dead.

**The Blood of the Insurrection:**

There is blood in the corridors. Some of it is Loony. Some of it is military. Some of it is civilian. The lines between them have blurred in the compression zone. The smell of blood is the smell of the Insurrection becoming what it was always going to become.

## THE VISUAL LANDSCAPE

Dante exits the NBN facility. The third thing he notices is the damage.

| Visual Element | Location | Meaning |
|----------------|----------|---------|
| **Cracked transplas** | Outer ring, StarScape | The facility has been breached |
| **Emergency seals (amber)** | Multiple corridors | Pressure differentials, damage control |
| **Fires (small, persistent)** | StarScape retail levels | The tourist paradise is burning |
| **Abandoned PADs** | Everywhere | People who tried to document, people who died |
| **Blood streaks** | Floors, walls | The Insurrection is not clean |
| **Graffiti** | Walls, "WE REMEMBER," "THEY TOOK THE MOON" | The rebellion is here |
| **The Beanpole, dark** | Visible through the transplas | The Beanstalk has stopped |

**The Cracks in the Dome:**

The transplas panels in StarScape are cracked in multiple places. Not breached—the emergency seals have held—but the cracks are visible. They are the visible evidence of the pressure differentials that have been building for twelve hours. They are the visible evidence of the Insurrection.

**The Emergency Seals:**

Amber lights mark the locations of emergency seals throughout the facility. They are the backup systems that activated when the primary atmo failed. They are the reason Kaguya is still pressurized. They are also the reason some corridors are sealed off, trapping people inside.

**The Fires in StarScape:**

The fires are small. They are the fires of burning retail inventory—clothing, electronics, furnishings. They are the fires of a place that was once a tourist paradise. They are the fires of the Insurrection.

**The Abandoned PADs:**

Everywhere Dante looks, there are abandoned PADs. They are the PADs of tourists who fled. They are the PADs of workers who died. They are the PADs of people who tried to document the Insurrection and failed. They are the PADs of people who are still alive, somewhere, wondering if their PAD is still there.

**The Graffiti:**

The walls of StarScape are covered in graffiti. It is not the graffiti of vandals—it is the graffiti of people who have something to say. "WE REMEMBER." "THEY TOOK THE MOON." "THE PROSPERITY DEPOT WAS MURDER." "THEY WILL NEVER TAKE HEINLEIN." It is the graffiti of the Insurrection.

**The Dark Beanpole:**

The Beanpole is visible through the cracked transplas. It is dark. The pods that would normally be running are idle. The light that would normally illuminate the tether is off. It is the visible evidence of the Insurrection's first victory—the Beanstalk is not running.

**Role:** Technician, schematics expert, mechanical problem-solver

**Origin:** Rio de Janeiro, Earth — 4 years on Luna

---

## APPEARANCE

Lean, quick-moving, always in motion. Dark hair perpetually messy from running his hands through it. Refinery jumpsuit that's too large for him—he's been meaning to get it tailored for two years. Wears a tool belt that's slightly too heavy for his frame but he refuses to take anything off because "you never know when you'll need it."

His hands are clean—he works the systems, not the physical labor. A scar on his left forearm from a conveyor belt incident he tells everyone was "way cooler than it actually was." He has an infectious grin that he deploys in almost every situation, even when things are going badly.

---

## PERSONALITY

**Good-Natured:** He genuinely likes people. He wants to help. He wants to be useful. He wants everyone to get along. This is sometimes his undoing—he trusts people too easily, assumes good intentions.

**Fast-Talking:** He speaks at the speed of thought, which is faster than his brain can always keep up with. He talks when he's nervous, talks when he's excited, talks when he's scared. He talks because silence feels like something is wrong. He has learned to use his chatter as a defense mechanism—if he's talking, people aren't shooting.

**Naive:** He hasn't been on the Moon long enough to be cynical. He still believes that things work out if you try hard enough. He still believes that people are basically good. He still believes that the rebellion is going to succeed because it has to. He hasn't seen enough failure to know that sometimes things just don't work out.

**Overconfident:** He is genuinely skilled. He knows the schematics better than anyone. He can bypass systems that would stump others. But he also thinks he can do things that are beyond his abilities. He sometimes takes risks that are unnecessary. He sometimes promises things he can't deliver. He is always surprised when things go wrong.

**Vulnerable:** He is terrified of failure. Underneath the fast-talking confidence is a young man who desperately wants to be useful, who is afraid of being the one who lets everyone down. He talks fast because if he stops talking, the fear catches up.

---

## MOTIVATIONS

| Motive | Detail |

| **Desire** | To be respected. Tired of being the junior guy nobody listens to. |
| **Fear** | Failure. Terrified of being the weak link. |
| **Strength** | Quick learner. Reads schematics better than anyone. |
| **Flaw** | Overconfidence. Talks about things he shouldn't. Takes risks he shouldn't. |

---

## BACKGROUND

Sully came to Luna four years ago on a Melange contract. He was twenty-three, fresh out of a technical training program in Rio, and he thought he was going to change the world. The Moon was supposed to be his adventure.

The adventure turned out to be long hours, low pay, and watching his friends get replaced by clones. He stayed because he didn't have anywhere else to go. He stayed because he got good at the work. He stayed because he made friends.

He joined the rebellion because Yusuf told him it was the right thing to do. He believed it. He still believes it. He's not sure what happens if it doesn't work.
