## THE MINER TEAM — OVERVIEW

These are not soldiers. They are workers. They agreed to do something dangerous because they saw no other path. They are scared. They are determined. They are following Dante because he's the only one who knows what to do.

### Team Dynamics

| Trait | How It Manifests in Combat |
|-------|---------------------------|
| **No Formal Training** | They fight reactively. They don't follow doctrine. They improvise. |
| **Facility Knowledge** | They know where everything is. They know which routes are safe and which are dangerous. |
| **Improvised Weapons** | They use tools as weapons. Plasma cutters, sledgehammers, rebar, conveyor chains. |
| **Fear** | They are afraid. They keep going anyway. |
| **Loyalty** | They are loyal to each other and to Dante. They don't want to let each other down. |

### Motivation — Everyone Has a Reason

| Character | Primary Motivation | Why They're Fighting |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Riker** | Obsession with mechanics | He's here because he wants to understand the system. He's staying because he's already committed. |
| **Mac** | Protection of his team | He's here because his team is here. He's staying because they need him. |
| **Sully** | Survival | He's here because he doesn't want to go back. He's staying because he doesn't have a choice. |
| **Fletcher** | Redemption | He's here because he was a guard and he wants to make up for it. He's staying because he doesn't want to be that person again. |
| **Junior** | Proving himself | He's here because he wants to be useful. He's staying because he doesn't want to let anyone down. |
| **Maeko** | Escape | She's here because she wants to leave the Moon. She's staying because this is the only way out. |
