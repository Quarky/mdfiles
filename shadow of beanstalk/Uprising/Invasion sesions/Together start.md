
### SCENE 1A — THE FIRST BREATH

Dante stands in the doorway of the NBN facility. The air hits him. The smoke, the ozone, the blood. It's not just smell—it's texture. The air is thicker here, heavier. The facility's atmospheric processors are failing.

He steps out. His foot crunches on something. He looks down. It's a PAD—cracked, dead. It has a name engraved on the back: *"K. Okonkwo."* The PAD is cold. It's been dead for hours.

There's a smear on the floor nearby. Dark. Not hydraulic fluid. Dante knows the difference.

He looks up. The StarScape retail level stretches before him. It's a graveyard. The moving walkways still run—automated systems don't stop for war. They carry nothing. They'll keep carrying nothing until the power fails.

**The Sound:** A distant voice. Someone is shouting—orders, probably. Military. The sound echoes oddly in the damaged space. Then silence. Then the voice again.

**The Detail:** A holographic ad flickers on a nearby wall. It's for "Heinlein's Finest Low-G Tourism — Book Now!" The ad is still playing. It still shows families laughing, couples holding hands, children playing in the recreation domes. The ad is playing to an empty StarScape.

**The Lore Connection:** The ad is from before the Insurrection—before the occupation, before the military. It shows a Kaguya that no longer exists. It's a ghost of what the facility was supposed to be.

## DISCOVERY 1 — THE ABANDONED PAD

On the floor of a StarScape corridor. Cracked screen, flickering. Still has power.

**The file:** A partial broadcast fragment. Four minutes of video.
Section F footage. The footage NBN cut. The fragment is still cached here
because whoever was watching it here when the battle began dropped it and ran.

**The footage, if Dante plays it:**

> *A woman — forties, worker's clothing, the elongated Moonborn bone structure
> of someone raised in low gravity — sitting in the center of a large space.
> An atrium. Dozens of people seated around her. Some in work clothing.
> Some in Jinteki's grey contractor coveralls. Some who look like they are
> dressed for somewhere else and have found themselves somewhere else entirely.*
>
> *The woman — Amara — is not speaking. She is sitting. The sitting is the
> statement. The people around her are the statement.*
>
> *Then: military. Entering from three directions simultaneously.
> Pressure suits. Weapons held down but not holstered.*
>
> *Amara stands. She holds up her hands — not in surrender, in identification.
> "We are workers. This is a workers' action. We have demands."*
>
> *The military does not slow.*
>
> *The frame shakes. Someone is running. The camera swings.*
>
> *A teenager's face — she can't be more than seventeen, Brazilian by
> her features, the specific expression of someone who has decided
> that recording this is the most important thing she can do.*
>
> *The footage corrupts. Stops.*

The full recording exists somewhere. The teenager still has it. She is somewhere
in StarScape Level 5. The PAD's location data, if Dante can read it, shows the
last sync point was in that direction.
