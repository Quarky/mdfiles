## ENCOUNTER TYPE 9 — THE EMP TRAP

### Setup
The military wants to disable the Loonies' network and electronic equipment. They deploy an electromagnetic pulse (EMP) device.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **EMP Deployment:** The military places an EMP device on the wall of a room or corridor. It's small, easy to miss.
2. **Activation:** The device activates remotely. A pulse of electromagnetic energy disables all unshielded electronics within 10-20 meters.
3. **Advance:** The military advances through the area. Their equipment is shielded; the Loonies' equipment is not. PADs, comms, night vision, and weapons with electronic sights are disabled.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Any location with electronic equipment |
| **Enemy** | Fire Team (4 troopers) — Advancing after EMP |
| **Objective** | Survive the loss of electronics, avoid capture, or counter-attack |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Hardwired Equipment** | Use equipment that isn't vulnerable to EMP (mechanical weapons, monoblade) | Average | You can still fight |
| **Find the EMP Device** | Locate and disable the EMP device | Hard | Removes the threat |
| **Retreat** | Move out of the affected area | Average | The military takes the position, but you escape |
| **Use Cover** | Wait out the EMP pulse (it's short-lived) | Average | The pulse lasts 10-30 seconds |
| **Fight Without Tech** | Rely on instinct and close-quarters combat | Hard | You're at a disadvantage, but you're still in the fight |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Riker** | Locate and disable the EMP device | Removes the threat |
| **Junior** | Identify where the device is likely placed | Guides the team to find it |
| **Mac** | Fight without electronics | Uses brute force |
| **Sully** | Use monoblade or other non-electronic weapons | Fights effectively |

### Dialogue Moment

>
> JUNIOR: "My PAD too. All my schematics... gone."
>
> FLETCHER: "We still have the hardwired route. I can guide us through without electronics."
>
