## ENCOUNTER TYPE 10 — THE DECOY ADVANCE

### Setup
The military wants to draw the Loonies into a kill zone. They use a decoy force to create a false threat.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Decoy Force:** A small military element (2 troopers) makes noise and fires weapons in one location, drawing attention.
2. **Main Force:** The main military force advances through a different route, attacking the Loonies from an unexpected direction.
3. **Pincer:** The decoy force then advances from their position, creating a pincer. The Loonies are caught between the decoy and the main force.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Any location with multiple routes |
| **Enemy** | Decoy Force (2 troopers), Main Force (6 troopers) |
| **Objective** | Avoid the pincer, escape, or counter-attack |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Identify the Decoy** | Realize that the noise is bait | Average | You can focus on the main force |
| **Hit the Decoy First** | Eliminate the decoy force | Hard | Removes one of the threats |
| **Fall Back** | Withdraw to a new position | Average | The military takes the ground, but you survive |
| **Hold the Line** | Fight both forces at once | Very Hard | High chance of casualties, but could break the pincer |
| **Use Environment** | Use the environment to block one of the forces | Average | Creates time to deal with one threat at a time |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Fletcher** | Identify the decoy | Guides the team to focus on the main force |
| **Junior** | Find an alternative route | Escapes the pincer |
| **Mac** | Hold the decoy force | Prevents the pincer from closing |
| **Maeko** | Move quickly to a new position | Outruns the pincer |
