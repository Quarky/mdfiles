## ENCOUNTER TYPE 2 — THE AIRLOCK STANDOFF

### Location Context
This encounter can be placed at any airlock or pressurized junction. In Kaguya, it's the connecting corridor between Dome Gamma and the Launch Core.

### Setup
The squad needs to pass through an airlock to reach the next dome. The military is holding the far side of the airlock—a fire team has set up a defensive position. The squad is trapped on the near side. The airlock is sealed.

### The Challenge
| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Airlock (3x4 meters), emergency lighting, sealed doors on both sides, cycling station |
| **Enemy** | Military Fire Team (far side). 4 troopers. Defensive position. Bipod mounted weapon. |
| **Objective** | Get through the airlock to the next dome. |

### Tactical Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Cycle the Airlock** | Cycle the airlock and rush the far side | Very Hard | The military expects this. High chance of casualties. |
| **Breach the Wall** | Cut through the wall next to the airlock | Average | Creates a new passage, but takes time |
| **Use the Ventilation System** | Go through the ventilation system above the airlock | Hard | Tight space, but avoids the fire team |
| **Negotiate** | Use the airlock as a bargaining chip | Hard | If the military wants the facility intact, they won't risk damaging the airlock |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Sully** | Cut through the wall | Creates a new passage |
| **Junior** | Find a ventilation hatch | Provides an alternative route |
| **Riker** | Bypass the airlock controls | Gives the squad more options |
| **Maeko** | Distract the fire team with a decoy | Gives the squad time to breach |

### Outcome
If the squad breaches the wall: They move into the next dome. The military loses track of them.
If the squad uses the ventilation: They move above the airlock. They bypass the fire team entirely.
If the squad cycles the airlock: The fire team is waiting. Casualties likely.
If the squad negotiates: The airlock is essential infrastructure. If the squad threatens to damage it, the military may back off.

### Dialogue Moment

> MAEKO: "I can get us through the airlock. I've driven through worse."
>

>

>

>
> SULLY: "A few seconds is all I need to cut through the wall."
