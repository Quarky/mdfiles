## ENCOUNTER TYPE 17 — THE SLOW DEPRESSURIZATION TRAP

### Setup
The military doesn't breach the airlock—they slowly depressurize the section, forcing the Loonies to move.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Atmo Override:** The military gains access to the atmospheric controls for a section.
2. **Slow Depressurization:** The oxygen level drops gradually—85%, then 70%, then 50%. The Loonies don't notice until they're disoriented.
3. **Wait:** The military waits for the Loonies to leave the section. Then they move in and secure it.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Sealed section of the facility |
| **Enemy** | Military (outside the section, waiting) |
| **Objective** | Notice the depressurization, escape, or survive |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Monitor Oxygen** | Watch the oxygen levels | Easy | You notice the drop early |
| **Leave the Section** | Exit the section before the oxygen drops | Average | You escape the trap |
| **Sealed Suit** | Put on a sealed suit | Average | You survive the depressurization |
| **Override the Atmo** | Reclaim control of the atmospheric system | Very Hard | You reverse the depressurization |
| **Cut Through the Wall** | Escape through the wall | Hard | You leave the section |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Monitor oxygen levels | Notices the drop |
| **Sully** | Cut through the wall | Creates an exit |
| **Riker** | Override the atmospheric controls | Reverses the depressurization |
| **Maeko** | Move quickly to the exit | Escapes the section |
