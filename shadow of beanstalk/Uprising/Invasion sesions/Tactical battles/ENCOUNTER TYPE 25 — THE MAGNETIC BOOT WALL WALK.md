## ENCOUNTER TYPE 25 — THE MAGNETIC BOOT WALL WALK

### Setup (Loony Initiated)
The Loonies use magnetic boots to walk on walls and ceilings, bypassing military defenses.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Magnetic Boots:** The Loonies activate their magnetic boots.
2. **Wall Walk:** They walk on the walls or ceiling of a corridor, bypassing floor-level defenses.
3. **Flank:** They attack the military from an unexpected angle (above, side).
4. **Escape:** They withdraw through a maintenance hatch.

### Loony Technology
- Magnetic Boots (standard lunar mining equipment)
- Facility Knowledge (structural supports)

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Corridor with metallic walls/ceiling |
| **Enemy** | Military (positioned at floor level) |
| **Objective** | Flank or bypass the military |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Wall Walk** | Walk on the wall to bypass the military | Average | You avoid the military's line of sight |
| **Ceiling Drop** | Drop on the military from above | Hard | The military is completely surprised |
| **Ambush from Above** | Attack from the ceiling | Hard | The military can't effectively return fire |
| **Escape** | Use the wall walk to escape | Average | You can evade the military |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Maeko** | Lead the wall walk | Guides the team |
| **Sully** | Open a vent cover | Creates an exit |
| **Fletcher** | Identify the military's position | Finds a good drop point |
| **Mac** | Drop on the military | Provides a devastating attack |

### Dialogue Moment

> MAEKO: "Magnetic boots. We can walk on the wall."
>
> SULLY: "The ventilation cover is above the military's position."
>
> FLETCHER: "If we drop on them, we can take them out before they know what happened."
>
> MAC: "I'll drop first. Clear the way."
