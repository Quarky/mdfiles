## ENCOUNTER TYPE 23 — THE VENTILATION SHAFT FLANK

### Setup (Loony Initiated)
The Loonies use the ventilation system to flank a military position.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Vent Access:** The Loonies enter the ventilation system through a maintenance hatch.
2. **Movement:** They move through the ventilation shafts to a position behind the military.
3. **Attack:** They attack the military from the flank or rear.
4. **Escape:** They exit through a different hatch and withdraw.

### Loony Technology
- Ventilation System Knowledge (Junior's schematics)
- Acoustic Baffles (to mask movement sound)
- Magnetic Boots (to walk on walls)

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Ventilation shaft (tight, 1-2 meters wide) |
| **Enemy** | Military (positioned in a corridor) |
| **Objective** | Flank the military, attack, and escape |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Flank Attack** | Attack from behind the military's position | Hard | The military is surprised and vulnerable |
| **Move Past** | Move through the vent to a position beyond the military | Average | You can bypass the military entirely |
| **Use the Vent** | Drop on the military from above | Very Hard | The military is completely surprised |
| **Escape** | Use the vent to escape a trapped position | Average | You can evade the military |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Find a vent path | Guides the team through the ventilation system |
| **Sully** | Open vent covers | Creates access points |
| **Fletcher** | Identify the military's position | Finds a flanking route |
| **Mac** | Drop on the military from above | Provides a devastating attack |

### Dialogue Moment

> JUNIOR: "I found a ventilation shaft that leads behind their position."
>
> SULLY: "I can open the cover. We can drop on them from above."
>
> FLETCHER: "If we do this, we need to be quick. They'll call for reinforcements."
>
> MAC: "I'll drop first. Clear the way. Then the rest follow."
