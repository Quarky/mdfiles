## ENCOUNTER TYPE 1 — THE MAINTENANCE CORRIDOR CHASE

### Location Context
This encounter can be placed in any maintenance corridor network. In Kaguya, it's the service tunnel beneath the inner dome cluster.

### Setup
The squad is moving through the maintenance tunnels. The military has breached the network—a fire team is behind them, and another fire team is ahead of them. The squad is trapped in a narrow corridor with no exits.

### The Challenge
| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Narrow tunnel (2 meters wide), low ceiling, emergency lighting only, multiple side hatches (some sealed, some open) |
| **Enemy 1** | Military Fire Team A (behind). 4 troopers. Controlled pair advance. 90 seconds away. |
| **Enemy 2** | Military Fire Team B (ahead). 4 troopers. Blocking the exit. 120 seconds away. |
| **Objective** | Escape the corridor before being trapped between the two teams. |

### Tactical Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Fight Through** | Engage one of the fire teams directly, break through their line | Hard | High chance of casualties, but could break the trap |
| **Use a Side Hatch** | Find an open side hatch and escape into a different section of the maintenance network | Average | Requires finding a hatch and a route through an unknown section |
| **Create a Distraction** | Trigger a decompression event in the corridor, forcing both teams to retreat | Daunting | Dangerous, but creates confusion and time |
| **Negotiate** | Use Fletcher's old uniform or social skills to convince the military that the squad is not the target | Hard | Requires specific conditions (Fletcher's uniform, believable story) |
| **Wait** | Wait for the military to make a mistake | Very Hard | The military doesn't make mistakes |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Riker** | Bypass a side hatch door | Opens an alternative route |
| **Junior** | Identify a side hatch that isn't on the schematics | Finds a route the military doesn't know about |
| **Fletcher** | Use his old uniform to bluff the fire team | Delays the fire team by 30 seconds |
| **Sully** | Cut through a sealed panel | Creates a new exit |
| **Mac** | Hold the corridor while the team escapes | Provides 30 seconds of cover |

### Outcome
If the squad escapes: They move into an unknown section of the maintenance network. The military loses track of them—temporarily.
If the squad fights: The fire team engages. The squad takes casualties. The military reinforces.
If the squad splits: Some escape, some are captured.

### Dialogue Moment

> FLETCHER: (looking at the military fire team ahead)
> "They're the same as me. They're just following orders. If I can talk to them—"
>
> RIKER: "You want to talk to the soldiers who are about to kill us?"
>
> FLETCHER: "I was a guard. I know how they think. If I can make them hesitate—"
>
> MAEKO: "We don't have time for hesitation. We need to move."
