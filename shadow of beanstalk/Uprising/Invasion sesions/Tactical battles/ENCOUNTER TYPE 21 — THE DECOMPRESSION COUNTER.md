## ENCOUNTER TYPE 21 — THE DECOMPRESSION COUNTER

### Setup (Loony Initiated)
The Loonies know the facility's degraded seals. They use them to create a decompression event, forcing the military to stop their advance.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Seal Identification:** The Loonies identify a degraded seal in a section the military is advancing through.
2. **Breach:** The Loonies breach the seal, creating a slow decompression event.
3. **Wait:** The military has to stop their advance while the decompression happens.
4. **Movement:** The Loonies use the time to reposition.

### Loony Technology
- Emergency Seal Breach Tools (makeshift)
- Knowledge of facility's degraded seals
- Oxygen monitoring (to keep themselves safe)

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Section with degraded seal |
| **Enemy** | Military (must stop their advance due to decompression) |
| **Objective** | Create time to reposition or escape |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Breach the Seal** | Create a controlled decompression event | Average | The military must stop their advance |
| **Wait for Military to Respond** | Wait for the military to seal the breach | Easy | Buys 5-10 minutes |
| **Move During the Chaos** | Move to a new position | Average | The military is distracted by the decompression |
| **Use the Decompression** | Attack while the military is distracted | Hard | The military is vulnerable during the decompression |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Identify a degraded seal | Finds a location to breach |
| **Sully** | Breach the seal | Creates the decompression event |
| **Riker** | Monitor oxygen levels | Ensures the team doesn't get caught in the decompression |
| **Maeko** | Move to a new position | The military is distracted |

### Dialogue Moment

> JUNIOR: "There's a degraded seal in section B-7. If we breach it, we can buy time."
>
> SULLY: "I can breach it. But the decompression will be slow."
>
> RIKER: "Slow is good. It gives us time to move."
>
> MAEKO: "I'll take the team to the next section while the military is distracted."
