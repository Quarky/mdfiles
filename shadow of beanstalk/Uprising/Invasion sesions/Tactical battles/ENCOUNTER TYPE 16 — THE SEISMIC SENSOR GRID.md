## ENCOUNTER TYPE 16 — THE SEISMIC SENSOR GRID

### Setup
The military has placed seismic sensors on the floor—they detect movement through the ground.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Sensor Deployment:** Small sensors are placed on the floor of a corridor or room.
2. **Detection:** The sensors detect footsteps, even through walls and floors.
3. **Targeting:** The military knows where the Loonies are moving and can plan an intercept.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Corridor, room, or open area with floor access |
| **Enemy** | Fire Team (4 troopers) — Positioning based on sensor data |
| **Objective** | Avoid detection, disable the sensors, or counter-attack |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Move Lightly** | Walk softly to avoid setting off sensors | Hard | The sensors don't detect you |
| **Find the Sensors** | Locate and remove the sensors | Average | Removes the threat |
| **Create False Movement** | Make noise somewhere else | Average | The military moves to the wrong position |
| **Use Cover** | Move through areas with no sensors | Average | The sensors aren't everywhere |
| **Move at Facility Noise** | Move when facility machinery is loud | Easy | The sensors can't distinguish your footsteps |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Identify where the sensors are likely placed | Guides the team to avoid them |
| **Riker** | Create false movement | Distracts the military |
| **Fletcher** | Move quietly through known routes | Avoids detection |
| **Sully** | Use his knowledge of facility sound patterns | Moves with the noise |

### Dialogue Moment

> FLETCHER: "Seismic sensors. They can feel us walking."
>
> RIKER: "Then we walk softer. Or we make them feel something else."
>
> JUNIOR: "The sensors are probably at the junction. We can go around."
>
> SULLY: "Or we wait for the ventilation to kick in. It's loud enough to mask us."
