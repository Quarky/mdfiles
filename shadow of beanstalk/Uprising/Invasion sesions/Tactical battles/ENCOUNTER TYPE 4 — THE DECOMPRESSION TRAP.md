## ENCOUNTER TYPE 4 — THE DECOMPRESSION TRAP

### Location Context
This encounter can be placed at any section with degraded atmo seals. In Kaguya, it's the corridor between Dome Beta and Ring 1 StarScape.

### Setup
The squad is moving through a corridor when a decompression event occurs. The corridor is partially depressurizing—oxygen levels dropping, seals activating, emergency systems engaging. The military is advancing through the decompressed corridor (they have sealed suits). The squad does not have sealed suits.

### The Challenge
| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Corridor (3 meters wide), emergency lighting, oxygen levels dropping (85%, then 70%, then 50%) |
| **Enemy** | Military Squad (advancing). 8 troopers. Sealed suits. |
| **Objective** | Escape the decompressing corridor before the oxygen level drops below survivable levels. |

### Tactical Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Run** | Run to the nearest sealed section | Average | Dangerous, but fast |
| **Find a Hardsuit** | Find an environmental hardsuit in the corridor | Hard | Provides oxygen, but takes time |
| **Cut Through the Wall** | Cut through a wall into a sealed section | Daunting | Creates a route, but requires time |
| **Fight** | Engage the military in the decompressing corridor | Very Hard | The military has sealed suits. The squad doesn't. |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Sully** | Cut through the wall | Creates an escape route |
| **Junior** | Find a hardsuit | Identifies a hardsuit location |
| **Riker** | Use oxygen reserves | Temporarily extends oxygen supply |
| **Maeko** | Move quickly | Runs to the nearest sealed section |

### Outcome
If the squad runs: They reach a sealed section. Some suffer oxygen deprivation.
If the squad finds hardsuits: They survive the decompression. The military loses track of them.
If the squad cuts through the wall: They escape into another section. The military is delayed.
If the squad fights: The military has the advantage. Casualties likely.

### Dialogue Moment

> RIKER: "Oxygen is dropping. 85%... 80%..."
>
> SULLY: "I can cut through this wall. But I need 90 seconds."
>
> JUNIOR: "There's a hardsuit 20 meters behind us. In a maintenance locker. I saw it on the schematics."
>
> FLETCHER: (looking at the advancing military) "We don't have 90 seconds. We have 60. Maybe 70."
>
> MAEKO: "Then we split. Some get the hardsuit. Some cut the wall. We move."
