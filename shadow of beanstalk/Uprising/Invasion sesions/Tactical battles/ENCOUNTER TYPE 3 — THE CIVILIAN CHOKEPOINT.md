## ENCOUNTER TYPE 3 — THE CIVILIAN CHOKEPOINT

### Location Context
This encounter can be placed at any corridor where civilians are present. In Kaguya, it's the connecting corridor between StarScape and the inner domes.

### Setup
The squad is moving through a corridor. A group of civilians is in front of them, trying to reach the inner domes. A military patrol is behind them, advancing through the corridor. The civilians are in the middle of the compression zone.

### The Challenge
| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Corridor (4 meters wide), normal lighting, multiple side exits |
| **Civilians** | 15-20 people, including families, elderly, and wounded |
| **Enemy** | Military Patrol (behind). 4 troopers. Advancing through the corridor. |
| **Objective** | Get the civilians to safety without the military catching them. |

### Tactical Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Escort the Civilians** | Move with the civilians, guiding them to the inner domes | Average | Slow, but safe |
| **Find an Alternative Route** | Lead the civilians through the maintenance network | Hard | Requires navigating the maintenance network, but avoids the military |
| **Hold the Corridor** | Use the civilians as a screen and hold the corridor | Very Hard | Military won't fire into civilians, but the civilians are in danger |
| **Negotiate with the Patrol** | Use Fletcher's old uniform to convince the patrol to let the civilians pass | Hard | Requires specific conditions (Fletcher's uniform, believable story) |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Fletcher** | Use his old uniform to bluff the patrol | Delays the patrol by 30-60 seconds |
| **Junior** | Find an alternative route | Leads the civilians through the maintenance network |
| **Mac** | Hold the corridor while the civilians move | Provides cover for the civilians |
| **Maeko** | Scout ahead for a safe route | Finds a route to the inner domes |

### Outcome
If the squad escorts the civilians: The civilians reach the inner domes safely. The squad is delayed.
If the squad finds an alternative route: The civilians escape through the maintenance network. The military loses track of them.
If the squad holds the corridor: The civilians reach the inner domes, but the squad takes casualties.
If the squad negotiates with the patrol: The patrol lets the civilians pass. The squad is detained or searched.

### Dialogue Moment

> CIVILIAN (older man, carrying a child): "Please. We just want to get to the shelter. We were told to go to the inner domes."
>
> FLETCHER: (to Dante) "They're going to get caught. The patrol is advancing. We need to move them now."
>
> MAEKO: "There's a maintenance hatch 50 meters ahead. It leads to the inner domes. I can take them."
>
> RIKER: "The patrol will be here in 90 seconds. If we're not moving, we're trapped."
