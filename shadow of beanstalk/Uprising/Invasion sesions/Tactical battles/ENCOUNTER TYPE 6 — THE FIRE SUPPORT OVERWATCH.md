## ENCOUNTER TYPE 6 — THE FIRE SUPPORT OVERWATCH

### Location Context
This encounter can be placed at any open area. In Kaguya, it's the central atrium or a large cargo bay.

### Setup
The squad needs to cross an open area to reach their objective. A military fire support element has set up a position overlooking the open area. They have a heavy weapon (LMG or Gauss rifle) and clear line of sight.

### The Challenge
| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Open area (20-50 meters), partial cover, no overhead cover |
| **Enemy** | Military Fire Support Element. 2-3 troopers. Bipod mounted LMG. |
| **Objective** | Cross the open area without getting pinned down. |

### Tactical Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Suppress** | Suppress the fire support element | Hard | Requires someone to lay down suppressive fire |
| **Flank** | Flank the fire support element through an alternative route | Average | Takes longer, but avoids the line of sight |
| **Smoke** | Use smoke grenades to obscure the open area | Average | Provides cover to cross |
| **Wait** | Wait for the fire support element to run out of ammunition | Very Hard | They have large ammunition reserves |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Fletcher** | Identify the fire support element's position | Provides information for the flank |
| **Riker** | Create a diversion | Draws the fire support element's attention |
| **Mac** | Provide suppressive fire | Delays the fire support element |
| **Sully** | Create a smoke cloud | Obscures the open area |

### Outcome
If the squad suppresses the fire support element: They cross the open area.
If the squad flanks the fire support element: They avoid the line of sight.
If the squad uses smoke: They cross the open area with less risk.
If the squad waits: The fire support element remains in position. The squad is delayed.

### Dialogue Moment

> FLETCHER: "I can see the fire support element. 11 o'clock, 50 meters, bipod mounted LMG."
>
> RIKER: "If I can get to that power conduit, I can cause a localized overload. It'll give us 10 seconds of confusion."
>
> MAEKO: "10 seconds. I can cross 50 meters in 10 seconds."
>
> SULLY: "We'll need smoke. I have two smoke grenades."
