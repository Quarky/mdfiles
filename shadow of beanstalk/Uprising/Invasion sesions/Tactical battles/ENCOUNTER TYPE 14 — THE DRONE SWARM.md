## ENCOUNTER TYPE 14 — THE DRONE SWARM

### Setup
The military deploys a swarm of small recon drones to search a section of the facility. The drones are small, hard to spot, and move quickly.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Drone Deployment:** 3-5 small drones are released into a section of the facility.
2. **Search Pattern:** The drones move through corridors and rooms, transmitting visual and audio data back to the military.
3. **Targeting:** The military identifies target positions and moves to engage.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Any location |
| **Enemy** | Drone Swarm (3-5 small drones) |
| **Objective** | Avoid detection, destroy the drones, or find cover |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Hide** | Hide in a location the drones can't see | Average | The drones move past you |
| **Destroy the Drones** | Shoot or destroy the drones | Hard | Attracts attention, but removes the threat |
| **EM Interference** | Use EM interference to disrupt the drones | Hard | The drones stop functioning for a short time |
| **Move** | Move quickly through the area | Average | The drones see you, but you're out of range |
| **Signal Jamming** | Use a jammer to block the drone's signal | Very Hard | Requires specialized equipment |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Riker** | Create EM interference | Disrupts the drones |
| **Junior** | Identify the drones' pattern | Finds a window to move |
| **Sully** | Use a flashbang to blind the drones | Temporarily disables their cameras |
| **Maeko** | Move quickly through the area | Outruns the drones |

### Dialogue Moment

> JUNIOR: "Drones. Small ones. They're searching the area."
>
> RIKER: "I can create EM interference. But it'll be local. They'll still see us if we're in the open."
>
> SULLY: "I have a flashbang. It'll blind their cameras for a few seconds."
>
> MAEKO: "A few seconds is all I need. I can move to the next section."
