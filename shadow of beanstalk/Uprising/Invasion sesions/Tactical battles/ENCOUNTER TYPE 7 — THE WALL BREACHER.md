## ENCOUNTER TYPE 7 — THE WALL BREACHER

### Setup
The military has advanced through the outer ring and established a forward position. They have identified the Loony position on the other side of a wall. They want to breach the wall and surprise the Loonies.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Sensor Deployment:** A trooper attaches a wall-penetrating sensor to the surface, scans the room on the other side. They identify enemy positions, heat signatures, movement patterns.
2. **Back Out:** The trooper pulls back from the wall. They don't stay in position.
3. **Mass Driver Target:** A trooper with a Gauss rifle aims at the identified positions through the wall.
4. **Shot Through Wall:** The Gauss rifle fires. The projectile penetrates the wall and hits the target on the other side. The Loonies are caught by surprise.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Any wall, any location |
| **Enemy** | Fire Team (4 troopers) — Sensor trooper, Gauss trooper, 2 security |
| **Objective** | Prevent the wall breach, escape the kill zone, or counter-attack |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Move Positions** | If you're the target, move immediately | Average | The Gauss trooper is targeting where you were; moving saves your life |
| **Find the Sensor** | The sensor is still on the wall. Remove or disable it. | Hard | Removes the military's ability to target through the wall |
| **Return Fire Through Wall** | Guess where the Gauss trooper is positioned and fire back | Very Hard | Requires knowing the military's position; if successful, suppresses the Gauss trooper |
| **Move the Fight** | Break contact and move to a different location | Average | Takes the fight away from the military's prepared killing zone |
| **Use the Wall as Cover** | Place a heavy object against the wall | Average | Provides protection against the Gauss fire |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Identify where the sensor is likely placed | Guides the team to disable it |
| **Riker** | Disable or spoof the sensor | Removes the military's targeting data |
| **Sully** | Cut through the wall at a different location | Creates a new avenue for attack |
| **Mac** | Place a heavy object against the wall | Provides cover |
