## ENCOUNTER TYPE 20 — THE LASER DESIGNATOR

### Setup
The military uses a laser designator to paint a target for orbital weapons.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Laser Painting:** A trooper uses a laser designator to mark a target (a position, a vehicle, a structure).
2. **Orbital Strike:** An orbital platform fires a weapon at the painted target.
3. **Destruction:** The target is destroyed by the orbital strike.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Open area (exposed to orbit) |
| **Enemy** | Military (laser designator), Orbital Platform (weapon) |
| **Objective** | Avoid being painted, escape the strike zone, or counter-attack |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Move** | Move away from the painted target | Easy | You avoid the strike |
| **Find the Laser** | Locate the laser designator and destroy it | Hard | Prevents the strike |
| **Shield the Target** | Move a heavy object over the painted target | Hard | The laser can't penetrate the shield |
| **Counter-Designate** | Use your own laser to confuse the orbital platform | Very Hard | Requires a laser designator and expertise |
| **Leave the Area** | Leave the open area entirely | Average | You're out of range of the orbital strike |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Identify the laser designator's location | Guides the team to destroy it |
| **Riker** | Create a laser decoy | Confuses the orbital platform |
| **Sully** | Use a reflective surface to deflect the laser | Makes it harder for the military to target |
| **Maeko** | Move the vehicle out of the strike zone | Saves the extraction vehicle |

### Dialogue Moment

> JUNIOR: "Laser designator! They're painting the extraction point."
>
> RIKER: "If they paint it, the orbital platform will fire. We have minutes."
>
> SULLY: "I can deflect the laser with a reflective surface. But it's temporary."
>
> MAEKO: "I can move the hopper. 90 seconds. Give me 90 seconds."
