## ENCOUNTER TYPE 22 — THE CONVEYOR BELT AMBUSH

### Setup (Loony Initiated)
The Loonies are being pursued down a corridor. They lead the military onto a conveyor belt—where they have prepared an ambush.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Lure:** The Loonies retreat down a corridor, leading the military onto a conveyor belt.
2. **Ambush:** The Loonies activate the conveyor belt in reverse, trapping the military in a crossfire.
3. **Fire:** The Loonies fire from concealed positions on either side of the corridor.

### Loony Technology
- Conveyor Belt Override (Riker's expertise)
- Concealed Positions (maintenance hatches)
- Improvised Weapons (Plasma Cutters, Rebar Clubs, etc.)

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Corridor with conveyor belt, elevated side positions |
| **Enemy** | Military Fire Team (4 troopers) — Trapped on the conveyor belt |
| **Objective** | Trapping the military, eliminating or disengaging |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Activate the Belt** | Reverse the conveyor belt, trapping the military | Average | The military can't move forward or backward |
| **Fire from Concealed Positions** | Fire from maintenance hatches on either side | Hard | The military is caught in a crossfire |
| **Wait for Reinforcements** | The military will call for reinforcements | Average | You have 2-3 minutes before they arrive |
| **Escape While They're Trapped** | Use the distraction to escape | Average | The military is delayed |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Riker** | Activate the conveyor belt | Traps the military |
| **Sully** | Fire from a concealed position | Engages the military |
| **Fletcher** | Provide suppressing fire | Pins the military |
| **Mac** | Cover the escape route | Allows the team to retreat |

### Dialogue Moment

> RIKER: "I can reverse the conveyor belt. They'll be trapped."
>
> SULLY: "I'm in position. I can fire from the maintenance hatch."
>
> FLETCHER: "When they're trapped, we fire. We need to be quick."
>
> MAC: "I'll cover the retreat. When we're done, we go."
