## ENCOUNTER TYPE 8 — THE GAS ASSAULT

### Setup
The military is clearing a section of the facility with civilians present. They want to flush out the Loonies without risking their own troopers. They deploy a non-lethal gas.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Gas Deployment:** The military fires a gas canister into the corridor or room. The gas is a non-lethal irritant (tear gas variant).
2. **Wait for Gas to Disperse:** The military waits 60-90 seconds, allowing the gas to affect the Loonies.
3. **Advance with Masks:** The military advances into the area wearing gas masks (or sealed suits). The Loonies are disoriented, coughing, unable to fight effectively.
4. **Secure the Area:** The military secures the Loonies, who are now non-threatening.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Corridor or room with limited ventilation |
| **Enemy** | Fire Team (4 troopers) — 2 advancing, 2 cover |
| **Objective** | Survive the gas, avoid capture, or counter-attack |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Hold Breath** | Hold your breath and move through the gas | Hard | Some gas still affects you, but you can move |
| **Use Gas Mask** | If you have a gas mask or respirator, use it | Easy | You're unaffected by the gas |
| **Ventilate the Area** | Open a vent or door to let the gas out | Average | Clears the gas, but exposes you |
| **Fire Through Gas** | Fire blindly through the gas, hoping to hit the military | Very Hard | Low chance of hitting anything, but could force the military to retreat |
| **Retreat** | Move out of the affected area | Average | The military takes the position, but you escape |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Riker** | Override the ventilation system | Clears the gas faster |
| **Junior** | Find an alternative route | Avoids the gas entirely |
| **Fletcher** | Identify where the military will advance from | Prepares an ambush |
| **Sully** | Create a smoke screen to hide your retreat | Obscures the military's vision |

### Dialogue Moment

> FLETCHER: "Gas! Tear gas variant. They want us to cough so they can hear where we are."
>
> RIKER: (coughing) "I can override the ventilation. But I need 60 seconds."
>
> JUNIOR: "There's a maintenance hatch behind that panel. It leads to the next section."
>
> SULLY: (already moving) "I'll open it. Everyone through. Now."
