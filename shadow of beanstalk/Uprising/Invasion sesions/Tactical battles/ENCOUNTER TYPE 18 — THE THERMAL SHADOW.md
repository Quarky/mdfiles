## ENCOUNTER TYPE 18 — THE THERMAL SHADOW

### Setup
The military uses thermal imaging to track the Loonies through walls. The Loonies need to move without being seen.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Thermal Detection:** The military uses thermal imaging to identify heat signatures.
2. **Tracking:** The Loonies' heat signatures are visible through walls and floors.
3. **Intercept:** The military moves to intercept the Loonies.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Any location |
| **Enemy** | Military (tracking your movement) |
| **Objective** | Move without being tracked, or disrupt the thermal imaging |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Thermal Shielding** | Use blankets, suits, or other materials to block thermal signature | Average | You're invisible to thermal imaging |
| **Move with Heat** | Move with the facility's heat sources (machinery, vents) | Average | Your signature blends in |
| **Create False Heat** | Create a heat source elsewhere | Average | The military tracks the wrong location |
| **Move in Groups** | Huddle together to confuse the thermal image | Hard | The military can't distinguish individual targets |
| **Move Fast** | Move quickly through the facility | Average | The military can't keep up with you |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Identify thermal shielding materials | Guides the team to use them |
| **Riker** | Create a false heat source | Distracts the military |
| **Sully** | Use a hot cutting torch as a distraction | Creates a false heat signature |
| **Mac** | Move in a way that confuses thermal imaging | Makes it hard to track him |

### Dialogue Moment

> JUNIOR: "They're using thermal imaging. They can see us through the walls."
>
> RIKER: "Then we need to hide our heat. Blankets, suit layers, anything that blocks heat."
>
> SULLY: "Or we move with the machinery heat. The ventilation system is warm."
>
> MAC: "I can create a decoy. A hot piece of metal will draw their attention."
