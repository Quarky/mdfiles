## ENCOUNTER TYPE 11 — THE ACOUSTIC LOCATOR

### Setup
The military has a device that detects sound—footsteps, voices, movement—and triangulates the source.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Acoustic Sensor Deployment:** The military places a sensor in a corridor or room. It listens for sounds and identifies their source.
2. **Targeting:** The military knows where the Loonies are, even if they can't see them.
3. **Suppression Fire:** The military fires in the direction of the sound, suppressing or eliminating the Loonies.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Any location with sound-carrying acoustics |
| **Enemy** | Fire Team (4 troopers) — 1 sensor, 3 firing |
| **Objective** | Silence the sensor, avoid detection, or counter-attack |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Move Quietly** | Move without making sound | Hard | The sensor doesn't detect you |
| **Create False Sound** | Make noise in a different direction | Average | The military fires at the wrong location |
| **Find the Sensor** | Locate and disable the acoustic sensor | Hard | Removes the threat |
| **Fire on the Sensor** | Destroy the sensor from a distance | Average | Removes the threat, but gives away your position |
| **Use Existing Sound** | Move while the facility machinery is loud | Easy | The sensor can't distinguish your footsteps |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Identify where the sensor is likely placed | Guides the team to disable it |
| **Riker** | Create a false sound source | Distracts the military |
| **Fletcher** | Move quietly through known routes | Avoids detection |
| **Sully** | Use his knowledge of facility sound patterns | Moves with the noise |

### Dialogue Moment

> FLETCHER: "They have acoustic sensors. They can hear us."
>
> RIKER: "Then we need to make them hear something else. I can trigger a noise on the other side of the corridor."
>
> JUNIOR: "The sensor is probably at the junction. I can see it."
>
> SULLY: "I'll move when the ventilation kicks in. It's loud enough to cover me."
