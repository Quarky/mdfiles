## ENCOUNTER TYPE 15 — THE TETHER TRAP

### Setup
The military uses a tether—a line across a corridor—to trip or entangle the Loonies.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Tether Deployment:** A trooper places a thin line across a corridor at ankle or head height.
2. **Ambush:** When a Loony trips the tether, the military opens fire from a concealed position.
3. **Capture:** If the Loonies don't trip, the military can still use the tether to control movement.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Corridor (3-4 meters wide) |
| **Enemy** | Fire Team (4 troopers) — Concealed, waiting |
| **Objective** | Avoid the tether, escape the ambush, or counter-attack |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Spot the Tether** | Notice the tether before you trip it | Average | Avoid the trap |
| **Cut the Tether** | Cut the tether before crossing | Average | Removes the trap |
| **Move Wide** | Move around the tether | Easy | Avoid the trap |
| **Trigger It Deliberately** | Trip the tether while you're not in the kill zone | Hard | The military fires at the wrong location |
| **Go Over** | Jump or climb over the tether | Average | Avoid the trap |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Notice the tether | Warns the team |
| **Sully** | Cut the tether with his torch | Removes the trap |
| **Fletcher** | Move wide around the tether | Avoids the trap |
| **Mac** | Trigger the tether deliberately | Draws fire away from the team |

### Dialogue Moment

> JUNIOR: "Stop! There's a line across the corridor."
>
> SULLY: "It's thin. Almost invisible. They're waiting for us."
>
> FLETCHER: "They're probably in cover at the far end. We need to avoid this."
>
> MAC: "Or trigger it deliberately. I can move fast enough to get out of the kill zone."
