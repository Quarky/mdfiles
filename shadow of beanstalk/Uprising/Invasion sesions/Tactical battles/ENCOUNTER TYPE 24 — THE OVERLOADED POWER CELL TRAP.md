## ENCOUNTER TYPE 24 — THE OVERLOADED POWER CELL TRAP

### Setup (Loony Initiated)
The Loonies use an overtensioned power cell as a trap. They place it in a military patrol's path and detonate it remotely.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Placement:** A power cell is placed in a corridor or room.
2. **Lure:** The military patrol is lured into the area.
3. **Detonation:** The power cell is detonated, creating a blast that incapacitates the military.

### Loony Technology
- Overtensioned Power Cell (improvised grenade)
- Remote Detonator (simple wire trigger)
- Knowledge of Military Patrol Patterns

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Corridor or room |
| **Enemy** | Military Patrol (4 troopers) — Caught in the blast |
| **Objective** | Trap and eliminate a military patrol |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Place the Trap** | Set up the power cell and detonator | Average | The trap is ready |
| **Lure the Patrol** | Draw the patrol into the trap | Average | The patrol enters the kill zone |
| **Detonate** | Detonate the power cell | Hard | The patrol is incapacitated |
| **Escape** | Leave the area after the blast | Average | The trap is complete |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Riker** | Overload a power cell | Creates the trap |
| **Junior** | Identify patrol pattern | Finds the best location |
| **Fletcher** | Lure the patrol | Draws them into the trap |
| **Maeko** | Escape after the blast | Leaves the area |

### Dialogue Moment

> RIKER: "This power cell is overtensioned. If I wire it right, it'll blow."
>
> JUNIOR: "The patrol passes this junction every 15 minutes."
>
> FLETCHER: "I can lure them into the kill zone."
>
> MAEKO: "After it blows, we need to be gone. They'll send reinforcements."
