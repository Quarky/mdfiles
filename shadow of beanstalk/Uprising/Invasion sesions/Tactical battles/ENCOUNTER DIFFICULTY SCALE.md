## ENCOUNTER DIFFICULTY SCALE

| Difficulty    | Description         | Typical Threat Level                          |
| ------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Easy**      | No immediate threat | Single patrol, no combat                      |
| **Average**   | Minor threat        | One fire team, limited cover                  |
| **Hard**      | Significant threat  | Multiple fire teams, open area, heavy weapons |
| **Daunting**  | Major threat        | Military force, no cover, limited options     |
| **Very Hard** | Extreme threat      | Exosuit, decompression, trapped               |
