## ENCOUNTER TYPE 19 — THE SONIC DISRUPTOR

### Setup
The military uses a device that emits a high-pitched sound to disorient and confuse the Loonies.

### The Tactical Pattern
1. **Sonic Deployment:** The military activates a sonic disruptor in a corridor or room.
2. **Disorientation:** The sound is painful and disorienting—the Loonies can't think clearly, can't hear each other, can't coordinate.
3. **Advance:** The military advances while the Loonies are disoriented.

### Tactical Challenge

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Environment** | Enclosed space (sound carries) |
| **Enemy** | Fire Team (4 troopers) — Advancing while the device is active |
| **Objective** | Survive the sonic attack, disable the device, or counter-attack |

### Options

| Option | Description | Difficulty | Outcome |
|--------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Ear Protection** | Use ear protection (earplugs, anything) | Average | You're less affected by the sound |
| **Find the Device** | Locate and disable the sonic device | Hard | Removes the threat |
| **Leave the Area** | Move out of the affected area | Average | The sound doesn't reach you |
| **Fire Through the Sound** | Fire blindly toward the military | Very Hard | Low chance of hitting anything, but could force the military to retreat |
| **Use Sealed Suit** | Sealed suits provide ear protection | Average | The sound is muffled |

### NPC Actions

| NPC | Action | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Junior** | Identify the sound's source | Guides the team to disable it |
| **Riker** | Override the sonic device | Disables the device |
| **Sully** | Use ear protection | Protects himself |
| **Mac** | Move toward the sound | Engages the military at close range |

### Dialogue Moment

> JUNIOR: "The sound is coming from the junction. They have a sonic device."
>
> RIKER: (covering ears) "I can override it. But I need to get to the junction."
>
> SULLY: "I have ear protection. I can go with you."
>
> MAC: "I'll move toward them. They can't shoot what they can't see."
