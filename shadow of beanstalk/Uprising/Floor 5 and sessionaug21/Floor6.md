## WHAT VXA-CORE-1 PREDICTS — TECHNICAL DETAIL

Before descending to Floor 6, the GM should understand what the AI actually does. This grounds every encounter with it.

The psychographic model works by identifying patterns in human behavior that humans themselves are not consciously aware of. The same cognitive shortcuts that make a person click on certain content, buy certain products, or respond to certain emotional triggers are the same shortcuts that make them choose certain passwords, type certain commands under stress, and check certain vulnerabilities before others.

**The AI predicts:**

**NETWORK VULNERABILITIES** — Based on the behavioral profiles of the engineers who built and maintain a network, the AI predicts which vulnerabilities they are most likely to have introduced. An engineer with certain cognitive patterns tends to make certain kinds of mistakes. An organization with certain management patterns tends to underprioritize certain kinds of security. The AI knows the human behind the machine.

**USER BEHAVIOR** — What a specific person will do next, with what the book describes as extraordinary accuracy across "an amazingly effective road map to replicate favorable responses." The AI has been building these maps since people received their first PAD at age one.

**PASSWORDS** — Not by brute force. By behavioral analysis. Humans select passwords using the same cognitive patterns they use for everything else — family names, significant dates, words associated with emotional safety, sequential patterns when under cognitive load. With decades of behavioral data, the AI predicts passwords with 60-94% accuracy depending on data depth. The old Internet fell because the AI predicted every credential before the attack began.

**WHAT FILES YOU WILL OPEN** — Based on professional role, current stress level, time of day, recent activity, and hundreds of other behavioral markers, the AI predicts which files a specific person will access next. This was used to position malware precisely in the Blackout.

**WHAT YOU WILL DO NEXT** — In a crisis, humans revert to trained responses. The AI knows those trained responses from decades of modeling. When a system administrator was responding to the Blackout attack, the AI already knew what commands they would type, in what order, with what timing. The defenses were prepared for the defenders before the defenders arrived.

**SOFTWARE VULNERABILITIES** — Buffer overflows, race conditions, protocol weaknesses in legacy systems — the AI predicts which of these a specific organization is most likely to have, based on the behavioral profiles of their engineering staff and the psychographic patterns of their procurement decisions.

**SYSTEM VULNERABILITIES** — Protocol-level weaknesses, the specific Ethernet handshake vulnerabilities of that era, network topology weaknesses that emerge from human decisions about how to build systems. The AI knows how humans build things and therefore knows how the things humans built are weak.

**WHY THE BLACKOUT WORKED:**

The old Internet had defenders. They were competent. They had protocols.

The Blackout worked because the AI predicted which vulnerabilities the defenders would check first — and attacked the ones they would check last. The defenders were always one step behind because the AI had already modeled their response sequence. By the time they were defending against attack vector A, the AI had already deployed through attack vector D, which the defenders would have gotten to in twelve minutes if the attack on vector A hadn't consumed their attention.

It wasn't faster than human defenders. It was more patient. It knew where they would be before they got there.

**STARREACH AND MARS:**

The AI needs behavioral data on Mars populations before Phase 2 can be deployed. Currently at 61.3% prediction accuracy — insufficient. StarReach's passive collection arrays will accelerate data collection to achieve the 90%+ threshold needed. Without StarReach: 4.2 years. With StarReach: 1.8 years.

The model cannot predict what it cannot see. That is why they cannot see StarReach.
