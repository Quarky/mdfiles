## SESSION FLOW — RECOMMENDED ORDER

---

### PHASE 1 — DESCENT & ARRIVAL (5-10 minutes)

**Establishing the Environment:**

The magnetic elevator car descends in near-silence. The display above the door ticks past Floor 1, Floor 2, Floor 3, Floor 4. Then Floor 5. The car settles with a faint magnetic click. The door slides open.

**Sensory Setup (Read to Players):**

The air hits you immediately — minus eight degrees Celsius. Your breath is visible before you've taken a full step. The corridor ahead is short, ending at a pressure door. Beyond that door: the sound. A deep, constant, layered mechanical hum that you feel in your sternum before you hear it with your ears. The cooling systems. Running continuously. They have been running for years.

**GM Notes:**
- Establish the physical reality of the cold before anything else
- Let players react to the temperature — dress warmly, breathe visible, etc.
- The hum should be felt, not just heard — it's in the body

**Optional Puzzle: The Pressure Door**
The pressure door has a key panel and a biometric scanner. The biometric scanner is offline — crisis broadcast mode deprioritizing non-broadcast admin requests. The key panel is active. It can be manipulated if Dante has the right tools or knowledge.

| Approach | Difficulty | Outcome |
|----------|------------|---------|
| Bypass the Lock | Mechanics (Average) | Open the door without the key |
| Find the Key | Perception (Average) | A maintenance key is in the elevator car's emergency kit |
| Force the Door | Athletics (Hard) | Force the door open — creates noise, may alert anyone inside |

### PHASE 2 — THE SERVER CORRIDOR (20-30 minutes)

**Arriving in Section A:**

Beyond the pressure door: the server corridor. Four rows of server racks stretching north to south. Cold blue-white LED lighting. The hum is louder here, more present. The cold has texture — it moves in currents, warmer in some spots, colder in others.

**Sensory Setup (Read to Players):**

The racks are 2.1 meters tall, arranged in four rows. The lighting is cold blue-white — the color temperature of efficiency. Each rack has its own indicator lights: green status, amber warning, red fault. The rows are numbered on the ceiling: ROWS 1 through 4. At a glance: Row 1, all green. Row 2, mostly green, two amber. Row 3, all green. Row 4 — the far row — mostly green, with something unusual.

**Key Discoveries in Section A:**

**1. VX-ARCHIVE-7 (Row 4, Northwest Corner)**

The frost pattern on the rack's northern face is the first clue — something is different about this rack. Under the LED lighting at the right angle, the frost traces the outline of the original Vertex Systems International logo. The rack's surface tells a story: blue-grey paint bleeding through black NBN paint at the corners.

**Discoverable Elements:**
- [ ] Frost pattern revealing Vertex logo
- [ ] Original label under NBN label (Average Perception) — "PROJECT: STATIC"
- [ ] Secondary cable with emergency repair tape
- [ ] Access log panel — 6 days ago, 4h17m, FOUNDER credentials
- [ ] Partition label — "Named for the first to trust us"
- [ ] Rear panel inscription — "THE NETWORK REMEMBERS EVERYTHING"
- [ ] Secondary inscription — "If you are reading this you are the 5.3%"

**GM Notes:**
- The cold makes everything feel more significant — use it
- Let players examine the rack from multiple angles before giving them the full picture
- The rear panel requires moving the rack — one anchor bolt is stripped

**2. Row 4-9 Morse Code Lights**

Three racks north of VX-ARCHIVE-7, a standard rack shows an unusual status light pattern: cycling between green and amber in a sequence that is not random. Every twelve seconds: green, green, amber, green, amber, amber, green.

**Discoverable Elements:**
- [ ] Pattern is not random (Hard Perception)
- [ ] Recognized as Morse code (Average Computers)
- [ ] Decoded message: "SA689 LENA VASQUEZ LOWER RING SECTION 7"

**GM Notes:**
- The Morse code discovery is a significant beat — it points to Lena Vasquez before she is encountered
- The automated monitoring system logs it as "non-critical amber cycling — under review" for 3 years

**3. The Haas-Bioroid Maintenance Unit (Row 2)**

A Haas-Bioroid maintenance unit is doing a routine inspection. It stops and assesses Dante if he approaches within two meters. It does not flag him. Its task list includes maintaining the servers, not investigating people in maintenance clothing.

**Discoverable Elements:**
- [ ] Diagnostic port access (unlocked panel on its back)
- [ ] Physical maintenance log
- [ ] Recording status: ACTIVE — local storage
- [ ] Next upload: 22:00 HLT tonight

**GM Notes:**
- The bioroid creates a ticking clock — if Dante doesn't address it, his presence will be recorded
- Give players choices: wipe storage, disable bioroid, redirect upload, or leave it

**Optional Puzzle: The Bioroid**

| Approach | Difficulty | Outcome |
|----------|------------|---------|
| Disable Recording | Computers (Average) | Wipe local storage, no upload |
| Disable Bioroid | Mechanics (Hard) | Stops functioning, triggers alert |
| Leave It | — | Footage uploads at 22:00 |
| Redirect Upload | Computers (Hard) | Routes data to Dante's destination |

### PHASE 3 — THE COOLING PLANT (20-30 minutes)

**Arriving in Section B:**

The pressure door to the cooling plant is the heaviest on this floor. The seal breaks with a sound like a breath exhaled. The temperature drops further — minus fourteen. The sound is enormous: industrial-scale compressors the size of vehicles.

**Sensory Setup (Read to Players):**

The machinery produces cold as a byproduct of producing more cold. The compressors are arranged in two parallel rows, connected by refrigerant piping insulated in yellow cryogenic wrapping. The pipes have frost on their outer surfaces despite the insulation. The cold is winning even against its own containment. The space smells of refrigerant — a faint chemical sweetness that sits at the back of the throat.

**Key Discoveries in Section B:**

**1. Lena Vasquez (NPC)**

She is sitting on a maintenance step beside compressor 1-A, coat pulled tight, a thermos of something hot. She looks up when the door opens. She does not look surprised.

**Key Dialogue Points:**
- She designed this floor thirty years ago
- She was told it was a standard server installation
- She also designed Floor 6 — told it was environmental systems
- She found SA-6-89 in the district archives after retirement
- She has been coming here for seven years, checking, waiting
- She built a secondary access route behind compressor 4-B
- She put something in the floor drain of the Diagnostic Room

**If Dante tells her anything:**
She listens without expression. Whatever he tells her, she absorbs it the way someone receives information they have been expecting for a long time and are now confirming.

**Lena VASQUEZ — RIVAL**
Br 2 / Ag 2 / Int 4 / Cun 4 / Will 4 / Pr 3 | Soak 2 | WT 11 | ST 14
Skills: Athletics 1, Knowledge (Education) 4, Mechanics 4, Perception 3, Streetwise 2, Vigilance 3.
*Will not call security. Has been waiting for this conversation for seven years.*

**2. The Maintenance Log**

Wall-mounted, waterproof case beside compressor 1-A. Three handwriting styles across years of entries.

**Discoverable Elements:**
- [ ] Entry — 8 years ago: FOUNDER access extended by 4 hours
- [ ] Entry — 5 years ago: VX-ARCHIVE-7 power anomaly documented
- [ ] Entry — 6 days ago: FOUNDER access 14:30-18:47

**GM Notes:**
- The most recent entries are in the most precise handwriting — written by someone who understood they were building a record for someone to follow

**3. The Power Breaker Panel**

Behind compressor 4-B. A secondary breaker panel. Single large breaker. Label: "VX-ARCHIVE — DO NOT TOUCH."

**Discoverable Elements:**
- [ ] Label is in Kane Sr.'s handwriting
- [ ] Cutting this breaker severs power to VX-ARCHIVE-7
- [ ] AI model continues running elsewhere — this only cuts the Floor 6 terminal connection

**4. The Hidden Service Hatch**

Behind compressor 4-B, below the breaker panel. A 0.6-by-0.6-meter hatch in the basalt wall. Standard physical latch — no electronic lock.

**Discoverable Elements:**
- [ ] Opens to a service crawl approximately 0.8 meters square
- [ ] Crawl runs east then south toward Diagnostic Room
- [ ] Added after main construction — Lena did it herself
- [ ] Not in any filing anywhere

**Optional Puzzle: The Crawl Navigation**

| Approach                  | Difficulty           | Outcome                                      |
| ------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Navigate Without Incident | Athletics (Average)  | Move through crawl to Diagnostic Room        |
| Notice Crawl Details      | Perception (Average) | Signs of recent use — footprint, dropped pen |
| Find the Dropped Item     | Perception (Hard)    | Pen with name engraved: "Miyako"             |

### PHASE 4 — THE MONITORING ALCOVE (15-20 minutes)

**Arriving in Section C:**

Not a separate room — a recessed space cut slightly deeper into the basalt. Eight screens in two rows of four. The screens produce a small amount of heat that makes the alcove marginally warmer.

**Sensory Setup (Read to Players):**

The alcove is slightly warmer than the corridor. Not because of the heating system — because of the screens. They have been running for years. The heat they produce has slowly warmed the surrounding rock. The warmth is visible in the frost pattern on the walls — the frost is thinner here, at the edges of the alcove, as if the cold is having a harder time claiming this space. The screens are winning against the cold. Slowly, but winning.

**Key Discoveries in Section C:**

**Screens 1-2: Environmental and Server Status**
Everything within spec. Everything has been within spec for years. VX-ARCHIVE-7 shows amber on power consumption — 14% above baseline. Status logged as "MONITORED — NO ACTION."

**Screen 3: Network Traffic**
One stream highlighted in amber, routing to destination: VXA-CORE-1. The crisis broadcast audience response data feeding directly to the AI. In real time. Right now.

**Screen 4: VXA-CORE-1 Status**
Current accuracy: 94.7%. 3,014 anomaly flags generated. Calibration window: 3 hours 44 minutes remaining. Kaguya event classified Category-A. Data archived to Mars comparative model.

**Screen 5: StarReach Status**
Phase 3 on schedule. Mars psychographic accuracy: 61.3%. With StarReach: 1.8 years to Phase 2. Without: 4.2 years. Signed by H. MOSE.

**Screen 6: VXA-CORE-1 Access Log**
Last modification: 6 days ago. FOUNDER // KRK.SR. Duration: 4h17m. Session status: NOT FORMALLY CLOSED.

**Screen 7: Sigma Protocol History**
Sigma-1 through Sigma-5 were pre-Blackout calibration tests. The Blackout was Phase 1. Sigma-6 and Sigma-7 were post-Blackout coverage operations.

**Screen 8: Mars Psychographic Comparative Model**
Mars map — almost entirely red. Insufficient data. Projected coverage with StarReach — mostly yellow and green. Manually typed line: "The model cannot predict what it cannot see. StarReach is how it will see Mars."

**GM Notes:**
- Screens 3-6 are the most immediately informative
- Screen 7 is a significant beat — Sigma existed before the Blackout
- Screen 8 raises a question about who typed the manually typed line

### PHASE 5 — THE DIAGNOSTIC ROOM (20-30 minutes)

**Arriving in Section D:**

The only fully locked door on Floor 5. Mechatix T-7 key lock plus biometric scanner currently offline. Getting in: Lena's key, service crawl, or mechanical bypass.

**Sensory Setup (Read to Players):**

The door shows wear that tells a story. The key panel is scratched around the keyhole — someone has been using the wrong key repeatedly, trying to open this door. The scratches are recent. Within the last few months. Someone else has been trying to get into this room.

**Key Discoveries in Section D:**

**1. The Diagnostic Room — Physical Space**

4 by 5 meters. Raw basalt walls with workbench surfaces. The cold here is minus five rather than minus eight — the room has its own micro-heating element.

**Discoverable Elements:**
- [ ] Left workbench: diagnostic hardware — cable testers, network analysis tools
- [ ] Right workbench: recent activity — printed schematic, annotations in two inks
- [ ] Workbench wear patterns — regular, long-term, specific use

**2. The Equipment Case**

Under the right workbench. Sealed. Combination lock — four digits.

**Combination Puzzle:**
- Clue 1: Sticky note on cable tester — "Always check the FIRST connection before diagnosing a fault."
- Clue 2: Old annotation on schematic — "RELAY 001 ACTIVATION + 2 YEARS." Plus carved year in workbench surface.
- Clue 3: Sticky note on right workbench — "LAST: confirm physical integrity of Phase 2 preparation documents."
- Clue 4: Engraved plate on case — "STATIC-BC — HARDWARE MAINTENANCE KIT — SERIES 1"

**The combination is the year scratched into the workbench. Four digits.**

**Inside the Equipment Case:**
- [ ] Item 1: VXA-CORE-1 Backup Instance — data chip, labeled in Kane Sr.'s handwriting
- [ ] Item 2: VX Hardware Schematic — annotated, how to shut down VXA-CORE-1
- [ ] Item 3: Kane Sr.'s Sealed Letter — wax seal, ring impression

**3. The Floor Drain — Lena's Cache**

Northeast corner. Standard floor drain. Removable cover. Below: small metal tin, waterproof, sealed with insulating tape that has yellowed with age.

**Inside the Tin:**
- [ ] Folded note — Lena's handwriting
- [ ] Chip 1 — Floor 6 complete schematics
- [ ] Chip 2 — Lena's personal construction log

### PHASE 6 — THE STORAGE ANNEX (10-15 minutes)

**Arriving in Section E:**

Accessed through a secondary door from the cooling plant. Smaller space — 3 by 4 meters. The cold here is minus fourteen. The air is completely still. Nothing generates heat. Everything has been frozen for years.

**Sensory Setup (Read to Players):**

The air is completely still. No moving air. No fans. No circulation. The cold is so absolute that the air itself has stopped moving. The only sound is the faint ringing of absolute silence — the sound your ears make when there is no sound. The stillness is unsettling. It makes the room feel like a tomb.

**Key Discoveries in Section E:**

**1. Obsolete Hardware**

Server components from multiple eras — some Vertex Systems International, same generation as VX-ARCHIVE-7. Others from Haas-Bioroid and Jinteki. A corporate hardware graveyard.

**2. Replacement Parts**

Spare components for VX-ARCHIVE-7, labeled in a precise technical hand. Most recent acquisition: eight months ago.

**3. The Burned Drive**

Bottom of a box, under other hardware. A single data drive with a cracked casing and exposed internal components. The data chip at the center is intact.

**Discoverable Elements:**
- [ ] Drive was physically damaged deliberately
- [ ] Data chip intact — partial log
- [ ] Log contains entry from six years ago — "The model is ready. I was there. I did not stop it."

### PHASE 7 — THE NETWORK RELAY JUNCTION (10-15 minutes)

**Arriving in Section F:**

East wall, midway between the server corridor and the monitoring alcove. A metal panel bolted to the basalt. Behind the panel: the building's physical network infrastructure termination point.

**Key Discoveries in Section F:**

**1. The STATIC-BC Cable**

A single cable, older than the others, labeled "STATIC-BC — PSYCHOGRAPHIC DATA RELAY." This is the physical connection carrying psychographic monitoring data from the Floor 3 uplink panel to VXA-CORE-1.

| Leave It | — | Data flow continues |
| Trace the Cable | Mechanics (Average) | Follow path back to Floor 3 uplink panel |

**2. The Fiber Optic Log**

Physical log confirming: Floor 5 → Floor 6 connection is labeled "secondary — non-standard." Floor 5 → STATIC-BC uplink is labeled "dedicated."

**3. The Maintenance Access Log**

### PHASE 8 — MIYAKO (10-15 minutes)

**Location:** Floor 5 HVAC service junction — found in the crawl near the Diagnostic Room, or in the HVAC service junction.

**Sensory Setup (Read to Players):**

The HVAC service junction is a slightly larger space than the crawl itself — enough room to sit, barely. The air is still. The cold is deep. The space shows signs of occupation: a crumpled piece of paper, a pen with dried ink, footprints that lead in and stop.

There is a person here. Slumped against the wall. Unresponsive. Eyes open. Unfocused. Clutching a stack of physical papers.

**NPC — MIYAKO**

**Physical Condition:**
- Unresponsive but breathing
- Eyes open, unfocused
- Clutching a stack of physical papers and notebooks
- Signs of being in the HVAC for approximately 10-12 hours

- Otherwise uninjured

**Recovery:** 24+ hours before he can speak coherently.

**Key Discoveries:**

- [ ] Observations from the cooling plant — overheard conversation

### PHASE 9 — DEPARTURE (5-10 minutes)

**Exiting Floor 5:**

The elevator car waits. The door is open. The cold is still present, but the body has adapted. The hum is quieter here, at the elevator shaft.

**Final Sensory Description (Read to Players):**

The elevator door is open. The car is waiting. The cold is still present, but the body has adapted — it no longer feels like an attack. The hum is quieter here, at the elevator shaft. The transition is beginning. In a moment, you will be somewhere else. But the cold will stay in your clothing for approximately twenty minutes after you leave. You will be warm again before you process everything you found. That is appropriate. Processing takes longer than warming up.

**GM Notes:**
- The cold stays in Dante's clothing for 20 minutes after leaving
- Processing takes longer than warming up
- Let players decide where to go next: Floor 6, back to Floor 4, or out of the building entirely
