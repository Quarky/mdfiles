---

> **DOCUMENT NOTE:** This packet contains all documents found on or near Miyako when he was discovered in the Floor 5 HVAC system. He was found in a catatonic state — unresponsive, staring at nothing, clutching a collection of physical notes. The notes span approximately 12 hours, from early morning through his time in hiding.

**OBFUSCATION NOTE:** Miyako did not have the full picture when he began writing. His understanding developed over time. Some of his notes use careful, indirect language. Some references are oblique. He did not name the "Blackout Virus" directly in his main notebook — he only alluded to it. However, torn fragments found nearby, which appear to have been written in moments of greater clarity or desperation, contain the explicit term. The pieces are physically torn — some may have been separated intentionally, others damaged by the cold. They can be given to players gradually, allowing the conspiracy to unfold piece by piece.
