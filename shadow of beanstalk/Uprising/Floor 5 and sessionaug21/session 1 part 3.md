# ACT 2 — THE BROADCAST (45-60 min)
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

## THE BROADCAST — THE DECISION

Dante has the Section F file. He has the teenager's footage. He has access to the Launch Core terminal. He can push the broadcast through the Authority uplink.

**What It Does:**
- 4 minutes of Section F evidence on SYNC's Network
- 4 minutes before NBN cuts it
- 4 minutes is enough for it to be cached on a thousand PADs

**What It Contains:**
- The Section F file
- The Nisei Project data
- The teenager's footage
- Zane's statement

**The Risk:**
- Pushing the broadcast exposes Dante
- The EWS will trace it
- The military will be alerted

**The Reward:**
- The story gets out
- The evidence is public
- The Insurrection has a voice

---

**The Status:**

**What This Means:**
