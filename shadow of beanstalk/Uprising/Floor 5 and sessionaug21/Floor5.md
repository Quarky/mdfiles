## DESCENT — THE ELEVATOR

The car descends in near-silence, the only sound the faint hum of the magnetic propulsion system and the occasional click of a floor indicator passing.

Descending from Floor 4 to Floor 5, three things happen:

**First:** The lighting changes. Floor 4 has the warm amber of NBN's corporate color scheme — the same amber as the lobby, the same amber as every NBN screen in Heinlein. The elevator car's interior lighting shifts from amber to cold white as it passes between floors. For approximately four seconds, the car is lit in the transition between two different kinds of institutional certainty.

Floor 5 produces its own sound: a deep, constant, layered mechanical hum that Dante feels in his sternum before he hears it with his ears. The cooling systems. Running continuously. They have been running for years and will continue running and they produce a sound that is less like noise and more like pressure.

The car settles at Floor 5 level with a faint magnetic click. The door slides open.

Beyond the door: a short corridor leading to a pressure door. Beyond that: Floor 5.

Dante's breath is visible immediately. The air is approximately minus eight degrees Celsius. This is not a malfunction. This is the specification.
