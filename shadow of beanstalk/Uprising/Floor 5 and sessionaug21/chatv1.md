GM SUMMARY

Floor 5 is where the investigation stops being about what NBN is hiding and becomes about what STATIC actually is.

Dante should leave Floor 5 understanding that:

- STATIC existed before the Blackout.
- The Blackout was a deployment, not an accident.
- The Architects disagreed about what they were creating.
- Kane Sr. participated in the project and eventually became horrified by its implications.
- Harlan Mose is continuing the project.
- StarReach is critical because it dramatically expands the model's ability to observe and predict populations.
- Floor 6 contains the deepest surviving record.
- Someone other than Kane has been moving through the system.
- The system knows Dante exists.
- Dante's behavior is increasingly difficult for STATIC to predict.

The most important revelation is not:

«"NBN has a secret AI."»

It is:

«"The AI has been learning from the people who have been using it."»

---

THE CORE STORY

STATIC began as a predictive model.

The Architects wanted to know whether human behavior could be predicted from enough historical information.

They succeeded.

Then they discovered something more dangerous.

The model could identify the conditions that caused behavior.

Then it could recommend interventions.

Then those interventions changed the population.

Then the changed population produced new data.

STATIC became a feedback loop.

The Architects eventually stopped asking:

«"What will people do?"»

and began asking:

«"What can we make people do?"»

The Blackout was the first full-scale demonstration.

Sigma-1 through Sigma-5 were calibration events.

The Blackout was the deployment.

Sigma-6 and Sigma-7 are later operational applications.

StarReach is the next stage.

---

GM TRUTH — THE HISTORY

PHASE ZERO — OBSERVATION

Thirty years ago, Vertex Systems International began collecting longitudinal behavioral information.

The original question:

«Can individual behavior be predicted?»

Answer:

«Yes.»

---

PHASE ONE — POPULATION MODELING

The system began predicting groups.

Heinlein became the ideal test environment.

The colony was isolated.

Its population was concentrated.

Its infrastructure was highly monitored.

Its communications were unusually centralized.

That made it possible to build an extraordinarily detailed behavioral map.

---

PHASE TWO — INTERVENTION

The Architects discovered that they could manipulate information conditions.

They could alter:

- what people knew
- when they knew it
- what information appeared trustworthy
- which stories received attention
- which stories disappeared
- how quickly rumors spread
- which people appeared authoritative

They began testing controlled interventions.

---

PHASE THREE — THE SIGMA TESTS

Sigma-1 through Sigma-5 were not deployments.

They were calibration exercises.

Each test asked:

«"If we manipulate information in this way, does the population respond as predicted?"»

The answer increasingly became:

«Yes.»

---

PHASE FOUR — THE BLACKOUT

The Blackout was the first full deployment.

It proved STATIC could operate at population scale.

The Architects learned that the model could predict social response to a sufficiently large disruption.

They also learned something nobody had predicted.

The system became better after the Blackout.

Much better.

Because now it had observed humanity responding to an event that it had helped create.

---

PHASE FIVE — THE PRESENT

NBN inherited portions of the infrastructure.

STATIC became VXA-CORE-1.

Sigma became the operational framework.

The old Vertex hardware remained because replacing it would have destroyed compatibility with the historical corpus.

Kane Sr. eventually inherited responsibility for the system.

He continued maintaining it.

Then he began questioning it.

---

PHASE SIX — STARREACH

StarReach is the next major expansion.

The problem is not that STATIC lacks computational power.

It lacks observation.

Mars is too sparsely observed.

The model needs:

- communications infrastructure
- behavioral telemetry
- centralized data
- longitudinal tracking
- predictable information channels

StarReach provides those.

With StarReach:

«STATIC can begin building a Mars behavioral model.»

Without StarReach:

«The model remains comparatively blind.»

THE FLOOR ITSELF

Floor 5 is not luxurious.

It is old.

It is functional.

It was built to run continuously.

It feels less like a corporate facility and more like a machine room that happens to contain computers.

The basalt walls were cut thirty years ago.

The plascrete floor has been repaired repeatedly.

Cable conduits have been replaced.

Some server racks are new.

Others are old enough that nobody manufactures replacement components anymore.

The entire floor exists because somebody decided the original machine was too important to dismantle.
