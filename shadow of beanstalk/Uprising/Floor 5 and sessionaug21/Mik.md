### DOCUMENT M-3: OBSERVATIONS — THE BROADCAST

**PAGE 2 — 24 SEPTEMBER 1388 — EVENING**

---

I watched the broadcast yesterday evening. The evening news. Standard coverage of the He-3 market, the Beanstalk updates, the usual.

But something was different. The psychographic monitoring flags on the archive terminal were active — I could see them. Normally the flags are invisible to me. They were visible because someone had left the monitoring interface open on the archive terminal. Mose's session — he had accessed the archive terminal.

SOURCE: VXA-CORE-1
NEXT CALIBRATION: 6 DAYS

Six days. The same day as the FOUNDER access.

I did not know what VXA-CORE-1 was. I did not understand the calibration note.

I wrote it down.

### DOCUMENT M-6: HIS DECISION — GOING TO FLOOR 5

**PAGE 6 — 25 SEPTEMBER 1388 — 18:00 HLT**

---

I decided to go to Floor 5. I needed to see the secondary circuit for myself. The monitoring screens showed it active. The access logs showed someone was in there.

I took the elevator to Floor 5.

The cold hit me immediately. I was not prepared.

I walked through the server corridor. I saw the racks. The cold. The hum.

I found the cooling plant. I heard voices — one was Lena Vasquez, the structural engineer. She was talking to someone. She was saying things about the facility. About Floor 6. About the secondary access.

I hid in the HVAC service junction. I did not want them to see me.

I listened.

I heard enough.

### DOCUMENT M-7: THE LAST ENTRY — WHY HE STAYED

**PAGE 7 — 25 SEPTEMBER 1388 — 19:00 HLT**

---

I stayed in the HVAC because I could not leave.

I had heard enough to understand what this facility was for. The AI. The psychographic system. The Sigma protocols. Project Static. The Blackout. It was all connected.

The same system. Two applications. One engine.

The psychographic AI that predicts content preferences and purchasing behavior is also the Blackout virus. It predicted passwords. Security questions. How people would respond to the attack. The Blackout succeeded because the AI knew the people defending the old Internet better than they knew themselves.

And StarReach is how it gets to Mars. The relay satellites are not just broadcast infrastructure. They are passive data collection arrays. They gather behavioral data from Mars population centers and feed it back to VXA-CORE-1.

The model cannot predict what it cannot see. That is why they cannot see StarReach.

I understood it. All of it. In that moment, in the cold, in the dark, I understood it.

And I could not leave.

I have been here for approximately 24 hours. I have written everything I remember. If you find me, I will not be able to tell you this myself. I am past that. I need someone to know.

— Miyako
