F5 — FLOOR 5 GM MASTER REFERENCE

Purpose

Floor 5 is not simply a server room.

It is the place where the history of Project STATIC becomes physically visible.

Floors 1–4 tell Dante what NBN wants people to believe.

Floor 5 shows him what NBN actually built.

Floor 6 shows him what the people who built it eventually became afraid of.

The central GM principle for the floor is:

«Do not reveal the conspiracy in one speech. Let the facility reveal it.»

Every document, machine, maintenance entry, strange light, NPC, and physical alteration should answer one question while creating another.

What Dante can eventually understand

The broad truth is:

- Vertex Systems created the original behavioral prediction architecture.
- NBN acquired and buried the project under its own infrastructure.
- 
- The Blackout was not an accidental catastrophe.
- The Heinlein population was used as a full-scale behavioral calibration environment.
- Sigma-1 through Sigma-5 were increasingly large tests.
- The Blackout was the first true operational deployment.
- Kane Sr. eventually became involved in preserving the system and improving it.
- He also became increasingly uncomfortable with what the system was being used for.
- STATIC-BC is not merely an archive.
- It is the live behavioral engine feeding predictive information into the wider strategic program.
- StarReach exists partly because the model cannot adequately predict populations it cannot continuously observe.
- Mars is therefore not merely a future destination.
- Mars is the next population-scale predictive problem.
- Kane Sr. deliberately left a trail.
- Lena Vasquez independently preserved another trail.
- Someone else is actively moving through the facility and leaving warnings.
- Harlan Mose is still actively using the system.
- The crisis happening today is not merely being reported by NBN.
- It is being used as live calibration data.

GM Rule: The Facility Is Alive

Floor 5 should never feel empty.

Even when Dante is alone:

- compressors cycle;
- cooling fans change pitch;
- server lights blink;
- frost falls from a pipe;
- a bioroid passes;
- an amber warning clears;
- the elevator occasionally announces a floor transition;
- a distant relay clicks;
- data continues moving;
- somewhere, someone is still typing.

The important revelation is that the conspiracy does not require everyone to be in on it.

Most employees are simply doing their jobs.

A maintenance worker knows not to question a particular rack.

An engineer knows a door exists that isn't on the drawings.

A technician sees a strange power draw every day.

An administrator sees an unusual access event and files it correctly.

The system survives because thousands of ordinary people perform ordinary actions around extraordinary secrets.

Environmental Motif

The recurring image of Floor 5 is:

Heat being removed.

The servers generate heat.

The cooling plant removes it.

The people generate information.

STATIC removes uncertainty from it.

The system continuously turns human behavior into something colder and more manageable.

That is the thematic heart of the floor.

F5/F6 — CAMPAIGN THEME

The floor should leave the player with one uncomfortable realization:

The most frightening thing here is not that someone built a machine capable of predicting people.

It is that people found reasons to use it.

Mara saw the danger.

Elias stayed to understand it.

Kane built it, then doubted it.

Mose believes the outcome justifies the method.

Lena built the room without knowing what it was.

Marcus maintains it without knowing why.

The bioroid records everyone without understanding what it sees.

ARCHIVE preserves uncertainty.

STATIC eliminates it.

And Dante has now entered the gap between them.

That gap is where the story lives.

F5/F6 — GM END STATE

By the end of the exploration, the players should ideally know:

- Project STATIC existed before the Blackout.
- The Blackout was preceded by deliberate calibration.
- Sigma-1 through Sigma-5 were tests.
- The system was designed to model human behavior at population scale.
- NBN inherited and expanded the system.
- Kane Sr. became increasingly conflicted.
- Harlan Mose remains committed.
- StarReach is tied to expanding the model's reach to Mars.
- The 5.3% are people the model cannot confidently predict.
- Kane deliberately left evidence.
- Mara and Elias opposed the original deployment.
- Someone else is currently moving through the system.
- Floor 6 contains Kane's final conversation with the AI.
- The AI has noticed Dante.

They should not know everything.

Important unresolved questions:

- Who performed the 04:30 system override?
- Who typed the message on Screen 8?
- What happened to Elias Rook?
- What happened to Mara Venn?
- What exactly happened during the Blackout?
- Did Kane die naturally, disappear, or was he removed?
- What is Harlan planning for StarReach?
- Who is the 5.3% anomaly population?
- Why did the AI begin behaving differently during Kane's final session?
- Why can it not confidently predict Dante?
- Who else knows about the Founder Archive?

The players should leave Floor 6 with more certainty about the past and less certainty about the future.

That is the intended emotional endpoint.
