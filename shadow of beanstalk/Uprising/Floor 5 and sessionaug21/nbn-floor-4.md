> **FLOOR 4 ORIENTATION**
> Floor 4 is the executive level. Thirty private offices in a single long corridor, plus two dedicated network rooms at either end. This is where the people who make decisions work — or were working, before the crisis broadcast consumed everything.
>
> **ACCESS:** Subdermal RFID implants. Executive staff carry chips that broadcast clearance automatically when near the elevator access panel or any floor reader. The system recognizes them by proximity and opens the floor without any action required on their part. No card, no scan, no gesture. The elevator simply opens at Floor 4 if they are authorized. If they are not, it does not stop here.
>

What remains: Brennan, standing in the corridor. Mose's empty office with things left behind. The legal affairs suite, recently abandoned. ## PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

Floor 4 is where the money shows. The lobby's warm amber NBN lighting continues here, warmer than anywhere below. The floor is not polished plascrete — it is a composite surface that resembles wood grain without being wood, designed to communicate warmth and permanence in an environment where neither is natural. The ceiling is higher than the floors below: 2.8 meters, with recessed lighting that produces a diffuse even glow, nothing harsh, nothing that would make an executive feel like they were being interrogated.

The corridor runs the full east-west length of the facility. Thirty offices on the south side, doors with physical key locks and secondary biometric readers. The biometric readers are the subdermal RFID system — the same system that controls elevator access. They are also currently deprioritized.

At the west end: **NETWORK ROOM WEST** — the primary network infrastructure room for executive communications.

At the east end: **NETWORK ROOM EAST** — secondary network, containing the uplink infrastructure connecting the executive floor to the building's external communications.

The north side of the corridor: six alcoves with seating, a shared coffee station, and the large transplas panels that look out into the elevator shaft — giving a view down into the rock, up toward the surface. The panels are currently showing mostly nothing — the shaft lighting is dim between floors.

A single wall screen in the main corridor, mid-length, showing the Kaguya broadcast. Serena's voice in the corridor. The executives who remain on this floor are watching the broadcast from their own offices, their own screens, their own perspectives on what is happening.

## THE EXECUTIVE IMPLANT SYSTEM — HOW IT WORKS

Every executive with Floor 4 access carries a subdermal chip in their left forearm — standard implant position, minimal interference with daily movement. The chip broadcasts a passive signal readable at approximately two meters by any building reader.

The chip does not just identify the executive. It broadcasts their current clearance level, their last three accessed floors, their most recent building entry timestamp, and a real-time status flag (IN BUILDING / OUT OF BUILDING / UNKNOWN).

The floor readers log all proximity events — not just access attempts, but every time an authorized chip comes within two meters of a reader. The log is continuous. It is stored in Network Room East.

**FOR DANTE:** If he can access the Network Room East log, he can see a complete record of every executive who was on Floor 4 in the last 72 hours, including:
- When they arrived
- When they left
- Which offices they accessed
- Whether they are currently in the building

This is the most comprehensive record of who knew what was coming — because the executives who were briefed on Sigma-7 came to this floor, and the log shows when and for how long.
