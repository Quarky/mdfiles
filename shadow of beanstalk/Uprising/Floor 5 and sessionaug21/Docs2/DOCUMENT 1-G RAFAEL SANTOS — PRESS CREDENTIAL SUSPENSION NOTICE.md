**PRESS CREDENTIAL SUSPENSION NOTICE**

**DATE:** 25 September 1388 — 06:00 HLT

**TO:** RAFAEL SANTOS — INDEPENDENT WIRE SERVICE

**SUBJECT:** Suspension of Building Access

---

This notice is to inform you that your press credentials for the NBN Heinlein Broadcast Facility have been suspended pending a security review. This action is effective immediately.

All external journalist access to the facility has been suspended until further notice. This action has been taken in accordance with crisis protocol Sigma-7 and is not subject to appeal.

Please direct any questions to the NBN Lunar Division Operations Office.

**H. Mose**
Director of Operations, NBN Lunar Division

---

*NOTE: Four journalists were suspended. Three received notices at 06:00. One was already in the building when the suspension took effect.*
