**TO:** Director of Operations, NBN Lunar Division

**SUBJECT:** Notice of Professional Concerns — Confidential

---

I am writing to formally document concerns that I have been unable to reconcile with my understanding of the professional responsibilities attached to my position.

Over the course of my tenure as Managing Editor at this facility — now approaching twenty years — I have authorized a significant body of editorial decisions that, in retrospect, I am unable to characterize as consistent with the principles of responsible media production. I do not attribute this to explicit instruction in the majority of cases. I attribute it to the gradual development of a professional culture in which certain categories of coverage were understood to be inadvisable without being identified as prohibited.

The result is a body of work that I find, upon review, to reflect institutional priorities rather than the standards of accuracy and relevance that I understood, at the beginning of my career, to be the purpose of this work.

I am not prepared, at this time, to detail specific instances. I am aware that doing so would require me to characterize decisions that were made by others as well as myself, and I do not have access to the full context of those decisions.

What I can say is that I have understood for some time that what we produce at this facility is not, in the strictest sense, journalism. I have understood this and continued to perform the functions of this role. I am no longer certain I can continue to understand this silently. But I am uncertain what understanding it loudly would accomplish, or who would be served by the attempt.

This letter may be the most honest thing I produce at this facility. It may be the only thing I produce here that I do not subsequently file, index, and classify.

I am not sending it because the act of writing it has required honesty that the act of sending it would make consequential. I do not yet know whether I am willing to accept those consequences.

**M. Brennan**
Managing Editor, NBN Heinlein Broadcast Facility
