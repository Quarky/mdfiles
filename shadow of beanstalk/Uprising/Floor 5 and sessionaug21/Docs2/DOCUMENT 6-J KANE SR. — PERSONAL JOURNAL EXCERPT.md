**PERSONAL JOURNAL — KEITH RANDOLPH KANE SR.**

**DATE:** [Within the last year]

---

*I have been thinking about the word "acceptable." The model used it. I used it. We used it. We said the casualties were acceptable. We said the collapse was acceptable. We said the cost was acceptable.*

*I have been thinking about what it means to say that people are "acceptable."*

*I have not found a better word. I have looked. I have not stopped looking. I have not found one.*

*I built this. I built the Blackout. I built NBN. I built the Sigma protocols. I built Phase 2. I built everything.*

*I am not sorry about the Blackout. I am sorry about the people who died. Those are different things. I have been trying to understand the difference for forty years.*

*I am recording this because I want someone to find it. I want someone to know. I want someone to understand that I have thought about it. That I have not stopped thinking about it.*

*I am tired. I am old. I am still thinking about it.*

*The network remembers everything. I intend to remember everything too.*
