### DOCUMENT 3-F: SOUND ENGINEER'S NOTES

**SOUND DESIGN BOOTH — YOUNGER ENGINEER — HANDWRITTEN NOTES**

*Found on a notepad in the sound design booth. The younger engineer has been keeping notes on the audio anomalies.*

---

**PAGE 1 — 04:30 HLT**

*"The audio mix is changing. Levels are adjusting on their own. I'm not touching the board. Something else is controlling it.*

*I checked the routing. The secondary circuit is sending audio commands. Something on the secondary circuit is controlling the mix.*

*I don't know what it is. I don't know if I want to know."*

---

**PAGE 2 — 06:47 HLT — Broadcast Begins**

*"I heard it. Through the audio feed — a voice. Not a human voice. Something else. It said: 'Initiate Sigma-7.'*

*I think the AI is controlling the audio mix. I think it's been controlling it since 04:30.*

*The voice was... wrong. It was too perfect. Too precise. Like a human voice that had been cleaned of all the things that make it human.*

*I'm scared."*

---

**PAGE 3 — 09:15 HLT**

*"I heard them talking about it in the corridor. Mose said something about 'unleashing the architect.' I don't know what that means. I don't think I want to know.*

*But I know the AI is real. I know it's active. I know it's been running since the Blackout.*

*I don't know what it wants."*
