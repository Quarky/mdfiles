### DOCUMENT   GRANDMOTHER'S FILE NOTE

**PERSONAL NOTE — OLU'S WALLET**

*Found in wallet during a search. A folded piece of paper with handwriting older, more deliberate. The paper is worn at the edges, folded many times.*

---

*"I worked for Vertex for twenty-three years before the Blackout. I worked for NBN for sixteen years after. I kept my job because I understood what the new job was. The new job was not asking questions. The new job was filing things and not reading them. The new job was being useful without knowing what you were useful for.*

*I saw things I shouldn't have seen. I filed things I shouldn't have filed. I learned to stop asking questions.*

*I told my grandson to ask questions. I hope he does."*
