### DOCUMENT 6-C: KANE SR. — HANDWRITTEN NOTE

**LEVEL 6 — FOUNDER ARCHIVE — ON TOP OF RIGHT-SIDE DRAWER**

*Loose paper. Recent. Same handwriting as rear panel inscription and photograph back.*

---

"The door will be found when it needs to be found. I have arranged for that. I have made sure the evidence is here and that it is correct and that it will survive me and those I trust to maintain it.

I am not certain this is enough. I have never been certain that keeping this record serves any purpose that I can clearly articulate. It is possible that whoever finds it will do nothing with it, and that the finding will have no consequence, and that I will have arranged all of this for a kind of honesty that produces no result.

I find that I cannot stop arranging it anyway.

The network remembers everything.

I am tired of being the only one who knows what it remembers."
