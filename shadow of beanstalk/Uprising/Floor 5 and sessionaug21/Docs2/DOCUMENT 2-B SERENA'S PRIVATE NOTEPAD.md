### DOCUMENT 2-B: SERENA'S PRIVATE NOTEPAD

**SERENA NIGHTSHADE — PERSONAL NOTEPAD — LEVEL 2 PREPARATION SUITE**

*Small spiral-bound notepad found on Serena's dressing table. The kind people use when they don't want things in any system. The last three pages, written during today's broadcast.*

---

**PAGE 1 — Written 06:30 HLT — Before Broadcast**

The runsheet says "anti-colonial extremists." I've covered enough of them to know what one looks like. These look like people who are very tired. I watched the raw footage this morning. The attackers weren't shouting slogans. They weren't demanding anything. They were fighting like people who had nothing left to lose.

The first Starport attack was at 05:12. I saw the footage. That wasn't a terrorist attack. That was a military assault. The attackers were wearing uniforms I've never seen before.

I keep thinking about what that means.

---

**PAGE 2 — Written 07:15 HLT — First Hour of Broadcast**

Version three covered Prosperity. Version seven covers Kaguya. They feel identical to perform. That is the most honest thing I've written in years. I am writing it here because it cannot be in any system.

The clone casualty note. Do not mention. This is the one that will follow me.

I asked the director why we weren't showing the bodies. He said it was "taste and discretion." I asked him whose taste and whose discretion. He didn't answer.

---

**PAGE 3 — Written 09:30 HLT — Minutes Before Dante Arrived**

Something is wrong with the broadcast. The segment timing is off. The editor is making decisions that don't match the runsheet. I can feel it in the rhythm of my cues. Someone is improvising up there.

I don't know if that's good or bad.
