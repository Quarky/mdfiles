**PROJECT OVERVIEW:**

StarReach is a proposed deep-space communications satellite network designed to synchronize broadcast signals across the solar system using planetary orbital data. The network will provide unprecedented coordination between Earth, Luna, and Mars.

**TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:**

- 12 satellites (initial deployment)
- Deep-space relay capacity
- Synchronized broadcast timing
- Latency management protocols

**ADDITIONAL USE CASES:**

- Psychographic data collection
- Predictive modeling integration
- Signal suppression capabilities
- Network infrastructure preparation
