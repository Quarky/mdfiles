### DOCUMENT 2-K: YEMI ADEYEMI — PERSONAL NOTE

**YEMI ADEYEMI — PERSONAL NOTEBOOK — LEVEL 2 COSMETICS SUITE**

*Found in Yemi Adeyemi's personal bag. A note written today.*

---

*"I've been putting her face on for nine years. I know the difference between the face she wears when she's performing a story she believes and the face she wears when she's performing a story she doesn't. Today is the second face.*

*I watched her this morning before the broadcast. She was looking at the runsheet like she was trying to understand something she'd been reading for eleven years.*

*I've been here for nine years. I know what this building does. I've known for most of them. I've kept my job by not saying anything.*

*Today I don't know if I can keep my job.*

*Olu is downstairs. His brother is at Kaguya. I've heard nothing from either of them.*

*Three generations of Adeyemis building something for someone else. Someone should count how many families that is."*
