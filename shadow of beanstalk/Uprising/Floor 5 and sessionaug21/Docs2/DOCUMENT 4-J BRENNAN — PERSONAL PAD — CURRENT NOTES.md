I've been standing in this corridor for eleven minutes. I don't know why. I don't know what I'm waiting for.

Mose said something last night that I can't stop thinking about. He said "We woke it up." He said "It's been sleeping since the Blackout."

I've been thinking about what that means.

I've been thinking about Sigma. I've been thinking about the template. I've been thinking about how it knows what to say before anyone says it. I've been thinking about how it's always right — how the predictions are always accurate.

I've been thinking about the Blackout. I was there. I was in New Angeles. I saw what happened. I saw the chaos. I saw the people die.

I've been thinking about what it means that someone woke something up.

I've been thinking about what it means that I've been doing this job for twenty years and I've never asked the right questions.
