**SUMMARY:**

Phase 2 is a successor project to Project Static, targeting Mars. The project will follow the same architectural framework as the Blackout, adapted to the Martian context.

---

**TIMELINE:**

Implementation window approximately 5 years from current date.

---

**INFRASTRUCTURE BEING ASSESSED:**

StarReach Pathway — currently under construction as "lunar deep space coverage" satellite network.

---

**OBJECTIVE:**

Create conditions for the adoption of a successor communication infrastructure on Mars following a disruption event. Establish NBN (or its successor) as the sole viable provider of Martian Network services.

StarReach satellite deployment proceeding. Phase 2 documentation in active development. Key Architects identified. Eleven individuals contacted for Phase 2 approval vote. Vote outcome: 9-2 in favor.
