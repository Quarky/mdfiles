**INCIDENT TYPE:** Clone Casualty — Mass Fatality Event

**SUMMARY:**

During routine helium-3 extraction operations at Outpost 14, a structural failure occurred in the primary mining shaft. Seventeen (17) Henry-line clone workers were killed. The cause of the failure was later determined to be cost-cutting measures implemented by Melange Mining management, which led to the use of substandard structural materials.

The incident was initially reported as an "industrial accident." However, internal NBN footage shows that the structural failure was foreseeable and that management had been warned of the risk three weeks prior to the incident.

The footage was suppressed under the Matheson-Taguchi Amendment. The incident was classified as "property damage" — not "casualties." No human casualties were reported.
