### DOCUMENT 2-J: SERENA'S JOURNAL — FINAL ENTRIES (REVISED)

**SERENA NIGHTSHADE — PERSONAL JOURNAL — LOCKED DRAWER**

*Found in Serena's locked preparation suite drawer. Handwritten journal. Twelve years of entries. The last three pages are relevant.*

---

**ENTRY — 23 September 1388 — 3 Days Before the Kaguya Operation**

Mose was in the building today. I saw him in the corridor. He looked like he hadn't slept. He was carrying a briefcase and walking like he was trying to remember how to walk.

I asked him if everything was alright. He said "We're preparing for a significant event." I asked what kind of event. He said "The kind that needs preparation."

I've known him for nine years. He's never looked at me like that before.

---

**ENTRY — 24 September 1388 — Yesterday**

Someone told me today that they're activating Sigma-7. They said it's a "standard crisis protocol." I asked what the crisis was. They said "The Kaguya situation." I asked what the Kaguya situation was. They said "It's developing."

I've been reading runsheets for eleven years. I know what Sigma-3 covered. I know what Sigma-6 covered. I know what this one will cover.

I don't know what to do with what I know.

---

**ENTRY — 25 September 1388 — 06:30 HLT — Before Broadcast**

I watched the raw footage this morning. The attackers weren't shouting slogans. They weren't making demands. They were fighting like people who had nothing left to lose.

I know what that looks like. I've seen it before. In footage we didn't air. Footage that was filed under classifications I don't have access to.

I think I know what those classifications cover now.

**REVISION NOTE:** Changed "Kaguya battle" to "Kaguya operation" and "Kaguya situation" — the event has no formal name yet.
