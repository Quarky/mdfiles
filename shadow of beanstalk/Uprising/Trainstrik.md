SESSION 3 — THE BLUE LINE STRIKE (HOPPERBIKE ASSAULT)

The Approach

The team is airborne. Hopperbikes cut through the thin lunar atmosphere—if you can call it that. There's no sound. There's just the vibration of the bike's frame, the distant glow of Heinlein's domes, and the train below. The Blue Line stretches across the lunar surface—a dark ribbon against the grey regolith. The train is a long, dark shape moving along it.

WHAT THE CHARACTERS EXPERIENCE:

· The Ride: Hopperbikes are fast, agile, and unforgiving. The wind—what little there is—presses against their envirosuits. The Earth hangs in the sky like a distant blue marble. The train is below them, moving at a steady speed.
· The Train: A cargo train—long, dark, and heavily armored. He-3 canisters are visible through cargo windows. The train's AI is driving it. There's no crew—just cargo and systems.
· The Target: The train's control car is at the front. If the team can reach it, they can stop the train. If they can't, the strike fails.
· The Track: The Blue Line stretches ahead of them. The track is surrounded by open regolith—no cover, no concealment. The team is exposed.

AMBIENT SOUND:

· The low hum of hopperbike engines.
· The wind rushing past their envirosuits.
· The distant, silent train.
· Comms crackling in their helmets.

NPC Reaction (Yusuf, over comms, as they approach):

(Static crackles. His voice is low, measured.)

"Train's below you. Moving at speed. Control car is at the front. If you can get to it, you can stop the train."

(A pause.)

"The train's AI is running the show. If you can get inside the control car, you can override it. But you'll need to breach the car first. It's sealed—standard corporate-grade locks."

(Another pause. He's not going to tell them what to do. He's going to let them decide.)

"There's no cover on the track. You're exposed. The corps will see you coming. You need to move fast. Get on the train, get inside, stop it. And then get out."

"I'll be monitoring. I won't be able to help you. This is your mission now. Good luck."

---

The Hopperbike Approach

The team descends toward the train. Hopperbikes are fast—they can keep pace with the train easily. But the train's sensors will detect them eventually. The team has a small window before the corps know they're coming.

WHAT THE CHARACTERS SEE:

· The Train Below: A long, dark shape moving along the track. The control car is at the front—a slightly larger car with antennas and a reinforced roof. The cargo cars behind it are sealed.
· The Track: The maglev track stretches ahead of them—a dark line across the lunar surface. The train's progress is steady, methodical.
· The Horizon: Heinlein's domes glow faintly in the distance. The Earth hangs above them. The sky is black.
· The Train's Roof: The control car has a roof access hatch. If the team can land on it, they can breach the car.

THE CHALLENGE:

· Piloting Check (Hard, ●●●●) : Land hopperbikes on the moving train without crashing.
· Failure Consequence: A hopperbike crashes. The team loses a member or is separated.

NPC Reaction (Team's Pilot, during the approach):

(Focused, steady, not looking away from the train.)

"I can get us on the train. But it's going to be tight. The train's moving fast, and the roof is narrow."

(He pauses, checking his instruments.)

"I'm going to bring us in low and fast. We'll land on the control car's roof. Once we're down, we'll need to move fast—get inside, stop the train, and get out."

(He looks at the rest of the team. His expression is focused.)

"Hold on. This is going to be rough."

---

The Landing

The team lands on the train's roof. The hopperbikes touch down—one by one—on the control car. The roof is narrow, slippery, and exposed. The train is moving fast. The team has seconds to breach the car before they're blown off.

WHAT THE CHARACTERS SEE:

· The Roof: The control car's roof is narrow and slightly curved. Antennas and access hatches are spaced along its length.
· The Access Hatch: A sealed hatch with a manual override. It's locked—standard corporate-grade lock. The team needs to breach it.
· The Train Below: The track is rushing past beneath them. The train is moving fast. If they fall, they're dead.
· The Horizon: Heinlein's domes are getting closer. The corps are watching.

THE CHALLENGE:

· Skulduggery Check (Average, ●●●) : Breach the access hatch without triggering an alarm.
· Failure Consequence: The alarm triggers. The train's systems lock down. The team has 5 minutes before a Globalsec response arrives.

NPC Reaction (Team's Breacher, on the roof):

(Kneeling by the hatch, working on the lock.)

"The lock is standard corporate-grade. I can get it open, but it's going to take a moment. And there's an alarm—if I don't bypass it, the train's AI will know we're here."

(He pauses, examining the lock.)

"If the alarm triggers, the train's systems lock down. We won't be able to stop it. So I need to do this right."

(He looks at the rest of the team. His expression is focused.)

"Cover me. I need a minute."

---

The Breach

The team breaches the access hatch. They drop into the control car. The car is dark—the only light comes from the consoles. The train's AI is running the show. The team has seconds to override it.

WHAT THE CHARACTERS SEE:

· The Control Car: A cramped, dark space. The walls are lined with consoles, displays, and cables. The air is recycled and faintly metallic.
· The Navigation Console: A screen showing the Blue Line's track map. A train icon is moving along the line. The console has a keyboard, a trackball, and a series of buttons labeled "ROUTE," "SPEED," "STOP," and "EMERGENCY."
· The AI Interface: A small panel labeled "AI CONTROL — AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY." A datacable port is visible.
· The Emergency Override: A large red switch with a glass cover. The label reads: "EMERGENCY STOP — MANUAL OVERRIDE. AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY."

THE CHALLENGE:

· Computers (Hacking) Check (Hard, ●●●●) : Override the train's AI and stop the train.
· Failure Consequence: The AI detects the intrusion and alerts Melange. The train's systems lock down. The strike fails.

NPC Reaction (Team's Runner, in the control car):

(Kneeling by the AI interface, working on the data cable.)

"I'm in. The AI is running the show. I can see the train's routing data. It's moving at standard speed—it'll reach Heinlein in about 2 hours."

(He pauses, examining the interface.)

"There's an emergency stop function in the AI. I can trigger it. But the AI has been modified—there's some kind of override lock. I'll need to break through the ice to get to it."

(He looks at the rest of the team. His expression is focused.)

"This is corporate ice. Not the best, but not the worst. I'll need a minute. Can you guys cover me?"

---

The Train Stops

The runner overrides the AI. The train decelerates. It comes to a stop on the track. The strike has succeeded.

WHAT THE CHARACTERS SEE:

· The Control Car: The consoles are dark. The train is still. The emergency lights are on.
· The Track: The train is motionless on the track. The Blue Line is dead.
· The Horizon: Heinlein's domes are glowing in the distance. The corps are watching.

THE CONSEQUENCES:

· The train is stopped. The strike has succeeded.
· The team is on the train. They need to escape before the corps arrive.
· The rebellion has sent a message. The corps know someone was in their systems.

NPC Reaction (Team's Runner, after the train stops):

(Leaning back from the console. He lets out a slow breath.)

"It's done. Train's stopped. Cargo's stuck. He-3 isn't going anywhere."

(He turns to look at the rest of the team. His expression is tired, but satisfied.)

"We did it. The Blue Line is down."

(A pause.)

"Now we need to get out of here before the corps show up. They're going to be sending a recovery team. We need to be gone before they arrive."

---

The Escape

The team leaves the control car. They mount their hopperbikes and take off. The train is behind them, motionless on the track. The corps are coming.

WHAT THE CHARACTERS SEE:

· The Train Below: A dark shape on the track, motionless. The He-3 canisters are visible through the cargo windows.
· The Track: The Blue Line is dead. No other trains are moving.
· The Horizon: Heinlein's domes are glowing in the distance. The corps are watching.

AMBIENT SOUND:

· The low hum of hopperbike engines.
· The wind rushing past their envirosuits.
· The distant, silent train.
· Comms crackling in their helmets.

NPC Reaction (Yusuf, over comms, during the escape):

(Static crackles. His voice is low, measured.)

"Train's stopped. I'm getting reports from the network. Melange is scrambling—they're sending a recovery team. They don't know what happened yet. They're calling it a 'routing error.'"

(A pause. He's processing.)

"You did it. The Blue Line is down. No casualties. That's a win."

(He pauses again.)

"But the response is coming. The corps are already moving. The patrol is going to find the intrusion. They're going to tighten security. The next strike won't be this easy."

"Get back to the Docklands. We'll debrief. And then we'll figure out what's next."
