# Kaguya Public Terminal Lockdown — Session Pack

## Session premise

The party exits a maintenance corridor into the shared public-terminal loop. SXC has occupied the terminal in force. The public terminal does not look directly into private boarding areas: each private terminal is reached through a controlled private corridor ending at a security checkpoint into the starport.

The party meets members of the existing miner team. During the confrontation, an SXC trooper deliberately fires into a vulnerable pressure boundary near the maintenance-side approach. The resulting hole is small, producing a controlled decompression that takes roughly **15–30 minutes** to become critical. Emergency bulkheads begin sealing corridors and compartments.

The military uses the emergency to tighten control, isolate witnesses, and restrict access to the enormous inner landing field.

## Core session beats

1. **Maintenance exit:** meet miners; read the military deployment; notice that the cordon looks pre-staged.
2. **Deliberate breach:** the SXC Breach Trooper fires; alarms and pressure loss begin; bulkheads sequence.
3. **Lockdown:** help civilians/miners, chase the shooter, gather evidence, bluff checkpoints, or fight.
4. **Service bypass:** use industrial and maintenance routes when public corridors become too controlled.
5. **Inner landing field:** reach the roughly 3 km-scale internal landing complex. Regolith remains visible between paved lanes and pads. The field contains scores upon scores of pads and terminal spurs. Ships pass through large pressure gates.
6. **Escape:** seize or access a Passenger Dropship, reach a private terminal, use service traffic, evacuate allies, or accept capture as a playable next-session state.

## Decompression clock

The breach is not an instant catastrophic vent. Use a 15–30 minute narrative clock. Early effects are alarms, moving air, loose debris, noise, and automatic bulkhead sequencing. Later effects increase pressure stress, cold, movement hazards, and the danger of crossing unsealed sections.

## Military presence

Use existing SXC actors as needed: Rapid Insertion Troopers, Squad Leaders, Snipers, Heavy Weapons Troopers, Medics, EWS Drone Operators, and Exosuit Pilots. New session actors are **Commander Adrien Vale**, **Sergeant Kira Sol**, and **SXC Breach Trooper**.

## New NPC roles

### Commander Adrien Vale
Senior occupation commander. Calm, coercive, and focused on controlling the story as much as the terminal. Uses the established Lieutenant Vasora mechanical chassis.

### Sergeant Kira Sol
Ground-level checkpoint commander. Practical and observant. Strong evidence can create doubt without making her automatically defect. Uses the established SXC Squad Leader mechanical chassis.

### SXC Breach Trooper
Plot-specific instance of the SXC Rapid Insertion Trooper. Deliberately causes the small pressure breach and then attempts to disappear back into the formation.

## Evidence

- camera footage of the shot
- weapon/ammunition telemetry
- bulkhead sequencing ordered before the breach
- troop deployments staged before the alarm
- miner and civilian witnesses

## Landing-field geography

The landing area is an immense pressurized interior field rather than a small hangar. It contains regolith, paved service lanes, pad lighting, cargo equipment, terminal spurs, and large pressure gates. The overview map represents the broad geography while the tactical sector map covers a smaller engagement zone. **Ships are separate placeable assets and are not baked into the maps.**
