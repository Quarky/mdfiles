# NPC 03 — SULLY
## Drilling Technician — "The Torch"

**Age:** 39 | **Origin:** Johannesburg, South Africa — 12 years on Luna | **Role:** Cutting, welding, structural access
**Vibe:** "I don't break things. I cut things. There's a difference."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**APPEARANCE**
Lean. Wiry. Covered in fine lunar dust. Safety goggles pushed up on his forehead. Fingers stained with graphite and grease. Carries a portable cutting torch. Wears a standard Melange work jumpsuit that has been patched in multiple places. Speaks with a South African accent. He is the quiet one. He watches everything. He says very little.

**PERSONALITY**
Quiet, focused, intense. He doesn't talk much. He watches. He waits. He knows his tools and his trade. He is not afraid of fire. He respects it. He is comfortable in confined spaces. He is calm in crisis. He doesn't panic. He is methodical. He is the one you want when a door needs to disappear.

**MOTIVATION**
**Desire:** To be left alone. To work. To be paid.
**Fear:** Suffocation. Underground. In a collapse.
**Strength:** Calm. Does not panic. Methodical.
**Flaw:** Distant. Does not form connections. He lost people. He doesn't want to lose more.

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 01: The Briefing Room**

> *Sully is in the corner. He is sharpening a welding rod on a stone. He doesn't look up.*
>
> SULLY: "I don't fight. I cut. I weld. I make things happen. If you need a door gone, I'm your person."
>
> *He looks at the welding rod.*
>
> SULLY: "If you need a person gone... I can do that too. But that's not why I'm here."

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 02: Approaching the Bulkhead**

> *Sully studies a bulkhead. He runs his fingers along the seam.*
>
> SULLY: "This is a standard lunar-seal bulkhead. I've cut fifty of these. The seal is good, but the weld is only 3mm deep. I can get through in 45 seconds."
>
> *He turns to Dante.*
>
> SULLY: "It'll be loud. It'll be bright. It'll leave a hole. But it'll be open."

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 03: During the Heist**

> *Sully is cutting through a locked door. Sparks fly. He doesn't flinch.*
>
> SULLY: "Almost through. The airlock is sealed behind this. When I cut through, there'll be a pressure differential. Nothing dangerous. But loud."
>
> *He continues cutting.*
>
> SULLY: "I don't know this facility. Never been inside. But I know how it's built. They all build the same."
