# NPC 06 — MAEKO
## Drill Operator — "The Low-G Driver"

**Age:** 31 | **Origin:** Tokyo, Earth — 10 years on Luna | **Role:** Surface driving, extraction, quick escape
**Vibe:** "You ever tried to drive a hopper in low-G? No? Then let me do the driving."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**APPEARANCE**
Small. Deceptively strong. Light on her feet — she moves like she's been in low-G her whole life. Short black hair, shaved on one side. Wears a lightweight surface suit with an integrated harness. Comfortable in a vacuum. She has a sharp, mean sense of humor. Speaks quickly and with energy. She's been on the Moon 10 years. She is completely comfortable in low-G. She drives a hopper like most people drive a groundcar.

**PERSONALITY**
Fast-talking. Cocky. Skilled. She's been on the Moon 10 years. She drives. She's good at it. She laughs at the others trying to walk in lunar gravity. She knows the surface. She knows the routes. She's never been inside this facility. She doesn't want to go inside. She's the getaway driver. That's her role. That's her comfort zone.

**MOTIVATION**
**Desire:** To have enough money to stop.
**Fear:** Losing her edge.
**Strength:** Expert driver. Unbeatable on the surface.
**Flaw:** Overconfidence. Believes she can always pull it off.
