## Hachi-Inu Security Unit (Modified)

### Original Hachi-Inu Lore

Jinteki designed the Hachi-Inu line of clones for police and private security use. About the size of a German Shepherd, these four-legged clones can detect illegal substances, track and pursue criminals, and secrete venom from their teeth that temporarily paralyzes victims without causing lasting damage. They are more intelligent than their canine predecessors, with hand-like paws that can open doors, use simple tools, and interface with computer terminals. They communicate using tail movements, which trained handlers can read.

### Modified Hachi-Inu (Metal Teeth & Armor-Piercing)

This modified version represents a dangerous upgrade—either a corporate prototype, a black-market modification, or a rogue unit that has been "improved" beyond its original design parameters.

**Origin:** Somewhere in the Heinlein Docklands, a disgruntled Jinteki technician or a black-market cyberneticist has been upgrading Hachi-Inu units. The modifications replace the standard venom-secreting teeth with reinforced metal alloy fangs capable of piercing light armor. Combined with enhanced jaw strength, these units are lethal rather than merely incapacitating.

**Appearance:** The Hachi-Inu appears largely identical to a standard unit—German Shepherd-sized, four-legged, with hand-like paws and insulating fur. However, a close inspection reveals a faint metallic glint from its teeth when it opens its mouth. The modifications are visible on a successful **Perception** check (Average difficulty). The unit may also have small cybernetic implants visible at the jaw hinge.
