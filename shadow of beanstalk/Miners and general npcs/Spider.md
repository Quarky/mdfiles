## Spider Drone (with Mounted SMG)


**Description:** A four-legged, insectoid drone roughly the size of a large dog. Its chassis is angular and armored, painted in matte blac. It skitters across walls, ceilings, and floors with disturbing speed and silence. A short-barreled SMG is mounted on its back, capable of swiveling to track targets independently. The drone's legs end in sharp points, allowing it to climb and even strike in melee. Often deployed in urban environments or mining facilities for patrol and anti-personnel duties.

**Skills:** Athletics (Br) 2, Brawl (Br) 2, Perception (Cun) 2, Ranged (Light) (Ag) 2, Stealth (Ag) 3, Vigilance (Will) 2

**Talents:**
- **Spider Climb:** The drone can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an Athletics check.
- **Unusual Nature:** The drone doesn't require air, food, or sleep.
- **Silent Hunter:** Add a Boost die to Stealth checks when moving.
- **Coordinated Attack:** If two or more Spider Drones are engaged with the same target, they each add a Boost die to their Brawl attacks.
- 
**Weapons:**
- **Mounted SMG (Ranged Light):** Damage 5, Critical 4, Range Medium, Auto-Fire
- **Leg Strike (Brawl):** Damage 3, Critical 4, Range Engaged, Knockdown
- 
**Special Abilities:**
- **Vulnerable to EMP:** If hit by an attack that deals Ion or EMP damage, the drone suffers 2 additional Strain (ignoring Soak).

- **Limited Ammo:** The drone carries enough ammunition for 3 full Auto-Fire attacks before it must return to base or be reloaded (takes 1 Round).

- **Grapple:** As an action, the drone may make an opposed **Brawl** check to immobilize a target. If successful, the target is immobilized until they take an action to break free (opposed Brawl check).
- 
**Handling & Risks:**
- Controlled remotely by a security operator or running on combat AI.
- If the controller is incapacitated, the drone continues its last command.
- Can be hacked (Computers check, **Daunting** difficulty) to either shut down or turn on its allies for 1 Round.

\
**1. Firewall Protocol (Defensive Counter-Hacking)**

- **Effect:** The spider drone's IFF (Identify Friend/Foe) system is hardened. Any character attempting to hack or disable the drone (a `Computers` or `Skulduggery` check) suffers 2 Setback dice. On a successful hack, the drone immediately transmits a warning signal to all allied units.
    
The program adds **two Setback dice** to the hacker's roll and initiates an **Alarm** state.
    

**2. Hunter-Killer Mode (Aggressive Targeting)**

- **Effect:** The drone sacrifices stealth for raw aggression. It can spend its turn to take an extra maneuver, allowing it to cover more ground or focus fire with incredible intensity. Once per round, it may add a Boost die to its attack after taking a specific maneuver to lock on.

    

**3. Self-Destruct (Last Resort)**

- **Effect:** When the drone's Wound Threshold is exceeded, it detonates in a final act of aggression, unleashing a blast of shrapnel and EMP. All characters within Short range suffer a critical hit or a significant amount of damage. 
    
  Upon destruction, the drone deals a high amount of damage that is mitigated by an agility check (e.g., `Damage 10, Critical 2`) to all targets within **Short range**. Possibility for critical of 50%.
    

**4. Scrambler (Comms Jamming)**

- **Effect:** The drone emits a localized field that disrupts all non-wired communication within Medium range. Characters allied and other cannot use their PADs to contact allies, and any remote signals (like calling for backup) are automatically downgraded in difficulty or impossible.
    
  All **Communications (Charm, Leadership, etc.)** checks attempted within Medium range of the drone suffer 2 Setback dice from interference if using comms. Any attempt to contact someone outside the area via Network is an automatic failure or requires a `Computers` check to bypass depending on the hardware.

**5. Swarm Coordinator (Tactical Networking)**

Effect:** The drone acts as a mobile command node for other drones in its vicinity. It processes targeting data from allied drones and relays optimized firing solutions, increasing the effectiveness of all friendly drone units within range.

 hen this drone is active within **Medium range** of other allied drones (Hover Drones, Spider Drones, Turrets), those allied drones gain **+1 Boost die** on all Ranged attacks. Additionally, allied drones within range gain **+1 Defense** (ranged and melee) as the coordinator predicts incoming fire and relays evasion data. If the coordinating drone is destroyed, this bonus is lost.
