# NPC 05 — JUNIOR SANTOS
## Conveyor Tech — (He's Here Because He Can Read Schematics)

**Age:** 26 | **Origin:** Rio de Janeiro, Earth — 4 years on Luna | **Role:** Navigation, schematic reading, nervous support
**Vibe:** "I'm not scared. I'm just... cautious. That's the word. Cautious."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**APPEARANCE**
Lean. Nervous energy. Oily hair. Refinery jumpsuit that's too large for him. Hands are clean — he's not a physical laborer. He works the systems. Fingers twitch when he's nervous, which is always. Scar on his left forearm from a conveyor belt. He doesn't talk about it. Chews on his lip when he's thinking. He is the youngest on the team. He knows it.

**PERSONALITY**
Anxious. Talkative. Desperate to prove himself. Youngest on the team. Talks too much when he's nervous. Asks too many questions. Wants to be useful. Terrified of being the one who makes a mistake. He looks up to anyone who seems competent. He hasn't been on the Moon long enough to be cynical. He's here because he can read schematics. That's his only skill.

**MOTIVATION**
**Desire:** To be respected.
**Fear:** Failure. Terrified of being the weak link.
**Strength:** Quick learner. Reads schematics better than anyone.
**Flaw:** Overshares. Talks about things he shouldn't.

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 01: The Briefing Room**

> *Junior sits on a crate, bouncing his knee. He's been quiet. Too quiet. Now he blurts out:*
>
> JUNIOR: "I've never been inside this facility. Never even seen it up close. But I've studied the schematics. Every night. For two weeks. I know where the corridors are supposed to be. I know where the panels are supposed to be. I don't know if they're actually there. But I know where they *should* be."
>
> *He looks at Dante.*
>
> JUNIOR: "That's something, right? That's useful?"

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 02: Approaching the Facility**

> *Junior is studying his PAD.*
>
> JUNIOR: "Okay, okay. The schematics say the conveyor control terminal is on the refinery level. Two floors down. We need to take the maintenance stairwell. Not the main lift."
>
> *He points.*
>
> JUNIOR: "The main lift has cameras. The stairwell... the schematics say it's 'unmonitored.' But they're old schematics. Yusuf said they're five years old. Things might have changed."
>
> *He looks at Dante nervously.*
>
> JUNIOR: "I don't know if they've changed. I've never been here."

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 03: During the Heist**

> *Junior is at a junction. He's comparing the schematics to the actual corridor. His face falls.*
>
> JUNIOR: "The schematics say there's a junction here. There's not. There's a wall. They—they changed it. They didn't update the plans."
>
> *He looks at Dante, panicked.*
>
> JUNIOR: "What do we do? I don't have a route for this. I didn't plan for this. I—"
>
> *He takes a breath.*
>
> JUNIOR: "Okay. Okay. I can figure this out. Just give me a minute. I can figure this out."
