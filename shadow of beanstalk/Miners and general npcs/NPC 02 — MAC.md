# NPC 02 — MAC
## Heavy Equipment Operator — "The Loader"

**Age:** 41 | **Origin:** Aberdeen, Scotland — 16 years on Luna | **Role:** Physical labor, heavy lifting, breaking things
**Vibe:** "I move heavy things. That's what I do. That's all I do."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**APPEARANCE**
Huge. Hands like shovels. Shoulders that don't fit through standard doorways. Bald. Scarred knuckles. Wears a heavy work jacket with no insignia. Walks with a slight stoop — too many years in low-G moving heavy loads. Speaks with a thick Scottish accent. He is the strongest person in the room. He knows this. He doesn't need to prove it.

**PERSONALITY**
Quiet. Slow. Deliberate. He is not stupid. He is careful. He's been on the Moon 16 years. He's moved thousands of tons of regolith. He knows how to read a load's center of gravity. He knows how to break things that are in his way. He speaks in short sentences. He doesn't waste words. He is loyal to his team. He has no patience for politics. He does what needs to be done.

**MOTIVATION**
**Desire:** To be left alone. To work and go home.
**Fear:** Losing his hands. They are his tools. Without them, he is nothing.
**Strength:** Immense physical strength. Can move anything.
**Flaw:** Slow. Not dumb. Slow. Needs time to process new information.

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 01: The Briefing Room**

> *Mac stands against the wall. He hasn't said anything. He looks at Dante.*
>
> MAC: "I move heavy things. That's what I do. You tell me where the canisters are. I'll move them."
>
> *He pauses.*
>
> MAC: "I don't know what's inside this place. I don't need to know. Just point me at the cargo."

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 02: Approaching the Cargo Bay**

> *Mac stops at a heavy blast door. He looks at it. He looks at Dante.*
>
> MAC: "This door. I can open it. The manual release is on the other side. But I can get through."
>
> *He hefts the rebar club.*
>
> MAC: "Might be loud. Might be fast. Can't be both."

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 03: During the Heist**

> *Mac is lifting a canister. He grunts. He sets it down.*
>
> MAC: "Six of these. That's the load. Six. I can move six."
>
> *He looks at Dante.*
>
> MAC: "If we need more, we need more time. I can only move so fast."
>
> *He hefts another canister.*
>
> MAC: "I'm not a soldier. I'm a loader. But I'll do what needs to be done."
