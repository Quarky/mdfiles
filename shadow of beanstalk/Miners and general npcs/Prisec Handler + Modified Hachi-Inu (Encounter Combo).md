## Prisec Handler + Modified Hachi-Inu (Encounter Combo)

| Unit | Count | Role |
|------|-------|------|
| **Prisec Sergeant** | 1 | Commander, coordinates attack |
| **Prisec Operative** | 2-4 | Fire support, flanking |
| **Modified Hachi-Inu** | 1-2 | Tracker, close assault, armor-piercing |

**Tactics:**
- Hachi-Inu uses **Keen Scent** to track and locate targets.
- Prisec operatives provide covering fire while the Hachi-Inu closes to melee.
- The Hachi-Inu's **Pierce 2** bite can shred light armor and incapacitate targets.
- If the handler is killed, the Hachi-Inu may go berserk (GM discretion).

**Encounter Difficulty:** Daunting (4 Purple) - coordinated human-droid team, armor-piercing threat
