## Prisec (Private Security)

### What They Are

Prisec is a slang term for private security as commonly employed by corporate facilities and arcologies. These aren't just rent-a-cops; they are professional, well-equipped forces hired by corporations to protect their interests. They operate in a gray area, often clashing with the NAPD over jurisdiction and evidence handling. On Luna and Mars, Prisec forces often outnumber official law enforcement. A gunfight with prisec mercenaries is a common threat in the Android universe.

### Common Equipment
- Light body armor, sidearms, and less-lethal weaponry (stunsticks, Synap pistols)
- Hoppers for rapid deployment and patrol
- Camdrones and advanced surveillance gear
- Uniforms marked with their employer's corporate logo
