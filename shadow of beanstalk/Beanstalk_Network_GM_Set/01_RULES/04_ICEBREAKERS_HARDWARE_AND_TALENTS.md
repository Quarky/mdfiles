## Published active-program limit

The published rule says activating one icebreaker automatically deactivates the runner’s other icebreakers: one active at a time. Hardware limits how many can be hosted, not how many may simultaneously contribute.

The current module can recognize multiple active programs and always applies only one compatible icebreaker’s strength to a Break ICE check. If the campaign permits multiple active programs, document that as a campaign option; do not add their strengths together.

## Computer hardware

| Hardware | Active capacity | Connections | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAD | 1 icebreaker and 1 ICE | Usually wireless | Small, ubiquitous, portable. Athena and Crypsis are too large. |
| Portable rig | 2 icebreakers and 2 ICE | Wired and wireless | Backpack/satchel sized; suitable for mobile runs. |
| Big rig | 4 icebreakers and 6 ICE | Wired and wireless | Fixed installation with serious processing capacity. |
| BMI | Not a computer | Connects to another computer | Go Deep as a maneuver; while deep, only Network actions/maneuvers, but a second Network maneuver does not cost strain. |
| Burst transmitter | Access aid | Dedicated narrow-band link | Reduce Access System difficulty once, minimum Simple. |
| Reader | Not a computer | Local device link | Can provide an access point into its associated system. |

Computers can store ordinary files and programs freely unless the GM defines an exceptional program; the listed limit concerns active ICE and icebreakers.

## Relevant talents and abilities

### Burn Through

After successfully overriding one ICE, suffer 3 strain to attempt a second ICE on the same system as an incidental. This is unusually valuable because layered ICE normally consumes separate actions across turns.

### Distinctive Style

Before a Hacking check, the runner may take the listed positive and negative dice. The benefit is power; the risk is a recognizable signature that accelerates identification and tracing.

### Quiet Code

Threat generated on Hacking cannot be spent merely to alert other users. Existing sentry effects and Despair still operate normally.

### Direct Access

When physically in the same building as the target server, the character automatically succeeds at Access System. This does not bypass ICE or grant commands.

### Reinforce

As a maneuver, increase one active ICE’s strength by 1 through the end of the sysop’s next turn.

### Net Warrior

While using a BMI, make an opposed Hacking versus Sysops check against a user in the system; inflict strain per Success and wounds as well when the target is immersed as specified by the ability.

### Sniffer and Swordbreaker programs

Elite defenders may add Setback to hostile checks on a system they control and use a Sysops check to damage an active icebreaker. These are adversary abilities, not default sysop actions.
