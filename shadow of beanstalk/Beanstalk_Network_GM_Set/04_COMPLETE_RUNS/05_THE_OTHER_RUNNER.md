# Network Run — The Other Runner

**Network:** Use any dossier; Public Terminal is the default  
**Length:** 45–60 minutes  
**Objective:** Complete a job while a second runner pursues a conflicting goal  
**NPC:** Imani “Lantern” Okafor or Rook Mercier  
**Purpose:** Demonstrate multiple-runner rules without turning cyberspace into separate combat.

## Setup

The PCs enter a system to obtain one protected record. Another runner is already inside.

- **Lantern version:** She wants to publish the full archive and prevent deletion.
- **Rook version:** He is paid to delete the same record and plant a false replacement.

The NPC is not automatically hostile. Both sides share the same suppressed-ICE openings and the same group trace state once the defenders correlate them.

## Opening reveal

After Access System, show one node already selected and a single line of text in the command log:

`YOU ARE LATE. DO NOT TOUCH THE ARCHIVE UNTIL WE TALK.`

The player can reply as an incidental. Negotiation, Charm, Deception, or Coercion can establish a temporary protocol; Computers (Hacking) can instead identify the other runner’s route.

## Shared access, separate intent

Use one initiative order. Only one successful Access System is needed for allied runners. If the NPC is opposed, they already have access but do not share commands or hidden information voluntarily.

When either runner suppresses ICE, everyone may exploit the opening. This makes turn order and trust matter:

- one runner breaks;
- another enacts the command;
- a third covers tracks or tackles a physical complication.

## Defender pressure

Use Arun Vale. The first obvious disagreement or destructive command raises Alert 1. Once alerted, Arun traces the **group**, not each runner separately. A Lockout ejects everyone.

This creates a reason to cooperate even when objectives conflict.

## Negotiated solutions

- Copy the record, then allow deletion of the original.
- Publish a redacted archive while privately retaining the full evidence.
- Plant a provably false replacement that frames neither party.
- Trade another dataset for the objective.
- Agree to race, with the loser accepting the result.

Any agreement can be enforced through a two-command escrow node: upload the promised asset, then release both when both conditions are met.

## If it becomes a contest

Do not use combat attacks unless a talent explicitly enables them. Use commands, routes, and narrative symbols:

- close or open routes;
- reactivate defensive ICE if the character controls it;
- move evidence to another node;
- expose the other runner to a Sweep;
- create or remove a back door;
- Burn only when a character knowingly accepts system-wide destruction.

An opposed Hacking check is appropriate for control of the same file or command at the same instant. The winner chooses the immediate state; Advantage and Threat determine copies, logs, and exposure.

## Physical twist

The other runner’s access point is within the same building. A physical PC can locate it by following a trace-like clue, camera route, or maintenance log. The discovery should create a conversation or chase, not automatically grant a kill.

## End states

- **Alliance:** NPC becomes a contact and shares one program lead.
- **Professional respect:** Objectives differ, but neither exposes the other.
- **Rivalry:** One side wins and the other records their style.
- **Mutual lockout:** The record remains; both now need a physical solution.
- **Burned system:** The immediate evidence is lost or damaged and every faction notices the outage.
