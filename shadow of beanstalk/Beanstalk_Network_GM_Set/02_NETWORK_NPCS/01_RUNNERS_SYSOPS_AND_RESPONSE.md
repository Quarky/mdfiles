# Network NPCs — Runners, Sysops, and Response

Use the named characters below as scene-ready versions of the published adversary profiles. The underlying profiles are summarized for play; names, voices, goals, and escalation cues are original scenario material.

## Corporate Manager — Marta Sayegh

**Role:** Rival; easy improvised sysop or social obstacle  
**Brawn 2, Agility 2, Intellect 3, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 3**  
**Soak 2; Wounds 9; Defense 0/0**

**Skills:** Charm 1, Computers (Sysops) 1, Deception 2, Knowledge (Society) 1, Leadership 3, Negotiation 2, Perception 2.  
**Corporate Oversight:** When assisting, add Success and two Threat rather than a Boost.  
**Gear:** Smartsuit, PAD, building credentials.

**Network behavior:** Marta Sweeps first, then calls a real sysop. She uses Lockout only after two traces and will spend commands protecting evidence that implicates her division.

## Corporate Sysop — Arun Vale

**Role:** Rival; standard defender  
**Brawn 1, Agility 1, Intellect 3, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 3**  
**Soak 1; Wounds 9; Defense 0/0**

**Skills:** Computers (Sysops) 2, Knowledge (The Net) 2, Mechanics 1, Perception 1, Vigilance 2.  
**Reinforce:** As a maneuver, raise one active ICE by 1 through the end of Arun’s next turn.  
**Gear:** PAD, corporate ID, snacks, stimulant tabs.

**Network behavior:** Reinforce the objective ICE, Trace when alerted, then Lockout. Arun is cautious, procedural, and concerned about losing his job; Charm or Coercion can cost him a maneuver if the runners establish a voice channel.

## SYNC Globalsec Agent — Sable-9

**Role:** Nemesis; elite defender  
**Brawn 1, Agility 2, Intellect 5, Cunning 3, Willpower 3, Presence 2**  
**Soak 2; Wounds 10; Strain 15; Defense 0/0**

**Skills:** Computers (Hacking) 2, Computers (Sysops) 3, Knowledge (The Net) 4, Mechanics 2, Perception 2.  
**Net Warrior:** While using a BMI, make opposed Hacking vs the target’s Sysops; target suffers strain per Success and wounds as well if immersed.  
**Sniffer Programs:** Add 2 Setback to hostile checks on a system Sable-9 controls.  
**Swordbreaker Programs:** Hard Sysops check to damage one active enemy icebreaker one step, plus one step per Advantage.  
**Gear:** PAD, BMI, offensive rig, Gordian Blade, Battering Ram, Garrote.

**Network behavior:** Sweep, identify, Trace, then attack the runner’s programs. Sable-9 prefers exact attribution over a fast Lockout. At Trace 4, a physical Globalsec team enters the scene.

## Principled Runner — Imani “Lantern” Okafor

**Role:** Nemesis; ally, rival, or competing intruder  
**Brawn 2, Agility 2, Intellect 4, Cunning 2, Willpower 3, Presence 3**  
**Soak 3; Wounds 12; Strain 13; Defense 1/1**

**Skills:** Charm 1, Computers (Hacking) 3, Computers (Sysops) 3, Cool 2, Knowledge (Science) 3, Knowledge (The Net) 4, Perception 3, Ranged (Light) 1.  
**Burn Through:** After a successful override, suffer 3 strain to attack a second ICE in the same system as an incidental.  
**Natural:** Once per session, reroll one Hacking or Knowledge (The Net) check.  
**Gear:** Synap pistol, armored utility vest, BMI, big rig with Athena and Yog.0, protected by Hawk’s Eye and Fire Wall.

**Network behavior:** Lantern wants proof, not destruction. She creates back doors, copies rather than deletes, and will sabotage a runner who tries to Burn a life-safety or transit system.

## Criminal Runner — Rook Mercier

**Role:** Nemesis; mercenary runner  
**Brawn 2, Agility 3, Intellect 3, Cunning 4, Willpower 2, Presence 2**  
**Soak 2; Wounds 14; Strain 16; Defense 0/0**

**Skills:** Computers (Hacking) 4, Computers (Sysops) 2, Cool 3, Deception 3, Knowledge (The Net) 2, Ranged (Light) 2, Skulduggery 3, Streetwise 3, Vigilance 2.  
**Adversary 1.**  
**Distinctive Style:** May take two Success and two Threat on a Hacking check; the Threat can expose a recognizable signature.  
**Direct Access:** Automatically succeeds at Access System when jacking in from the same building as the server.  
**Gear:** SMG, skulljack, portable rig with Femme Fatale and Corroder, protected by Shinobi, fake IDs.

**Network behavior:** Rook moves fast, accepts trace exposure, and prioritizes the paid objective. He bargains when the runners can prove they hold something worth more than his contract.

## Yakuza Assassin — Kaede Mori

**Role:** Nemesis; physical infiltrator with quiet Network capability  
**Brawn 3, Agility 4, Intellect 2, Cunning 3, Willpower 4, Presence 1**  
**Soak 3; Wounds 15; Strain 15; Defense 2/2**

**Skills:** Athletics 2, Brawl 4, Computers (Hacking) 3, Cool 4, Coordination 5, Discipline 2, Melee 4, Perception 2, Skulduggery 3, Stealth 5, Vigilance 2.  
**Adversary 2. Quiet Code:** Threat on Hacking cannot be spent merely to alert users.  
**Gear:** Charged crystal katana, optical camouflage suit, climbing gear, pocket rig with one-shot icebreakers.

**Network behavior:** Kaede uses physical service ports to bypass edge defenses, opens one route, and immediately returns to meatspace. She is an ideal parallel threat when the runner thinks the encounter is purely digital.

## Network response clock

| Alert | Response |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Normal operation; sysop unaware. |
| 1 | Suspicion; sysop may Sweep. Routine cameras review access point. |
| 2 | Confirmed anomaly; sysop acts every round and local security receives a quiet notice. |
| 3 | Major alert; credentials rotate, physical patrol moves, objective logs mirror elsewhere. |
| 4 | Crisis response; elite defender or physical response team arrives, but the objective remains possible at a cost. |
