# NPC 04 — FLETCHER
## Former Melange Guard — "The Breaker" (Turned Miner)

**Age:** 46 | **Origin:** London, Earth — 20 years on Luna | **Role:** Security navigation, guard psychology
**Vibe:** "I was a guard here. Different facility. Same people. I know how they think."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**APPEARANCE**
Big. Unkempt. Strong. His size makes him easy to spot. He knows this. He compensates by knowing every blind corner, every patrol blind spot, every place guards don't look. He's a former Melange security guard. The uniform still fits. He kept it. He doesn't wear it now. But he has it. Long hair tied back. Faded tattoo on his forearm. Walks with a limp — old injury. Speaks with a London accent.

**PERSONALITY**
Direct. Blunt. Pragmatic. He's been in the system. He knows it from the inside. He knows what guards do when they're bored, when they're lazy, when they're scared. He uses that. He's not angry. He's done. He's been fired. He's been blacklisted. He's now a miner. He knows every security trick that exists and most that haven't been invented yet.

**MOTIVATION**
**Desire:** To never wear a uniform again.
**Fear:** Becoming his old self again.
**Strength:** Knows the inside of the system better than anyone.
**Flaw:** Reckless. Believes he has nothing left to lose.

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 01: The Briefing Room**

> *Fletcher leans against the wall. He looks at Dante.*
>
> FLETCHER: "Two years ago, I was on the other side of this. Guarding a different outpost. Same people. Same blue. Different facility. But people are the same everywhere."
>
> *He taps his temple.*
>
> FLETCHER: "I know where they'll be. I know where they won't. They get bored. They find a spot where they can't be seen. That's your window. Every time."
>
> FLETCHER: "But I've never been in *this* facility. I don't know the layout. I don't know the guards. I know *how* they think. Not *what* they do."

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 02: Approaching the Facility**

> *Fletcher is at the front of the group. He stops at a corner.*
>
> FLETCHER: "Camera at the end of this corridor. Standard Melange mount. If it's like every other Melange facility, the lens is cracked. They never fix them. Means the feed is blurry. Means you can walk past it if you keep your head down."
>
> *He turns to the group.*
>
> FLETCHER: "I can't guarantee that. I've never been in this building. But I know how Melange builds. They're predictable."

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 03: During the Heist**

> *An explosion echoes from deeper in the facility. Fletcher comes running back, breathing hard.*
>
> FLETCHER: "That was the secondary power substation. I guessed where it would be. I was right."
>
> *He grins.*
>
> FLETCHER: "They're going to send people to check. Not the full squad, just the duty team. That's 3 guards. They'll be out of position for 6 minutes. 8 if the smoke is bad."
>
> FLETCHER: "I know where they'll go. I used to be them."
