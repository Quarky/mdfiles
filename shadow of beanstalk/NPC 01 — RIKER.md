# NPC 01 — RIKER
## Maintenance Mechanic — "The Breaker"

**Age:** 34 | **Origin:** Chicago, Earth — 8 years on Luna | **Role:** Physical access, cutting, demolition
**Vibe:** "I fix things. Sometimes I break them first. That's the same thing, really."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**APPEARANCE**
Stocky. Grease-stained coveralls permanently worn. Goggles pushed up on his forehead. Heavy tool belt with more tools than seems practical. Fingers calloused, scarred from burns. Slight tremor in his hands when he's not working — too much stim, too many long shifts. Pale skin from 8 years underground. His eyes are sharp when you mention machinery. He loves his work with the kind of obsession that makes others uncomfortable. The plasma torch on his belt has a custom grip. He made it.

**PERSONALITY**
Obsessive, focused, dangerous when cornered. He loves his work more than people. He understands machines better than people. He will take apart anything — doors, panels, locks, security systems. He carries a plasma torch and a smile. Not socially adept. Gets excited about technical details. Long explanations nobody asked for. When he's focused on a system, he stops noticing anything else. This is both a strength and a weakness.

**MOTIVATION**
**Desire:** To understand how things work. Deeply. Completely. He can't stop himself.
**Fear:** Obsolescence. Terrified of the day the machines are better than he is.
**Strength:** Technical genius. He can bypass, build, or break anything.
**Flaw:** Obsessive focus. Stops noticing the world when he's working. Needs someone to watch his back.

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 01: The Briefing Room**

> *Riker is in the corner, not listening. He's disassembling a power cell. The others ignore him. He speaks without looking up.*
>
> RIKER: "I've never been inside this place. But I know how things are built. Same panels, same relays, same safety interlocks. Melange builds everything the same way. If I can get to a service panel, I can figure it out."
>
> *He reassembles the power cell with practiced speed.*
>
> RIKER: "The schematics Yusuf gave us are trash. But they show me where the panels *should* be. I'll find them."
>
> *He looks at Dante.*
>
> RIKER: "Just point me at the thing you need broken. I'll break it."

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 02: Approaching the Service Panel**

> *Riker is at a service panel. He's never seen this one before. He studies it like a puzzle.*
>
> RIKER: "Right. This one. I can feel it."
>
> *He leans in.*
>
> RIKER: "Three screws. The middle one is stripped. Someone's been here before. But they sealed it wrong. Look at this weld — that's a maintenance weld. You can get through it without triggering the sensors if you know how."
>
> *He pulls out the plasma torch.*
>
> RIKER: "I don't know what's on the other side. But I'm about to find out."
>
> *The torch flares. He's smiling.*

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**DIALOGUE — SCENARIO 03: During the Heist**

> *Riker is deep in a terminal panel. Wires everywhere. He's muttering to himself.*
>
> RIKER: "No, no, no — that's not right. They have the relays routed through the safety interlock. That's clever. That's really clever. Whoever designed this knows what they're doing."
>
> *He smiles.*
>
> RIKER: "But they didn't account for the thermal cycling. The weld on the secondary conduit is weak. If I cut there—"
>
> *The panel sparks. He grins.*
>
> RIKER: "—I can bypass the entire barrier."
>
> *He looks at Dante.*
>
> RIKER: "I'm a genius. Tell me I'm a genius."
