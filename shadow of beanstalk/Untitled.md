# Hover & Spider Drones (with Mounted SMG) - Custom Adversary Stats

*Note: The following statistics are custom creations for the Genesys/Shadow of the Beanstalk system, built using the game's adversary creation guidelines. While FFG produced an "Androids, Drones, and Synthetics Adversary Deck", specific stats for these drone types are not included in the search results and are provided here for your campaign.*

---

## Hover Drone (with Mounted SMG)

**Description:** A sleek, disc-shaped drone about the size of a large dinner plate, hovering silently on four downward-facing rotors. It is equipped with a compact SMG mounted on an articulated arm beneath its chassis. A small red sensor eye glows at its center, tracking targets with unnerving precision. Commonly used by corporate security forces for patrol and crowd control, these drones are fast, agile, and deadly.

| Characteristic | Value |
|----------------|-------|
| **Brawn** | 1 |
| **Agility** | 3 |
| **Intellect** | 1 |
| **Cunning** | 2 |
| **Willpower** | 1 |
| **Presence** | 1 |
| **Soak** | 2 |
| **Wound Threshold** | 5 |
| **Strain Threshold** | 8 |
| **M/R Defense** | 2/0 |

**Skills:** Perception (Cun) 2, Ranged (Light) (Ag) 2, Stealth (Ag) 2, Vigilance (Will) 1

**Talents:**
- **Hover Platform:** The drone ignores difficult terrain and can move vertically up to its speed. It cannot be knocked prone.
- **Target Lock:** Once per round, the drone may perform a **Maneuver** to gain a Boost die on its next attack against a target it has previously attacked this encounter.
- **Silent Operation:** Add a Boost die to Stealth checks made to remain undetected.

**Weapons:**
- **Mounted SMG (Ranged Light):** Damage 5, Critical 4, Range Medium, Auto-Fire

**Special Abilities:**
- **Vulnerable to EMP:** If hit by an attack that deals Ion or EMP damage, the drone suffers 2 additional Strain (ignoring Soak).
- **Limited Ammo:** The drone carries enough ammunition for 3 full Auto-Fire attacks before it must return to base or be reloaded (takes 1 Round).

**Handling & Risks:**
- Controlled remotely by a security operator (Prisec) or running on simple combat AI.
- If the controller is incapacitated, the drone continues its last command (e.g., "defend this area") until it runs out of ammo or is destroyed.
- Can be hacked (Computers check, **Hard** difficulty) to either shut down or turn on its allies for 1 Round.

## Spider Drone (with Mounted SMG)

**Description:** A four-legged, insectoid drone roughly the size of a large dog. Its chassis is angular and armored, painted in matte black or corporate colors. It skitters across walls, ceilings, and floors with disturbing speed and silence. A short-barreled SMG is mounted on its back, capable of swiveling to track targets independently. The drone's legs end in sharp points, allowing it to climb and even strike in melee. Often deployed in urban environments or mining facilities for patrol and anti-personnel duties.

| Characteristic | Value |
|----------------|-------|
| **Brawn** | 2 |
| **Agility** | 3 |
| **Intellect** | 1 |
| **Cunning** | 2 |
| **Willpower** | 1 |
| **Presence** | 1 |
| **Soak** | 3 |
| **Wound Threshold** | 8 |
| **Strain Threshold** | 10 |
| **M/R Defense** | 1/0 |

**Skills:** Athletics (Br) 2, Brawl (Br) 2, Perception (Cun) 2, Ranged (Light) (Ag) 2, Stealth (Ag) 3, Vigilance (Will) 2

**Talents:**
- **Spider Climb:** The drone can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an Athletics check.
- **Unusual Nature:** The drone doesn't require air, food, or sleep.
- **Silent Hunter:** Add a Boost die to Stealth checks when moving.
- **Coordinated Attack:** If two or more Spider Drones are engaged with the same target, they each add a Boost die to their Brawl attacks.

**Weapons:**
- **Mounted SMG (Ranged Light):** Damage 5, Critical 4, Range Medium, Auto-Fire
- **Leg Strike (Brawl):** Damage 3, Critical 4, Range Engaged, Knockdown

**Special Abilities:**
- **Vulnerable to EMP:** If hit by an attack that deals Ion or EMP damage, the drone suffers 2 additional Strain (ignoring Soak).
- **Limited Ammo:** The drone carries enough ammunition for 3 full Auto-Fire attacks before it must return to base or be reloaded (takes 1 Round).
- **Grapple:** As an action, the drone may make an opposed **Brawl** check to immobilize a target. If successful, the target is immobilized until they take an action to break free (opposed Brawl check).

**Handling & Risks:**
- Controlled remotely by a security operator or running on combat AI.
- If the controller is incapacitated, the drone continues its last command.
- Can be hacked (Computers check, **Daunting** difficulty) to either shut down or turn on its allies for 1 Round.

## Sample Encounter: Security Response

*The mining facility's alarm blares. Two Spider Drones skitter down the walls, their SMGs swiveling toward the intruders. A Hover Drone hums into view from around the corner, its red sensor eye locking onto the nearest target.*

**Encounter Difficulty:** Hard (3 Purple) - multiple drones, coordinated attack

**Tactics:**
- Spider Drones attempt to **flank** and use **Coordinated Attack**.
- Hover Drone stays at **Medium range**, using **Target Lock** to focus fire.
- Drones prioritize **threats** (players with heavy weapons or obvious cybernetics).

**Reward:** Disabling or hacking the drones may yield useful intel, such as patrol routes or security codes.

## Hacking Rules (Genesys)

| Action | Difficulty | Result |
|--------|------------|--------|
| **Shut Down Drone** | Hard (3 Purple) | Drone is disabled for 1 Round. |
| **Override Drone** | Daunting (4 Purple) | Drone attacks its allies for 1 Round. |
| **Gain Control** | Formidable (5 Purple) | Drone follows your commands for the encounter. |

*These checks require a **Computers** skill check and access to the drone's control node (either via the Network or a physical terminal).*
