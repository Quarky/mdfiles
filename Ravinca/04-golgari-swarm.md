# Golgari Swarm - Black/Green

## Main-era leader
**Jarad vod Savo**

## Civic function
reclamation, waste, food cycles, corpse handling, undercity ecology.

## How it actually works
factional swarm of Devkarin, medusas, kraul, reclaimers, rot farmers, shamans, undead, trolls; Ochran provide covert violence.

## Roles you can immediately play
reclaimer, rot farmer, shaman, spore druid, warrior, Ochran assassin, advisor, high chancellor, matka.

## Major places
Korozda, Deadbridge, Zanikev, Umerilek, Stonefare, Grekospen.

## Why ordinary people join
nothing wasted, death feeds life, hidden labor matters.

## Characteristic danger
assassination, vengeance, predatory undercity politics.

## Internal politics
Devkarin privilege; kraul influence; medusa power; ordinary workers ignored; undead personhood.

## NPC voice
You call it refuse because you stopped looking. / We remember what the buildings were built on. / Everything returns.

## Immediate NPC recipe
Role + sincere belief + guild grievance + personal need + line they will not cross.

## Reaction sequence
Local worker notices; middle rank decides whether to report; internal faction asks who gains status; official response begins; opportunist moves faster than policy; ordinary residents bear the cost.

## Six ready archetypes
- loyalist who embodies the best case;
- opportunist using membership for status/security;
- reformer convinced the guild betrayed itself;
- exhausted professional finishing a shift;
- ambitious middle rank competing with rival;
- anomaly who fits philosophy but not stereotype.
