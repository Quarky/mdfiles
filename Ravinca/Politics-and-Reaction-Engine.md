# Politics and Reaction Engine

Politics is issue-specific. Score each guild on jurisdiction, material interest, ideology, security, and prestige.

## First political question
Azorius: what rule or authority governs this?  
Boros: who is in danger and what must happen now?  
Dimir: who knows and what information advantage appears?  
Golgari: what waste, death, food, or undercity interest is hidden?  
Gruul: what expansion or enclosure does this represent?  
Izzet: can it be measured or engineered?  
Orzhov: who owns it, owes for it, or depends on it?  
Rakdos: who is pretending not to want what they want, and who is watching?  
Selesnya: what happens to community cohesion?  
Simic: what must adapt?

## Escalation
Azorius: notice -> hearing -> injunction -> warrant -> emergency decree.  
Boros: observe -> patrol -> cordon -> deployment -> military response.  
Dimir: listen -> verify -> leverage -> redirect -> erase/replace information.  
Golgari: observe below -> alter access -> mobilize faction -> reclaim.  
Gruul: warning -> trespass -> sabotage -> raid -> clan mobilization.  
Izzet: inspect -> test -> prototype -> emergency engineering -> catastrophic success.  
Orzhov: offer -> contract -> call debt -> acquire asset -> coercive collection.  
Rakdos: joke -> humiliation -> spectacle -> riotous performance -> lethal excess.  
Selesnya: invitation -> mediation -> collective pressure -> mass action.  
Simic: sample -> study -> adaptation -> deployment -> ecosystem intervention.

Distinguish official policy, middle-management interpretation, local improvisation, rogue action, and what rivals claim was ordered.
