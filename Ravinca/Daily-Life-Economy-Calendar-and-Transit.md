# Daily Life, Economy, Calendar, and Transit

Ravnica uses a 365-day year beginning on 1 Seleszeni. Months: Seleszeni, Dhazo, Prahz, Mokosh, Paujal, Cizarm, Tevnember, Golgar, Quaegar, Xivaskir, Griev, Zuun. The Guildpact celebration begins on 28 Zuun and runs into 1 Seleszeni.

A city-world requires constant labor: heat, water, conduits, freight, food, waste, corpse handling, records, courts, construction, metallurgy, research, medicine, security, entertainment, information.

Track more than coin: rent, wages, debt, credit, permits, favors, secrets, access, introductions, protection.

Golgari reclamation and Selesnyan cultivation both contribute to food systems. Food problems immediately become freight, public-order, health, land, and price problems.

Transguild Promenade is a major cross-district commercial and ceremonial route. Tin Street is a major Tenth District commercial corridor. Augustin Station is a major passenger/freight/airship node. Underground transit and sewers are not identical to the deeper undercity. Vertical travel matters as much as horizontal distance.
