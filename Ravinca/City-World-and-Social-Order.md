# City-World and Social Order

Ravnica is a city-world built in accumulated layers. Core Ravnica is divided into ten enormous numbered districts, but all ten guilds operate across boundaries. Beyond the core are numerous districts that began as outlying settlements before urban expansion swallowed the gaps.

Guild membership is chosen rather than automatically inherited. Families can split across guilds. Use four useful NPC membership models: loyalist, opportunist, rebel, anomaly.

Guildless people are not one faction. They include merchants, laborers, academics, criminals, professionals, rich families, tenants, landlords, and activists. Gateless is a stronger political identity: opposition to guild privilege.

Ravnican inequality often appears as access: safe transit, reliable heat/water, legal representation, credit, medicine, protection, information, and influence over zoning.

At every address ask: who built this layer, who uses it, who owns it legally, who keeps it functioning, and what older layer lies below.
