# Cult of Rakdos - Black/Red

## Main-era leader
**Rakdos**

## Civic function
entertainment, satire, mining, manual labor, spectacle.

## How it actually works
troupes, venues, blood witches, ringmasters, labor crews, stars, reputation; little conventional bureaucracy.

## Roles you can immediately play
laborer, stagehand, performer, MC, blood witch, star, ringmaster, venue operator.

## Major places
Rix Maadi, Smelting Quarter, Gore House, mobile stages, Carnarium Rings.

## Why ordinary people join
artistic freedom, satire, emotional honesty, fearless labor.

## Characteristic danger
cruelty, nihilism, exploitation, unsafe spectacle.

## Internal politics
performers vs laborers; satire vs sadism; venue profit vs danger; blood-witch authority.

## NPC voice
If the decree is ridiculous, why read it seriously? / Nobody paid to watch us be careful. / Put truth under a spotlight.

## Immediate NPC recipe
Role + sincere belief + guild grievance + personal need + line they will not cross.

## Reaction sequence
Local worker notices; middle rank decides whether to report; internal faction asks who gains status; official response begins; opportunist moves faster than policy; ordinary residents bear the cost.

## Six ready archetypes
- loyalist who embodies the best case;
- opportunist using membership for status/security;
- reformer convinced the guild betrayed itself;
- exhausted professional finishing a shift;
- ambitious middle rank competing with rival;
- anomaly who fits philosophy but not stereotype.
