# Jump Into Any NPC

Choose seven things: affiliation, immediate job, today's inconvenience, long-term aspiration, person or institution feared, principle sincerely believed, thing hidden.

## Information by position

| Position | Main concern | Likely knowledge |
|---|---|---|
| laborer | shift, safety, rent | routes, accidents, supervisors |
| shopkeeper | customers, suppliers | local faces, protection, rumor |
| professional | reputation, clients | procedure, specialists, access |
| enforcer | orders, threat | patrols, evidence, command |
| middle manager | targets, blame | rivals, policy gaps |
| senior leader | faction survival | alliances, succession, strategy |
| ideologue | doctrine | philosophy, history |
| opportunist | self-interest | loopholes, leverage |

## Reaction ladder
routine -> attentive -> guarded -> bargaining -> threatened -> institutional response -> personal escalation

## Guild cognitive habits
Azorius defines terms and authority. Boros identifies danger and action. Dimir asks who knows and why. Golgari asks what is buried, wasted, dying, feeding, or returning. Gruul asks who claimed the land. Izzet identifies mechanism and test. Orzhov turns relationship into obligation. Rakdos finds hypocrisy and audience. Selesnya places the issue inside community health. Simic identifies the system and adaptation.

## One-third should disagree with their own guild
Examples: arrester troubled by precognition; Boros medic tired of reckless heroics; Dimir courier who wants an honest friendship; Golgari farmer tired of elite politics; Gruul parent who values survival over glory; Izzet maintenance engineer who resents flashy research; Orzhov priest who gives real charity; Rakdos rigger obsessed with safety; Selesnyan healer who needs privacy; Simic guardian insisting on bodily autonomy.
