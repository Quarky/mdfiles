# Living Guildpact Era Stories

ORIGINAL FICTION - non-canon examples.

## When the Guildpact Returned
**Guilds/themes:** Jace / functionaries

The first week after Jace became Living Guildpact, clerks discovered magic had not abolished filing. Petitions still had to be read. Witnesses still had to appear. Representatives still lied, omitted, delayed, and objected. The miracle was not that Jace could decide. It was that an argument finally had somewhere to stop. Then he vanished for three days. The clerks stared at the new stack and quietly invented provisional procedure.

GM lesson: each participant should pursue a recognizable institutional or personal need; Ravnica emerges when those needs collide without erasing ordinary life.

## The Petition Nobody Wanted
**Guilds/themes:** Golgari / Chamber

The Golgari petition was simple: a surface district had blocked a corpse route. Azorius called it jurisdiction. Orzhov owned the building. Boros called it sanitation. Selesnya called it public health. The Golgari envoy waited. “It is a door,” she said. “Open it.” Jace did. The crisis was discovering how many institutions profited from pretending the door was complicated.

## Absence
**Guilds/themes:** All guilds

Every guild learned a different lesson from Jace’s absence. Azorius wrote procedures. Boros delegated emergency authority. Dimir mapped his patterns. Orzhov priced delay. Izzet measured departures. Selesnya built local consensus. Simic treated it as an environmental variable. Rakdos wrote jokes. Gruul ignored the office. Golgari waited. The surface always came down eventually.

## The Ruling After Midnight
**Guilds/themes:** Jace / neighborhood

Jace expected a dispute about guild law. Thirty tenants wanted to know whether Izzet could cut through their wall. The engineer had a permit. The landlord had an Orzhov lien. The Azorius file was correct. The Selesnyan garden behind the wall was not on any map. Jace listened for an hour. Then he asked the tenants what they wanted. Everyone looked surprised except the tenants.
