# Named Locations Deep Dives

## New Prahv
CANON: three enormous towers aligned with Sova, Jelenn, and Lyev.  
SYNTHESIS: public lower halls become administrative floors, interview rooms, archives, cells, and offices. The deeper you go into process, the less monumental and more bureaucratic it feels.  
No-PC outcome: procedure continues; unchallenged filings become the practical truth.

## Sunhome
CANON: Boros fortress and guildhall.  
SYNTHESIS: command halls and angelic spaces above barracks, medics, mess halls, training, memorials, armories.  
No-PC outcome: the Legion chooses a course and commits enough resources to make reversal politically costly.

## Nivix
CANON: Izzet guildhall/tower.  
SYNTHESIS: constantly modified; old stairs meet new platforms; pipes bear generations of notation; wings can be sealed after experiments.  
No-PC outcome: a failed test becomes another test unless outside pressure stops it.

## Vitu-Ghazi
CANON: Selesnya city-tree/guildhall.  
SYNTHESIS: public paths transition into increasingly communal and ritual space.  
No-PC outcome: the community solves the problem collectively, potentially overriding individual preference.

## Zameck and Zonot Seven
CANON: Simic guildhall in the Tenth District zonot.  
SYNTHESIS: visitors meet in accessible chambers; deeper laboratories and residences become wet, flooded, or fully aquatic.  
No-PC outcome: researchers gather data before politics catches up.

## Deadbridge and Wayport
CANON: major undercity opening and trade/warehouse node.  
SYNTHESIS: stacked bridges and stairs create several economies at different elevations.  
No-PC outcome: whoever controls safe passage controls goods.

## Rix Maadi
CANON: Rakdos guildhall.  
SYNTHESIS: less like an office and more like a court around the unpredictable attention of a demon.  
No-PC outcome: unresolved tension becomes performance material.

## Korozda
CANON: main-era Golgari guildhall, Maze of Decay.  
SYNTHESIS: living walls, fungal routes, corpse flows, insects, faction offices, damp archives.  
No-PC outcome: factions bargain while ordinary reclaimers keep the system functioning.

## Duskmantle
CANON: secret Dimir guildhall.  
SYNTHESIS: access is the adventure; false routes, intermediaries, memory gaps, cover identities.  
No-PC outcome: a visitor leaves knowing less about the route than about the message.

## Orzhova and Vizkopa
CANON: Orzhov institutional/financial heart in the Obzedat era.  
SYNTHESIS: wealth itself becomes architecture: silence, scale, controlled staff, spirits, private legal chambers, vaults.  
No-PC outcome: delay increases someone else's leverage.

## Skarrg
CANON: Gruul gathering place and closest thing to guildhall.  
SYNTHESIS: politics by feast, boasting, challenge, gift, hunting story, strength, followers.  
No-PC outcome: the strongest narrative can become clan truth even when facts are messier.
