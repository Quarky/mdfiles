# Tenth District Master Gazetteer

The Tenth District is the primary detailed campaign hub. Each precinct is city-scale.

District-wide routes include Transguild Promenade, Tin Street, Gnat Alley, and the Millennial Platform.

Precinct One: wealth, diplomacy, neutral plaza, elite hospitality, espionage.  
Precinct Two: law, professional residential life, respectable corruption, New Prahv, Augustin Station.  
Precinct Three: Greenbelt, Vitu-Ghazi, beasts, vernadi, cultivation.  
Precinct Four: Boros, Izzet, Gruul pressure, fortifications, laboratories.  
Precinct Five: universities, libraries, Zonot Seven, knowledge economy.  
Precinct Six: factories, warehouses, Rakdos labor, Golgari access, Deadbridge.

When crossing precincts, describe building material, crowd composition, patrol style, smell/noise, guild signage, and what changes below street level.
