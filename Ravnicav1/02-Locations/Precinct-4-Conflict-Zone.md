# Precinct Four - Conflict Zone

Feel: fortifications, repaired tenements, laboratories, soldiers, goblin gangs, old blast scars, rubblebelt pressure.

Sunhome is Boros guildhall and fortress. Show both angelic command and ordinary barracks, medics, armor repair, mess halls, and memorials.

Nivix is Izzet guildhall, a tower that should never feel still. Bulwark contains reinforced low-rent homes and shops between powerful institutions. Red Wastes form a northern ruin zone. Skarrg is the closest thing to Gruul guildhall: clan gathering, feast, boasting, challenge, negotiation.

If ignored: controlled escalation. Boros fortifies, Izzet tests, Gruul probes, civilians adapt. A minor event can become a three-guild crisis quickly.
