# Undercity and Below

Do not call all underground space the undercity. Immediately below streets are sewers, transit, foundations, water systems, maintenance passages. The deeper undercity includes buried architecture, caverns, rot farms, Golgari communities, covert routes, monsters, and ancient civic layers.

Golgari sites: Korozda, Svogthos, Stonefare, corpsefloat valves, Felden Pipe, Grek'ospen, Umerilek, House of the Ochran.  
Dimir: Duskmantle, Nightveil, Fiend's Crawl.  
Rakdos: Demon's Vestibule, Rix Maadi.  
Izzet: Boilerpits.  
Simic: zonots connecting to ancient subterranean waters.

Underground rules: sound travels strangely, routes change, surface jurisdiction weakens with depth, trade and smuggling overlap, apparently abandoned rooms may still belong to somebody.

If ignored: things disappear underground. The question is who receives them.
