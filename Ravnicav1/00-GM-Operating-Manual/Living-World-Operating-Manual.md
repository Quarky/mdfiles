# Living World Operating Manual

Ravnica is already moving before the PCs arrive. Deliveries, inspections, rent disputes, shift changes, guild recruiting, sewer work, debt collection, performances, couriers, and patrols continue whether or not an adventure is happening.

## Five moving layers

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| Civic function | What must happen here every day? |
| Formal authority | Who claims jurisdiction? |
| Practical authority | Who can actually make the place work? |
| Hidden pressure | Who profits if conditions change? |
| Ordinary life | Who must live with the result? |

A strong scene usually has two legitimate interests and one exploitative interest.

## Think vertically

Above: towers, bridges, rocs, angels, freight platforms, signals, rooftop gardens.  
Street: markets, trams, carts, patrols, pipes, crowds, guild signage.  
Below: foundations, service corridors, sewers, transit, crypts, rot farms, buried streets, undercity.

One address can have an Azorius roadway above it, an Orzhov owner, an Izzet conduit through the wall, and a Golgari route underneath.

## Time of day changes control

Dawn: sanitation, food, labor crews, deliveries.  
Day: courts, permits, schools, research, formal markets.  
Evening: workers, meals, shopping, second shifts, stage setup.  
Night: covert movement, illegal trade, undercity emergence, Rakdos shows.  
Festival: public guild identity dominates and neutral space becomes politically charged.

## Five-beat description

Give one vertical fact, one civic job, one social fact, one current tension, and one independent NPC action unrelated to the PCs.

## No-PC trajectory

Every scene gets: If nobody intervenes, in one hour...

## Guilds are not alignments

Azorius can defend due process or weaponize it. Boros can save lives or escalate. Dimir can expose corruption or manufacture it. Golgari can feed a district or weaponize resentment. Gruul can defend dispossessed people or endanger bystanders. Izzet can keep the city alive or treat citizens as data. Orzhov can finance recovery or create dependency. Rakdos can expose hypocrisy or indulge cruelty. Selesnya can build community or suppress dissent. Simic can cure disease or treat consent as an inconvenience.
