# Timeline and Fiction Reading Map

Original Ravnica cycle: Ravnica: City of Guilds, Guildpact, Dissension. Use for Agrus Kos, Szadek, Savra, Utvara, Agyrem, Momir Vig, Project Kraj, old institutional states.

Return to Ravnica era: Return to Ravnica / Gatecrash / Dragon’s Maze setting material and The Secretist. Use for restored guild system, Implicit Maze, Jace becoming Living Guildpact.

Living Guildpact era: public stories involving Jace’s work on Ravnica model the problem of legitimate magical arbiter plus frequent absence.

Guilds of Ravnica / Ravnica Allegiance: transition into Bolas-infiltration overlay.

War of the Spark public story sequence: use for wartime dialogue, distrust, leadership collapse, and forced cooperation.
