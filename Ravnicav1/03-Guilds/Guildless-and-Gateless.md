# Guildless and Gateless

Guildless is not a faction. It includes merchants, workers, academics, professionals, criminals, landlords, tenants, rich families, poor families, and people who never joined anything.

Gateless is a stronger political identity: active resistance to guild privilege.

Guildless people often understand overlapping jurisdiction better than guild ideologues because they survive between systems.

NPC voices:
“I do not care whose insignia is on the work order. I care whether the water is back before lunch.”
“The Senate says it is legal. The Syndicate says it is contracted. We are the ones sleeping beside it.”
