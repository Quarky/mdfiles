# Law, Crime, Property, and Justice

Azorius Lyev performs formal law enforcement, investigation, arrest, and surveillance. Boros acts as army, emergency force, and justice-driven protector. Orzhov enforces private obligations through contracts, property, advocates, and coercive collection. Guild properties maintain internal security.

Jurisdiction questions: who has authority over the person, who owns the building, who maintains the road, who holds the debt, who controls the tunnel below, who can act immediately, whose record becomes official?

Property is vertical and historical. A surface deed may ignore buried foundations, old roads, living roots, Izzet easements, sewer rights, or corpse routes.

Justice differs by institution. Azorius emphasizes predictable process, Boros protection and wrongdoers, Orzhov enforceable obligation, Selesnya restorative community, Gruul rejection of imposed authority, Dimir consequence and information.
