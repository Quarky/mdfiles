# GM Side: Arbooine Wilds – Journey to Quolas

This document contains the Game Master’s private notes for running the overland travel from the PCs’ landing site to the tree‑city of Quolas. It includes navigation, natural hazards, the bearsloth ambush, and the optional traveler encounter with the Jairo clan.

---

## Encounter Secrets & Hidden Details

| **Secret** | **Relevance** |
|------------|---------------|
| The forest is ancient and full of predators. The bearsloth is the most dangerous. | Builds tension; the bearsloth can be foreshadowed by sudden silence or claw marks on trees. |
| The Jairo clan knows about Marcolf’s tyranny and the resistance. They can be allies or informants. | Provides a peaceful info‑dump and a moral choice (help them or ignore them). |
| The river bridge is unstable. If the PCs cross carelessly, it may collapse. | Creates a skill challenge with potential for dramatic falls. |
| The dead end cliff forces a climbing check or a long detour. | Uses Athletics or Force powers; good for spotlighting a strong or agile PC. |
| The bearsloth will flee if wounded badly, but it may return later (e.g., at night if the PCs camp). | Allows the encounter to be a recurring threat. |

## Forest Environment – Detailed Descriptions

### General Atmosphere

| **Sense** | **Description** |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Sight** | Towering elrit trees, trunks wider than houses. Shafts of golden sunlight pierce the dense canopy. Vines hang like curtains. Moss covers everything. In the distance, massive roots form natural bridges. |
| **Sound** | Constant chorus of insects and birds. Occasional howl of a distant predator. Wind rustling leaves high above. The crunch of your own footsteps on fallen leaves. |
| **Smell** | Damp earth, blooming flowers, the sweet rot of fallen fruit. After rain: petrichor and fresh ozone. |
| **Touch** | Humid air, sticky on skin. Rough bark, smooth moss. Vines that catch at clothing. |

### Time of Day

| **Time** | **Lighting** | **Effects** |
|----------|--------------|--------------|
| Midday | Bright shafts of light, deep shadows under trees. | Normal visibility. |
| Dusk | Golden, dimming rapidly. | Adds ◊ to Perception checks. |
| Night | Near total darkness under canopy. Glow rods or night vision required. | Adds ◊◊ to all checks. |

## Player Actions & Responses – Navigation

### Action: “I try to find the fastest route to Quolas”

| **Check** | **Result** |
|-----------|------------|
| **Survival (Average ◊◊)** | Success: “You find a game trail heading southwest – likely used by hunters. You’ll reach Quolas by nightfall.” |
| Failure: “The trail splits. You take the wrong fork. You’ll have to camp overnight and try again tomorrow.” |
| ◊ (Advantage) | “You spot edible fruit on a low branch. Each PC recovers 1 strain.” |
| ◊ (Threat) | “A vine trips you. You suffer 1 strain.” |

### Action: “I climb a tree to get a better view”

| **Check** | **Result** |
|-----------|------------|
| **Athletics (Easy ◊)** | Success: “From the canopy, you see the top of Quolas – a massive tree rising above the rest. It’s about two hours southeast.” |
| Failure: “The bark is slippery. You slide back down, unhurt but frustrated.” |

### Action: “I use the Force to sense the direction”

| **Check** | **Result** |
|-----------|------------|
| **Discipline (Average ◊◊)** or **Sense** power | Success: “You feel a faint pull – a concentration of life. Quolas is that way.” (Gestures correctly.) |
| Failure: “The forest’s life force is overwhelming. You can’t distinguish one direction from another.” |

## Optional Encounter: Jairo Clan (Travelers)

### When to Use

After a successful navigation check or as a random encounter. Best used early in the journey to provide exposition about Marcolf.

### Description

*“A rustle in the undergrowth ahead makes you pause. Four Sathari emerge – their plumage a mix of russet and gold. They carry woven packs and hunting spears. The eldest, a female with long facial feathers, raises a hand in greeting. ‘Travelers,’ she says in Basic. ‘We rarely see your kind so deep in the wilds.’”*

### Jairo Clan Members

| **Name** | **Role** | **Disposition** |
|----------|----------|------------------|
| Elder Jairo | Matriarch, leader | Cautiously curious, weary of off‑worlders |
| Taro Jairo | Hunter (adult male) | Suspicious, hand on weapon |
| Sena Jairo | Hunter (adult female) | Quiet, observant |
| Lira Jairo | Youngling | Wide‑eyed, hides behind elder |

### Player Actions & Dialogue

| **Player Action** | **Response** |
|-------------------|---------------|
| “We mean no harm. We’re looking for Quolas.” | Elder Jairo: “Quolas? *She spits on the ground.* A place of sorrow. The reeve there – Marcolf – he is not a good leader. But yes, follow the setting sun and you will find it by nightfall.” |
| “What do you know about Marcolf?” | Elder Jairo (lowering voice): “He came with a Jedi, long ago. The Jedi left, but Marcolf stayed. Now he rules with fear. His guards take what they want. We Jairo keep to the wilds – safer.” |
| “Is there a resistance?” | Elder Jairo (glancing around): “Some speak of a young one – Pathran Helshar. But he is a fool if he thinks he can defy the reeve. *She signals the group to move on.* The forest has ears.” |
| “Can you help us?” | Elder Jairo: “We can give you water and roots. And a warning: stay off the low ground at dusk. The bearsloths hunt then.” (Offers 1 ration per PC, recovers 1 strain.) |
| (Threatening or attacking) | The hunters raise spears but flee into the trees. Attacking earns 5+ Conflict. The Jairo will not forget. |

### What the Jairo Know (GM Secrets)

- Marcolf has ruled for nearly 20 years.
- The guards are cruel and tax everything.
- Pathran Helshar leads a secret resistance.
- The Jairo know a safe route to Quolas – reduces navigation difficulty by one if the PCs befriend them.

## Bearsloth Ambush

### When to Trigger

After the Jairo encounter, or during navigation, especially if the PCs fail a check or camp overnight. Best as an action set‑piece.

### Foreshadowing

Before the ambush, describe:
- *“The forest goes silent. Even the insects stop chirping.”*
- *“You see deep claw marks on a tree trunk – fresh.”*
- *“A shadow moves above, too large for a bird.”*

### Ambush Description

*“The forest falls silent. A shadow drops from the canopy – a massive, six‑limbed beast with four glowing eyes. It lands behind the rearmost of you, claws extended. The bearsloth roars, a sound like grinding stone.”*

### Player Actions During Combat

| **Action** | **Check / Result** |
|------------|--------------------|
| “I shoot the bearsloth before it attacks” | Initiative check. If successful, they act first. |
| “I try to scare it away” | **Cool or Survival (Hard ◊◊◊)** . Success: The beast hesitates, then lumbers back into the trees. |
| “I use the Force to calm it” | **Influence power** or **Discipline (Daunting ◊◊◊◊)** . Success: The bearsloth becomes docile and leaves. |
| “I climb a tree to escape” | **Athletics (Average ◊◊)** . Success: You’re out of reach for one round. |
| “I attack its eyes” | Called shot: upgrade difficulty once. If successful, the bearsloth suffers +10 to its next Critical Injury. |

### Bearsloth Tactics (GM Only)

- **Turn 1:** Ambush the rear target. Use **Linked 1** if enough Advantage.
- **Turn 2:** If wounded, use **Envenomed Bite** (flip dark side point). If not wounded, continue attacking the same target.
- **Flee threshold:** If the bearsloth suffers a Critical Injury or wounds > 8 (half its threshold), it flees into the trees. It may return later (e.g., at night).
- **If PCs flee:** The bearsloth pursues for one range band, then gives up unless it has a wounded victim.

### Aftermath

| **Outcome** | **Result** |
|-------------|------------|
| Bearsloth killed | Can harvest venom: **Survival or Medicine (Hard ◊◊◊)** . Success yields 2 doses (150 credits each, rarity 3). |
| Bearsloth driven off | Award 5 bonus XP for mercy. |
| PC poisoned | Requires **Resilience (Daunting ◊◊◊◊)** to resist. Failure inflicts *Wracking Venom* critical injury (no damage, but ongoing penalties). |

## River Crossing (Optional)

### Description

*“A river blocks your path, flowing swiftly through a rocky canyon. An ancient rope bridge sways in the wind – frayed and weathered. Below, white water crashes against sharp rocks.”*

### Crossing the Bridge

| **Method** | **Check** | **Result** |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| One at a time, slowly | **Coordination (Easy ◊)** | Success: Cross safely. |
| Multiple on bridge, or rushing | Upgrade difficulty once per extra person / rushed action | Failure: Fall. Spend ◊◊ to grab a handhold. |
| Bridge collapses (◊◊◊ on check) | All on bridge fall. | Damage from fall (short range) + possible drowning. |

### Alternative Crossings

| **Method** | **Check** | **Result** |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| Swim across | **Athletics (Average ◊◊)** | Success: Reach other side, suffer 2 strain. |
| Find another crossing | – | Adds 2 hours travel time. |
| Use the Force (Move) to create a path | **Move power** | Spend ◊◊ to move debris to form stepping stones. |

## Dead End – Cliff (Optional)

### Description

*“The trail ends at a sheer cliff, moss‑covered and slick. Above, you can see the upper forest floor. A detour into the undergrowth to the east might find a longer route – or you could climb.”*

### Climbing the Cliff

| **Check** | **Result** |
|-----------|------------|
| **Athletics (Hard ◊◊◊)** | Success: You reach the top. |
| Failure: Fall (damage per falling rules, page 221). | Spend ◊◊ to grab a ledge – take 1 strain instead of falling. |
| ◊ (Advantage) | Find a hidden root or crevice – upgrade next climber’s check. |
| ◊ (Threat) | Loose rock falls – next climber adds ◊ to their check. |

### Alternative Approaches

| **Method** | **Check** | **Result** |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| Use the Force (Enhance) to leap | **Enhance power** | Spend ◊ to leap to the top. |
| Find a longer path | **Survival (Easy ◊)** | Success: Detour adds 1 hour. |
| Use climbing gear (if any) | Remove ◊ from the check. | |

## Camping Overnight (If PCs Get Lost)

If the PCs fail their navigation check, they must camp. Use this for additional encounters.

### Camp Description

*“You find a sheltered spot beneath a massive root. The forest grows dark. Bioluminescent fungi cast a faint blue glow. The sounds of the day give way to nocturnal chirps and howls.”*

### Night Encounters (Optional)

| **Encounter** | **Trigger** |
|---------------|--------------|
| Bearsloth returns | Roll 1d6; on 1–2, the bearsloth from earlier attacks at night. |
| Jairo clan visit | Friendly – they share food and warn about Marcolf. |
| Strange noises | No combat – just atmosphere. A **Vigilance (Easy ◊)** check reveals it’s just a tree creature. |
| Imperial patrol (rare) | If the GM wants to foreshadow Lieutenant Devrays, a scout party passes nearby. |

## Arrival at Quolas – Transition

When the PCs finally reach Quolas, read:

*“The forest parts, and you see it: an elrit tree of gargantuan proportions, its trunk easily three hundred meters across. Suspended orbs, carved platforms, and rope bridges cling to its heights like a vertical village. This is Quolas – the tree‑city. A massive wooden gate blocks the entrance at ground level. Two guards watch you from above.”*

Then proceed to the main gate encounter.

## Summary of GM Secrets – Wilds

| **Secret** | **Reveal?** |
|------------|--------------|
| The bearsloth is hunting for food, not malice. | PCs can deduce from its behaviour (flees when wounded). |
| The Jairo clan knows about the resistance. | Only if PCs befriend them and ask. |
| The bridge will collapse if too many cross at once. | Foreshadow: “The ropes creak ominously.” |
| The cliff has a hidden handhold halfway up. | A ◊◊ on Athletics reveals it. |
| The forest is a vergence (minor Force site). | Force‑sensitive PCs may feel “something watching.” |

## GM Tips for This Section

| **Tip** | **Why** |
|---------|---------|
| **Keep travel brisk.** Describe each scene, then move on. Don’t let navigation bog down. | Maintains pacing for a 3‑hour session. |
| **Use the bearsloth as a set‑piece.** Describe its movements, its roar, the silence before it attacks. | Creates memorable Star Wars action. |
| **Reward creative solutions.** Using the Force, climbing to safety, or negotiating with the Jairo should feel effective. | Encourages player agency. |
| **Foreshadow Marcolf’s tyranny.** The Jairo’s warnings and the fearful atmosphere of Quolas should echo each other. | Builds thematic consistency. |
| **Track resources.** If the PCs camp, they may use rations. This adds a survival element. | Makes the wilderness feel real. |
