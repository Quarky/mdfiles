# Narglatch
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 5, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 3, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 2, Discipline 2, Perception 1, Resilience 2, Stealth 2
**Talents:** None
**Abilities:** Fierce Pounce (gain Knockdown on next attack after using the Aim maneuver), Silhouette 2[reference:17]
**Soak:** 4
**Wound Threshold:** 25
**Equipment:** Claws (Brawl; Damage 9; Crit 2; Linked 1, Vicious 3)[reference:18]
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Narglatch)

A narglatch is a stealthy apex predator that can be found in diverse environments, from the swamps of Naboo to the tundra of Orto Plutonia[reference:19]. They are solitary hunters that move with preternatural silence, stalking their prey with incredible focus before a single, devastating pounce. The narglatch has a sleek, muscular build, powerful claws, and a fan-like tail that acts as a rudder at high speeds[reference:20], making it a terrifying ambush predator.
