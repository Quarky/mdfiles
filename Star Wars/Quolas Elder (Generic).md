# Quolas Elder (Generic)

**Type:** Rival  
**Role:** Respected family representative  
**Location:** Helshar or Tumris districts  
**Personality:** Dignified, cautious, tired of conflict. They remember a time before Marcolf and wish for peace but fear the cost.

## GM Notes
- Use this profile for any unnamed elder or for Koh/Odir if their specific profiles are not needed.
- Elders have the Inspiring Rhetoric talent, which can rally allies.
- They are key to brokering a truce between the clans.
