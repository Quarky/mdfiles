## 7. Charhound (Juvenile)

**Picture:** [Charhound Image](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/starwars/images/5/55/EmberCharhound-THR.jpg)  
**Role:** Guardian / Tracker  
**Source:** *Star Wars: The High Republic*  
**Type:** Rival  

| **Characteristic** | **Value** |
|-------------------|-----------|
| Brawn | 2 |
| Agility | 4 |
| Intellect | 1 |
| Cunning | 3 |
| Willpower | 2 |
| Presence | 2 |

**Skills:** Athletics 2, Brawl 1, Perception 2, Stealth 2, Survival 2  
**Talents:** None  
**Abilities:** Furnace Body (internal heat; deals 1 damage to anyone grappling it), Fire Breath (can exhale a short plume: Brawl; Damage 4; Critical 4; Burn 1), Silhouette 0  
**Soak:** 2  
**Wound Threshold:** 8  
**Equipment:** Claws (Brawl; Damage 3; Critical 4)

Small, lithe canines with muted grey and black fur and spots of red. Fiercely loyal and intelligent, a juvenile charhound can breathe fire, making it both a cute friend and a dangerous guardian.
