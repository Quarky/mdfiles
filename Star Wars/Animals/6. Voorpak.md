## 6. Voorpak

**Picture:** [Voorpak Image](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/starwars/images/f/fc/Voorpak.jpg)  
**Role:** Lap Pet / Social Companion  
**Source:** *Star Wars Resistance* & *The Bad Batch*  
**Type:** Minion  

| **Characteristic** | **Value** |
|-------------------|-----------|
| Brawn | 1 |
| Agility | 2 |
| Intellect | 1 |
| Cunning | 2 |
| Willpower | 1 |
| Presence | 3 |

**Skills:** Charm 1, Perception 1  
**Talents:** None  
**Abilities:** Endearing (any creature watching a voorpak gains +Boost to social checks to interact with its owner), Silhouette 0

**Wound Threshold:** 3

Small, energetic creatures that look like a cross between a cat and a dog, with fluffy coats and floppy ears. They are high-energy, loyal, and nearly irresistible in social situations.
