## 1. Vulptex (Crystal Fox)

**Picture:** [Vulptex Image](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/starwars/images/8/87/Vulptex_SWCT.png)  
**Role:** Tracker & Scout  
**Source:** *Star Wars: The Last Jedi*  
**Type:** Minion  

| **Characteristic** | **Value** |
|-------------------|-----------|
| Brawn | 1 |
| Agility | 3 |
| Intellect | 2 |
| Cunning | 3 |
| Willpower | 1 |
| Presence | 2 |

**Skills:** Athletics 1, Perception 2, Survival 2  
**Talents:** None  
**Abilities:** Crystalline Fur (+1 Soak, adds ■ to personal-scale blaster/laser attacks targeting the animal, except lightsabers), Silhouette 0  
**Soak:** 2  
**Wound Threshold:** 3  
**Equipment:** Teeth (Brawl; Damage 2; Critical 5; Pierce 1)

Small, white-furred foxes native to the mineral planet Crait, their most striking feature is the glittering, crystalline fur that covers their bodies. Curious and friendly, they have an uncanny knack for finding hidden entrances and alerting their masters to danger.
