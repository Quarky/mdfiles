## 2. Porg

**Picture:** [Porg Image](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/starwars/images/0/0c/Porg_TLJ.png)  
**Role:** Comic Relief / Lookout  
**Source:** *Star Wars: The Last Jedi*  
**Type:** Minion  

| **Characteristic** | **Value** |
|-------------------|-----------|
| Brawn | 1 |
| Agility | 3 |
| Intellect | 1 |
| Cunning | 2 |
| Willpower | 1 |
| Presence | 2 |

**Skills (group only):** Perception, Survival  
**Talents:** None  
**Abilities:** Flyer & Pelagic Avian (flying rules, can swim/dive without penalty), Silhouette 0  
**Soak:** 1  
**Wound Threshold:** 2  
**Equipment:** Bite (Brawl; Damage 3; Critical 5; Range [Engaged])

These charming, puffin-like seabirds show little fear of sentient species and are known for their curiosity and tendency to get into trouble. A porg is perfect for comic relief or as a watchful lookout.
