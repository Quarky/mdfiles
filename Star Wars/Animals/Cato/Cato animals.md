# CATO NEIMOIDIA CREATURE ROSTER – COMPLETE BESTIARY

Here’s a complete bestiary for the 20 creatures of **Cato Neimoidia**, formatted to match the *Star Wars: Force and Destiny* style (with stats, abilities, and game traits).  
Each entry notes whether the creature is **Star Wars canon** or original, gives a **Silhouette**, full profile, and the **Animal Empathy** difficulty and flavor.
