# Encounter Ideas for Act 1 (Arbooine Wilds)

Use these encounters to integrate the 20 creatures into your session. Each assumes the party is traveling through the ancient forests of Arbooine, heading toward Quolas or exploring its outskirts.

| Creature | Encounter Idea |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bearsloth** | A bearsloth has claimed a massive fallen elrit tree that blocks the main trail. It’s feeding on a tarvix carcass and will attack if approached. The party can sneak around (Stealth vs. Perception) or try to scare it off with fire or loud noises. |
| **Tarvix** | The party finds a wounded tarvix caught in a hunter’s snare. Its Sathari owner is nearby, unconscious after a fall. Healing the animal and the owner earns goodwill with a local clan, who may offer a guide to Quolas. |
| **Mudhorn** | A mudhorn has been driven from its wallow by something worse (a Bonzami). Now it rampages through a grove of edible fungi that the Sathari rely on. The party must drive it away or lure it elsewhere without killing it (if they want to avoid Conflict). |
| **Falumpaset** | A caravan of Sathari traders lost their falumpaset during a bearsloth attack. The beast is panicked and running in circles deeper in the woods. Calming it requires a Hard Survival or Cool check; success yields a reward and a ride to Quolas. |
| **Blurrg** | A wild blurrg has been stealing food from Sathari camps. The party tracks it to its lair—a small cave behind a waterfall. Inside, they find not only the blurrg but also a hidden Sathari shrine with a minor Force echo. |
| **Kaadu** | While crossing a river, a family of kaadu emerges from the water and blocks the party’s path. They are protecting their young. The party must find a way to cross without provoking them (e.g., using a rope bridge or moving upstream). |
| **Bonzami** | A Bonzami has taken up residence near the only safe passage through a ravine. The creature is old and scarred, with a missing eye. Locals whisper it’s been driven mad by pain. The party can fight it, find a dangerous alternate route, or attempt to sedate it using herbs and a Medicine check. |
| **Gundark** | The party hears terrified Sathari screams from a hunting party. A gundark has trapped three hunters in a dead-end canyon. The gundark is more curious than aggressive at first—it will watch the party before attacking. Clever roleplay or offering food might distract it. |
| **Manka Cat** | A pride of manka cats has been picking off lone travelers. The party is hired by a Sathari village to eliminate the threat. Tracking them leads to a moss-covered ruin where the cats have made a den. Inside, the party finds the remains of a Clone Wars soldier with a still-functioning datapad (lore about Warde). |
| **Narglatch** | While camping at night, the party wakes to absolute silence. A narglatch is circling the camp, waiting for someone to wander off alone. It will not attack a group by firelight. The encounter is about tension and perception checks to keep it at bay until dawn. |
| **Vine Cat** | The party finds a Sathari child stuck on a high branch, unable to climb down. A vine cat is stalking from a nearby tree. Rescuing the child requires a risky climb (Athletics) while the rest of the party distracts or fights the cat. |
| **Nexu** | The forest has an area of complete darkness under a dense canopy. A nexu hunts there, using its infrared vision. The party must navigate with glow rods or torches, which attract the nexu. Turning off lights allows stealth but risks stumbling into pits or venomous plants. |
| **Wraid** | A pack of wraid has been contaminated by a strange fungus that makes them hyper‑aggressive. Their usual fear of fire is gone. The party must find the source of the fungus (an old Separatist bio‑weapon canister) and destroy it to calm the beasts. |
| **Zakkeg** | The party finds a hidden valley where a zakkeg lairs. Locals say the creature is a “demon from the old wars.” If the party manages to avoid or pacify it (using the Force to calm it, not fight), they discover a crashed Republic supply shuttle with valuable gear and a clue to Warde’s path. |
| **Kroykah** | A Sathari elder has been exiled from Quolas for speaking against Marcolf. He now wanders the wilds, and a pack of kroykah stalk him. The party must find him before the kroykah do. The encounter is a race against time, with tracking checks and the sounds of howls getting closer. |
| **Reek** | A reek, escaped from an off‑world slaver’s transport, has been goring trees and destroying Sathari mushroom farms. The slavers want it back, but the Sathari want it dead. The party can choose sides or try to lead the reek into a ravine to trap it alive. |
| **Acklay** | Heavy rains flood a low area, and an acklay swims up from an underground river. It surfaces near the party’s camp, confused and hungry. The acklay isn’t hunting intentionally yet – the party can retreat quietly or fight. If they kill it, they find a corroded kyber crystal shard in its stomach (a clue for later). |
| **Terentatek** | (High‑level option / finale of Act 1) The party discovers a cave that once held a Sith artifact. A terentatek sleeps inside, drawn by the dark side residue. The party must steal the artifact (a holocron component?) without waking it. Any Force use risks waking the beast, forcing a desperate escape. |
| **Bantha** | A lost baby bantha wanders into the party’s camp, crying. Its mother is nearby but trapped in a sinkhole. Helping the mother free requires teamwork (Athletics, Mechanics to build a ramp). The bantha herd later appears when the party is in danger, trampling enemies. |
| **Mynock** | Deep in a cave system the party uses as a shortcut, a swarm of mynocks has drained the power cells of a crashed droid carrier. The cave is pitch black. The party’s glow rods flicker and die. They must navigate by memory and sound while mynocks try to latch onto their gear, draining system strain. |
