**Varactyl** – [Rival]  
*Source: Savage Spirits (Page 79) / Stay on Target (Page 89)*

These majestic reptavians are the preferred mounts of the Utai on Utapau, prized for their incredible speed and remarkable climbing abilities. They are known to be exceptionally loyal and obedient, willing to descend dangerous slopes at their rider's command.
