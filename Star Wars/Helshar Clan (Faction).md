# Helshar Clan (Faction)

**Type:** Noble family  
**Leader:** Koh Helshar  
**Key NPCs:** Pathran Helshar (resistance leader – unaffiliated officially)  
**Allies:** None permanent; potential ally to PCs if truce brokered  
**Rivals:** Tumris clan

## Overview
The Helshar clan is one of the two dominant families in Quolas. They control roughly half the city’s districts. Their feud with the Tumris clan predates Marcolf’s rule. Under Marcolf, both families have lost power, but the Helshars have endured largely through Koh’s patience. A younger generation, led by Pathran, is more willing to fight.

## Key Info for GMs
- The clan can provide food, shelter, and workers if befriended.
- They will not openly rebel unless Koh gives her blessing or Marcolf falls.
- Healing the feud with the Tumris clan is a major milestone (10 XP reward).
