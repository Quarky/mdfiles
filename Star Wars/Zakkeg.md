# Zakkeg
**Type:** Nemesis
**Characteristics:** Brawn 7, Agility 1, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 3, Presence 1
**Skills:** Brawl 3, Perception 2, Resilience 4, Vigilance 2
**Talents:** Adversary 2, Durable 3, Feral Strength 3, Lethal Blows 2
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2, Force Resistance (upgrades difficulty of Force powers targeting it twice)
**Soak:** 10
**Wound Threshold:** 45
**Equipment:** Claws and Bite (Brawl; Damage 12; Crit 2; Pierce 3, Vicious 3)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zakkeg)

The zakkeg is a walking nightmare: a massive, scaly quadruped whose thick, armor-like hide is nearly impervious to blaster fire and even resistant to lightsabers[reference:29]. Native to the jungle moon of Dxun, these terrifying omnivores are also highly resistant to the Force, making them a deadly threat to any Force-sensitive. A zakkeg is a legendary creature, serving as a superb "boss" encounter for high-level parties.
