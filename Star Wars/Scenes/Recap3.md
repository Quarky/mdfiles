Last episode our hero, Charlie, had an exciting new time with new friends.

After meeting a fog manta which Charlie had befriended, they had gone to a cafe to find out more about what was going on with the ward and how to find his crystal. 

There the cafe owner, a huge animal lover like Charlie, had talked about a friend of his that was having a hard time. The son had been enticed by a local gang and the father had been depressed. 

Charlie, in the lingering effect of his light side cookies, had convinced the dealer to have new life plans and then later convinced his son to have a change in life plans as well. 

After the happy son and father reunion, Charlie was invited to the owner's private Saint Wilderness sanctuary, where she had set up a little refuge for animals. 

After spending time in the refuge and getting information from the owner on some next steps, they had retired back to the hotel. 

Looking for a relaxing evening, it looked like this just would not happen as there was a crash and they had an attack by a Kree Hawk, which wanted to know why questions were being asked. 

The Creehawk was barricaded into a bathroom and then they had decided on their next steps.
