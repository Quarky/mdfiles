# 🌑 Overheard Argument With Odir  
*A GM‑ready Markdown scene where the Force‑sensitive PC slips into an animal’s senses and overhears Odir arguing with his daughter.*

---

# 🌿 Scene Setup
The PCs are moving through the Tumris district’s upper canopy.  
A **branch‑crawler** — a four‑eyed, squirrel‑like creature highly attuned to emotional tension — sits on a high limb overlooking a private gliding platform.

A Force‑sensitive PC feels a sudden spike in the Force:

> A pulse of anger.  
> A flare of fear.  
> A knot of grief.  
>  
> Something below is vibrating through the canopy.

Their senses slip.

---

# 🌬️ Force‑Sensing Through the Animal
The PC doesn’t choose to connect — the Force pulls them in because the creature is already overwhelmed.

> Your vision blurs.  
> The world tilts.  
>  
> Suddenly you’re seeing through **four eyes**,  
> claws gripping bark,  
> heart racing
> 
>You didn’t choose this.
The Force simply… opened a door.

The PC is now perceiving the world through the branch‑crawler.

---

# 🔥 The Argument Below  
On the platform beneath the branch, **Odir Tumris** stands rigid, feathers bristling, voice low but sharp.  
His daughter stands opposite him, trembling but defiant.

### **Odir (controlled fury):**
> “You will end this foolishness.  
> Pathran is Helshar — and Helshar is weakness.”

### **Daughter (voice shaking):**
> “He is not weakness.  
> He is hope.  
> And you’re so blinded by pride you can’t see it.”

### **Odir (stepping closer):**
> “Hope does not keep our clan alive.  
> Strength does.  
> And aligning yourself with Helshar makes you a liability.”

### **Daughter (tears in her voice, not her eyes):**
> “I’m not aligning with a clan.  
> I’m aligning with *someone I love*.”

Odir’s feathers flare — a sign of deep emotional conflict, not just anger.

### **Daughter (voice steady but shaking underneath):**
> “I’m telling you before the clan hears it from someone else.  
> I am performing the **choice‑kin ceremony**.”

Odir freezes.  
Not in shock — in **contained fury**.
### **Odir (low, dangerous):**
> “With *Pathran*.”

### **Daughter (defiant):**
> “He is someone I trust.  
> Someone who sees a future beyond this feud.”

Odir steps closer, feathers flaring — a sign of deep emotional conflict.

### **Odir:**
> “You will not bind yourself

### **Daughter (voice rising):**
> “You don’t get to choose my kin.  
> The ancestors do.  
> And they have made their will clear.”


---

# ⚡ Force Surge Through the Animal
The PC feels the creature’s instincts spike:

- fear  
- territorial alarm  
- protective instinct  

The PC’s emotions bleed into the animal’s, and the animal’s into the PC’s.

> A hot surge floods your limbs —  
> not your own, but the creature’s.  
>  
> It wants to leap.  
> To warn.  
> To flee.  
>  
> And for a heartbeat, you can’t tell  
> **who is influencing whom.**

The animal reacts.

---

# 🐾 The Animal Gives You Away
The branch‑crawler chitters sharply, claws scraping bark.

Both figures below freeze.

### **Odir (instantly alert):**
> “Someone’s listening.”

### **Daughter (fear rising):**
> “Father, please — it’s just an animal—”

### **Odir (cold):**
> “Nothing in this city is *just* anything anymore.”

The PC feels the creature’s panic spike again — then the connection snaps.

---

# 🌫️ Breaking the Connection
> The world lurches.  
> Four eyes become two.  
> The forest returns to its normal sounds.  
>  
> You’re yourself again —  
> but the echo of their argument clings to your mind like static.

---

# 🌱 What the PCs Now Know
- Odir is **furious** about the relationship.  
- His daughter is **in love** and refuses to back down.  
- Odir sees Pathran as a **threat to Tumris strength**, not a suitor.  
- The daughter sees Pathran as **hope for unity**, not politics.  
- Their bond is **real**, emotional, and dangerous.  
- This conflict is **deeply personal**, not strategic.
