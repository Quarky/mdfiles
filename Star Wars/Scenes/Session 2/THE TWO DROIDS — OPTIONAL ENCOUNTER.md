## THE TWO DROIDS — OPTIONAL ENCOUNTER

In the lower Commerce Level, near the junction with the residential district,
two BX commando droids are still active.

Their last orders were to hold this junction against Republic advance.
The Republic never came back. The droids have been holding the junction
for fifteen years.

They are not glitching. They are not malfunctioning. They are doing
exactly what they were told to do with the intelligence and adaptability
that made BX commandos genuinely dangerous. They have repaired each other
using parts from destroyed droids in the area. They have maintained their
weapons. They have adapted their patrol patterns to account for the
structural changes of fifteen years.

They will not attack first. They will challenge anyone who approaches the junction.

> **DROID CHALLENGE:**
> *"Identify yourself. This area is under Confederacy of Independent Systems*
> *military authority. Unauthorized personnel will be detained.*
> *State your affiliation and purpose."*

**The situation is genuinely complicated:**

- The CIS does not exist
- These droids do not know this
- They are not wrong that they were given orders
- They are not wrong that no one countermanded those orders
- They are, in a strange way, the most loyal soldiers of the Clone Wars still standing

**Possible resolutions:**

1. **Combat:** They are BX commandos. It is a real fight. They use the
   junction's architecture for cover intelligently. They have had fifteen years
   to study the terrain.

2. **Social/Deception:** A Hard Deception check claiming to be a CIS officer
   with orders to stand down the position. The droids will ask for codes.
   The codes no longer exist in any database but the party could attempt
   to fake them with a Hard Computers check.

3. **The honest approach:** Telling the droids the war is over.
   This requires a Daunting Discipline or Knowledge (Lore) check —
   not because the information is wrong but because the droids need
   to be convinced, not just told. They have been loyal for fifteen years.
   They will not simply accept a stranger's word. But they are not
   incapable of processing new information. They are BX commandos, not B1s.
   If convinced, they stand down. They ask what they are supposed to do now.
   This question has no good answer.

4. **The Pathfinder's approach:** She cannot talk to droids through animal empathy.
   But she might find a way around the junction entirely using the Treveel tunnels
   or the flooded route while Sigg handles the conversation.
   Sigg is genuinely uncertain how to handle this situation.
   He tells the droids the truth. He is not sure what else to do.

> *"The war ended fifteen years ago. The Confederacy lost.*
> *The Republic became the Empire. The people this city belonged to*
> *were made to leave. I'm sorry. I don't know what you're supposed*
> *to do with that information. Neither do I, most of the time."*
