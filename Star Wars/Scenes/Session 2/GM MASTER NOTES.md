## GM MASTER NOTES

### Pacing

The ventral city can be as long or as short as the session requires.
The essential path — down to the Ossivane, into the vault, back out —
can be accomplished in one session if the party moves with purpose.

A full exploration covering all districts takes two to three sessions.
The GM should let the players lead. If they want to explore every room,
let them. The ventral city rewards exploration. If they are moving fast,
streamline the districts to key beats and discovered documents.

### The Pathfinder's Experience

The ventral city is unusual for the Pathfinder in that it is full of animals —
and those animals have been here long enough to have developed distinct
personalities and territorial relationships. She is not walking through
an abandoned city. She is walking through a city that belongs to its
animals now, and they have their own society here.

The Rusted Stalker is the apex of this society. Everything else has
accommodated to its presence. If she has bonded it, she is walking
through a city that has, in a very real sense, accepted her as part of
its structure. This should feel meaningful.

### The Moral Weight

Everything the party finds in the ventral city is evidence of what Warde damaged
and what the Empire wants erased. The GM should not underline this — the objects
do the work. But by the time the party climbs back to the upper city with Jaral's
war bonds in hand, they should feel the full weight of what happened here.

This is Act 2's emotional core. The holocron gives them Warde's side of the story.
The ventral city gives them everyone else's.
