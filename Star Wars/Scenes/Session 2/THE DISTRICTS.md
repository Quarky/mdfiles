## THE DISTRICTS

### DISTRICT 1: THE UPPER CONCOURSE
**Levels 1-3 below bridge surface**

The transition zone between upper and lower city. This is where services existed —
maintenance facilities, worker housing, administrative offices. Less beautiful than
the residential districts below, more utilitarian. The architecture here is standard
bridge-city construction without the organic embellishment of the lower levels.

**Condition:** Moderate damage. Structurally the most sound part of the ventral city
because it was built most recently and most practically. Some sections have been
used by squatters over the years — there are signs of recent (within five years)
habitation. Ash marks from fires. Improvised shelving. Someone's blanket.

**What can be found here:**

- Imperial notices posted on walls ordering the area cleared, dated Year 3 post-war.
  Some have been torn down. Some have graffiti on them in Pak Pak (Neimoidian language).
  A Knowledge (Xenology) check translates one as: *"We were here first."*
- A maintenance office with intact filing cabinets. The files are water-damaged but
  partially readable. They contain maintenance schedules, complaint logs from residents,
  requests for repairs. A Perception check finds a complaint log entry from six months
  before the war: *"Resident Kuto Jaral (Unit 7, Ossivane) reports persistent vibration
  in southwest wall. Cause unidentified. Request structural assessment."*
  (The vibration was from the early Republic bombardment targeting the factories.
  No one connected it at the time.)
- A Stone Hopper family has colonized a storage room. The mother has pups.
  The Pathfinder will know they are there before anyone opens the door.

**GM Note:** The upper concourse is where the party first acclimates to the ventral city.
Give them time here. Let them find things. Establish that this place is full of information
before they go deeper.

### DISTRICT 2: THE COMMERCE LEVEL
**Levels 3-5 below bridge surface**

The ventral city had its own small commercial district — not Trade Federation commerce,
but the everyday commerce of a residential community. Restaurants, tailors, a small
medical facility, a school, a community center, several small temples to the ancestors.

This was the social heart of the ventral city. More people passed through here than
anywhere else.

**Condition:** Significant damage. The commerce level was directly below several
factory installations and absorbed a great deal of the fighting. Several buildings
have partially collapsed. The street level is navigable but requires care.
The school building survived. The medical facility did not.

**THE SCHOOL — KEY LOCATION**

The school building is three stories. The ground floor collapsed during the fighting
but the upper two floors are accessible via an external staircase that is damaged
but passable (Average Athletics).

The upper floor classrooms are intact. Fifteen years of abandonment have not been
kind — desks are overturned, the windows are broken, and moss has begun growing
along the interior walls — but the murals painted by the students are still visible.

> *The murals run the full length of the corridor wall, done in bright pigment
> by small hands — the scale of them is slightly off, the way children draw things,
> everything a little too large or a little too small. They show the Neimoidian
> homeworld: the great hatchery lakes, the elders tending the grub pools,
> families traveling between the purse worlds on trading ships. In one panel
> a group of children are shown playing in what is clearly this city — the bridge
> architecture is recognizable, the canyon visible in the background.*
>
> *Someone has added to the mural after the fact, in different pigment,
> adult handwriting. Along the bottom of the homeworld panel, in small careful
> letters in Basic: "They did not deserve what happened to them."*
>
> *The handwriting does not match any Trade Federation era style.
> It is recent — within the last few years.*

**Who wrote it?** The GM can decide. Possibilities: Tacker, on a solitary visit.
A Neimoidian resident with access. Tieka Dalken. It is not necessary to resolve this.
The mystery is the point.

**What else is in the school:**

- A classroom with student work still on the walls — mathematical exercises
  (Neimoidian education heavily emphasized economics and logistics from an early age),
  illustrated reports on Trade Federation history, one child's drawing of their
  family labeled in Pak Pak. A Knowledge (Xenology) check reads it as:
  *"My father, my mother, my clutch-sister, our home."*
- A teacher's desk with a grade book. The last entry is three days before
  the Republic assault. The grades are meticulous. The last note in the margin
  reads (translated): *"Unusual quiet today. Children can feel something is wrong.
  Did not teach the scheduled lesson. We talked instead about the homeworld.*
  *I think that was the right choice."*
- A Neimoidian Nightcrawler colony has established itself in the basement.
  Their bioluminescence leaks through the floor cracks, making the ground
  floor glow faintly blue. It is beautiful and slightly eerie.
  The Pathfinder can sense them clearly.

**THE COMMUNITY CENTER**

A large single-story building, partially collapsed on the east side, intact on the west.
The intact section contains what was a meeting hall. The chairs are still arranged
in concentric circles — Neimoidian meeting culture preferred circular arrangements
to emphasize equal participation.

On the wall behind the meeting dais there is a large carved relief showing the founding
of the ventral city, with Neimoidian text and Basic translation (added later for
off-world business visitors).

The Basic translation reads:

> *"We built downward because we are not afraid of the depths.*
> *We built in the air because we are not afraid of heights.*
> *We built together because we are not afraid of each other.*
> *This is Jorra-Below. This is ours."*

**GM Note:** Read this aloud if the party spends time in this room.
Let it land. The party is in the middle of investigating Suljo Warde's crimes
against these people. This is the full weight of what he damaged.

**ANIMALS IN THE COMMERCE LEVEL:**

- Arch Serpents have colonized the restaurant district — they like the old
  kitchen warmth that still lingers near the industrial cooking equipment.
  Several nest in the ventilation shafts.
- A pair of Stone Hoppers have made a den in the community center's storage room.
- The Nightcrawler colony in the school basement has a satellite group
  in the medical facility ruins.

### DISTRICT 3: THE RESIDENTIAL MID-LEVELS
**Levels 5-8 below bridge surface**

The main residential district. This is where most of the ventral city's population lived.
Apartment blocks ranging from modest to comfortable, organized around small communal
courtyards with garden spaces.

**Condition:** Heavily damaged. The flooding three years ago hit here worst.
Ground level is impassable in most sections — standing water knee to hip deep,
possibly deeper in the interior courtyards. Upper floors are accessible but
structurally questionable.

**NAVIGATION OPTIONS:**

The flooding creates a routing challenge. There are three ways through this district:

1. **High Route:** Travel via the external walkways and bridges connecting the upper
   floors of the apartment blocks. These bridges were designed for convenience not
   weight-bearing — Average Coordination per crossing, Hard in sections where
   the bridge has partially separated from the wall.

2. **Water Route:** Wade through the flooded ground level. Slow, cold, and loud.
   The water hides debris — every twenty meters, Average Resilience or suffer
   1 strain from cold and minor injury. Also: the Mist Eel population
   has moved into the flooded sections. They are not dangerous to large creatures
   but their electrical field activity can interfere with electronics.
   Comlinks and scanners are unreliable in the flooded zone.

3. **Treveel Tunnels:** If the party has bonded a Treveel, or befriended one here,
   it can guide them through maintenance tunnels that run above the flood line.
   These tunnels are narrow (silhouette 1 creatures only, or silhouette 0 with
   gear removed) but dry and direct. The Treveel treats this as completely
   routine. The party may not.

**WHAT CAN BE FOUND:**

Walking the upper bridges of the residential district is like walking through
a city frozen mid-moment. Through windows:

- An apartment where the table is still set for a meal. The food is long gone
  but the dishes remain. Three place settings. The chairs are pushed back
  as if the occupants stood up quickly.
- An apartment where someone tried to take things with them and gave up
  halfway through — half-packed containers, items sorted into piles,
  abandoned on the floor.
- An apartment where the wall is covered in a hand-drawn calendar.
  Someone was tracking the days. The calendar continues for
  approximately six months after the evacuation date, then stops.
  Who was counting? The apartment shows no signs of habitation
  after the initial evacuation. The calendar was drawn before the war.
  Someone planned to be here for six months. They were not.

**FOUND DOCUMENTS — RESIDENTIAL DISTRICT:**

The GM should distribute these as the party explores. Each one is a fragment of a life.

> **Document 1 — Water-damaged letter, partially legible:**
> *"...cannot tell you when we will return. The situation in the upper city is...
> ...your father says it will be resolved quickly but I think...
> ...please keep the grubs warm and do not let them near the edge of...
> ...I love you all. We will be home soon."*
> *(The letter was never sent. It was found on a desk.)*

> **Document 2 — Child's drawing, remarkably well preserved inside a sealed container:**
> A detailed crayon rendering of the view from a lower residential window —
> the canyon, the mist, a tiny figure in the foreground identified by an arrow
> and the word (translated from Pak Pak): *"Me."*

> **Document 3 — Business ledger, partially readable:**
> Trade routes, cargo manifests, the ordinary commercial activity of a
> Trade Federation logistics officer. Buried in the middle of a routine
> entry, in different ink, clearly added later: *"They came today.*
> *We are leaving tonight. I am going to try to finish this entry tomorrow.*
> *I do not think I am going to finish this entry tomorrow."*

> **Document 4 — Personal journal, water-damaged:**
> Most illegible. One passage survives: *"The Jedi came again today.*
> *The tall one, the one with the strange eyes. He arrested Preem's father.*
> *No one knows why. Preem is eight years old. She stood in the street*
> *and watched and did not cry. I think she was too frightened to cry.*
> *I think we all are."*
> *(This is a reference to Warde. "The tall one, the one with the strange eyes"
> matches his description. This is the kind of thing the party can find
> that makes the abstract story of his crimes specific and human.)*

**ANIMALS IN THE RESIDENTIAL MID-LEVELS:**

- The flooding has attracted a Mist Eel population — visible as faint
  electrical shimmer in the water
- A Rusted Stalker secondary den is in the upper floors of one apartment block —
  the party may find its cached food stores (mostly birds and small mammals,
  disturbingly tidy)
- Bridge Geckos are everywhere on the exterior walls — the most numerous
  animal in this section

### DISTRICT 4: THE DEEP RESIDENTIAL
**Levels 8-11 below bridge surface**

The prestigious lower levels. Larger apartments, better construction, more
elaborate architecture. This is where Kuto Jaral's estate — the Ossivane — is located.

**Condition:** Paradoxically better than the mid-levels in some ways. The deep
residential was built to higher standards and the flooding from three years ago
did not reach below Level 9 (the canyon winds created enough air pressure
to hold the water line). The damage here is from the war itself — blaster scarring,
a few structural collapses from ordnance — and from fifteen years of abandonment.

**THE APPROACH:**

Coming down from the mid-levels the party notices the architecture changing.
The ceilings get higher. The corridors get wider. The detailing gets more intricate —
carved border work on every door frame, mosaic floor patterns, small alcoves
for ancestor sculptures (most empty now, the sculptures taken during evacuation
or stolen since).

The view changes too. At Level 9 the first canyon-facing windows appear —
large, floor to ceiling, perfectly positioned to frame the mist and the
distant forest far below. In the right conditions (dawn, or when the
canyon thermals are running) you can see the Fog Rays from here,
drifting in the updrafts.

> *This is what they built down here for. Standing at one of these windows
> you understand immediately why someone would spend a fortune
> to live at this depth. The upper city does not look like this.
> The upper city looks like a city. This looks like the edge of the world.*

**FOUND IN THE DEEP RESIDENTIAL:**

The abandoned estates of the deep residential are richer in objects than
the mid-levels because the wealthier residents had more to leave behind
and less time to take it.

> **Estate 1 — Unlocked, door open:**
> A large apartment, clearly belonging to a high Trade Federation officer.
> The walls are hung with framed certificates of commendation from the
> Trade Federation Council, a portrait of Nute Gunray, navigation charts
> of major trade routes. In the study, a desk with a locked drawer.
> Hard Skulduggery opens it. Inside: a personal journal in excellent condition
> (the drawer is sealed and waterproof) describing the last month before
> the Republic assault. The final entry, two days before the assault:
> *"We have been told to trust in the droid armies. I have seen the droid armies.*
> *I am packing tonight."*

> **Estate 2 — Partially collapsed, accessible through broken wall:**
> A family residence. Children's rooms on the far side — still decorated,
> toys on shelves, a mobile hanging from the ceiling still turning slowly
> in the air current from the broken wall. On the child's desk, an
> unfinished letter to a friend on the homeworld:
> *"I am going to tell you about my room because you have never been here.*
> *From my window I can see the clouds..."*
> *The letter ends there.*

> **Estate 3 — Locked (Hard Skulduggery):**
> An art collector's residence. The art is still here — sculptures, paintings,
> ceramics. All Neimoidian work, all pre-war. Most of it is damaged —
> a pipe burst in the far room and the water destroyed a third of the collection.
> What remains is extraordinary. This is not loot — it is cultural heritage
> sitting in the dark getting slowly destroyed.
> **GM Note:** What the party does with this information is a choice.
> They could tell no one. They could tell Tacker, who might be able
> to quietly ensure it is protected. They could tell Jaral, who would
> know who to contact in the Neimoidian community. They could tell
> Duke Irbian, which would be catastrophic for the collection and
> good for Irbian's career. They could take something, which is theft
> and worth Conflict.

**ANIMALS IN THE DEEP RESIDENTIAL:**

- The Rusted Stalker's primary den is in the Ossivane building itself —
  specifically in the basement level adjacent to the vault
- A Canyon Manta pair nests on the external platforms of Level 10 —
  enormous creatures, their wing-edges occasionally brushing the windows
  of the deep residential as they bank in the thermals. They have been
  here for years. The ventral city is theirs as much as anyone's now.
- A small Loros herd has somehow established itself on Level 9 —
  grazing on the moss and vine growth that has colonized the old garden
  terraces. They are completely peaceful and deeply incongruous.
  The Pathfinder will have feelings about the Loros.

### THE OSSIVANE — KUTO JARAL'S ESTATE BUILDING
**Level 10-11, Deep Residential District**

A twelve-unit residential block, curved facade following the outer edge of the bridge,
every unit with floor-to-ceiling canyon-facing windows. Built approximately sixty years
before the war, maintained to an exceptional standard — the Ossivane was considered
one of the finest addresses in the ventral city.

**Exterior Condition:**
The facade is intact but weathered. The main entrance — a carved stone archway with
Trade Federation symbols above it — is open, the doors long since rusted off their hinges.
Vine growth has colonized the left side of the building and is spreading across the
upper floors. One corner of the roof has partially collapsed. The rest is sound.

**Interior Condition:**
The lobby is grand in the bones — high ceiling, mosaic floor, a reception desk that
was staffed around the clock before the war. The mosaic on the floor shows a stylized
map of the Neimoidian purse worlds, Cato Neimoidia at the center. Half of it has been
obscured by the debris from the roof collapse. The other half is still clear and
remarkably beautiful.

> *The stones are the deep greens and golds of the Trade Federation at its height.*
> *Someone swept this floor. That is the first thing that strikes you —*
> *in a building that has been abandoned for fifteen years, the undamaged*
> *half of this mosaic is clean. Something has been keeping it clear of debris.*

**What swept it:** The Rusted Stalker. It has no idea why it does this.
It has been doing it for about three years. Animal behavior is sometimes
inexplicable even through the Force. The Pathfinder, if bonded with the Stalker,
will find this inexplicable and deeply endearing.

**THE FLOORS:**

The Ossivane has eleven residential floors plus a basement/sublevel.
Most units are on floors 1-10. Jaral's unit — the lowest, with the best view — is
on floor 11, but his vault is in the basement sublevel below floor 1.

- **Floors 1-5:** Accessible, moderate water damage, intact enough to navigate.
  Units mostly empty of anything valuable — stripped by looters in the years
  immediately after the war, before the Imperial prohibition locked things down.
- **Floors 6-9:** Intact, less looted because fewer people made it this deep.
  Several units still fully furnished. This is where the best found documents
  and objects are.
- **Floor 10:** The Rusted Stalker's active territory. The door to the stairwell
  at Floor 10 has deep claw marks on both sides. The Stalker is not currently
  here if the party has already bonded it — it is with them — but its
  scent marking is everywhere and will make any other animal or creature
  aware that something large and territorial has been through here recently.
- **Floor 11 — Jaral's Unit:** The unit at the bottom. The door is locked
  (Jaral's combination does not open this — the lock is a physical deadbolt,
  Average Skulduggery). Inside: a large apartment, beautifully positioned,
  the windows looking directly out over the canyon. Completely stripped.
  Jaral's family took everything they could carry and fifteen years of
  sporadic looters took most of the rest. What remains is the furniture —
  too heavy to move — and the view.
  
  > *The windows are floor to ceiling and they have not broken.*
  > *The canyon is right there — the mist, the thermals, the distant*
  > *suggestion of forest far below. A Canyon Manta drifts past at*
  > *eye level, close enough to see the texture of its wing surface.*
  > *This was someone's home. This was someone's view every morning.*
  > *You understand exactly what they lost.*

**THE BASEMENT/SUBLEVEL:**

Accessed via a service staircase behind the lobby reception desk.
One flight down. The stairs are solid.

The basement is utilitarian — storage units for each apartment, maintenance equipment,
the building's power junction (long dead). Half the basement is open storage,
half is divided into individual locked units for each resident.

Jaral's storage unit is Unit 12, in the far corner. The outer door has been breached —
the lock was forced, probably by early looters. The door hangs open. The storage unit
beyond the outer door is empty of anything obviously valuable. The looters took what
they could see.

**The Inner Vault:**

Behind a false back panel in the storage unit — Average Perception to notice
the panel, or the Treveel immediately scratches at it if bonded —
is Jaral's personal vault. A recessed door, combination lock.

**4-7-2-1-9.**

The lock turns. The door opens.

> *The vault is approximately two meters by two meters. It smells like*
> *sealed paper and old metal and something faintly floral — the wine,*
> *or the materials of the objects within. A small chemical desiccant*
> *in the corner has kept the humidity controlled for fifteen years.*
> *Everything Jaral told you is here. The shelves are arranged carefully,*
> *everything labeled in small neat handwriting.*
>
> *On the center shelf, the crystal sculpture catches your light*
> *and throws small rainbows across the vault walls.*
>
> *On the inner shelf, behind the locked drawer — the combination*
> *is the same as the outer vault — the war bonds are bundled in red*
> *ribbon. The paper is still crisp. The Confederacy seal is still bright.*
> *They are absolutely worthless and absolutely intact.*
