## SIGG IN THE VENTRAL CITY

Sigg was never in the ventral city during his previous posting. He saw it from above.
He thought about it. He never went down.

Being here now, with the party, he is quieter than usual. He does his job —
he navigates, he assesses structural risk, he handles the droids if that encounter occurs —
but he is processing something.

At some point, probably in the residential district, possibly at one of the canyon-view
windows, he says:

> *"I spent six weeks in the upper city trying to understand what happened here.*
> *I read the reports. I talked to people. I understood it intellectually.*
> *This is different."*

He does not elaborate. He does not need to.

If the party finds the journal entry about "the tall one with the strange eyes" —
the reference to Warde arresting a child's father — Sigg reads it if shown.
He is quiet for a long moment. Then:

> *"This is what we're actually doing here. Not the crystals. This."*

He is not wrong.
