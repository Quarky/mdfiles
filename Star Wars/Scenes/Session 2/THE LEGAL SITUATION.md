## THE LEGAL SITUATION

Access to the ventral city is prohibited under Imperial Decree 7-Neimoidia-C, issued four years
after the end of the Clone Wars. The official reason is structural safety. The actual reasons are
multiple:

- The Empire does not want Neimoidians reclaiming property and reasserting pre-war
  economic identities
- The ventral city contains evidence of Trade Federation activity the Empire prefers
  remain buried
- Several Imperial officers have quietly looted the more accessible sections and
  prefer no witnesses

There are three main crossing points from the upper city to the ventral city:

### Crossing Point A: The Transit Hub
**The Magrail Terminal — Bridge Core Level 7**

The magrail that serves Southside Prison runs through the bridge core and technically passes
a ventral junction at Level 7. The terminal is staffed and monitored. The prison train runs
on a schedule. The junction door is sealed with an Imperial lock.

- **To use legitimately:** Impossible without Imperial authorization
- **To bypass:** Hard Skulduggery or Computers check to deal with the lock.
  The terminal has two guards on rotating shift. The shift change is every four hours.
  The window between one guard leaving and the next arriving is approximately
  three minutes if the schedule holds, which it usually does not.
- **Syndicate knowledge:** The Whitescar Syndicate used this route for eight months.
  Hasar's people know the schedule irregularities. If the party has any Syndicate
  contact, this information is available.

### Crossing Point B: The Maintenance Descent
**Structural Access Shaft 14-C — Factory District Edge**

A maintenance shaft runs from the factory district floor down through the bridge superstructure
and emerges in the upper ventral concourse. It was used by maintenance workers before the war
and by Syndicate runners more recently. It is not guarded but it is not easy.

- **Physical route:** Average Athletics to navigate the shaft itself.
  The lower section is partially collapsed — Hard Athletics or Coordination
  to get through the collapse without injury.
- **What's in the shaft:** A Tension Cable Bat colony has taken up residence
  in the middle section. They are not aggressive but the space is tight and
  dark and full of bats. The Pathfinder handles this with no difficulty.
  Other party members may find it memorable.
- **At the bottom:** The shaft emerges behind a collapsed wall in the upper
  ventral concourse. The wall has to be moved — Average Athletics or
  the Arch Crab, if bonded, solves this entirely.

### Crossing Point C: The External Descent
**The Open Air — Bridge Exterior**

The bridge has exterior maintenance ladders and cable access points on its underside.
In theory a person could descend from the exterior of the bridge to the ventral city's
external platforms. In practice this is extremely dangerous and extremely illegal.

- **Visibility:** Anyone looking up from the upper city or the Ducal Palace can see
  someone on the exterior. This route is best attempted at night or in heavy fog.
  Cato Neimoidia obliges with fog approximately sixty percent of the time.
- **Physical route:** The descent is roughly forty meters of ladder and cable work.
  Average Athletics per section, Hard in high wind.
- **The reward:** This route bypasses all security entirely and emerges directly
  on an exterior platform of the ventral residential district — very close to
  the Ossivane. It is also the route the Canyon Manta uses as a thermal updraft.
  If the Pathfinder has bonded a Canyon Manta this crossing becomes something
  else entirely.
- **GM Note:** A party that descends this way in the fog, possibly riding a Canyon
  Manta, arrives in the ventral city having seen it from the outside first —
  the whole inverted skyline, the crumbling towers, the mist. This is the best
  possible arrival scene. Give it the description it deserves.
