## THE SYNDICATE REMNANT — OPTIONAL COMPLICATION

Eight months ago the Whitescar Syndicate was running a small storage operation
in the Commerce Level of the ventral city — using it as a dead drop and temporary
warehouse for contraband moving through Jorra. They abandoned it when an Imperial
patrol got too close to the access shaft.

They left things behind.

**The Syndicate Cache** is in the back room of what was once a restaurant on Commerce Level,
now accessible through a collapsed wall. It contains:

- Four crates of spice (medium quantity, Syndicate-marked)
- A datapad with an encrypted manifest (Hard Computers to decrypt —
  reveals shipment routes and drop points throughout Jorra)
- Two disassembled blaster rifles
- A Syndicate comm unit, inactive but potentially reactivatable
- A handwritten note in Lor Keln's handwriting:
  *"Leave the crates. This location is burned. Do not return.*
  *Hasar will sort it."*

**What the party does with this is a choice** with significant downstream consequences:

| Action | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Take the spice | Syndicate notices when they eventually return to clear it, party becomes persons of interest |
| Take the datapad | Major intelligence asset, Syndicate will want it back |
| Leave everything | Status quo, Syndicate eventually retrieves it |
| Destroy it | Costs the Syndicate significant resources, they investigate |
| Report it to Tacker | Creates an interesting dynamic between Tacker and his Imperial obligations |
| Tell Hasar about it | Earns significant Syndicate goodwill, is also somewhat self-incriminating |
