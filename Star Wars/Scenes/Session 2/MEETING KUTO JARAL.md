## MEETING KUTO JARAL

### Who He Is

**Kuto Jaral** is a Neimoidian male in his late sixties. He was once a mid-ranking logistics officer
for the Trade Federation — not powerful enough to have been a true player, but comfortable enough
to have lived well. He had an estate on the underside of Jorra's bridge, in what was then one of
the most desirable residential districts on Cato Neimoidia. The ventral city was cooler than the
upper bridge, quieter, and the views from the lower levels looked out over the misty canyon forests
below. Wealthy Neimoidians prized it for exactly those reasons.

When the Clone Wars came to Jorra, Kuto evacuated to the upper city with what he could carry.
He left behind almost everything. He has spent fifteen years telling himself he would go back.
He has not gone back.

He now lives in a small two-room apartment in the commercial district, upper city. He shares it
with his adult daughter **Mira**, who has never seen the ventral city and does not understand why
her father cannot let it go. She is not hostile to the party — she is just tired of watching him
grieve a place.

### Where to Find Him

He is not hard to find if the party is asking around about people who lost property in the ventral
city. Sanna Olpan knows him. The proprietor at Torna's Cafe has seen him. He posts on the
community job board at the cafe occasionally — always the same listing, always slightly different
reward, always no takers.

> **JOB BOARD POSTING — handwritten on a small card:**
> *"Recovery of personal items from lower residential district, pre-war estate. Access required.*
> *Hazardous. Generous compensation for success. Ask for K. Jaral, room 4, Merchant's Rest.*
> *I am serious about the compensation. I have been waiting a long time."*

### First Meeting

Jaral's apartment is cluttered with Trade Federation-era objects — not valuable ones, the things
that were easy to carry. Framed certificates. A model of a starship he once helped route through
his logistics network. A holographic portrait of his family on the estate, taken maybe twenty years
ago. In the portrait the ventral city is visible through tall windows behind them, the canyon
forests below glowing green in the afternoon light.

He is formal at first. He has had people express interest before and then decline when they
understood what "hazardous" meant. He does not waste time.

> *"My estate is in the lower residential section of the ventral city, six levels below the bridge
> surface. The building is called the Ossivane — it was a private residence block, twelve units,
> all owned by Trade Federation personnel. I owned the lowest unit, which had the best view.*
>
> *I left a number of items in the vault on sublevel two. I was going to go back for them.
> The war made that impossible. The Empire made it illegal. I am old and I am not going back
> myself. But I want those things back before I die.*
>
> *I will pay five hundred credits per item recovered. There are eleven items. I have a list."*

He produces a handwritten list. It is meticulous. Every item described in precise detail.

> *"The war bonds are worthless. The Confederacy does not exist. But my wife bought them.*
> *She believed in the cause. I did not argue with her. I want them back."*

He pauses.

> *"Everything on that list has monetary value of essentially zero. I am aware of this.*
> *That is not the point."*

### What He Knows About the Route

Jaral has not been to the ventral city in fifteen years but he spent years there before the war.
He can provide:

- A hand-drawn map of the ventral residential district as it was before the war
- The address of the Ossivane estate block
- Knowledge that the main transit route — the Descending Concourse — was partially
  destroyed but structurally survived
- Warning that the lower levels flooded during a storm cycle three years ago
  and may still have standing water
- The vault combination: **4-7-2-1-9** (he recites it from memory without hesitation.
  He has been reciting it to himself for fifteen years.)

### What He Does Not Know

- That the Rusted Stalker has made the Ossivane its primary den
- That a small Whitescar Syndicate storage operation was running in the ventral city
  until about eight months ago and left things behind when they abandoned it
- That two BX commando droids from the war are still active in the lower concourse
  and have been for fifteen years
- That his vault was partially breached — the outer door is damaged — but the inner
  drawer where the most valuable personal items are stored is intact

### GM Note — Jaral as a Thread

Jaral is not a quest giver and then done. He is a person who lost something that mattered.
If the party brings back items, each one is a scene. He does not effusively thank them.
He holds each object quietly. The locket gets the longest silence.

If the party brings back the war bonds and mentions they are worthless, he says:

> *"I know. She knew too, I think, by the end. She bought them because she wanted to
> believe the galaxy could be different. I thought she was foolish. I was wrong about
> that."*

He does not elaborate.
