# Clone Wars Era Droid & Vehicle Statistics

This guide compiles droid and vehicle statistics for the Clone Wars era, drawn from official Fantasy Flight Games sourcebooks and the adventure module. For any stat not directly sourced from an official book, I've marked it as **homebrew** or **estimated** for your reference.

---
