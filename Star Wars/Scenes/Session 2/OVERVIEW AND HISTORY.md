## OVERVIEW AND HISTORY

The ventral city of Jorra was built over approximately three hundred years as the Trade
Federation's wealth on Cato Neimoidia grew. The upper bridge surface was for commerce and
administration. The ventral surface was for living.

At its peak the ventral city had a population of approximately forty thousand — all Neimoidian,
all wealthy or in service to the wealthy. It was organized into distinct districts running along
the underside of the bridge from the north arch to the south arch. The deepest point, nearest
the canyon below, was the most prestigious — the further down you built, the better your view,
and the more you had spent to get there.

### What the Ventral City Tells You About Neimoidian Culture

Neimoidians are often characterized by their avarice and cowardice. The ventral city tells
a more complicated story.

The architecture is beautiful. Not in a showy, competitive way — in a way that suggests people
who had thought carefully about what beauty meant to them. The buildings are organic in their
lines, curved rather than angular, built to maximize the view of the canyon and the mist below.
Public spaces are numerous — small squares with fountains (dry now), covered walkways that
would have been cool and pleasant, communal gardens planted with species from the Neimoidian
homeworld.

The art is everywhere. Carvings in the stone, mosaics in the floors of public buildings,
painted murals on interior walls — many still visible beneath the water damage and the growth.
Neimoidian art of this period is primarily abstract, using geometric patterns to represent
concepts: wealth as interlocking circles, family as nested triangles, the homeworld as a
vertical line with roots extending downward.

There are children's spaces. Small playgrounds, low benches sized for grubs, a school building
near the residential district with painted walls showing scenes of Neimoidian history.
The murals in the school are the best preserved because the school is on a high level and
avoided the flooding.

The ventral city was a home. This is what the Empire wants forgotten.
