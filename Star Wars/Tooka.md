# Tooka
**Role:** Symbiote / Moral Support
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tooka)
**Type:** Minion
**Characteristics:** Brawn 1, Agility 3, Intellect 2, Cunning 3, Willpower 2, Presence 3
**Skills:** Coordination 2, Stealth 2, Vigilance 1
**Talents:** None
**Abilities:** Silhouette 0; Keen Senses (adds Boost die to Perception checks for detection)
**Soak:** 1
**Wound Threshold:** 4
**Equipment:** Claws (Brawl; Damage 1; Crit 5; Range [Engaged])
**Picture:** [Tooka Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tooka?file=Tooka_SWCT.png)

The tooka is a common sight across the galaxy: a small, furry, long-tailed creature with a pointed face and a habit of perching on shoulders. While they offer little in combat, they are fiercely loyal and have an uncanny knack for sensing danger. A tooka's presence can ease the strain of long journeys through the wilderness, and their hunting instincts make them excellent at keeping a camp free of vermin.
