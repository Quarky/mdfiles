# Cast, Factions, Creatures, and Agendas

## Suljo Warde

Wants the future to be fixed because a fixed death excuses decades of fear. Fears evidence that he chose his isolation and can still choose differently. Performs calm certainty, watches compassion, and provokes execution. Never play him as a cackling stranger; he is a former teacher who made prophecy into a prison.

## Gatekeeper

Wants its students prepared and Warde recovered if possible. Fears repeating its creator’s certainty. It differentiates memory, inference, and hope and should sometimes be wrong without becoming useless.

## Onrein Hasar / Lor Keln / Kreehawk gangers

Hasar wants a viable return and something valuable enough to justify losses. Keln wants decisive violence before authority slips. Gangers vary; give at least one a wound, doubt, loyalty, or practical skill that matters beyond combat.

## Nex Pavros alternative

Pavros wants a collectable outcome and a reputation for finishing contracts. The hunter studies relationships and offers bargains that reveal priorities. Use instead of Hasar’s group when the campaign established a bounty-hunter pursuit.

## Moraband creatures and remnants

Skitterers scavenge; dust-stalkers hunt vibration; phantasmal beasts manufacture encirclement; Sith phantasms tempt and accuse; tomb sentinels enforce old covenants; echo-bound seekers carry incomplete, morally complicated histories. None exists solely as a combat bag of wounds.
