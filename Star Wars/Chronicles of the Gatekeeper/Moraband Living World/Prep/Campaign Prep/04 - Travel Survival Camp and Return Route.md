# Travel, Survival, Camp, and Return Route

## Travel procedure

For each meaningful leg: establish distance and weather; ask who navigates, scouts, carries vulnerable supplies, and watches rivals; offer two routes with distinct costs; resolve at most one primary travel check; apply symbols; give one living-world beat; update the return route.

## Supplies

Track water, shelter, light/power, medical capacity, climbing/recovery gear, and communications. Use condition tags—secure, strained, depleted—rather than counting every ration unless the table enjoys logistics.

## Camp

Camp creates scenes: watch conversations, animal reactions, equipment maintenance, rival negotiations, dreams, and disagreements. A successful camp should provide recovery and information. Threat during camp changes privacy, security, or rest; it does not automatically trigger combat.

## Getting back

Marked routes, repaired shelters, allies, a restored Delta-7 signal, and spared creatures can all ease departure. Destroyed bridges, open contracts, awakened sentinels, and abandoned companions complicate it. Resolve the return in an epilogue unless new decisions make it playable.
