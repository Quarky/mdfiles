# Six Session Route and Sandbox Switchboard

## Session 1: Arrival Under a Red Sky

**Purpose:** Land, establish the expedition, learn how Moraband changes ordinary decisions, and decide whether the pursuers are enemies, rivals, captives, or uneasy allies.

**Decision:** Which risks remain at the ship, which travel with the expedition, and what will the group do when another expedition arrives?

**Exit:** The party leaves the landing region with a route, a rival situation, and consequences that will continue moving without them.

## Session 2: The Search for a Dead Jedi

**Purpose:** Turn the search into investigation rather than a sequence of Survival rolls. The causeway, Delta-7B, and false refuge provide different kinds of evidence and different temptations.

**Decision:** Does the group trust physical evidence, the gatekeeper, the Force, or what it most wants to be true?

**Exit:** The party possesses proof that Warde survived his landing, but the planet has learned what each character hopes to find.

## Session 3: Night of Teeth and Voices

**Purpose:** Escalate from environmental danger to active spiritual predation while preserving alternatives to combat and giving the players evidence that light-side conduct changes the environment.

**Decision:** What will the group sacrifice for speed, safety, or forbidden certainty?

**Exit:** The group reaches the obsidian stair carrying fear tags, injuries, and evidence of how Moraband manufactures certainty.

## Session 4: Storm Shelter and the Wanderer

**Purpose:** Give the expedition a defensible pause, expose interpersonal strain, and introduce Warde as a person before he becomes the final antagonist.

**Decision:** Will they accept aid from a stranger who knows too much, and what do they reveal while testing him?

**Exit:** The Wanderer joins or shadows the expedition, and Warde Trust becomes an active clock.

## Session 5: The Long Road to the Tomb

**Purpose:** Run the trek as a social and investigative journey. The party can prove the future is mutable before Warde ever draws a weapon.

**Decision:** Do the characters confront him, gather evidence, protect one another, or allow the journey to become a test of obedience?

**Exit:** The palace is revealed, the party chooses its approach, and every unresolved rival or temptation takes a final position.

## Session 6: The Palace and the Unfixed Future

**Purpose:** Explore the palace, assemble the case against fatalism, confront Warde, and resolve the holocron without reducing the finale to a hit-point race.

**Decision:** Can the party demonstrate that a foreseen future is not a command—and will it keep trying when violence begins?

**Exit:** Warde is redeemed, defeated, or gone; the final crystal changes the gatekeeper; and Moraband leaves personal consequences rather than a clean reset.

## Compressing or expanding

For a shorter run, use scenes 1, 3 or 5, 6, 8, 9, 12, 15, 16, 18, 19, and 21. For a long sandbox, allow route reversals, rival activity, return trips, shelter decisions, and follow-up investigations. Never require all 21 maps merely because they exist.
