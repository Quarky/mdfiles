# Living World Clocks and Consequence Ledger

| Clock | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Expedition | Prepared | Wear visible | Supply choice | Injury/exhaustion | Retreat threatened |
| Pursuit | Unknown | Signs | Contact | Rival acts first | Rival controls an objective |
| Warde Trust | Hostile assumption | Curious | Conflicted | Will listen | Helps resist the dark side |
| Temptation | Quiet | Personal offer | Accepted comfort | Compromise | Dark-side leverage |
| Palace Awakening | Dormant | Watching | Manifesting | Hostile architecture | Full spiritual assault |

Advance a clock only when fiction justifies it. Reduce one when the party makes a costly preparation, protects another person, repairs harm, returns a relic, creates a true alliance, or disproves a prior assumption through action.
