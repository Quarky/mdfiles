# Reek
**Type:** Nemesis
**Characteristics:** Brawn 6, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 3, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 4, Perception 2, Resilience 3
**Talents:** Adversary 2, Durable 2, Feral Strength 3, Knockdown
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2, Powerful Charge (may add two Advantage to a Brawl check when moving at full speed)
**Soak:** 6
**Wound Threshold:** 35
**Equipment:** Trample (Brawl; Damage 9; Crit 3; Disorient 3, Knockdown, Pierce 1)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reek)

A creature of living battering ram, the reek is a thick-skinned, horned quadruped known for its incredible strength and aggression[reference:30]. Standing over two meters tall and measuring four meters long, a charging reek is an unstoppable force, capable of pulverizing stone and armor with its three horns[reference:31]. Their tough hide provides excellent protection, making them a serious threat in any direct confrontation.
