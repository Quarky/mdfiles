# Blurrg
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 6, Agility 4, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 1, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 2, Survival 1
**Talents:** None
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2
**Soak:** 5
**Wound Threshold:** 20
**Equipment:** Bite (Brawl; Damage 7; Crit 2; Ensnare 2)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blurrg)

These two-legged reptiles are a common sight across the galaxy, prized as fast and resilient mounts once broken[reference:7]. In the wild, blurrgs are fiercely territorial, using their powerful jaws and surprising speed to chase down intruders. A blurrg's bite is its primary weapon, capable of latching onto prey and holding it fast.

**Role:** Mount / Pack Animal
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blurrg)
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 5, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 2, Perception 2, Survival 2
**Talents:** Domesticable
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2; Trained Mount (treated as a vehicle when ridden)
**Soak:** 5
**Wound Threshold:** 20
**Equipment:** Bite (Brawl; Damage 6; Crit 4; Range [Engaged])
**Picture:** [Blurrg Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blurrg?file=Blurrg_SWS.png)

The blurrg is a sturdy two-legged reptile known for its stamina and bad temper. A domesticated blurrg is a loyal and resilient mount, capable of carrying a rider and gear through the roughest terrain. Training a wild blurrg requires patience, but the result is a companion that will fearlessly charge into danger to protect its bonded rider.
