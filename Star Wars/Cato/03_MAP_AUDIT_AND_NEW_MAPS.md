# Map Audit and Expansion Requests

This audit uses the canonical SP01-SP19 functions and the current inverted-palace continuity. It is written as a **map revision brief**, not a pixel-level critique.

## Global changes to preserve across every map

- Keep NPCs/creatures out of baked map art; use Foundry tokens.
- Deep exterior openings show fog, nearby support silhouettes, cables, partial adjacent structures - not clear panoramic canyon views.
- Formal palette: pale/bone stone, dark metal, navy/blue and gold accents, ceremonial Neimoidian geometry.
- Broken/service palette: same architectural DNA plus wet rock, rust, utility metal, water, fog, maintenance lighting.
- Player-facing images stay unlabeled. Put names, hazard zones, clue numbers, and recommended routes on separate overlays.
- Use darkness and localized warm/blue light. Avoid broad white fill.
- Every map should have at least one alternate route, side room, or environmental clue that is not required for completion.

## SP01 - Level 12 Exhibition Hall

**Keep:** huge stripped cultural space, mosaic floor, intact cases, melancholy scale.  
**Modify/add:**

- make one quadrant visibly darker because the secondary backup is failing;
- build the mosaic as eight readable visual zones without text labels;
- include intact cases with empty mounts and dust silhouettes;
- include one case where a small child's handprint can plausibly be revealed by angled light;
- add a narrow wall-service seam that can open through the Mosaic Path puzzle;
- show evidence of systematic looting: clean tool marks, opened/reclosed cases, tagged mounts;
- do **not** bake the manta into the art; show broad scuff/wet marks where a huge creature sometimes rests.

## SP02 - Cultural Vault & Manta Passage

**Keep:** back-of-house storage and ecological obstruction.  
**Modify/add:**

- museum packing racks, inventory drawers, padded object cradles, empty transport cases;
- a looter staging area with abandoned nonvaluable packing material;
- a three-meter breach to exterior fog large enough for a canyon manta;
- two routes around the obstruction: formal door and cramped staff passage;
- a recoverable partial catalogue linking one stolen piece to an upper-city collection;
- drainage channels and old animal moisture marks to show the palace has become habitat.

## SP03 - Diplomatic Quarter & Canyon Walkway

**Keep:** elite suites plus exterior circulation.  
**Modify/add:**

- visually distinct suite types: conventional seating, low/cushion layout, standing/braced layout;
- three cultural seals that can support the Hospitality Protocol puzzle;
- service cubbies with visitor comfort supplies and personal staff notes;
- exterior windows/doors that show cables traveling upward toward the bridge;
- an emergency route marker pointing toward SP04 and a staff-only route toward SP05.

## SP04 - Suspended Canyon Crossing

**Keep:** traversal tension.  
**Modify/add:**

- damaged railing sections, cable anchor housings, and at least one stable refuge platform;
- a lower maintenance line players can use if the main span becomes dangerous;
- fog channels that sweep across the bridge rather than covering the entire map uniformly;
- an abandoned repair trolley that can become moving cover or a rescue device;
- visible neighboring palace masses fading into fog so the crossing feels part of a megastructure.

## SP05 - Sealed Archive

**Keep:** investigation focus.  
**Modify/add:**

- a physical three-selector access station for the Hospitality Protocol puzzle;
- document carousels, secure cylinders, a small paper/physical log area, and a dead terminal bank;
- an obsolete privacy field generator that can activate on failed access attempts;
- one emergency duplication cabinet containing mundane administrative records that humanize staff;
- a narrow archive service exit that makes SP05 a genuine alternate path rather than a cul-de-sac.

## SP06 - Level 14 Mural Gallery

**Keep:** sixty-meter history walk.  
**Modify/add:**

- six primary mural stations with enough floor between them for movement and conversation;
- a recurring green continuity motif and one subtle red unlabelled marker in the appropriate history panel;
- small artist/maintenance repairs that show the mural was cared for over decades;
- two support-room doors that allow the gallery to feel embedded in a working diplomatic level;
- a sightline at the far end toward the colder, darker SP07 threshold.

## SP07 - Ghost Chamber

**Keep:** small, intense, non-routine-combat space with seven presences/impressions.  
**Modify/add:**

- seven floor positions implied by wear/inlay rather than literal ghost art;
- acoustically isolating wall geometry or sealed stone ribs that make the silence believable;
- a central witnessing focus - not an altar, but a formal diplomatic speaking position;
- a concealed facility-manager message or maintenance seam near the entry, so archaeology precedes the supernatural beat;
- no corpses and no baked apparitions; the room should be playable before/after the encounter.

## SP08 - Level 15 Grand Atrium & Preparation Suites

**Keep:** grand symmetry and palace reawakening.  
**Modify/add:**

- chemical lights arranged in visible sequential circuits from stair arrival to atrium;
- a reception desk with guest-book storage and an inactive administrative droid cradle;
- central Trade Federation mosaic with the same visual language as Level 12 but more formal/political;
- clearly distinct west and east preparation suites;
- a visible staff/service door to SP10 so maintenance feels integrated, not hidden by dungeon logic.

## SP09 - Warde's Preparation Suite

**Keep:** intimate, personal evidence.  
**Modify/add:**

- one table, one chair, one cup, one dead terminal, one notation board - resist visual clutter;
- precise geometry, straight objects, and deliberate placement;
- subtle water/light protection explaining why one portion of the board survived;
- no overt Sith decoration, blood shrine, or obvious corruption; the horror is that the room is controlled and ordinary;
- a small ventilation/service opening that carries the first distant mechanical sound from Level 16.

## SP10 - Service Gallery & Chemical-Light Works

**Keep:** systems explanation and alternate travel.  
**Modify/add:**

- three distinct reservoirs/manifolds for the chemical-light puzzle;
- pressure lines that visibly feed SP08/09/16;
- catwalks and bypass shutters allowing technical PCs to reshape routes;
- staff lockers, shift board, improvised repair notes, and one abandoned meal station;
- a low-power monitoring panel that can show droid movement without giving perfect tactical information.

## SP11 - BX Security Junction

**Keep:** principal tactical droid space.  
**Modify/add:**

- integrate the published final-approach **chasm/destroyed floor** here if the current art lacks it;
- three retreat/flank lanes so BX commandos can harry instead of fighting to destruction;
- scavenged power-cell racks and repair nests showing years of self-maintenance;
- an original security command relay containing obsolete last-order packets;
- a strong sightline toward the final meeting-hall doors so the droideka functions as a sentinel.

**Conditional new map:** if SP11 cannot physically support both junction tactics and the chasm, create **SP29 - Final Approach Chasm & Meeting-Hall Doors** rather than forcing both beats into a cramped scene.

## SP12 - Preliminary Conference Suite

**Keep:** political/forensic function.  
**Modify/add:**

- several room configurations, including one large working room and one seating-chart room;
- pushed-back furniture that implies interrupted but orderly procedure;
- draft cease-fire language, position-paper stations, and a side observer position for N'got;
- a window/door that lets players hear but not clearly see the foggy exterior;
- an optional staff route to SP13 that is narrower and damaged.

## SP13 - Final Meeting Hall

**Keep:** tiered chamber, Keloythian oak table, Warde chair, battle scars, broken windows, Durael balcony, concentrated Force residue.  
**Modify/add:**

- make Warde's chair visibly pulled back rather than overturned;
- build readable attack geometry: first-strike damage at table, bodyguard fire toward Republic side, Jedi-vs-Jedi scars moving outward;
- physically include or connect the ruined balcony where Jiv Durael died;
- place enough dead-droid debris for evidence but leave tokens/corpses separate if Foundry implementation needs interactable assets;
- include broken transplas opening to fog and nearby support silhouettes;
- keep the room large enough for investigation after combat, not optimized as a symmetric arena.

## SP14 - Mist-Flooded Hall

Use as an optional **memory-of-normal-life** route. Add ruined salons, table settings left under covers, a collapsed cloak storage, and staff attempts to seal windows. Let fog intensity change during play.

## SP15 - Collapsed Cliffside Gallery

Use as a physical version of Warde's theme: many possible paths, one chosen under pressure. Add multiple survivable routes with different costs rather than one correct bridge. Include old emergency bridging hardware.

## SP16 - Cavern Breach

Use to show architecture colliding with support geology. Add anchor drilling, a small workers' shrine, a hidden emergency cache, and a bypass tunnel that comes out behind a formal wall elsewhere.

## SP17 - Flooded Service Cavern

Tie directly into SP10's light/pump systems. Add pump controls, submerged conduit, a dead droid cradle bay, and water marks showing years of changing levels. This is the best place for a technical salvage scene.

## SP18 - Observation Spire

Use for partial information, not a sniper tower. Sensors should be degraded by fog. A restored optic can show **one** meaningful thing: a route, lower anchor movement, or droid patrol - never the entire palace.

## SP19 - Substructure Anchor Cavern

Make this the definitive "how the palace hangs" map. Huge pylons should disappear upward toward Jorra and laterally into rock. Add the Anchor Load Balance puzzle and allow successful stabilization to make the return route safer.

# Proposed new maps

## SP27 - Ventral Threshold & Palace Exterior Approach - HIGH PRIORITY

**Why it is new:** none of SP01-SP19 clearly owns the moment when players understand that Sankar is hanging under the bridge.  
**Function:** transition from known ventral city into the dedicated palace complex; establish scale, fog, supports, Syndicate watcher, Rusted Stalker boundary, and the palace as a descending mass.  
**Gameplay:** route choice, observation, optional stealth, first visual contact with expanding droid patrol evidence.  
**Do not:** turn it into a combat arena. The payoff is orientation and dread.

## SP28 - Staff Spine & Evacuation Node - MEDIUM PRIORITY

**Why it is new:** current maps emphasize diplomats, ceremony, maintenance, and battle, but not the ordinary staff who made the building work.  
**Function:** lockers, scheduling board, food/tea station, cleaning droid bays, lost-and-found, emergency notices, and Eleth Sarrino's office or staff records.  
**Story payoff:** makes Sankar feel inhabited before it died; provides mundane names the party can recognize later on logs and notes.  
**Placement:** branch from SP06 or SP10 and reconnect one level lower.

## SP29 - Final Approach Chasm & Meeting-Hall Doors - CONDITIONAL

Only create if SP11 cannot naturally contain the official chasm/droideka approach. It should be a short, brutal tactical threshold with a lower maintenance bypass, not another large dungeon floor.

# Maps I would not add

- another generic ceremonial hall;
- another droid combat room;
- a conventional rooftop;
- a huge Sith temple directly under Sankar unless you intentionally revive the old discarded ventral draft;
- a clear-sky observation platform deep below the fog;
- a second archive that duplicates SP05.
