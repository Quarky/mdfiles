
# LEVEL 12 — THE EXHIBITION HALL
### *"They built this to last. They were right."*



---

## GM BRIEFING

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Story Purpose** | First contact with deep ventral. Establish the Trade Federation as people, not a faceless empire. Plant seeds of what was lost. |
| **Tone** | Wonder with melancholy underneath. Beautiful and abandoned. Not threatening yet. |
| **Pacing** | Keep moving. Short sharp beats. Let the player discover rather than be told. |
| **Dark Side Foreshadowing** | Subtle. One moment only. Something at the edge of perception. Gone when examined. |
| **Key Emotional Beat** | The child's handprint on the display case. |
| **Animals** | Canyon Manta pair. Bridge Geckos stop at the threshold — first sign something is different here. |
| **Exit Hook** | The Manta shifts. The passage clears. The descent continues. The player wants to know what's next. |

---

## THE THRESHOLD — BEFORE THEY OPEN THE DOOR

The service passage ends at a door that stops them.

> *It's not a maintenance door.*
> *Stone frame. Carved border — geometric,*
> *precise, the kind of work that takes weeks.*
> *Someone cared about this door.*
> *This was the back entrance to somewhere important.*

**GM NOTE:** Pause here. Let them look at it.
Then say:

> *You notice the walls beside the door.*
> *Back in the Ossivane — back up in the residential levels —*
> *the Bridge Geckos were everywhere.*
> *Every surface. Every corner.*
> *They are not here.*
> *The wall beside this door is bare.*
> *They stopped. Right here.*
> *And did not come further.*

**If the Pathfinder reaches through the bond:**

> *She can feel where they stopped.*
> *Not why.*
> *Just — the edge.*
> *A line they decided not to cross.*
> *Years ago.*
> *And have not crossed since.*

**Beat:** Let that sit for one second. Then:

> *The door opens inward.*
> *The air that comes through is different.*
> *Not colder. Not warmer.*
> *Cleaner.*
> *Like a room that has been sealed*
> *and has had nothing happen in it*
> *for a very long time.*

---

## ENTERING THE EXHIBITION HALL

The party steps through. The lights do not ignite here —
this level runs on the palace's secondary backup,
which has been slowly dying for fifteen years.
Three quarters of the hall is lit by what remains.
One quarter is dark.

> *The ceiling is eight meters above you.*
> *You know this because your glow rod*
> *reaches the top and finds it.*
> *The space is enormous.*

> *And it is empty.*
> *Not damaged-empty.*
> *Stripped-empty.*
> *Display cases with their glass intact*
> *and nothing inside them.*
> *Mounting brackets on the walls*
> *where objects used to hang.*
> *Outlines on the floor where plinths stood —*
> *you can see the marks, slightly darker stone*
> *where the bases sat for years*
> *and the light could not reach.*

> *Whatever was here is gone.*
> *Taken. Methodically.*
> *Not smashed. Taken.*
> *Someone came here and took everything*
> *they could carry.*

**GM NOTE:** The looting happened in the first two
years after the war. Syndicate, mostly. Some Imperial
personnel. The objects that were here were Neimoidian
art, Trade Federation artifacts, historical items
of significant value. They are scattered across
the galaxy now. Most are in private collections.
None of the people who took them knew what they were.

---

## THE MOSAIC FLOOR

> *But they couldn't take the floor.*

> *Look down.*

> *The floor of the Exhibition Hall is a mosaic.*
> *The entire floor.*
> *Every centimeter.*
> *Neimoidian artists worked this for two years.*
> *You can tell because it is not one thing —*
> *it is a hundred things, each panel*
> *connecting to the next,*
> *telling a story across the whole surface.*

**GM NOTE:** Read these slowly. One at a time.
Let the player look at each panel mentally.

> *Near the entrance: the Neimoidian homeworld.*
> *The great hatchery lakes.*
> *Tiny figures tending the grub pools.*
> *The colors are extraordinary —*
> *deep greens and silvers,*
> *the lake surfaces catching light*
> *that no longer comes through these windows.*

> *Moving inward: the first ships.*
> *The first trade routes.*
> *A Neimoidian captain at a viewscreen,*
> *stars ahead of him,*
> *the homeworld behind.*
> *His expression is not greedy.*
> *It is curious.*

> *The center panel: Cato Neimoidia.*
> *The bridge-cities shown from above —*
> *you can see Jorra in the design,*
> *the specific span of this bridge.*
> *The canyon below it.*
> *And on the canyon floor,*
> *barely visible because the artist*
> *wanted you to look closely:*
> *the Canyon Mantas.*
> *In the mosaic.*
> *They were already here when this was made.*

**If the Pathfinder is paying attention:**

> *Through the bond, distantly,*
> *she can feel the actual mantas.*
> *Somewhere below.*
> *Warm. Slow.*
> *Present.*
> *The mosaic knew they were here.*
> *The mosaic was right.*

---

## THE DISPLAY PLAQUES

The display cases are empty but the plaques
that identified their contents are still mounted.
The party can read them.

**GM NOTE:** Read these as found objects.
The player is learning Trade Federation history
through what they chose to display about themselves.

---

**PLAQUE 1** *(near entrance, beside an empty case)*

> *"First Trade Agreement — Neimoidia to Cato Neimoidia.*
> *Signed in the year of the Third Expansion.*
> *Original document. The founding of the purse world system.*
> *For the first time, Neimoidians lived among the stars.*
> *They did not forget where they came from."*

---

**PLAQUE 2** *(center of the hall, large empty mounting bracket)*

> *"The Sakoyan Navigator.*
> *Gift from the Sakoyan people to the Trade Federation*
> *upon the signing of the Outer Rim Commerce Accord.*
> *We did not only trade goods.*
> *We traded trust."*

**GM NOTE:** The Sakoyan Navigator was a sculpture,
approximately one meter tall, considered one of the
finest examples of cross-species diplomatic art
in the sector. It is currently in Duke Irbian's
private quarters. He acquired it from a Syndicate
contact three years ago for a price he considers
a bargain. He does not know its provenance.
He likes the way it looks on his shelf.

---

**PLAQUE 3** *(east wall, a row of small empty cases)*

> *"Personal effects of the founding families.*
> *Objects carried from the homeworld*
> *on the first colony ships.*
> *Not valuable. Not decorative.*
> *Carried because they were home."*

> *The cases are empty.*
> *Someone took objects that were carried*
> *from the Neimoidian homeworld*
> *on the first ships.*
> *Someone took them and sold them*
> *and they are somewhere now*
> *with no one who knows what they were.*

---

## THE CHILD'S HANDPRINT

On the far side of the hall, in the section
that is partially dark, there is a display case
that was not fully looted.

The case is large — floor to ceiling, built into the wall.
The objects that were inside it were too heavy to move
easily and whoever looted the hall gave up on this one.
What they left: fragments of what were once
scale models of Neimoidian ships. Broken.
The models were damaged in the war and were being
held here for restoration. They were never restored.

But that is not the important thing about this case.

> *At the base of the case,*
> *on the outside of the glass,*
> *at the height a child would reach:*
> *a handprint.*
>
> *Not painted.*
> *Not decorative.*
> *Pressed.*
> *A child's hand, pressed against the glass*
> *hard enough and long enough*
> *that the oils from the skin*
> *left a permanent mark.*
>
> *Fifteen years of sealed air*
> *have preserved it perfectly.*
>
> *A child stood here.*
> *Looked at whatever was in this case.*
> *Put their hand on the glass.*
> *The way children do.*
> *The way children always do.*

**GM NOTE:** Do not explain this. Do not contextualize it.
Just describe it and stop. Let it land.

If the player wants to examine it further:

> *The hand is small.*
> *Four fingers and one opposable digit —*
> *Neimoidian anatomy.*
> *Young. Maybe five, six years old.*
> *They were pressing their face close too, probably.*
> *Looking at the ships.*
> *Looking at the stars their people sailed.*

---

## THE DARK SIDE FORESHADOWING — FIRST SIGN

**GM NOTE:** This is subtle. One beat only.
Do not linger. Do not explain.
Plant the seed and move on.

It happens while the party is examining the hall —
whenever feels natural. Possibly at the child's handprint.
Possibly at the center mosaic. Whenever the player
is most engaged with something beautiful.

> *For just a moment —*
> *less than a second —*
> *the light in the far corner of the hall*
> *does something it should not do.*
>
> *Not flicker. The chemical lights don't flicker.*
> *Something else.*
> *Like a shadow moved*
> *where there was nothing to cast a shadow.*
>
> *And then it is gone.*
> *The corner is empty.*
> *The light is steady.*
> *Whatever you saw — or thought you saw —*
> *is not there.*

**If anyone investigates the corner:**

> *Nothing.*
> *Empty floor.*
> *The stone is cold.*
> *The air is still.*
>
> *But the Pathfinder —*
> *if she reaches through the Force —*
> *finds something she cannot quite name.*
> *Not a presence.*
> *The echo of a presence.*
> *Something that was here once*
> *and is not here now*
> *but has not entirely left.*
>
> *It does not feel dangerous.*
> *It feels sad.*

**GM NOTE:** This is the first thread.
By Level 14 it will be a rope.
By the palace it will be a chain.
For now — just this. Just the corner.
Just something that moved.

---

## THE CANYON MANTA — THE OBSTRUCTION

The south exit of the Exhibition Hall —
the passage that leads to Level 13 —
is blocked.

> *You see it before you reach it.*
> *The south passage is approximately four meters wide.*
> *Something is in it.*
> *Something large.*

> *It is one of the Canyon Mantas.*
> *In the passage.*
> *Wings folded.*
> *Resting.*
> *It takes up the entire width of the corridor*
> *and most of the height.*
> *Its eyes — set wide on a flat, ancient head —*
> *are open.*
> *It is looking at you.*
> *It is not alarmed.*
> *You are in its space.*
> *It is not particularly interested in that fact.*

**GM NOTE:** This is not a combat encounter.
This is a presence encounter. The manta will not move
on command. It will not move on request.
It will move when it decides to move.

**If anyone tries to push past it:**

> *The manta does not move.*
> *It shifts its weight slightly —*
> *the way something very large shifts its weight*
> *when it has been mildly inconvenienced.*
> *The floor vibrates.*
> *You reconsider.*

**If the Pathfinder approaches:**

> *She can feel it clearly.*
> *Not the bond — she has not bonded this one.*
> *Just its presence.*
> *Old. Very old.*
> *Older than the building around it.*
> *This manta was alive when this hall was built.*
> *It has been in this canyon since before*
> *the Trade Federation arrived.*
> *It was here before any of this.*
> *It will be here after.*
>
> *It is not frightening.*
> *It is humbling.*
> *There is a difference.*

**Communicating with it:**

The Pathfinder can reach toward it through the Force —
not a bond attempt, just a presence acknowledgment.
No roll required. She simply extends attention
and it registers her.

> *It turns its head.*
> *Slightly.*
> *The way something turns its head*
> *when it has decided you are worth a second look.*
>
> *What comes back through that attention*
> *is not words and not images.*
> *It is something older than either.*
> *It is the manta's relationship to this space:*
> *I was here. I am here. I will be here.*
> *You may pass when I am ready.*

After a moment:

> *The manta shifts.*
> *Slowly.*
> *With the unhurried dignity of something*
> *that has never needed to be fast.*
> *It moves its body to the left,*
> *folding its wings tighter,*
> *creating a gap along the right wall.*
> *Not large.*
> *Enough.*
>
> *It does not look at you again.*
> *It has done what it decided to do.*
> *You may go.*

**Sigg:**

> *He watches the Pathfinder and the manta.*
> *He does not comment.*
> *He steps through the gap.*
> *He pauses on the other side and looks back.*
>
> *"It was here before the war," he says.*
> *"Wasn't it."*
>
> *Not a question.*
> *He already knows.*

---

## BEFORE LEAVING — THE LAST LOOK

As the party passes through the gap and exits:

> *Look back.*

> *The Exhibition Hall behind you:*
> *the empty cases.*
> *The mounting brackets.*
> *The plaques with nothing left to describe.*
> *The floor mosaic — all of Neimoidian history*
> *laid in stone, intact, unlooted,*
> *because you cannot carry a floor.*
>
> *And the child's handprint.*
> *Still there.*
> *Still exactly the size it was.*
>
> *The manta settles back into the passage behind you.*
> *It is already closing its eyes.*
