# Session 00 - Return to the Deep Ventral and Reach Sankar

**Target:** 60-120 minutes depending on travel roleplay.  
**Primary map:** proposed SP27, with optional prior ventral maps.  
**Session job:** reconnect the party to the living ventral city, show what changed since last visit, and make the palace's inverted scale unmistakable.

## Cold open

Return to a familiar route and show three small changes:

1. the Rusted Stalker's patrol has shifted downward but stops short of the deepest route;
2. a Syndicate observer has passed through recently;
3. droid movement can be heard or inferred from below.

None of these needs immediate combat.

## Beat 1 - Familiar place, altered behavior

Let players notice what is new rather than tell them. A bonded animal's caution is especially useful: it has learned that the lower levels are no longer still.

## Beat 2 - Route choice

Offer at least two approaches:

- public/known descent: easier navigation, more chance of being observed;
- service route: tighter, more technical, better concealment, stronger environmental clues.

A third broken-wing approach can exist for creative PCs.

## Beat 3 - First exterior view

The party emerges where the bridge overhead becomes visible as a massive ceiling. Sankar descends from it into fog. Use nearby support lines and broken palace masses to establish direction.

## Beat 4 - Optional complication

Choose one:

- Syndicate operative watching from a parallel walkway;
- distant BX silhouette crossing a lower gantry;
- fog pressure surge forcing a brief route change;
- maintenance collapse revealing a staff access.

## End hook

The threshold to Level 12 is not a battle door. It is an old formal entry whose stonework says that someone cared about what lay beyond. The geckos stop there.
