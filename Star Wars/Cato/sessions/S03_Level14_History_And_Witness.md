# Session 03 - Level 14: History and Witness

**Target:** 2-3 hours.  
**Maps:** SP06-SP07.  
**Session job:** shift from institutional record to Force memory without losing player agency.

## Beat order

1. arch/threshold inscription;
2. mural walk - player chooses what to study;
3. red-world recurrence and war panel;
4. support waiting rooms;
5. communications log with blank outcome;
6. Eleth Sarrino message;
7. approach to Ghost Chamber;
8. seven-presence witnessing puzzle;
9. release/fade and recognition of one deeper presence.

## Mandatory discoveries

- history is curated;
- the final delegation expected to report a successful outcome;
- Warde hesitated for about two seconds;
- the dead/echoes want accurate witness, not simple revenge.

## Avoid

- treating the seven presences as a combat encounter;
- explaining exactly what every ghost "is";
- making the player recite trivia to progress;
- revealing Warde's final destination.

## Cliffhanger

The deeper presence remains. Down the stair, something physical clicks awake as the palace lights begin responding to pressure.
