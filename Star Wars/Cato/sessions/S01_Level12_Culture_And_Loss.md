# Session 01 - Level 12: Culture and Loss

**Target:** 90-150 minutes.  
**Maps:** SP01-SP02.  
**Session job:** make players care about the people whose political tragedy they will later investigate.

## Beat order

1. animal threshold / changed air;
2. stripped exhibition hall;
3. player-selected mosaic panels;
4. empty plaques and provenance clues;
5. child's handprint;
6. Mosaic Path puzzle / cultural vault;
7. manta obstruction and nonviolent resolution;
8. quiet exit toward Level 13.

## Pacing control

If attention drops, shift from description to action: ask what case they open, which panel they step onto, how they reach the handprint, or whether they use the direct path past the manta.

## Mandatory discoveries

- systematic looting;
- Neimoidian culture existed beyond war/greed stereotypes;
- children and ordinary visitors used the hall;
- canyon life has reclaimed the ruin.

## Optional rewards

- provenance trail to a stolen cultural object;
- staff catalogue;
- ambiguous Curator impression;
- map/service route shortcut.

## Cliffhanger

From the next level comes architecture built for visitors rather than exhibits - broad corridor, multiple seals, and the first real evidence that diplomacy happened here routinely.
