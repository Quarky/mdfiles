# Session 06 - The Final Meeting Hall and the Crystal

**Target:** 2-3 hours.  
**Map:** SP13.  
**Session job:** let players reconstruct the tragedy, encounter the final witness, recover the crystal, and earn the Moraband handoff.

## Do not start with a vision

Give the players the room first.

### Evidence sequence

- tiered chamber and oak table;
- Warde's deliberately pulled-back chair;
- delegate-side remains and first-strike geometry;
- droid wreckage and later blaster response;
- crossed lightsaber scars moving away from the table;
- Warde's wound/escape trail;
- broken windows and fog;
- ruined balcony;
- Jiv Durael and the crystal.

Run `The Table Tells the Story` puzzle before supernatural confirmation.

## The two-second memory

If a Force-sensitive PC uses Foresight/Foresee here, the saturated room can answer with the past rather than the future. Show only the moment before violence: Warde looking at the people across from him, the hesitation, the choice. Do not make the vision an omniscient movie.

## Kaddo Moll - final witness

Use the latest custom version if desired: Moll was mortally wounded by Warde, escaped long enough to warn the Council, died nearby, and a Force witness/impression returns to the room.

Her role is not to forgive Warde on behalf of everyone. Her role is to articulate the tragedy: he saw a real danger, misidentified people as the danger itself, and chose to make possibility into inevitability.

Keep her message personal and limited. She does not know what Warde has become on Moraband.

## Jiv Durael

Durael's body on the balcony is physical confirmation that his fight did not end at the table. His broken lightsaber and Warde's kyber crystal show that he succeeded at one task even while losing his life: keeping the crystal from leaving with Warde.

## Crystal recovery

Treat the crystal as evidence and key, not loot. It has remained in the Force residue of the room for years. A Force-sensitive PC may experience endurance, memory, or warmth without the crystal being "corrupted."

## Moraband reveal discipline

Earlier red-world clues and departure direction may have made Moraband a strong hypothesis. **This is where the quest makes it reliable.** When the crystal is later inserted into the holocron/Gatekeeper system, it supplies Warde's intended destination.

## Exit image

The room should remain damaged. If a presence releases, only the emotional pressure changes. Physical consequences remain. The party leaves with the truth, not with a magically healed room.
