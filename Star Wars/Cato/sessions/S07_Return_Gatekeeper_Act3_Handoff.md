# Session 07 - Return, Gatekeeper, and Act III Handoff

**Target:** 45-90 minutes plus any unresolved ventral choices.  
**Session job:** show consequences, let players see the route differently on return, and transition cleanly to Moraband.

## The return should not be a montage unless the table wants one

Use three callbacks:

- a route the PCs repaired is now easier;
- an animal/droid/NPC reacts to what they changed;
- a room that felt threatening on descent now feels merely empty because its secret is understood.

## Possible consequence callbacks

- SP19 stabilization reduces creaks and closes one hazard.
- SP17 pump repair lowers water.
- SP10 lighting choices remain in effect.
- droids stand down, return to a junction, or continue fighting depending on resolution.
- the manta is gone, or still resting elsewhere in the hall.
- the Syndicate observer is waiting farther up with new questions.

## Gatekeeper scene

The crystal provides a fuller picture of Warde's final hours and confirms his intended destination as Moraband. Emphasize the thematic lesson: foresight showed possibilities; Warde's choices turned fear into action.

The Gatekeeper can unlock the next stage of Warde's Foresight instruction as appropriate to the campaign, but the emotional handoff should be a question, not a reward screen:

**What will the PCs do with foresight now that they have seen what certainty did to Warde?**

## Act III hooks

- tell Tacker what the palace proved;
- confront or inform Hasar;
- return cultural provenance evidence;
- decide what to do about surviving droids;
- decide whether Eleth Sarrino's message should be preserved publicly;
- leave Jorra for Moraband with unresolved human consequences behind them.
