# Session 02 - Level 13: Diplomacy and Records

**Target:** 2-3 hours.  
**Maps:** SP03-SP05.  
**Session job:** establish that the final delegation had real authority and sincere intent.

## Beat order

1. diplomatic corridor and cultural seals;
2. choose two of three suites;
3. Hospitality Protocol puzzle;
4. sealed archive and cease-fire documents;
5. red-world comparison clue;
6. exterior walkway / fog reveal;
7. suspended crossing with fail-forward hazard;
8. sense of something below becoming aware.

## Mandatory discoveries

- Sankar was designed to accommodate difference;
- the cease-fire was real enough to matter;
- the delegation knew there was danger;
- warning did not stop them from trying.

## Action injection

If the table needs motion, the crossing sheds a railing section or a lower maintenance trolley begins moving after an old brake fails. Make it a rescue/coordination scene, not a random enemy spawn.

## Cliffhanger

At the Level 14 threshold, official history literally covers the walls - and a colder presence waits beyond it.
