# Session 05 - Level 16: Reconstruction and Old Orders

**Target:** 2-3 hours.  
**Maps:** SP11-SP12 and the threshold of SP13.  
**Session job:** combine political procedure, tactical danger, and physical evidence before the final hall.

## Beat order

1. preliminary conference room;
2. seating chart / N'got observer position;
3. optional smaller rooms for mundane final-day details;
4. droid evidence at SP11;
5. BX ambush or challenge;
6. retreat/reprogram/stand-down possibilities;
7. droideka/chasm final threshold if used;
8. meeting-hall doors.

## Mandatory discoveries

- the talks had process and working drafts;
- N'got's viewing angle makes his testimony credible;
- droids are executing surviving orders;
- the physical approach was violently damaged after Warde's attack.

## Droids without a fight

The strongest peaceful resolution requires evidence gathered in prior sessions. Archive authorization plus final-day logs plus obsolete command hierarchy can be more persuasive than a single Computers roll.

## End choice

Ask explicitly: "Before you open the doors, is there anything you do?" Let players rest, repair, talk, prepare, or simply enter.
