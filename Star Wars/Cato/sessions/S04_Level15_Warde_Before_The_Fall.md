# Session 04 - Level 15: Warde Before the Fall

**Target:** 2-3 hours.  
**Maps:** SP08-SP10.  
**Session job:** turn Warde from historical culprit into a specific person who knowingly approached a terrible choice.

## Beat order

1. reflection window;
2. pressure sensor and staged light awakening;
3. grand atrium / guest book / reception;
4. western delegation suite and personal stone/message;
5. eastern Warde suite;
6. notation board in four layers;
7. chemical-light/service puzzle as decompression or alternate order;
8. first mechanical sound from below.

## Mandatory discoveries

- the delegates were sincere;
- Warde believed danger still existed in the larger future;
- he saw branching alternatives;
- he was exhausted;
- he had decided to attack before entering the hall;
- he apologized beforehand.

## GM emotional note

Do not use exhaustion to excuse murder. Use it to explain why a once-capable person became vulnerable to certainty, isolation, and moral narrowing. The room should make the players understand him more and judge the choice more clearly at the same time.

## Cliffhanger

The party reaches Level 16 knowing more about Warde's mind than the witnesses did. Now they must compare that private truth to what the room physically proves.
