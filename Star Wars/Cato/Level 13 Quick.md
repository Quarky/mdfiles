# LEVEL 13 — THE DIPLOMATIC QUARTER APPROACH
## FAST-PACED GM GUIDE
### *"People came here from across the galaxy to talk instead of fight. That counts for something."*

---

## BEFORE YOU START — READ THIS

> This level teaches the Trade Federation's diplomatic ideal through DOING not reading.
> Every lore beat is attached to an ACTION.
> Keep things moving. If the player goes quiet — throw the next beat immediately.
> Never let the room sit empty for more than thirty seconds.
> This level has two distinct zones: INTERIOR (diplomatic discovery) and EXTERIOR (canyon exposure).
> The dark side presence grows stronger here — building toward Level 14.

## ENTERING LEVEL 13 — FIRST BEAT

**Say this the second they step through the door from Level 12:**

> *Stop.*
> *Look at the stone.*

**Wait for them to notice.**

> *The gap the manta made closed behind you.*
> *You're in a new passage.*
> *The stone is darker.*
> *One shade. Maybe two.*
> *The joins between the stones are tighter.*
> *More precise.*
> *The same hands that built the Exhibition Hall built this —*
> *but these hands were working on something*
> *they knew mattered more.*
>
> *The ceiling rises.*
> *Three meters in the service passage.*
> *Four in the Exhibition Hall.*
> *Here: five.*
> *You are going somewhere that expected*
> *important people.*

**The smell:**

> *Something changes in the air.*
> *Not bad. Not good.*
> *Specific.*
> *Old paper. Old fabric.*
> *The smell of rooms that held meetings.*
> *The smell of decisions.*

**Sigg:**
> *"This is what diplomacy smells like.*
> *Old paper, old fabric, old decisions.*
> *The smell of people trying to solve problems*
> *without killing each other."*

## THE DIPLOMATIC CORRIDOR — ACTION BEAT

> *The passage opens into a wide corridor —*
> *the widest you've seen in the ventral city.*
> *Four meters across. Five meters high.*
> *The chemical lighting here is full and steady.*
> *Everything is lit. Equally. Completely.*
>
> *The corridor stretches ahead of you.*
> *Forty meters.*
> *Doors on both sides.*
> *Not maintenance doors.*
> *Formal doors.*
> *Each one with a different diplomatic seal*
> *carved into the stone above the frame.*

**Ask:**

> *Which seal do you look at first?*

---

**BANKING CLAN SEAL:**

> *A circle bisected by a horizontal line.*
> *Wealth above. Debt below.*
> *The whole philosophy of the Muun*
> *in a single symbol.*

**Inside:**
> *A meeting room. Small. Private.*
> *A round table — no head. No hierarchy.*
> *Whoever sat here was considered equal*
> *for the duration of the meeting.*
>
> *On the table: a ledger. Open.*
> *Enormous trade figures.*
> *Whole economies, flowing through this table.*
>
> *At the bottom of the last page:*
> *handwriting — personal — not the administrative script.*
>
> *Read it:*
> *"If we stop talking, the numbers stop mattering.*
> *Remind them of this at the start of every session.*
> *They forget. They always forget."*

**What They Learn:**
- The Banking Clan valued dialogue
- They knew talking was the important part
- They expected to return — left advice for successors
- They never returned

**Sigg:**
> *"They knew. They knew the talking was the important part.*
> *The numbers were just the evidence of the talking.*
> *If you stop talking, the numbers stop meaning anything."*

---

**UNKNOWN SEAL — POSSIBLE CHOICE:**

> *This seal you don't recognize.*
> *Smaller than the others.*
> *A stylized creature — something with wings.*
> *Below it: three words in a script*
> *that is not Basic and not Pak Pak.*

**Inside:**
> *No meeting table.*
> *Instead: cushions on the floor.*
> *A low platform against the far wall.*
> *Whoever came here conducted their diplomacy differently.*
> *The Trade Federation accommodated this.*
> *They built a room for it.*
>
> *On the platform: a single object.*
> *A small carved figure.*
> *Left behind.*
> *Not forgotten — placed.*
> *Deliberately set on the platform.*
>
> *An offering.*
> *Or a marker.*
> *Or a goodbye.*

**If the Pathfinder touches it:**
> *Nothing through the Force.*
> *Just stone.*
> *Old stone.*
> *Smooth from handling.*
> *Someone held this many times*
> *before they set it down here.*

**What They Learn:**
- The Federation accommodated different diplomatic traditions
- They built rooms for specific species
- Some delegations left offerings
- Not everyone returned to collect them

**Sigg:**
> *"They built this for someone.*
> *Someone who didn't use tables, who didn't sit in chairs.*
> *And the Federation built a room for them.*
> *They accommodated them. They made space for them.*
>
> *That's what diplomacy is.*
> *Making space for the other person.*
> *Meeting them where they are."*

---

**THE PREPARATION LIBRARY:**

> *This room is different from the suites.*
> *Larger. Lined with shelves.*
> *A reference library for delegations*
> *preparing for negotiations.*
>
> *Most of the shelves are empty — taken in the looting.*
> *But some things were too heavy to carry.*
>
> *A globe. Mounted on a stand.*
> *The galaxy at the height of the Trade Federation.*
> *Routes marked in gold.*
> *The Federation's territory shaded.*
> *A third of the known galaxy.*
>
> *And in the outer rim:*
> *a single dot.*
> *Red.*
> *No label.*
> *No route.*
> *Just the dot.*
> *Red.*

**If they examine it closely:**
> *The dot is in the correct position*
> *for the Horuset system.*
> *For Moraband.*
> *Red.*
> *No label.*
> *The Trade Federation knew it was there.*
> *They marked it.*
> *They marked it in red and put no label on it*
> *and left it that way.*

**What They Learn:**
- The Federation knew Moraband existed
- They marked it with caution (red)
- They deliberately did not label it
- They avoided it

**Sigg:**
> *"They knew something was wrong with it.*
> *They just didn't say what."*

## THE DARK SIDE — SECOND SIGN

**GM NOTE:** This happens in the library, or in whichever room the party is in when they are most absorbed. Time it to interrupt a moment of discovery.

> *The temperature drops.*
> *Not dramatically.*
> *Two degrees. Maybe three.*
> *Enough to notice.*
> *Enough that you can see your breath*
> *for just a moment.*
>
> *And then it is gone.*
> *The temperature returns.*
> *The air is the same.*

**Pathfinder — Force Sensitivity:**
> *Something is different from the corner in Level 12.*
> *That was an echo.*
> *This is a presence.*
> *Faint. Distant.*
> *Like hearing a voice in another room —*
> *you know someone is speaking*
> *but you cannot hear the words.*
>
> *It is not here.*
> *It is below.*
> *It is coming from below.*
> *And it is aware that you are here.*

**Sigg — if she tells him:**
> *He is quiet.*
> *Then: "How far below?"*
>
> *She cannot tell him exactly.*
> *He nods. "Keep moving then.*
> *Better to know what it is*
> *than to stand here wondering."*

## THE SEALED ARCHIVE — ACTION BEAT

> *At the end of the interior corridor,*
> *past the last of the diplomatic suites,*
> *there is a room that is different from all the others.*
>
> *No seal above the door.*
> *No diplomatic designation.*
> *The door is heavier than the others —*
> *reinforced, built to protect its contents.*
>
> *It is locked.*
> *But the lock is mechanical —*
> *fifteen years old, no power required.*
> *Average Skulduggery to open.*

**If they open it:**

> *Inside: a small room.*
> *No windows.*
> *Shelves on three walls.*
> *Most empty.*
> *But in the back corner,*
> *in a sealed case — waterproof, airtight —*
> *a stack of papers.*

**What They Learn:**
- Someone wanted to protect these documents
- They expected someone to come for them
- That someone is the party

## THE CEASE-FIRE DOCUMENTS — THE CLIMAX

**GM NOTE:** Read these slowly. One piece at a time. Let the player absorb each one before moving to the next. This is the most important discovery on this level.

---

**DOCUMENT 1 — Cover Sheet:**

> *"Briefing Package — Separatist Diplomatic Delegation.*
> *Destination: Sankar Palace, Jorra, Cato Neimoidia.*
> *Purpose: Cease-Fire Negotiations.*
> *Classification: Delegation Eyes Only."*

---

**DOCUMENT 2 — Objectives:**

> *"1. Establish a complete cease-fire for all of Cato Neimoidia.*
> *2. Negotiate terms for Republic withdrawal from Jorra within sixty days.*
> *3. Establish a neutral monitoring presence to verify compliance.*
> *4. Create conditions for permanent peace talks to begin within ninety days."*
>
> *At the bottom:*
> *"These objectives were approved by the Separatist High Command.*
> *The delegation is authorized to reach agreement*
> *on all four points without further consultation."*

**GM NOTE:** They had the authority to end the war. Right here. Right now. At this table. Let that land before continuing.

**Sigg:**
> *"They had the authority.*
> *They didn't need to check with anyone.*
> *They could have ended the war.*
> *Right here. Right now. All of it.*
>
> *They came here to do that.*
> *They came here to make peace."*

---

**DOCUMENT 3 — Republic Assessment:**

> *Under Jedi presence:*
> *"Jedi Knight Suljo Warde.*
> *Human male. Clone Wars veteran.*
> *Prognosticative ability assessed: HIGH.*
> *Recommend: No deception-based negotiating positions.*
> *He will know."*

**Sigg:**
> *"They knew about Warde.*
> *They knew about his ability.*
> *They planned to be honest.*
> *They were going to negotiate in good faith.*
>
> *And he killed them anyway."*

---

**DOCUMENT 4 — The Risk Assessment:**

> *A single page. Separate from the others.*
> *Different paper. Added later.*
>
> *"Risk Assessment — Addendum.*
> *Source: Embedded Observer [DESIGNATION REDACTED]*
>
> *Primary risk has been reassessed.*
> *Secondary risk elevated to PRIMARY.*
> *Psychological assessment of Jedi Knight Warde*
> *indicates potential instability under*
> *prolonged operational stress.*
>
> *Recommend: Keep negotiations brief.*
> *Reach agreement quickly.*
> *Do not allow extended session.*
>
> *Note: This assessment should be shared with all delegation members.*
> *They should understand what they are walking into."*

**GM NOTE:** The embedded observer was N'got. The party knows this. They are holding N'got's assessment of Warde in their hands. He told them Warde was unstable. He told them to keep it brief. They did not keep it brief.

**Sigg — reading over their shoulder:**
> *He reads it twice.*
> *Then he sets it down carefully.*
>
> *"They knew. They all knew.*
> *N'got told them.*
> *He gave them every warning he had.*
>
> *They came anyway.*
> *Because the war was too costly to let*
> *one man's instability stop the talks.*
>
> *They were right about that.*
> *And they died for being right."*
>
> *He looks at the document.*
> *"N'got ran. I understand now why he ran.*
> *He had already done everything he could do.*
> *He had told them everything he knew.*
> *When it went wrong, there was nothing left for him to do*
> *except survive it.*
>
> *He has spent fifteen years thinking he failed them.*
> *He didn't fail them.*
> *He warned them.*
> *They made their choice.*
> *He made his."*

## THE EXTERIOR WALKWAY — ACTION BEAT

> *At the south end of the interior corridor,*
> *a door leads outside.*
>
> *Not to another building.*
> *Outside.*

> *The door opens and the sound hits first.*
> *The canyon.*
> *You have been hearing it as background —*
> *as context — since you entered the deep ventral.*
> *Out here it is not background.*
> *It is everything.*
>
> *The walkway extends thirty meters ahead.*
> *Four meters wide.*
> *Metal grating underfoot.*
> *Railing on the right side.*
> *Nothing on the left.*
>
> *You are beyond the edge of the bridge above.*
> *You are suspended between.*
>
> *The walkway sways.*
> *Just slightly.*
> *Just enough to feel.*
> *Canyon wind.*
> *It does not stop.*

**GM NOTE:** Give everyone a moment with this. The physical exposure of the walkway is important. They have been in enclosed spaces since the Ossivane. This is the first time they have been truly outside, truly suspended, truly aware of the depth below them.

---

## WHAT THEY SEE FROM THE WALKWAY

**Looking down:**
> *The mist is thick today.*
> *The canyon floor is not visible.*
>
> *Below one of the lower platforms: movement.*
> *Large. Slow. Graceful.*
>
> *The Canyon Mantas.*
> *Both of them.*
> *Circling.*
> *In the thermal that rises from the canyon floor.*
> *They have been using this thermal*
> *since the palace was built.*

**Pathfinder:**
> *The bond with the Ossivane manta reaches this far.*
> *Faintly.*
> *Like hearing a familiar voice across a large room.*
> *They are aware of her.*
> *They are not concerned.*
> *They have been riding the air*
> *since before any of this existed.*

**Sigg — watching the mantas:**
> *"I've been thinking about continuity.*
> *About what persists.*
> *The mosaic floor in the Exhibition Hall.*
> *The child's handprint.*
> *These animals.*
>
> *The people who built all of this — they're gone.*
> *The Trade Federation is gone.*
> *The Separatists are gone.*
> *The Jedi are almost gone.*
>
> *The mantas are still here.*
> *The canyon is still here.*
> *The handprint is still here.*
>
> *I find that — I don't know what I find that.*
> *I find it something."*

**Looking at the palace:**
> *From the walkway you can see*

> *The building extends below you —*
> *Level 15, 16, 17, 18 —*

>
> *The windows of the meeting hall*

> *Some broken.*
> *Dark behind the intact ones.*

## THE DARK SIDE — THIRD SIGN

**GM NOTE:** This one is stronger. This one does not disappear. Time it to the moment the party looks at the palace windows below.

> *The Pathfinder feels it before anyone sees it.*

> *"Something is—"*
>
> *And then they all feel it.*
> *The way you notice when the sound in a room*
> *changes without any identifiable cause.*
>
> *The walkway is the same.*
> *The canyon is the same.*
> *The mantas below are still circling.*
>
> *But something about the air*
> *has changed quality.*
> *Something about standing on this walkway*
> *looking at those windows*
> *is different from what it was*
> *thirty seconds ago.*

**Pathfinder:**
> *Through the Force:*
> *The presence she felt in Level 13*
> *is closer.*
> *Closer and more defined.*
> *It is not one thing.*
> *It is multiple things.*
> *All below.*
> *All aware of her.*
>
> *Not hostile.*
> *Not welcoming.*
> *Waiting.*
> *The way things wait*
> *when they have been waiting*
> *for a very long time*
> *and have learned patience.*

**Sigg:**
> *He feels something too.*
> *He does not have her sensitivity*
> *but he has been Force-sensitive long enough*
> *to recognize the texture of wrongness.*
>
> *He does not look away from the windows.*
>
> *"They're in the palace," he says.*
> *Quietly.*
> *"Whatever they are.*
> *They're in the palace.*
> *Not here."*
>
> *He picks up his pace.*
> *"Then let's not keep them waiting."*

## EXIT NOTE FOR GM

The party leaves Level 13 knowing:

- The cease-fire was genuine — the delegation had full authority to end the war
- N'got warned them. In writing. They came anyway.
- Warde's instability was documented before the meeting happened
- Moraband was marked red on the Trade Federation's maps — no label, just red
- Something is waiting in the palace. Multiple somethings. They know the party is coming.
- The palace windows are visible from the walkway — the party has seen the room from outside
- The delegation's last message was optimistic — they expected to succeed

**What they carry forward:**
The cease-fire documents. The knowledge that N'got did everything right and it still happened. The red dot with no label. The cold that came and did not fully leave. The memory of the mantas circling below. The sight of the palace windows from the outside.

**The question they carry into Level 14:**
*If they knew — if everyone knew — what is the difference between a tragedy and a crime?*
