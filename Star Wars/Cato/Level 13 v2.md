
# LEVEL 13: THE DIPLOMATIC QUARTER APPROACH
## *"People came here from across the galaxy to talk instead of fight. That counts for something."*

---

## GM OVERVIEW

**Purpose:** This level represents the Trade Federation at its most idealistic—a place where diplomacy was conducted, relationships were built, and peace was negotiated. The party moves through the diplomatic quarter, discovering the Federation's commitment to dialogue and the tragic irony of what happened here. The level ends with an exterior walkway overlooking the canyon, where the party sees the palace windows for the first time and feels the full weight of what awaits them.

**Tone:** Starts dignified and historical. Gets heavier as the party discovers the cease-fire documents. The canyon walkway is physically and emotionally exposing—the first time the party has been truly outside since the Ossivane. The dark side presence is stronger here, building toward the palace.

**Key GM Goals:**
- Show the Trade Federation's diplomatic ideal
- Reveal the cease-fire documents and the delegation's good faith
- Establish N'got's role as a warning that went unheeded
- Create a powerful moment on the exterior walkway
- Build the dark side presence toward the palace
- Keep narrative engaging for players with attention challenges

## ENTERING LEVEL 13

### The Passage from Level 12

The gap the manta made closes behind them as they move into the passage connecting Level 12 to Level 13.

The passage is short—twenty meters—and it changes as they walk it.

**GM Description:**
> *"The stone gets darker. One shade. Maybe two. You might not notice if you weren't paying attention. But you are paying attention now."*

> *"The joins between the stones get tighter. More precise. The same hands that built the Exhibition Hall built this—but these hands were working on something they knew mattered more. This stone was chosen more carefully. This stone was meant to last longer."*

> *"The ceiling rises. Three meters in the service passage. Four in the Exhibition Hall. Here: five. You are going somewhere that expected important people."*

**The Smell:**

> *"Something changes in the air. Not bad. Not good. Specific. Old paper. Old fabric. The smell of rooms that held meetings. The smell of decisions."*

### Sigg's Observation

> *"Sigg stops, inhales deeply. 'This is what diplomacy smells like,' he says. 'Old paper, old fabric, old decisions. The smell of people trying to solve problems without killing each other.'"*

> *"He looks around at the stone, the higher ceiling, the more precise construction. 'They built this place for that. For talking. For solving problems. They believed it was possible.'"*

## THE DIPLOMATIC QUARTER — INTERIOR

### The Corridor

The passage opens into a wide corridor—the widest the party has seen in the ventral city. Four meters across. Five meters high. The chemical lighting here is full and steady—this level is on the main palace backup system, which has more reserve than the exhibition hall above.

Everything is lit. Equally. Completely.

**GM Description:**
> *"The corridor stretches ahead of you. Forty meters. Doors on both sides. Not maintenance doors. Formal doors."*

> *"Each one with a different diplomatic seal carved into the stone above the frame."*

> *"You recognize some of them. The Banking Clan. The Commerce Guild. The Techno Union. Others you don't know—smaller factions, independent systems, trading partners the Trade Federation maintained relationships with across three centuries."*

> *"Each seal above each door. Each door representing a relationship. Forty meters of relationships. Most of them gone now."*

### What the Seals Mean

**GM Description:**
> *"You walk past the doors. Each seal tells a story. Some you know—the Banking Clan's wealth, the Commerce Guild's reach, the Techno Union's ambition. Others are mysteries—symbols from worlds and species you've never encountered."*

> *"Forty meters of diplomacy. Forty meters of trust. Forty meters of relationships that built the Trade Federation's power."*

> *"And now, forty meters of silence."*

### Sigg's Observation

> *"Sigg walks slowly, looking at each seal. 'They had relationships with all of these people. They built rooms for them. They accommodated their customs. They created spaces where they could talk.'"*

> *"'And then the war came. And the talking stopped. And all of this—all of these relationships—they just... ended.'"*

> *"He stops at a seal he doesn't recognize. 'I wonder if anyone remembers who was in this room. I wonder if anyone remembers what they talked about.'"*

## THE ROOMS — SELECT AS NEEDED

### ROOM A — THE BANKING CLAN SUITE

**GM Description:**
> *"The Banking Clan seal above the door: a circle bisected by a horizontal line. Wealth above. Debt below. The whole philosophy of the Muun in a single symbol."*

> *"Inside: a meeting room. Small. Private. A round table—not the Trade Federation's formal rectangular style. A round table means no head. No hierarchy. Whoever sat here was considered equal for the duration of the meeting."*

> *"On the table: a ledger. Open. The columns are in Muun script but the numbers translate. These are trade figures. Enormous trade figures. The kind of numbers that represent not goods but systems. Whole economies, flowing through this table."*

> *"And at the bottom of the last page, in Basic, in handwriting that is not the careful administrative script of the ledger—someone's personal handwriting:"*

> *"'If we stop talking, the numbers stop mattering. Remind them of this at the start of every session. They forget. They always forget.'"*

**GM NOTE:** This is advice from a Banking Clan negotiator to whoever came after them. Left in the ledger because the ledger was coming back. The negotiator expected to return.

### Sigg's Observation

> *"Sigg reads the note. He reads it twice."*

> *"'They knew. They knew that the talking was the important part. The numbers were just... the evidence of the talking. If you stop talking, the numbers stop meaning anything.'"*

> *"He looks at the empty chairs around the round table. 'They expected to come back. They left this note for their successors. They thought there would be successors.'"*

---

### ROOM B — THE UNKNOWN SEAL SUITE

**GM Description:**
> *"This seal you don't recognize. Smaller than the others. A stylized creature—something with wings, native to a world you can't identify. Below the creature, three words in a script that is not Basic and not Pak Pak."*

> *"The room inside is different from the others. No meeting table. Instead: cushions on the floor. A low platform against the far wall that might be an altar or might be a stage. Whatever species came here conducted their diplomacy differently. The Trade Federation accommodated this. They built a room for it."*

> *"On the platform: a single object. A small carved figure. Left behind. Not forgotten—placed. Deliberately set on the platform before whoever came here left."*

> *"An offering. Or a marker. Or a goodbye."*

**If the Pathfinder touches it:**

> *"Nothing through the Force. Just stone. Old stone. Smooth from handling. Someone held this many times before they set it down here."*

### Sigg's Observation

> *"Sigg stands at the doorway, not entering. He looks at the cushions, the platform, the carved figure."*

> *"'They built this for someone. Someone who didn't use tables, who didn't sit in chairs, who conducted their diplomacy differently. And the Federation built a room for them. They accommodated them. They made space for them.'"*

> *"'That's what diplomacy is. Making space for the other person. Meeting them where they are.'"*

> *"He looks at the carved figure. 'They left something behind. An offering. A marker. A goodbye. They knew they might not come back.'"*

---

### ROOM C — THE PREPARATION LIBRARY

**GM Description:**
> *"This room is different from the suites—larger, lined with shelves, a reference library for delegations preparing for negotiations."*

> *"Most of the shelves are empty—the books and datapads were taken in the looting. But the shelves themselves remain, and on some of them, objects too heavy or too awkward to carry easily were left behind."*

> *"A globe. Mounted on a stand. It shows the galaxy as it was at the height of the Trade Federation—the routes marked in gold, the Federation's territory shaded. The shaded area covers a third of the known galaxy."*

> *"And in the outer rim of the map, in a system with no trade routes touching it, a single dot. Marked in red. No label. No route. Just the dot. Red."*

**If anyone examines it closely:**

> *"The dot is in the correct position for the Horuset system. For Moraband. Red. No label. The Trade Federation knew it was there. They marked it. They marked it in red and put no label on it and left it that way."*

### Sigg's Observation

> *"He looks at the dot for a long moment."*

> *"'They knew something was wrong with it. They just didn't say what.'"*

> *"He traces the edge of the map with his finger. 'They marked all the routes. All the relationships. All the worlds they traded with. But this one—they marked it red. No label. No route. Just... warning. Caution.'"*

> *"'They knew Moraband existed. They knew something was there. They just didn't want to talk about it.'"*

## THE DARK SIDE — SECOND SIGN

**GM NOTE:** This happens in the library, or in whichever room the party is in when they are most absorbed. Time it to interrupt a moment of discovery.

**GM Description:**
> *"The temperature drops."*

> *"Not dramatically. Two degrees. Maybe three. Enough to notice. Enough that you can see your breath for just a moment."*

> *"And then it is gone. The temperature returns. The air is the same."*

**If the Pathfinder reaches through the Force:**

> *"Something is different from the corner in Level 12. That was an echo. This is a presence. Faint. Distant. Like hearing a voice in another room—you know someone is speaking but you cannot hear the words."*

> *"It is not here. It is below. It is coming from below. And it is aware that you are here."*

**Sigg — if she tells him:**

> *"He is quiet. Then: 'How far below?'"*

> *"She cannot tell him exactly. He nods. 'Keep moving then. Better to know what it is than to stand here wondering.'"*

## THE SEALED ARCHIVE

### The Door

At the end of the interior corridor, past the last of the diplomatic suites, there is a room that is different from all the others.

No seal above the door. No diplomatic designation. The door is heavier than the others—reinforced, the kind of door that was built to protect its contents.

It is locked. But the lock is mechanical—fifteen years old, no power required to bypass it.

**Skill Check:**
- **Average Skulduggery** to open
- On a failure: the mechanism jams
- **Hard Mechanics** to clear the jam
- On a Despair: the mechanism breaks
- The door can still be forced—**Hard Athletics**—but it will be loud. Very loud. Sound travels in the deep ventral.

**Inside:**
> *"A small room. No windows. Shelves on three walls. Most empty. But in the back corner, in a sealed case—waterproof, airtight, the kind used for irreplaceable documents—a stack of papers."*

### Sigg's Observation

> *"Sigg looks at the sealed case. 'Someone wanted to protect these. They knew they were important. They expected someone to come for them.'"*

> *"He looks at you. 'That someone is us.'"*

## THE CEASE-FIRE DOCUMENTS

**GM NOTE:** Read these slowly. One piece at a time. Let the player absorb each one before moving to the next. This is the most important discovery on this level.

---

### DOCUMENT 1 — Cover Sheet

> *"Briefing Package — Separatist Diplomatic Delegation. Destination: Sankar Palace, Jorra, Cato Neimoidia. Purpose: Cease-Fire Negotiations. Classification: Delegation Eyes Only."*

---

### DOCUMENT 2 — Objectives

> *"The delegation's goals, listed in order of priority:"*

> *"1. Establish a complete cease-fire for all of Cato Neimoidia."*
> *"2. Negotiate terms for Republic withdrawal from Jorra within sixty days."*
> *"3. Establish a neutral monitoring presence to verify compliance by both parties."*
> *"4. Create conditions for permanent peace talks to begin within ninety days of the cease-fire."*

> *"At the bottom, in the same administrative hand: 'These objectives were approved by the Separatist High Command. The delegation is authorized to reach agreement on all four points without further consultation.'"*

**GM NOTE:** They had the authority to end the war. Right here. Right now. At this table. Let that land before continuing.

### Sigg's Reaction

> *"Sigg reads the objectives. He doesn't speak for a moment."*

> *"'They had the authority. They didn't need to check with anyone. They could have ended the war. Right here. Right now. All of it.'"*

> *"'They came here to do that. They came here to make peace.'"*

> *"He looks at you. 'And Warde killed them for it.'"*

---

### DOCUMENT 3 — Republic Assessment

> *"A briefing on the Republic forces in Jorra. Clone trooper numbers. Defensive positions. Jedi presence."*

> *"Under Jedi presence:"*

> *"Jedi Knight Suljo Warde. Human male. Clone Wars veteran. Prognosticative ability assessed: HIGH. Ability appears to function at tactical and strategic level simultaneously. Recommend: No deception-based negotiating positions. He will know."*

### Sigg's Reaction

> *"Sigg reads this document carefully."*

> *"'They knew about Warde. They knew about his ability. They knew he could see through deception. So they planned to be honest.'"*

> *"'They were going to negotiate in good faith. They knew he would know if they weren't. So they were going to be honest.'"*

> *"'And he killed them anyway.'"*

---

### DOCUMENT 4 — The Risk Assessment

> *"A single page. Separate from the others. Different paper. Added later."*

> *"Risk Assessment — Addendum. Source: Embedded Observer [DESIGNATION REDACTED] Date: [REDACTED]"*

> *"Primary risk has been reassessed. Jedi Knight Warde's prognosticative ability presents less risk than previously assessed. His accuracy at the strategic level has declined over the past three months. His accuracy at the personal level—predicting individual actions—remains high."*

> *"Secondary risk elevated to PRIMARY. Psychological assessment of Jedi Knight Warde indicates potential instability under prolonged operational stress. Recommend: Keep negotiations brief. Reach agreement quickly. Do not allow extended session."*

> *"Note: Secondary risk assessment based on direct observation over eight months. Confidence: HIGH. This assessment should be shared with all delegation members. They should understand what they are walking into."*

**GM NOTE:** The embedded observer was N'got. The party knows this. They are holding N'got's assessment of Warde in their hands. He told them Warde was unstable. He told them to keep it brief. They did not keep it brief.

### Sigg's Reaction

> *"He reads it twice. Then he sets it down carefully."*

> *"'They knew. They all knew. N'got told them. He gave them every warning he had."*

> *"'They came anyway. Because the war was too costly to let one man's instability stop the talks."*

> *"'They were right about that. And they died for being right.'"*

> *"He looks at the document. 'N'got ran. I understand now why he ran. He had already done everything he could do. He had told them everything he knew. When it went wrong, there was nothing left for him to do except survive it."*

> *"'He has spent fifteen years thinking he failed them. He didn't fail them. He warned them. They made their choice. He made his.'"*

## THE EXTERIOR WALKWAY

### The Door

At the south end of the interior corridor, a door leads outside.

Not to another building. Outside.

**GM Description:**
> *"The door opens and the sound hits first. The canyon."*

> *"You have been hearing it as background—as context—since you entered the deep ventral. Out here it is not background. It is everything."*

> *"The walkway extends thirty meters ahead. Four meters wide. Metal grating underfoot. Railing on the right side. Nothing on the left."*

> *"You are beyond the edge of the bridge above. The bridge is no longer above you. The structure that supports this walkway goes up—cables, thick as your arm, vanishing into the bridge structure somewhere overhead—and down—the same cables, descending into the mist."*

> *"You are suspended between."*

> *"The walkway sways. Just slightly. Just enough to feel. Canyon wind. It does not stop."*

**GM NOTE:** Give everyone a moment with this. The physical exposure of the walkway is important. They have been in enclosed spaces since the Ossivane. This is the first time they have been truly outside, truly suspended, truly aware of the depth below them.

## WHAT THEY SEE FROM THE WALKWAY

### Looking Down

**GM Description:**
> *"The mist is thick today. The canyon floor is not visible."*

> *"What you can see: the cables descending, the lower platforms of the palace structure extending below you at different levels, the canyon walls on either side going down into white."*

> *"And below one of the lower platforms: movement. Large. Slow. Graceful."*

> *"The Canyon Mantas. Both of them. Circling. In the thermal that rises from the canyon floor and travels up along the palace's south face."*

> *"They are using the palace. The palace's structure creates their thermal. They have been using this thermal since the palace was built."*

**For the Pathfinder:**
> *"The bond with the Ossivane manta reaches this far. Faintly. Like hearing a familiar voice across a large room."*

> *"They are aware of her. They are not concerned. They are riding the air. They have been riding the air since before any of this existed."*

### Sigg's Reflection — Continuity

> *"He stands at the right railing and watches them for a long time."*

> *"'I've been thinking about continuity,' he says. Not to anyone specifically. 'About what persists. The mosaic floor in the Exhibition Hall. The child's handprint. These animals.'"*

> *"'The people who built all of this—they're gone. The Trade Federation is gone. The Separatists are gone. The Jedi are almost gone.'"*

> *"'The mantas are still here. The canyon is still here. The handprint is still here.'"*

> *"'I find that—I don't know what I find that. I find it something.'"*

### Looking Up

**GM Description:**
> *"The underside of the bridge above. From this angle you can see its full depth—the layers of construction, the structural elements, the ventral city hanging from it like something grown downward."*

> *"And above that, the upper city. The factory district. The residential blocks. The workers on their schedules. The cargo haulers on their tracks."*

> *"All of that is up there. Right now. While you are down here. The two worlds existing simultaneously. The one that continues and the one that stopped."*

### Looking at the Palace

**GM Description:**
> *"From the walkway you can see the full south face of Sankar Palace. The building extends below you—Level 15, 16, 17, 18—each level slightly smaller than the one above, the whole structure tapering toward the canyon."*

> *"The windows of the meeting hall are visible from here. Some intact. Some broken. Dark behind the intact ones. Open air behind the broken ones."*

> *"That is the room. Right there. You can see the windows of the room from here."*

## THE DARK SIDE — THIRD SIGN

**GM NOTE:** This one is stronger. This one does not disappear. Time it to the moment the party looks at the palace windows below.

**GM Description:**
> *"The Pathfinder feels it before anyone sees it."*

> *"'Something is—'"*

> *"And then they all feel it. Or not feel—notice. The way you notice when the sound in a room changes without any identifiable cause."*

> *"The walkway is the same. The canyon is the same. The mantas below are still circling."*

> *"But something about the air has changed quality. Something about standing on this walkway looking at those windows is different from what it was thirty seconds ago."*

**For the Pathfinder specifically:**
> *"Through the Force: The presence she felt in Level 13 is closer. Closer and more defined. It is not one thing. It is multiple things. All below. All aware of her."*

> *"Not hostile. Not welcoming. Waiting. The way things wait when they have been waiting for a very long time and have learned patience."*

### Sigg's Response

> *"He feels something too. He does not have her sensitivity but he has been Force-sensitive long enough to recognize the texture of wrongness."*

> *"He does not look away from the windows."*

> *"'They're in the palace,' he says. Quietly. 'Whatever they are. They're in the palace. Not here.'"*

> *"He picks up his pace. 'Then let's not keep them waiting.'"*

## CROSSING THE WALKWAY

**GM Description:**
> *"The thirty meters of walkway. The sway of the structure beneath your feet. The canyon sound. The mantas below. The palace windows ahead."*

> *"You cross."*

> *"At the far end: another door. Heavier than the one you came through. Stone. The Trade Federation's formal motifs. A handle."*

> *"You open it."*

> *"The air inside is different again. Colder than the walkway. Colder than anything above. The chemical lights in the passage beyond are dimmer than the levels above—the backup system here is running lower."*

> *"The passage slopes downward. Gently. But unmistakably."*

> *"You are going further in. You are going further down."*

## EXIT NOTE FOR GM

The party leaves Level 13 knowing:

- The cease-fire was genuine—the delegation had full authority to end the war
- N'got warned them. In writing. They came anyway.
- Warde's instability was documented before the meeting happened
- Moraband was marked red on the Trade Federation's maps—no label, just red
- Something is waiting in the palace. Multiple somethings. They know the party is coming.
- The palace windows are visible from the walkway—the party has seen the room from outside

**What they carry forward:**
The cease-fire documents. The knowledge that N'got did everything right and it still happened. The red dot with no label. The cold that came and did not fully leave. The memory of the mantas circling below.

**The question they are carrying into Level 14:**
*If they knew—if everyone knew—what is the difference between a tragedy and a crime?*
