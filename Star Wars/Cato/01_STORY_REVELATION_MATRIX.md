# Story Revelation Matrix - What the Palace Teaches About Warde

The PCs should never need a single exposition scene to understand Warde. This matrix distributes the story across rooms, objects, and events.

| Stage | Environmental evidence | What the PCs can reasonably infer | What should remain unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|

## Redundancy map for mandatory truths

Each mandatory truth has at least three possible sources.

### The delegates were sincere

- Level 13 authorization and working documents.
- Level 14 communications log: negotiations proceeding / good spirits.
- Level 15 Warde notation board: he explicitly distinguishes sincere delegates from a potentially treacherous command structure.

### Warde was deteriorating before the meeting

- Testimony from Tacker and prior investigation.
- Warde's Level 15 notes about exhaustion and inability to hold branching futures.
- Physical precision in his room: an overcontrolled environment around a person losing internal control.

### Warde chose the attack

- Level 15 notation board shows premeditation.
- Level 14 memory fragment shows the two-second hesitation.
- Level 16 physical geometry shows a deliberate rise from his chair and first strike.

### The other Jedi tried to stop him

- Prior testimony.
- Crossed lightsaber marks and movement arcs in the hall.
- Durael's body and damaged lightsaber on the balcony; Kaddo's message/echo.

### Moraband is the next destination

- Earlier red-dot clues establish the world as ominous and known to Federation navigators.
- Departure-vector evidence can point generally toward the relevant region.
- **Only the crystal/Gatekeeper should be treated as the final reliable confirmation that Warde intended to go there.**

## Optional truths for explorers

These are rewards, not mandatory plot gates:

- who looted the Exhibition Hall and where some objects ended up;
- Eleth Sarrino's role as facility manager and the staff culture she created;
- Pelri Dass's personal stone and the emotional life of the delegation;
- why the chemical-light system lasted so long;
- whether the droids have formed anything resembling habit, identity, or preference;
- whether the seven presences are literal spirits, Force echoes, or a composite memory phenomenon;
- what the lower anchor crews did during the evacuation;
- which Jorra elites now possess stolen cultural objects.
