# ZONE 3 — THE CONFERENCE ROOMS

---

## GM NOTE — HOW TO RUN THE ROOMS

> There are several conference rooms on this level.
> The party can explore them or move directly to the meeting hall.
> Room 1 (the large preliminary conference room) and Room 2 (the seating chart) are the most important.
> Let the player choose. Don't force all of them.

---

## ROOM 1 — THE LARGE PRELIMINARY CONFERENCE ROOM

**GM Description:**

> *The door is open.*
> *Inside: a large conference room.*
>
> *Tables pushed back against the walls.*
> *Not overturned —*
> *pushed back deliberately.*
> *The way you push back tables*
> *when you need to change the configuration of a space.*
>
> *Whatever was happening in here*
> *when the evacuation came —*
> *someone took time to move the furniture.*
>
> *This is striking, given what was apparently happening*
> *on the floor below.*
>
> *Someone in this room, at the moment of crisis,*
> *moved the furniture.*
>
> *Some habits do not break even in extremity.*

**What They Learn:**
- Even during the evacuation, people followed protocol
- They expected to return
- They took time to prepare the room for the next use
- The next use never came

**On the largest table:**

> *Still in the configuration of an interrupted working session:*
> *Position papers.*
> *Draft language.*
> *A seating chart.*
> *The cease-fire terms in their preliminary form.*

---

## ROOM 2 — THE SEATING CHART — N'GOT'S POSITION

**GM NOTE:** This is the key discovery on this level. Run it slowly.

**GM Description:**

> *The seating chart is a single page.*

>
> *The off-side position is labeled:*
> *"Tactical Support — Republic Military."*
>
> *N'got's embedded position.*
>
> *He sat here during the preliminary discussions.*
> *He listened and reported and watched.*
> *This is where he was.*

**Sigg — looking at the off-side position:**

> *He looks at it for a long time.*
>
> *"He watched everything from there.*
> *He had the best angle on the room.*
> *He saw Warde's face during the whole thing.*
> *He saw the decision when it was forming.*
> *He saw the two seconds.*
>

>
> *He is not judging N'got.*

> *and N'got survived*

**What They Learn:**
- N'got was here
- He had the best position to watch
- He saw Warde's face
- He saw the decision forming
- He saw the two seconds
- He ran

# OPTIONAL COMBAT ENCOUNTER — BX COMMANDO DROIDS

---

## GM DECISION POINT

**Use this if:**
- The session needs action
- The player is energized and wants a fight
- You want to escalate tension before the meeting hall
- The party is too comfortable

**Skip this if:**
- The player is deeply engaged in the narrative and wants to move to the meeting hall
- The session is running long
- You want to preserve the emotional weight of the meeting hall

Any point in Level 16 — the corridor, the conference rooms, or just before the meeting hall doors.

**GM NOTE:** This is the room. Everything — every level, every document, every NPC conversation — has been leading to this room. Run it slowly. Let each discovery breathe.

**GM Description:**

> *You step through the doors.*
>
> *The room is large.*

> *large in the way of a room*
> *designed for serious work*
> *rather than spectacle.*

> *as the rest of the conference level*
> *but finished here to a higher degree —*
> *smooth, polished, the joins between stones*
> *barely visible.*
>

> *Purple-white, chemical, steady.*
> *No shadows.*
> *Everything in this room is visible.*

> *is almost impossible to damage.*

> *It resists moisture.*

## EXIT NOTE FOR GM

**The party leaves Level 16 knowing:**

- N'got sat in the off-side chair and watched. He saw the two seconds. He ran.
- The delegation prepared in these rooms. They believed they would succeed.
- The meeting hall has been waiting for fifteen years.
- The table has cuts in it. The oak outlasted everything.
- The one presence is Warde. Changed. Darkened. Still present.
- The two seconds still exist in the Force. The moment of hesitation. The choice that could have been different.
- The kyber crystal is somewhere in this room. Durael held onto it after he couldn't hold onto anything else.

**What they carry forward:**
The seating chart. N'got's position. The weight of the two seconds. The knowledge that the one presence is Warde himself.

**The question they carry into the balcony:**
*What has fifteen years done to the man who hesitated for two seconds?*
