## GM NOTE — HOW TO RUN THE MURALS **[ENHANCED]**

> Keep moving. Don't stop at every panel for five minutes.
> Read each panel description in 30-45 seconds.
> Let Sigg add ONE line after each.
> Let the player react or move on.
> The panels build on each other — each one changes how the next one lands.
> Panel 6 is the big stop. Everything else is buildup.
>
> **NEW:** After each panel, ask the player one quick question:
> - "What stands out to you in this panel?"
> - "What do you notice that Sigg might have missed?"
> - "Does anything in this panel remind you of something you've seen before?"
>
> This keeps the player engaged and makes them an active participant.
