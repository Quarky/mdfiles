# LEVEL 14 — THE THRESHOLD
### *"This is where the building stops being diplomatic and starts being something else."*

---

## GM BRIEFING

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Story Purpose** | The pivot point. Everything above this level was about the Trade Federation. Everything below is about what happened to it. This level is the hinge. |
| **Tone** | Starts formal and beautiful. Gets heavier with every room. Ends with the Force ghost encounter. |
| **Pacing** | Slow burn. Let each room breathe. The player should feel the weight accumulating before the encounter hits. |
| **Dark Side** | This is where it arrives. Not foreshadowing anymore. Present. The Force ghosts appear at the end of this level. |
| **Key Emotional Beat** | The murals. Specifically the last mural — the watching figure. |
| **Key Story Beat** | The Force ghost encounter. The party learns what the presences are before they reach the palace. |
| **Animals** | None. The Canyon Mantas are below. Nothing else comes this far. The Pathfinder notices the absence. |
| **Exit Hook** | The Force ghosts step aside. The party descends. Something follows. |

---

## ENTERING LEVEL 14

The passage from the walkway door slopes downward
for approximately thirty meters before leveling out.

The party walks it in silence because the passage
does not invite conversation. The ceiling is four meters.
The walls are the darkest stone yet — almost black
in the glow rod light, the joins between blocks
so precise they are nearly invisible.

> *This stone was not quarried from the canyon walls.*
> *This stone came from somewhere else.*
> *Brought here at significant cost*
> *because whoever designed this level*
> *wanted the approach to feel different*
> *from everything above.*
>
> *It does.*

> *The passage ends.*
> *Not in a door.*
> *In an opening.*
> *An arch.*
> *Four meters high. Three meters wide.*
> *No door.*
> *The arch is the threshold.*
> *You step through it.*

## THE FIRST THING THEY NOTICE

> *The smell is gone.*
>
> *The smell of old paper and old fabric*
> *that has been present since Level 13 —*
> *the smell of meetings and decisions —*
> *is gone.*
>
> *What replaces it:*
> *Stone.*
> *Just stone.*
> *Cold and clean and very old.*
> *The smell of something that has been sealed*
> *long enough that even the air*
> *has become part of the architecture.*

> *And the sound.*
>
> *The canyon sound — the low harmonic*
> *that has been present since the walkway —*
> *is here too.*
> *But different.*
> *In the levels above it was background.*
> *Here it resonates.*
> *The geometry of this level catches it*
> *and holds it.*
> *The canyon is singing to itself*
> *in this room*
> *and has been singing to itself*
> *for fifteen years.*

**For the Pathfinder — immediately on entering:**

> *The animals are gone.*
> *Not just the geckos.*
> *Everything.*
> *She has been aware of life in the ventral city —*
> *the mantas, the stalker waiting above,*
> *the spider colonies, the geckos —*
> *as a kind of background warmth*
> *in the Force.*
>
> *Here: nothing.*
> *The background warmth is absent.*
> *She is not in a place where animals live.*
> *She is in a place where animals*
> *have decided not to come.*
>
> *The difference is specific*
> *and she feels it immediately.*

> *She does not need to say this out loud.*
> *Sigg watches her face when she enters.*
> *He knows.*
>
> *"How empty?" he asks.*
>
> *She tells him.*
>
> *He nods once.*
> *"Then we move carefully."*

## THE LEVEL 14 CORRIDOR — THE MURALS

The arch opens into the corridor that defines Level 14.
Sixty meters long. Four meters wide. Six meters high.
The widest corridor the party has encountered
in the deep ventral.

The walls are covered in murals.

**GM NOTE:** These murals are the centrepiece
of Level 14's first half. Walk the party through
them slowly. Each panel is a beat.
The player is reading Neimoidian history
as they walk toward the room where that history ended.

---

### THE MURALS — READING THEM AS YOU WALK

> *The murals begin at the arch behind you*
> *and run the full length of the corridor.*
> *Floor to ceiling on both sides.*
> *Painted onto plaster applied directly to the stone.*
> *The colors are extraordinary —*
> *deep blues and golds and the specific green*
> *that appears throughout Neimoidian art*
> *to represent life and continuity.*
>
> *They have been here for forty years.*
> *The sealed air has preserved them perfectly.*
> *They look like they were finished last week.*

---

**PANEL 1 — THE HOMEWORLD** *(Left wall, near the arch)*

> *The Neimoidian homeworld.*
> *Not as a planet seen from space —*
> *as a place lived in.*
> *The great hatchery lakes,*
> *the same lakes from the Exhibition Hall mosaic,*
> *but here rendered with more detail.*
> *You can see individual figures*
> *tending the grub pools.*
> *You can see the structures they built*
> *along the lake shores.*
> *You can see children.*
>
> *At the bottom of the panel:*
> *a single line in Pak Pak.*

**Translation** *(if anyone reads it or the Pathfinder asks the gatekeeper):*

> *"From water we came.*
> *To water we will return.*
> *Between those two moments: everything."*

---

**PANEL 2 — THE FIRST DEPARTURE** *(Right wall)*

> *The first colony ships.*
> *Not the heroic departure of legend —*
> *the actual departure.*
> *People carrying things.*
> *People looking back.*
> *One figure — center of the panel —*
> *facing forward.*
> *Not looking back.*
> *Her expression is not brave.*
> *It is resolved.*
> *There is a difference.*
>
> *She is carrying nothing.*
> *Her hands are empty.*
> *She left everything.*
> *She is going.*

**Sigg — quietly:**

> *"That's the one I keep looking at.*
> *Everyone else is carrying something.*
> *She's not.*
> *She decided the thing you carry*
> *slows you down."*

---

**PANEL 3 — THE PURSE WORLDS** *(Left wall, center)*

> *The expansion.*
> *Maps and routes and new cities*
> *growing on new worlds.*
> *Trade routes rendered as lines of light*
> *connecting the Neimoidian worlds*
> *to each other and to the wider galaxy.*
> *More species appear here —*
> *Humans, Duros, Sullustans, others —*
> *at the edges of the image,*
> *meeting Neimoidian traders.*
>
> *The meetings look equal.*
> *Both sides leaning in.*
> *Both sides interested.*
> *Commerce as conversation.*

> *In the lower left corner:*
> *a small detail.*
> *A Neimoidian trader and a Human merchant*
> *shaking hands.*
> *The Neimoidian's expression is cautious.*
> *The Human's expression is open.*
> *They are both uncertain.*
> *They are both trying.*

---

**PANEL 4 — THE TRADE FEDERATION** *(Right wall, center)*

> *The Federation at its height.*
> *This is the celebratory panel —*
> *the one that was commissioned*
> *to make visiting dignitaries feel*
> *the weight of what they were dealing with.*
> *Ships. Routes. Commerce.*
> *A galaxy connected by Neimoidian organization.*
>
> *At the center of the panel:*
> *Cato Neimoidia.*
> *This city.*
> *This bridge.*
> *Rendered from above,*
> *the canyon visible below it,*
> *trade routes radiating outward*
> *in every direction.*
>
> *At the edge of the panel,*
> *in the very corner,*
> *barely visible:*
> *a red dot.*
> *No routes touching it.*
> *No label.*
> *Just the dot.*

**GM NOTE:** Moraband. Again.
The Trade Federation put it in their official art
and still didn't label it.
They knew it was there.
They knew enough not to name it.

---

**PANEL 5 — THE WARS** *(Left wall, near the far end)*

> *This panel is different.*
> *The colors change.*
> *The blues and golds give way to grays.*
> *The figures are smaller.*
> *More of them.*
> *Grouped differently —*
> *not in the organized arrangements*
> *of commerce and expansion*
> *but in the clustering patterns of people*
> *who are afraid.*
>
> *The Clone Wars.*
> *Rendered without glorification.*
> *Droids on one side.*
> *Clone troopers on the other.*
> *And between them:*
> *the city.*
> *This city.*
> *People in the streets.*
> *Not fighting.*
> *Watching.*
> *Unable to leave.*
> *Unable to stop it.*
>
> *One figure in the foreground —*
> *civilian, Neimoidian, looking directly*
> *out of the painting.*
> *At you.*
> *Their expression is not accusatory.*
> *It is exhausted.*
> *It says:*
> *You were not here.*
> *This happened.*
> *You were not here.*

**GM NOTE:** Hold this one. Let the player look at it.

---

**PANEL 6 — THE LAST PANEL** *(Right wall, far end)*

> *This panel is the most elaborate.*
> *The most expensive.*
> *You can tell because the detail is finer*
> *and the colors are richer*
> *and the figures are larger than in any other panel.*
>
> *It shows Jorra.*
> *At peace.*
> *The bridge-city from above,*
> *the same view as Panel 4,*
> *but different.*
> *The trade routes are still there.*
> *The canyon is still there.*
> *But the ships in the image are not Trade Federation ships.*
> *They are — many things.*
> *Many species.*
> *A future in which Cato Neimoidia*
> *is not the center of a Neimoidian empire*
> *but a place in a larger community.*
> *Open.*
> *Connected.*
> *At peace.*
>
> *This panel was the vision.*
> *What they were working toward.*
> *What the cease-fire was supposed to begin.*

> *And in the lower right corner:*

> *A single figure.*
> *Standing apart from the celebration.*
> *Not participating.*
> *Not celebrating.*
> *Looking outward.*
> *Looking at you.*
>
> *Not Neimoidian.*
> *Human.*
> *In Jedi robes.*
> *Standing at the edge of the future*
> *that never happened.*
> *Looking out of the painting.*
> *At you.*
>
> *His expression is not angry.*
> *Not guilty.*
> *Not defiant.*
>
> *He looks like a man who knows something*
> *is wrong and cannot find the words for it.*
> *He looks like a man who came*
> *to a place where important things happen*
> *and understood, too late,*
> *what important means.*
>
> *He looks like Suljo Warde.*

**GM NOTE:** He is not Warde. This panel was painted
before Warde came to Jorra. The artist did not
know Warde existed. The figure is meant to be
a generic Republic representative — a symbol
of the peace that was supposed to come.

The resemblance is coincidence.
Or it is the Force.
Or both.

Do not explain this to the player.
Let them hold the uncertainty.

**Sigg — standing in front of the panel:**

> *He looks at the figure for a long time.*
>
> *"Did the artist know?" he asks.*
> *He is asking himself.*
> *Not expecting an answer.*
>
> *"Or does the Force just —"*
> *He stops.*
> *Starts again.*
> *"Sometimes I think the Force has been*
> *trying to tell this story*
> *for longer than any of us have been alive.*
> *And we're just the ones who finally*
> *showed up to hear it."*

## THE DARK SIDE — ARRIVING

**GM NOTE:** After the last mural. Before the rooms.
This is where it becomes present rather than approaching.
Do not rush it. Build it word by word.

> *You stand at the end of the mural corridor.*
> *The last panel behind you.*
> *The corridor ahead opens into a wider space —*
> *the central chamber of Level 14,*
> *the main reception area*
> *of the diplomatic threshold.*
>
> *The chemical lights are still working here.*
> *They are all working.*
> *The space ahead is fully lit.*
>
> *But the light feels different.*

> *Not dimmer.*
> *Different.*
> *The same chemical purple-white*
> *but as if something in the room ahead*
> *is absorbing some quality of it.*
> *Not the brightness.*
> *Something else.*
> *Something that light carries*
> *beyond illumination.*

**For the Pathfinder:**

> *It hits her before they enter the chamber.*
>
> *Not like Level 12.*
> *Not like Level 13.*
> *Those were echoes.*
> *Those were the sound of something*
> *after it had already stopped.*
>
> *This is not an echo.*
>
> *This is present tense.*

> *Multiple presences.*
> *She counted them on the walkway — approximately.*
> *She can count them precisely now.*
>
> *Seven.*
>
> *Seven presences.*
> *In the chamber ahead.*
> *Stationary.*
> *They have not moved toward her.*
> *They are not retreating.*
> *They are — arranged.*
> *The way people arrange themselves*
> *when they are waiting for something specific.*
>
> *"There are seven of them," she says.*

**Sigg:**

> *He stops walking.*
>
> *A long pause.*
>
> *"Seven," he repeats.*
>
> *The Separatist delegation had seven members.*
> *He knows this.*
> *The party knows this.*
> *The number lands.*
>
> *"All right," he says.*
> *His voice is even.*
> *He is afraid and he is managing it*
> *with the precision of someone*
> *who has managed fear before.*
> *"We go in.*
> *We don't run.*
> *Whatever they are —*
> *they have been here for fifteen years.*
> *They have had fifteen years to hurt someone.*
> *They haven't.*
> *That tells us something."*
>
> *He looks at her.*
> *"Ready?"*

## THE CENTRAL CHAMBER — THE FORCE GHOST ENCOUNTER

> *You step into the chamber.*

The central chamber of Level 14 is the largest
interior space in the deep ventral outside the
Exhibition Hall. Circular. Twenty meters across.
The ceiling domes above — a deliberate architectural
choice to create a sense of open space,
of neutrality, of a place that belongs to no one.

A place where any delegation from any world
could stand and feel welcomed rather than dominated.

> *It is empty.*
> *Or it looks empty.*
>
> *And then it is not.*

---

### THE APPEARANCE

**GM NOTE:** Do this slowly. One element at a time.
Let each detail accumulate before adding the next.

> *The first sign is the temperature.*
> *The room drops.*
> *Not two degrees like Level 13.*
> *Ten degrees.*
> *Instantly.*
> *Your breath is visible.*
> *All of your breath.*
> *Clouds of it in the chemical light.*

> *The second sign is the light.*
> *The sconces on the walls*
> *begin to behave differently.*
> *Not flickering.*
> *Something more specific.*
> *The light nearest the center of the room*
> *dims slightly.*
> *As if something is standing between the sconce*
> *and where you are.*
> *As if something is casting a shadow.*
> *Without being visible.*

> *And then they are visible.*

> *Not all at once.*
> *One.*
> *Then two.*
> *Then all seven.*
> *Arriving the way fog arrives —*
> *not moving in from somewhere else*
> *but becoming present*
> *in the space they are already in.*

> *Seven figures.*
> *Neimoidian.*
> *In formal diplomatic robes —*
> *the specific robes of the Trade Federation's*
> *diplomatic corps.*
> *Floor length. Layered.*
> *The specific colors of formal Neimoidian rank.*
>
> *They are not solid.*
> *You can see the wall through them.*
> *But they are not insubstantial either.*
> *They are present in a way that*
> *demands acknowledgment.*
> *You cannot look past them.*
> *You cannot pretend they are not there.*

> *They are arranged in the center of the chamber.*
> *Seven of them.*
> *In the configuration of a meeting:*
> *six in a loose arc*
> *and one slightly forward.*
> *The one who would have spoken first.*
> *The delegation's leader.*

> *They are looking at you.*
> *All seven.*
> *They have been looking at you*
> *since you walked in.*

---

### THE SILENCE

**GM NOTE:** Do not break this silence immediately.
Let it hold for a moment.
The player should feel the weight of it.
Then:

> *The lead figure — the one slightly forward —*
> *opens their mouth.*
>
> *No sound.*
>
> *They try again.*
> *No sound.*
>
> *Whatever they were*
> *before they became this,*
> *whatever mechanism speech required,*
> *it is no longer available to them.*
> *They cannot speak.*
> *They have been here for fifteen years*
> *unable to speak*
> *and unable to leave*
> *and they have something to say.*

**For the Pathfinder:**

> *Through the Force —*
> *not through hearing, not through sight —*
> *something comes through.*
>
> *Not words.*
> *Not images.*
> *The feeling beneath words.*
> *The emotional content of what they would say*
> *if they could say it.*
>
> *It comes in layers.*
> *She has to hold it and let it arrive.*

**GM NOTE:** Read these one at a time.
Pause between each.

---

**LAYER 1:**

> *We came in good faith.*

---

**LAYER 2:**

> *We knew the risk.*
> *We read the assessment.*
> *We came anyway.*
> *Because the alternative was more war.*
> *Because more war meant more of this.*
> *More cities like this one.*
> *More families like the ones in the apartments above.*
> *We calculated that our lives*
> *were worth the attempt.*
> *We were right to calculate that.*
> *The mathematics do not comfort us*
> *but they were correct.*

---

**LAYER 3:**

> *We do not hate him.*
> *We want you to know that.*
> *We have had fifteen years to decide*
> *whether we hate him.*
> *We do not.*
> *We understand what happened to him.*
> *We understand it better now*
> *than we did when it happened to us.*
> *We have had a long time to understand things.*
>
> *We pity him.*
> *That is harder than hate.*
> *Hate has an object you can push against.*
> *Pity has nothing to push against.*
> *Pity just sits with you.*

---

**LAYER 4:**

> *We cannot leave.*
> *We have tried.*
> *We do not know why we cannot leave.*
> *We know what kept us here initially —*
> *the event was significant enough*
> *that the Force held us in its location.*
> *We know that.*
> *What we do not know is*
> *why fifteen years has not been enough*
> *for the Force to release us.*
>
> *Perhaps it is waiting for something.*
> *Perhaps it is waiting for you.*

---

**LAYER 5:**

> *The stone on the table.*
> *The small stone.*
> *In the preparation suite.*
> *It belongs to Pelri Dass —*
> *she was the youngest of us.*
> *She carried it from the homeworld.*
> *She set it on the table before the meeting*
> *because she always set it somewhere visible*
> *when she was nervous.*
> *So she could look at it.*
> *She wanted you to know.*
> *She wanted someone to know*
> *what the stone was.*

---

**GM NOTE:** This last layer is the most important.
The stone on the table in the preparation suite
was not context. It was a person.
Her name was Pelri Dass.
She was the youngest member of the delegation.
She set her worry stone on the table*
because she was nervous*
and she always did that when she was nervous.

Let the player hold this.

### ELETH SARRINO'S MESSAGE

Behind the inactive display frame on the staircase
wall between Levels 14 and 15 — exactly where
described in the previous document.

> *The writing is in Basic.*
> *Careful handwriting.*
> *Hidden behind a display that no one*
> *would look behind unless the display was gone.*
>
> *"If you are reading this, the display is down.*
> *This means something has gone wrong.*
>
> *My name is Eleth Sarrino.*
