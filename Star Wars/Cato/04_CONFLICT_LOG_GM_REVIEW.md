# Conflict Log and GM Review

## Resolved in this pack

### 1. Sankar geometry

**Older language:** "tower," "palace spire," and some early drafts can read like a conventional upright building.  
**Resolution:** current continuity wins. Sankar descends from Jorra's ventral underside; the bridge is above it. "Tower" may remain as local terminology, but movement deeper is generally downward/farther from the bridge.

### 2. Level range

**Older draft:** Sankar sometimes described as Levels 15-18, with Levels 12-14 as approach.  
**Current continuity:** Levels 12-16 are the full narrative progression and map catalogue.  
**Resolution:** this pack treats 12-16 as the Sankar/deep-ventral campaign sequence. Level 12 and 13 may still be structurally adjacent to the palace proper, but they belong to the playable Sankar arc.

### 3. Moraband reveal timing

**Conflict:** an older `Under 2` summary says Tacker confirms Moraband. The published adventure says Tacker does not know Warde's destination; the kyber crystal/Gatekeeper reveals it. Later custom location notes also treat the crystal as the destination reveal.  
**Resolution:** Tacker can provide a departure vector and his observations. Level 13-14 red-dot clues can foreshadow or identify Moraband as a restricted world. **They do not establish that Warde went there.** Final confirmation comes from the crystal/Gatekeeper.

### 4. Kaddo Moll's death

**Published shorthand:** Warde "slew" Moll and Durael in the fight.  
**Custom expansion:** Moll is mortally wounded, survives long enough to send a warning, then dies nearby; her body is not present in the hall.  
**Resolution:** compatible interpretation - Warde inflicts the fatal wounds, Moll escapes/survives briefly, sends the warning, then dies. Her later Force appearance is an optional custom expansion, not evidence that she physically survived the episode.

### 5. Droids

**Published encounter:** surviving BX commandos and a droideka harry the PCs on the way down, with the droideka at the final doors/chasm.  
**Custom drafts:** droid patrols have expanded from another ventral area and can potentially be reasoned with.  
**Resolution:** use the published force as the available baseline, but stage them using the custom patrol logic. They can retreat, regroup, and potentially accept proof that their orders are obsolete.

### 6. Old "Temple of the Fallen" ventral route

An early ventral sandbox adds an ancient Sith temple under/near Jorra. It is not carried into the current Sankar continuity or map catalogue and risks pre-empting Moraband's role as the dark-side destination.  
**Resolution:** exclude from the default Sankar route. Keep only as a separate optional campaign idea if deliberately restored later.

### 7. Visual palette

Some older notes use red stone / black shadow as the primary palace look.  
**Resolution:** current reference wins: pale/bone ceremonial stone, dark metal, blue/navy-and-gold accents; broken/service areas become wetter, rockier, industrial, and fog-heavy while retaining the same architectural lineage.

## GM REVIEW - unresolved or interpretive

### A. Seven diplomatic presences vs. Hasar surviving

Current map continuity explicitly preserves **seven presences/ghostly impressions** in the Level 14 Ghost Chamber. Custom level drafts also use a seven-seat Separatist delegation, while the published text names Hasar plus Niot, Petri, and Morm but does not clearly enumerate every delegate. Hasar definitely survives.

**Default used by this pack:** seven diplomatic seats/presences are retained. Treat the Level 14 phenomenon as **Force impressions rather than seven literal dead souls**. Six can correspond to dead delegates; the seventh can be a living trauma-imprint of Hasar or a composite memory. This preserves both the seven-presence scene and Hasar's survival.

**GM decision if you want literal ghosts:** reduce the literal dead spirits to six while keeping seven positions/impressions, or explicitly define the seventh as a non-delegate witness. Do not make Hasar dead.

### B. Exact full delegation roster

Published names: Onrein Hasar, Niot, Petri, Morm.  
Custom later material adds Pelri Dass and implies seven Separatist seats. Older NPC notes use inconsistent names such as Peli/Norin.

**Default used by this pack:** use the published names plus Pelri Dass as a custom additional delegate; leave remaining seats unnamed unless you deliberately name them. Avoid the older Peli/Norin names until reviewed.

### C. Metaphysics of Kaddo Moll's presence

The latest custom finale treats Kaddo as a conscious Force presence who can communicate. The published adventure does not include this scene.

**Default used by this pack:** keep it, because it is the newest finale-specific custom material and it strongly supports the user's living-world/Warde-story goal. Run her as a Force echo/witness rather than asserting universal rules about post-death consciousness.

### D. Whether Warde's notation board is fully reliable

The board is custom material and gives Warde unusually clear insight that the delegates are sincere while higher command may betray the truce. This enriches the tragedy but slightly complicates earlier witness testimony that he treated the talks as a sham.

**Default used by this pack:** both are true from different viewpoints. Tacker/others understood Warde as believing the talks were a trap. Warde's private notes show a more precise internal distinction he did not successfully communicate: sincere delegates, untrustworthy future command behavior. This makes his choice worse, not easier.

## No-review-needed omissions

The pack intentionally removes or downplays early ideas that duplicate newer maps, make every room combat-focused, or introduce large new lore beats that the current continuity does not carry forward.
