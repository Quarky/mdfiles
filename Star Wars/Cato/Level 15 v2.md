---

## GM NOTE — THIS IS A THEATRICAL MOMENT

> Run this sequence slowly.

> This is fifteen years of darkness ending.

**GM Description:**

> *The first sconce ignites.*
>
> *To your left.*
> *At the entrance to the corridor.*
