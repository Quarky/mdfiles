# THE ONE PRESENCE

---

## WHAT IT IS

The one presence the Pathfinder has been feeling
since Level 14 is here.

It is not one of the seven.
It is not a dark side phantasm like Level 14.

It is Kaddo Moll.

The Ithorian Jedi who fought alongside Warde
and Durael. Who survived the battle injured.
Who sent the message to the Jedi Council.
Who died shortly after from her wounds —
not in this building, but close enough
that the Force carried something of her back here.

She has been in this room for fifteen years.
Not suffering. Not angry.
Witnessing.

She was the third Jedi.
The party does not know her name yet.
The gatekeeper knows it.
Tacker knew it.
The cease-fire documents reference her.

She is why the room has never felt empty.
She has been here the whole time.
Watching.
Waiting for someone to come and understand
what she understood too late.
