
# LEVEL 14 — THE THRESHOLD
### *"This is where the building stops being diplomatic and starts being something else."*
### *"The Force has been trying to tell this story for longer than any of us have been alive."*

---

## GM MASTER BRIEFING

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Story Purpose** | The pivot point. Everything above was the Trade Federation's story. Everything below is what happened to it. This level is the hinge between civilization and tragedy. |
| **Tone** | Opens formal and grand. Builds through history. Turns heavy at the murals. Ends in encounter. |
| **Structure** | Three zones: The Arch Entry → The Mural Corridor → The Diplomacy Hall → The Ghost Chamber |
| **Dark Side** | This is where it ARRIVES. Not foreshadowing. Present. The ghosts appear at the end. |
| **Key Emotional Beat** | The watching figure in the last mural. Then Pelri Dass. |
| **Key Story Beat** | The delegation's ghost encounter. Witness. Release. One presence remains. |
| **Animals** | NONE. The Pathfinder notices immediately. This matters. |
| **ADHD/Autism Note** | Each zone has a CLEAR transition. Announce each shift. Keep beats SHORT and PUNCHY. Action before description. Discovery before explanation. |
| **Exit Hook** | The ghosts release. One remains below. Different. The party has to go find it. |
