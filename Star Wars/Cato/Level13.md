# LEVEL 13 — THE DIPLOMATIC QUARTER APPROACH  
### *"People came here from across the galaxy to talk instead of fight. That counts for something."*

---

## GM BRIEFING

| Element | Detail |  
|---|---|  
| **Story Purpose** | The Trade Federation at its most idealistic. Show what diplomacy looked like before the war made it impossible. Then show what the war did to it. |  
| **Tone** | Starts dignified. Gets heavier. The canyon is very present here — the drop, the air, the sound. |  
| **Pacing** | Two distinct zones: the interior diplomatic quarter, then the exterior canyon walkway. Interior is discovery. Exterior is exposure — physically and emotionally. |  
| **Dark Side Foreshadowing** | Stronger than Level 12. Two moments. The second one doesn't go away when examined. |  
| **Key Emotional Beat** | The archive. The cease-fire documents. N'got's risk assessment. The delegation knew Warde was unstable. They came anyway. |  
| **Key Physical Beat** | The exterior walkway over the canyon. The platform sways. The bottom is not visible. |  
| **Animals** | No new animals. The Canyon Manta pair visible from the exterior walkway — far below, circling. |  
| **Exit Hook** | The archive document's last line. The party knows something the delegation didn't know they were walking into. |

## ENTERING LEVEL 13

The gap the manta made closes behind them  
as they move into the passage connecting  
Level 12 to Level 13.

The passage is short — twenty meters —  
and it changes as they walk it.

> *The stone gets darker.*  
> *One shade. Maybe two.*  
> *You might not notice if you weren't paying attention.*  
> *But you are paying attention now.*  
>  
> *The joins between the stones get tighter.*  
> *More precise.*  
> *The same hands that built the Exhibition Hall*  
> *built this — but these hands were working on something*  
> *they knew mattered more.*  
> *This stone was chosen more carefully.*  
> *This stone was meant to last longer.*

> *The ceiling rises.*  
> *Three meters in the service passage.*  
> *Four in the Exhibition Hall.*  
> *Here: five.*  
> *You are going somewhere that expected*  
> *important people.*

**Beat — the smell:**

> *Something changes in the air.*  
> *Not bad. Not good.*  
> *Specific.*  
> *Old paper. Old fabric.*  
> *The smell of rooms that held meetings.*  
> *The smell of decisions.*

## THE DIPLOMATIC QUARTER — INTERIOR

The passage opens into a wide corridor —  
the widest the party has seen in the ventral city.  
Four meters across. Five meters high.  
The chemical lighting here is full and steady —  
this level is on the main palace backup system,  
which has more reserve than the exhibition hall above.

Everything is lit. Equally. Completely.

> *The corridor stretches ahead of you.*  
> *Forty meters.*  
> *Doors on both sides.*  
> *Not maintenance doors.*  
> *Formal doors.*  
> *Each one with a different diplomatic seal*  
> *carved into the stone above the frame.*  
>  
> *You recognize some of them.*  
> *The Banking Clan.*  
> *The Commerce Guild.*  
> *The Techno Union.*  
> *Others you don't know —*  
> *smaller factions, independent systems,*  
> *trading partners the Trade Federation*  
> *maintained relationships with*  
> *across three centuries.*  
>  
> *Each seal above each door.*  
> *Each door representing a relationship.*  
> *Forty meters of relationships.*  
> *Most of them gone now.*

**GM NOTE:** The doors are not locked.  
Every room the party enters tells a piece  
of the same story from a different angle.  
Pick two or three based on what the player  
engages with. Don't force all of them.

### ROOM A — THE BANKING CLAN SUITE

> *The Banking Clan seal above the door:*  
> *a circle bisected by a horizontal line.*  
> *Wealth above. Debt below.*  
> *The whole philosophy of the Muun*  
> *in a single symbol.*

> *Inside: a meeting room.*  
> *Small. Private.*  
> *A round table — not the Trade Federation's*  
> *formal rectangular style.*  
> *A round table means no head.*  
> *No hierarchy.*  
> *Whoever sat here was considered equal*  
> *for the duration of the meeting.*  
>  
> *On the table: a ledger.*  
> *Open.*  
> *The columns are in Muun script*  
> *but the numbers translate.*  
> *These are trade figures.*  
> *Enormous trade figures.*  
> *The kind of numbers that represent*  
> *not goods but systems.*  
> *Whole economies, flowing through this table.*

> *And at the bottom of the last page,*  
> *in Basic, in handwriting that is not*  
> *the careful administrative script of the ledger —*  
> *someone's personal handwriting:*  
>  
> *"If we stop talking, the numbers stop mattering.*  
> *Remind them of this at the start of every session.*  
> *They forget. They always forget."*

**GM NOTE:** This is advice from a Banking Clan  
negotiator to whoever came after them.  
Left in the ledger because the ledger was coming back.  
The negotiator expected to return.

> *This seal you don't recognize.*  
> *Smaller than the others.*  
> *A stylized creature — something with wings,*  
> *native to a world you can't identify.*  
> *Below the creature, three words in a script*  
> *that is not Basic and not Pak Pak.*  
>  
> *The room inside is different from the others.*  
> *No meeting table.*

> *A low platform against the far wall*

> *The Trade Federation accommodated this.*  
> *They built a room for it.*

> *On the platform: a single object.*  
> *A small carved figure.*  
> *Left behind.*  
> *Not forgotten — placed.*  
> *Deliberately set on the platform*  
> *before whoever came here left.*  
>  
> *An offering.*  
> *Or a marker.*  
> *Or a goodbye.*

**If the Pathfinder touches it:**

> *Nothing through the Force.*  
> *Just stone.*  
> *Old stone.*  
> *Smooth from handling.*  
> *Someone held this many times*  
> *before they set it down here.*

### ROOM C — THE PREPARATION LIBRARY

This room is different from the suites —  
larger, lined with shelves, a reference library  
for delegations preparing for negotiations.

Most of the shelves are empty — the books  
and datapads were taken in the looting.  
But the shelves themselves remain, and  
on some of them, objects too heavy or  
too awkward to carry easily were left behind.

> *A globe.*  
> *Mounted on a stand.*  
> *It shows the galaxy as it was*  
> *at the height of the Trade Federation —*  
> *the routes marked in gold,*  
> *the Federation's territory shaded.*  
> *The shaded area covers*  
> *a third of the known galaxy.*  
>  
> *And in the outer rim of the map,*  
> *in a system with no trade routes touching it,*  
> *a single dot.*  
> *Marked in red.*  
> *No label.*  
> *No route.*  
> *Just the dot.*  
> *Red.*

**If anyone examines it closely:**

> *The dot is in the correct position*  
> *for the Horuset system.*  
> *For Moraband.*  
> *Red.*  
> *No label.*  
> *The Trade Federation knew it was there.*  
> *They marked it.*  
> *They marked it in red and put no label on it*  
> *and left it that way.*

**Sigg:**

> *He looks at the dot for a long moment.*  
> *"They knew something was wrong with it.*  
> *They just didn't say what."*

**GM NOTE:** This happens in the library, or  
in whichever room the party is in when they  
are most absorbed. Time it to interrupt

>

**If the Pathfinder reaches through the Force:**

> *but you cannot hear the words.*

**Sigg — if she tells him:**

> *"How far below?"*  
>  
> *She cannot tell him exactly.*  
> *He nods.*  
> *"Keep moving then.*  
> *Better to know what it is*  
> *than to stand here wondering."*

## THE SEALED ARCHIVE

At the end of the interior corridor,  
past the last of the diplomatic suites,  
there is a room that is different from all the others.

No seal above the door.  
No diplomatic designation.  
The door is heavier than the others —  
reinforced, the kind of door that  
was built to protect its contents.

It is locked. But the lock is mechanical —  
fifteen years old, no power required to bypass it.

> **Average Skulduggery** to open.  
> On a failure: the mechanism jams.  
> **Hard Mechanics** to clear the jam.  
> On a Despair: the mechanism breaks.  
> The door can still be forced — **Hard Athletics** —  
> but it will be loud. Very loud.  
> Sound travels in the deep ventral.

**Inside:**

> *A small room.*  
> *No windows.*  
> *Shelves on three walls.*  
> *Most empty.*  
> *But in the back corner,*  
> *in a sealed case — waterproof,*  
> *airtight, the kind used for*  
> *irreplaceable documents —*  
> *a stack of papers.*

## THE CEASE-FIRE DOCUMENTS

**GM NOTE:** Read these slowly.  
One piece at a time.  
Let the player absorb each one  
before moving to the next.  
This is the most important discovery  
on this level.

---

**DOCUMENT 1 — Cover Sheet**

> *"Briefing Package — Separatist Diplomatic Delegation.*  
> *Destination: Sankar Palace, Jorra, Cato Neimoidia.*  
> *Purpose: Cease-Fire Negotiations.*  
> *Classification: Delegation Eyes Only."*

---

**DOCUMENT 2 — Objectives**

> *The delegation's goals, listed in order of priority:*  
>  
> *"1. Establish a complete cease-fire*  
> *for all of Cato Neimoidia.*  
> *2. Negotiate terms for Republic withdrawal*  
> *from Jorra within sixty days.*  
> *3. Establish a neutral monitoring presence*  
> *to verify compliance by both parties.*  
> *4. Create conditions for permanent peace talks*  
> *to begin within ninety days of the cease-fire."*  
>  
> *At the bottom, in the same administrative hand:*  
> *"These objectives were approved by*  
> *the Separatist High Command.*  
> *The delegation is authorized to reach agreement*  
> *on all four points without further consultation."*

**GM NOTE:** They had the authority to end the war.  
Right here. Right now. At this table.  
Let that land before continuing.

---

**DOCUMENT 3 — Republic Assessment**

> *A briefing on the Republic forces in Jorra.*  
> *Clone trooper numbers. Defensive positions.*  
> *Jedi presence.*  
>  
> *Under Jedi presence:*  
>  
> *"Jedi Knight Suljo Warde.*  
> *Human male. Clone Wars veteran.*  
> *Prognosticative ability assessed: HIGH.*  
> *Ability appears to function at tactical*  
> *and strategic level simultaneously.*  
> *Recommend: No deception-based negotiating positions.*  
> *He will know."*

---

**DOCUMENT 4 — The Risk Assessment**

> *A single page. Separate from the others.*  
> *Different paper. Added later.*  
>  
> *"Risk Assessment — Addendum.*  
> *Source: Embedded Observer [DESIGNATION REDACTED]*  
> *Date: [REDACTED]*  
>  
> *Primary risk has been reassessed.*  
> *Jedi Knight Warde's prognosticative ability*  
> *presents less risk than previously assessed.*  
> *His accuracy at the strategic level*  
> *has declined over the past three months.*  
> *His accuracy at the personal level —*  
> *predicting individual actions —*  
> *remains high.*  
>  
> *Secondary risk elevated to PRIMARY.*  
> *Psychological assessment of Jedi Knight Warde*  
> *indicates potential instability under*  
> *prolonged operational stress.*  
> *Recommend: Keep negotiations brief.*  
> *Reach agreement quickly.*  
> *Do not allow extended session.*  
>  
> *Note: Secondary risk assessment*  
> *based on direct observation over eight months.*  
> *Confidence: HIGH.*  
> *This assessment should be shared with all delegation members.*  
> *They should understand what they are walking into."*

**GM NOTE:** The embedded observer was N'got.  
The party knows this.  
They are holding N'got's assessment of Warde  
in their hands.  
He told them Warde was unstable.  
He told them to keep it brief.  
They did not keep it brief.

**Sigg — reading over their shoulder:**

> *He reads it twice.*  
> *Then he sets it down carefully.*  
>  
> *"They knew.*  
> *They all knew.*  
> *N'got told them.*  
> *He gave them every warning he had.*  
>  
> *They came anyway.*  
> *Because the war was too costly to let*  
> *one man's instability stop the talks.*  
>  
> *They were right about that.*  
> *And they died for being right."*

> *He looks at the document.*  
> *"N'got ran. I understand now why he ran.*  
> *He had already done everything he could do.*  
> *He had told them everything he knew.*  
> *When it went wrong, there was nothing left for him to do*  
> *except survive it.*  
>  
> *He has spent fifteen years thinking he failed them.*  
> *He didn't fail them.*  
> *He warned them.*  
> *They made their choice.*  
> *He made his."*

## THE EXTERIOR WALKWAY

At the south end of the interior corridor,  
a door leads outside.

Not to another building.  
Outside.

> *The door opens and the sound hits first.*  
> *The canyon.*  
> *You have been hearing it as background —*  
> *as context — since you entered the deep ventral.*  
> *Out here it is not background.*  
> *It is everything.*  
>  
> *The walkway extends thirty meters ahead.*  
> *Four meters wide.*  
> *Metal grating underfoot.*  
> *Railing on the right side.*  
> *Nothing on the left.*

> *You are beyond the edge of the bridge above.*  
> *The bridge is no longer above you.*  
> *The structure that supports this walkway*  
> *goes up — cables, thick as your arm,*  
> *vanishing into the bridge structure somewhere overhead —*  
> *and down — the same cables,*  
> *descending into the mist.*  
> *You are suspended between.*

> *The walkway sways.*  
> *Just slightly.*  
> *Just enough to feel.*  
> *Canyon wind.*  
> *It does not stop.*

**GM NOTE:** Give everyone a moment with this.  
The physical exposure of the walkway is important.  
They have been in enclosed spaces since the Ossivane.  
This is the first time they have been truly outside,  
truly suspended, truly aware of the depth below them.

>  
> *The Canyon Mantas.*

## THE DARK SIDE — THIRD SIGN

**GM NOTE:** This one is stronger.  
This one does not disappear.  
Time it to the moment the party  
looks at the palace windows below.

> *The Pathfinder feels it before anyone sees it.*

> *"Something is—"*

> *And then they all feel it.*  
> *Or not feel — notice.*  
> *The way you notice when the sound in a room*  
> *changes without any identifiable cause.*  
>  
> *The walkway is the same.*  
> *The canyon is the same.*  
> *The mantas below are still circling.*  
>  
> *But something about the air*  
> *has changed quality.*  
> *Something about standing on this walkway*  
> *looking at those windows*  
> *is different from what it was*  
> *thirty seconds ago.*

**For the Pathfinder specifically:**

> *Through the Force:*  
> *The presence she felt in Level 13*  
> *is closer.*  
> *Closer and more defined.*  
> *It is not one thing.*  
> *It is multiple things.*  
> *All below.*  
> *All aware of her.*  
>  
> *Not hostile.*  
> *Not welcoming.*  
> *Waiting.*  
> *The way things wait*  
> *when they have been waiting*  
> *for a very long time*  
> *and have learned patience.*

**Sigg:**

> *He feels something too.*  
> *He does not have her sensitivity*  
> *but he has been Force-sensitive long enough*  
> *to recognize the texture of wrongness.*  
>  
> *He does not look away from the windows.*  
>  
> *"They're in the palace," he says.*  
> *Quietly.*  
> *"Whatever they are.*  
> *They're in the palace.*  
> *Not here."*  
>  
> *He picks up his pace.*  
> *"Then let's not keep them waiting."*

## EXIT NOTE FOR GM

The party leaves Level 13 knowing:

- ✦ The cease-fire was genuine — the delegation had full authority to end the war  
- ✦ N'got warned them. In writing. They came anyway.  
- ✦ Warde's instability was documented before the meeting happened  
- ✦ Moraband was marked red on the Trade Federation's maps — no label, just red  
- ✦ Something is waiting in the palace. Multiple somethings. They know the party is coming.  
- ✦ The palace windows are visible from the walkway — the party has seen the room from outside

**What they carry forward:**  
The cease-fire documents.  
The knowledge that N'got did everything right  
and it still happened.  
The red dot with no label.  
The cold that came and did not fully leave.

**The question they are carrying into Level 14:**  
*If they knew — if everyone knew —*  
*what is the difference between*  
*a tragedy and a crime?*
