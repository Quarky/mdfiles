# Level 16 - Conference Level and Final Approach

**Maps:** SP11 BX Security Junction; SP12 Preliminary Conference Suite; SP13 Final Meeting Hall  
**Core question:** *Can the party reconstruct the choice before the Force tells them what it meant?*  
**Tone:** forensic clarity under physical danger.

## Conference rooms - diplomacy as work

The preliminary rooms should look used, not dramatic. Drafts, moved tables, scribbled amendments, and a side observer position make the talks real.

### Large working room

Furniture was pushed back deliberately during the crisis. This is a subtle detail: even while the building was failing, someone followed procedure and tried to make space. A half-finished task survives because its worker expected there to be a next step.

### Seating chart

Use the custom seven-seat Separatist delegation model plus three Republic/Jedi positions and an off-side tactical-support/observer position associated with N'got's cover. Do not name all delegates unless the GM resolves the full roster.

The chart establishes lines of sight. N'got's position makes his later testimony geometrically plausible.

## Security junction - old orders still walking

### Evidence before combat

- scavenged power cells with replacement dates;
- BX components swapped from different chassis;
- a patrol schedule repeatedly corrected after structural collapses;
- a last-order packet referencing protection of the proceedings;
- an authentication hierarchy whose superior nodes have been dead for years.

Let players realize the droids have been maintaining **a mission**, not merely themselves.

### Encounter structure

Use BX commandos as mobile ambushers. They retreat when badly outmatched and use side routes. The droideka should be the final heavy sentinel near the meeting-hall doors/chasm, matching the published encounter logic.

### Non-combat route

If players can present enough evidence that the proceedings ended and both governments are gone, a droid can enter a contradiction state. Computers, Leadership, Deception, or a literal archive credential can all matter. A peaceful outcome may still require the PCs to give them a new directive or permission to stand down.

The emotionally strongest question is simple: **"What do we do when the war is over?"**

## The final approach chasm

If present on SP11, make it a broken floor opening into fog/abyss with a maintenance bypass around or below it. Failure puts PCs in danger but does not erase them from the campaign. Use the chasm as the war's physical damage, not a generic lava pit.

## At the meeting-hall doors

Before opening, offer a choice: investigate last side rooms, deal with surviving droids, rest, repair lighting, or go in. This is the final point where the party can decide what kind of witnesses they will be.

## Forensic setup inside SP13

Do not fire the Force vision immediately. Let players walk the room.

Evidence stations:

- Warde's pulled-back chair;
- delegate side and body positions;
- droid wreckage/bodyguard response;
- lightsaber cuts that begin near the table and spread outward;
- blaster impacts that occur after the initial melee begins;
- Warde blood/impact trail toward escape;
- ruined balcony and Durael.

Use Puzzle 6 (`The Table Tells the Story`) before the supernatural confirmation.
