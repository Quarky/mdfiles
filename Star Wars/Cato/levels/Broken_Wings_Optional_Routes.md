# Broken Wings and Under-Palace Routes

**Maps:** SP14-SP19  
**Purpose:** travel stories, hazards, alternate routes, salvage, structural understanding, and consequences. These maps should not become mandatory lore corridors unless the party's choices make them so.

## SP14 - Mist-Flooded Hall: ordinary luxury ruined by weather

Tell a staff/visitor story rather than another battle story. Covered tables, cloak storage, a small service bar, emergency window seals, and a failed attempt to save furnishings from fog. A forgotten guest item can tie back to a Level 13 seal.

**Dynamic event:** fog pulse reveals one silhouette outside for a few seconds - manta, support strut, droid, or simply another palace wing.

## SP15 - Collapsed Cliffside Gallery: choices under uncertainty

Offer three routes:

- fast exposed route across emergency bridging;
- slow interior route through unstable rooms;
- technical route on hanging maintenance access.

Each should have a different cost. There is no single correct path. This visually echoes the theme of futures and choices without stating it.

## SP16 - Cavern Breach: architecture meets geology

This is the best location for workers' history. Add names scratched into an anchor drilling housing, a little shrine or luck charm, and a hidden cache of emergency gear. The shrine should be worker-made and local, not a new Sith temple.

## SP17 - Flooded Service Cavern: systems after abandonment

Connect pumps to Level 15 lights and lower drainage. A maintenance droid cradle can be empty, damaged, or contain a noncombatant droid that has been running the same diagnostic for years.

Players can improve the world by restoring pump function. If they do, lower routes are drier on the return trip.

## SP18 - Observation Spire: partial truth

Fog makes long-range optics unreliable. Restoring one sensor should reveal one valuable piece of information, chosen based on current needs:

- droid patrol route;
- a safe lower walkway;
- movement of a canyon manta;
- location of a damaged anchor;
- the Syndicate observer retreating.

The spire teaches that information is useful without being complete.

## SP19 - Substructure Anchor Cavern: the palace explained

This is where the PCs can finally see the structural truth: huge pylons and tension members run upward toward the bridge and into support geology. Use Puzzle 7 to let technical characters stabilize a section.

**World consequence:** if stabilized, later creaks reduce, one dangerous crossing becomes easier, and on the return trip the PCs can see that their intervention mattered. The palace does not reset like a dungeon.

## Optional recurring NPC/event - the Syndicate observer

Keep the observer as a moving world element rather than an obligatory fight. Evidence of them can appear in SP14-SP18. If confronted, their goal is to learn what the PCs found for Hasar, not to die for the information.

## Salvage philosophy

Useful salvage should be specific and story-bearing:

- old security transponder;
- intact lamp compound;
- museum inventory tags;
- service map cylinder;
- droid memory fragment;
- worker's tool with initials;
- emergency line launcher.

Avoid turning the palace into a generic credits mine. Valuable cultural property should create ownership questions.
