# Level 14 - History, Memory, and the Seven Presences

**Maps:** SP06 Mural Gallery; SP07 Ghost Chamber  
**Core question:** *What survives when official history and living witnesses disagree?*  
**Tone:** beauty -> unease -> direct encounter with memory.

## GM goals

This level transitions from records to emotional truth. The murals give the institution's story; the support rooms give ordinary people's story; the Ghost Chamber gives memory that cannot be filed cleanly.

## The mural gallery

Keep the walk moving. Each panel should contain one thing the Federation wanted remembered and one detail the PCs can notice that complicates it.

### Panel sequence

1. **Homeworld:** hatchery lakes, children, continuity. Complication: individual faces are carefully distinct - this was painted by someone who cared about particular people.
2. **First departure:** colonists leaving with fear and resolve. Complication: an empty-handed figure challenges heroic propaganda; migration required loss.
3. **Purse worlds:** commerce shown as conversation. Complication: both parties look uncertain; trust was never automatic.
4. **Federation at peak:** galaxy-spanning routes and the red unlabelled world. Complication: warning is literally painted at the edge of triumph.
5. **Wars:** prosperity collapses into militarization. Complication: repairs/overpainting reveal that the panel was altered more than once as official messaging changed.
6. **The last panel / watching figure:** future-facing imagery with an anomalous figure at the edge. Complication: the figure's position visually echoes the red world's location. Do not state that the artist predicted Warde; let the players decide whether this is coincidence, later alteration, or Force-influenced art.

## Support-room micro-stories

### Waiting rooms

Use three different body-plan accommodations and one personal comfort note: "use this room if you can; the chairs are better." The note should recur by handwriting/initial elsewhere so players can recognize a real staff member's path through the building.

### Communications room

The last physical log says negotiations are proceeding, morale is good, and an outcome will be transmitted after the meeting. The blank line where the outcome should be is more effective than a dramatic final message.

### Eleth Sarrino

Retain the facility-manager message from the custom material. Its function is not to add another mystery; it is to make someone love the building before the PCs see its worst room. Place clues to Eleth elsewhere (maintenance authorizations, staff scheduling, a repaired display) so the message feels like a person rather than a monologue planted for adventurers.

## Puzzle - The Missing Route

If not completed on Level 13, compare mural and globe here. Reward: a restricted-display maintenance note, not Warde's destination.

## The Ghost Chamber - seven presences/impressions

Run the chamber as **Force impressions** unless the GM resolves the metaphysics differently. This keeps the current seven-presence continuity without contradicting Hasar's survival.

The room should do four things:

1. stop the canyon sound;
2. change temperature/light quality;
3. present seven formal diplomatic positions;
4. communicate the need to be witnessed rather than avenged.

### Information the presences can communicate

- the delegation came in good faith;
- they knew there was risk and judged peace worth it;
- Warde hesitated for two seconds;
- in those seconds he looked at the people across the table rather than at an abstract future;
- one small homeworld stone belonged to a young delegate and was used as an anxiety anchor;
- they do not need the PCs to "win" against Warde on their behalf - they need the event remembered accurately.

## Puzzle - Witness, Not Exorcism

Use Puzzle 4. Reward attention to prior discoveries. Do not require a particular moral speech.

## The one deeper presence

Maintain a distinct deeper presence separate from the seven impressions. It should be detectable as a watcher from below/Level 16. In the finale this can resolve as Kaddo Moll's Force witness, per the latest custom material.

## FFG mechanics

- Fear check only if the table prefers it; the scene works better as awe/unsettling memory than horror combat.
- Force-sensitive characters receive impressions; non-Force-sensitive PCs still experience temperature, silence, equipment behavior, and emotional body language.
- Advantage on social/discipline checks can reveal tiny differences between the seven presences.
- Triumph can let a PC notice the seventh impression is *different* from the six others - supporting the Hasar-living-imprint interpretation without explaining it.

## Exit

After the presences release/fade, the building should not become cheerful. It becomes **quieter**. One layer of pressure is gone. The remaining presence below is now easier to distinguish.
