## 3. Vorn Kaal – Syndicate Accountant

**Type:** Rival  
**Role:** Syndicate finance chief  
**Description:** Vorn Kaal is a dour, meticulous Neimoidian who keeps the Syndicate's books clean. He runs a network of shell companies, front businesses, and off‑world accounts. He is utterly loyal to Hasar because she saved his life during the war.

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Kaal works from a secure office in Hasar's manor, managing accounts and laundering money.
- **Attitude:** He is paranoid and loyal to Hasar. He will not betray her easily.

**Encounter Notes:** Kaal is a potential source of financial information about the Syndicate. If captured, he might be turned.
