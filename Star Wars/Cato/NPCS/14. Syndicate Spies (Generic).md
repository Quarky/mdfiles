## 14. Syndicate Spies (Generic)

**Type:** Minion / Rival (varies)  
**Role:** Spies working for Onrein Hasar  
**Description:** Informants who report to Sella Vex. They are everywhere in Jorra.

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** They blend in, listening for information about Warde, Force‑users, and threats to the Syndicate.
- **Attitude:** They are loyal to the Syndicate out of fear or profit.

**Encounter Notes:** The PCs may be followed by Syndicate spies. Successful perception checks reveal them.
