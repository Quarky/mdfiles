## 8. Separatist Veteran / Loyalist (Optional NPC)

**Type:** Rival  
**Star Wars Native:** Generic  
**Silhouette:** 1  
**Description:** An older Neimoidian who served the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. They are bitter, nostalgic, and may have useful information.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-----:|:-------:|:---------:|:-------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| 2     | 2       | 2         | 2       | 3         | 2        |

- **Wound Threshold:** 10  
- **Strain Threshold:** 12  
- **Soak:** 2 (old military gear)  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Knowledge (War) 2, Perception 2, Ranged (Light) 2, Survival 2

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:**
- **War Stories:** Add 1 boost die to checks to recall information about the Clone Wars.

**Equipment:**
- Old blaster pistol (Ranged [Light]; Damage 6; Critical 4; Range [Medium])
- Comlink (old model)

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Loyalists stay out of sight, meeting in secret locations. They are paranoid and cautious.
- **Attitude:** They distrust the Empire and the Syndicate, but may help those who share their goals.

**Encounter Notes:** The PCs may encounter a Separatist loyalist as a source of information, a potential ally, or a complication.
