## 4. Medical Personnel

**Type:** Rival (non-combat)  
**Star Wars Native:** Generic  
**Silhouette:** 1  
**Description:** A doctor or medic serving the citizens of Jorra. They are dedicated but overworked and underfunded.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-----:|:-------:|:---------:|:-------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| 2     | 2       | 3         | 2       | 3         | 2        |

- **Wound Threshold:** 8  
- **Strain Threshold:** 12  
- **Soak:** 1  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Medicine 3, Perception 1, Resilience 1

**Talents:**
- **Surgeon 1:** When making Medicine checks, add 1 automatic success.

**Abilities:** None

**Equipment:**
- Medpac
- Diagnostic scanner

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Medics work in clinics, tending to the sick and wounded. Many citizens cannot afford treatment.
- **Attitude:** They are compassionate but cynical about the city's future.

**Encounter Notes:** Part of the "Medical Aid" Healing the Wounds task. PCs with Medicine skill can assist; PCs without can donate credits or steal supplies.
