## 5. Sella Vex – Syndicate Whisper (Intelligence Chief)

**Type:** Rival  
**Role:** Syndicate intelligence / espionage chief  
**Description:** Sella Vex is a Twi'lek former Separatist intelligence agent who runs the Syndicate's spy network. She knows everything that happens in Jorra – including the PCs' movements.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-----:|:-------:|:---------:|:-------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| 2     | 3       | 3         | 4       | 3         | 3        |

- **Wound Threshold:** 10  
- **Strain Threshold:** 12  
- **Soak:** 2 (light armour)  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Deception 3, Perception 3, Streetwise 3, Vigilance 3

**Talents:**
- **Dedicated Spy:** Add 1 automatic success to Perception checks to notice or follow someone.

**Abilities:**
- **Network:** Vex has informants across the city. She can find anyone within 24 hours.

**Equipment:**
- Holdout blaster (Ranged [Light]; Damage 5; Critical 4; Range [Short])
- Comlink

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Vex coordinates informants, analyses intelligence, and reports to Hasar.
- **Attitude:** She is calculating and pragmatic. She might be turned if the PCs offer something she values.

**Encounter Notes:** Vex is a shadowy figure. The PCs may never meet her directly – but her spies are everywhere.
