## 1. Hinv Opan – Torna's Cafe Owner

**Type:** Rival  
**Role:** Cafe owner, neutral information broker  
**Description:** Hinv Opan is a cautious, middle‑aged Neimoidian who runs Torna's Cafe. He has seen the war, the occupation, and the Syndicate's rise. He tries to keep his cafe neutral, but he sympathises with the anti‑Imperial sentiment. He is a potential source of information – if the PCs can be trusted.

**Abilities:**
- **Neutral Ground:** Opan does not tolerate violence in his cafe. Anyone who starts a fight is banned – and may be reported to the Syndicate or Imperials.

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Opan opens the cafe at dawn, serves customers until late evening, and closes after the last patrons leave. He is always present, watching.
- **Attitude:** He is polite but guarded. He will help the PCs if he believes they are not a threat to his establishment.

**Encounter Notes:** Opan can provide information about the city, the Syndicate, and the Clone Wars. He may also offer jobs (Healing the Wounds tasks).
