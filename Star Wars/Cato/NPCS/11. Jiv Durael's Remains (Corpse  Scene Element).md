## 11. Jiv Durael's Remains (Corpse / Scene Element)

**Type:** Environmental interaction  
**Star Wars Native:** Original  
**Silhouette:** 1 (corpse)  
**Description:** The skeleton of a Twi'lek Jedi Knight, Jiv Durael, Warde's former ally and victim. He was killed by Warde during the battle that followed the cease‑fire talks. His remains still wear the tattered brown and tan robes of a Jedi. His right hand is clenched in a fist – it contains the second kyber crystal.

**Living World Details:**
- **History:** Durael was one of the Jedi who attempted to arrest Warde after he murdered the Separatist delegates. Warde killed him.
- **Symbolism:** Durael is a reminder of Warde's betrayal. His death is a tragedy that the PCs must acknowledge.

**Encounter Notes:** The PCs find Durael on the balcony of the meeting hall. They must retrieve the crystal from his clenched fist. The GM should describe the scene with appropriate gravity – this is a fallen Jedi, a victim of the dark side.
