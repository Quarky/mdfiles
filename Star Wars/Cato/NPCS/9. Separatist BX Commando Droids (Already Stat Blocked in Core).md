## BX COMMANDO DROID

**Type:** Minion (groups of 3)  
**Star Wars Native:** Yes (Canon)  
**Silhouette:** 1  
**Description:** Relics of the Separatist droid army of the Clone Wars, BX-series commando droids served as infiltrators and assassins. More cunning, skilled, durable, and dangerous than the more common B1 battle droids, commando droids were more expensive to produce, and their use was thus limited to vital missions and support duties. While their less deadly counterparts are still seen occasionally across the galaxy, serving at the behest of criminals and mercenaries, active commando droids are, thankfully, all but unheard of in current times.

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** The droids are in a low‑power state, patrolling set routes. They only activate when they detect movement.
- **Origin:** Remnants of the Clone Wars. They were never deactivated; they have been salvaging parts from ruined droids for decades.
- **Attitude:** Relentless; they follow their last orders – eliminate Republic forces. They are cunning enough to lay ambushes and retreat to regroup.

**Encounter Notes:** The droids are encountered in the Droids' Nest of the ventral undercity. They attack in waves – initially as an ambush, then retreating to reconvene with other units. They are skilled at using the environment for cover and surprise attacks.
