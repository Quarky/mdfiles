## 3. Spice Pusher (Generic)

**Type:** Rival  
**Star Wars Native:** Generic  
**Silhouette:** 1  
**Description:** A small-time drug dealer operating in the residential district. Jendo Shrin is a specific example of this type – an energetic, predatory Neimoidian with a wicked grin who targets the vulnerable.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-----:|:-------:|:---------:|:-------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| 2     | 3       | 2         | 3       | 2         | 2        |

- **Wound Threshold:** 10  
- **Strain Threshold:** 10  
- **Soak:** 2 (light armour)  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Coercion 1, Deception 2, Perception 1, Ranged (Light) 1, Streetwise 3

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:**
- **Slippery:** Add 1 setback die to attempts to track or capture the spice pusher.

**Equipment:**
- Holdout blaster (Ranged [Light]; Damage 5; Critical 4; Range [Short]; Stun setting)
- 5 doses of various spices

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Shrin works the residential district, selling spice to addicts and the desperate. She moves frequently to avoid the Syndicate and the Imperials.
- **Attitude:** She is aggressive and predatory, but will flee if confronted by superior force.

**Encounter Notes:** Shrin is part of the "Spice Problems" Healing the Wounds task. Convincing her to leave the business or reporting her to authorities earns the PCs trust.
