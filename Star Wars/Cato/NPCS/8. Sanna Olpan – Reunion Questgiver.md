## 8. Sanna Olpan – Reunion Questgiver

**Type:** Rival (elderly civilian)  
**Role:** Mother seeking her son, questgiver for "Reunion"  
**Description:** Sanna Olpan is an elderly Neimoidian woman who has not seen her son Gren since Warde arrested him during the Clone Wars. She is desperate for news.

- **Wound Threshold:** 6  
- **Strain Threshold:** 12  
- **Soak:** 1  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Charm 1, Perception 1

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:**
- **Desperate Hope:** She will offer anything for news of her son.

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Olpan spends her days at home, hoping for news. She rarely leaves.
- **Attitude:** She is sad but hopeful. She will trust the PCs if they show compassion.

**Encounter Notes:** Part of the "Reunion" Healing the Wounds task. The PCs must find out what happened to Gren.
