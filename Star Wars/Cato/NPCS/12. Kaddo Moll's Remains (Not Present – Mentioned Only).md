## 12. Kaddo Moll's Remains (Not Present – Mentioned Only)

**Type:** NPC (off-screen)  
**Star Wars Native:** Original  
**Description:** The Ithorian Jedi Knight who fought alongside Warde and Durael. She was mortally wounded by Warde and died shortly after sending a message to the Jedi Council. Her body was never recovered.

**Living World Details:**
- **History:** She attempted to arrest Warde after he killed the delegates. She was gravely wounded but managed to warn the Council.
- **Legacy:** Her warning saved the Republic from being completely surprised, but Warde escaped.

**Encounter Notes:** Moll is mentioned in the adventure background and by NPCs. She is not encountered directly.
