## 7. Jendo Shrin – Spice Problems Questgiver

**Type:** Rival  
**Role:** Spice dealer, antagonist for "Spice Problems"  
**Description:** Jendo Shrin is a young, energetic Neimoidian with a predatory grin. She sells spice to the vulnerable and has no remorse.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-----:|:-------:|:---------:|:-------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| 2     | 3       | 2         | 3       | 2         | 2        |

- **Wound Threshold:** 10  
- **Strain Threshold:** 10  
- **Soak:** 2 (light armour)  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Coercion 1, Deception 2, Perception 1, Ranged (Light) 1, Streetwise 3

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:**
- **Slippery:** Add 1 setback die to attempts to track or capture Shrin.

**Equipment:**
- Holdout blaster (Ranged [Light]; Damage 5; Critical 4; Range [Short])
- 5 doses of various spices

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Shrin works the residential district, selling spice to addicts and the desperate.
- **Attitude:** She is aggressive but will flee if confronted.

**Encounter Notes:** Part of the "Spice Problems" Healing the Wounds task. The PCs must convince her to leave the business or report her.
