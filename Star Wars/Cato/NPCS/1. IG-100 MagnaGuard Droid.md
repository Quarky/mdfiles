## 1. IG-100 MagnaGuard Droid

**Type:** Nemesis-level bodyguard  
**Star Wars Native:** Yes (Canon)  
**Silhouette:** 1  
**Description:** The IG-100 MagnaGuard is a highly advanced combat droid, originally manufactured by the Confederacy of Independent Systems to serve as a bodyguard for Separatist leaders. Each MagnaGuard is a masterpiece of droid engineering, equipped with a phrik alloy staff that can withstand lightsaber strikes. They are swift, silent, and utterly lethal. Onrein Hasar keeps two of these droids as her personal protectors, a clear violation of Imperial law.

**Skills:**
- Athletics 2, Coordination 3, Discipline 2, Melee 4, Perception 2, Ranged (Heavy) 2, Vigilance 3

**Talents:**
- **Adversary 2:** Upgrade difficulty of all combat checks against this target twice.
- **Bodyguard 2:** When a character the MagnaGuard is protecting would suffer a Critical Injury, the MagnaGuard may suffer the injury instead.
- **Parry 4:** When struck by a melee attack (before soak), suffer 4 strain to reduce damage by 8.

**Abilities:**
- **Droid:** Does not need to breathe, eat, or drink; immune to poisons and toxins.
- **Phrik Staff Mastery:** When making a Melee attack with its staff, the MagnaGuard may spend advantage to knock the target prone or disarm them.

**Equipment:**
- **Electrostaff** – Melee; Damage 8; Critical 3; Range [Engaged]; Cortosis (cannot be sundered by lightsabers), Defensive 1, Stun Damage, Stun 3.
- **Integrated blaster** – Ranged (Light); Damage 6; Critical 4; Range [Medium]; Stun setting.

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** The MagnaGuards flank Hasar at all times. They do not sleep, eat, or speak. They stand motionless until needed, then move with terrifying speed.
- **Origin:** These droids were salvaged from a Separatist facility after the war and reprogrammed by Hasar's technicians.
- **Fear Factor:** The sight of MagnaGuards in a city where combat droids are illegal is a statement of Hasar's power.

**Encounter Notes:** Hasar's MagnaGuards are her ultimate protection. They will sacrifice themselves to save her. If destroyed, Hasar becomes vulnerable and may negotiate.
