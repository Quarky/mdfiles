## 13. Separatist Diplomat Corpses (Scene Elements)

**Type:** Environmental interaction  
**Star Wars Native:** Original  
**Description:** The skeletal remains of the Neimoidian delegates Warde murdered. They still sit at the meeting table, their necks cleanly severed. One corpse is Onrein Hasar's former colleagues – Peli, Norin, and others.

**Living World Details:**
- **History:** These delegates came to Jorra to negotiate a cease‑fire. They were sincere in their efforts. Warde killed them.
- **Symbolism:** They represent Warde's tragic mistake – his preemptive strike that doomed the peace.

**Encounter Notes:** The corpses are a powerful reminder of Warde's fall. The GM should describe them in detail to evoke the horror of the moment.
