## 6. Kuto Jaraal – Lost Treasures Questgiver

**Type:** Rival (elderly civilian)  
**Role:** Former Trade Federation officer, questgiver for "Lost Treasures"  
**Description:** Kuto Jaraal is an elderly Neimoidian who once lived in a lavish estate on Jorra's ventral side. He lost everything in the war and now lives in modest circumstances in the residential district. He wants his personal belongings retrieved from his old home.

**Living World Details:**

- **Attitude:** He is sad and nostalgic. He offers 500 credits per PC for retrieval.

**Encounter Notes:** Part of the "Lost Treasures" Healing the Wounds task. The PCs must journey to the ventral ruins and retrieve Jaraal's belongings.
