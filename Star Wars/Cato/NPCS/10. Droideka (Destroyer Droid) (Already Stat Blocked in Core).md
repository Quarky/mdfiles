## DROIDEKA (DESTROYER DROID)

**Type:** Rival  
**Star Wars Native:** Yes (Canon)  
**Silhouette:** 2  
**Description:** Droidekas, greatly feared by Republic troops during the Clone Wars, were manufactured by the Colicoid species. These durable droids are capable of collapsing into a wheel‑like form for swift movement or unfolding into a tripodal walking gun platform protected by a powerful deflector shield. A single droideka can unleash tremendous salvos of blaster fire while remaining all but immune from return fire thanks to its shields, making droidekas more than a match for many times their own number in soldiers. Thankfully, few of these fearsome machines remain active.

- **Wound Threshold:** 16  
- **Strain Threshold:** – (droid)  
- **Soak:** 5 (bronzium plating)  
- **Melee Defense:** 0 (shield active: defense 2)  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0 (shield active: defense 2)  

**Skills:**
- Gunnery 2
- Perception 3
- Resilience 3

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:**
- **Droid:** Does not need to breathe, eat, or drink; can survive in vacuum and underwater; immune to poisons and toxins.
- **Fire Sweep:** As an action, a droideka can make a Hard Gunnery check to inflict one hit that deals base damage with its built‑in twin heavy blasters on up to two targets within medium range, plus one additional target per advantage on the check; it can only hit each target once this way.
- **Shield Projector:** Maneuver to activate or deactivate; while active, gains defense 2 and upgrades the difficulty of all combat attacks targeting the droideka once, but droideka cannot use the Move maneuver.

**Equipment:**
- Two built‑in twin droideka heavy blasters (Gunnery; Damage 12; Critical 3; Range [Long]; Auto‑fire, Linked, Pierce 1, Vicious 1)
- Stomping mechanical leg (Brawl; Damage 5; Critical 5; Range [Engaged]; Knockdown 1)

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** The droideka is in a low‑power state, guarding the entrance to the meeting hall. It activates when it detects intruders.
- **Origin:** Remnants of the Clone Wars. It has been waiting for decades, following its last directive.
- **Attitude:** It attacks any non‑Separatist life form with overwhelming firepower.

**Encounter Notes:** The droideka is encountered just before the meeting hall of Sankar Palace. It begins combat at long range, rolling into view from a side corridor. Any surviving commando droids attempt to surprise the PCs during the second round of combat. The droideka's shield makes it a significant threat – the PCs must find ways to bypass or deactivate it.
