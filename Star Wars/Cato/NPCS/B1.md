### How to Use B1 Battle Droids

*   **Minion Groups:** B1s are Minions, so they operate in groups. A group shares a single skill rank and acts together on the same initiative slot.
*   **Flimsy but Numerous:** As Minions with a Wound Threshold of 5, they are easily destroyed. A solid hit from a standard blaster pistol (Damage 6) has a good chance of taking one down. Their strength is in numbers and the ability to swamp opponents with overwhelming firepower.
*   **Weapon:** A Blaster Rifle gives them a long reach and respectable damage (9), making them a threat at range.
*   **Droid Abilities:** They are immune to poisons, toxins, and environmental hazards like vacuum or water.
