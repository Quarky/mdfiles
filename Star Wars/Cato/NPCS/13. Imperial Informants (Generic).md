## 13. Imperial Informants (Generic)

**Type:** Minion / Rival (varies)  
**Role:** Spies working for Duke Iribian  
**Description:** Citizens who report to Iribian's advisors on anything that happens in Jorra. They are motivated by fear, greed, or ambition.

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** They blend in with the population, listening and reporting.
- **Attitude:** They are paranoid and self‑serving.

**Encounter Notes:** The PCs may notice they are being followed by an informant. A successful Perception check reveals the tail.
