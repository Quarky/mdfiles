## 4. Kael Dorn – Syndicate Enforcer

**Type:** Nemesis  
**Role:** Syndicate head of security / muscle  
**Description:** Kael Dorn is a human former mercenary who commands the Syndicate's armed forces. He is brutal, efficient, and has no qualms about collateral damage. He personally leads the most violent operations.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-----:|:-------:|:---------:|:-------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| 4     | 3       | 2         | 3       | 3         | 2        |

- **Wound Threshold:** 18  
- **Strain Threshold:** 14  
- **Soak:** 5 (heavy armour)  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Coercion 3, Discipline 2, Melee 3, Ranged (Heavy) 3, Vigilance 2

**Talents:**
- **Adversary 1:** Upgrade difficulty of all combat checks against him once.
- **Fearsome 1:** Add 1 automatic threat to Coercion checks made against him.

**Abilities:**
- **Fearless:** Dorn is immune to fear effects.

**Equipment:**
- Heavy blaster rifle (Ranged [Heavy]; Damage 10; Critical 3; Range [Long]; Auto‑fire)
- Vibro-axe (Melee; Damage 7; Critical 3; Range [Engaged]; Vicious 2, Pierce 2)
- Heavy armour (+3 soak)

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Dorn oversees Syndicate security, leads enforcement actions, and reports to Hasar.
- **Attitude:** He is cold, professional, and utterly loyal to Hasar.

**Encounter Notes:** Dorn is a significant combat threat. He may be encountered if the PCs attack Syndicate operations.
