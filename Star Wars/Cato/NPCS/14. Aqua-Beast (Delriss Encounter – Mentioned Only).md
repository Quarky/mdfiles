## 14. Aqua-Beast (Delriss Encounter – Mentioned Only)

**Type:** Nemesis (Environmental Threat)  
**Star Wars Native:** Original  
**Silhouette:** 5 (immense)  
**Description:** A massive aquatic predator encountered on the ocean moon Delriss. It is hundreds of metres long, covered in thick scales, with a gaping maw of teeth. It destroys the Jedi sanctuary and pursues the PCs' ship.

**Living World Details:**
- **Habitat:** The deep oceans of Delriss's ocean moon.
- **Behaviour:** It is attracted to vibration and movement. It attacks structures and ships.
- **Encounter Style:** This is not a combat encounter – the creature is too large to defeat. The PCs must flee.

**Encounter Notes:** The creature appears in the "Time to Go!" sequence. The GM should resolve it narratively – the creature is a threat to escape from, not fight.
