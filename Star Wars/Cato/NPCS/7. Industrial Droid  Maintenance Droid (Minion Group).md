## 7. Industrial Droid / Maintenance Droid (Minion Group)

**Type:** Minion  
**Star Wars Native:** Generic  
**Silhouette:** 1  
**Description:** A worker droid found in factories, warehouses, and maintenance tunnels. They are not combat droids, but they may be armed with tools.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-----:|:-------:|:---------:|:-------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| 2     | 2       | 1         | 1       | 1         | 1        |

- **Wound Threshold:** 4 (per droid)  
- **Strain Threshold:** – (minion group)  
- **Soak:** 3  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills (group only):**
- Mechanics 1, Perception 1

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:**
- **Droid:** Immune to poisons, toxins, and vacuum.

**Equipment:**
- Cutting torch (Melee; Damage 5; Critical 4; Range [Engaged]; Burn 2)

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Maintenance droids roam the tunnels, performing repairs. They are largely ignored.
- **Attitude:** Indifferent; they follow their programming.

**Encounter Notes:** The droids may be used as cover, distractions, or guides through the maintenance tunnels.
