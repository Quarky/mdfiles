## 9. Gren Olpan – Sanna's Son (Optional NPC)

**Type:** Rival (varies)  
**Role:** Missing person, potential plot complication  
**Description:** Gren Olpan is Sanna's son. He was arrested by Warde during the Clone Wars. His fate is up to the GM – he may be in prison, dead, or a member of the Syndicate.

**Living World Details:**
- **Location:** Varies – prison, Syndicate, or free.
- **Attitude:** He is traumatised by his experience.

**Encounter Notes:** The PCs must determine Gren's fate. This may lead to a prison break, a Syndicate encounter, or a reunion.
