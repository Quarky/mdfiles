# Duke Erron Iribian

**Type:** Nemesis  
**Role:** Imperial ruler of Jorra  
**Description:** The cold, calculating Imperial governor of Jorra. He cares little for day-to-day affairs so long as taxes are paid and rebellion is suppressed. Ambitious, paranoid about his career, and secretly employs bounty hunters and spies to eliminate threats before they reach his superiors. He seldom leaves the Ducal Palace.

---

## Backstory & Origins

Duke Erron Iribian was born into a minor noble family on Coruscant's upper levels – wealthy enough to afford privilege, but not influential enough to be noticed. He clawed his way through the Imperial bureaucracy with a combination of sycophancy and ruthless efficiency. His assignment to Cato Neimoidia was intended as a punishment: a backwater posting to remind him that his ambitions had outpaced his usefulness. Instead, Iribian turned it into an opportunity. He realised that the Neimoidians' hatred of Jedi and their economic desperation made them easy to control. By playing on their fears and offering protection from the Syndicate's excesses (while secretly tolerating them), he has maintained a stable, profitable world. His dream is to be recalled to Coruscant and given a sector governorship. He will destroy anyone – including the PCs – who threatens that dream.

---

## Personality & Mannerisms

Iribian is a master of the calculated pause. He speaks slowly, letting his words hang in the air to unsettle those he addresses. He rarely raises his voice; threats are delivered with a smile and a glass of Corellian wine. He projects an air of bored sophistication, but beneath it burns a desperate ambition. He despises the Neimoidians he rules, viewing them as cowardly merchants who got what they deserved during the Clone Wars. He has no loyalty to the Empire – only to his own career. He is genuinely afraid of Force‑users, not because he believes in their power, but because the Emperor has made it clear that Jedi survivors are to be eliminated. Failing to capture a Force‑user could end his career.

**Daily Routine:** Iribian wakes at dawn, reviews intelligence reports over breakfast, and holds court in the Ducal Palace's audience chamber from mid‑morning until midday. Afternoons are spent reviewing factory output quotas and signing off on Imperial requisitions. Evenings are for private dinners with his advisors – and occasionally, clandestine meetings with his spies. He sleeps poorly, fearing assassination.

---

## Relationships

- **Onrein Hasar:** He despises her but tolerates her existence because she keeps crime organised and predictable. He has an unspoken agreement: she provides kickbacks and stability; he looks the other way.
- **TH‑313 "Tacker":** Iribian views Tacker as a relic, but a useful one. He values the clone's tactical experience and has no idea of Tacker's secret loyalty to Warde.
- **Dalan Oberos:** A paid asset. Iribian sees him as a tool to be discarded once the Jedi threat is eliminated.
- **The PCs:** If they attract his attention, he will first try to learn their value. If they are Force‑users, he will attempt to capture them alive to curry favour with the Emperor.

---

## Rumours & Hooks

- **Rumour:** Iribian's wife left him years ago, and he keeps a holopic of her in his private quarters – the only sentimental object he owns.
- **Rumour:** He secretly funnels credits to an account on the Banking Clan's private servers, preparing for a life after the Empire.
- **Plot Hook:** Iribian keeps a sealed dossier on the Silver Star incident – the very incident that led the PCs to Warde's holocron. If the PCs can access it, they learn he ordered the bounty hunters to kill the crew.
- **Plot Hook:** He is terrified of a whispered name: "Darth Vader." If the PCs mention the Sith Lord, Iribian becomes visibly shaken and may flee.

---

## Encounter Notes

Iribian avoids direct combat, using stormtroopers and hired bounty hunters (like Dalan Oberos) to do his dirty work. He will attempt to capture Force‑sensitive PCs to advance his career. If cornered, he uses Imperial Valor to sacrifice underlings. He will bargain for his life if captured, offering access to Imperial records, safe passage, or even Hasar's location.

# Dalan Oberos (Knight‑Level Threat)

**Type:** Nemesis  
**Role:** Bounty hunter specialising in Jedi and Force‑sensitive targets  
**Description:** A ruthless human bounty hunter who first cut his teeth hunting Jedi for Count Dooku during the Clone Wars. He now works for the Empire, tracking down Force‑users with obsessive zeal. He is patient, methodical, and utterly remorseless. His equipment is specifically designed to counter lightsabers.

---

## Backstory & Origins

Oberos was born on a distant Outer Rim world that was devastated by the Clone Wars. His family died in a Separatist orbital bombardment – an event he blames on the Jedi for "failing to protect" the system. He was recruited by Separatist intelligence as a young man, and his natural talent for tracking and ambush made him invaluable. He spent the war hunting Jedi survivors and Republic officers, developing a reputation for cold efficiency. When the Empire rose, he transitioned seamlessly, becoming one of the first non‑clone specialists to work for the Inquisitorius on a contract basis. He has killed or captured over a dozen Force‑users in his career. He sees himself as a cleanser, ridding the galaxy of a dangerous and arrogant species of "wizards." He respects only skill and cunning; he has no interest in credits beyond what they buy.

---

## Personality & Mannerisms

Oberos speaks in a low, measured tone, rarely showing emotion. He is patient to a fault – he will stalk a target for weeks, learning their habits, waiting for the perfect moment. He views combat as a puzzle to be solved, not a brawl to be won. He has a macabre sense of humour, often leaving calling cards (a single Cortosis round) on his victims' ships. He is obsessive about his targets and will not abandon a hunt once he has accepted it. If the PCs humiliate him, it becomes personal – he will pursue them across the galaxy.

**Daily Routine:** Oberos has no fixed routine. He travels light, moves often, and sleeps in secure hideouts. He spends hours studying holo‑footage of his targets, analysing their combat styles. On Cato Neimoidia, he is based in a rented room in the commercial district, close to the Ducal Palace. He takes meals at the Dropview Cantina (see page 48), watching the streets below.

---

## Relationships

- **Duke Iribian:** Oberos sees him as a useful patron, nothing more. He has no loyalty to the Empire and would betray Iribian for a better offer.
- **Onrein Hasar:** They have crossed paths before. Hasar once tried to hire him; he refused, finding her "too emotional." He respects her ruthlessness but not her hatred.
- **The PCs:** He views them as his greatest challenge in years. If they escape, he will follow.

---

## Rumours & Hooks

- **Rumour:** Oberos once spared a young Force‑sensitive child, leaving her on a backwater world. He has never explained why.
- **Rumour:** He carries a scar on his chest from a lightsaber wound – a Jedi Master nearly killed him years ago, and he has never forgotten.
- **Plot Hook:** Oberos has a personal code: he never harms civilians or non‑combatants unless they attack him. This could be used to parley.
- **Plot Hook:** If the PCs defeat him, they find a datapad with the locations of other surviving Jedi – he was building a list for the Inquisitorius.

---

## Encounter Notes

Oberos attacks when the PCs least expect it – ideally during another encounter or while they walk the streets. He uses his jetpack for mobility, fires Cortosis rounds to disable lightsabers, and will retreat only if critically wounded. If he survives, he becomes a recurring antagonist.

# Onrein Hasar

**Type:** Nemesis  
**Role:** Crime lord, leader of the Whitescar Syndicate, survivor of Warde's attack  
**Description:** Onrein Hasar was once a Trade Federation executive and Separatist diplomat. Suljo Warde's assault left her scarred and cybernetically reconstructed – her face is a patchwork of grafts and mechanical components, her voice a synthesized rasp. She built a criminal empire from nothing, driven by a burning hatred for Warde and all Force‑users. She rarely leaves her fortified manor‑spire.

---

## Backstory & Origins

Hasar was born into a minor Neimoidian family on the homeworld, but her ambition and sharp mind propelled her quickly through the ranks of the Trade Federation. By the time the Clone Wars began, she was a rising executive with a reputation for ruthless negotiations. She genuinely believed in the Separatist cause – not for ideology, but because a fractured galaxy meant more opportunities for commerce. Her diplomatic assignment to Cato Neimoidia was a sign of trust from Count Dooku himself. She was present at the cease‑fire talks when Warde attacked. She survived because she was the furthest from the door, and her survival cost her everything: her face, her career, her faith in the future. After months of reconstructive surgeries, she emerged as something new – a creature of hatred. She built the Whitescar Syndicate from the rubble of Jorra, turning the city's despair into her personal empire. She has never forgiven Warde, and she will never stop hunting him.

---

## Personality & Mannerisms

Hasar is calm and businesslike, but her voice carries an edge of cold fury. She never raises her voice; her threats are quiet and precise. She has the unnerving habit of tilting her head when she speaks, as if her cybernetic eyes are processing every word. She is utterly pragmatic – she does not kill for pleasure, only for profit or necessity. But Warde... Warde is the exception. She would burn her entire empire to see him dead. She views the PCs with curiosity at first, but if they reveal themselves as Force‑users, her interest turns to calculation. She may try to use them, manipulate them, or kill them – depending on which serves her goal.

**Daily Routine:** Hasar rarely leaves her manor-spire in the palace district. She wakes early, reviews her syndicate's accounts, and conducts most business via holographic communication. She takes meals alone in her private chamber, surrounded by the trophies of her career. She sleeps with a cortosis staff within arm's reach. Once a week, she takes a hidden airspeeder to the Dropview Cantina to observe the city's mood from a distance.

---

## Relationships

- **Duke Iribian:** A cold truce. They exchange payments and information, but both know they would betray each other in an instant.
- **TH‑313 "Tacker":** She knows he was Warde's ally and despises him, but she has not moved against him – he is too visible.
- **Lor Kelin & the Crimson Kreehawks:** Her most visible agents. She uses them for the dirty work, keeping her own hands clean.
- **The PCs:** If they mention Warde, she will become intensely interested. She will offer them information in exchange for his location – or try to follow them to him.

---

## Rumours & Hooks

- **Rumour:** Hasar's manor contains a sealed room where she keeps a holographic recreation of the cease‑fire massacre, which she watches when she cannot sleep.
- **Rumour:** She has a bounty on any Jedi or Force‑user – alive or dead – worth 50,000 credits.
- **Plot Hook:** Hasar has a mole inside Iribian's palace. If the PCs can identify and turn her spy, they gain valuable intelligence.
- **Plot Hook:** Hasar's personal physician, a Twi'lek named Voren, is the only person she trusts implicitly. He could be a way to reach her peacefully – or a target to leverage.

---

## Encounter Notes

Hasar is always accompanied by two IG-100 MagnaGuard droids and a squad of Syndicate thugs. She is calm and businesslike unless Warde is mentioned – then her hatred surfaces. She will cooperate with the PCs only if she believes they can lead her to Warde. If betrayed or threatened, she unleashes her full criminal might.

# TH-313 "Tacker"

**Type:** Rival (Stormtrooper Captain)  
**Role:** Clone veteran, now a stormtrooper captain under Duke Irbian  
**Description:** Tacker is one of the few clone troopers still serving the Empire. He fought alongside Suljo Warde during the Clone Wars and secretly retains a deep loyalty to the Jedi Knight. He is aged but sharp, and his memories of Order 66 are jumbled – he feels guilt and confusion about that day. He quietly sympathises with Force‑users, a secret that could cost him his life.

---

## Backstory & Origins

TH‑313, known as "Tacker" to his squadmates, was cloned on Kamino and trained for the Grand Army of the Republic. He served with distinction in the Outer Rim campaigns, and his tactical acumen caught the attention of Jedi General Suljo Warde. Tacker became one of Warde's trusted subordinates, serving alongside him on Arbooine and later on Cato Neimoidia. He respected Warde's foresight and believed the Jedi's predictions were sincere. When Warde fell and Order 66 was issued, Tacker's memories are clouded – he recalls a sudden compulsion, a voice in his mind telling him to turn on his Jedi allies. He resisted, but he saw his brothers obey without question. He has never fully recovered from that day. He stayed in Imperial service out of a sense of duty – and because he has nowhere else to go.

---

## Personality & Mannerisms

Tacker is weary. He moves with the deliberate care of an aging soldier, and his eyes carry the weight of decades of combat. He speaks slowly, often pausing to search for the right word. He is deeply loyal to his men, and he treats his stormtrooper squad with a paternal care that is unusual for an Imperial officer. He is haunted by Warde's fate and secretly hopes the Jedi Knight found peace. He hates the Empire's brutality but feels powerless to resist. Meeting the PCs – especially if they remind him of Warde – stirs up old emotions.

**Daily Routine:** Tacker reports to the Ducal Palace each morning, reviews patrol schedules, and conducts inspections of the Southside Prison (see page 47). He takes his meals in the barracks mess with his troopers, refusing the officers' mess. Evenings are spent in his small quarters, cleaning his blaster and staring at a holopic of his old squad.

---

## Relationships

- **Duke Irbian:** A professional relationship. Tacker serves without complaint, but he does not respect the duke.
- **Onrein Hasar:** He is aware of her syndicate's influence and has orders to ignore it. He resents this.
- **The PCs:** If they prove they are allies of Warde or Force‑users of good intention, he will help them – but he will be terrified of discovery.

---

## Rumours & Hooks

- **Rumour:** Tacker still wears an old Republic command code cylinder – a keepsake from the war.
- **Rumour:** He secretly keeps a journal detailing Warde's last days on Jorra.
- **Plot Hook:** If the PCs are captured, Tacker may deliberately leave their cell unlocked.
- **Plot Hook:** He knows the precise location of Sankar Palace and the safest route through the ventral ruins.

---

## Encounter Notes

Tacker can be found in the Ducal Palace or patrolling Jorra. He will help the PCs if they can convince him they are not enemies of Warde's legacy. He knows the full story of Warde's fall and the location of Sankar Palace. If his sympathy is exposed, he becomes a target for Imperial purges – the PCs gain Conflict if they recklessly out him.

# Tor Haido

**Type:** Rival  
**Role:** Factory supervisor, former informant for Suljo Warde  
**Description:** A middle‑aged Neimoidian of unremarkable appearance, Haido worked as an informant for Warde during the Clone Wars, hoping to profit from a Republic victory. When Warde fell from grace, Haido was left with no reward and constant fear of being associated with the disgraced Jedi. He now keeps his head down and resents Warde for ruining his chances.

---

## Backstory & Origins

Haido was born into a family of modest traders on Cato Neimoidia. When the Republic invaded Jorra, he saw an opportunity to advance his family's standing by offering information to the occupation forces. Warde personally recruited him – the Jedi had foreseen that Haido would not betray him. For several months, Haido provided useful intelligence, rooting out Separatist spies. But as Warde grew paranoid, he demanded more and more arrests, and Haido began to fear for his own safety. He saw Warde's downfall coming, and he tried to distance himself. After Warde fled, Haido was interrogated by both Republic and Imperial forces, but he was never charged. He returned to factory work, burying his past. He is haunted by the feeling that he was used and discarded.

---

## Personality & Mannerisms

Haido is nervous, glancing over his shoulder constantly. He speaks in a low, hurried tone, as if afraid of being overheard. He is deeply pragmatic – he wants to be left alone. He resents Warde, not because of the Jedi's fall, but because Warde promised him a future and delivered only danger. He will help the PCs if he believes it will protect him, but he will try to extract a guarantee of safety first.

**Daily Routine:** Haido arrives at factory DE‑13 at dawn, works through the morning, takes a brief lunch break in his office, and leaves at dusk. He does not socialise. He carries a holdout blaster for protection but has never fired it.

---

## Relationships

- **Zel N'got:** Haido discovered N'got's activities but never reported him. He resents that N'got escaped unscathed.
- **The PCs:** Initially evasive, he will open up if they can offer protection or convincingly threaten him.

---

## Rumours & Hooks

- **Rumour:** Haido still has a datapad containing some of Warde's old intelligence reports.
- **Plot Hook:** If the PCs help him escape the Crimson Kreehawk attack, he becomes a grateful ally.
- **Plot Hook:** He knows the location of a hidden Separatist cache in the ventral ruins – a useful distraction.

---

## Encounter Notes

Haido works at factory DE‑13. He is evasive and fearful. PCs must succeed at social checks (Charm, Deception, or Coercion) to extract information. Threats earn Conflict. He knows about Zel N'got and the cease‑fire talks. If the Crimson Kreehawks follow the PCs to his factory, he may be caught in the crossfire.

# Zel N'got

**Type:** Rival (elderly civilian)  
**Role:** Former Separatist spy and saboteur  
**Description:** An ancient, frail Neimoidian who spied on Warde and the Republic during the Clone Wars. He has lived in terror for decades – first of the Republic, then of the Empire, and always of Warde's possible return. He is paranoid, reclusive, and armed only with a holdout blaster that he fires mostly as a warning.

---

## Backstory & Origins

N'got was a mid‑level Trade Federation bureaucrat when the war broke out. He was recruited by Separatist intelligence to monitor Republic activities on Cato Neimoidia. He was good at his job – he was cautious, patient, and meticulous. He provided valuable intelligence on troop movements, supply routes, and Jedi activities. He was assigned to watch Warde specifically, tracking the Jedi's movements and predicting his next moves. After Warde's attack on the delegates, N'got went into hiding. He has not received a single communication from his handlers since. He lives in constant fear that one day, the Empire – or Warde – will come for him. His paranoia has only deepened with age.

---

## Personality & Mannerisms

N'got is frail, jittery, and suspicious. He startles at loud noises and rarely leaves his apartment. He speaks in a whisper, as if sharing secrets. He is deeply lonely but too afraid to trust anyone. He carries a holdout blaster in his pocket at all times and has a habit of patting it for reassurance. He is not a fighter – he will fire warning shots and then try to flee. If cornered, he will break down and confess everything.

**Daily Routine:** N'got wakes at dawn, checks his door locks, and listens to local news broadcasts. He eats a simple meal, paces his apartment, and spends hours staring out his window at the bridge traffic. He sleeps poorly, often waking from nightmares of lightsabers and screaming.

---

## Relationships

- **Tor Haido:** N'got does not know Haido discovered him. If he did, he would be terrified.
- **Onrein Hasar:** He is aware of her syndicate and fears her reach.
- **The PCs:** He initially mistakes them for Imperial agents or Warde's allies. If they can calm him, he becomes a font of information.

---

## Rumours & Hooks

- **Rumour:** N'got still has a coded Separatist communication device that could reveal hidden caches across Cato Neimoidia.
- **Plot Hook:** He is convinced he is being followed – and he may be right. The Syndicate has been monitoring him for years.

---

## Encounter Notes

N'got lives in an apartment in the residential district. He fires warning shots first; the encounter is meant to be resolved narratively rather than through combat. Injuring or killing him earns significant Conflict. He can reveal that Onrein Hasar and TH‑313 were present at the cease‑fire talks and that Warde attacked first.

# Tiela Dalken

**Type:** Rival (civilian)  
**Role:** Neimoidian who knew Warde as a child; Warde saved her family  
**Description:** A young adult Neimoidian whose skin is mottled from stress. She was a child when Warde rescued her family during the battle for Jorra. Despite everything she has heard about his later crimes, she remembers him as a good man and speaks of him with fondness. She lives modestly in the residential district.

---

## Backstory & Origins

Tiela was only a grub (Neimoidian child) when the Republic invaded Jorra. Her family's home was caught in the crossfire, and she would have died if not for Suljo Warde. The Jedi Knight carried her and her siblings to safety, shielding them with his body from falling debris. That moment imprinted on her: Warde was a hero. As she grew older, she heard the stories of his fall – the arrests, the murders, the flight from justice. She has struggled to reconcile the kind Jedi who saved her with the monster others describe. She has come to believe that Warde was fundamentally good but broken by war. She is one of the few people in Jorra who will speak well of him.

---

## Personality & Mannerisms

Tiela is gentle, soft‑spoken, and deeply empathetic. She has a nervous habit of rubbing her hands together when discussing difficult topics. She is not naive – she knows Warde did terrible things – but she chooses to remember the good. She is brave in her own way, willing to defy the city's hatred by speaking his name aloud. She is a beacon of hope in a city of fear.

**Daily Routine:** Tiela works as a clerk in a small shop in the residential district. She takes her meals at home, alone. She reads old Republic news holos in her spare time, searching for any mention of Warde.

---

## Relationships

- **The PCs:** She is immediately friendly if they show an interest in Warde's good side. She becomes a key ally.
- **Tor Haido:** She knew Haido as an associate of Warde's and hopes the PCs can find him.
- **Onrein Hasar:** She fears the syndicate but has never been targeted.

---

## Rumours & Hooks

- **Rumour:** She keeps a small shrine to Warde in her apartment – a holopic and a Republic coin.
- **Plot Hook:** If the PCs are ever in danger, Tiela may risk her safety to shelter them.

---

## Encounter Notes

Tiela is a key lead. If the PCs have earned the community's trust, she freely shares her story and points them toward Tor Haido. She believes Warde was fundamentally good and can help the PCs humanise him, which may aid in redeeming him later.

# Lor Kelin (Crimson Kreehawks Leader)

**Type:** Rival  
**Role:** Gang leader of the Crimson Kreehawks, subordinate to Onrein Hasar  
**Description:** A young, reckless Neimoidian with a taste for speed and violence. She leads the Kreehawks with aggressive charisma, using swoops to terrorise Jorra's streets. She is loyal to Hasar out of fear and profit, but she is also impatient and prone to acting before orders arrive.

---

## Backstory & Origins

Lor Kelin was born in the aftermath of the Clone Wars, into a world of rubble and despair. Her parents were killed in the fighting, and she was raised by older siblings who turned to crime to survive. She grew up on the streets, learning to fight, steal, and pilot swoops before she was a teenager. She joined the Crimson Kreehawks as a scout and rose through the ranks through sheer audacity. She is fiercely proud of her gang and will defend them to the death – but she is also terrified of Hasar's displeasure. She walks a knife's edge between ambition and fear.

---

## Personality & Mannerisms

Lor is loud, cocky, and confrontational. She laughs in the face of danger and enjoys the thrill of the chase. She has a habit of revving her swoop's engines when she is impatient, which is often. She respects strength and speed; she is dismissive of anyone who cannot match her. She is not stupid – she knows when to retreat – but she sometimes lets her pride override her caution.

**Daily Routine:** Lor spends most of her day patrolling the streets of Jorra on her swoop, collecting protection money and intimidating shopkeepers. She gathers with her gang at the Dropview Cantina in the evenings, drinking and boasting. She sleeps in a cramped room above a cantina.

---

- **The PCs:** She views them as a challenge to prove herself.

---
