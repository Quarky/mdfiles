## 2. Provincial Law Enforcement Officer

**Type:** Rival  
**Star Wars Native:** Generic  
**Silhouette:** 1  
**Description:** Neimoidian security personnel employed by the Jorra Spaceport Authority and local law enforcement. They are not stormtroopers – they are civilian officers, armed with light blasters and scanners. They are suspicious of outsiders and diligent in their duties, but they are not fanatical.

- **Wound Threshold:** 10  
- **Strain Threshold:** 10  
- **Soak:** 3 (armoured vest)  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Coercion 1, Perception 2, Ranged (Light) 2, Streetwise 1, Vigilance 2

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:**
- **Diligent Inspector:** Add 1 boost die to Perception checks when searching for contraband or forged documents.

**Equipment:**
- Light blaster pistol (Ranged [Light]; Damage 5; Critical 4; Range [Medium]; Stun setting)
- Handheld scanner
- Comlink

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Officers patrol the spaceport in shifts, checking identification and scanning cargo. They are generally bored and underpaid, making them susceptible to bribes.
- **Attitude:** They are professional but not aggressive. They will not escalate to violence unless provoked.

**Encounter Notes:** These officers appear in the "This Looks Suspicious" encounter. They may be bribed (100-200 credits), deceived (Deception check), or intimidated (Coercion check – earns Conflict).
