## 6. Spacer / Merchant (Generic)

**Type:** Rival (non-combat)  
**Star Wars Native:** Generic  
**Silhouette:** 1  
**Description:** A trader or starship crew member passing through Jorra. They are a potential source of information, goods, or rumours.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-----:|:-------:|:---------:|:-------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| 2     | 2       | 2         | 3       | 2         | 2        |

- **Wound Threshold:** 10  
- **Strain Threshold:** 10  
- **Soak:** 2  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Charm 1, Knowledge (Underworld) 1, Negotiation 2, Perception 2, Piloting (Space) 2

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:** None

**Equipment:**
- Holdout blaster (optional)

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Spacers visit the Dropview Cantina, trade goods, and share news. They are often oblivious to local politics.
- **Attitude:** They are friendly if there is profit to be made.

**Encounter Notes:** Spacers can be a source of rumours, information on off-world events, or even a means of leaving Cato Neimoidia.
