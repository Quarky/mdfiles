## 2. Kelo Poy – Dropview Cantina Barkeep

**Type:** Rival  
**Role:** Barkeep, Syndicate informant  
**Description:** Kelo Poy is a Neimoidian of average appearance who runs the Dropview Cantina. On paper, he is a joint venture with the Jorra Spaceport Authority. In reality, he is a Syndicate front – he reports directly to Onrein Hasar. He is a source of information, but also a danger.

- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Deception 2, Perception 2, Streetwise 3

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:**
- **Syndicate Ear:** Poy can call for Syndicate reinforcements if he is threatened.

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Poy works the bar, serves drinks, and listens. He reports anything unusual to Hasar's lieutenants.
- **Attitude:** He is friendly to customers, but he is always evaluating them.

**Encounter Notes:** The PCs should be cautious around Poy. He will report them to the Syndicate if they cause trouble or ask too many questions about Warde.
