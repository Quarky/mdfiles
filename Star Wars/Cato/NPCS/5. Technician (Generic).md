## 5. Technician (Generic)

**Type:** Rival (non-combat)  
**Star Wars Native:** Generic  
**Silhouette:** 1  
**Description:** A mechanic or technician working for the Crimson Kreehawks or a civilian workshop.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-----:|:-------:|:---------:|:-------:|:---------:|:--------:|
| 2     | 2       | 3         | 2       | 2         | 1        |

- **Wound Threshold:** 8  
- **Strain Threshold:** 10  
- **Soak:** 2  
- **Melee Defense:** 0  
- **Ranged Defense:** 0  

**Skills:**
- Computers 2, Mechanics 3, Perception 1

**Talents:** None

**Abilities:**
- **Grease Monkey:** Add 1 automatic success to Mechanics checks on vehicles or droids.

**Equipment:**
- Tool kit
- Holdout blaster (optional)

**Living World Details:**
- **Daily Routine:** Technicians work in garages or workshops, repairing swoops, droids, and machinery.
- **Attitude:** They are practical and often disinterested in politics – they just want to work.

**Encounter Notes:** Technicians may be encountered as informants, mechanics for hire, or as part of the Kreehawk gang.
