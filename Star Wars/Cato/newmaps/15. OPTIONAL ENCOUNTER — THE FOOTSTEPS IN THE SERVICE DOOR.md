# 15. OPTIONAL ENCOUNTER — THE FOOTSTEPS IN THE SERVICE DOOR

## 15.1 Purpose

Give the party an immediate situation in which limited evidence could be mistaken for a complete threat.

This encounter follows naturally if the reception droid has been restored and can move.

If it remains immobile, omit this encounter or use the established surviving BX branch below.

## 15.2 Setup

Reception’s local service indicator reports a problem near the eastern suite.

The droid approaches through the utility vestibule carrying a tool and a replacement lamp component.

In the corridor, its movement is partially concealed.

### Warning

> Three measured impacts sound beyond the service door.
>
> Something pauses.
>
> A metal edge touches the frame.
>
> In the room’s lingering pressure, the pause feels very long.

Ask what the characters do.

Do not call for initiative before they have a chance to identify the approach.

## 15.3 Available information

- Listening reveals a familiar motor sound.
- Calling out receives a response.
- Looking through the service slot reveals the lamp component.
- Waiting allows the droid to announce itself.
- A character using a sensible observation method identifies it without a roll if there is no real uncertainty.

## 15.4 If they attack immediately

Resolve the attack normally using the droid’s established or GM-selected profile.

Do not automatically label the act a dark-side fall.

The consequence is concrete:

- Reception may be damaged.
- Its tool falls where the party can see it.
- A possible witness and assistant has been harmed.
- The players decide what to do next.

If appropriate:

> “The indicator said this room required assistance.”

## 15.5 If they identify it first

It reports:

> “I brought the replacement.”
>
> “You have sufficient light, but the fitting is unsafe.”

It repairs the light or asks for assistance.

Nothing else attacks.

Let a reasonable decision produce a quiet, useful outcome.

## 15.6 Surviving BX alternative

Use only if hostile or unresolved BX droids remain below.

A scout checks the renewed service signal.

It approaches cautiously, using a doorway rather than walking into the center of the room.

Possible responses:

- Hide and allow it to inspect.
- Shut down the local signal before it arrives.
- Present genuine maintenance activity and attempt a relevant social approach.
- Observe its patrol timing.
- Fight or retreat.

Reception badges alone do not override military orders.

Use the BX profile and security disposition already established for the campaign.

A retreating scout reports what it actually observed. It does not give every enemy supernatural knowledge of the party’s plans.
