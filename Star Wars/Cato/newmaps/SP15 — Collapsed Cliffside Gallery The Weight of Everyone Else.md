# SP15 — Collapsed Cliffside Gallery: The Weight of Everyone Else

**Suggested placement:** After SP14, before SP16; optional branch to SP18  
**Session length:** 2–3 hours  
**Primary encounters:** Traversal, rescue, negotiation over shared equipment  
**Story purpose:** Make the party build or choose a route that works for more than its most capable member

## The Story

A narrow surviving strip crosses the ruined gallery.

An agile person can get through.

That is not the problem.

The route must also carry injured people, tools, a recovered regulator, and perhaps evidence brought back from the palace.

A salvage crew has installed a temporary line using one secure anchor and one decorative fitting. It worked for light loads. It will not safely carry the next heavy transfer.

The party can cross alone, improve the route, negotiate for proper equipment, or find another way.

Their choice determines whether this becomes a usable passage for others.

## Hooks

- Vessa identifies the crossing as the next obstacle.
- Nemi is preparing to send a carrying frame across.
- The party needs a route suitable for a companion, droid, or bulky object.
- A maintenance doorway across the gap leads toward SP16.

## Arrival

> The gallery’s outer wall has fallen away.
>
> Fog fills the space beyond the broken floor.
>
> A rope crosses the gap.
>
> On the far side, it is secured to a maintenance eye set into the structure.
>
> On this side, the rope pulls against a decorative metal bracket.
>
> The bracket has begun to bend.

## Physical Layout

Use these features where the existing art supports them:

- An intact inner landing.
- A broken floor gap.
- A secure maintenance anchor hidden behind a loose decorative panel.
- A lower catch shelf.
- A fallen floor or wall panel.
- An overhead inspection rail.
- An exit to SP16.
- An optional service stair toward SP18.

The lower shelf must be visible or discoverable before a failed crossing uses it.

## Encounter One: Inspect Before Crossing

The party can immediately see that the near anchor is inadequate.

Further inspection reveals:

- The proper anchor is behind a removable panel.
- The fallen slab is large enough to help bridge the gap.
- One edge of the slab is cracked.
- The overhead rail can carry a hauled line but needs a reliable attachment.
- The outer railing is not trustworthy.

An Average Mechanics check can assess uncertain load-bearing details.

A character with appropriate experience should receive obvious information without a roll.

## Crossing Options

### A. A Proper Rope Route

Expose the maintenance eye, attach the line, and test it.

Individual crossings may require Average Athletics or Coordination if conditions make them uncertain.

People secured by a proper tether do not automatically fall to their deaths on failure.

### B. A Braced Walkway

Use the fallen panel and additional supports.

An Average Mechanics check establishes a usable arrangement.

Failure reveals a weakness before everyone commits. Another brace or different placement is needed.

### C. Separate People and Cargo

Cross light, then haul objects on a carrying frame.

This takes longer but reduces risk.

### D. Use Character Abilities

Climbing equipment, repulsor devices, capable droids, or Force abilities can solve parts of the problem.

Ask how the rest of the group crosses.

### E. Turn Back

The party can return to the ceremonial route.

The detour remains optional.

## Encounter Two: The Frame Hangs Below

Use after the party chooses a crossing method.

A loaded carrying frame catches beneath the edge.

The line remains secure, but the load cannot move forward.

### What Is Inside

Choose according to campaign state:

- Rennik’s tools.
- Supplies for the shelter.
- A recovered component.
- A disabled utility droid.
- Carefully packed historical records.

### Available Responses

- Lower the frame onto the catch shelf.
- Send a secured climber.
- Use a second line to change the angle.
- Unload part of the frame.
- Release a nonessential attachment.
- Use appropriate equipment or abilities.

### Mechanical Resolution

One meaningful check should resolve an uncertain maneuver.

Do not require three identical rolls for tying, pulling, and lifting unless each introduces a distinct risk.

### Failure

The frame settles onto the shelf, a strap breaks, or a tool becomes difficult to retrieve.

The contents remain recoverable unless the party knowingly abandons them.

## Historical Story: The Emergency Route

An old plate reads:

> PUBLIC EGRESS.
>
> KEEP CLEAR.

A later military fitting partly covers it:

> MOVEMENT AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED.

At a lower height, someone scratched arrows toward the inspection door.

The arrows repeat where a person helping someone crawl could see them.

### Discovery

A damaged evacuation note says:

> “Do not send them back to the main doors.”
>
> “The main doors are not moving.”

This establishes a local evacuation problem.

It does not prove exactly when the final hall violence occurred relative to this evacuation.

## Optional NPC: Daro Pell

**New homebrew NPC.** A Neimoidian salvager who owns the better hauling line.

Daro wants it back after the crossing.

Daro’s concern is reasonable: the line is part of their livelihood.

### Dialogue

> “You can leave a route here.”
>
> “You cannot leave my only line here and call it generosity.”

### Solutions

- Recover the line after building a walkway.
- Trade equivalent equipment.
- Leave a replacement.
- Borrow it with a specific return plan.
- Cross by another method.

This is a disagreement about who bears the cost of helping.

## A Living Detail

Daro recognizes an old knot left by another worker.

> “That one was tied by someone who expected to come back.”

The comment concerns technique and reuse, not mystical knowledge of the worker’s fate.

## Optional Hazard: A Moving Panel

Wind begins lifting a loose panel near the crossing.

Its movement is visible before it breaks free.

The party can secure it, avoid its path, or wait for the gusts to subside.

If ignored, it blocks a route or strikes equipment. Do not use it as an untelegraphed instant-death event.

## Resolution

A successful repair creates a persistent crossing.

Mark whether it supports:

- Individuals.
- Carried loads.
- A trolley.
- A disabled droid.
- A heavy regulator.

Do not claim a light rope route can carry everything.

## Return Payoff

Someone else is using the crossing carefully.

A new note has been added:

> INNER ANCHOR CHECKED.
>
> PANEL BRACED.
>
> CROSS ONE LOAD AT A TIME.

The repair has become local knowledge.
