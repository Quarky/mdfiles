# 23. GM QUICK REFERENCE

## Essential path

1. Arrive and activate the lights.
2. Discover the unfinished visit.
3. Examine the delegation’s preparation.
4. Read Warde’s surviving notes.
5. Choose what to preserve.
6. Descend toward the final reconstruction.

## Optional depth

- Restore reception.
- Investigate private correspondence.
- Repair the light works.
- Allocate electrical service.
- Repair the maintenance landing.
- Resolve the service-door encounter.
- Preserve or remove historical objects.
- Return to finish the ledger.

## Three useful questions

Ask these only when the party needs a prompt.

> “What would convince you that your current explanation is wrong?”

> “Which of these things belongs in a record, and which is still your interpretation?”

> “What do you want to leave different when you go?”

## Final transition

> Behind you, the arrival lights illuminate rooms prepared for people who expected to return.
>
> Ahead, the conference corridor descends farther into the suspended palace.
>
> You have seen what they brought with them.
>
> You have read what Warde wrote before he joined them.
>
> The meeting itself is still below.
