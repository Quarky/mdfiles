# 2. RUNNING THE SESSION

## 2.1 Three things players should understand before leaving

1. **The delegation expected negotiations and a return to ordinary life.**
2. **Warde recognized uncertainty and alternatives, yet anticipated taking the action that would destroy those people.**
3. **The final meeting must still be examined below. Level 15 establishes preparation and intention, not a complete forensic account of the confrontation.**

These discoveries are not locked behind checks.

If characters inspect the obvious papers, ledger, and board, give them the essential information. Checks reveal additional context, improve preservation, or resolve risks around the investigation.

## 2.2 Suggested session structure

### One-session version: approximately 3–4 hours

| Segment | Approximate time | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Arrival and lights | 10–15 minutes | Reawaken the palace and establish the new atmosphere. |
| Atrium and reception | 30–40 minutes | Discover the unfinished visit; choose whether to restore the droid. |
| Western suite | 30–40 minutes | Meet the delegates through their belongings and unfinished work. |
| Service gallery | 35–50 minutes | Solve a practical problem and discover the people who maintained the palace. |
| Warde’s suite | 35–50 minutes | Read, investigate, and discuss the central evidence. |
| Consequences and descent | 15–25 minutes | Resolve player choices and establish the Level 16 approach. |

These are pacing guides, not mandatory timers.

### Two-session version

**Session 15A — The People Who Expected to Leave**

Arrival, reception, western suite, and service works. End with the eastern suite available for exploration and a question raised by its silence.

**Session 15B — The Man Who Expected the Worst**

Warde’s suite, deeper investigation, an optional encounter, preservation choices, and the descent.

If the players enter Warde’s room early, let them. Do not lock it to enforce the preferred order.

## 2.3 Three entry states

### A. The seven were released on Level 14

The absence of their collective presence is perceptible to a character who previously sensed them. The remaining presence feels distinct.

### B. The seven were encountered but not released

Their residue remains behind. The presence below is still distinguishable, but the party has not brought closure to the earlier encounter.

Do not punish them with a combat encounter for leaving. Their unfinished business is enough.

### C. The Ghost Chamber was bypassed

Pelri’s stone is initially an unidentified personal possession. Her name can be connected to it through the western suite’s papers.

Sigg does not know its owner by intuition.

## 2.4 Character agency

- Ask what the characters notice and do.
- Describe Force impressions without dictating the character’s emotional response.
- Use Sigg’s dialogue as optional prompts, not required speeches.
- If Sigg is player-controlled, give his player relevant observations and let them choose his words.
- The Pathfinder may share or conceal impressions.
- Respect earlier decisions about whether the Rusted Stalker enters the palace. If it remains above, record who is attending it and where the party intends to reunite.
