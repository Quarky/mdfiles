# 21. MAP USE AND MODIFICATIONS

These suggestions are grounded in the story/map reference catalogue. They are not a visual audit of the current ZIP’s wall placements.

## 21.1 SP08 — Grand Atrium & Preparation Suites

### Preserve

- Formal symmetry.
- Pale stone with dark metal and blue-and-gold detail.
- Central Trade Federation mosaic.
- Reception.
- Opposing preparation suites.
- Ceremonial route between upper and lower stairs.

### Add as props or overlays where space permits

- A visibly dormant reception droid token.
- The open guest ledger.
- A trolley beside the service door.
- One faulty light indicator.
- A folding chair in the western suite.
- Pantry access on the staff side.
- Small maintenance repairs interrupting decorative perfection.

### Lighting

Use several localized light groups for the ignition sequence.

The purple-white lamps should reveal the artwork without washing the entire scene white. Service lighting may use subdued warm or blue tones consistent with the reference.

### Doors

Keep Warde’s suite accessible.

The service door may need manual operation, but it should not become a mandatory slicing gate.

## 21.2 SP09 — Warde’s Preparation Suite

### Preserve

- One table.
- One chair.
- One cup.
- Dark communications equipment.
- The notation board.
- A restrained, personal scale.

### Add

- A readable close-up handout for the board.
- A sheltered wall area explaining the preserved writing.
- A hospitality strip with the postponement request.
- Utility access placed to fit the existing image.

### Avoid

- A large Sith altar.
- A second secret laboratory.
- A combat arena hidden behind the wall.
- A fully intact recording of the massacre.
- Decorative bodies implying unestablished deaths on this level.

The room’s strength comes from its ordinary function.

## 21.3 SP10 — Service Gallery & Chemical-Light Works

### Preserve

- Maintenance routes.
- Industrial access and utility infrastructure.
- The physical logic of a palace suspended from above.
- Fog at exterior breaks.

### Add as separate overlays

- Admission, return, release, and bypass controls.
- A load gauge for the electrical bus.
- A collection vessel.
- A visible damaged coupling.
- A drain trough.
- The unsafe landing’s inspection marks.
- A workers’ bench.

### Tactical usefulness

Provide:

- A route back to reception.
- A route toward the western pantry.
- A utility connection near Warde’s suite.
- A return joining the lower-stair approach.

Keep these connections consistent between maps and journal notes.

## 21.4 Additional assets worth creating

| Asset | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Warde’s board close-up | Lets players examine the text without leaving the scene to hear a long reading. |
| Reception ledger close-up | Makes the blank departure column tangible. |
| Light-control panel handout | Gives the machinery puzzle a clear visual interface. |
| Three-state lighting overlay | Supports dormant, waking, and stabilized versions of SP08. |
| Staff notices sheet | Adds lived-in detail economically. |
| Suite evidence markers | Allows discoveries to be tracked without permanently labeling the art. |

## 21.5 New full maps

No new full battlemap is required for this chapter.

The existing SP08–SP10 functions already support its major scenes.

If more room is needed, the most useful addition would be a compact hospitality annex containing the pantry, staff bench, and delivery corridor. It should serve ordinary palace operations, not duplicate the atrium or Warde’s room.

Maintain the established map conventions:

- No NPCs baked into artwork.
- GM labels and hazards on separate overlays.
- Walls following actual pictured architecture.
- Existing 20 px odd-column hex presentation retained unless the map project changes it deliberately.
- Fog and nearby suspended structures at exterior openings.
- No conventional rooftop or ground-level basement geometry.
