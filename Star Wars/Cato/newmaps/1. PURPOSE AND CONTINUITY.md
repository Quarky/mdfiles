# 1. PURPOSE AND CONTINUITY

Level 15 is where Warde stops being a historical name and becomes a person whose choices can be examined.

The levels above have introduced the palace’s culture, politics, victims, and supernatural aftermath. Here, the party enters the rooms used immediately before the final negotiations.

They discover people preparing to continue their lives:

- Staff arranged meals for after the meeting.
- A delegate left a coat because the conference was supposed to be brief.
- An administrator maintained a departure ledger.
- Someone repaired a chair instead of replacing it.
- Warde sat alone, examining futures he increasingly refused to believe could end differently.

The level’s central question is:

> **What happens when someone can imagine several futures but begins treating only the worst one as real?**

Do not answer that question through a single speech. Let the rooms, records, choices, and players’ own disagreements develop it.
