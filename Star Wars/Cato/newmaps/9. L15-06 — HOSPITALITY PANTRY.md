# 9. L15-06 — HOSPITALITY PANTRY

## 9.1 Read aloud

> Behind the delivery door, the ceremonial stone ends.
>
> The walls are washable panels. Shelves carry empty containers and numbered trays. A strip of tape holds a broken latch shut.
>
> Someone drew a chair on the staff noticeboard.
>
> Beneath it:
>
> “This is what a break looks like.”

## 9.2 What the pantry contributes

This room shows the negotiations through the people who supported them.

They were not debating galactic strategy. They were trying to:

- Serve the correct food.
- Keep translations working.
- Finish a shift.
- Avoid embarrassing mistakes.
- Get people home.

## 9.3 The meal ticket

**New handout:**

> CONFERENCE HOSPITALITY
>
> Retain evening service.
>
> Delegation may continue after scheduled adjournment.
>
> Keep one plain portion aside.
>
> Do not discard the second tea preparation. West suite requested it for afterward.

The untouched second preparation is spoiled beyond use.

The important discovery is the word **afterward**.

## 9.4 Staff disagreement

Two notes share the same board.

> “No personal items in the preparation rooms.”

Beneath it:

> “Then provide somewhere to put them.”

A third hand has added:

> “Hooks installed. Stop writing on this notice.”

This establishes a workplace that changed in response to ordinary complaints.

## 9.5 A practical cache

A sealed maintenance drawer contains:

- Two insulated leads.
- An intact hand lamp.
- A small mirror on an articulated stem.
- Absorbent cloths.
- A seal suitable for the works’ damaged return coupling.

These are tools, not high-value treasure.

If the party searched carefully, give them the drawer without a roll.

## 9.6 A glimpse of Warde before the final day

Inside an older maintenance folder is a copied service request.

**New homebrew handout:**

> REQUEST: Temporary extension of civilian waiting area.
>
> Existing seating insufficient during checkpoint reviews.
>
> Authorization appended: S. Warde.
>
> Note: Provide water before further interviews.

A later sheet uses the same administrative format:

> REQUEST: Additional holding-space partitions.
>
> Release conditions awaiting command clarification.

The later request is not signed clearly enough to attribute it to Warde.

### Interpretation

The earlier record supports a concrete act of care by Warde.

The later record shows a worsening institutional situation but does not, by itself, establish who ordered it.

This adds complexity without converting a sympathetic detail into an excuse for the killings.

### Connection to Tacker

If Tacker described Warde’s early concern for people, this independently supports that portion of his testimony.

If the party never met Tacker, it works on its own.
