# 10. L15-07 — THE SERVICE GALLERY

## 10.1 Read aloud

> Pipes run along the wall behind numbered access covers.
>
> The floor slopes slightly toward a narrow drain. Inspection tags hang from valves at shoulder height.
>
> Through gaps in the outer structure, fog moves across black support members.
>
> The palace makes a different sound here: relays, dripping water, and the slow movement of something carrying weight.

## 10.2 Navigation

The gallery connects:

- Reception.
- The pantry.
- The light works.
- Warde’s utility vestibule.
- The lower-stair approach.

Every junction has a service symbol and a directional mark.

Translation or basic comparison with the reception directory is enough to navigate. Do not require a sequence of rolls to understand ordinary signage.

## 10.3 The workers’ bench

A bench is fixed between two pipe supports.

Under it:

- A cup with a chipped handle.
- A dried cleaning cloth.
- A packet of replacement screws.
- A scratched game board with several missing pieces.

No tragic revelation accompanies every item.

Let the players encounter a place where people also wasted time, complained, and entertained themselves.

## 10.4 Eleth’s maintenance priorities

A work sheet carries the facility manager’s name.

**New handout:**

> ELETH SARRINO — OUTSTANDING WORK
>
> 1. West circulation drain.
> 2. Loose support at service landing.
> 3. Translation sockets, preparation suites.
> 4. Reception docking lead.
> 5. Cosmetic repairs, atrium outer ring.
>
> Do not reverse this list because the ceremonial office is expecting visitors.

This supports the portrait of Eleth as someone who loved the building while understanding its practical needs.

## 10.5 Quiet environmental warning

Near the works:

- Moisture beads on an insulated line.
- A pressure indicator trembles near its marked limit.
- A stained inspection tag reads “RETURN FIRST.”
- A lamp pulses in time with a faint knock inside the pipe.

These are visible warnings for the puzzle ahead.
