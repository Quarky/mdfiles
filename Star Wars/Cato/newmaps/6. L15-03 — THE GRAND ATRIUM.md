# 6. L15-03 — THE GRAND ATRIUM

## 6.1 Read aloud

> The atrium opens roughly thirty meters across, beneath a ceiling six meters above the floor.
>
> A mosaic covers the central space. Trade routes spread outward from the purse worlds in fine bands of metal and colored stone.
>
> Reception stands to the right. Two broad corridors lead toward preparation rooms on opposite sides.
>
> Along one wall, a narrow line interrupts the decorative symmetry: a service door, painted to disappear.
>
> A trolley stands beside it with one wheel turned sideways.

## 6.2 The mosaic

Preserve the red, restricted world marking from the earlier drafts.

Let characters inspect:

- The purse worlds at the center.
- Trade routes and commercial relationships.
- Areas left outside the trade network.
- Repairs to tiles along a frequently used path.
- The restricted marking at the outer edge.

### Player-facing inscription

> RESTRICTED  
> NO COMMERCIAL ACTIVITY  
> HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE ONLY  
> CONSULT DIPLOMATIC RECORDS BEFORE CONTACT

### What it establishes

The palace’s makers included a restricted location in their institutional geographic display.

### What it does not establish

It does not prove:

- Every employee knew Sith history.
- The delegates knew Warde would travel there.
- The floor provides a safe hyperspace route.
- The palace was built around a secret Sith agenda.

If the party already identified the location as Moraband, this is corroboration.

If they have not, record it as **the restricted red world**. Its identification remains a later discovery.

## 6.3 The repair that interrupts the grandeur

One trade-route line contains a tile of the wrong shade.

Beside it, nearly invisible unless someone kneels, are tiny workshop marks.

A maintenance slip in the reception drawer explains:

> “Replacement batch rejected by ceremonial office. Installed anyway. Original fracture was catching walking aids.”

The slip is signed with initials, not a famous name.

This is the first clear sign that the palace’s staff sometimes chose usefulness over appearance.

## 6.4 The stranded trolley

The trolley carries:

- A sealed package of clean writing sheets.
- Translation earpiece housings.
- An empty hot-drink carrier.
- Seven folded place cards with the names no longer readable.
- A maintenance tag identifying the jammed wheel.

The wheel can be repaired without a roll if the party takes time and has basic tools.

The trolley can then carry recovered evidence, a disabled droid, or supplies.

**Future consequence:** If used for evidence, it is cumbersome on the service stair. The party must repack or choose the broader ceremonial route.

## 6.5 Optional conversation prompt

If a companion is needed to start discussion:

> “They were still bringing things in.”

Stop there. Let the party decide what that means.
