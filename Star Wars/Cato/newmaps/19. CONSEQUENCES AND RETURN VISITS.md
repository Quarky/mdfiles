## 19.2 Return scenes

Choose those caused by the party’s actions.

### If reception was restored

> “You have returned.”
>
> The droid turns the ledger toward you.
>
> “Is the record more complete?”

### If the lights were repaired

The same corridor lights steadily on the return. Their work is visible.

### If the lamp bank was isolated

Their own lights reveal the dark fixtures they intentionally shut down.

### If the board was copied

They can compare the words with what they actually found in the final hall.

### If the stone was taken

Ask its carrier what they do with it after the investigation.

Do not assume they forget.

### If the landing was repaired

A companion checks the brace, then crosses without the earlier hesitation.

### If the private datapad was left unopened

The party may reconsider after identifying the dead. The decision has acquired context.

## 19.3 Consequences outside the palace

The party can eventually:

- Give a careful account to Tacker.
- Return identified belongings.
- Preserve the names of victims.
- Share practical repair knowledge with people using the ventral routes.
- Distinguish their findings from local rumor.
- Decide what the gatekeeper should be told and shown.

Do not make every recovered object immediately find its owner’s surviving family. Some endings remain incomplete.
