# 18. EVIDENCE NETWORK

The party should reach useful conclusions through more than one kind of evidence.

Not every source has equal authority.

| Conclusion | Primary evidence | Corroboration | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| The delegates expected to continue their lives | Coat, drawing, private correspondence | Later meal arrangements | Personal expectation does not guarantee political success. |
| They prepared substantive negotiations | Working clauses | Hospitality and scheduling records | Drafts were not ratified agreements. |
| Seven delegates attended preparation | Reception ledger | Seven initial marks and seventh seat | Does not reconstruct their positions in the final hall. |
| Warde saw the delegates as sincere | His notation board | Delegation’s practical working papers | His judgment about distant commanders remains unverified. |
| Warde acknowledged alternatives | His notation board | Optional Force impression of repeated narrowing | The echo is subjective residue, not independent proof. |
| Warde expressed exhaustion and isolation | His own writing | Room arrangement and optional impression | Does not prove nobody offered help. |
| Warde previously authorized a humane measure | Older service request | Tacker, if his testimony matches | One good act does not erase later harm. |
| The palace’s staff expected continued operation | Shift form and maintenance records | Prepared supplies and deferred repairs | Individual staff fates remain unknown. |
| A restricted world appeared in institutional records | Atrium inscription | Earlier markings, if found | Not a navigational solution or proof of a conspiracy. |
| The final confrontation remains below | Public directory and conference route | Reception’s last appointment record | Level 15 is not the final scene itself. |

## 18.1 If the players miss a room

### They skip the western suite

Reception can mention the retained evening service. The physical delegation evidence remains available on their return.

### They skip the works

They can still read the ledger and notation board and use the main stair.

### They skip reception

The suite papers and public directory establish the level’s function.

### They skip Warde’s room

Do not force them back.

At the lower stair, an ordinary suite directory identifies his preparation room. If they continue, note that his board remains undiscovered.

The campaign can proceed, but their understanding of his preparation is less complete.

### They destroy or remove evidence

Track the real consequences. Do not spontaneously create perfect duplicate copies in every other room.

The evidence network preserves progress through different facts, not through unlimited replacements.
