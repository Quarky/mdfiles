# Palace Evacuation Interchange

**Campaign:** Chronicles of the Gatekeeper — Cato Neimoidia  
**Suggested placement:** Optional route before Level 15 or between the broken wing and the conference approach  
**Suggested length:** One session, approximately 3–4 hours  
**Map status:** Proposed new map  
**Content status:** New homebrew expansion  
**Primary themes:** Evacuation, conflicting priorities, movement, and decisions under uncertainty

## Session Premise

The interchange once connected public circulation, cargo movement, and emergency evacuation.

During the palace’s crisis, people attempted to use those functions at the same time.

The surviving layout records the collision:

- Civilian routes.
- Military restrictions.
- Cargo equipment.
- Emergency shutters.
- Improvised signs.
- Departures that occurred and departures that did not.

The party must now move through the interchange while an old automated carrier resumes a limited cycle.

Their present problem echoes the historical one: how to keep people moving when procedures and machinery do not agree.

## Continuity Boundaries

- This is an expansion of the palace’s logistical aftermath.
- It does not establish a new detailed timeline for Moll or Durael.
- It does not relocate the final meeting.
- It contains evidence of departures but not a complete list of everyone who survived.
- The present carrier event is caused by restored power or an established local activation.
- No activation should occur if the party has kept that system isolated.

## Story Hooks

### A Route for Heavy Objects

The interchange offers a way to move a damaged droid, regulator, or recovered evidence without carrying everything over narrow stairs.

### A Missing Person Record

A transfer list from the processing hall references this interchange.

### A Route Warning

Nemi reports that an old carrier has begun moving again.

### A New Approach

A service directory identifies an exit joining the Level 16 approach before the security junction.

## Map Layout

Include:

- A pedestrian concourse.
- A cargo platform.
- Two carrier docking positions.
- An emergency stair.
- A military checkpoint added to one route.
- A control booth.
- A baggage and equipment area.
- Emergency shutters.
- A maintenance passage connecting the booth and machinery.
- A visible shaft or exterior void beyond protected barriers.

The carrier serves local suspended structures. It is not an unexplained transport system across the galaxy.

## The Place Before the Crisis

Ordinary surviving details include:

- Scuffs from luggage.
- A height-adjustable loading counter.
- A family waiting marker.
- A place to secure wheeled mobility equipment.
- A damaged snack dispenser.
- A maintenance notice about a noisy door.
- A bench facing an arrival indicator.

These functions explain why civilians and staff came here.

## The Historical Layers

### Original Signage

> PUBLIC CONNECTION.
>
> CARGO TRANSFER.
>
> EMERGENCY EXIT.

### Later Restriction

> MOVEMENT SUBJECT TO AUTHORIZATION.

### Improvised Correction

An arrow bypasses the checkpoint toward a staff door.

A note says:

> “Emergency stair remains open.”

### What This Establishes

Someone preserved an evacuation route despite added movement restrictions.

It does not identify that person automatically.

## Key NPC: A Surviving Route Droid

Use an existing suitable utility droid Actor.

**Role:** Local transport attendant  
**Condition:** Limited mobility; damaged clock; reliable knowledge of its immediate carrier  
**Goal:** Complete a safe local cycle  
**Limitation:** It does not understand the current status of every old authorization

### First Words

> “Please clear the marked lane.”
>
> “The lane has been obstructed for longer than expected.”

The obstruction is a military barrier installed during the crisis.

## What the Droid Knows

- Which local platform the carrier serves.
- Whether its docking mechanism is engaged.
- Which sensors are faulty.
- That its normal walking lane is blocked.
- That a scheduled cycle was interrupted.

It does not know:

- Why Warde attacked the delegates.
- Whether every person in an old manifest boarded.
- The current location of departed passengers.
- Any interstellar destination.

## Puzzle: Three Records of One Departure

The party finds:

1. A boarding authorization.
2. A load manifest.
3. A completed carrier cycle.

The documents are not interchangeable.

### Example

A person appears on the authorization but not the final load list.

A crate appears on the manifest but was removed before departure.

The carrier completed a cycle, but that does not prove every authorized passenger traveled.

### Supporting Clues

- An amended count.
- A baggage item left behind.
- A handwritten boarding correction.
- The attendant’s limited event record.

### Resolution

The party can establish one specific departure with reasonable confidence and leave another uncertain.

If connected to Keth’s story, this may identify the next local stop after release.

It should not immediately solve his entire later life.

## Present Encounter: The Carrier Is Waking

The carrier begins a diagnostic cycle.

Its sensors detect the old barrier as cargo.

If allowed to proceed, it will attempt to move while an access route is obstructed.

### Warning Sequence

1. Amber indicator.
2. Audible lane-clear announcement.
3. Docking clamps release partially.
4. The carrier pauses when load readings disagree.

The party has several opportunities to act.

### Objectives

- Keep the pedestrian lane clear.
- Decide whether to use or shut down the carrier.
- Move a current load safely.
- Avoid trapping someone behind an emergency shutter.

## Safe Solutions

### A. Restore the Intended Route

Remove or reposition the later barrier.

Check the docking area.

Allow the attendant to complete diagnostics.

### B. Keep the Carrier Isolated

Use the marked local cutoff.

Travel by the emergency stair.

This is safe and valid.

### C. Supervised Manual Cycle

A technician operates the carrier while another character observes the docking connection.

Use Average Mechanics or Computers for an unfamiliar but intact system.

### D. Use It for One Load

Inspect the carrier, secure the cargo, complete a single transfer, then return it to safe standby.

Do not let the party operate it only once if nothing in the fiction prevents further use; one load is simply a practical option.

## Complication: Conflicting Door States

An emergency shutter closes when the carrier departs, while a later military lock keeps the alternate door shut.

The original safety design assumed the alternate door was available.

### Clues

- The old diagram shows both routes.
- The military lock has a different installation date.
- The attendant identifies the shutter’s function.
- The maintenance passage allows access to the lock from the safe side.

### Resolution

Open the alternate door before beginning the carrier cycle, or hold the shutter in approved maintenance mode.

The puzzle concerns incompatible modifications, not guessing a secret code.

## Failure Consequences

- A load remains stranded at a docking point.
- A shutter blocks the convenient route.
- The carrier stops and requires a reset.
- A character must use the maintenance passage.
- A loose object falls into a recoverable service tray.

Avoid a surprise fatal fall from an ordinary control failure.

## Optional Social Encounter: Two Loads

Two groups want the same carrier.

One carries repair equipment.

The other carries personal belongings from an unsafe dwelling.

Both have legitimate reasons to hurry.

The carrier can eventually move both, but its inspection takes time.

Possible arrangements:

- Share a load within safe limits.
- Move people first and equipment second.
- Use the emergency stair for lighter objects.
- Repair the delay.
- Ask one group to help the other.

Do not make one claimant secretly evil merely to simplify the choice.

## Warde’s Story Connection

A copied restriction bears his command designation.

A later evacuation note overrides local movement restrictions for emergency departure.

The author of the override is unclear.

This allows the party to see people adapting to a crisis without attributing every compassionate action to Warde or every restriction solely to him.

## A Historical Detail Worth Preserving

Beside the carrier door, someone marked a child’s height.

A later mark sits slightly higher.

The interchange was used by families long before the evacuation.

The final crisis should not erase the place’s whole life.

## Resolution

If the party restores a useful route, record:

- Its actual destination.
- Its load limitations.
- Who knows how to operate it.
- Whether it remains powered.
- Whether current residents can use it.

The route can later help with the return from Level 16.
