# 16. OPTIONAL ENCOUNTER — THE LANDING THAT MOVES

## 16.1 Location

The maintenance return between SP10 and the lower-stair approach.

This provides action without introducing another combatant.

## 16.2 Warning

> The handrail moves when you touch it.
>
> Beneath the landing, a bracket shifts against its mounting plate.
>
> Someone marked the plate with yellow maintenance paint.
>
> The painted line no longer meets itself.

The gap is visible before anyone commits.

## 16.3 The situation

A short landing has lost one secondary support.

The main route remains available through the atrium.

This is a choice, not a mandatory obstacle.

## 16.4 Approaches

| Approach | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Return to the ceremonial route | Safe; costs travel time. |
| Brace the landing | Average Mechanics with suitable material. |
| Cross individually using an independent tether | Average Athletics or Coordination if uncertainty remains. |
| Use established movement equipment or Force abilities | Resolve through existing character rules. |
| Send a light scout first | Learns where the movement is greatest; does not prove the landing supports everyone. |

## 16.5 Failure

A character slips onto a lower maintenance catch platform or hangs from their tether.

Suggested immediate cost: 2 strain, followed by an assisted recovery.

Do not turn a single routine failure into a fatal plunge.

Dropped objects land on the catch platform unless the player knowingly handled them over an unprotected opening.

## 16.6 Reward for repair

A repaired landing remains repaired.

On the return journey:

- The party has a reliable alternate route.
- A carried droid or evidence trolley may still require the broader stair.
- The repaired bracket is a visible sign that the party did more than take things from the palace.
