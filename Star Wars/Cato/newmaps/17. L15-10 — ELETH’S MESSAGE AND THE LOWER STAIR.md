# 17. L15-10 — ELETH’S MESSAGE AND THE LOWER STAIR

## 17.1 Placement rule

If Eleth’s message was already found above, do not reveal a second identical message here.

Instead, place a maintenance label bearing the same name beside an inactive display frame.

If the message was missed, the frame provides its fallback location.

## 17.2 Discovery

The frame is visibly loose. An edge of writing shows behind it.

Moving it reveals the message without a roll.

## 17.3 Preserved message

> If you are reading this, the display is down.
>
> This means something has gone wrong.
>
> My name is Eleth Sarrino.
>
> I was the facility manager of Sankar Palace
> from the Year of the Fourth Expansion
> to the evacuation.
>
> I lived here for eleven years.
>
> I loved this building.
>
> If something has gone wrong,
> I want someone to know
> that this was a good building.
>
> That people met here and talked
> and sometimes reached agreements
> that made things better.
>
> That the mosaic in the atrium took two years to lay
> and the artists were proud.
>
> That the canyon view from the conference level
> is the most beautiful view I have ever seen.
>
> I want someone to know that
> before the building becomes
> whatever it becomes next.
>
> I am sorry if things went wrong.
>
> I hope they did not.
>
> I hope this is never read.

The reference to a beautiful canyon view describes Eleth’s remembered experience. It does not require the present fog to clear.

## 17.4 The two apologies

Players may connect Eleth’s apology with Warde’s.

Do not announce that connection unless they need a prompt.

One person feared what might become of a place they maintained.

Another anticipated what he himself would do.

The comparison is available. The interpretation belongs to the party.

## 17.5 Descending

> The lower stair is narrower.
>
> The same pale stone continues, but the decorative bands thin into practical edging.
>
> Through a gap between the landings, you see the conference corridor below.
>
> Its doors are closed.
>
> Along the wall, a service light changes from amber to white.

If surviving droids are active:

> From farther along the corridor comes a measured mechanical sound.
>
> It stops.
>
> Then resumes.

If the droids were destroyed or peacefully resolved, replace that cue with:

> Water falls somewhere beyond the landing.
>
> No footsteps answer it.

If the remaining Force presence has been established:

> The distinct presence below is nearer now.
>
> Its nature is still unclear.

End by asking:

> “Which route do you take into the conference level?”
