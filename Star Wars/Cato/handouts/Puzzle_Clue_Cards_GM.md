# GM Puzzle Clue Cards

## Mosaic Path clue

"Five maintenance contacts are older than the decline panel. Their wear follows the same sequence as the museum's founding chronology."

## Hospitality Protocol clue

"The standing braces are worn at shoulder height far above Neimoidian reach. The winged seal repeats on the brace, not on the floor cushions."

## Missing Route clue

"The red marker appears in two independent maps, but route tables skip it rather than listing a broken route. The absence is deliberate."

## Chemical-Light Works clue

"A maintenance stencil beneath the panel reads: STABILIZER BEFORE ACTIVATOR AFTER LONG SHUTDOWN. Someone wrote 'YES, REALLY' beside it."

## Table reconstruction clue

"Damage does not all belong to the same moment. The cuts at the table occur before the blaster impacts; the crossed saber marks begin later and move outward."

## Anchor puzzle clue

"One pylon is carrying load for a neighbor that no longer exists. The system is failing because it is faithfully compensating for a structure that is gone."
