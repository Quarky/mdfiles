# GM Evidence Cards - Final Meeting Hall

Print/copy each block onto a separate card or journal entry. Reveal only when the PCs inspect the relevant area.

---

## Card A - The Chair

Warde's chair is not overturned. It is pulled back from the table in a controlled line. The floor beneath it has no skid or impact mark.

**Inference:** he stood deliberately rather than being knocked from his seat.

---

## Card B - The First Cuts

The earliest lightsaber damage at the table is on the delegation side, concentrated at seated height. Nearby bodies show little evidence of movement before being struck.

**Inference:** the first violence was sudden and directed at the delegates.

---

## Card C - The Blaster Response

Blaster impacts begin after the lightsaber damage. Their angles travel from the delegation/bodyguard side toward the Republic side and door approaches.

**Inference:** bodyguards reacted after the initial strike.

---

## Card D - Jedi Against Jedi

A second set of lightsaber scars crosses the first at different angles and moves away from the table toward the balcony/corridor. Some cuts are defensive rather than killing blows.

**Inference:** Moll and Durael engaged Warde after the attack began.

---

## Card E - Warde Was Wounded

Blood/impact evidence and disrupted debris continue toward the exit path. The pattern is inconsistent with an uninjured victor calmly leaving.

**Inference:** Warde escaped badly hurt.

---

## Card F - Durael's Last Position

Jiv Durael is on the ruined balcony with a broken lightsaber nearby and Warde's kyber crystal still in hand.

**Inference:** Durael separated the crystal from Warde during the battle and died preserving it.

---

## GM synthesis

If the players place these in a sensible sequence, let them state the conclusion. Do not correct wording unless they contradict physical evidence. A Force vision can confirm emotional context afterward but should not replace their reconstruction.
