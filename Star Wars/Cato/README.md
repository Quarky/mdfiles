# Sankar Palace Expanded GM Pack

**Campaign:** *Chronicles of the Gatekeeper* - Cato Neimoidia / Jorra  
**Scope:** Ventral-city approach through Sankar Palace Levels 12-16, final meeting hall, return, and optional broken-wing routes.  
**Use:** GM-facing expansion and continuity pack for Star Wars FFG Foundry play.

This pack consolidates the custom Cato material, the current Sankar Palace story/map logic reference, and the adventure's hard continuity. It is designed around one central principle: **the palace should tell Suljo Warde's story through things the PCs can touch, notice, repair, misread, compare, and finally understand.** NPC exposition can confirm or complicate discoveries, but it should not be the only way the story exists.

## Continuity priority used

1. **Current Sankar Palace Story and Map Logic Reference** for physical geometry, palette, map catalogue, and the level-to-level dramatic progression.
2. **Newest custom draft for a specific level or scene** when multiple Cato drafts cover the same material.
3. **Repeated/majority custom details** when a newer draft is clearly experimental or silent on a point.
4. **Published adventure** for hard plot facts and baseline encounter logic unless the newer custom material intentionally expands rather than contradicts it.
5. If none of the above resolves a contradiction cleanly, the issue is marked **GM REVIEW** in `04_CONFLICT_LOG_GM_REVIEW.md` and the rest of the pack proceeds without depending on it.

## Canonical macro-geometry

Sankar Palace hangs from the **ventral underside of Jorra's bridge-city**. The bridge is the structural ceiling. The deeper the party travels, the farther **down and away from the bridge foundation** they go. Exterior openings on the deep levels normally show fog, nearby struts, cables, neighboring suspended architecture, and only limited distance. Natural rock exists where the palace intersects huge support masses or anchor geology; it should look as if architecture has wrapped around rock rather than like a normal basement.

## Running philosophy

- **Do not gate the main plot behind a single roll.** Checks change cost, clarity, danger, or bonus information. Critical revelations have at least three routes.
- **No room exists only to contain a fight.** A combat space should still tell the story of who worked there, what the last orders were, or how the building failed.
- **Do not make Warde a simple monster or a victim of destiny.** The environment should show competence, exhaustion, fear, foreknowledge, rationalization, hesitation, choice, and consequence.
- **The palace was a workplace before it was a tomb.** Add staff routines, maintenance labels, comfort choices, imperfect repairs, personal notes, old scheduling systems, and evidence that people expected to come back tomorrow.
- **The droids are surviving orders, not wandering monsters.** Their tactics and dialogue should point backward to the last day of the war.
- **The fog is a location mechanic.** It hides routes, carries sound, changes exterior visibility, beads water on surfaces, affects footing, and gives the building a pulse of conceal/reveal.

## Source-derived vs. new expansion

The core level themes, Warde evidence, key bodies/crystal, droid threat, inverted geometry, and canonical map functions come from the supplied materials. Puzzles, side rooms, staff micro-stories, fail-forward structures, and some map modifications in this pack are **new GM expansion** built to support those established facts. They are labeled when the distinction matters.
