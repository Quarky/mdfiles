## ENCOUNTER VARIANT — THE PHANTASM
### Overview
This variant is a combat encounter that can be scaled to the party's power level. The ghost attacks with phantasmal weapons and environmental effects, draining the party's strength and resolve.
**Combat Setup:**
| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Encounter Type** | Structured Combat |
| **Initial Distance** | Short Range |
| **Terrain** | Passage or open room with shadows and cover |
| **Environmental Effects** | Darkness, cold, flickering lights |
| **Surprise** | Ghost has the element of surprise (add 1 Setback to party's Initiative) |
**The Ghost's Appearance:**
> *"The figure's form distorts. Its face splits—not into a scream, but into something worse. The suggestion of a smile. A smile made of nothing but teeth, and the teeth are not human teeth."*
> *"'You think you are different from him. You think you can resist. But you will fall. They always fall.'"*
> *"It reaches out. A hand that is not a hand extends toward you. The air in the passage turns to ice."*

## RESOLVING THE ENCOUNTER
### Combat Resolution
The Phantasm can be defeated by:
| Method | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Strain Reduction** | Reduce its strain threshold to 0 |
| **Light Side Force Use** | Successful Force check to banish it (opposed Discipline) |
| **Resolving the Past** | Addressing the wound that created it (roleplay) |
**Description on Defeat:**
> *"The Phantasm's form wavers. Its features distort—for a moment, it is not a monster, but a person. Someone who was here. Someone who died here."*
> *"It speaks one last time, its voice barely a whisper: 'Remember us. Remember what we were.'"*
> *"And then it is gone. The cold recedes. The shadows retreat."*
> *"You are alone again."*
### Roleplay Resolution
The Phantasm can also be resolved through roleplay—showing respect for the dead, acknowledging what was lost, or making a moral choice that honors what happened here.
| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Show respect for the dead** | The Phantasm's hostility lessens; reduce difficulty of checks by 1 |
| **Acknowledge the Federation's loss** | The Phantasm becomes sorrowful instead of angry; gain 5 XP |
| **Promise to remember** | The Phantasm fades peacefully; gain 2 Light Side Destiny Points |
| **Attempt to banish with the Force** | Opposed Discipline check; success ends encounter, failure results in 4 Conflict |
**Roleplay Resolution Description:**
> *"The Phantasm's form wavers. Its anger seems to falter."*
> *"'You... you see it. You understand. There are so few who do.'"*
> *"'Remember what you saw here. The handprint. The mosaic. Remember that we were people, not just an empire.'"*
> *"'That is all I ask.'"*
> *"It fades, peacefully, like smoke dissolving in morning light."*

## NPC INTERACTIONS — SIGG
### Sigg's Role in Combat
Sigg will fight the Phantasm with his blaster, though he recognizes that his attacks are less effective than Force-based attacks.
**Sigg's Dialogue:**
> *"'I can't hurt it the way you can. But I can distract it. Do what you need to do.'"*
**If the party is struggling:**
> *"'It feeds on fear. On anger. On the darkness. Don't give it what it wants.'"*
**If the party defeats the Phantasm:**
> *"Sigg lowers his blaster. He looks at the spot where the Phantasm was."*
> *"'That was... that was someone who died here. Someone who couldn't leave. I don't know if that's worse than being alive.'"*

## SESSION NOTES
### Running This Encounter
- **Pace:** Fast, urgent, but with narrative weight
- **Tone:** Menacing, sorrowful, challenging
- **Player Engagement:** Combat should feel meaningful—the Phantasm is not just a monster, it's a victim
- **Narrative Importance:** The encounter reveals the dark side's influence and foreshadows what awaits in Sankar Palace
### Modifications
| Modification | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| **Increase Difficulty** | Add Adversary 2, add additional Phantasms |
| **Decrease Difficulty** | Reduce Adversary to 1, remove environmental effects |
| **Emphasize Roleplay** | The Phantasm is more talkative; combat can be avoided with good rolls |
---
## END OF ENCOUNTER
**Key Moment for GM:** The Phantasm is defeated or resolved. The party has faced the darkness of this place and emerged, for now. But the darkness is not gone. It is waiting.
**What the Party Carries:**
- The memory of the Phantasm's words
- The weight of the dark side's influence
- The knowledge that Warde was consumed by this same darkness
- The understanding that they must resist, or fall as he did
**What Awaits:** The deeper levels, the approach to Sankar Palace, the truth of what happened here.
---
*This encounter can be placed at any point in the deep descent—particularly in Levels 12, 13, 14, or just before entering Sankar Palace.*
