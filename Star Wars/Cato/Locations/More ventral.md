# THE DESCENT – A SANDBOX GUIDE TO JORRA'S VENTRAL UNDERCITY

*"The ventral city is not a place. It is a wound. A wound that never healed, a memory that never faded, a scream that never ended. The dorsal city lives; the ventral city remembers."*

---

## PART ONE – THE VENTRAL SOUL: A CITY INVERTED

The ventral undercity is Jorra's shadow self. It is what the dorsal city tries to forget – the cost of war, the weight of history, the truth of what was lost. The dorsal city is occupied; the ventral city is abandoned. The dorsal city dreams of prosperity; the ventral city mourns it.

The ventral city hangs upside‑down. Its towers descend from the bridge like stalactites, their foundations anchored in the bridge's superstructure. The streets are the undersides of walkways, and the "ground" is the roof of the structure below. The mist that perpetually fills the canyon is the breath of the dead. The ruins are their bones. The creatures that hunt here are the scavengers of memory.

**The Ventral Culture:** The undercity has its own culture – one of survival, memory, and desperation. Those who live here (few as they are) are the forgotten: smugglers, fugitives, outcasts, and those who prefer the silence of the dead to the noise of the living. They speak in whispers, move in shadows, and carry the weight of the war in their eyes. They have a saying: *"The dorsal city has forgotten us. We have not forgotten them."*

**The Ventral Pain:** Every street, every building, every shattered window is a reminder of the war that destroyed this half of the city. The dead are everywhere – skeletons in uniform, droids with rusted limbs, the ghosts of a thousand battles. The dark side of the Force lingers here, fed by decades of suffering.

**The Ventral Pride:** Despite everything, the ventral city is not dead. It is a refuge. It is a place where the Empire's reach is weak, where the Syndicate's control is thin, and where the forgotten can hide. It is a living graveyard – and graveyards, after all, are places of memory.

---

## PART TWO – THE VENTRAL DISTRICTS: A CITY OF MEMORY

The ventral undercity is divided into distinct districts, each with its own character, history, and hazards. These districts are not neatly separated – they bleed into each other, and the boundaries are often unclear. But each has a distinct identity.

---

### DISTRICT V1 – THE HANGING PROMENADE

*"The streets are empty, but the ghosts are not. They walk where they once sold spices, haggled over prices, and dreamed of profit. Now they sell nothing. They have nothing left."*

**Sister District:** Commercial District (Dorsal)  
**Vibe:** Shattered, silent, mournful  
**Colour Palette:** Grey, rust, fog-white, faded blue  
**Primary Hazards:** Unstable walkways, Rusted Stalkers, Pale Spider webs

---

#### The District's Soul

The Hanging Promenade was once the commercial heart of the ventral city. It mirrored the commercial district above – shops, markets, and trading halls. The merchants here dealt in goods that were slightly less legal than those above, but the district was prosperous. Now it is a ghost town. The shops are empty, their wares long since looted or decayed. The walkways are cracked, some collapsed into the levels below. The mist drifts through broken windows, and the silence is broken only by the drip of water and the distant howl of a Stalker.

**The Pain:** The Promenade is a testament to the fragility of prosperity. The wealth that once flowed here is gone, and the merchants who lived here are dead or displaced.

**The Pride:** The Promenade refuses to die. Some signs still glow, their power cells fading but not yet extinguished. A few shops still have goods – rusted but recognisable. The architecture, though damaged, is still elegant. This was a place of beauty.

### DISTRICT V2 – THE COLLAPSED HABITATION DISTRICT

*"These were homes. People lived here. They raised children, cooked meals, dreamed of futures. Now the only dreams are the ones that haunt the sleeping."*

**Sister District:** Residential District (Dorsal)  
**Vibe:** Crushing, hollow, haunted  
**Colour Palette:** Brown, grey, faded green, dark shadows  
**Primary Hazards:** Unstable structures, Rusted Stalkers, feral Neimoidians, Jaraal's Estate

---

#### The District's Soul

The Collapsed Habitation District was once the ventral city's residential area. It mirrored the residential district above – apartments, community kitchens, and temples. It was the heart of the ventral community. The war destroyed it. The buildings are partially collapsed, their walls scarred by artillery. The streets are littered with debris. The only residents are the desperate – the feral, the hiding, and the dead.

But the district is not entirely empty. A few Neimoidians still live here, hiding from the Empire and the Syndicate. They have formed a small community, sharing food and shelter. They are suspicious of outsiders, but they know the district better than anyone.

**The Pain:** This district is a tomb. Every building holds the memory of a family, a life, a dream. The loss is overwhelming.

**The Pride:** The community that remains refuses to leave. They are the last guardians of their history. They keep the old traditions alive.

### DISTRICT V3 – THE MAINTENANCE CATACOMBS

*"The city has veins. They are dark, narrow, and full of secrets. Those who know them can go anywhere. Those who don't will never be found."*

**Sister District:** None (infrastructure)  
**Vibe:** Claustrophobic, damp, secretive  
**Colour Palette:** Rust, black, pale green (bioluminescence)  
**Primary Hazards:** Gas leaks, collapsed sections, Syndicate smugglers

---

#### The District's Soul

The Maintenance Catacombs are the circulatory system of Jorra. They are a network of tunnels, conduits, and crawlspaces that run through the bridge's superstructure. They are used by maintenance droids, but also by smugglers, fugitives, and the Syndicate. The tunnels are a labyrinth – it is easy to get lost, and even easier to disappear.

The Catacombs have their own culture. The smugglers who use them have developed a code – a system of chalk marks and whispers that guide the initiated. They trade in secrets, contraband, and safe passage. They are not friendly, but they are pragmatic.

**The Pain:** The Catacombs are a place of desperation. Those who live here have nowhere else to go.

**The Pride:** The Catacombs are the last refuge. They offer freedom to those who cannot survive in the light.

---

| **The Underground Market** | A secret trading post where illegal goods are bought and sold. | The price of survival. |

### DISTRICT V4 – THE INDUSTRIAL BONES

*"The factories never stopped. They still produce. But what they produce now is silence."*

**Sister District:** Factory District (Dorsal)  
**Vibe:** Decayed industry, mechanical carnage  
**Colour Palette:** Grey, orange (fire), rust, oil-black  
**Primary Hazards:** BX Commando Droids, Droidekas, unstable machinery

---

#### The District's Soul

The Industrial Bones are the wreckage of the ventral city's manufacturing sector. Once, these factories produced droids, weapons, and starship parts for the Trade Federation. Now they are hollow shells. The machinery is rusted, the conveyor belts are broken, and the assembly lines are silent. But not everything is dead. The Separatist droids that were never deactivated still patrol these halls. They are remnants of the war, following orders from a regime that no longer exists.

**The Pain:** The Industrial Bones are a monument to the futility of war. The droids that were built to fight now fight nothing. The factories that were built to produce now produce nothing.

**The Pride:** The Industrial Bones are a graveyard of machines. The droids that remain are terrifying, but they are also a testament to Neimoidian engineering. These were the best droids the Confederacy ever built.

---

#### Points of Interest

| Location | Description | Emotional Note |
|----------|-------------|----------------|

| **The Droideka Watch** | A Droideka guarding the entrance to Sankar Palace. | A final sentinel. |

| **The Control Room** | A room where the droids' programming was once managed. Now it is a nest of cables and sparking terminals. | The ghost of command. |

### DISTRICT V5 – THE PALACE REMNANT (SANKAR PALACE)

*"This is where it ended. This is where he fell. This is where the light died."*

**Sister District:** Palace District (Dorsal)  
**Vibe:** Dark side resonance, tragedy, gravity  
**Colour Palette:** Red stone, black shadows, pale grey  
**Primary Hazards:** Dark side influence, Droideka, structural collapse

---

#### The District's Soul

The Palace Remnant is the lowest level of Sankar Palace, the site of Warde's fall. It is a place of immense tragedy – the air is cold, the Force is heavy, and the walls seem to hold the memory of screams. The meeting hall where the delegates were murdered is a tomb. Jiv Durael's body still lies on the balcony, clutching the kyber crystal.

**The Pain:** This is the heart of Warde's betrayal. The loss here is personal – not just of lives, but of the trust and hope that once existed.

**The Pride:** The Palace Remnant is a monument to the truth. The evidence of Warde's fall is here for anyone who can bear to see it.

---

### DISTRICT V6 – THE MIST-FISHER'S WALK

*"The mist is alive. It breathes, it watches, it hungers. Those who walk here must be careful – the mist remembers."*

**Sister District:** None (edge of the city)  
**Vibe:** Mysterious, dangerous, hidden  
**Colour Palette:** White, pale blue, dark grey  
**Primary Hazards:** Mist Lurkers, Fog Rays (schools), sheer drops

---

#### The District's Soul

The Mist-Fisher's Walk is the lowest level of the ventral city – a series of precarious walkways and platforms that hang just above the fog layer. It is rarely visited, even by the desperate. The mist here is thick, and the creatures that hunt in it are dangerous. But it is also a place of beauty – the Fog Rays are graceful, and the view of the canyon below is awe‑inspiring.

**The Pain:** The Mist-Fisher's Walk is a place of isolation. Those who come here are truly alone.

**The Pride:** It is the most beautiful district in Jorra. In a city of death, this is a place of life – strange, alien life, but life nonetheless.

### DISTRICT V7 – THE TEMPLE OF THE FALLEN

*"This place was old before the city was built. It remembers things that the city has forgotten."*

**Sister District:** Cultural District (Dorsal) – but this predates it  
**Vibe:** Ancient, dark, mysterious  
**Colour Palette:** Red stone, black, faint gold  
**Primary Hazards:** Dark side resonance, traps, Pale Spiders

---

#### The District's Soul

The Temple of the Fallen is a pre-Republic structure – a Sith temple or a shrine to an ancient power. It is older than Jorra, older than the Trade Federation, older than the Republic itself. The Neimoidians built their bridge-city around it, and then they forgot it. Now it is a crumbling ruin, its halls filled with dust and the ghosts of a forgotten age.

**The Pain:** The temple is a reminder that Jorra was built on a foundation of ancient darkness. The sins of the past are never truly buried.

**The Pride:** The temple is a testament to the resilience of memory. Even after millennia, it still stands.

---

#### Points of Interest

| **The Echo Chamber** | A room where whispers from the past can still be heard. | The weight of history. |
