
# VENTRAL UNDERCITY – SUB-LOCATION DETAILS

This section expands the **ventral undercity** into distinct sub-locations, detailing the journey from the dorsal city to Sankar Palace. Each entry includes living world atmosphere, NPC/creature activity, tactical decisions, GM notes, and the storylines/events that unfold there.

---

## A. ACCESS POINTS – CROSSING TO THE UNDERSIDE

**Type:** Transition zones / entry points  
**Location:** Junctions where the dorsal city meets the ventral undercity

---

### Living World Description

There are three primary ways to reach the ventral undercity, each with its own character and risks. The journey itself is a psychological threshold – crossing from the relative safety of the occupied city into the forgotten ruins below.

**Maintenance Tunnel Route:** A network of narrow, dark passages lined with pipes and cables. The air is stale and warm, thick with the smell of grease and rust. Utility droids and maintenance crews occasionally pass through, but the deeper tunnels are abandoned.

**Spaceport Access:** A series of cargo lifts and gantries that descend from the docking bays. This route is controlled by port security – but it is the most direct path for those with the right clearance.

**Climbing Route:** The exterior of the bridge itself. This is the most dangerous option – a sheer drop into the mist, with only handholds and the occasional cable to support the climber. It is also the most discreet.

---

### What NPCs/Creatures Do Here

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Maintenance Droids** | Perform routine repairs | Ignore anyone who looks like they belong. |
| **Port Security** (Spaceport) | Guard the lifts, check credentials | They are alert but can be bribed or deceived. |
| **Treveel** | Burrow through the tunnels | Can be used as guides. |
| **Stone Hopper** | Scurry through vents and small gaps | Can scout ahead. |
| **Scavenger Drone‑Beetle** | Common in maintenance shafts | Ignored by security. |

---

### Tactical Decisions

- **Choosing a Route:** Each route has trade‑offs. Tunnels are slow but stealthy. Spaceport is fast but risky. Climbing is dangerous but completely undetected.
- **Bribing Guards:** Port security can be bribed (100–200 credits) or persuaded with a successful Deception check.
- **Using Animals:** A Treveel can guide the party through the tunnel network, finding the safest path. A Bridge Gecko can scout ahead.
- **Cover Story:** If using the spaceport, the PCs should have a plausible reason for descending to the lower levels (e.g., "inspecting cargo containers").

---

### GM Notes

- **Atmosphere:** Emphasise the transition – the light fading, the sounds of the city receding, the cold creeping in.
- **Skill Checks:** Navigating the tunnels requires Average Survival or Mechanics checks. Climbing requires Hard Athletics checks.
- **Discovery:** The PCs may find old Republic or Separatist graffiti on the walls – a reminder of the war that destroyed this place.

---

### Storylines & Events at Access Points

- **Crossing the Threshold:** The PCs' decision on how to descend sets the tone for the entire ventral exploration.
- **Optional Encounter:** If using the tunnels, the PCs may encounter a feral Rusted Stalker – not aggressive if the PCs are calm, but a sign of the dangers below.
- **Hasar's Watch:** If Hasar's people are tracking the PCs, they may be spotted at the access point. Syndicate spies might follow.

---

## B. THE ABANDONED PROMENADE

**Type:** Mid‑level ruin / urban decay  
**Location:** Just below the bridge's primary superstructure, the first layer of ventral buildings

---

### Living World Description

The Abandoned Promenade was once a bustling commercial district, a mirror of the dorsal city's marketplaces. Now it is a ghost town – empty shops, collapsed walkways, and the skeletal remains of signs that once advertised Trade Federation goods. The mist drifts through broken windows, and the silence is broken only by the drip of water and the occasional howl of a feral creature.

**Atmosphere:** Eerie, melancholic, and haunting. The PCs are walking through a graveyard of prosperity.

---

### What NPCs/Creatures Do Here

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Rusted Stalkers** | Hunt for prey, patrol their territory | Dangerous, but can be bonded. |
| **Tension Cable Bats** | Roost in collapsed buildings | Provide echolocation maps. |
| **Neimoidian Nightcrawlers** | Swarm in the darkest corners | Emotional sensors. |
| **Pale Spiders** | Spin webs in doorways | Invisible tripwires. |
| **Arch Crabs** | Scavenge in the rubble | Can clear debris, sense structural stability. |

---

### Tactical Decisions

- **Finding a Safe Path:** The Promenade is littered with unstable floors and collapsed walkways. The PCs should proceed cautiously, using Coordination and Athletics checks.
- **Using a Rusted Stalker:** If the PCs have bonded with a stalker, it can guide them through the safest routes.
- **Scouting Ahead:** A Stone Hopper or Tension Cable Bat can scout ahead, identifying hazards and potential ambushes.
- **Avoiding the Mist:** The mist in the Promenade is thick. The PCs should use glow rods or the Force to navigate.

---

### GM Notes

- **Random Encounters:** The GM should roll for encounters – a pack of stalkers, a swarm of nightcrawlers, or a structural collapse.
- **Clues of the Past:** The PCs may find old Republic or Separatist equipment, datapads, or graffiti that provide context for Warde's story.
- **Atmosphere:** This is where the war's devastation is most visible. The GM should describe the wreckage in detail – shattered droids, scorched walls, and the bones of the fallen.

---

### Storylines & Events at the Abandoned Promenade

- **The Weight of History:** The PCs see firsthand the cost of the war – and Warde's role in it.
- **Optional Encounter – Rusted Stalker Pack:** A pack of stalkers may attack if the PCs enter their territory. Defeating them or driving them off earns respect.
- **Lost Treasure:** The PCs may find a cache of old Trade Federation credits or equipment. If they return it to a citizen (e.g., Kuto Jaraal's lost treasures), they earn trust.

---

## C. THE COLLAPSED BRIDGE / CHASM CROSSING

**Type:** Natural / structural hazard  
**Location:** A major gap in the ventral walkways, where the bridge infrastructure has given way

---

### Living World Description

The Collapsed Bridge is a yawning chasm where the original ventral walkways have crumbled into the mist below. A few twisted metal beams and a single, swaying cable bridge offer the only crossing. The mist swirls below, obscuring the bottom, and the sound of dripping water echoes from the depths. The bridge groans and sways with every step.

**Atmosphere:** Tense, vertiginous, and dangerous. The crossing is the most exposed point of the journey.

---

### What NPCs/Creatures Do Here

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Mist Lurkers** | Hunt in the fog below | They may attack from the mist. |
| **Tension Cable Bats** | Roost under the remaining supports | Can be used as scouts. |
| **Fog Rays** | School in the mist below | Can create concealment. |

---

### Tactical Decisions

- **Crossing Methods:**
  1. **The Rope Bridge:** Swaying, unstable. Requires Average Coordination checks. Failure may cause a fall.
  2. **Climbing Around:** Using the remaining support beams. Requires Hard Athletics checks.
  3. **Using the Force:** Telekinesis or leaping across.
  4. **Using Animals:** A Canyon Manta or Fog Ray could carry the PCs across.
- **Securing the Crossing:** The PCs can anchor ropes to provide safety for the group.
- **Dealing with Mist Lurkers:** If the PCs are attacked, they must defend themselves while crossing. Losing balance is a significant risk.

---

### GM Notes

- **Danger:** Falling is a real threat. The GM should allow the PCs to use Destiny Points to catch handholds or land on lower ledges.
- **Mist Lurker Ambush:** If the PCs generate Despair or Threat on their crossing checks, a Mist Lurker may attack from below.
- **Atmosphere:** The crossing is a moment of high tension. Describe the groaning of the bridge, the swirling mist, and the distant howls.

---

### Storylines & Events at the Collapsed Bridge

- **The Crossing:** A skill challenge that tests the PCs' coordination and courage.
- **Optional Encounter – Mist Lurker Attack:** The creature attacks the PCs as they cross, trying to drag them into the mist.
- **Moral Decision:** If a PC falls, others must decide whether to risk the crossing to save them.

---

## D. THE HANGING TOWER

**Type:** Ruined building / vertical obstacle  
**Location:** A tall, partially collapsed tower that must be traversed to reach Sankar Palace

---

### Living World Description

The Hanging Tower is one of the ventral city's tallest structures – a spire that once housed Trade Federation officials and wealthy merchants. Now it is a ruin, leaning at a dangerous angle, its walls peppered with blast holes. The interior is a maze of collapsed floors, exposed rebar, and dangling cables. The wind howls through its broken windows, and the structure groans with every shift.

**Atmosphere:** Tense and claustrophobic. The tower feels alive, as if it could collapse at any moment.

---

### What NPCs/Creatures Do Here

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Kreehawks** | Nest near the top | May attack intruders. |
| **Arch Serpents** | Nest in cable conduits | Vibration scouts. |
| **Stone Hoppers** | Scurry through vents | Can be used to scout ahead. |
| **Pale Spiders** | Spin webs on stairwells | Invisible traps. |

---

### Tactical Decisions

- **Ascending:** The PCs must climb from floor to floor, navigating collapsed sections and unstable stairs. Coordination and Athletics checks are frequent.
- **Avoiding Collapse:** The tower is unstable. The PCs should avoid making loud noises or using explosives.
- **Dealing with Kreehawks:** If the PCs disturb a nest, the birds may attack. They are aggressive but not excessively dangerous.
- **Shortcuts:** The PCs may use the exterior of the tower (climbing) or the Force to bypass interior hazards.

---

### GM Notes

- **Environmental Hazards:** The tower's instability is a constant threat. The GM should impose setbacks on combat checks due to the shifting floor.
- **Falling Debris:** If the PCs or enemies collapse a floor, everyone nearby must make an Average Coordination check or fall.
- **Atmosphere:** Describe the creaking metal, the falling dust, and the distant howl of the wind.

---

### Storylines & Events at the Hanging Tower

- **The Climb:** A vertical challenge that tests the PCs' physical and mental endurance.
- **Optional Encounter – Kreehawk Nest:** If the PCs disturb the birds, they attack. Defeating them or avoiding them earns respect.
- **Clue:** The PCs may find an old Republic soldier's identification tag – a reminder of the clone troopers who died here.

---

## E. THE DROIDS' NEST

**Type:** Combat zone / hazardous area  
**Location:** A wide, open chamber or corridor complex near the palace, where the BX Commando Droids and Droidekas are concentrated

---

### Living World Description

The Droids' Nest is a large, open space – perhaps a former hangar or assembly hall – where the Separatist droids have gathered. They are old, their paint scratched and rusted, but they are still functional. They patrol in a predictable pattern, their red optical sensors scanning for movement. The chamber is littered with the wreckage of their fallen comrades, creating a maze of cover.

**Atmosphere:** Tense and dangerous. The droids are ancient, but they are still lethal. The air is cold and still, and the only sound is the steady, rhythmic clanking of their patrol steps.

---

### What NPCs/Creatures Do Here

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **BX Commando Droids** | Patrol, guard, ambush | Minion groups of 3. |
| **Droidekas (Destroyer Droids)** | Guard key access points | Rivals. High threat. |
| **Arch Crabs** | Scavenge in the wreckage | Can be used to create cover or distractions. |

---

### Tactical Decisions

- **Stealth:** The droids have set patrol routes. The PCs can observe their patterns and slip through, rather than fighting.
- **Ambush:** If combat is inevitable, the PCs can use the wreckage for cover and set up an ambush.
- **Ion Weapons:** The droids are vulnerable to ion damage. Any PC with ion weapons or Disruptor settings should use them.
- **Lightsabers:** Lightsabers are effective but the droids are numerous. The PCs should be careful not to be overwhelmed.
- **Using Animals:** An Arch Crab's shell can provide cover. A Mist Eel could disable a Droideka's shield generator.

---

### GM Notes

- **Wave Combat:** The droids attack in waves – first the commandos, then the droidekas. The GM should manage the pacing to avoid overwhelming the PCs.
- **Environmental Hazards:** The wreckage can be used as cover, but it can also explode or collapse.
- **Awareness:** The droids are on a low‑power state. They will not pursue the PCs beyond their designated patrol zone.

---

### Storylines & Events at the Droids' Nest

- **Sleeping Dangers:** The primary combat encounter of the ventral journey. The PCs must defeat or bypass the droids.
- **Legacy of Disgrace:** The droids are a remnant of the Clone Wars – a physical embodiment of the conflict that Warde helped perpetuate.
- **Destruction:** If the PCs destroy the droids, they clear a path – but the noise may attract other dangers.

---

## F. THE PALACE APPROACH / GATE TO SANKAR

**Type:** Transition / threshold  
**Location:** The entrance to Sankar Palace, just before the meeting hall

---

### Living World Description

The Palace Approach is a wide, colonnaded entrance, once grand and imposing, now crumbling and defaced. Massive statues of Trade Federation officials lie toppled and broken. The doors are heavy, carved with scenes of Neimoidian commerce and conquest. The air here is colder, and a palpable sense of foreboding hangs over the entrance – the presence of the dark side is faint, but discernible.

**Atmosphere:** Foreboding, heavy, and charged with the memory of Warde's betrayal.

---

### What NPCs/Creatures Do Here

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Pale Spiders** | Spin webs on the statues | Invisible tripwires. |
| **Arch Serpents** | Nest in the pillars | Vibration sense. |
| **Droidekas** | May guard the entrance | If not already dealt with. |

---

### Tactical Decisions

- **Approach:** The PCs should proceed cautiously, checking for traps (Pale Spider webs) and sentries.
- **Entering:** The doors are heavy but can be opened with a Hard Athletics check or by slicing a control panel.
- **Using the Force:** The PCs may sense the dark side presence within. This is a moment of decision – do they proceed, or hesitate?

---

### GM Notes

- **Atmosphere:** The approach is a moment of reflection – the PCs are about to enter the site of Warde's fall.
- **Clues:** The PCs may find evidence of old battles – blaster marks, lightsaber cuts, and the remains of both droids and clones.
- **The Dark Side:** The GM should describe the subtle feeling of dread, the flickering of shadows, and the whispers of the past.

---

### Storylines & Events at the Palace Approach

- **The Threshold:** The PCs pass into the palace, crossing from the ventral ruins into the site of Warde's betrayal.
- **Legacy of Disgrace:** The approach is the final leg of the journey before the meeting hall.
- **Optional Encounter – Pale Spider Webs:** The spiders have spun webs across the entrance. A PC may be caught, causing a delay.

---

## G. SANKAR PALACE – MEETING HALL (THE RESTLESS GRAVE)

**Type:** Climax location  
**Location:** The lowest level of Sankar Palace, the site of Warde's attack on the Separatist delegates

---

### Living World Description

The meeting hall is a large, tiered auditorium, its balconies and tables now scarred and ruined. The great table of Kelethjohan oak sits in the centre, slashed and burned. Chairs are overturned. Skeletal corpses of Neimoidian delegates still sit at the table, their necks cleanly severed. Other bodies lie nearby – clones, droids, and the fallen Jedi Jiv Durael.

The air here is cold, and the Force is heavy. The presence of Warde's betrayal lingers – a palpable sense of anger, despair, and tragedy. The PCs can feel it, even if they are not Force‑sensitive.

**Atmosphere:** Sombre, tragic, and charged with the dark side. This is the heart of Warde's fall.

---

### What NPCs/Creatures Do Here

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Jiv Durael's Corpse** | Holds the kyber crystal | A dead Jedi, still clutching the crystal. |
| **None (living)** | The hall is abandoned | The droids outside are the only guardians. |

---

### Tactical Decisions

- **Exploration:** The PCs should search the hall thoroughly. The kyber crystal is in Durael's hand.
- **The Crystal:** Retrieving the crystal is a simple action – but the weight of the place may cause a moral or emotional reaction.
- **Avoiding the Dark Side:** The dark side lingers here. The GM may call for Hard Discipline checks to resist its influence (fear, anger, or despair).
- **Taking a Moment:** The PCs may wish to pay respects to the fallen Jedi or examine the evidence of Warde's actions.

---

### GM Notes

- **Atmosphere:** The GM should describe the scene with gravity – the slashed table, the skeletal diplomats, the fallen Jedi. This is a place of tragedy.
- **Kyber Crystal:** The crystal is the key. When the PCs take it, the gatekeeper gains Warde's final memories and reveals Moraband.
- **Morality:** The PCs may be tempted to take something from the dead (the lightsaber hilt, credits, etc.). This may earn minor Conflict.
- **The Gatekeeper's Response:** When the PCs activate the holocron with the crystal, the gatekeeper is sorrowful but resolute, urging the PCs to continue to Moraband.

---

### Storylines & Events at the Meeting Hall

- **Legacy of Disgrace (Climax):** The PCs discover the full truth of Warde's fall and retrieve the second kyber crystal.
- **The Restless Grave:** The hall is a tomb of Warde's betrayal. The PCs witness the aftermath of his actions.
- **Moral Choice:** The PCs must decide how to respond to what they find. Do they honour the dead, or do they see Warde as a tragic figure?
- **Jiv Durael's Lightsaber Hilt:** If recovered, it contains an unmodified Dragite crystal – a valuable lightsaber component.

---

## H. THE PALACE'S UPPER LEVELS (EXPLORATION)

**Type:** Optional exploration area  
**Location:** The upper levels of Sankar Palace, above the meeting hall

---

### Living World Description

The upper levels of Sankar Palace are less damaged than the lowest floors, though they still bear the scars of the Clone Wars. They include offices, living quarters, and storage rooms. The PCs may find personal belongings of the delegates, Republic equipment, and clues about the events that led to Warde's attack.

**Atmosphere:** Quiet, dusty, and abandoned. The rooms are frozen in time, as if their occupants simply vanished.

---

### What NPCs/Creatures Do Here

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Treveel** | May have burrowed into the walls | Can be used as a guide. |
| **Stone Hopper** | Scurries through empty rooms | Can scout. |
| **Pale Spider** | May have spun webs in doorways | Traps. |

---

### Tactical Decisions

- **Searching:** The PCs can search for supplies, clues, or additional equipment.
- **Finding Records:** The PCs may find datapads with information about Warde's activities or the Separatist delegation.
- **Avoiding Collapse:** The upper levels are more stable, but still dangerous.

---

### GM Notes

- **Optional Content:** This area is not necessary for the main plot, but it rewards exploration with flavour and minor loot.
- **Clues:** The PCs may find personal effects of the delegates – holopics, letters, or journals that humanise the tragedy.
- **Loot:** The PCs may find a few credits, medical supplies, or grenades.

---

### Storylines & Events at the Palace's Upper Levels

- **Optional Exploration:** The PCs may choose to search for additional clues or resources.
- **Clues to Warde's Mind:** The PCs may find evidence of Warde's increasing paranoia – private journals, arrest records, etc.
- **Honouring the Dead:** The PCs may find the remains of fallen soldiers or civilians, and choose to give them a proper burial.
