# ACT 2 SUB-LOCATION GUIDE – ALL MAJOR AREAS

This guide covers all major locations in Jorra that are large enough to contain sub-locations, including detailed living world information, tactical notes, GM advice, creature/NPC placement, and a breakdown of which storylines and events occur there and how they affect the adventure.

---

## 1. THE SPACEPORT – SUB-LOCATIONS

**Type:** Arrival point / security checkpoint  
**Location:** Dorsal side of the bridge, near the commercial district  
**Overall Atmosphere:** Bustling, tense, heavily monitored. The air smells of ozone and recycled oxygen. Droids and sentients move with purpose.

---

### 1A. DOCKING BAYS

**Description:** A series of hangars built into the bridge structure. Each bay can accommodate a medium freighter. Cargo containers, fuel lines, and maintenance equipment line the walls.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Docking Staff | Manage bay assignments, collect fees | 50 credits/day fee. |
| Customs Officers | Inspect ships and cargo | Suspicious of outsiders. |
| Cargo Droids | Load/unload freight | Ignored by security. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Ship Security:** Leaving the ship unattended is risky – the Syndicate may plant tracking devices.
- **Contraband:** The PCs should hide illegal items before docking.
- **Bribery:** A small bribe (100–200 credits) can smooth over "misunderstandings."

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Arrival:** The PCs' first impression of Jorra.
- **This Looks Suspicious:** Optional security encounter. Failure may lead to detention or impound.
- **The Silver Star Connection:** Iribian ordered the attack on the Silver Star. If the PCs cause trouble, he may connect them.

---

### 1B. PASSENGER TERMINAL

**Description:** A cavernous hall of transparisteel and durasteel. Shops, cafes, and information kiosks line the walls. Travellers, traders, and officials move in a constant flow.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Customs Officers | Check IDs, scan luggage | Can be bribed or deceived. |
| Stormtroopers | Patrol the terminal | Will not bother PCs unless flagged. |
| Informants | Watch arrivals and report | May spot the PCs and report to Syndicate. |
| Merchants | Sell goods, offer services | Potential sources of information. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Blending In:** The PCs should dress appropriately and avoid drawing attention.
- **Information Gathering:** Merchants and travellers may have useful rumours.
- **Avoiding Attention:** The PCs should not openly discuss Force-sensitivity.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **This Looks Suspicious:** The primary security encounter.
- **Unwanted Attention:** If the PCs are identified as Force-users, they may be followed.

---

### 1C. CARGO BAYS & MAINTENANCE TUNNELS

**Description:** The underbelly of the spaceport – a maze of cargo holds, conveyor belts, and maintenance passages. It is dark, noisy, and largely ignored by security. This is the smuggler's route.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Cargo Droids | Move freight | Ignore anyone who looks like they belong. |
| Maintenance Techs | Repair equipment | May be bribed for access. |
| Treveel | Burrow through tunnels | Can be used as guides. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Smuggling Route:** The tunnels are a discreet way to enter the city without passing through security.
- **Treveel Guide:** A bonded treveel can navigate the tunnels safely.
- **Bribery:** A tech can be bribed (50–100 credits) for access or information.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Alternative Entry:** The PCs may bypass security entirely using the tunnels.
- **Syndicate Activity:** The Syndicate uses these tunnels for smuggling – the PCs may encounter Syndicate operatives.

## 2. THE DUCAL PALACE – SUB-LOCATIONS

**Type:** Imperial seat of power / military barracks  
**Location:** Southeastern tip of the bridge, palace district  
**Overall Atmosphere:** Stark, cold, and imposing. The palace is a fortress draped in Imperial banners. Paranoia radiates from every corridor.

---

### 2A. AUDIENCE CHAMBER

**Description:** A large, high-ceilinged hall where Duke Iribian holds court. Imperial banners hang from the walls. A raised dais supports a throne of durasteel and black marble. The air is cold and still.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Duke Iribian | Receives reports, issues orders | Rarely leaves his private chambers. |
| Advisors | Present intelligence | Iribian's network of informants. |
| Stormtroopers | Guard the entrances | Alert and professional. |
| TH-313 "Tacker" | May be present for briefings | If the PCs meet him here, caution is required. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Social Approach:** The PCs can request an audience if they have a legitimate cover story.
- **Observation:** The PCs can learn about Iribian's habits and priorities by observing.
- **Avoiding Attention:** The PCs should not openly display Force-sensitivity.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Duke Iribian:** The PCs may encounter him if captured or if they arrange a meeting.
- **TH-313 "Tacker":** A key ally. Tacker can provide information about Warde's fall and Sankar Palace.
- **Dalan Oberos:** If active, he may be summoned here.

---

### 2B. IMPERIAL BARRACKS

**Description:** Spartan, functional living quarters and training areas for the stormtroopers. The barracks are always busy with the sound of marching boots and shouted orders. The air smells of sweat, metal, and cleaning chemicals.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Stormtroopers | Drill, eat, sleep, patrol | Professional and disciplined. |
| TH-313 "Tacker" | Oversees training | He is a visible but weary figure. |
| Sergeants | Lead squads | Follow orders without question. |
| Scavenger Drone-Beetles | Maintain equipment | Ignored by all. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Infiltration:** The barracks are heavily guarded. A solid plan (disguises, bribes, or slicing) is required.
- **Tacker's Aid:** If the PCs win Tacker's trust, he may grant access or warn of danger.
- **Sabotage:** The PCs could sabotage equipment to cause delays or confusion.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Tacker's Loyalty:** The PCs may meet him here and earn his trust.
- **Imperial Valor:** If the PCs fight Iribian, he uses his Imperial Valor talent to sacrifice stormtroopers.

---

### 2C. PRIVATE QUARTERS / IRIBIAN'S CHAMBERS

**Description:** Iribian's personal quarters – opulently furnished with Coruscanti art, fine silks, and a holopic of his estranged wife. The room is a mask of sophistication hiding a desperate ambition.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Duke Iribian | Sleeps, plans, reviews secret documents | He is most vulnerable here. |
| Security Systems | Holocams, lock codes | Can be bypassed with slicing. |
| Personal Servants | Attend to Iribian's needs | They are loyal but can be bribed. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Infiltration:** Breaking into Iribian's chambers requires careful planning (slicing, stealth, or deception).
- **Intelligence:** Iribian's private datapad may contain secrets about the Silver Star incident and his hidden accounts.
- **Assassination:** Killing Iribian is a significant act – but it has major consequences.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Silver Star Records:** Iribian's datapad contains the truth about the bounty hunters he hired.
- **Personal Secrets:** The holopic of his wife is a vulnerability – it shows he is not entirely cold.

---

### 2D. THE PRISON WING (ADJACENT TO PALACE)

**Description:** A small detention block within the palace complex, used for holding prisoners before transfer to Southside Prison. Cells are bare durasteel, cold, and cramped.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Imperial Guards | Manage prisoners | Professional but not necessarily cruel. |
| Prisoners | Awaiting transfer or execution | May include political prisoners. |
| TH-313 "Tacker" | May inspect the wing | He may be a source of aid. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Break-Out:** Freeing a prisoner here is possible but risky.
- **Tacker's Aid:** Tacker may be able to grant access or information.
- **Intelligence:** Prisoners may have knowledge of Warde or the Syndicate.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Reunion:** Sanna Olpan's son Gren may be held here (or in Southside Prison).
- **Political Prisoners:** A prisoner with knowledge of Moraband's coordinates could be here (though the main solution is Delriss).

## 3. THE DROPVIEW CANTINA – SUB-LOCATIONS

**Type:** Cantina / neutral meeting ground / Syndicate hub  
**Location:** Edge of the bridge, accessible from the docks  
**Overall Atmosphere:** Dim, smoky, and tense. The floor-to-ceiling windows offer a spectacular view of the drop. Clone Wars memorabilia covers the walls.

---

### 3A. MAIN BAR

**Description:** A long, polished bar dominates the room. Bottles of spirits line the shelves behind it. Patrons sit on stools or at small tables, drinking and talking in low voices. The air smells of alcohol and smoke.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Kelo Poy (Barkeep) | Serves drinks, watches | He reports to Hasar. |
| Crimson Kreehawks | Drink, brag, recruit | A visible presence. |
| Spacers / Traders | Exchange news, trade goods | Potential sources of information. |
| Informants | Listen and report | Some work for the Syndicate. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Information Gathering:** The cantina is an excellent place to hear rumours.
- **Surveillance:** The PCs should assume they are being watched – the cantina is under Syndicate control.
- **Fight or Flee:** If a fight breaks out, the narrow windows and open balcony create environmental hazards.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Meeting Onrein Hasar:** The PCs may arrange a meeting here or encounter her unexpectedly.
- **War Stories:** The cantina may be a place to hear rumours about Warde's past.
- **Syndicate Activity:** The PCs may overhear Syndicate plans.

---

### 3B. PRIVATE BOOTHS

**Description:** Secluded booths at the back of the cantina, away from the main bar. They are used for private meetings and deals. Soundproofing is minimal – a good ear can still overhear.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Onrein Hasar | Occasionally observes from a booth | She may appear if the PCs attract her interest. |
| Syndicate Operatives | Conduct business | Meetings here are often secretive. |
| Spacers | Discuss sensitive deals | Could be eavesdropped on. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Private Meeting:** The PCs can request a booth for a discreet conversation.
- **Eavesdropping:** A successful Perception check can overhear nearby conversations.
- **Observation:** The booths offer good sightlines but also make the PCs visible to the bar.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Meeting Hasar:** A private meeting can be arranged here.
- **Syndicate Secrets:** The PCs may overhear Syndicate plans or intelligence.

---

### 3C. THE BALCONY

**Description:** An open-air balcony outside the cantina, offering a stunning view of the mist below. The drop is hundreds of metres. A low railing is the only barrier.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Patrons | Smoke, talk, admire the view | Some may be Syndicate operatives. |
| Bridge Geckos | Cling to the exterior walls | Inconspicuous surveillance. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Private Conversation:** The balcony offers a place to talk without being overheard – but falling is a real risk.
- **Environmental Hazard:** Combat near the balcony can result in a fall.
- **Escape:** The balcony offers a potential escape route – if the PCs can climb or fly.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Threats:** The drop can be used as a threat during interrogations.
- **Dramatic Exits:** A PC or NPC may be pushed or fall.

## 5. THE RESIDENTIAL DISTRICT – SUB-LOCATIONS

**Type:** Civilian housing / impoverished community  
**Location:** Dorsal city  
**Overall Atmosphere:** Crowded, desperate, and worn down. Apartments are cramped; streets are narrow. The sound of children playing and the smell of cooking food fill the air.

---

### 5A. APARTMENT BLOCKS

**Description:** Tall, cramped buildings housing the city's poorer citizens. The architecture is functional, with some war damage still visible. Each block houses dozens of families.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Citizens | Live, work, socialise | Many are willing to help if trust is earned. |
| Syndicate Informants | Watch and report | Some residents report to the Syndicate. |
| Stone Hoppers | Scurry through ventilation shafts | Can be used for scouting. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Laying Low:** The residential district offers anonymity – but the PCs should be careful not to draw attention.
- **Information Gathering:** Citizens are more likely to share information if the PCs have helped them.
- **Safe House:** A citizen (like Jyl Korlis on Arbooine) may offer lodging.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Healing the Wounds:** The district is the setting for many tasks – Spice Problems, Medical Aid, and Reunion.
- **Unwelcome Visitors:** If the PCs are identified as Force-users, residents may become hostile.
- **An Old Debt:** Leads to Tiela Dalken's apartment.

---

### 5B. TIELA DALKEN'S APARTMENT

| Entity | Activity | Notes |

**Tactical Decisions:**

## 6. THE FACTORY DISTRICT – SUB-LOCATIONS

**Type:** Industrial zone / workplace  
**Location:** Dorsal city  
**Overall Atmosphere:** Hot, loud, and industrial. The air smells of molten metal and ozone. Smoke and sparks pour from chimneys.

---

### 6A. FACTORY DE-13

**Description:** A towering, smoke-belching factory complex. Pipes, chimneys, and gantries cover the exterior. Inside, the factory floor is a maze of conveyor belts, smelting tanks, and assembler droids. The noise is deafening.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Tor Haido | Supervisor, works in his office | He is evasive and fearful. |
| Factory Workers | Operate machinery, assemble parts | Too busy to pay attention to the PCs. |
| Administrative Droid | Manages front desk | May report suspicious visitors. |
| Scavenger Drone-Beetles | Eat rust and oxidation | Everywhere. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Approach:** The PCs can enter through the front door (risking attention) or the loading docks (more discreet).
- **Meeting Haido:** He is in his office, accessible by gantries.
- **Combat Environment:** If the Crimson Kreehawks attack, the factory becomes a dangerous battlefield with moving machinery, vats of chemicals, and collapsing walkways.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Tor Haido (War Stories):** The PCs meet Haido, who leads them to Zel N'got.
- **Gang Attack!:** Lor Kelin and her Kreehawks attack if the PCs were followed.
- **Unwanted Attention:** Defeating the Kreehawks earns a reputation as dangerous fighters.

---

### 6B. WORKER HOUSING

**Description:** Cramped, basic housing blocks near the factories. Workers and their families live here. The conditions are poor, but there is a sense of community.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Factory Workers | Live, sleep, eat | Potential allies if the PCs help them. |
| Families | Raise children, survive | Many are struggling. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Laying Low:** Worker housing offers anonymity but less comfort.
- **Information:** Workers may know about Syndicate activity or factory secrets.
- **Safety:** Worker housing is often targeted by the Syndicate for "protection" payments.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Healing the Wounds:** The district is the setting for tasks like Medical Aid.
- **Reunion:** Bein Kreiwic may live here.

---

### 6C. LOADING DOCKS

**Description:** Areas where goods are loaded and unloaded. They are busy, noisy, and largely unregulated – perfect for smuggling or discreet meetings.

| Entity | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Cargo Handlers | Load and unload goods | Too busy to notice the PCs. |
| Syndicate Operatives | Conduct illegal shipments | The Syndicate uses these docks. |
| Scavenger Drone-Beetles | Eat rust and oxidation | Everywhere. |

**Tactical Decisions:**
- **Discreet Entry:** The loading docks are a good way to enter a factory without going through the front door.
- **Smuggling:** The PCs could use the docks to move illegal goods or themselves.
- **Observation:** The docks are a good place to observe Syndicate activity.

**Storylines & Events:**
- **Gang Attack!:** The Kreehawks may attack through the loading docks.
- **Syndicate Activity:** The PCs may witness a syndicate operation.
