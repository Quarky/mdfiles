# 1. CATO NEIMOIDIA – PLANETARY OVERVIEW

**Astronavigation Data:** Cato Neimoidia system, Colonies region  
**Orbital Metrics:** 278 days per year / 25 hours per day  
**Government:** Federation under Imperial control  
**Population:** 8.5 million (Neimoidian 99.9%)  
**Languages:** Pak Pak, Basic  
**Terrain:** Temperate plains and forests, mountains, rock arches  
**Major Cities:** Zarra (capital), Tarko‑se, Jorra, At‑la'o  
**Trade Routes:** Quellor Run

---

## Living World Atmosphere

Cato Neimoidia is a world of contrasts. From orbit, it appears as a verdant, mist‑shrouded planet, its surface broken by towering rock arches that rise from dense forests. The bridge‑cities are marvels of engineering – vast urban sprawls suspended between these natural pillars, their topside spires gleaming in the sun while their undersides vanish into perpetual fog. The air is humid and thick, carrying the scent of damp earth, industrial smoke, and the faint metallic tang of the bridges themselves.

The Neimoidians are a species in mourning. They walk with their heads down, their voices hushed. The grandeur of the Trade Federation is a distant memory; the Empire's shadow is a present reality. Every corner of the city bears scars – blast marks on walls, buildings that were never fully rebuilt, and the ever‑present tension of a conquered people.

**Weather:** Cato Neimoidia is temperate and humid, with frequent rain and mist. The fog layer that blankets the lower reaches of the bridges is perpetual, obscuring the ventral ruins and providing cover for smugglers, criminals, and wildlife.

The PCs' arrival on Cato Neimoidia triggers the broader investigation of Warde's past. The planet's atmosphere of fear and resentment is the backdrop for all Act 2 encounters. The GM should emphasise:

- The Syndicate's invisible control – every shopkeeper and citizen is watching, and some are reporting to Hasar.

# 2. JORRA – THE BRIDGE‑CITY

**Type:** Major urban centre  
**Population:** ~1.5 million  
**Bridge Span:** Approximately 800 metres between two rock arches  
**Structure:** Built on the dorsal (top) and ventral (bottom) surfaces of the bridge, with buildings extending both up and down.

---

## Living World Atmosphere

Jorra is a city of two halves. The dorsal city is alive – bustling streets, factories churning out goods, and markets filled with the chatter of merchants. But it is a city under occupation. Stormtroopers patrol the main thoroughfares, and the Whitescar Syndicate's presence is felt in every bribe and every protection payment. The citizens are wary, their eyes darting as they move. They have learned not to trust strangers.

The ventral city – the underside – is a tomb. Once prosperous, it was devastated during the Clone Wars and never rebuilt. Now it is a dark, crumbling labyrinth of collapsed towers and empty streets, accessible only by the brave or the desperate. The fog that perpetually shrouds it hides dangers both natural and unnatural.

**Lighting:** Dorsal city is well‑lit by natural sunlight (filtered through amber lenses) and artificial lights. Ventral city is perpetually dark, lit only by the occasional glow‑rod or bioluminescent creature.

**Sounds:** The dorsal city hums with traffic, machinery, and voices. The ventral city is silent except for dripping water, the creak of metal, and the distant howl of predators.

## Storylines & Events in Jorra (General)

- **Unwelcome Visitors:** The PCs are treated with suspicion. All social checks suffer penalties unless the PCs earn trust.
- **Enemies of the City:** If the PCs reveal themselves as Force‑users, locals may form mobs (see Welcoming Committee encounter).
- **Healing the Wounds:** The overarching storyline of Act 2 – the PCs must help the city to gain trust and information. This includes Spice Problems, Medical Aid, Lost Treasures, and Reunion.

- **War Stories:** Meeting Tor Haido, Zel N'got, TH‑313, and Onrein Hasar.
- **Legacy of Disgrace:** The climax of Act 2 – venturing to Sankar Palace.

---

## GM Notes for Jorra

- **Social Difficulty:** All Charm, Negotiation, and Deception checks suffer at least one setback die (from the citizens' suspicion). This decreases as the PCs complete Healing the Wounds tasks.
- **Patrols:** Stormtroopers and Kreehawks patrol the streets. The GM should roll for random encounters if the PCs linger.
- **Spies:** The Syndicate and the Empire both have informants. If the PCs draw attention, they will be followed.
- **Morality:** The GM should track Conflict for acts of intimidation or violence against civilians.

# 3. JORRA SPACEPORT / LANDING BAYS

**Type:** Arrival point, security checkpoint  
**Location:** Dorsal side of the bridge, near the commercial district  
**Access:** Docking bays, passenger terminals, customs inspection

---

## Living World Description

The spaceport is a sprawling complex built into the side of the bridge structure. Docking bays jut out from the main body like the spines of a creature, each one capable of handling a medium‑sized freighter. The main terminal is a cavernous hall of transparisteel and durasteel, filled with the bustle of travellers, traders, and customs officials. The air smells of ozone and recycled oxygen. Droids and sentients alike move with purpose.

Security is present but not oppressive – stormtroopers stand at key checkpoints, and Neimoidian customs officers inspect luggage and ships. The Imperials are more interested in contraband and sedition than in ordinary trade.

---

- **Concealment:** The PCs should consider hiding any lightsabers or contraband before landing.
- **Bribery:** A small bribe (100–200 credits) can smooth over a "misunderstanding" with customs.
- **Deception:** The PCs can claim to be traders, mercenaries, or refugees. A successful Deception check can bypass scrutiny.
- **Ship Security:** Leaving the ship unattended is risky – the Syndicate may plant tracking devices.

- **Failure Consequences:** If the PCs are caught with illegal items, they may be detained, their ship impounded, or their Force‑sensitivity exposed.
- **Success:** If the PCs pass security smoothly, they enter Jorra proper with no immediate complications.

# 4. DORSAL CITY – STREETS, DISTRICTS & COMMON AREAS

**Type:** Urban environment  
**Includes:** Residential District, Commercial District, Factory District, Palace District

---

## Living World Description

The dorsal city is a vertical maze. Streets are narrow and winding, often covered by bridges and walkways that connect buildings at multiple levels. Speeders zip overhead, and the occasional swoop gang races through the lower levels, scattering pedestrians. The architecture is a mix of Neimoidian elegance and Imperial utilitarianism – ornate spires stand next to grey, functional barracks.

**Residential District:** Cramped apartments, small shops, and crowded marketplaces. The heart of the city's civilian life. Many citizens live in poverty, their homes barely repaired from war damage.

**Commercial District:** Once the thriving hub of Trade Federation commerce, now a shadow of its former self. Imperial bureaucrats monitor transactions. The great banking halls are empty.

**Factory District:** The industrial engine of Jorra. Smoke and sparks pour from chimneys. The air is thick with the smell of molten metal and ozone. Labour droids and workers move in a constant cycle.

**Palace District:** Where the wealthy (and the Imperial governor) live. Grand manses and fortified estates, many owned by former Trade Federation executives who have managed to retain their wealth.

---

## What NPCs Do Here

| NPC / Group | Activity | Notes |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| **Citizens** | Work, shop, socialise, avoid trouble | They are wary of outsiders. |
| **Merchants** | Sell goods, pay protection, pass information | Some are Syndicate informants. |
| **Kreehawk Gangers** | Collect protection, intimidate shopkeepers | Visible and aggressive. |
| **Stormtroopers** | Patrol, conduct random ID checks | They are more concerned with Rebels than with the Syndicate. |
| **Informants** | Monitor the PCs and report to Hasar or Iribian | The PCs may notice they are being watched. |

---

## Tactical Decisions

- **Blending In:** The PCs should dress appropriately and avoid drawing attention. Openly carrying lightsabers or wearing Jedi‑style robes will provoke hostility.
- **Information Gathering:** The best places to gather information are cafes, markets, and cantinas (e.g., Torna's Cafe, Dropview Cantina).
- **Safe Houses:** Jyl Korlis's farm on Arbooine had a parallel – in Jorra, the PCs may need to find a safe place to lay low. The residential district offers cheap lodgings.
- **Avoiding Patrols:** The PCs can use the city's verticality to their advantage – moving along rooftops or through maintenance tunnels can bypass checkpoints.

---

## GM Notes

- **Random Encounters:** If the PCs linger, roll for a patrol, a Syndicate shakedown, or a citizen seeking help.
- **Morality Checks:** The PCs will witness injustices (beatings, extortion, arrests). The GM should offer opportunities to intervene and track Conflict for inaction.
- **Reputation:** The PCs' reputation spreads quickly. If they help citizens, trust grows; if they intimidate or harm locals, fear and hostility grow.

# 5. DUCAL PALACE & IMPERIAL BARRACKS

**Type:** Imperial seat of power, military barracks  
**Location:** Southeastern tip of the bridge, palace district  
**Access:** By invitation or force; heavy security

---

## Living World Description

The Ducal Palace is a fortress dressed as a palace. It rises from the bridge's edge, its towers overlooking the drop into the mist. The architecture is Imperial – stark, functional, imposing. The walls are reinforced durasteel, and turrets bristle with sensors and blaster cannons. Inside, the halls are cold and echoing, decorated with Imperial banners and holopics of the Emperor.

The barracks adjoin the palace, housing the stormtroopers who enforce Iribian's will. The barracks are functional, spartan, and always busy with the sound of marching boots and shouted orders.

**Atmosphere:** The palace is a place of fear and ambition. Servants move silently; officers speak in clipped tones. Iribian is a constant, unseen presence – his paranoia radiates through the walls.

---

## What NPCs Do Here

| NPC / Group | Activity | Notes |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| **Duke Iribian** | Reviews reports, issues orders, plots his next move | He rarely leaves his private chambers. |
| **TH‑313 "Tacker"** | Reports to Iribian, oversees troop rotations | He is a visible but weary figure. |
| **Stormtroopers** | Guard entrances, patrol corridors | They are alert and professional. |
| **Advisors / Spies** | Deliver intelligence, report on citizens | Iribian's network of informants is vast. |

---

## Tactical Decisions

- **Infiltration:** The palace is heavily guarded. The PCs would need a solid plan (disguises, bribes, or slicing) to enter undetected.
- **Social Approach:** If the PCs have a legitimate reason to meet Iribian (e.g., they pose as traders or bounty hunters), they may gain audience.
- **Extraction:** If the PCs are captured, Tacker may be a potential ally for escape – but exposing him endangers him.
- **Slicing:** The palace's computer systems contain valuable intelligence – including records of the Silver Star incident and Iribian's secret accounts.

---

## GM Notes

- **Duke Iribian's Response:** Iribian will attempt to capture Force‑sensitive PCs to advance his career. He will not engage in combat personally if he can avoid it.
- **Tacker's Loyalty:** If the PCs meet Tacker and win his trust, he may provide information, assistance, or a warning.
- **Dalan Oberos:** If the PCs are Knight‑level, Iribian will hire Oberos to hunt them. Oberos may attack in the palace or elsewhere.

---

## Storylines & Events at the Ducal Palace

# 11. THE VENTRAL UNDERCITY & SANKAR PALACE

**Type:** Ruined district / ancient battlefield  
**Location:** Underside of the bridge, accessible through maintenance tunnels, the spaceport, or via climbing

---

## Living World Description

The ventral undercity is a dark mirror of Jorra's dorsal half – a city of towers and bridges that hangs upside‑down, shrouded in perpetual mist. It was destroyed during the Clone Wars and never rebuilt. Buildings are crumbling, walkways are collapsed, and the only light comes from the occasional bioluminescent creature or the glow of a starship passing overhead.

Sankar Palace is the largest structure in the ventral undercity. It once housed the Trade Federation's elite; now it is a hollow ruin. The lowest levels, where Warde's fall occurred, are the most damaged – and the most dangerous.

**Atmosphere:** The undercity is cold, damp, and silent. The mist muffles sound. The only company is the occasional predator and the ghosts of the past. The force lingers here – the memory of Warde's betrayal is thick in the air.

## Tactical Decisions

- **Navigation:** The PCs must navigate crumbling bridges, collapsed towers, and unstable walkways. Athletics and Coordination checks are common.
- **Avoiding Droids:** The droids are on autopilot. The PCs can try to sneak past them or set up ambushes.
- **Combat:** The droids (BX Commandos and Droidekas) are the main combat threats. The environment offers opportunities for using terrain – pushing enemies into chasms, collapsing walkways, etc.
- **Kyber Crystal:** The crystal is in the lowest level of Sankar Palace, in the hand of the dead Jedi Jiv Durael.

---

## GM Notes

- **Atmosphere:** The GM should emphasise the sense of dread and decay. This is a place of death and dark history.
- **Droid Encounters:** The droids are old but deadly. They attack in waves – first the commandos, then the droidekas.
- **The Palace:** The meeting hall is where Warde fell. The GM should describe the scene with appropriate gravity – the dead Jedi, the slashed table, the weight of history.
- **Falling:** The chasm in the lowest level is a significant hazard. Players who fall may be killed – but the GM may allow a Destiny Point to save them (a handhold, a ledge, etc.).

## Smart Strategies for the PCs

1. **Recruit a Rusted Stalker:** If the PCs bond with a stalker, navigating the ventral ruins becomes significantly easier – the stalker knows the safe paths and where the droids are.
2. **Use the Environment:** The walkways and balconies are unstable. The PCs can collapse them on enemies or use them to escape.
3. **Bypass Combat:** The droids are old and have limited patrol routes. The PCs can sneak past many of them.
4. **Prepare for Droids:** The droids are resistant to blasters. Lightsabers and ion weapons are ideal. Cortosis rounds (from Oberos) may also be useful.
5. **Secure the Exit:** The PCs should ensure their ship is accessible in case they need to retreat quickly.

## Storylines & Events in the Ventral Undercity

- **Legacy of Disgrace:** The climax of Act 2. The PCs descend into Sankar Palace, battle the droids, and retrieve the second kyber crystal from Jiv Durael's corpse.
- **Through the Darkened City:** The journey through the ventral ruins is its own challenge – navigation, hazards, and potential ambushes.
- **The Restless Grave:** The meeting hall where Warde fell. The PCs witness the aftermath of his betrayal.
- **Kyber Crystal Discovery:** The PCs find the crystal and learn Warde's final destination: Moraband.
- **TH‑313 and Onrein Hasar:** The PCs may have already learned the full story from these NPCs; the palace is the physical confirmation.
