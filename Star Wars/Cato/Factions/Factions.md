# ACT 2 FACTION ROSTER – COMPLETE STATS, BACKSTORY & LIVING WORLD DETAILS

This section details the major factions operating in Jorra during **Episode II: Chasing Ghosts**. Each entry includes origins, structure, goals, relationships, daily operations, and plot hooks to bring the city to life. All information is drawn from the source material and expanded for a living, breathing campaign setting.

# The Whitescar Syndicate

**Type:** Criminal cartel / Shadow government  
**Influence:** Controls nearly all organised crime in Jorra, with reach across Cato Neimoidia and beyond.  
**Leader:** Onrein Hasar (see NPC entry)  
**Headquarters:** Hasar's fortified manor‑spire in the palace district  
**Public Face:** A network of legitimate businesses – import/export, manufacturing, and transport – all fronts for criminal activity.

---

## Origins & Backstory

The Whitescar Syndicate was born from the ashes of the Clone Wars. When the fighting on Cato Neimoidia ended, Jorra lay in ruins. The Empire was slow to establish order, and the local populace was starving, desperate, and unprotected. Into this void stepped Onrein Hasar – a former Trade Federation executive and Separatist diplomat who had been left disfigured and bitter by Suljo Warde's attack. She had lost her career, her face, and her faith in the system. She had nothing left but her intelligence, her ruthlessness, and her hatred.

She began small – smuggling, protection rackets, and information brokering. But she was patient. She recruited the desperate, bought the corrupt, and eliminated the stubborn. Within five years, she had consolidated all criminal activity in Jorra under a single banner: the Whitescar Syndicate. The name comes from the pale, angry scar that runs across her face – a constant reminder of her enemy. Today, the Syndicate is the true power in Jorra. Duke Iribian may rule from his palace, but Hasar's will shapes the city's daily life.

---

## Structure & Leadership

The Syndicate is built like a corporation, with clear hierarchies and divisions of labour. Hasar is the CEO – she makes the strategic decisions and rarely involves herself in day‑to‑day operations. Beneath her are three lieutenants, each responsible for a major arm of the organisation:

| Title | Role | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **The Accountant** (Vorn Kaal – Neimoidian male) | Finance & Laundering | A dour, meticulous number‑cruncher who keeps the Syndicate's books clean. He runs a network of shell companies, front businesses, and off‑world accounts. He is utterly loyal to Hasar because she saved his life during the war. |
| **The Enforcer** (Kael Dorn – Human male) | Security & Muscle | A former mercenary who commands the Syndicate's armed forces. He is brutal, efficient, and has no qualms about collateral damage. He personally leads the most violent operations. |
| **The Whisper** (Sella Vex – Twi'lek female) | Intelligence & Espionage | A former Separatist intelligence agent who runs the Syndicate's spy network. She knows everything that happens in Jorra – including the PCs' movements. |

Below them are dozens of middle managers, gang leaders, and specialised operatives. The Crimson Kreehawks are one such subsidiary gang, reporting to The Enforcer.

---

## Territory & Influence

The Syndicate controls:
- **The commercial district:** Most shops and businesses pay protection fees.
- **The factory district:** Labour unions and factory supervisors are on the payroll.
- **The residential district:** Spice dens, gambling halls, and loan sharks operate openly.
- **The Dropview Cantina:** A neutral meeting ground and information hub.
- **The ventral ruins:** Used for smuggling, hiding bodies, and secret meetings.

The Syndicate does not openly challenge Imperial authority – it coexists with it. Iribian receives a cut of the profits in exchange for turning a blind eye.

---

## Goals & Motivations

The Syndicate's primary goal is profit and stability. Hasar wants to maintain her empire and grow it. However, beneath this surface lies a personal obsession: finding Suljo Warde. If Warde is alive, Hasar will stop at nothing to reach him. If he is dead, she will desecrate his grave. This obsession is a weakness that the PCs can exploit – but it also makes her unpredictable and dangerous.

---

## Relationships

| Faction | Relationship |
|---------|--------------|
| **Imperial Garrison (Iribian)** | A cold truce. They exchange bribes and information, but both would betray the other without hesitation. |
| **Crimson Kreehawks** | A subsidiary. They report to The Enforcer and are used for visible enforcement and intimidation. |
| **Citizens of Jorra** | Feared and resented. Citizens pay protection, but they also hate the Syndicate. |
| **The PCs** | Unknown quantities. Hasar will attempt to assess their value – as potential allies, tools, or threats. |

---

## Daily Life & Operations

**Morning:** Syndicate accountants review the previous day's profits. Enforcers patrol key streets, collecting payments. Whisper's spies monitor comlink chatter and report any unusual activity (such as outsiders asking about Warde).

**Afternoon:** Lieutenants report to Hasar via hologram. Enforcement actions are planned. Spice shipments are coordinated with off‑world contacts.

**Evening:** The Dropview Cantina fills with Syndicate operatives and informants. Hasar often reviews the day's intelligence over a private dinner in her manor.

**Night:** Smuggling operations move through the ventral ruins. Spice dens operate at full capacity.

---

## Rumours & Plot Hooks

- **Rumour:** Hasar has a mole inside Iribian's palace – a trusted advisor who feeds her intelligence. Finding this mole could earn the Syndicate's gratitude or blackmail the duke.
- **Rumour:** The Syndicate is looking to expand into Arbooine's tree‑cities. Gel Marcolf may have already made contact.
- **Plot Hook:** A group of citizens has formed a secret resistance against the Syndicate. They are small and weak, but they need help – and they have information on Hasar's personal schedule.
- **Plot Hook:** The Enforcer, Kael Dorn, has a personal feud with a rival gang that operates in the ventral ruins. If the PCs eliminate his rivals, he owes them a favour.
- **Plot Hook:** The Whisper is secretly a Rebel sympathiser. She could be turned into an ally against both the Syndicate and the Empire – but she is terrified of Hasar's wrath.

# The Crimson Kreehawks

**Type:** Swoop gang / Subsidiary of the Whitescar Syndicate  
**Influence:** Operates openly in Jorra's streets, primarily in the commercial and factory districts.  
**Leader:** Lor Kelin (see NPC entry)  
**Headquarters:** A converted warehouse in the factory district, filled with swoops and spare parts.

---

## Origins & Backstory

The Crimson Kreehawks emerged in the decade after the Clone Wars, formed by the children of Neimoidian survivors – young men and women who grew up in poverty and resented their parents' generation for their "cowardice." They saw the older Neimoidians as weak, having hidden behind droids and trade agreements while their world burned. The Kreehawks rejected that caution, embracing speed, violence, and risk. They are named after the predatory kreehawk, a native raptor that strikes from above without warning. They painted their swoops crimson to symbolise the blood they were willing to spill.

Initially, they were just a street gang – petty theft, turf wars, and minor spice dealing. But when the Whitescar Syndicate expanded into Jorra, Hasar recognised their potential. She absorbed them, offering protection and resources in exchange for loyalty. Today, the Kreehawks are the Syndicate's visible enforcers. They extort shops, shake down citizens, and intimidate anyone who questions Hasar's rule. They are reckless, loud, and fiercely proud of their reputation.

---

## Structure & Leadership

| Title | Role | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Leader** (Lor Kelin) | Overall command | Young, aggressive, and ambitious. She is loyal to Hasar out of fear and profit. |
| **Lieutenant** (Vek Torr – Neimoidian male) | Second‑in‑command | Older and more cautious than Lor. He often reins in her worst impulses. |
| **Riders** (Gangers) | Enforcers and scouts | They patrol the streets, collect payments, and deliver messages. |
| **Mechanics** (Technicians) | Maintain swoops and weapons | They keep the gang's equipment running. |

---

## Territory & Activities

The Kreehawks operate primarily in the commercial and factory districts. They:
- Collect protection money from shops and businesses.
- Run spice dens and gambling operations.
- Serve as couriers for the Syndicate.
- Intimidate witnesses and disrupt investigations.
- Race swoops through the bridge‑city's treacherous routes, endangering civilians.

They are not subtle. They wear bright red jackets, rev their engines loudly, and make their presence known. They are a visible reminder of Hasar's power.

---

## Goals & Motivations

- **Profit:** The Kreehawks are motivated by money and status.
- **Fear:** They want to be feared, as it makes their jobs easier.
- **Loyalty to Hasar:** They serve the Syndicate because Hasar pays well and punishes betrayal swiftly.
- **Reckless Thrills:** Many Kreehawks genuinely enjoy the danger and speed of their lifestyle.

---

## Relationships

| Faction | Relationship |
|---------|--------------|
| **Whitescar Syndicate** | Employers. They answer to The Enforcer and, ultimately, Hasar. |
| **Imperial Garrison** | They avoid direct confrontation but will attack if Imperial forces threaten their operations. |
| **Citizens of Jorra** | They terrorise and exploit them. Citizens fear and despise the Kreehawks. |
| **The PCs** | Potential targets if the PCs interfere. They may also be hired as temporary allies (for a price). |

---

## Daily Life & Operations

**Morning:** Riders gather at the warehouse, receive their assignments from Lor or Vek, and head out on patrol.

**Afternoon:** Collection routes are completed. Intimidation and enforcement actions are carried out.

**Evening:** Riders return to the warehouse to report, drink, and race their swoops. Spice consumption is common.

**Night:** Some riders operate late‑night spice dens or conduct smuggling runs through the ventral ruins.

---

## Rumours & Plot Hooks

- **Rumour:** Lor Kelin is secretly saving to buy a custom swoop from a pirate on Tatooine – she is planning to leave the Kreehawks and start her own gang.
- **Rumour:** One of the Kreehawks, a young Neimoidian named Tohn Kreiwic, is planning to leave the gang – his father is desperate to rescue him (see "Reunion" in the adventure, page 56).
- **Plot Hook:** The Kreehawks have been hired to sabotage a rival factory. If the PCs stop them, they gain the factory owner's gratitude and information.
- **Plot Hook:** Lor Kelin has a personal grudge against a local swoop gang called the "Shadow Runners." If the PCs help her settle the score, she owes them a favour.

# The Imperial Garrison of Jorra

**Type:** Imperial military occupation force  
**Influence:** Official government of Jorra; controls the Ducal Palace, Southside Prison, and spaceport security.  
**Leader:** Duke Erron Iribian (see NPC entry)  
**Headquarters:** Ducal Palace, southeastern tip of the bridge.

---

## Origins & Backstory

The Imperial Garrison was established in the aftermath of the Clone Wars, when the newly formed Empire seized control of Cato Neimoidia's purse worlds. The Neimoidians were seen as potential Separatist sympathisers, and the Empire was determined to keep a tight grip on the trade routes and factories that made the region valuable. Duke Erron Iribian was appointed as governor – a political punishment that he transformed into a stepping stone.

The garrison consists of several hundred stormtroopers, a handful of Imperial officers, and a small naval detachment that patrols the system. Their primary mission is to maintain order, ensure factories meet production quotas, and suppress any signs of rebellion. They are not beloved – they are feared. The Neimoidians see them as occupiers, but they are too beaten down to resist openly.

---

## Structure & Leadership

| Title | Role | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Governor** (Duke Iribian) | Overall command | Makes policy, handles external relations, and answers to the Imperial Security Bureau. |
| **Captain** (TH‑313 "Tacker") | Troop commander | Oversees patrols, prison security, and tactical operations. Secretly sympathetic to Jedi. |
| **Lieutenant Salial Devrays** (optional) | Detachment leader | Sent to Arbooine for patrols, may appear if the PCs cause trouble on Cato Neimoidia. |
| **Sergeants** | Squad leaders | Command stormtrooper squads on the ground. |
| **Stormtroopers** | Enforcers | Carry out patrols, arrests, and combat operations. |

---

## Territory & Influence

The Imperial Garrison controls:
- **The Ducal Palace:** The seat of Imperial authority and the governor's residence.
- **The Southside Prison:** A heavily fortified detention facility for political prisoners and criminals.
- **The Spaceport:** They control all arrivals and departures, inspecting ships and passengers.
- **The Factory District:** They monitor production and ensure quotas are met.
- **Major thoroughfares:** Stormtroopers patrol the streets, reminding citizens of Imperial power.

They do not control the commercial or residential districts directly – they rely on fear and occasional patrols to keep order. The Syndicate operates freely in these areas, and Iribian allows it.

---

## Goals & Motivations

- **Maintain order:** Prevent open rebellion and keep the factories running.
- **Profit:** Ensure taxes and production quotas are met.
- **Career advancement:** Iribian wants a promotion. He will eliminate any threat to his record.
- **Eliminate threats:** Force‑users, Rebels, and troublemakers must be captured or killed.

---

## Relationships

| Faction | Relationship |
|---------|--------------|
| **Whitescar Syndicate** | A cold truce. They exchange bribes and information. |
| **Crimson Kreehawks** | They are aware of the gang but do not interfere unless public order is threatened. |
| **Citizens of Jorra** | Feared and resented. Citizens obey out of fear, not loyalty. |
| **The PCs** | Potential threats. If the PCs reveal themselves as Force‑users, the garrison will hunt them. |

---

## Daily Life & Operations

**Morning:** Roll call. Patrols are dispatched to key points. Spaceport security checks incoming ships.

**Afternoon:** Factory inspections. Prison rotations. Iribian reviews intelligence reports.

**Evening:** Officers dine in the Ducal Palace. Stormtroopers patrol the streets. Spies report to Iribian's advisors.

**Night:** Prison lockdown. Patrols continue, but with reduced numbers. The Ducal Palace is heavily guarded.

---

## Rumours & Plot Hooks

- **Rumour:** Iribian is secretly siphoning credits from the factory quotas to fund his retirement. If this were exposed, he would be executed.
- **Rumour:** The Southside Prison holds a political prisoner who knows the location of a hidden Rebel base. The Rebels are desperate to free him.
- **Plot Hook:** A stormtrooper squad has been harassing citizens in the residential district. If the PCs stop them, they earn the locals' trust.
- **Plot Hook:** Iribian has a sealed order from the Imperial Security Bureau – if the PCs can access it, they learn that a purge of "unreliable" officers is planned. Tacker is on the list.

# The Neimoidian Citizens of Jorra

**Type:** Civilian population  
**Influence:** The populace – workers, merchants, families – who make up the city's lifeblood.  
**Leadership:** No formal leader; several elders and community figures hold informal influence.  
**Headquarters:** The residential district and the commercial district.

---

## Origins & Backstory

The Neimoidians of Jorra are a proud but broken people. Before the Clone Wars, they were among the wealthiest citizens of the galaxy, benefiting from the Trade Federation's vast commercial empire. They were traders, diplomats, and executives – a species that valued commerce above all else. But the war devastated them. Their droids turned against them; their factories were bombed; their children were killed. They lost everything – their wealth, their pride, and their future.

The rise of the Empire only worsened their plight. They were punished for their Separatist ties, stripped of their status, and reduced to second‑class citizens. Today, they are a defeated people, living in the shadow of their former glory. They hate the Jedi for what Warde did – they see him as the symbol of the Republic's betrayal. They fear the Empire and resent the Syndicate's exploitation. They are survivors, but they have lost hope.

---

## Structure & Leadership

The citizens have no formal government. However, several informal leaders hold influence:
- **The Elders:** Older Neimoidians who remember the pre‑war days. They are respected but powerless.
- **The Shopkeepers' Association:** A loose group of merchants who coordinate against extortion – but they are weak and often pay protection.
- **The Factory Workers' Union:** A secret organisation that meets in back rooms to discuss better working conditions. The Empire and Syndicate both suppress it.

Most citizens keep their heads down and try to survive. Some have joined the resistance (see "The Man Who Would Resist" in the adventure).

---

## Territory & Influence

The citizens are everywhere – they work the factories, run the shops, and live in the residential district. They have no formal territory, but they are the city's economic engine. Without them, Jorra would collapse.

---

## Goals & Motivations

- **Survival:** Most citizens just want to live another day.
- **Protection:** They want safety from the Empire, the Syndicate, and the monsters in the ventral ruins.
- **Justice (for some):** Many citizens want revenge against Warde and the Jedi.
- **Freedom (for a few):** Some secretly dream of a future without Imperial rule.

---

## Relationships

| Faction | Relationship |
|---------|--------------|
| **Imperial Garrison** | Feared and resented. They obey, but they do not love the Empire. |
| **Whitescar Syndicate** | Exploited and bullied. They pay protection but hate every credit. |
| **Crimson Kreehawks** | Terrorised by them. Citizens avoid the Kreehawks' patrol routes. |
| **The PCs** | Suspicious of outsiders, but potentially willing to help if the PCs prove themselves. |

---

## Daily Life & Operations

**Morning:** Citizens wake early and head to factories, shops, or markets. Children go to school (if they have one).

**Afternoon:** The workday continues. Some citizens take breaks at cafes, where they whisper rumours.

**Evening:** Families gather for meals. Some attend secret meetings. Many stay indoors after dark.

**Night:** The streets are empty except for patrols and Syndicate operations.

---

## Rumours & Plot Hooks

- **Rumour:** A hidden cache of Republic credits is rumoured to be buried in the ventral ruins. Citizens whisper about it but are too afraid to search.
- **Rumour:** The factory workers are planning a strike – but they are terrified of the Syndicate's response.
- **Plot Hook:** A citizen named Sanna Olpan has not seen her son Gren since Warde arrested him years ago. She is desperate for news (see "Reunion" in the adventure, page 56).
- **Plot Hook:** A group of citizens has formed a small resistance in the residential district. They need weapons and training – they are willing to trade information for help.

# Separatist Sympathisers (Minor Faction)

**Type:** Underground movement / Remnant loyalists  
**Influence:** Small, scattered. Operate in secret.  
**Leader:** None – they are a network of informants, old soldiers, and true believers.  
**Headquarters:** No fixed location – they meet in back rooms, private homes, and the ventral ruins.

---

## Origins & Backstory

Not all Neimoidians have accepted Imperial rule. A small but dedicated group of Separatist loyalists still dream of a Confederacy restored. They are a mix of former Trade Federation executives, ex‑Separatist soldiers, and civilians who remember a time when Cato Neimoidia was powerful. They hate the Empire for stripping their status and the Jedi for betraying them. They see the Empire as a worse tyranny than the Republic ever was.

They are not organised enough to be a threat to the Empire – but they can provide information, shelter, and contacts to those who share their goals.

---

## Goals & Motivations

- **Restore Neimoidian independence:** They dream of a free Cato Neimoidia.
- **Revenge against the Empire:** They want to strike back against the oppressors.
- **Preserve Separatist history:** They keep old records, weapons caches, and droid schematics.

---

## Relationships

| Faction | Relationship |
|---------|--------------|
| **Imperial Garrison** | They are enemies. The Empire would execute them if discovered. |
| **Whitescar Syndicate** | They distrust the Syndicate but may use its resources. |
| **Crimson Kreehawks** | They see the Kreehawks as sell‑outs. |
| **Citizens of Jorra** | They recruit among the disaffected. |

---

## Rumours & Plot Hooks

- **Rumour:** A Separatist weapons cache is hidden in the ventral ruins – still functional and untouched.
- **Plot Hook:** A Separatist loyalist named Zel N'got (see NPC) was a key intelligence asset. If the PCs can reach him, he may still have contacts.
- **Plot Hook:** A Separatist commander from the Clone Wars is hiding in Jorra, planning a small uprising. He needs help to arm his followers.
