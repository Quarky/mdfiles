# LEVEL 0: THE OSSIVANE BASEMENT — DEPARTURE

---

## GM OVERVIEW

**Purpose:** This is the starting point for the descent into Sankar Palace. The party has completed the investigation that led them here—they have the second kyber crystal, they know where Warde went, and they understand enough of the story to know that going down is not an act of investigation anymore, but an act of witness.

**Tone:** Heavy, deliberate, significant. The party is about to enter a place that has been sealed for fifteen years, a place where something terrible happened. The atmosphere should be one of quiet anticipation and solemn purpose.

**Key GM Goals:**
- Establish the emotional weight of the descent
- Introduce the stalker's warning as a recurring theme
- Create a clear transition from the "upper city" to the "deep route"
- Set the tone for the rest of the journey
- Allow players to learn the story through environmental details

## ENVIRONMENTAL DETAILS — CULTURE AND HISTORY

### The Ossivane District

The Ossivane is part of the residential expansion that the Trade Federation built during its peak years. It was designed to house administrators, diplomats, and their families—the people who kept the machinery of the Federation running.

**Architectural Style:**
- Formal, imposing, designed to impress
- Wide corridors, high ceilings, decorative elements
- Stone quarried from standard Cato Neimoidian sources
- Chemical lighting system that has lasted for decades
- Built to last, built to convey power

**What This Tells the Players:**
- The Trade Federation valued appearances
- They built for permanence
- They expected their power to last forever
- They were wrong

**The Name "Ossivane":**
In Pak Pak, "Ossivane" translates roughly to "Place of Records" or "Archive." This district was originally built to house the administrative records of the Trade Federation's operations on Cato Neimoidia. The name reflects the Federation's obsession with documentation, protocol, and control.

### The Residential Expansion

The residential expansion occurred during the Federation's Fourth Expansion period, approximately 40 years before the Clone Wars. It was a time of unprecedented growth and prosperity for the Trade Federation.

**What the Players Can Learn:**
- The Federation was at its peak
- They were expanding their influence across the galaxy
- They built grand structures to house their growing bureaucracy
- They believed their power would never end
- The expansion created the residential districts that now lie empty

**Evidence in the Basement:**
- Quality of construction
- Attention to detail
- Signs of prosperity (the crystal sculpture)
- The ambitious scale of the building

### The Trade Federation's Culture

The Neimoidians who built the Ossivane were part of a culture that valued:
- Wealth and status
- Protocol and procedure
- Appearance and reputation
- Control and order
- Profit above all else

**What the Party Might Learn:**
- The Federation valued documentation (the ledger in the palace)
- They valued ceremony (the formal corridors, the mosaic)
- They valued permanence (the stone construction, the sealed vault)
- They valued control (the locked doors, the restricted access)

## THE OSSIVANE BASEMENT — DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Overall Space

The Ossivane basement is a large, rectangular chamber approximately 15 meters by 20 meters. The ceiling is 3.5 meters high, with exposed support beams and ductwork running along the top. The walls are roughly finished stone, patched in places where repairs have been made over the decades.

**Layout:**
- **North Wall:** The entrance from the Ossivane lobby
- **South Wall:** The vault door and the sealed passage to the deep route
- **East Wall:** The shelf where the crystal sculpture sat, stacks of equipment
- **West Wall:** More equipment stacks, a workbench

**Lighting:** Chemical lights mounted at regular intervals along the walls and ceiling cast a warm amber glow. Several of the lights flicker or have failed entirely, creating pools of shadow throughout the space.

**Sound:** The basement is not silent. There is:
- A faint hum from the chemical lights
- The whisper of air through the ventilation system
- The occasional drip of water from a leak somewhere in the ceiling
- The creak of the structure settling
- Distant sounds from the upper levels—cargo haulers, workers, the general noise of the ventral city

**Smell:** The air carries the scent of dust, old stone, and something metallic. There is also a faint organic smell from the stalker's presence—warm fur and musk.

### The Vault

The vault door is set into the southern wall. It is a massive durasteel construction, approximately 2.5 meters tall and 2 meters wide, with a complex locking mechanism that has been sealed.

**Historical Context:**
The vault was originally built to store valuable documents and artifacts from the residential expansion. It was later used by the Republic occupation forces to secure sensitive materials. After the evacuation, it was sealed and forgotten.

**What the Players See:**
- The door is closed, its mechanisms locked
- The control panel beside it is dark, its power supply disconnected
- A faint outline of dust shows where the door has not been opened recently
- The door bears the Trade Federation triangle-and-circle motif

**What the Players Might Learn:**
- The combination is memorized and no longer needed
- The vault contained Jaral's items, which have been retrieved
- The vault was used for storage during the residential expansion
- The Trade Federation used this symbol to mark their property

### The Shelf

The shelf where the crystal sculpture once sat is located against the eastern wall. It is a simple stone shelf, approximately 1 meter wide and 0.5 meters deep, built into the wall.

**Historical Context:**
The crystal sculpture was a gift from Jaral to the Ossivane, a symbol of the bond between the residential district and the diplomatic complex. It was placed here when the Ossivane was built and remained until Jaral retrieved it.

**What the Players See:**
- The shelf is empty
- A perfect rectangle of lighter stone shows where the sculpture sat for 15 years
- The dust around the rectangle is undisturbed
- Small scratches on the shelf surface show where the sculpture was handled

**What the Players Might Learn:**
- The crystal sculpture was here for 15 years
- It was important enough to be kept in a vault
- It has now been returned to Jaral
- The sculpture was a symbol of something—perhaps a connection between the Ossivane and the palace above

### The Equipment Piles

Throughout the basement are stacks of old maintenance equipment—heavy frames for some kind of mechanical system long since removed. These are the frames that the stalker used as a bedding area.

**Historical Context:**
The frames are from an old ventilation system that was replaced during the Republic occupation. The original system was too inefficient for the needs of the occupying forces, so it was removed and replaced. The frames were left in the basement, forgotten.

**What the Players See:**
- Metal frames, approximately 2 meters tall, stacked against the walls
- Some have been moved, leaving clear paths through the basement
- The frames are rusted, their original purpose unclear
- The stalker's bedding area is visible—a flattened space among the frames

**What the Players Might Learn:**
- The stalker moved these over several weeks
- The movement revealed the southern passage
- Whether the stalker did this deliberately is unclear
- The original ventilation system was replaced during the occupation
- The frames have been here for decades

### The Workbench

Against the western wall is a workbench, covered in dust and debris. It was used by the maintenance workers who serviced the Ossivane.

**What the Players See:**
- A metal workbench, approximately 2 meters long
- Scattered tools: wrenches, pliers, screwdrivers
- A few empty containers that once held lubricant
- A clipboard with faded notes
- A broken glow rod, its casing cracked

**What the Players Might Learn:**
- The workbench was used until the evacuation
- The maintenance workers left in a hurry
- The notes on the clipboard might contain information about the deep route

**The Clipboard:**
The faded notes on the clipboard are in Pak Pak. They read:
- *"Ventilation system replacement approved. New units to be installed by end of week."*
- *"Deep route access door requires lubrication. Schedule maintenance."*
- *"Stalker sightings increasing. Recommend securing lower levels."*

### The Stalker's Mosaic

The mosaic is swept into the dust of the basement floor. It is located near the entrance to the basement, where the stalker has been sweeping for years.

**Historical Context:**
The stalker has been creating this mosaic for years, without understanding what it is doing. It is a pattern that the beast has developed through instinct, a way of marking territory or creating something beautiful in the emptiness.

**What the Players See:**
- Intricate patterns swept into the dust with geometric precision
- The patterns extend approximately 3 meters across
- The stalker has clearly spent considerable time on this
- The patterns are repetitive, almost ritualistic
- The mosaic is centered on the entrance to the basement, as if guarding it

**What the Players Might Learn:**
- The stalker has been creating this for years
- It does not understand what it is creating
- It is something beautiful in a place of abandonment
- The mosaic might be a form of communication or a ritual
- The stalker is connected to this place in ways it does not understand

## SOUNDSCAPE DETAILS — ATMOSPHERE

### The Hum

The chemical lights emit a constant low hum, barely audible but present. It is a sound that the players will notice only when they stop to listen.

**GM Description:**
> *"There is a hum in the basement. Not loud—barely audible, really. But once you notice it, you can't stop noticing it. It comes from the lights, from the old power system that still operates after all these years."*

**Significance:**
The hum is a reminder that the Ossivane is still alive, still powered, still functioning. It has been doing so for fifteen years without anyone to maintain it. The Federation built this place to last, and it has.

### The Drip

Somewhere in the ceiling, there is a leak. Water drips at irregular intervals, each drop landing in a puddle on the floor.

**GM Description:**
> *"You hear a drip. It comes from the ceiling, somewhere in the shadows above. The water hits the stone floor with a soft sound that echoes slightly in the quiet space."*

**Significance:**
The drip is a reminder that the Ossivane is decaying. Even the Federation's best construction cannot last forever. The water is slowly wearing away the stone, eroding the building from within.

### The Creak

The structure of the Ossivane settles constantly. The creaks and groans are the building adjusting to temperature changes, the weight of the city above, the slow movement of the canyon.

**GM Description:**
> *"You hear the creak of the structure settling. It is a low sound, like the building is breathing, shifting in its sleep."*

**Significance:**
The creak is the sound of the Ossivane aging. It has been standing for decades, and it will continue to stand for decades more. But it is not eternal. Nothing is.

### Distant Sounds

From the upper levels, the sounds of the ventral city filter down: cargo haulers, workers, the general noise of a living city.

**GM Description:**
> *"From somewhere above, you hear the sounds of the city. The rumble of cargo haulers on their tracks. The distant voices of workers on their schedules. The city is alive, even if this basement is not."*

**Significance:**
The distant sounds are a reminder that the party is leaving the living city behind. They are descending into a place that has been dead for fifteen years. The upper city will still be there when they return—if they return.

## NPC INTERACTIONS — STORY AND CULTURE

### The Rusted Stalker

The stalker is the primary NPC in this level. It has been the party's companion through the ventral city, and it is now warning them about what lies below.

**Stalker's Current State:**
- Alert, watchful, concerned
- Has been patrolling the boundary with increasing frequency
- Will not go further, but will wait
- Has a deep connection to the Ossivane and the deep route

**Stalker's Understanding of the Deep Route:**
The stalker has been in the ventral city for years. It has explored the upper levels thoroughly. But it has never gone past Level 14. Something down there stopped it.

**What the Stalker Knows:**
- The deep route is dangerous
- Something down there is powerful enough to stop an apex predator
- The stalker does not know what it is, only that it is there
- The stalker fears it

**What the Stalker Doesn't Know:**
- What exactly is down there
- Why it stopped
- What happened in the palace
- Who Warde was

**Stalker's Body Language:**
- Sits at the entrance, not blocking the path
- Ears forward, eyes fixed on the Pathfinder
- Fur slightly raised along the spine
- Tail curled around its feet
- Breathing slow and deliberate

**What the Stalker Communicates Through the Bond:**

> *"You feel through the bond what the stalker wants you to know. Not words. The stalker does not have words. What it has is the memory of going to the edge of Level 14 and stopping. The feeling that stopped it—not fear of a predator, not instinct about structural danger, but something the stalker has no category for. Something that made its fur stand and made it turn around and not go further."*

> *"The stalker has been the top predator in the ventral city for years. It does not avoid things. It avoids this."*

> *"It is telling you this as clearly as it can. It is also telling you: I will be here when you come back. That part is the clearest of all."*

**Stalker's Dialogue (If the Pathfinder Speaks to It):**

The stalker does not speak in words. But through the bond, it responds to the Pathfinder's intent with feelings:

- *"I will wait."* — The feeling of patient endurance, of sitting in one place for as long as needed.
- *"I trust you."* — The feeling of giving something precious to someone, expecting its return.
- *"Be careful."* — The feeling of worry, of watching someone walk into danger.
- *"Come back."* — The feeling of hope, of expectation, of waiting.
- *"I love you."* — A feeling of deep connection, of a bond that transcends words.

### Sigg

Sigg is the party's companion, a survivor of the Clone Wars who has been with them through the investigation. He is preparing for the descent with the thoroughness of habit.

**Sigg's Current State:**
- Quiet, focused, ready
- Has been checking his equipment with deliberate care
- Waiting for the party's commitment
- Has been in similar situations before

**Sigg's Understanding of Cato Neimoidia:**
Sigg has been on Cato Neimoidia for some time now. He has learned about its history, its culture, and its people. He knows that the Trade Federation's fall was a long time coming.

**What Sigg Knows:**
- The Trade Federation was a corrupt institution
- The Clone Wars devastated Cato Neimoidia
- The Empire has tightened its grip on the planet
- Warde was a hero who fell
- The deep route leads to the heart of that fall

**Sigg's Body Language:**
- Pack on his back, checking straps
- Hands moving with practiced efficiency
- Eyes scanning the shadows, alert to threats
- Face set in a serious expression
- Breathing steady and controlled

**Sigg's Dialogue:**

*"Ready?"*

**GM Note:** This is an invitation for the party to commit. Sigg is not rushing them, but he is ready to go.

*"It's not going to come with us, is it."*

**GM Note:** A statement, not a question. Sigg has accepted this.

*"All right. Let's go."*

**If the Party Delays:**

*"We don't have to go now. But we're going to have to go eventually. What's down there isn't going anywhere."*

**If the Party Asks About the Stalker:**

*"It knows something. Something about what's down there. I don't know what, exactly—I can't feel it the way you can. But I trust it. If it says we need to be careful, we need to be careful."*

**If the Party Asks About Cato Neimoidia:**

*"This planet was built on trade. On profit. On control. The Trade Federation thought they were untouchable. They thought their power would last forever. And then the Clone Wars happened. And the Empire happened. And now look at it."*

*"This basement is a reminder. The Federation built this place to last. But nothing lasts forever. Not power. Not wealth. Not control. The only thing that lasts is the damage we do."*

**If the Party Asks About Warde:**

*"Warde was like this planet. Built up, powerful, untouchable. And then he fell. Not in one moment, not in one battle. It was gradual. Over months, maybe years. He started making choices. Small ones at first. Then bigger ones. And then the choices made him."*

*"We're going to find out what he became. I'm not sure I want to know. But I need to know. That's why I'm going."*

## ATMOSPHERE NOTES

### Running This Level

- **Pace:** Slow, deliberate. This is not a race—it is a moment of commitment.
- **Tone:** Heavy, respectful, solemn.
- **Player Engagement:** This level is about emotional investment, not action. The players should feel the weight of what they are about to do.
- **Rising Tension:** The stalker's warning, the sealed door, the darkness beyond—all of these build tension without requiring combat or skill checks.
- **Narrative Importance:** The party will remember this moment. This is where they chose to descend.

### Music Suggestions

- Slow, ambient music with low strings or drones
- Subtle sounds of dripping water and distant wind
- Faint, almost inaudible low tones
- Minimalist, atmospheric pieces that create tension without drama

### Lighting Suggestions

- Dim, warm amber light in the basement
- Flickering chemical lights
- Pools of shadow in corners
- Cool, pale light from glow rods once the party enters the passage
