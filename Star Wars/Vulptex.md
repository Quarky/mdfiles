# Vulptex
**Role:** Tracker / Scout (Utility)
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vulptex)
**Type:** Minion
**Characteristics:** Brawn 1, Agility 3, Intellect 2, Cunning 3, Willpower 2, Presence 2
**Skills:** Perception 2, Stealth 2, Survival 2, Vigilance 2
**Talents:** Keen Senses (adds Boost die to Perception checks for hearing and smell)
**Abilities:** Crystalline Fur (adds Setback die to attacks targeting the vulptex at medium range or greater)
**Soak:** 1
**Wound Threshold:** 6
**Equipment:** Bite (Brawl; Damage 2; Crit 5; Range [Engaged])
**Picture:** [Vulptex Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vulptex?file=Vulptex_SWCT.png)

These small, fox-like creatures are native to the mineral planet of Crait, where their crystalline fur provides camouflage. A vulptex is an excellent tracker, able to follow a scent through the densest undergrowth and alert its master to hidden dangers. They are curious and friendly by nature, often forming deep emotional bonds with a single individual.
