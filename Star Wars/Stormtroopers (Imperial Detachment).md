# Stormtroopers (Imperial Detachment)

**Type:** Minion (groups of 3)  
**Role:** Imperial enforcers  
**Location:** Quolas (optional encounter)  
**Personality:** Faceless, disciplined, loyal to the Empire.

## GM Notes
- Use the standard stormtrooper profile from the Force and Destiny Core Rulebook.
- They follow Lieutenant Devrays’ orders without question.
- If the PCs are identified as Force‑sensitive, stormtroopers will use lethal force.
