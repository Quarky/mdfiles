# Tarvix
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 3, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 1, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 2, Brawl 1, Perception 2
**Talents:** None
**Abilities:** Arboreal Creature (ignores difficult terrain from trees and branches), Silhouette 2
**Soak:** 3
**Wound Threshold:** 12
**Equipment:** Claws and teeth (Brawl; Damage 5; Crit 4; Range [Engaged])
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tarvix)

The tarvix is the sturdy, dependable workhorse of the Sathari, domesticated for millennia as a riding and pack animal[reference:2]. Its squat head features four small eyes, though it relies more on its powerful sense of smell to navigate the dense forest undergrowth[reference:3]. Covered in a thick coat of dark bristles, the tarvix is tough, sure-footed, and capable of scaling sheer tree trunks as easily as it can walk on the ground.
