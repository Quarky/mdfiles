# Puffer Pig
**Role:** Foraging / Ore Detection (Utility)
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Puffer_pig)
**Type:** Minion
**Characteristics:** Brawn 1, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 3, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Perception 2
**Talents:** None
**Abilities:** Ore Sniffer (adds Boost die to checks to locate valuable minerals or hidden caches), Defensive Inflation (as a reaction, may double Soak until start of next turn)
**Soak:** 1 (3 when inflated)
**Wound Threshold:** 5
**Picture:** [Puffer Pig Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Puffer_pig?file=Puffer_pig_SWCT.png)

This timid, rotund mammal is prized by miners and scavengers for its incredible sense of smell, which can sniff out valuable ores and underground streams. The puffer pig's skin can be inflated to twice its volume, making it appear larger and providing a brief burst of protection. It is entirely non-combative and prefers to flee from danger.
