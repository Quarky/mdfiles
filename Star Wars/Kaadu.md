# Kaadu
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 4, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 3, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 2, Brawl 1, Perception 1, Survival 2
**Talents:** Aquatic, Domesticable, Trained Mount, Swift
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2
**Soak:** 3
**Wound Threshold:** 18
**Equipment:** Bite (Brawn; Damage 6; Crit 4; Pierce 1)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaadu)

These large, duck-billed reptilians are swift on land and in the water, making them perfectly suited for Arbooine's river-rich forests[reference:8]. Kaadu are often domesticated as mounts, valued for their speed and loyalty. In the wild, they travel in herds, using numbers and their powerful legs to escape predators or drive off threats.

# Kaadu
**Role:** Swift Mount / Water Crossing
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaadu)
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 3, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 2, Brawl 1, Perception 1, Survival 2
**Talents:** Domesticable
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2; Aquatic (can move at normal speed in water)
**Soak:** 3
**Wound Threshold:** 15
**Equipment:** Bite (Brawl; Damage 5; Crit 4; Range [Engaged])
**Picture:** [Kaadu Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaadu?file=Kaadu_DDB.jpg)

The elegant and swift kaadu is a common mount among swamp-dwelling species like the Gungans. It is an excellent swimmer and can navigate rivers and lakes that would slow other mounts to a crawl. A kaadu is loyal, easy to train, and forms a strong emotional bond with its rider.
