# Mudhorn
**Type:** Nemesis
**Characteristics:** Brawn 6, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 3, Perception 2, Resilience 3
**Talents:** Adversary 1, Durable 2, Feral Strength 2, Knockdown
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2
**Soak:** 7
**Wound Threshold:** 30
**Equipment:** Horns (Brawl; Damage 9; Crit 3; Knockdown, Pierce 2) 
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mudhorn)

A massive, shaggy quadruped, the mudhorn is a solitary creature that wallows in the muddy pools of Arbooine's forests. Known for its immense strength and a devastating horn thrust, it is highly territorial and will charge at any perceived threat with relentless fury[reference:4]. Its thick hide provides natural armor, making it a formidable challenge for even well-armed hunters[reference:5].
