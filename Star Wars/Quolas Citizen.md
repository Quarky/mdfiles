# Quolas Citizen

**Type:** Minion (group only)  
**Role:** Downtrodden inhabitants  
**Location:** Throughout Quolas  
**Personality:** Fearful, wary of strangers, but retain a spark of pride. They avoid eye contact with guards.

## GM Notes
- Citizens will not openly oppose Marcolf unless the PCs inspire them or the resistance launches an attack.
- They can be armed as a mob (use stats below) but have little combat training.
- If the PCs help them (e.g., stopping the pillar destruction), citizens become more willing to share information.
