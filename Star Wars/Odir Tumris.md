# Odir Tumris

**Role:** Patriarch of the Tumris clan  
**Species:** Sathari (aging, scarred plumage)  
**Location:** Tumris district  
**Voice / Demeanor:** Bitter, ambitious, speaks in short, clipped sentences. He eyes everyone as potential tools.

## Personality & Motivation
Odir is consumed by his family’s lost chance at ruling Quolas. He blames Warde and Marcolf equally, and he dreams of seizing power for himself. He is cunning and patient, but his hatred makes him reckless when victory seems close. He will ally with anyone – even the PCs – if it helps him depose Marcolf, but he will betray them the moment he sees an opportunity to claim the reeve’s throne.

## GM Notes
- He can be convinced to join an assault, but he will try to turn the mobs to his own ends.
- If the PCs kill Marcolf, Odir may declare himself reeve, leading to a new conflict.
- He knows Warde went to Cato Neimoidia but will only share that information if it serves his interests.
