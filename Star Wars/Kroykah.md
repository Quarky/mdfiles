# Kroykah
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 4, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Perception 3, Stealth 2, Survival 2, Vigilance 2
**Talents:** Adversary 1
**Abilities:** Silhouette 1, Pack Hunter (grants Boost dice to allies' combat checks)
**Soak:** 4
**Wound Threshold:** 24
**Equipment:** Claws and Bite (Brawl; Damage 8; Crit 4; Vicious 1)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kroykah)

Kroykah are predatory, repto-mammals known for their cunning and their terrifying, high-pitched calls that echo through the forests where they hunt. They are pack hunters, using their numbers and a deadly combination of speed and strength to overwhelm prey. Their distinct shrieks are used for communication, scouting, and corralling prey into ambushes, making them a frightening opponent in the dark.
