# Bonzami
**Type:** Nemesis
**Characteristics:** Brawn 7, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 3, Willpower 3, Presence 1
**Skills:** Brawl 3, Perception 3, Resilience 3, Stealth 1, Vigilance 2
**Talents:** Adversary 2, Durable 2, Feral Strength 3, Knockdown
**Abilities:** Silhouette 3
**Soak:** 8
**Wound Threshold:** 40
**Equipment:** Claws (Brawl; Damage 12; Crit 2; Concussive 1, Pierce 2, Vicious 2)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bonzami)

An apex predator of terrifying proportions, the bonzami stands taller than a bantha and is covered in matted, snow-white fur that allows it to stalk prey silently in the forest twilight. Its most distinguishing feature is its massive, spiraling horn, which can punch through armor with ease. A bonzami is a once-in-a-campaign threat that will test even the most experienced party[reference:9].
