# Jyl Korlis

**Role:** Farmer / Room‑renter  
**Species:** Sathari (middle‑aged, plumage dusty)  
**Location:** Near the exterior of the tree‑city, by the mushroom farms  
**Voice / Demeanor:** Practical, hospitable to paying customers, but weary of Marcolf’s taxes.

## Personality & Motivation
Jyl is a hardworking farmer who grows fungi on the tree’s exterior. She has suffered under Marcolf’s rising taxes and now rents out empty warehouse space to travelers to make ends meet. She is not a revolutionary, but she sympathises with the resistance. She will offer shelter at 10 credits per room per day and can provide local gossip and news.

## GM Notes
- If the PCs mention Elder Jairo (from the optional traveler encounter), Jyl becomes much more trusting and may offer a discount or free information.
- She can be convinced to back the resistance if the PCs demonstrate a good chance of success.
