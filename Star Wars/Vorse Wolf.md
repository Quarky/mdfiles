# Vorse Wolf
**Role:** Combat Tracker (Aggressive)
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vorse_Wolf)
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 4, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 3, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Brawl 2, Perception 2, Stealth 3, Survival 3
**Talents:** Pack Instincts (Friendly vorse wolves increase each other's damage by 1 when engaged with the same target)
**Abilities:** Silhouette 1; Force Perception (sense Force-sensitive beings within medium range)
**Soak:** 4
**Wound Threshold:** 14
**Equipment:** Claws (Brawl; Damage 6; Crit 3; Range [Engaged]; Pierce 1)
**Picture:** [Vorse Wolf Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vorse_Wolf?file=Vorse_wolf.png)

The vorse wolf is a large, lupine predator feared for its cunning and its connection to the Force. These beasts can sense Force-sensitive beings and are drawn to them, for good or ill. A vorse wolf bonded to a Jedi is a formidable ally in combat, using its pack instincts and keen senses to track and harry enemies.
