# Nexu
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 4, Agility 4, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 1, Cool 2, Stealth 2, Vigilance 3
**Talents:** None
**Abilities:** Fierce Pounce (gains Knockdown on its next attack after using the Aim maneuver)[reference:23]
**Soak:** 4
**Wound Threshold:** 18
**Equipment:** Bite (Brawl; Damage 7; Crit 3; Ensnare 1, Pierce 1)[reference:24]
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nexu)

A sleek, agile feline predator from the planet Cholganna, the nexu has become an alpha predator on many worlds[reference:25]. It is covered in a leathery hide and has a defensive mane of sharp quills, but its most dangerous feature is its four red eyes, which grant it exceptional vision. Its secondary pair of eyes can track prey by body heat using infrared, making it a terrifyingly effective hunter in the dark[reference:26].
