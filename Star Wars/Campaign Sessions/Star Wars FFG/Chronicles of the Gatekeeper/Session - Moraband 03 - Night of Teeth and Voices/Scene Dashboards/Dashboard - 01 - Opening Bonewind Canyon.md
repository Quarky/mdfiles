## GM fast scan
- Resume at the canyon entrance. The route is not merely difficult ground; it is a place that turns speech into accusation. Wind passes through thousands of round holes in the stone, producing tones like distant voices.
- The canyon walls rise until the red sky becomes a narrow wound overhead. Wind moves through the rock in long, breathy notes. At first the sound resembles mourning. Then one voice separates from the rest and speaks a name belonging to someone in the party.
- Ask who answers. If nobody does, the canyon uses a phrase from the previous session. If someone answers, a second voice replies from farther ahead in that character’s own voice.
- The party needs shelter before full darkness. Offer an upper ledge that is defensible but hard to reach, a ruin with intact walls but only one exit, and a low basin with water-stained stone but many approaches. Survival identifies tradeoffs; it does not reveal a correct answer.
