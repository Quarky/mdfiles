## GM fast scan
- At the landing basin, the ship should bear the consequences of absence: dust accumulation, repairs completed or worsened, anxious companions, and possibly evidence that something approached but did not enter.
- As the ship lifts, ask each player what their character watches until the planet disappears. End with Moraband’s red pall falling away and ordinary starlight returning—not as proof the darkness is gone, but as proof they can leave it.
