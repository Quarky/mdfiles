## GM fast scan
- Warde leads toward the central chamber, but the characters may stop him or search. Present three discoveries:
- The prophecy wall: hundreds of future events carved into plaster. Many are crossed out. Some failed. Some happened differently. Warde labels these “errors of interpretation,” but the evidence shows mutable outcomes.
- The camp datapad: fragmented entries alternate between precise predictions and paranoid revisions. Several refer to the PCs before they were born or before they began the quest. Others predict companions who are not present, deaths that did not occur, and choices the party already refused.
- The mercy cache: supplies Warde gathered for visitors he claimed he intended to kill. It includes water, medical packs, and several sizes of breathing masks. He prepared to save the people he believed would execute him.
- These are not locked behind rolls. Checks provide dates, authorship, and additional leverage. The central conclusion must be visible: Warde’s foresight contains truth, but his belief that one ending is compulsory is unsupported.
- The gatekeeper confronts him here if his identity is known:
- “You made me to teach discernment.”
