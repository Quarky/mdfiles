## GM fast scan
- Gatekeeper, PCs, any companions/animals already aboard.
- Uncertainty, not immediate danger.
- If nobody activates it, a reasonable companion can ask whether the holocron knows another archive. The lead should emerge from the characters' need, not from a disembodied quest marker.
- The ship is finally quiet enough for small sounds to matter again: cooling metal, a loose utensil ticking against a galley surface, an animal shifting in its enclosure. On the navigation display, the route field remains blank. The recovered crystal has given you history—but not a way to Moraband.
- Pause. Let one player decide what their character does before the gatekeeper appears.
- Cato Neimoidia falls behind you, but the next course cannot be plotted. Every useful route calculation ends at the same missing point. The holocron rests nearby, apparently silent while the crew argues over what can still be known.
- Let the gatekeeper listen before speaking. It should respond to a question, not interrupt with a prepared speech.

## Checks / mechanics to have ready
- Allow one downtime action per PC. Each action may create a specific later advantage.
- Plot the route. Resolve the Average Astrogation check and record the travel time/consequence.
- [MODULE] Missing Moraband data, the gatekeeper's conditional Coruscant suggestion, the Delriss lead, sanctuary purpose, Warde's prior visit, known system coordinates, Average Astrogation, and travel consequences.

## Branch / failure-forward matrix

| Trigger / branch | GM response |
|---|---|
| **If the crew is exhausted** | The ship is finally quiet enough for small sounds to matter again: cooling metal, a loose utensil ticking against a galley surface, an animal shifting in its enclosure. On the navigation display, the route field remains blank. |
| **If the crew is energized** | Cato Neimoidia falls behind you, but the next course cannot be plotted. Every useful route calculation ends at the same missing point. |
| **If the crew distrusts the gatekeeper** | Delriss appears in the navicomputer as an ordinary, neglected system: old coordinates, no current traffic advisories, no reason for anyone to visit. The gatekeeper offers only one verifiable claim at first: a hidden Jedi sanctuary once existed beneath the ocean of its habitable moon. |
| **If the party refuses Delriss** | Respect the refusal long enough for the choice to be real. Other research can confirm three facts: Moraband information has been systematically erased; common archives do not contain a legal route; and Delriss is the only concrete location tied to Warde that the gatekeeper can name. |
