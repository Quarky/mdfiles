## GM fast scan
- Your lamps cut across a dark basin. Curved shelves descend beneath the water. Rotted cases drift open when disturbed, releasing scraps too damaged to read. The air smells of salt, wet insulation, and old paper. Every surviving object has slid toward the same low wall.
- Weak emergency strips divide the archive into islands of amber light. They do not illuminate the bottom. A terminal wakes just long enough to display a broken Jedi seal, then fades.
- Light returns unevenly—and a blue-white arc snaps through the water along the low side of the room. Somewhere under the surface, a damaged circuit has energized the flood.
- Add: “Each slow flex of the structure sends a new rain of dust and corrosion from the ceiling.”
- Show the 360 view and pause. Let players choose where to look before calling for a check.
- State the stakes. Most records are destroyed; careful work can still save individual pieces.
- Divide tasks. Search, stabilize, power/isolate circuits, watch structure, and protect recovered works.

## Checks / mechanics to have ready
- [MODULE] Hard Perception or Knowledge (Education) recovers meaningful material. Expand that into a table procedure:
- One successful Hard check recovers one work, plus another for strong Advantage/Triumph at GM discretion.
- At Power 2+, the terminal is easier to read but current leaks into the water. An Average Mechanics/Computers action isolates it. Failure still gets the clue but inflicts strain, damages a tool, or advances Instability.
- [MODULE] Ruined library, Hard Perception/Knowledge search, five named example works, absence of route data, hangar discovery, and automatic Delta-7 inference for skilled pilots/navigators.

## Pressure / hazards / clocks
- Let the party choose whether to continue salvage. Explain rising clocks before they decide.
- End with an external pressure wave. Water moves through the room without a local cause; smaller life flees.
- Failure recovers a damaged work or critical clue but costs time, gear condition, strain, or clock pressure.
- At Power 2+, the terminal is easier to read but current leaks into the water. An Average Mechanics/Computers action isolates it. Failure still gets the clue but inflicts strain, damages a tool, or advances Instability.
- At Awareness 4, it is followed by a glancing scrape and Instability +1.
- Tell them what is still possible: another work, a cleaner copy, or more context—not the Moraband route. Each additional substantial search after the clue advances Awareness or Instability once, with a visible warning first.

## Clues / discoveries / reveals
- **The negative clue:** Say this plainly after a fair search: Whatever the archive once knew about Moraband is gone. You find references to navigation storage and small-craft maintenance, but no intact route solution here.
- **Archive echoes:** Use one optional atmospheric recording: These are additions. None should provide a new secret history that overrides the published adventure.
- **If the party tries to remove the whole archive:** Most of it is physically ruined. They can save selected works and perhaps one sealed case.
