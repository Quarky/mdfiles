## GM fast scan
- The water presses from every direction, turning each movement deliberate. Your lamps illuminate suspended particles, shell fragments, and the rough growth covering the station. Beyond the light, the ridge falls away into an abyss with no visible bottom.
- A vibration travels through the anchor struts. The entire facility shifts—not enough to tear free, but enough for a slow cloud of silt to rise from every contact point at once.
- Let the party inspect before rolling. Name collar, airlock, conduit, rent, and supports.
- Ask where the ship remains. Docked, hovering nearby, or at safer depth each creates a different escape condition.
- Resolve one forensic read. Different skills reveal different truths; do not make characters repeat the same check.
- Choose entry route. Explain visible risks before the roll.
- Resolve docking or exposed transfer. Failure changes condition but does not bar entry.

## Checks / mechanics to have ready
- [MODULE] A non-aquatic craft requires Hard Piloting (Space) to dock. The upgraded vessel's aquatic controls help hold position but do not eliminate the precision of aligning a damaged collar. Use Hard when the collar is flexing, Average if the crew first stabilizes or repairs it.
- [MODULE] Average Mechanics or Skulduggery, or Daunting Athletics.
- Advantage isolates the outer door and protects the return route.
- Threat jams the outer door partly open.
- Triumph restores one local pressure indicator and grants warning before later failure.
- The conduit is narrow, quiet, and partially occupied by scuttlers. Average Coordination is appropriate for a character in ordinary gear; bulky equipment must be passed separately. Success reaches Level One maintenance behind a sealed bulkhead. Failure costs time or leaves an item temporarily snagged. Despair should split gear from characters, not arbitrarily split the party.
- Make scope visible: they can brace one support or improve one route, not restore a three-level pressure complex during a visit. A successful Hard Mechanics check may reduce Instability by 1 once or protect a chosen zone. It also takes time and may increase Awareness through vibration.

## Pressure / hazards / clocks
- Open or bypass the pressure boundary. Record outer-door state, water intrusion, and route markers.
- One character can establish the basic visible truth without a roll: corrosion and pressure alone do not explain the largest rents.
- Failure opens it after delay; advance Instability or admit a cold stream.
- Triumph restores one local pressure indicator and grants warning before later failure.
- If befriended or simply not harmed, mark Scuttlers Tolerant. During escape they reveal a duct or flee before a dangerous impact. If attacked, mark Scuttlers Scattered; their movement later complicates one path.
- Make scope visible: they can brace one support or improve one route, not restore a three-level pressure complex during a visit. A successful Hard Mechanics check may reduce Instability by 1 once or protect a chosen zone. It also takes time and may increase Awareness through vibration.
