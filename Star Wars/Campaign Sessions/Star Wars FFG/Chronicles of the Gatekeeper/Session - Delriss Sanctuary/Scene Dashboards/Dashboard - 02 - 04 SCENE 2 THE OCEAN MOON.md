## GM fast scan
- The leviathan is not waiting beside the sanctuary. At Awareness 0 it is far away. Active scans, power, and vibration give it reasons to turn toward the ridge. Smaller creatures notice its movement first.
- Delriss turns below as a scarred, inhospitable world. Ancient extraction cuts still mark its surface, but no lights answer from them. No traffic control hails you. No transponder crosses the sensor board. The system feels less abandoned than passed over.
- Allow routine scans to confirm [MODULE] a rocky world with a Type IV atmosphere, exhausted ancient mines, and no settlement.
- Beyond the planet, one barren moon hangs like chipped stone. The other catches the star in cloud and blue water. No continent breaks its surface. Weather curls across an ocean that circles the entire globe.
- Passive sensors show abundant animal life and no sentient population.
- The ship drops through cloud into hard rain and wind. When the cloud finally breaks, water fills every horizon. Broad shapes surface and vanish in the swell. Far below, shadows move that are wider than the vessel—and one of them does not move with the others.
- Do not identify the shadow. If measured, it dives below safe sensor depth before a clean reading.
