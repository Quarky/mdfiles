## GM fast scan
- Run ten to twenty minutes of investigation before the pursuers arrive. Give the party three points of interest:
- A toppled guardian statue whose face was deliberately removed. Beneath it is a narrow hollow containing a corroded travel token from a pre-Rule-of-Two Sith pilgrim. It is not powerful, but touching it produces the certainty that the palace lies east—even though the physical evidence points north.
- A campsite that appears recent until examined. Ash lies in a fire pit, a blanket remains folded, and a cup contains a thin film of water. No footprints lead in or out. This is Moraband constructing the idea of safety from the party’s expectations.
- A ridge overlooking the ship. Fresh scoring on the stone marks where a probe or rifle rest was placed minutes ago. The pursuers have already observed the landing if the Pursuit clock is high; otherwise this belongs to an automated scout.
