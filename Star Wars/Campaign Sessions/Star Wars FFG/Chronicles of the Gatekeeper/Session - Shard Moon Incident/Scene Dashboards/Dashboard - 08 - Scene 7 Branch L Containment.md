## GM fast scan
- Containment cells line a humid service chamber. Transparent tubes once carried nutrient fluid. Many are dry. Some are not. The ceiling has too many maintenance cavities and too many of them show fresh movement.
- Branch L creatures were designed to weaken captives and make ritual/conditioning results easier to control. Run them as ambush hazards that latch, drain, and separate rather than as bags of hit points.
- Players can solve the encounter through combat, fire/heat control, sealing ducts, environmental manipulation, bait, chemical knowledge, or restoring a containment field. A technical solution may be cleaner than a fight but can raise Wakefulness.
- The leech program's notes reveal the researchers' underlying fear: they could not reliably control a strong Force-sensitive subject while that subject retained full access to the Force. Suppression was a workaround, not mastery.
- This undermines Vael Serrin's later claim that the program was close to success.
- If leeches escape this zone, note it. Do not automatically turn them into a future campaign problem, but give the party a chance to recognize that leaving a live containment breach has consequences.
