## GM fast scan
- Let exploration establish three stories at once:
- people once tried to leave in a hurry;
- security tried to stop some of them;
- something biological escaped anyway.
- At the evacuation lane, the party finds abandoned cases, restraint hardware, and a wall where emergency directions were overwritten by a red restricted-access symbol. A careful search can distinguish ordinary staff evacuation from captive movement.
- At the failed checkpoint, a security station contains damaged authorization logs. Technical success can recover a partial designation: ASH / WARD-... or a Branch letter. Threat can awaken a local alarm while still granting the data.
- Run the hound as a tracker, not a random monster. It is drawn toward Force activity and old conditioning cues. It tries to isolate a straggler, test defenses, then withdraw and follow if hurt. If captured or examined, it bears signs of surgical modification and an old control implant.
