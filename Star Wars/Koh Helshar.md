# Koh Helshar

**Role:** Matriarch of the Helshar clan  
**Species:** Sathari (aged, plumage muted)  
**Location:** Helshar district, upper levels  
**Voice / Demeanor:** Weary, measured, speaks softly but with authority. She has seen too much bloodshed.

## Personality & Motivation
Koh has led the Helshar family through the feud with the Tumris clan and the oppression of Marcolf. She is tired of conflict and secretly hopes for peace, but she believes the best way to survive is to endure and wait. She loves her son Pathran but fears his resistance will bring destruction. If approached with a plan to depose Marcolf without mass casualties, she may lend quiet support.

## GM Notes
- She will not openly rebel but may provide supplies or information.
- She remembers Warde and can confirm he went to Cato Neimoidia (requires a Charm check to convince her to recall).
- If the PCs broker a truce between the clans, she gives her full support.
