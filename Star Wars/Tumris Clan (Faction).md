# Tumris Clan (Faction)

**Type:** Noble family  
**Leader:** Odir Tumris  
**Allies:** None  
**Rivals:** Helshar clan

## Overview
The Tumris clan is the Helshar’s traditional enemy. Odir is ambitious and sees Marcolf as an obstacle to his own rise. He will cooperate with anyone to depose the reeve, but his ultimate goal is to install himself as the new ruler. He is a dangerous and unpredictable ally.

## Key Info for GMs
- Odir may betray the PCs if victory seems assured.
- The clan has fewer resources than the Helshars but more militant members.
- Brokering a truce between the clans is possible but requires convincing Odir to set aside his ambition (very hard).
