# Mynock
**Type:** Minion
**Characteristics:** Brawn 1, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 1, Presence 1
**Skills:** Coordination 2, Perception 2, Stealth 3
**Talents:** None
**Abilities:** Silhouette 0, Energy Parasite (can drain system strain from vehicles), Vacuum Dweller (takes no damage from exposure to vacuum)
**Soak:** 1
**Wound Threshold:** 4
**Equipment:** Bite (Brawl; Damage 2; Crit 5; Pierce 1)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mynock)

Though typically found in space, mynocks are silicon-based parasites that can be a nuisance in any dark, enclosed space, including deep forest caves[reference:38]. These flapping, leathery creatures are drawn to energy sources and will latch onto power cables or even starships to feed. Swarms of mynocks are not individually deadly but can cause significant system strain and become a major problem in large numbers[reference:39].
