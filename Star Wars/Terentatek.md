# Terentatek
**Type:** Nemesis
**Characteristics:** Brawn 8, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 4, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 5, Perception 3, Resilience 4, Vigilance 3
**Talents:** Adversary 3, Durable 3, Feral Strength 4, Lethal Blows 3, Knockdown
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2, Force Resistance (upgrades difficulty of Force powers targeting it three times), Blood of the Force-Sensitive (Special)
**Soak:** 12
**Wound Threshold:** 60
**Equipment:** Claws and Tusks (Brawl; Damage 14; Crit 2; Concussive 2, Pierce 4, Vicious 4)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Terentatek)

The terentatek is a creature of pure, concentrated dark side energy, a legendary monster that feeds on the blood of Force-sensitives[reference:34]. Resembling a rancor but covered in spines and tusks, a terentatek is more than a beast; it is a dark side vergence made flesh. Its hide is highly resistant to physical and Force attacks, and its very presence is a terror to all who wield the Force, making it a supreme challenge for any group of Force-users[reference:35].
