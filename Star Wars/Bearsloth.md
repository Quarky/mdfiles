# Bearsloth
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 4, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Brawl 3, Cool 3, Stealth 3, Survival 2
**Talents:** Adversary 1
**Abilities:** Arboreal Predator (ignores difficult terrain from trees and branches), Envenomed Bite (Flip a Destiny Point to inflict poison)
**Soak:** 4
**Wound Threshold:** 16
**Equipment:** Claws and fangs (Brawl; Damage 6; Crit 3; Linked 1)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bearsloth)

A terror of the canopy, the bearsloth is a six-limbed, four-eyed nightmare that hunts from the treetops of Arbooine[reference:0]. Their mottled fur provides perfect camouflage kamidst the high branches, allowing them to drop silently onto unsuspecting prey. A bearsloth's real danger, however, is its venomous bite, which weakens and eventually paralyzes its victim[reference:1].
