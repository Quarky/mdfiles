# Quolas Guard Captain

**Type:** Rival  
**Role:** Overseer of guards  
**Location:** Reeve’s manor and primary guard station  
**Personality:** Stern, practical. Some secretly dislike Marcolf but fear his power; others are loyal to him and enjoy their privilege.

## GM Notes
- Guard captains can be negotiated with if the PCs have earned respect (e.g., stopped the Pillar destruction).
- They have the Restraint talent, allowing them to deal Stun damage and Knockdown.
- A captain may be swayed to help the PCs if Marcolf appears weak.
