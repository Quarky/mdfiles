# Quolas Town Watch (Guard Force)

**Type:** City guard / Marcolf’s enforcers  
**Leader:** Gel Marcolf (indirect)  
**Members:** Quolas Guards and Quolas Guard Captains  
**Location:** Throughout Quolas, concentrated at reeve’s manor

## Overview
The Town Watch has swelled under Marcolf’s rule. Guards collect taxes, arrest dissidents, and protect the reeve. Some are loyal to Marcolf, others serve out of fear. They are armed with slugthrowers and venom‑tipped diiros. The force includes ~18 on duty at the manor at any time, plus patrols.

## Key Info for GMs
- Reinforcements arrive 2 rounds after an alarm.
- Guard captains can grant the Restraint ability to nearby minion groups.
- If Marcolf is killed or flees, some guards will surrender or switch sides.
