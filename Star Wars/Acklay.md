# Acklay
**Type:** Nemesis
**Characteristics:** Brawn 6, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 3, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 4, Perception 3, Stealth 2
**Talents:** Adversary 2, Feral Strength 4, Knockdown, Lethal Blows 2
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2
**Soak:** 7
**Wound Threshold:** 40
**Equipment:** Claws and Bite (Brawl; Damage 10; Crit 2; Pierce 3, Vicious 2)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acklay)

An amphibious reptilian crustacean of nightmare, the acklay is a six-legged, razor-clawed terror used as an execution beast in the Geonosian arenas[reference:32]. Over three meters tall, its hard, leathery carapace protects it from most attacks, while its sharp claws and serrated teeth can rend through metal[reference:33]. An acklay is a brutal, relentless predator that can attack from land or water, making it a terrifying opponent.
