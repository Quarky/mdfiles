# Lieutenant Salial Devrays (Optional Imperial Officer)

**Role:** Imperial Officer  
**Species:** Human  
**Location:** Arrives at Quolas with stormtroopers (optional encounter)  
**Voice / Demeanor:** Sharp, efficient, suspicious. She sees rebels everywhere.

## Personality & Motivation
Devrays is a by‑the‑book Imperial officer who has been tasked with rooting out Rebel activity on Arbooine. She is not inherently cruel but believes wholeheartedly in the Empire’s mission. She is suspicious of off‑worlders and will interrogate the PCs if she encounters them. She prefers to avoid violence if she can achieve her goals through intimidation, but she will not hesitate to order an attack.

## GM Notes
- This encounter is optional (page 34). Use it to add Imperial pressure or to foreshadow later events.
- Devrays leads nine stormtroopers and a stormtrooper sergeant.
- If the PCs are revealed as Force‑sensitive, she will prioritise capturing or eliminating them.
