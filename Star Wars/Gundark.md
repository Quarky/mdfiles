# Gundark
**Type:** Nemesis
**Characteristics:** Brawn 7, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 4, Perception 2, Resilience 4
**Talents:** Adversary 1, Feral Strength 3, Knockdown, Lethal Blows 1
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2
**Soak:** 8
**Wound Threshold:** 32
**Equipment:** Fists (Brawl; Damage 8; Crit 3; Knockdown, Concussive 1)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gundark)

Few beasts are as feared for their raw, brute strength as the gundark, a creature known for its ferocity and four powerful arms[reference:10]. Standing between one and two and a half meters tall, these mammals are covered in brownish-gray fur and driven by a relentless aggression that makes them a threat to anything in their territory[reference:11]. A gundark's visage is so synonymous with power that a common galactic phrase for strength is to say someone looks “strong enough to pull the ears off a gundark”[reference:12].
