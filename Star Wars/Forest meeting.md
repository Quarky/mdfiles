# GM Guide: Wandering Merchants (Jairo Clan)

This encounter can happen while the PCs travel through the forests of Arbooine toward Quolas. The Jairo are a nomadic Sathari family who trade goods and news. They are peaceful, wary of outsiders, and can become valuable allies.

---

## When to Use

- After the PCs make a navigation check (success or failure).
- As a “calm before the storm” moment before the bearsloth ambush.
- When the party is low on strain or supplies – the Jairo can offer aid.

---

## Encounter Setup

Read or paraphrase:

> *“A rustle in the undergrowth ahead makes you pause. Four Sathari emerge from between the trees — their plumage a mix of russet and gold, with long facial feathers that twitch as they survey you. They carry woven packs and hunting spears. The eldest, a female with deep‑set eyes and a weathered beak, raises a hand in greeting.*
>
> *‘Travelers,’ she says in Basic, her voice raspy but calm. ‘We rarely see your kind so deep in the wilds. The forest has ears, and not all who wander are lost — but some are.’ She glances at your weapons, then back to your faces. ‘What brings you to Arbooine?’”*

## Conversation Flow

### Opening (Elder Jairo)

| PC Action / Question | Elder Jairo’s Response |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| “We’re looking for Gel Marcolf.” | *Spits.* “The tyrant. He calls himself reeve, but he is a parasite. You’d do well to avoid him.” |
| “We’re just passing through.” | *Nods.* “Then pass quickly. The forest is safer than his shadow.” |
| “We want to help the people of Quolas.” | *Eyes narrow.* “Many have said that. Few have tried. Fewer have lived. But… there is a young one, Pathran Helshar. He speaks of resistance. Find him — if you dare.” |
| “Who are you?” | “We are the Jairo. We keep to the wilds, as our ancestors did. The trees do not demand taxes, and the animals do not lie.” |

### Follow‑up Questions

| PC asks | Information given |
|---------|-------------------|
| “What do you know about Marcolf?” | “He came with a Jedi, long ago. The Jedi left. Marcolf stayed. Now he rules with fear. His guards take what they want.” |
| “Is there a resistance?” | “Pathran Helshar — the youngest of the Helshar clan. He and his friends meet beneath the roots. They speak of revolution, but they are children playing at war.” |
| “What about the Jedi who came with him?” | “Warde? A fool. Or a liar. He promised peace, then disappeared. Now his name is cursed.” |
| “How can we enter Quolas safely?” | “The main gate is watched day and night. But there is a cave — a root‑tunnel — that leads into the lower city. Hunters use it. Dangerous, but unguarded.” |

### Interacting with Taro

If a PC approaches Taro:

> *Taro grips his diiro.* “Step back. I’ve seen off‑worlders before — they bring only trouble.”

- **Charm (Average ◊◊)** or **Cool (Hard ◊◊◊)** calms him.
- If a Force‑sensitive PC succeeds on a **Discipline (Average)** check: “You feel a faint warmth. Taro’s hand relaxes, confusion in his eyes.”
- Taro then mutters: “Fine. But one wrong move…”

### Interacting with Lira

> *Lira peeks from behind Elder Jairo. “Is it true you can make fire from your hands? A trader told me off‑worlders can do that.”*

- A PC can demonstrate a small Force effect (e.g., lighting a fire with a spark). Lira gasps and grins.
- This earns **+5 XP for roleplaying** and makes the entire Jairo clan **Friendly**.

## Player Actions & Consequences

| Action | Checks | Outcome |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Attack the Jairo | Combat (they flee) | **5+ Conflict**. Jairo become hostile; word spreads. |
| Offer food/medicine | Give supplies worth 50+ credits | Jairo become **Friendly**. Free root‑tunnel info + 1 venom dose. |
| Ask about resistance | **Charm (Hard)** or **Negotiation (Average)** | Elder reveals Pathran’s name and location. |
| Sense Taro’s Force sensitivity | **Discipline (Average)** or Sense power | Taro admits he “knows things before they happen.” Future plot hook. |
| Threaten them | **Coercion (Average)** | They reveal root‑tunnel but lie about bearsloth danger (ambush). **2 Conflict**. |
| Be kind to Lira | Roleplay | Jairo become Friendly; Lira gives a good luck charm (Boost to one check). |

---

## Rewards

| Outcome | Reward |
|---------|--------|
| Friendly Jairo | Root‑tunnel secret, +Boost to next Survival check, contact Jyl Korlis |
| Trade with Jairo | 2 doses bearsloth venom (150cr) |
| Save them from a threat (optional) | 10 XP, Jairo become permanent allies |
| Heal or gift supplies | Jairo may later appear to help the PCs (e.g., during the final battle in Quolas) |

---

## GM Tips

- **Use the Jairo to foreshadow** Marcolf’s cruelty and Pathran’s resistance.
- **Keep the tone warm but wary** – they are not enemies, but they have been hurt by outsiders before.
- **Reward compassion** – helping the Jairo should feel meaningful. The root‑tunnel secret is especially valuable.
- **If the PCs are in a hurry**, the Jairo can quickly give directions and a minor boon without a long scene.
- **Consider a follow‑up** – if the PCs later need to leave Quolas secretly, the Jairo may meet them again.

---

## Sample Dialogue (Short Exchange)

> **Elder Jairo:** “You’re not with the reeve’s men — too ragged for that, and you don’t smell of fear. So, wanderers, what business do you have in Quolas?”  
> **PC:** “We’re looking for someone. A man named Gel Marcolf.”  
> **Elder Jairo:** *Spits.* “The tyrant. You’d do well to avoid him. But if you must go, take the low path by the river. The guards watch the bridge.”  
> **PC:** “Thank you. Can we offer you anything in return?”  
> **Elder Jairo:** *Smiles, showing worn teeth.* “Kindness is rare. Share our fire tonight, and we will talk more.”


# Jairo Clan Camp – Night Encounter

If the PCs accept the Jairo’s invitation to share their fire, the encounter extends into the evening. This is an opportunity for roleplay, recovery, and gathering information. The camp is a temporary shelter, built among the massive roots of an elrit tree.

---

## Camp Description

The Jairo’s camp is tucked into a natural hollow formed by the exposed roots of an ancient elrit tree. A small stream trickles nearby. A low fire burns in a ring of blackened stones, its smoke curling up through the canopy.

> *“The roots of the elrit tree arch overhead like the ribs of a great beast, forming a natural shelter. A small fire crackles in the centre, casting dancing shadows on the woven mats spread across the ground. Leather packs hang from low branches, and the scent of roasting meat mixes with the earthy smell of damp moss. Somewhere in the darkness above, a night bird calls.”*

---

## Time Progression

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| Dusk | Arrival, introductions, sharing of food |
| Evening | Storytelling, trade, questions and answers |
| Late night | Camp settles; watch is set |
| Night | Random events (optional) |
| Dawn | Departure, final gifts or warnings |

---

## Activities Throughout the Night

### 1. Sharing the Meal (Dusk)

The Jairo offer food to the PCs. See **What food would they have in camp** (previous output). If the PCs share their own rations, the Jairo become more friendly.

**Mechanical benefit:** Eating a full meal removes 2 strain from each PC.

### 2. Storytelling (Evening)

Elder Jairo tells a story about Arbooine’s past. Read or paraphrase:

> *“Long ago, before the off‑worlders came, the Sathari built no cities. We lived in the trees, and the trees provided. Then the Republic came with its promises and its wars. They brought the Jedi — a man named Warde — and they brought the tyrant Marcolf. Now the trees weep.”*

**Player participation:** A PC can share a story from their own past. A **Charm (Average)** or **Cool (Easy)** check impresses the Jairo, granting +Boost to future social checks with them.

### 3. Trading (Evening)

The Jairo have goods for trade:

| Item | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Bearsloth venom (2 doses) | 150 credits or trade | Harvested themselves |
| Smoked meat (5 rations) | 25 credits | Keeps for weeks |
| Hand‑woven blanket | 10 credits | Warm, decorative |
| Root‑tunnel map | 50 credits or free with trust | Drawn on bark |

### 4. Questions and Answers

The Jairo answer questions honestly if trust has been earned. See previous output for information they possess.

**New information they might share at night:**

- “The stars here are different from your worlds. We navigate by them. See that cluster? We call it the Hunter’s Spear. Follow it and you will find water.”
- “Marcolf fears the old places. He never enters the root‑tunnels. They say a spirit dwells there — the ghost of a Sathari he murdered. I do not know if it is true, but he believes it.”

### 5. Repairing Gear

Taro has basic leatherworking tools. He can repair worn clothing or straps. A PC can assist with a **Mechanics (Easy)** check to help; success allows Taro to finish repairs overnight, removing 1 setback from the next physical skill check for that PC.

### 6. Medical Aid

Sena knows herbal remedies. If a PC is wounded or poisoned, she can treat them with an **Average (◊◊) Medicine** check. Success removes 2 wounds or 1 strain from one PC. Failure does nothing.

### 7. Setting Watch (Late Night)

The Jairo take turns keeping watch. They invite the PCs to share the duty. A PC who volunteers gains **1 Conflict reduction** (small act of kindness). While on watch, a PC may make **Perception** or **Vigilance** checks to notice any lurking threats.

---

## Random Night Events

Roll 1d10 each hour of night (3–4 hours). On a 1, an event occurs.

| d10 | Event |
|-----|-------|
| 1 | A bearsloth sniffs at the edge of camp. It is curious but not aggressive. Loud noises or fire scare it away. |
| 2 | Strange lights flicker in the distance — glow‑fungus spores. No danger. |
| 3 | A branch snaps nearby. It is only a tarvix passing. |
| 4 | One of the Jairo has a nightmare and cries out. A PC can calm them with **Cool (Easy)** . |
| 5 | Rain begins to fall. The camp stays dry under the roots, but travel in the morning will be muddy (adds ■ to next day’s Survival check). |
| 6 | A rustle in the darkness — a vine cat stalks the camp. It flees if spotted (opposed Perception vs. Stealth). |
| 7 | Nothing — peaceful silence. |
| 8 | Lira wakes and asks for a story from a PC. Good roleplay earns +5 XP. |
| 9 | A shooting star crosses the sky. Elder Jairo interprets it as an omen (GM decides — good or bad). |
| 10 | False alarm — just wind in the leaves. |

---

## Morning Departure (Dawn)

At first light, the Jairo pack their camp. They offer parting gifts if the PCs were kind.

> *“Elder Jairo hands you a small pouch. ‘Elrit nuts. For the road. May the roots guide your feet.’ She nods once, then signals her family. They melt into the trees without a sound.”*

**Parting gifts (if Friendly):**
- 2 elrit nut cakes (each restores 1 strain)
- A small charm made of feather and wood (grants one **Boost** to a single check of the GM’s choice)
- The root‑tunnel map (if not already given)

**If the PCs were hostile or threatening:** The Jairo disappear during the night, leaving the PCs alone and without guidance.

---

## GM Tips

- **Keep the night scene relaxed.** This is a roleplay opportunity, not a combat encounter (unless the random event triggers danger).
- **Use the camp to deepen the world.** The Jairo are the PCs’ first glimpse of Sathari culture. Make them sympathetic.
- **Track time.** If the PCs stay the whole night, they arrive at Quolas the next morning, fully rested.
- **Let the PCs initiate activities.** If they want to trade, ask questions, or help with chores, encourage it.
- **The random events should be atmospheric, not deadly.** The goal is to create tension or wonder, not to kill the party.
