# Handout and Reveal Schedule

| Artifact | Reveal | Keep hidden |
|---|---|---|
| Moraband field observations | After orbital/landing survey | Vergence thresholds and clock triggers |
| Delta-7B evidence | After safe data recovery | Which detail Warde fears most |
| Rival contract or route | When physically acquired or willingly shared | Client agenda and betrayal trigger |
| Contradictory prophecies | After documenting the statues/gallery | Exact redemption bonus |
| Warde datapad entries | After recovery | Which future hooks the GM intends to use |
| Final crystal/holocron | When installed | Long-term teaching schedule |
| NPC dossier/portrait | Once clearly observed or identified | Stats, private agenda, retreat threshold |

Never title visible material “Player Handout,” “GM Notes,” or “generated file.” Put keeper guidance in separate private notes or this schedule.
