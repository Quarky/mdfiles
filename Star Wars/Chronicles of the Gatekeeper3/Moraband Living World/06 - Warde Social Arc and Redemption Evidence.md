# Warde Social Arc and Redemption Evidence

## Before the reveal

Track courtesy, compassion, threats, accepted hospitality, willingness to admit uncertainty, treatment of enemies, and whether the party tests claims without humiliating the claimant. Warde notices behavior but does not read private thoughts.

## Evidence categories

- **Changed futures:** predictions or expected outcomes contradicted by player action.
- **Revised records:** fallen-statue inscriptions and prophecy-gallery edits.
- **Living witnesses:** rescued rivals, companions, or creatures who act differently because of mercy.
- **Voluntary vulnerability:** lowered weapons, surrendered attacks, disclosed fears, or rescue during danger.
- **Warde’s ordinary life:** camp, tools, shelter, and habits proving he lived through decades of choices.

## Focused appeal procedure [MODULE + ADDITION]

A character spends an action and gives up attacking to make a specific appeal opposed by Warde’s Discipline—effectively Daunting and upgraded by his Discipline while the dark side resists. Cite evidence or a witnessed choice. Generic speeches receive no automatic bonus. Success or Triumph makes Warde help resist the dark side; later checks become plain Daunting and may gain Boost. Violence does not close redemption unless the party abandons communication entirely.

Warn the players before the published Conflict consequence for killing Warde without attempting communication.
