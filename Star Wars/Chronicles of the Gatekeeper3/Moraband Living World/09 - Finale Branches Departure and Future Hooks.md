# Finale Branches, Departure, and Future Hooks

## Warde redeemed

He relinquishes the crystal, refuses the role of master, and chooses atonement. The party may offer transport, help with the Delta-7B, or allow a solitary departure. Redemption does not erase harm.

## Warde killed

Recover the crystal, lightsaber, supplies, and unstable prophecy datapad. Let the gatekeeper react with unease rather than deliver a triumph speech. Apply the published Conflict warning and result when appropriate.

## Party defeated

Warde stabilizes survivors because their survival disproves his expected ending. He leaves the crystal and weapon and disappears. This is continuation, not campaign failure.

## Departure

Pay off return-route preparation, allies, repaired shelters, pursuit, ship damage, and remaining Moraband influence. Give every character one final image or dream that can recur without dictating future choices.
