# Encounters, Hazards, Relics, and Rewards

## Encounter rule

Every encounter must do at least one: change allegiance, reveal evidence, alter a route/resource, test a moral claim, or create a later witness. If it does none, cut it.

## Hazard palette

Red electrical storms; glass-sharp obsidian; collapsing causeways; dry debris surges; magnetic navigation drift; static-charged power systems; long exposed climbs; false water; acoustic voices; heat loss after dusk; awakened architecture.

## Relic rule

Before a relic is taken, show ownership, purpose, or consequence. Offer documentation, return, trade, disablement, or preservation as alternatives. Relics can be valuable without being safe.

## Rewards

Use information, reliable routes, witnesses, shelters, repaired equipment, recovered history, Warde Trust, and reduced clocks as rewards alongside physical objects. Preserve the published final crystal, Warde’s lightsaber, datapad, supplies, holocron teaching, and XP.
