# Session 2 — The Search for a Dead Jedi

## Session purpose

Turn the search into investigation rather than a sequence of Survival rolls. The causeway, Delta-7B, and false refuge provide different kinds of evidence and different temptations.

## Opening state

The landing site falls below the horizon. From this point forward, every route choice commits time and supplies.

## Central decision

Does the group trust physical evidence, the gatekeeper, the Force, or what it most wants to be true?

## Scenes and optional map supports

- [[04 - 04 Broken Colossal Causeway]]
- [[05 - 05 The Half Buried Delta 7B]]
- [[06 - 06 Deceptions and Reflections False Oasis]]

These are story situations, not mandatory map stops. Combine adjacent scenes when pacing is fast; expand an optional route when the group investigates. A session may end between these files without breaking continuity.

## Session rhythm

1. Re-establish carried injuries, companions, rival position, Conflict, and unresolved promises.
2. Give each character one decision or relationship beat before the main hazard.
3. Present a concrete goal with at least two approaches.
4. Let the world act after delay or noise; do not wait for a failed roll.
5. Resolve one major encounter, one discovery, and one interpersonal consequence.
6. End on a changed situation: The party possesses proof that Warde survived his landing, but the planet has learned what each character hopes to find.

## Questions likely to matter

- What does the group currently believe about Warde, and what evidence supports it?
- Which character is most tempted by certainty, control, forbidden instruction, reunion, or safety?
- Which companion or rival has earned trust, and what do they now ask in return?
- What route, object, witness, or promise could matter during the final redemption attempt?

## Between-scene world motion

Roll or choose once only when travel needs texture:

| d6 | Development |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Weather changes the safe route but exposes a useful landmark. |
| 2 | A companion requests a private conversation about the last decision. |
| 3 | Rivals leave evidence of injury, haste, or disagreement. |
| 4 | A mundane equipment problem demands time, parts, or a workaround. |
| 5 | Moraband reproduces a familiar sensory detail without explaining itself. |
| 6 | A prior mercy or betrayal returns as practical help or obstruction. |

## End-of-session accounting

Update Expedition, Pursuit, Warde Trust, Temptation, and Palace Awakening. Record evidence in the players’ own words. Ask which unresolved issue they expect to matter next; use that expectation as pressure, not as a guarantee.
