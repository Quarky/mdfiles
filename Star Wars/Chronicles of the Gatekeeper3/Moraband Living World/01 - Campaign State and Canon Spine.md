# Campaign State and Canon Spine

## Published spine [MODULE]

The journey to Moraband takes roughly forty-eight hours on a successful Hard Astrogation check. Failure adds twelve hours; the published severe failure causes four system strain and a Critical Hit. Moraband lies in the Horuset system, beneath perpetual crimson dusk, and its dark vergence adds one automatic dark-side result to Force checks.

The canonical sequence remains: arrival and pursuit; broad search; Delta-7B and orbital-ring evidence; Deceptions and Reflections; Beasts of Shadow; Echoes of the Sith; the Wanderer; journey to the tomb; Sith palace; Warde’s reveal, battle, and possible redemption; final crystal, lightsaber, holocron, and published XP.

## Expansion [ADDITION]

The planet is treated as a long expedition rather than three map encounters. Added travel legs, ruins, shelter, rivals, creatures, evidence, items, social scenes, and alternate routes create a sandbox around the published spine. They may be shortened, skipped, recombined, or revisited.

## Campaign bridge [CAMPAIGN]

Before play, record ship damage, supplies, companions, animals, injuries, Conflict, Shard Moon artifacts, rival status, and what the group believes about Warde. Do not silently reset any of these at the landing.

## Deliberate deviations [DEVIATION]

Maps are optional story supports rather than a fixed linear route. Additional evidence can make redemption more achievable, but it never guarantees success or replaces the published opposition. No published ending is removed.
