# Bogwing
**Role:** Utility / Scouting (Sentinel)
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bogwing)
**Type:** Minion
**Characteristics:** Brawn 2, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Perception 2, Stealth 2, Vigilance 1
**Talents:** None
**Abilities:** Glider (can fall safely and glide short distances; based on Sathari Glider ability), Aquatic (can breathe underwater for up to 4 hours)
**Soak:** 2
**Wound Threshold:** 6
**Equipment:** Claws and teeth (Brawl; Damage 3; Crit 5; Range [Engaged])
**Picture:** [Bogwing Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bogwing?file=Bogwing-SWH.png)

A small, territorial reptavian native to Naboo and other watery worlds, the bogwing is deceptively loyal once its trust is earned. Its ability to navigate swamps, dense forests, and caves makes it an ideal scout for the hazardous terrain of Arbooine. It can hold its breath for extended periods and uses a series of clicks and trills to communicate.
