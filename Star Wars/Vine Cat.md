# Vine Cat
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 4, Agility 4, Intellect 1, Cunning 3, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 2, Perception 2, Stealth 3, Survival 2
**Talents:** Adversary 1, Feral Strength 1, Knockdown
**Abilities:** Leaping Pounce (may spend a maneuver to move up to Medium range vertically)
**Soak:** 4
**Wound Threshold:** 26
**Equipment:** Claws and Bite (Brawl; Damage 7; Crit 3; Pierce 1, Vicious 1)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vine_cat)

Originally from the remote jungles of Haruun Kal, vine cats are powerful reptomammals that are perfectly adapted to life in the trees[reference:21]. Their compact, muscular frames allow them to leap up to eight meters, and they are prized by some off-worlders for their ferocity and cunning[reference:22]. A vine cat's primary tactic is to ambush from above, using its powerful leaps to close the distance before its prey can react.
