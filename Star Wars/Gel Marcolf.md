# Gel Marcolf

**Role:** Reeve of Quolas (Tyrannical Ruler)  
**Species:** Mirialan  
**Force Sensitive:** Yes (unconscious dark side user)  
**Location:** Reeve’s Manor (apex of the tree‑city)  
**Voice / Demeanor:** Cold, bitter, resentful. Speaks with quiet menace, often staring off as if lost in dark thoughts. He is paranoid and expects betrayal.

## Personality & Motivation
Gel Marcolf was once a hopeful young man, eager to learn the ways of the Force. Decades of isolation and perceived abandonment by Suljo Warde have twisted him. He believes Warde stole his destiny and left him to rot on a backwater world. Now he rules Quolas through fear, using his unconscious Force abilities to command loyalty. He secretly craves validation but expresses it through cruelty. He will do anything to keep the kyber crystal, as it is his only link to Warde and the power he feels he deserves.

## GM Notes
- He is not a Jedi – he has no lightsaber training (uses a cortosis sword).
- His Force powers manifest as Battle Meditation, which he uses to bolster his guards.
- If the PCs mention Warde, Marcolf becomes irate and may attack or order their arrest.
- He wears the kyber crystal on a chain around his neck. At night he locks it in a chest (Hard Skulduggery to pick).
- He will bargain only if threatened; he values the crystal above all else.
- If defeated, he may reveal that Warde went to Cato Neimoidia.
