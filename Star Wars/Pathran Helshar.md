# Pathran Helshar

**Role:** Leader of the Resistance  
**Species:** Sathari (blue plumage with shock of white facial feathers)  
**Location:** Root‑tunnels below Quolas  
**Voice / Demeanor:** Earnest, idealistic, slightly nervous. Speaks quickly, often pacing. He wants change but fears the cost.

## Personality & Motivation
Pathran is the youngest son of Koh Helshar. He has known only Marcolf’s tyranny and dreams of a free Quolas. He is brave but inexperienced, and his passion sometimes overrides caution. He respects the PCs if they oppose Marcolf and will eagerly accept their help. He is not prejudiced against Force users, though he resents Warde for installing Marcolf. His ultimate goal is to overthrow the reeve, but he worries about the consequences for his family.

## GM Notes
- He can be convinced to launch an assault on the manor (Revolution, page 34).
- If the PCs aid the resistance, Pathran provides a small force of fighters (Quolas Citizen minions).
- He knows the root‑tunnel entrance to the lower city, which can bypass the main gate.
- His emotional strength is Justice; his weakness is Recklessness.
