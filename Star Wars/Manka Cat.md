# Manka Cat
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 5, Agility 4, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 1, Cool 2, Stealth 2
**Talents:** Pack Instincts (grants two Boost dice when performing the assist maneuver)[reference:13]
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2
**Soak:** 5
**Wound Threshold:** 22
**Equipment:** Tusks and teeth (Brawl; Damage 5; Crit 3; Pierce 2, Vicious 1)[reference:14]
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Manka_cat)

Native to worlds like Tython and Alderaan, these fur-covered quadrupeds are a rare example of a feline predator that hunts with pack instincts[reference:15]. A manka cat's powerful tusks and sharp teeth allow it to pierce thick armor, and they work in coordinated prides to bring down much larger prey[reference:16]. They are as intelligent as they are dangerous, making them a cunning and relentless threat.
