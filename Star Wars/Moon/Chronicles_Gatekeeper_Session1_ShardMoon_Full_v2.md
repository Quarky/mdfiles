# Chronicles of the Gatekeeper — Session 1 (Full v2)
## The Shard Moon Incident

**Status:** Expanded GM draft  
**Purpose:** Full moon-side adventure aligned to the existing maps and the revised Sith cloning / experiment premise  
**Scope:** Story, living world, exploration, encounters, history, experiments, artifacts, loot, complications, and outcome  
**NPCs:** Keep full mechanical NPC writeups in the separate NPC document

---

# High Concept
The party crash-lands on the **Shard Moon**, a Sith auxiliary moon in the Moraband system. The moon was once used for observation, ritual preparation, and **secret experimentation involving dark side corruption, force-reactive biology, and attempts to reproduce controllable versions of Warde through cloning and alchemical manipulation**.

The session should feel like a **full adventure chapter**, not a brief obstacle. It should reveal that the Sith here were not merely guarding relics — they were trying to **manufacture power**.

---

# Core Themes
- the dark side as pressure, temptation, and contamination
- ruins that feel used, abandoned, and dangerous rather than decorative
- science and Sith ritual mixed together in disturbing ways
- failed attempts to control destiny through cloning and corruption
- survival, investigation, moral choices, and escalating threat

---

# Session Objectives
By the end of the session, the PCs should ideally:

- stabilize the ship enough to leave the moon later
- explore multiple linked moon locations
- discover the history of the Shard Moon facility
- learn that the hidden project involved **Warde clone-lines or Warde-derived replication experiments**
- uncover at least one active or semi-active experiment
- recover information, parts, or artifacts needed to continue the campaign
- survive a final confrontation in the hidden laboratory / fortress
- leave convinced that Moraband is worse

---

# Big Secret of the Moon
The Shard Moon was a **Sith satellite research site** supporting Moraband. Officially it served as an outlying observation and ritual support post. Secretly it hosted **Project Ash Warde** (working label), a classified attempt to grow, shape, or reproduce suitable dark side vessels / clones patterned after Warde.

The theory behind the project was:

- Warde represented or was linked to a rare alignment of bloodline, destiny, Force sensitivity, and symbolic weight.
- A pure copy might be impossible.
- But through clone-stock, dark side conditioning, Sith alchemy, blood markers, Force-reactive media, and vergence exposure, the Sith believed they could create **controllable versions**, vessels, or degraded but usable analogues.

## What went wrong
Possible combined truth:
- the vergence in the Moraband system amplified all work done here
- the clones or proto-clones became unstable
- the dark side imprint could not be cleanly controlled
- some subjects developed independent will or madness
- biological corruption spread through the facility
- the site was partially abandoned, partially sealed, and partially left to fail

# Map Use and Sequence

## Map 1 — Crash Landing Zone
**Primary map:**
- `crashed_freighter_in_volcanic_ruins.png`

**Alternate / supplemental:**
- `crash_site_on_an_alien_moon.png`

**Only this map should feature the ship.**

### Story role
The wreck site is both refuge and pressure point. The PCs are safe only temporarily.

### What the players see
- their upgraded ship scarred and grounded among volcanic stone
- old carved road markers and ruined approach stones
- heat shimmer and mineral haze
- partial evidence the crash site once served as a monitored access corridor

### Things to do here
- triage damage
- revive Sigg
- search immediate ruins
- fight or evade a first threat
- pick the first inland route

### Suggested encounters
#### 1. Ship stabilization
A short but meaningful skill challenge.

**Complications**
- power bleed
- coolant instability
- distressing whispers heard through the internal comms
- a beast or droid disturbed by the impact

#### 2. Outer scavengers
Use something light here:
- carrion vermin
- scavenger droids
- a lone corrupted animal

#### 3. Vergence pulse
A wave rolls through the crash site.

Possible effects:
- a PC sees clone-vat silhouettes for a second
- one obelisk emits a burst of old Sith script
- a nearby maintenance marker reveals a route toward the ruins

### Story clues found here
- route sigils showing regular traffic toward the observatory and ritual grounds
- a broken transport marker stamped with a restricted research code
- a damaged case or field cylinder carrying medical inventory tags
- a warning glyph indicating “containment zone” deeper within

## Map 2 — Volcanic Ruins Traverse
**Primary map:**
- `volcanic_ruins_battle_map.png`

### Story role
This is the long journey outward from the crash site. It should not be a corridor. It should be a sequence of **small lived-in ruin stories**.

### Environmental identity
- black basalt roads
- broken arches over lava cracks
- ruined watch posts
- shrines and markers half-buried in ash
- blast-scarred structures showing former conflict or panic

### Living-world storytelling incidents
#### Story incident A — The evacuation lane
The PCs find transport sled tracks fossilized in ash and signs people fled in haste.

What this implies:
- the site did not empty in an orderly way
- support staff and test personnel may have been routed inland under emergency conditions

#### Story incident B — Failed checkpoint
A checkpoint contains:
- dead comm relays
- broken defense hardware
- a melted med-case
- a wall scratch-log from personnel who were waiting for extraction that never came

#### Story incident C — Creature den in a shrine
A local beast nest has been built in a former ceremonial alcove. This reinforces the passage of time and the blending of sacred and ruined spaces.

### Encounters
#### 1. Traversal hazard
Choose one or more:
- unstable bridge crossing
- lava vent timing challenge
- falling scree and heat burst
- choosing between safe route and shortcut through cursed ground

#### 2. Research escapee
A minor failed experiment survives here. Examples:
- half-blind clone-hound
- chemically warped carrion beast
- skittering vat-bred scavengers

#### 3. Sith probe droids or caretakers
A few old automated units still patrol the approach network, identifying intruders as contamination threats.

### Rewards
- salvage for ship repairs
- map fragments
- one old security cylinder
- evidence that the observatory routed data to a hidden lower facility

## Map 3 — Ruined Observatory Over the Lava Abyss
**Primary map:**
- `ruined_observatory_over_a_lava_abyss.png`

### Story role
This is the first major lore location. It explains the moon’s operational role and hints at the secret one.

### What the players see
- broken alignment towers and cracked viewing arcs
- scorched measurement rings
- an abyss below glowing with volcanic light
- cracked consoles or astromantic stations pointing toward Moraband and the local moon chain

### What the observatory once did
Official purpose:
- monitor celestial and ritual timing
- observe Moraband conditions
- detect dangerous Force phenomena

Hidden purpose:
- track vergence intensity
- calculate ideal exposure windows for experimental growth cycles
- measure deviations among clone subjects exposed to Sith-infused conditions

### Major discoveries
Use several of these:
- a research notation using terms like **Subject Line W**, **W-derivative**, **Host Viability**, or **Crown Candidate**
- records of “dark side drift” in subjects after exposure
- warnings that exact duplication failed repeatedly
- a note that ritual preparation at the plaza improved obedience in some lines
- a partial map or access code pointing toward restricted sublevels

### Encounters here
#### 1. Observatory defense
An old turret cluster, guardian droid, or sensor-wraith activation.

#### 2. Memory echo
The PCs briefly witness the observatory in use:
- robed supervisors arguing
- technicians carrying samples
- an urgent order to seal “the lower biovault”

#### 3. Sigg’s collapse or insight
Sigg may identify that the moon was not merely monitoring Moraband — it was using Moraband’s dark side environment as part of a program.

### Practical benefits
The observatory should grant at least one mechanical advantage:
- route to the ritual plaza
- partial route to the hidden base
- calibration data for the ship
- partial containment override

## Map 4 — Volcanic Temple Ruins and Ritual Plaza
**Primary map:**
- `volcanic_temple_ruins_and_ritual_plaza.png`

### Story role
This is where the session shifts from archaeological mystery into deliberate Sith intent.

### What the players see
- processional paths
- ritual dais and monoliths
- carved doctrine walls
- sacrificial or initiatory architecture
- places where candidates were judged, broken, or conditioned

### What this place was used for
The ritual plaza was a **preparation ground** for personnel and subjects entering the deeper facility.

Uses included:
- indoctrination
- fear conditioning
- obedience rites
- dark side attunement
- symbolic “purification” before lab immersion or testing

### Moral / thematic punch
The PCs should realize that the scientific work below was not separate from Sith belief. It was an extension of it.

### Encounters
#### 1. Spirit / echo challenge
Not necessarily a fight. The plaza itself can pressure the minds of the PCs.

Examples:
- a PC sees themselves kneeling to power
- a PC is tempted with certainty, revenge, or order
- one PC is offered an easier route if they submit to the dark side tone of the place

#### 2. Guardian confrontation
Options:
- ritual guardian droids
- shade-like sentries
- corrupted acolyte remnants

#### 3. Access trial
The party must interpret a rite, symbol set, or ceremonial control method to gain access deeper in.

### Key revelations
- the deeper facility handled living subjects
- “successes” were rare and often unstable
- there were multiple lines of experiments beyond the Warde-clones
- the site also developed **dark side animals, obedience parasites, and force-reactive tissue grafts**

### Loot / finds
- Sith ceremonial key / data spike
- partial holocron shard or ritual mnemonic plate
- dark side warning tablets
- symbolic crest marking Project Ash Warde

## Interlude — The Clone Vaults
**This can be run as intermediate chambers between Map 4 and Map 5, using theater of the mind or smaller improvised maps if needed.**

### Purpose
This is where the session becomes much bigger than a standard ruins crawl. It gives the players a tour through the actual experiment layers.

### Areas within the Clone Vaults
#### 1. Vat Gallery
Rows of broken or darkened clone tanks.

What may be found:
- collapsed subjects
- inert tissue masses
- broken fetal forms
- one or two intact but dormant subjects
- notes on accelerated growth and behavioral collapse

#### 2. Conditioning Ward
Cells and mental imprint stations.

Clues:
- obedience scripts
- pain-conditioning rigs
- Force-response test slates
- evidence some clones developed original personalities or resisted control

#### 3. Beast Pens
Experimental creatures bred or altered for Sith purposes.

Possible inhabitants:
- warped hounds
- bone-thin tunnel stalkers
- blind leech-swarms
- one failed W-guardian prototype

#### 4. Specimen Archive
Storage for Sith artifacts or organic materials used in the experiments.

Could contain:
- preserved blood samples
- alchemical reagents
- bones from dark side creatures
- kyber fragments or corrupted crystals
- a record naming several experiment branches

### Experiment branches the PCs can discover
These help make the moon feel like a full living-world dungeon.

#### Branch W — Warde Replication Program
Goal: produce controllable bodies or analogues shaped by Warde’s profile.

#### Branch H — Hound / Hunter Program
Goal: breed tracker-beasts that could sense Force signatures and hunt escaped subjects.

#### Branch L — Leech Program
Goal: create parasites that weaken Force users and make capture easier.

#### Branch V — Vessel Program
Goal: create empty or semi-empty hosts capable of accepting dark side imprint.

#### Branch C — Crown Program
Goal: identify or force a subject capable of serving as a pinnacle stable product.

## Map 5 — Sith Volcanic Fortress Battlemap
**Primary map:**
- `sith_volcanic_fortress_battlemap.png`

**Supplemental visual:**
- `crimson_core_in_the_sith_laboratory.png`

### Story role
This is the climax chamber or core complex. It should answer the central question: what were they trying to build, and why did it fail?

### Core reveal
At the center of the complex lies a Force-reactive system — perhaps a **Crimson Core**, alchemical reactor, or vergence focus-engine — used to saturate clone tissue, stabilize experiments, and pressure subjects toward compliance.

### What the players see
- volcanic-fortress architecture
- ritual-scientific machinery
- cracked containment wells
- crimson or blood-red energy channels
- monitoring balconies and test pits
- one or more sealed central pods or ruined command stations

### Final truths they can learn
Pick several:
- no clone line truly reproduced Warde
- some lines came close in fragments: physical resilience, Force responsiveness, symbolic affinity, aggression, or survivability
- the Sith increasingly conflated cloning with prophecy
- the site was abandoned when the experiments became too unstable or when internal conflict erupted
- at least one line may still survive elsewhere or may have been transferred off-site

### Major climax options
#### Option A — Surviving prototype
A partially successful dark-side clone awakens or is already active.

Traits:
- uncanny but incomplete
- unstable identity
- strong Force pressure
- may be tragic rather than purely monstrous

#### Option B — Corrupted overseer
A Sith scientist, warden, or preserved intelligence remains active and tries to resume the program using the PCs.

#### Option C — Core meltdown
The Crimson Core destabilizes under current conditions, forcing a battle + shutdown + escape sequence.

You can combine these.

### Final encounter structure
A good final encounter has three layers:
1. active threat (prototype / guardian / overseer)
2. environmental instability (heat, collapsing platforms, surges)
3. choice under pressure (destroy, take, stabilize, or flee)

### Possible rewards
#### Ship / plot rewards
- power regulator or energy lattice that helps repair the ship
- navigation data for safer Moraband approach
- encoded project records leading to Moraband destinations

#### Sith / artifact rewards
- ritual key focusing crystal
- corrupted or unstable kyber shard
- alchemical reagent set
- research tablet on vergence behavior
- a symbolic signet / command credential that may open later facilities

#### Narrative rewards
- the PCs understand that Moraband itself may contain the origin or higher command layer of these projects
- a PC may now be targeted by a lingering dark side call
- Sigg’s warnings gain credibility and urgency

# Living World Details
Use these often.

## Sound and atmosphere
- low subsonic humming under stone
- sudden metal creaks from thermal strain
- whispers only partly audible
- fluid dripping in empty clone halls
- distant groans of old machinery restarting and dying again

## Visual details
- pale stains around drain channels
- shattered glass and nutrient lines
- ritual carvings over medical steel
- heat shimmer over sacrificial platforms
- half-burned records, tags, or insignia

## Storytelling props
- identification tags from technicians and handlers
- intake slates naming subjects by code numbers
- stitched journals or data cylinders from fearful staff
- broken cages, restraints, and transport stretchers
- sample vials marked with branch codes

# Ending the Session
A strong end includes:
- the ship is repairable or repaired enough to depart
- the party leaves with evidence of Sith cloning and corruption experiments
- one or more artifacts or records point toward Moraband
- one or more PCs feel the dark side is now aware of them

### Final line option
> The moon was not a dead outpost. It was a workshop for an impossible future — and Moraband is where the masters of that future waited.
