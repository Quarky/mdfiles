## Vergence in the Moraband System

**Status:** Revised working draft  
**Campaign placement:** Immediately before the Moraband arc  
**Purpose:** Pre-session / Session 0.5 opener that flows directly into the Shard Moon storyline  
**System:** Star Wars FFG / Force and Destiny compatible

---

## Correction Note
This document uses the term **vergence**, not “convergence.” The event Sigg recognizes is a **dark side vergence phenomenon** in the Moraband system.

In practical play, this means the disturbance is not just navigational or astral interference — it is a **concentrated Force nexus / surge point** that distorts emotion, perception, hyperspace timing, and the spiritual atmosphere of the region.

---

## Premise
The party exits hyperspace into the **Moraband system** at the wrong angle and wrong depth. Warning klaxons begin sounding immediately. The ship is drifting off course, pulled by a **dark side vergence** that twists local conditions and seems to thicken the Force itself into a hostile pressure.

**Sigg** recognizes what is happening first:

> “Oh crap! It’s a vergence—”

He collapses before he can fully explain.

The ship is dragged toward an outer volcanic moon. For these notes, that moon is called **the Shard Moon**. The party must survive the approach, the crash, and the first pulse of the vergence before the main moon session begins.

---

## What the Pre-Session Must Accomplish
By the end of the pre-session, the group should:

- understand they were knocked off course by a dark side vergence
- survive a dramatic emergency descent
- crash on the **Shard Moon** with the ship damaged but not destroyed
- get their first clue that the moon itself was once used for **Sith experimentation and controlled Force research**
- have a strong reason to move into the larger moon-side session

## The Shard Moon
A broken outer moon in the Moraband system.

### Identity
- volcanic and impact-scarred
- black basalt ridges and red mineral dust
- venting fissures and heat plumes
- Sith-era ruins and buried facilities
- spiritually tainted by proximity to Moraband

### Core story of the moon
The moon was once used as a **secondary experimental and oversight site** by Sith-aligned forces. It was not a grand capital. It was a dangerous frontier installation — part watch-post, part ritual ground, part cloning and corruption laboratory.

Its most secret work centered on an experiment to produce **controlled dark side replicas / clone lines connected to Warde**, or at least to reproduce and manipulate traits associated with his power, bloodline, destiny, or symbolic function. The moon held both ritual and pseudo-scientific infrastructure for those efforts.

Something went terribly wrong there.

## Pre-Session Scene Flow

### Scene 1 — Bad Reentry to Realspace
The ship tears out of hyperspace already under strain. Sensors are unstable and the ship is being pulled off vector.

#### Read-aloud
> The ship slams out of hyperspace with a shriek of overstressed hull plating. Red warning lights flood the cockpit. Moraband hangs in the distance like a wound in space, and nearby a broken moon rolls beneath sheets of red storm-glow. Every alarm on the ship seems to be competing for your attention.
>
> Sigg stares at the sensor display, face draining white.
>
> “Oh crap,” he whispers. “It’s a vergence—”
>
> Then he collapses.

---

### Scene 2 — Emergency Skill Challenge
The party tries to control the ship as it is dragged toward the moon.

#### Goals
- reduce crash severity
- protect vital systems
- preserve supplies
- keep Sigg alive
- choose the least deadly crash vector

#### Useful checks
- **Piloting (Space):** line up the ship, reduce spin, avoid total impact failure
- **Mechanics:** reroute power, vent heat, protect engines
- **Computers:** stabilize nav data, filter false readings, isolate hostile interference
- **Astrogation:** identify the moon and pattern of the vergence
- **Cool / Discipline:** function under supernatural pressure
- **Coordination / Athletics:** physically move through a violently shaking craft

#### Vergence complications
- gravity flickers
- controls lag behind input
- whispers bleed through inactive comms
- sensor ghosts show impossible facilities or false landing paths
- Force-sensitive characters feel the **light side become distant and muffled**

---

### Scene 3 — The Vergence Touches the Ship
This should feel personal, not merely mechanical.

#### Possible manifestations
- the canopy briefly reflects a ruin skyline that is not actually ahead of the ship
- someone hears a voice from their past
- Sigg mutters “don’t let it root in you” while unconscious
- one PC sees black spires and crimson vats
- one PC gets a flash of rows of half-formed bodies in pale fluid

This primes the party for the cloning / experimentation reveals later on.

### Scene 5 — First Moon-Side Threat
Just after landing, the party faces an immediate complication.

#### Recommended default
A **vergence pulse** rolls across the crash site and activates traces of old Sith infrastructure in the nearby ruins.

Possible visible effects:
- marker obelisks glow dim red
- a buried sensor ping appears on the ship scanner
- a ruined path becomes visible from elevated ground
- one sealed subsystem begins broadcasting a repeating emergency / containment warning

This is the hook that pushes the party into **Session 1: The Shard Moon Incident**.

---

## Transition into Session 1
Session 1 begins once the party has:
- secured the ship enough to regroup
- revived or stabilized Sigg
- chosen to investigate nearby ruins, laboratories, ritual grounds, and observatory structures

The moon should immediately feel like a place with **layers of history**:
1. old Sith occupation
2. hidden experimentation
3. cloning and corruption programs
4. collapse, purge, or containment failure
5. present-day dark side residue sharpened by the vergence
