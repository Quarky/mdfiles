# Core Returning NPC
## Sigg
**Role:** Knowledgeable ally / lore bridge / warning voice  
**Use in story:** Recognizes the vergence, collapses during arrival, later helps interpret findings.

### Story function
- establishes the danger immediately
- confirms that Moraband-related Force phenomena are real and serious
- helps decode observatory and laboratory clues
- should not know the full truth, only enough to be afraid of it

# Optional / Major Moon NPCs

## 1. Survey Custodian DR-9
**Role:** Half-functional observatory droid  
**Use:** Observatory guide, exposition source, possible skill challenge focus

### Concept
A service / survey droid with fragmented memory and corrupted directives.

### Good uses
- reveals official moon purpose
- only gradually reveals restricted purpose
- may mistake PCs for authorized staff

---

## 2. Warden-Construct Vesh-Null
**Role:** Hidden facility guardian  
**Use:** Lower laboratory enforcer / boss-tier obstacle

### Concept
A hybrid sentinel of ritual and machine logic, built to guard the lower complex and suppress contamination.

### Good uses
- major combat threat
- exposition through interrogation or fragmented monologue
- symbol of how science and Sith ritual merged on the moon

---

## 3. The Red Whisper
**Role:** Dark-side presence / psychological antagonist  
**Use:** Recurring whisper-presence throughout the session

### Concept
A semi-intelligent dark-side echo tied to the vergence, the ritual plaza, or the core experiments.

### Good uses
- temptation scenes
- false guidance
- speaking to PCs as if it knows them
- hinting that the project never truly died

---

## 4. Overseer Vael Serrin
**Role:** Surviving recorded overseer, spirit-imprint, or partially preserved intellect  
**Use:** Mid-to-late session lore reveal or final confrontation voice

### Concept
The lead architect or one of the chief overseers of Project Ash Warde. May survive only as recordings, a chamber-bound hologlyphic intelligence, or a deeply corrupted half-living remnant.

### Story use
- explains the goal of the Warde-clone project
- justifies Sith rationale for the experiments
- may try to recruit, test, or repurpose the PCs

---

## 5. Failed Subject W-12
**Role:** Tragic semi-successful experimental subject  
**Use:** Clone vault encounter / moral dilemma

### Concept
A subject from Branch W that achieved partial viability. It is not a true Warde copy, but it carries echoes of the project’s ambitions.

### Traits
- strong but unstable
- flashes of identity and confusion
- may beg for release, lash out, or recognize the party as “wrong” or “late”

### Good uses
- emotional encounter
- tragic warning of what the facility was trying to do
- combat if things go badly

---

## 6. Handler Beast / Branch H Hound
**Role:** Force-sensitive tracker beast  
**Use:** Traverse or Beast Pen threat

### Concept
A dark side-conditioned hound line created to track escaped subjects and sense nearby Force activity.

### Good uses
- stalking encounter
- chase sequence
- sign that the lower facility manipulated living species as tools

---

## 7. Branch L Leech-Swarm
**Role:** Parasitic experiment threat  
**Use:** Clone vault hazard or later recurring threat

### Concept
An experiment line intended to weaken or suppress Force-sensitive captives through parasitic drain.

### Good uses
- atmospheric horror
- swarm / environmental hazard
- foreshadowing for later Force-draining creatures elsewhere
