# Wraid
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 5, Agility 3, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 2, Brawl 2, Perception 2, Survival 2
**Talents:** Feral Strength 1
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2
**Soak:** 6
**Wound Threshold:** 20
**Equipment:** Claws and Bite (Brawl; Damage 8; Crit 3; Pierce 2)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wraid)

These large, reptilian quadrupeds are known for their aggressive nature and powerful front limbs[reference:27]. Their scaly hide ranges from pinkish-red to greenish-yellow, providing natural camouflage in the forests. When threatened or agitated, a wraid can use its powerful front legs to move with bounding strides, making it deceptively fast for its size[reference:28]. They are fearless predators, often seen in small clusters, and will attack prey much larger than themselves.

**Role:** Combat Guardian (Defender)
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wraid)
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 5, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Brawl 3, Perception 2, Survival 2, Vigilance 2
**Talents:** Feral Strength 1
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2; Scaly Hide (adds Setback die to melee attacks targeting the wraid)
**Soak:** 5
**Wound Threshold:** 18
**Equipment:** Claws and Bite (Brawl; Damage 7; Crit 3; Range [Engaged]; Pierce 2)
**Picture:** [Wraid Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wraid?file=Wraid_negwt.jpg)

A wraid is a tough, reptilian quadruped known for its aggressive nature and powerful claws. Its scaly hide makes it highly resilient, allowing it to serve as a living shield for its master. They are fearless and will attack prey much larger than themselves. Once tamed, a wraid is relentlessly devoted and will defend its home and owner to the death.
