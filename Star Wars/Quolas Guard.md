# Quolas Guard

**Type:** Minion (group only)  
**Role:** Enforcers of Marcolf’s rule  
**Location:** Main gate, guardhouses, reeve’s manor, patrols  
**Personality:** Varies – some are cruel and enjoy power, others are fearful and do only what is necessary to avoid punishment.

## GM Notes
- Guards are suspicious of outsiders and will report any mention of Warde or Marcolf.
- They use slugthrower pistols (primitive but effective) and diiros coated with bearsloth venom.
- If a guard captain uses Restraint, nearby guards may also gain the ability for one round.
- Reinforcements arrive 2 rounds after an alarm.
