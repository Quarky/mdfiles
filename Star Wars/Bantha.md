# Bantha
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 4 (adult), Brawn 2 (young), Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 2, Brawl 2, Resilience 3, Survival 3
**Talents:** Beast of Burden
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2 (Large), Herd Mentality (adds Boost die to Survival checks when with herd)
**Soak:** 5
**Wound Threshold:** 20 (adult), 10 (young)
**Equipment:** Trample (Brawl; Damage 6; Crit 4; Knockdown, Concussive 1)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bantha)

The bantha is the galaxy's quintessential herd animal, a shaggy-haired, horned mammal used by countless species as a mount and beast of burden[reference:36]. While generally docile, a cornered or mother bantha protecting its young can be surprisingly fierce, using its powerful body and horns to trample threats. The young are particularly vulnerable, making them a tempting target for predators in the wild[reference:37].
