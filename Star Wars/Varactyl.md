# Varactyl
**Role:** Fast Mount / Cliff-Scaling
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Varactyl)
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 4, Agility 4, Intellect 1, Cunning 3, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Athletics 3, Coordination 2, Perception 2, Survival 2
**Talents:** Domestication Potential, Sure-Footed
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2; Gecko Grip (can scale vertical surfaces without a check)
**Soak:** 4
**Wound Threshold:** 22
**Equipment:** Claws and Bite (Brawl; Damage 6; Crit 3; Range [Engaged]; Pierce 1)
**Picture:** [Varactyl Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Varactyl?file=Varactyl_SWCT.png)

A varactyl is a swift, lizard-like creature capable of scaling sheer cliffs and darting through dense canopy with equal ease. It is both an ideal mount for navigating the treacherous terrain of Arbooine’s high trees and a steadfast companion in combat, striking with powerful claws. Varactyls are intelligent and fiercely loyal, often choosing their own rider.
