# Jairo Clan Camp Food

The Jairo are nomadic foragers and hunters. Their meals are simple, portable, and drawn from the forest. They do not farm; they gather and hunt sustainably.

---

## Typical Campfare

| **Food Item** | **Description** | **Preparation** |
|---------------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Smoked bearsloth strip** | Lean, dark meat from the hindquarters, cut thin and smoked over elrit wood. | Eaten cold as jerky or warmed briefly over coals. |
| **Roast tarvix haunch** | Young tarvix is a delicacy — tender, slightly sweet. | Skewered on green branches and roasted over an open fire. |
| **Elrit nut paste** | The nut of the elrit tree is oily and rich. Ground into a thick paste. | Spread on flatbread or eaten with a finger. |
| **Cave fungus flatbread** | A dense, chewy bread made from powdered cave fungus and water. | Baked on heated stones. Keeps for weeks. |
| **Moss‑berry jam** | Small, tart purple berries that grow on wet rocks. Cooked down with a touch of tree sap. | Smeared on flatbread or mixed with water as a sour drink. |
| **Forest tuber mash** | A starchy, potato‑like root vegetable found near riverbanks. | Boiled in a hide pot, then mashed with a grinding stone. |
| **Spiced grub skewer** | Large, protein‑rich grubs (roasted until crisp). Seasoned with crushed elrit bark. | Impaled on a thorn and held over the fire until popping. |
| **Glow‑water (fermented)** | Water stored in a hollow gourd with wild yeasts and a handful of moss‑berries. Slightly effervescent and mildly alcoholic. | Drunk from carved wooden cups. |

## Sample Camp Meal (Evening)

> *“Elder Jairo gestures toward the fire. A wooden platter is passed around: strips of smoked bearsloth, a bowl of forest tuber mash, and a stack of warm cave fungus flatbread. Lira hands you a cup of glow‑water — it fizzes gently on your tongue, tasting of berries and earth.”*

---

## If the PCs Share Their Own Food

The Jairo are curious about off‑world rations. Offering something unusual (chocolate, synth‑steak, bread from grain) is seen as a generous gift. In return, they may offer **two doses of bearsloth venom** or the **root‑tunnel secret** more freely. A PC who shares food without asking for anything in return gains **1 Conflict reduction** (or avoids gaining 1 Conflict from a previous selfish act).
