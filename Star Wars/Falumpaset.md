# Falumpaset
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 7, Agility 2, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Brawl 2, Resilience 2, Survival 2
**Talents:** Domesticable, Trained Mount, Beast of Burden
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2
**Soak:** 6
**Wound Threshold:** 20
**Equipment:** Trample (Brawl; Damage 8; Crit 5; Knockdown, Concussive 1)
**Picture:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Falumpaset)

Though native to the swamps of Naboo, the hardy falumpaset has been introduced to other forest worlds as a beast of burden[reference:6]. These loud, odious beasts have tough, leathery skin and a hardy build, making them ideal for hauling heavy loads through the underbrush. They are generally docile but can become fiercely protective of their herd.

# Falumpaset
**Role:** Beast of Burden / Cargo Transport
**Source:** [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Falumpaset)
**Type:** Rival
**Characteristics:** Brawn 6, Agility 1, Intellect 1, Cunning 2, Willpower 2, Presence 1
**Skills:** Brawl 1, Resilience 3, Survival 2
**Talents:** Beast of Burden
**Abilities:** Silhouette 2; Durable 1
**Soak:** 6
**Wound Threshold:** 25
**Equipment:** Trample (Brawl; Damage 7; Crit 5; Range [Engaged]; Knockdown)
**Picture:** [Falumpaset Image](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Falumpaset?file=Falumpaset_SWCT.png)

Falumpasets are loud, ill-tempered beasts with incredibly tough hides. They are stronger than they are fast, making them perfect for hauling supplies through the deep woods of Arbooine. Their stubbornness is legendary, but once bonded to a master, they become fiercely protective and will stomp any threat that endangers its herd.
