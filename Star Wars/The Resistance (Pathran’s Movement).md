# The Resistance (Pathran’s Movement)

**Type:** Underground revolutionary group  
**Leader:** Pathran Helshar  
**Members:** Young Sathari, some disillusioned adults  
**Location:** Root‑tunnels below Quolas

## Overview
The resistance is a secret network of Sathari who want to overthrow Marcolf. They lack weapons, training, and numbers, but they have passion and knowledge of the city’s hidden passages. Pathran is their figurehead, though he lacks combat experience.

## Key Info for GMs
- The resistance can mobilize up to 200 citizens, but most are non‑fighters (use Quolas Citizen stats).
- If the PCs help them, they may provide a diversion or direct assault force.
- Casualties will be high without PC support.
- A full‑scale attack (Revolution) is described on page 34.
