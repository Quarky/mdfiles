**What is actually happening:** The hidden Matrix activity becomes more coherent. Shared language, timing, and orientation appear among people who should not be coordinating conventionally. Deus is moving from passive observation toward an organized White network.

**Player-facing hint:** Angie feels that the noise has a direction; Frank's isolated file changes or reacts despite being offline; separate sources begin using the same unusual words or cadence.

**Angie-facing hint:** Angie: Hidden Matrix presences that once felt scattered all orient in the same direction at 15:23:12, like a crowd turning toward a stage.
