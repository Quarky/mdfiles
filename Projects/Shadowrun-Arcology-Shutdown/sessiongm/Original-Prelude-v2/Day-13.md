# December 13 — Ordinary People Remember

## Scene 1: Rowan refuses to become a clue dispenser

**Time:** 17:00  
**Location:** performance terrace  
**Cast:** Angie, Rowan, ordinary audience

Let Angie find Rowan doing something boring: replacing a string, arguing with the licensing interface, eating noodles out of a paper bowl.

> Rowan: “If you are here to perform, excellent.”
>
> “If you are here because every light turned amber last time, less excellent.”

Rowan remembers December 7 clearly. He does not know what caused it.

A nearby public screen starts playing the song from that performance. At the remembered refrain, the audio drops into **nine seconds of perfect silence** while the waveform display continues as though sound is present.

Rowan stops eating.

> “That is not my mix.”

Ask Angie whether she allows the conversation to remain human before investigating. Rowan can talk about music, life in the Arcology, Angie's performance, or why residents accept so much automation.

> Rowan: “Most days the building makes life easier. That is why nobody wants to imagine the easier thing can also be the cage.”

Do not turn Rowan into a philosopher every time; one line is enough.

## Scene 2: Eli knows something he should not

**Time:** 18:10  
**Cast:** Frank, Eli, customers

Eli waves Frank over and offers the promised meal voucher redemption.

> Eli: “No ginger unless requested. We have restored civilization.”

Then, casually:

> “How is the scrubber kid?”

Frank never told Eli about Angie.

If Frank asks what he means, Eli explains that a dispenser maintenance notice appeared the morning after the incident:

```text
ASSOCIATED MEDICAL EVENT RESOLVED
NO ACTION REQUIRED
```

Eli assumed Frank had helped a younger employee because Frank's name was attached to the service chain.

**Average Perception:** the message came from the dispenser's service channel, not from a person.

Eli becomes uncomfortable when he realizes he has been given personal information he did not request.

> “Tell me what to delete.”

If Frank asks him to preserve it instead, Eli will print or hand-copy the wording. This creates a low-tech evidence trail.

## Scene 3: Human memory versus system memory

If the players meet today, make the theme explicit through situation rather than lecture.

Rowan remembers Angie even if the performance record is altered. Eli remembers the message even if the dispenser no longer does. Marisol remembers the fountain rhythm. Niko remembers the impossible relays.

Let Frank or Angie realize they are accumulating *people* who remember things the system denies.

No roll is required.

That insight becomes one of the first seeds of a survivor network after the Shutdown.
