## Threat pacing & causal ambiguity

Do not make seven consecutive secret-tech betrayals. Alternate procedural accidents, opportunist crime, routine corporate pressure, genuine ambiguous system faults and warmer daily scenes. A rescuer gains a relationship, route or witness. A failed attempt should create strain, harm to replaceable property or delayed assistance, not lock away critical continuity or guarantee a civilian death.

**Narrative dice options** (check actual system skill labels in native sheets): Average for basic practical interventions, Hard for dangerous duct crawl or significant technical workaround. Success saves immediate stakes; advantage reduces collateral damage or wins a witness; Triumph produces sustained help; failure sends PCs to alternate method or staff support; threat raises public attention; despair triggers a credible lasting difficulty (damaged tools, official citation, separated NPC).

## Scene setup and reveal boundaries

Use only maps, actors, items and Foundry journals that actually exist in the current running install. In their absence, treat all new scenes as theater-of-the-mind. Narrate player-facing status contradictions and NPC observations; hide internal controller interpretation. Original scene source remains canonical in [archived Prelude](../../Original-Prelude-v2/README.md), and hidden hints in [GM edition](../../Original-GM-Prep/README.md).

**Avoid overfitting all accidents to Deus**. A human contractor can genuinely lose patience; an old rolling cart can fail through negligence; the mystery is that records and routing often contradict reality, not that every bystander is a puppet.

## Contingencies

- If PCs are still separated: crosscut each day, give each a real choice, never force them into collaboration before established December 19 return.
- If they become public heroes: staff may call them for help and security may recognize them; both consequences.
- If they hide: witness and scene events occur offscreen, changing later trust/routes.
- If NPCs are endangered: clarify distances and actual options, don't retroactively force harm.
- If players investigate one mystery for hours: move table boundaries, not fixed campaign timestamps.
- If they ask who controls the system: answer only with evidence the scene can plausibly yield.
