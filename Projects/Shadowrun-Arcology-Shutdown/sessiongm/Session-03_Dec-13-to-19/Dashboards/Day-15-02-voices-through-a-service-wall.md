## Quick beats

- Present alternatives: official rescue dispatch, manual bypass, duct crawl, cutting power and opening hydraulics.
- Inside is a cramped, warm service space with two accessible people; the third worker's location can remain uncertain without asserting a death.
- A leaking coolant line adds urgency but no cinematic flames; workers are capable adults who help.
- Evidence of a deliberate administrative disappearance can come later; saving people matters now.

## Voices / action

> A voice from inside: “Don't cycle it again. It locks harder every time.”

- Rescue first, preserve logs, split tasks, or escalate to supervisors.

## Reveals and branching

**Player-facing:** workers and residents have their own agendas; public safety and procedural contradictions are visible.  
**GM-only:** keep Deus and later revelations unspoken; do not assign a secret cause to this incident without confirming established canon.  
**Failure-forward:** slower route, witness cost, technical setback or public attention; never delete a mandatory prelude beat.

## Exit state

Survivors, if reached, give imperfect human testimony; whether all three are found becomes a player-driven unresolved thread.
