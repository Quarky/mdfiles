## Quick beats

- Preserve the original scene of warm daily life and later uninterrupted conversations.
- Offer a harmless but playable small errand: retrieve a delayed parcel, help prepare a performance, fix a heater, find a familiar comfort food.
- After one choice, shift to the canonical separate personal conversations. Do not pollute promises with ominous glitch interruptions.
- Write down tomorrow's exact plans and what each PC said, word for word.

## Voices / action
