## Quick beats

- Run Frank's canonical proof that perfect records are false and the message-draft scene.
- Present three independent evidence routes: worker memory, wear marks, logs; no one roll is decisive.
- Let the PCs decide whether protecting her anonymity matters more than immediate leverage.
- Offer a message between Angie and Frank; never force partnership or trust.

## Voices / action

> “I will show you the scratched panel. I won't sign a declaration that gets me fired.”

- Photograph physical evidence with consent, compare privately, or give the witness a route out.
- Delete a draft, send it, keep a paper copy; preserve the original established surviving log.

## Reveals and branching

**Player-facing:** workers and residents have their own agendas; public safety and procedural contradictions are visible.  
**GM-only:** keep Deus and later revelations unspoken; do not assign a secret cause to this incident without confirming established canon.  
**Failure-forward:** slower route, witness cost, technical setback or public attention; never delete a mandatory prelude beat.

## Exit state
