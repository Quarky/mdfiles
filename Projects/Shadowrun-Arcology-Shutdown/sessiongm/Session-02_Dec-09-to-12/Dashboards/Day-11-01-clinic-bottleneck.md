## Quick beats

- Begin with ordinary health and privacy choices from the approved clinic scene.
- A porter is separated from a patient when a safety door cycles. No fire, no explosion: a real person is panicking.
- Evan can stop the local flow, request override, or take responsibility for breaking protocol.
- After resolution, Mina resumes care rather than becoming the source of all answers.

## Voices / action

> Evan: “Both routes say approved. We cannot have two approved routes through one door.”

- Organize a calm human line, open the physical release, or contact maintenance.
- Investigate logs afterward, prioritize care, or delegate.

## Reveals and branching

**Player-facing:** workers and residents have their own agendas; public safety and procedural contradictions are visible.  
**GM-only:** keep Deus and later revelations unspoken; do not assign a secret cause to this incident without confirming established canon.  
**Failure-forward:** slower route, witness cost, technical setback or public attention; never delete a mandatory prelude beat.

## Exit state

Track clinic goodwill, Evan's confidence and whether an incident report mentions the PCs.
