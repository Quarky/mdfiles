## Quick beats

- Hidoshi checks whether Angie wants company; he sets a small doll within reach rather than forcing it into her hands.
- Invite Angie to describe the doll's appearance or choose from a soft hand-sewn figure, an old-fashioned cloth doll, or a small articulated figure. All are ordinary objects, never preloaded with a sinister secret.
- The hack remains a valid source of stress; do not have the gift erase it. Let Angie ask questions, refuse, laugh, hug him, change the subject or ask him to stay.
- Hidoshi still has ordinary consultancy calls and an errand; the day moves independently.

## Voices / action

> “I know I cannot unmake what happened. I wanted to give you one thing that did not ask you for a password.”

- Accept it, examine it, put it aside, or give it a name; keep response entirely player-led.
- Discuss boundaries or security after the earlier hack; Hidoshi offers practical help rather than a command.

## Reveals and branching

**Player-facing:** workers and residents have their own agendas; public safety and procedural contradictions are visible.  
**GM-only:** keep Deus and later revelations unspoken; do not assign a secret cause to this incident without confirming established canon.  
**Failure-forward:** slower route, witness cost, technical setback or public attention; never delete a mandatory prelude beat.

## Exit state

Record where the doll goes only if Angie chooses. Do not retroactively mark it as tracking equipment. Hidoshi may remain nearby or leave for work depending on her answer.
