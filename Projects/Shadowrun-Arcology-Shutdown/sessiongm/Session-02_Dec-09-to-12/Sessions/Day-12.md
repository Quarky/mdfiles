## Added scene 1: The lounge evacuation

**Suggested time:** 12:20, alongside canonical Matrix lounge  
**Location:** public Matrix lounge and adjoining retail passage  
**Purpose:** a first practical alliance emerges from the same evidence that threatens reputations. **Scene status:** optional additive play; cross-check original `December 12` scene order before insertion.

### Open at the table

> Headsets hang in tidy rows and the lounge smells of disinfectant. A teenager is pleading with the attendant for one more minute when the nearest shutters drop halfway and stop.

**Meanwhile, the living Arcology is already in motion:**
- **Working:** public Matrix lounge staff replace a dead headset and log every checkout.
- **Living:** a teenager needs a school assignment recovered before closing.
- **System:** user sessions quietly terminate at identical-looking error screens.

### People, agendas, and conversation

The attendant needs everyone out without losing wages; Sana wants customers redirected safely away from her shop.

> Sana: “If you close that shutter with people under it, you had better have a very good explanation.”

### Playable sequence

1. Continue original lounge evidence investigation; the logs retain their canonical evidentiary limits.
2. One shutter jams with two civilians divided across it; no one is intentionally trapped by the GM.
3. Choices to cut power, lift shutter, reroute the crowd or summon responders work differently.
4. The teen's assignment is a small object people care about; it is not a magical key to Deus.

### Meaningful player approaches

- Risk a visible rescue and the scrutiny it brings, or trust a professional response.
- Share an observation with staff or keep evidence private.

**Useful Genesys/Shadowrun checks:** Average Mechanics or Athletics for mechanical clearance, Average Leadership to prevent a crush, Average Computers to prove wrong status. Failure delays opening but staff reaches a manual latch.

**Pressure and stakes:** The crowd builds after 2 beats; threats should be tangible, not lethal by fiat.

### Consequences / handoff

Set public attention plus or minus; surviving witnesses remember behavior.

**Failure-forward safety:** never deny the original fixed event because one check fails. A failure means delay, cost, relationship tension, or loss of one evidence avenue; witnesses, physical traces, and alternative approaches stay available. Choose strain/damage only from credible hazards and system-native resolution.

## Added scene 2: The witness who wants to go home

**Suggested time:** 15:00–evening  
**Location:** service annex and ordinary public transit point  
**Purpose:** a first practical alliance emerges from the same evidence that threatens reputations. **Scene status:** optional additive play; cross-check original `December 12` scene order before insertion.

### Open at the table

> A tired worker waits with a coat over one arm, looking at a schedule that keeps moving her shift to tomorrow. She saw a service slot empty when the system swore it was occupied.

**Meanwhile, the living Arcology is already in motion:**
- **Working:** public Matrix lounge staff replace a dead headset and log every checkout.
- **Living:** a teenager needs a school assignment recovered before closing.
- **System:** user sessions quietly terminate at identical-looking error screens.

### People, agendas, and conversation

The witness wants to keep her job and catch a lift home; Frank and Angie may want the truth more than she does.

> “I will show you the scratched panel. I won't sign a declaration that gets me fired.”

### Playable sequence

1. Run Frank's canonical proof that perfect records are false and the message-draft scene.
2. Present three independent evidence routes: worker memory, wear marks, logs; no one roll is decisive.
3. Let the PCs decide whether protecting her anonymity matters more than immediate leverage.
4. Offer a message between Angie and Frank; never force partnership or trust.

### Meaningful player approaches

- Photograph physical evidence with consent, compare privately, or give the witness a route out.
- Delete a draft, send it, keep a paper copy; preserve the original established surviving log.

**Useful Genesys/Shadowrun checks:** Average Charm or Negotiation to gain consent without pressure; Average Computers for timestamp comparison; failure yields a later opportunity through another route.

**Pressure and stakes:** Witness needs to leave in 20 minutes, but can be found after if player sacrifices a social obligation.

### Consequences / handoff

The session ends with an actionable shared concern and no revealed mastermind.
