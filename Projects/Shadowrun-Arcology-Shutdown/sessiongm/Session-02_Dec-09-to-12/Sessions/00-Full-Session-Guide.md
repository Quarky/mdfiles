## Added scene 1: A gift with no conditions

**Suggested time:** morning, before Angie's approach to 7-G  
**Location:** breakfast table, garden bench, or a familiar quiet shop  
**Purpose:** ordinary affection before the known first-contact event. **Scene status:** optional additive play; cross-check original `December 9` scene order before insertion.

### Open at the table

> The Arcology is already awake. Coffee cools beside a folded paper bag. Hidoshi sits with it between his hands, evidently more nervous about a small conversation than any corporate meeting.

**Meanwhile, the living Arcology is already in motion:**
- **Working:** a concierge is wrestling with two incompatible room-delivery tickets.
- **Living:** a family tries to photograph a child on the balcony, losing the younger sibling behind shopping bags.
- **System:** the atrium directory discreetly reorders routes to favor premium shops.

### People, agendas, and conversation

Hidoshi wants to comfort Angie after the earlier hack without interrogating her or taking her choices away.

> “I know I cannot unmake what happened. I wanted to give you one thing that did not ask you for a password.”

### Playable sequence

1. Hidoshi checks whether Angie wants company; he sets a small doll within reach rather than forcing it into her hands.
2. Invite Angie to describe the doll's appearance or choose from a soft hand-sewn figure, an old-fashioned cloth doll, or a small articulated figure. All are ordinary objects, never preloaded with a sinister secret.
3. The hack remains a valid source of stress; do not have the gift erase it. Let Angie ask questions, refuse, laugh, hug him, change the subject or ask him to stay.
4. Hidoshi still has ordinary consultancy calls and an errand; the day moves independently.

### Meaningful player approaches

- Accept it, examine it, put it aside, or give it a name; keep response entirely player-led.
- Discuss boundaries or security after the earlier hack; Hidoshi offers practical help rather than a command.

**Useful Genesys/Shadowrun checks:** No mandatory roll. Optional Average Computers to discuss a sensible *player-chosen* device-security practice; failure identifies a tradeoff and invites help, not renewed compromise.

**Pressure and stakes:** A routine door alert interrupts the public space for a minute, but staff resolve it; no need to link every event to hidden systems.

### Consequences / handoff

Record where the doll goes only if Angie chooses. Do not retroactively mark it as tracking equipment. Hidoshi may remain nearby or leave for work depending on her answer.

**Failure-forward safety:** never deny the original fixed event because one check fails. A failure means delay, cost, relationship tension, or loss of one evidence avenue; witnesses, physical traces, and alternative approaches stay available. Choose strain/damage only from credible hazards and system-native resolution.
