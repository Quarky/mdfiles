### People, agendas, and conversation

1. Begin with ordinary health and privacy choices from the approved clinic scene.

3. Evan can stop the local flow, request override, or take responsibility for breaking protocol.
4. After resolution, Mina resumes care rather than becoming the source of all answers.

**Pressure and stakes:** 2 rounds/short beats before crowding makes access harder; choose a complication from equipment, security, and frightened relative.
