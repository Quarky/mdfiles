## One-minute GM reading

The arcology is a city full of sincere care, absurd procedures, and real danger. The authorities have explanations but the discrepancies remain. Hidoshi's doll is not a twist; it is a human gift. The first urgent rescue after December 10 should be interesting because a real person may be harmed, not because a named villain appears. Let the players decide whether evidence matters more than privacy, time, family, or a stranger.
