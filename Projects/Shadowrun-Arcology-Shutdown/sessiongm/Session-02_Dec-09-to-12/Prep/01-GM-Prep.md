## Rules and encounter quick mechanics

Genesys skill options are examples using Haakonsen-compatible native skills where present; confirm skill labels in actual actor sheets. **Average = two difficulty dice, Hard = three**. For combat, use existing NPC actors only if verified; otherwise favor a timed hazard/skill contest instead of fabricated combat statistics. Success grants the immediate goal. Advantage can earn trust, improve time or salvage evidence; Triumph gives a lasting useful witness or safe secondary route. Failure changes method or consumes time, not removal of required contact/log. Threat means noise, cost or attention; Despair may cause collateral consequence or a credible security inquiry, not a forced revelation.

## Deus-hint discipline

- **GM-only:** the original hidden hints and mechanisms. Consult [original GM hint edition](../../Original-GM-Prep/README.md).

## Safety/agency and hooks

Let the players choose whether to treat, report, investigate, rescue or go home. Some scenes can end when staff competently handle the situation. If players prolong December 10, let one real-world session stop there and carry unused dates forward without in-fiction montage over their expressed priorities.

Potential hooks at exit: the same physical fault disproves a perfect audit; a civilian whom the PCs helped knows a service shortcut; the message draft between Angie and Frank is still unwritten or sent; a witness fears retaliation. These are choices, not mandatory plot coupons.
