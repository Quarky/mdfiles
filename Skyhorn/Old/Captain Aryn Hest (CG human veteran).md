# Captain Aryn Hest (CG human veteran)

## Official Description
**Appearance:** Trim, pointed beard, gold tooth. Jagged electrical burns on hands and arms (from eelfolk).  
**Mannerisms:** Belly laughs so loud it rattles windows.  
**Secret:** Became a sailor when a mermaid saved his life and stole his heart.  
**Official Dialogue:** *“Bog me brains if I let a damn walking eel have me ship!”*

## Immersive Enhancements
*[Enhancement] Carries a locket with a tiny painting of a mermaid. The locket is tarnished but never leaves his neck. His voice is hoarse from screaming (eelfolk torture).*  
*[Enhancement Mannerisms] Laughs even when hurt. Calls everyone “sailor” or “son/daughter.” Spits when he says “eelfolk.”*

## Example Dialogue & Player Interactions
- **When rescued:** *“Bog me brains! You're not eels. Sheila sent you? Ha! That woman owes me a bottle of rum from ’09.”*
- **Warning about eelfolk:** *“They're clever. Don't let the hissing fool you. They set ambushes. And that big one — the Scourge — he'll rip your arm off and beat you with it.”*
- **About the summoning:** *“They've got Grigor in there. Poor sod. He's been chanting for hours. If he finishes, another elemental comes. You have to stop it.”*
- **After rescue (to Sheila, later):** *“You always did have a soft spot for strays. These ones are worth it.”*

## DM Notes
- Aryn is tough but exhausted. He can't fight until he rests.
- He knows the layout of the caves (he was dragged through them). Use him to give directions.
- His mermaid secret is a great roleplay moment if players ask about the locket.
