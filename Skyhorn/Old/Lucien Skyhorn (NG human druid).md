# Lucien Skyhorn (NG human druid)

## Official Description
**Appearance:** Short stature, wild white hair, grey eyes.  
**Mannerisms:** Constantly waggles bushy eyebrows. Prone to lecturing about the sea’s wonders.  
**Secret:** Feels responsible for his wife’s death many years ago.  
**Official Dialogue:** *“A Skyhorn will always be a beacon for good.”*

## Immersive Enhancements
*[Enhancement] Smells of salt, seaweed, and goodberries. Druidic focus is a polished nautilus shell. Mutters to a small pet crab named “Cinder” that lives in his pocket. His hands are gnarled from age and weather.*  
*[Enhancement Mannerisms] Waggles eyebrows for emphasis. Uses sea metaphors for everything (“We're in the doldrums now, but the wind will come.”).*

## Example Dialogue & Player Interactions
- **Through the door:** *“Who's there? Speak quickly, or I'll boil the hinges!”*
- **Once convinced:** *“Thank the tides. I thought I'd be eating goodberries until I turned into one. The eelfolk have the bowl. You have to stop them.”*
- **Explaining the bowl:** *“My family has kept it secret for six generations. It's fixed in place — draws on the sea's power. Twice a day it can summon elementals. We used it to help ailing ships. Now the eelfolk use it to capture them.”*
- **Giving potions:** *“These will let you breathe water. Go down through the trap door in the store room. Hurry — they're summoning another elemental soon.”*
- **If players try to take the bowl (afterward):** *“No. That stays here. It's bound to this place. Take it, and it becomes a useless rock. And you become my enemy.”*

## DM Notes
- Lucien is a font of information. Use him to steer players toward the caves.
- He is grateful but not a pushover. He will defend the bowl to the death.
- If the party saves the lighthouse, he becomes a valuable ally (scrying via speak with animals, healing potions).
