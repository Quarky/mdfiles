# Eelfolk Stormcaller

- **AC 11** (natural armor), **HP 78** (12d8+24), speed 30 ft, swim 40 ft.  
- **Str 11, Dex 16, Con 14, Int 8, Wis 14, Cha 18**  
- **Skills:** Intimidation +7, Perception +5, Stealth +8  
- **Damage Immunities:** lightning  
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft, passive Perception 15  
- **Challenge:** 6 (2,300 XP)

**Death Burst:** DC 13 Dex save, 7 (2d6) lightning.

**Hold Breath:** 1 hour.

**Innate Spellcasting (Charisma, save DC 15):**  
- At will: *detect magic, thunderwave*  
- 2/day each: *call lightning, fog cloud, lightning bolt*

**Multiattack:** Two claw attacks.

**Actions:** *Claws.* Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d6+3) slashing plus 7 (2d6) lightning damage.

**Reactions:** *Lightning Reflexes.* The stormcaller adds 3 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. Must see the attacker.

*[Enhancement] Stormcallers are created when an eelfolk is struck by lightning during a tempest – a rare, terrifying event. Sailors whisper that seeing one means a ship is doomed.*
