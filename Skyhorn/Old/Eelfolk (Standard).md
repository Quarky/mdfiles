# Eelfolk (Standard)

- **AC 15** (hide armor, shield), **HP 19** (3d8+6), speed 30 ft, swim 40 ft.  
- **Str 10, Dex 13, Con 14, Int 6, Wis 10, Cha 7**  
- **Skills:** Stealth +3  
- **Damage Immunities:** lightning  
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft, passive Perception 10  
- **Languages:** Eelfolk  
- **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP)

**Death Burst:** When the eelfolk dies, it explodes in a burst of electricity. Each creature within 10 ft must make DC 12 Dex save, taking 7 (2d6) lightning damage on a failed save, half on success.

**Hold Breath:** Can hold breath for 1 hour.

**Lunge:** If the eelfolk moves at least 20 ft straight toward a target and hits with a long knife attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) piercing damage.

**Actions:** *Long Knife.* Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. Hit: 4 (1d6+1) slashing damage.
