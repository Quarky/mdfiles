# Gaspar (NE human bandit captain)

## Official Description
**Appearance:** Tall, hunched, scar across nose. Grinds teeth, spits frequently.  
**Secret:** Fell from grace after betraying a lord.

## Immersive Enhancements
*[Enhancement] Crude hook for left hand (lost in the betrayal). His right hand never rests — always flexing. His clothes are salt-stained and patched. He has a gold tooth that he shows when he grins (which is rarely).*  
*[Enhancement Mannerisms] Spits on the ground after every sentence. Calls everyone “ye” or “land-lover.” Laughs like a barking seal.*

## Example Dialogue & Player Interactions
- **Confronting Sheila:** *“Ye owe us, Sheila! Yer ship is ours – and maybe that pretty tricorn, too.”*
- **To players:** *“Stay out of this, land-lovers. Unless ye want to feed the crabs.”*
- **If intimidated:** *“Fine, fine. But this ain't over.”* (He spits and backs away.)
- **If bribed:** *“Gold talks. But I'll be watching.”*

## DM Notes
- Gaspar is a bully, not a mastermind. He follows Neeno's lead.
- He will flee if reduced to 10 HP or if Neeno falls.
- His betrayal secret can be used as leverage in social checks.
