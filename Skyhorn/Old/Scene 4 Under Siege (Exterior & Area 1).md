# Scene 4: Under Siege (Exterior & Area 1)

## Immersive Area Description – Exterior

The stairs are slick with seawater and green algae. They switchback up the cliff face — 40 feet of treacherous stone, each step worn smooth by centuries of wind and wave. Salt spray mists your face. The wind howls, carrying the smell of low tide and something else… ozone. Like lightning. Like the eelfolk.

**The Summit:** At the top, a flat plateau 100 feet in diameter. A crumbling stone wall surrounds it, 10 feet high, breached in places. The gate door — once sturdy oak banded with iron — hangs from one hinge, flapping open and closed in the wind. *Thump. Creak. Thump.*

**The Lighthouse:** 60 feet of grey stone, stained black near the top. Its beam blazes into the sky, but it flickers — pulsing erratically. The light is still on. Lucien is still alive.

**The Grounds:** Broken barrels. A shattered cart. A dead seagull, its neck twisted. No bodies — the eelfolk must have dragged them inside. Or into the sea.

*[Enhancement] The air smells of salt, rust, and a faint electrical tang — the residue of eelfolk death bursts. The stone wall has scorch marks, as if lightning struck it recently. A single boot lies near the gate, still laced. No blood.*

## Entry Options (GM Notes)

- **Main door:** Barricaded from inside. DC 18 Strength check to break down. Breaking it alerts eelfolk in Area 1 (no surprise).
- **Climb walls:** DC 20 Strength (Athletics) to scale the damp lighthouse walls. A fall from 40 feet deals 4d6 bludgeoning.
- **Windows (40 ft up):** Lead to stairwell between floors. Shatter glass DC 12 Strength, open with tools DC 15 Dexterity (thieves' tools). Breaking glass creates noise (audible in Area 1).
- **Lantern room windows (60 ft up):** Same checks. [Lucien](#lucien-skyhorn-ng-human-druid) can open from inside if he knows the party is friendly.

## Area 1: Main Hall

### Immersive Area Description

The door groans open (if forced) or swings silently (if picked). Inside, the main hall of the lighthouse is a wreck.

**Lighting:** Dim. Arrow-slit windows let in pale grey light. The only other illumination comes from a guttering oil lamp on the far wall, nearly out of fuel.

**Furnishings:** Smashed barrels and splintered chairs litter the floor. Tapestries hang in shreds from the walls — once bearing the Skyhorn family crest (a nautilus shell), now torn down and trampled. A grand table lies on its side, legs in the air. Another has been flipped to form a crude barricade.

**The Enemy:** Six toothy eelfolk sit at the remaining tables, jabbering in their wet, grinding language. They haven't noticed you yet. They're arguing — pointing toward the stairs, making stabbing motions. Discussing how to break into Area 4 and kill Lucien.

*[Enhancement] The eelfolk are drunk on stolen rum. One has a bottle, tipping it back, amber liquid spilling down his chin. Another is trying to pry open a silver locket with a jagged knife, hissing in frustration. The floor is slick with seawater they've tracked in. Puddles everywhere. And in the corner, a pile of coral and shell trinkets — their loot.*

### NPCs (Official + Enhancement)

- **4 Eelfolk (Standard):** Marked E on map. Armed with long knives. Drunk and belligerent.
- **2 Eelfolk Hunters:** Marked H. Leaner, more alert. They have javelins and spears. One keeps looking toward the door, nervous.
- *[Enhancement] One hunter (the nervous one) has a crude tattoo of a lightning bolt on his forearm — a mark of respect for stormcallers.*

### Example Dialogue & Mannerisms

**Eelfolk (arguing):** *Hissss… “The stone-lung hides above. We break door. Kill. Take light.”* (He stabs the air with a knife.)

**Another eelfolk:** *“No. Shaman says wait. Elementals come. Let water do work.”* (He drinks from the bottle.)

**Nervous hunter:** *“Quiet! I heard something. Outside.”* (He licks his eyes nervously. Peers toward the door.)

**Drunk eelfolk (if confronted):** *“Land-meat! More land-meat! Kill them! Kill them all!”* (He charges, bottle still in hand.)

**If a hunter spots the party:** *“Intruders! To arms! To arms!”* (He hurls a javelin while flipping a table for cover.)

### Example Player Interactions

- **Stealth approach:** Group Dexterity (Stealth) check vs. hunters' passive Perception **12**. Success means the party can act first (surprise round). Failure means the eelfolk notice and attack.
- **Distraction:** A player could throw a rock or create a noise elsewhere (e.g., *minor illusion* of footsteps on stairs). DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) for the throw; if successful, one hunter goes to investigate, splitting the group.
- **Talk?** The eelfolk don't speak Common, but a character who knows Aquan or Deep Speech might understand them. No peaceful resolution — they want to kill Lucien and will attack anyone who interferes.
- **Use the environment:** The flipped tables provide half cover (+2 AC). The oil lamp can be shot (AC 10) to create a fire hazard — eelfolk hate fire (disadvantage on attacks if within 10 ft of open flame).

### Combat Tactics (Official + Enhancement)

- **Round 1:** The four standard eelfolk rush to melee, trying to surround weaker-looking PCs. The two hunters flip a table for half cover and throw javelins from range.
- **Reinforcements:** On round 2 of combat (excluding a possible surprise round), four more standard eelfolk and two more hunters arrive from Area 2 (Barracks). They enter through the north door.
- **Eelfolk tactics:** They use Lunge whenever possible (move 20 ft straight toward a target before attacking). They focus fire on wounded PCs. If a hunter is reduced to half HP, it tries to retreat toward Area 2 to warn reinforcements.
- *[Enhancement] The drunk eelfolk has disadvantage on attack rolls (due to intoxication) but advantage on saving throws against fear.*

### Treasure (Official)

- 24 gp total, scattered in pouches and on the floor.
- An assortment of coral and shell trinkets (worth 5 gp each, 6 total).
- *[Enhancement] The silver locket (10 gp) contains a miniature painting of a human woman. If returned to Lucien, he is grateful (grants inspiration or a minor magic item).*

## Area 2: Barracks (Official)

### Immersive Area Description

A narrow room off the main hall. Old bunk beds line the walls, their linens frayed and stained. A weapons rack stands empty — the eelfolk have taken everything. The floor is damp, and the air smells of unwashed bodies and spoiled food.

*[Enhancement] A half-eaten barrel of salted fish sits in the corner, flies buzzing around it. A child's doll lies trampled on the floor — remnants of a sailor's belongings.*

**Reinforcements:** If the party hasn't cleared Area 2, the four eelfolk and two hunters here will join the fight in Area 1 on round 2. If the party enters Area 2 first, they find these enemies here instead.

## Area 3: Store Room (Official)

### Immersive Area Description

Moldy food and clawed-apart barrels are scattered around the room. The air is thick with the smell of rot. A heavy barrel in the corner — larger than the others — has been shifted slightly. Beneath it, a faint outline of a trap door.

*[Enhancement] The barrel is labeled “PICKLED EEL – NOT FOR SALE” in faded Common — a dark joke from a previous lighthouse keeper. The trap door is iron, rusted, but opens with a groan.*

**Hidden Floor Grate:** DC 20 Intelligence (Investigation) to locate. Once found, it requires a DC 12 Strength check to lift. Leads to [Area 5](#area-5-something-in-the-deep) (the caverns below). Moving the heavy barrel quietly requires a DC 15 Stealth check — failure alerts enemies in Area 1.

## Dramatic Question (Official)

*Will the group avoid or defeat the eelfolk?*

## DM Prep Help

- **Battle map:** Draw the main hall (Area 1) with tables, barricades, doors to Area 2 and stairs up. Mark positions of 6 eelfolk.
- **Reinforcement timer:** Use a visible countdown (round 2) so players know more are coming.
- **Stealth mechanics:** Remind players about group checks. If half or more succeed, the group succeeds.
- **Trap door:** Have a description ready for when they find it. It's their gateway to the climax.

## GM Notes

- **Pacing:** This is the first major combat. Let it be messy and chaotic. The eelfolk are not tactical geniuses — they fight with ferocity but little coordination.
- **Reinforcements:** If the party is struggling, delay reinforcements by a round. If they're steamrolling, have reinforcements arrive early (round 1).
- **Alternate routes:** Players might bypass Area 1 entirely by climbing to windows. Reward creativity — but they'll still need to come down to reach the trap door.
- **Aftermath:** Once the eelfolk are dead, the party can search freely. Describe the shredded tapestries — a hint of the Skyhorn family's history.
