# Appendices: Maps, Treasure, Conflicts

## Appendix A: Maps Reference

The original PDF contains the following maps (player-safe and GM versions):

1. **The Docks** – Where Scene 2 (Scurvy Dogfight) takes place.
2. **The Annabel Lee** – The ship used for travel.
3. **Skyhorn Lighthouse** – Floor plans for Areas 1-4.
4. **Skyhorn Lighthouse Caves** – Areas 5-9, including shark positions, treasure chests, prisoner locations, and the summoning room.

**DM Tip:** Print or have digital copies ready. Use player-safe versions (without monster markers) for exploration, and GM versions for your reference.
