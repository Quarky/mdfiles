# Neeno (NE half-orc bandit captain)

## Official Description
**Appearance:** Remarkably tidy, oiled-back hair. Looks at nails, cleans them while talking.  
**Secret:** Plans to assassinate Gaspar and take his ship.

## Immersive Enhancements
*[Enhancement] Carries two daggers – one silver, one plain. Never draws them unless combat starts. Wears a black leather coat with brass buttons. His boots are polished.*  
*[Enhancement Mannerisms] Speaks softly, almost a whisper. Never raises his voice. Cleans his nails with a silver pick. Smiles thinly.*

## Example Dialogue & Player Interactions
- **Confronting Sheila:** *“We know you use weighted dice. We've caught you, wench. Hand over the ship quietly, and I'll let you keep your tongue.”*
- **To Gaspar, under his breath:** *“Keep your voice down. We want the ship, not a fight… yet.”*
- **If Insight check reveals his secret:** A player might whisper to Gaspar: *“Your friend plans to kill you.”* Gaspar's reaction: *“What? Neeno? We'll settle this later.”* (This could split the pirates.)
- **If defeated:** *“This isn't over. The sea remembers debts.”*

## DM Notes
- Neeno is calculating. He will surrender if outmatched to fight another day.
- His betrayal secret is a great lever for social resolution of the encounter.
- He carries a small vial of poison (2d6 damage, DC 13 Con save) for Gaspar.
