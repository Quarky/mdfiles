# Raf Frogwater (CG halfling commoner)

## Official Description
**Appearance:** Big green eyes, freckles.  
**Mannerisms:** Pretends not to be frightened by eelfolk.  
**Secret:** Wants to become an adventuring sailor, not a merchant.  
**Official Dialogue:** *“Those horrible eel-men, they took my friends! My Pa will get them for it!”*

## Immersive Enhancements
*[Enhancement] Carries a slingshot and three smooth stones. His clothes are torn and damp. He has a small wooden boat charm on a string around his neck.*  
*[Enhancement Mannerisms] Puffs out his chest when trying to be brave. Chews his lip when scared. Talks very fast when excited.*

## Example Dialogue & Player Interactions
- **When found:** *“I hid in the cabinet. They didn't find me. I was quiet. See? I'm brave.”*
- **If asked about the eelfolk:** *“The big one — he has scars all over. He yells at the others. They're scared of him.”*
- **If told he's going home:** *“But I wanted to see the adventure! Pa never lets me do anything.”*
- **Combat “help” (if not restrained):** Roll d4: 1 = hits ally (1 damage), 2-3 = miss, 4 = hits enemy (1 damage). Raf shouts: *“Take that, eel-face!”*

## DM Notes
- Raf is a liability in combat. The party will want to protect him.
- He can provide a crude map of the caves (drawn in charcoal on a torn sail) — DC 10 Investigation to interpret.
- His rescue is a key emotional beat. Play up his fear and relief.
