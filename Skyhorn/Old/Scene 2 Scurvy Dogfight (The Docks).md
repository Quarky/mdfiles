# Scene 2: Scurvy Dogfight (The Docks)

## Immersive Area Description


**The Atmosphere:** A thick, low-hanging fog—the kind the locals call "The Widow’s Shroud"—clings to the fjord’s surface. Ships sit at anchor, their sails furled. Somewhere a heavy iron bell tolls from the Jarl’s watchtower; it sounds mournful.

**The People:** Sailors cluster in small groups, crossing their fingers or touching iron charms to ward off "Sea Wraiths." Dockworkers move slowly, as if expecting something to rise from the black water.

*The Atmosphere:** A thick, low-hanging fog clings to the water's surface, muffling sounds. The air smells of low tide and nervous sweat. Ships sit at anchor, their sails furled, crews lounging on decks with nothing to do. Ropes creak against masts. Somewhere a bell tolls the hour — it sounds mournful.

**The People:** Sailors cluster in small groups, speaking in hushed tones. They point out to sea, then cross their fingers or touch iron charms. Dockworkers move slowly, as if expecting something to rise from the water. A few have already left for the day.

**The Lighthouse:** On the horizon, barely visible through the fog, Skyhorn Lighthouse glimmers faintly. Its beam is a weak pulse — not the strong, steady light the locals remember. Normally it would be a comforting presence, but now it seems distant, almost threatening.

The docks are choked with ships — fishing boats, merchant cogs, and one sleek schooner at the far end. The *Annabel Lee* is tied to the main pier, her rigging humming in the sea breeze. Her hull is dark wood, her sails furled but ready. A figurehead of a woman with long hair stares out to sea.

It's quiet compared to the normal bustle. No one is loading cargo. No one is shouting orders. The only sounds are the lapping of water against pilings, the creak of ropes, and the distant cry of gulls.

[Captain Sheila Coppertree](#captain-sheila-coppertree-cg-half-elf-spy) stands near the gangplank, one foot on the dock, one on the ship. She's flipping a pair of black tar dice in her palm, watching the party approach.

*[Enhancement] A single black gull perches on a piling, watching the party. The water is unusually still — almost oily. Somewhere a dog barks, then falls silent. The fog is thinner here, but the lighthouse beam is still weak.*

## NPCs Present

- [Sheila Coppertree](#captain-sheila-coppertree-cg-half-elf-spy)
- [Gaspar](#gaspar-ne-human-bandit-captain) and [Neeno](#neeno-ne-half-orc-bandit-captain)
- *[Enhancement] Four thugs (use bandit stats) hiding behind cargo crates on nearby ships. They peek out occasionally, waiting for a signal.*

## The Conflict (Official)

As the characters speak with Sheila, two figures emerge from behind a stack of barrels. Gaspar and Neeno. They swagger forward, hands on weapons.

Gaspar points a hook at Sheila. *“Ye owe us, Sheila! Yer ship is ours – and maybe that pretty tricorn, too.”*

Neeno adds, quieter, *“We know you use weighted dice. We've caught you, wench. Hand over the ship quietly, and I'll let you keep your tongue.”*

Sheila doesn't flinch. *“These two have been trailing me for days. They're all bluster until someone bleeds.”* She glances at the party. *“What do you say? Help an old sailor out?”*

**Starting Conflict Score:** 3 (collective). Success if score reaches 0, combat if reaches 5.

**Resolving the Conflict (Official):**
- A large bribe (50 gp or more) reduces score by 1.
- DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) or (Deception) check reduces score by 1. Failed check increases by 1.
- Charisma (Intimidation) increases score by 1 (they're proud).
- DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) reveals one of the following: Neeno's secret plan to assassinate Gaspar, Gaspar's fall from grace, or the current conflict score.

## Example Dialogue & Mannerisms

**Gaspar:** *“Ye owe us, Sheila! Yer ship is ours – and maybe that pretty tricorn, too.”* (He spits on the dock. Grinds his teeth audibly.)

**Neeno:** *“We know you use weighted dice. We've caught you, wench. Hand over the ship quietly, and I'll let you keep your tongue.”* (He doesn't look at Gaspar. He's watching Sheila's hands.)

**Sheila (quietly to party):** *“These two have been trailing me for days. They're all bluster until someone bleeds.”* (She flips her dice once more, then pockets them.)

**Neeno (to Gaspar, under his breath):** *“Keep your voice down. We want the ship, not a fight… yet.”* (A PC with passive Perception 14 or higher overhears this.)

**Gaspar (if intimidated):** *“Fine, fine. But this ain't over.”* (He spits again and backs away.)

**Neeno (if bribed):** *“Gold talks. But I'll be watching.”* (He counts the coins slowly, then pockets them. His eyes never leave the party.)

## Example Player Interactions

- **Use Insight to sow discord:** A player who learns Neeno's secret can whisper to Gaspar: *“Your friend plans to kill you.”* Gaspar's reaction: *“What? Neeno? We'll settle this later.”* (Conflict score reduces by 2 as they argue.)
- **Intimidate the thugs:** A successful DC 15 Intimidation check aimed at the hidden thugs might make them hesitate, delaying their appearance by one round if combat starts.
- **Bribe with a promise:** If the party has no gold, they can promise a share of future treasure. Deception DC 15 to make it believable. If they intend to honor it, Persuasion DC 15.
- **Physical intimidation:** A character can smash a barrel or break a crate with a weapon. DC 12 Strength check. If successful, Gaspar flinches (conflict score -1). If failed, he laughs (score +1).

## Combat (Official + Enhancement)

If a fight breaks out:

**Round 1:** Gaspar and Neeno draw weapons. They try to shove characters into the water (opposed Athletics check). A character in the water must spend half movement to climb out (DC 12 Athletics).

**Round 2:** Four thugs pop up on nearby ships (two on each side) and fire crossbows. *[Enhancement] Thugs shout: “Boss said we get first pick of their boots!”*

**Tactics:**
- Gaspar focuses on Sheila or the strongest-looking PC.
- Neeno hangs back, throwing daggers, and tries to flank.
- Thugs target spellcasters or anyone already wounded.
- Enemies flee if reduced below one-third of their hit points.

**Environmental Hazards:**
- **Slick dock:** A character who moves at full speed must make DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics) or fall prone.
- **Water:** Swimming characters are at disadvantage for melee attacks unless they have a swim speed. Ranged attacks are at disadvantage unless using a crossbow, net, or thrown weapon (dagger, javelin, spear, trident).
- **Crates and barrels:** Provide half cover (+2 AC).

## Treasure

If the pirates are defeated or flee, the party can loot:
- **Gaspar:** 15 gp, weighted dice (worth 5 gp to a gambler), rusty shortsword, a silver locket (worth 10 gp) containing a lock of hair.
- **Neeno:** 20 gp, two daggers (one silver, worth 25 gp), a small pouch of caltrops, a vial of serpent venom (2d6 poison damage, DC 13 Con save).
- **Thugs (each):** 5 gp, light crossbow with 10 bolts, padded armor (AC 11).

## DM Prep Help

- **Battle map:** Draw or print a dock map with the *Annabel Lee* on one side, crates for cover, and two nearby ships where thugs appear.
- **Monster cards:** Have bandit captain and bandit stats ready.
- **Underwater rules cheat sheet:** Print a small card for swimming, holding breath, and underwater combat.
- **Social resolution path:** Prepare a quick flowchart for the conflict score system so you can adjust on the fly.

## GM Notes

- **Dramatic Question:** *Can the characters appease or defeat the pirate threat?*
- This encounter can be entirely social. Reward creative solutions — bribing the pirates with a promise of future treasure, revealing Neeno's betrayal to Gaspar (which turns them against each other), or using magic (e.g., *charm person* or *suggestion*) to diffuse the situation.
- The pirates are not suicidal. If Gaspar or Neeno drops below 10 HP, they surrender or flee.
- The dock is narrow; large area spells might hit civilians or damage ships. Warn players indirectly: *“There are fishing nets and barrels everywhere — and people watching from nearby ships.”*
- **Aftermath:** If the pirates are defeated, the dockworkers who were watching might cheer. If the party resolved it peacefully, sailors nod respectfully.

## Transition

The PCs collect what they need and strike out on the *Annabel Lee* toward the lighthouse. This leads to [Scene 3: Sea Monster Attacks](#scene-3-sea-monster-attacks).
