To further flesh out **Gundbarg** as your campaign’s central hub, here are detailed descriptions of its districts, atmosphere, and local landmarks based on the **Gundarlun Island** lore.

### **The Atmosphere of Gundbarg**

Gundbarg is a city of verticality and stone. Because the fjords are so steep, the city is built in "tiers" connected by winding stairs and crane-lifts.

- **The Scent:** The air is a constant battle between the sharp, clean smell of the salt spray and the heavy, earthy scent of peat fires used to heat the stone longhouses.
    
- **The Sound:** It is never truly quiet. Even when the wind dies down, you can hear the rhythmic _clack-clack_ of looms in the weavers' district and the deep, melodic chanting of Northlander sailors as they row their longships out to sea.
    
- **The Light:** Because the cliffs are so high, the sun only hits the harbor floor for a few hours a day. The rest of the time, the city is bathed in a cool, blue twilight, illuminated by whale-oil lamps hanging from iron brackets.

### **Key Districts & Locations**

#### **1. The Finger-Fjord Docks (The Lowlands)**

This is where the adventure begins. The docks are not a single wharf but a series of wooden piers pinned directly into the cliff walls.

- **The Maw:** The widest part of the fjord where the deep-water merchant cogs anchor. It is a forest of masts and rigging.
    
- **Crane’s Perch:** A massive wooden crane powered by a treadmill of giant goats. It is used to haul heavy cargo from the ships up to the High Market.
    
- **The Sea-Gates:** Two massive iron-banded timber gates that can be closed to protect the harbor from storm surges or pirate raids.
    

#### **2. The High Market (The Mid-Tier)**

Built on a natural stone shelf halfway up the cliff, this is the heart of Gundbarg’s trade.

- **The Amber Exchange:** A specialized market where merchants trade the famous Gundarlun amber (fossilized resin found in the northern forests).
    
- **The Bone-Carver’s Row:** Shops selling intricate jewelry and tools made from whalebone and walrus ivory.
    
- **The Ice-Pits:** Deep cellars carved into the permafrost used to store the city’s massive supply of salted fish and seal meat.
    

#### **3. The Jarl’s Reach (The High-Tier)**

The upper-most part of the city, where the wind is strongest and the view is most spectacular.

- **Kingsholm:** The longhouse of Jarl Olav Antonsen. It is built of dark pine logs with a roof of overlapping slate tiles that look like dragon scales.
    
- **The Pillar of Oghma:** A small, stone shrine to the god of knowledge, maintained by a few lonely acolytes who keep the island’s navigation charts.
    
- **The Widow’s Peak:** A lookout point where families watch for the return of the fishing fleets. From here, on a clear day, you can see the faint, flickering pulse of the **Skyhorn Lighthouse** on the horizon.

### **Local Landmarks for PCs to Explore**

- **The Frost-Forge:** A smithy known for its "sea-tempered" steel. The blacksmith, a burly Northlander named Bjorn, claims that quenching blades in the freezing fjord water makes them resistant to rust.
    
- **The Salty Skald’s Hall:** A rival establishment to the _Cracked Cask_. This is where the local poets and storytellers (Skalds) perform. It’s the best place to find rumors about the "Sea Wraiths" (Eelfolk) or the history of the Skyhorn family.
    
- **The Grotto of Deep Currents:** A small, natural sea cave at the water's edge that serves as a communal shrine to **Valkur**, the Northlander god of sailors. It is filled with small wooden boat models left as offerings.
    

### **The "Dragon’s Teeth" (Navigational Hazard)**

These jagged black rocks rise from the water just outside the fjord mouth like the fangs of a beast. At high tide, they are hidden just beneath the surface.

- **The Wreckage:** A sharp-eyed PC can see the skeletal remains of at least three ships pinned against the rocks—victims of the failing lighthouse.
    
- **The Warning:** The locals say that if you listen closely to the wind whistling through the rocks, it sounds like the screams of the sailors who died there.
    

### **Impact on the Quest**

Because the city is so reliant on its harbor, the failing lighthouse isn't just a mystery; it’s an economic crisis.

- **Benji’s Desperation:** He isn't just worried about his son; he’s losing thousands of gold pieces every day his ships remain trapped in the harbor or at risk on the rocks.
    
- **Sheila’s Urgency:** She knows the weather is turning. If the party doesn't leave soon, the "Widow's Shroud" fog will become so thick that even the _Annabel Lee_ won't be able to find the lighthouse island.
### **Scene 1: The Request (The Cracked Cask)**

The adventure begins in the **Cracked Cask**, an amber-lit tavern that serves as a warm refuge from the bitter winds outside.

**Atmosphere:** The tavern is filled with the low, gruff tones of Northlanders and the smell of roasting meat. Benji Frogwater, a halfling merchant dabbing sweat from his brow, stands out in the room of bearded giants.

**Benji’s Plea (Word-for-Word):**

- “My boy… my cargo… I’ll pay anything. 200 gold each just to investigate, and another 200 if you bring back my son and crew. I’ve asked everyone. The harbor master laughed at me. The Northlanders say to wait for the storm to pass. I can't wait. Raf is only eight. He's never even seen a real fight. Please.”
    

**Locations within The Cracked Cask:**

- **The Hearth:** A massive stone fireplace where sailors gather to dry their gear and swap stories about "Sea Wraiths".
    
- **The Bar:** A thick slab of salt-stained oak where the barkeep serves potent Northlander ale and warm cider.
    
- **The Merchant’s Corner:** A slightly quieter section of the tavern where traders like Benji conduct business away from the main bustle.
    
- **The Maps Table:** A scarred wooden table often covered in charcoal drawings of the Finger-Fjords and the surrounding Dragon's Teeth.
