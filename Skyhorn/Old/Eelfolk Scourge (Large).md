# Eelfolk Scourge (Large)

- **AC 13** (hide armor), **HP 85** (10d10+30), speed 30 ft, swim 40 ft.  
- **Str 18, Dex 13, Con 16, Int 7, Wis 13, Cha 9**  
- **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +5  
- **Damage Immunities:** lightning  
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft, passive Perception 13  
- **Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP)

**Death Burst:** DC 13 Dex save, 7 (2d6) lightning.

**Electrified Body:** A creature that touches the scourge or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 ft takes 5 (1d10) lightning damage.

**Hold Breath:** 1 hour.

**Lunge (claw or bite):** Extra 7 (2d6) piercing if moved 20 ft.

**Multiattack:** Three melee attacks: one bite, two claws.

**Actions:**  
- *Bite.* Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 9 (2d4+4) piercing.  
- *Claws.* Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6+4) slashing.
