# Scene 5: The Light in the Tower (Area 4)

## Immersive Area Description

The stairs wind up — 60 feet of narrow stone steps, spiraling around the inside of the lighthouse. The walls are damp, streaked with salt. Arrow-slit windows let in pale light and cold wind.

**The Door:** At the top, an iron door — black, studded with rivets. It's barricaded from the inside with a heavy wooden beam. A faint light glows through the cracks. The smell of burning oil is strong here.

**Lucien's Voice (through the door):** *“Who's there? Speak quickly, or I'll boil the hinges!”* His voice is hoarse but defiant.

**Inside (if the door opens):** The lantern room is a circular chamber encased in thick, salt-crusted glass. The center is dominated by the Great Lens — a massive assembly of brass and crystal, polished to a mirror shine. A flame burns within, its light focused into a blazing beam that cuts through the fog.

The floor is littered with goodberry seeds, a half-empty waterskin, and a small pile of kindling. A tiny crab — Cinder — skitters near Lucien's feet. The old druid stands by the lens, one hand on the brass housing, the other holding a staff.

*[Enhancement] The air is warm and heavy with oil vapor. The glass windows vibrate slightly with each crash of the waves below. One panel has a spiderweb crack — from a thrown rock or a javelin.*

## NPC: Lucien Skyhorn (NG human druid)

### Immersive Description

**Appearance:** Short, wiry, with wild white hair that stands on end. His grey eyes are sharp, intelligent. His clothes are simple — wool and leather, stained with salt. He wears a polished nautilus shell on a cord around his neck.

**Mannerisms:** Constantly waggles his bushy eyebrows for emphasis. Talks to his crab (“Cinder, stay close”). Uses sea metaphors for everything. When nervous, he runs his fingers through his hair, making it even wilder.

**Secret:** He blames himself for his wife's death — she drowned in a storm while he was tending the light. He could have saved her if he'd left his post, but the light would have gone out. He chose duty. He's never forgiven himself.

### Example Dialogue & Player Interactions

**Through the door (hostile):** *“Who's there? Speak quickly, or I'll boil the hinges!”* (He has *heat metal* ready.)

**If the party identifies themselves as allies:** *“Prove it! Kill an eelfolk and bring me its head! No — too slow. Tell me something only a friend would know.”* (He knows about the bowl, the trap door, the cove.)

**Once convinced:** *“Thank the tides. I thought I'd be eating goodberries until I turned into one. The eelfolk have the bowl. You have to stop them.”*

**Explaining the bowl:** *“My family has kept it secret for six generations. It's fixed in place — draws on the sea's power. Twice a day it can summon elementals. We used it to help ailing ships. Now the eelfolk use it to capture them.”*

**Giving potions:** *“These will let you breathe water. Go down through the trap door in the store room. Hurry — they're summoning another elemental soon.”* (He hands each PC a Potion of Water Breathing.)

**If asked about his wife:** *“That's a sorrow for another day. Focus on the living.”* (His eyes cloud, and he turns back to the lens.)

**If players try to take the bowl (afterward):** *“No. That stays here. It's bound to this place. Take it, and it becomes a useless rock. And you become my enemy.”* (He raises his staff. He's not bluffing.)

### Lucien's Spells (Official)

- **Cantrips:** *druidcraft, poison spray, produce flame*
- **1st level (4 slots):** *create/destroy water, cure wounds, goodberry, speak with animals*
- **2nd level (3 slots):** *animal messenger, heat metal*

### Example Player Interactions with Lucien

- **Convincing him to open the door:** DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) or a creative answer to his “something only a friend would know” test (e.g., describing the Skyhorn crest, mentioning the bowl's location, or saying “Sheila sent us”).
- **Asking about the caves:** He provides a rough mental map — the trap door in Area 3, the flooded tunnels, the hidden cove (Area 7).
- **Requesting healing:** He has *cure wounds* and will use it if the party agrees to help.
- **Learning about eelfolk:** He knows they hate fire, are immune to lightning, and explode when killed.

## Dramatic Question (Official)

*Will the characters help Lucien defeat the eelfolk and save the Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals?*

## `[CONFLICT]` Gemini's “Grishtak the Burner” (hostile shaman in lantern room) **directly contradicts** the official module where Lucien is alone and friendly. *Do not use unless you rewrite the module entirely.*

## DM Prep Help

- **Voice for Lucien:** Gruff but warm once trust is established. Slightly breathless from exhaustion. Use his eyebrow-waggling as a visual cue.
- **Potion of Water Breathing reminder:** Make sure players note these on their sheets. They last 1 hour.
- **Information delivery:** Lucien is the main exposition dump. Have a bullet-point list of what he knows so you don't forget anything.

## GM Notes

- **Pacing:** This is a roleplay breather after the combat in Area 1. Let the party ask questions and plan their next move.
- **Lucien's trust:** He's paranoid but not unreasonable. If the party saved him from the eelfolk downstairs, he'll trust them immediately.
- **The bowl:** Emphasize its importance. This is the macguffin. The party should understand that stopping the summoning ritual is the goal.
