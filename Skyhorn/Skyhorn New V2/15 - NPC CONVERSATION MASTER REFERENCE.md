# 15 - NPC CONVERSATION MASTER REFERENCE

## Benji Frogwater

### Voice

Fast when anxious; sentences run together; becomes very clear when discussing Raf.

### What He Knows

- *Jade Lion* is overdue.
- Raf is aboard.
- Aryn Hest is captain.
- Sheila is willing to investigate.
- Witnesses describe unnatural water near Skyhorn.
- Lucien is lighthouse keeper.

### What He Doesn't Know

- Current condition of Raf/Aryn/crew.
- Eelfolk numbers.
- Bowl.
- Caves.
- Exact nature of "sea monster."
- Smuggler involvement.

### Useful Lines

**Opening:** "My son Raf is aboard the *Jade Lion*. Captain Hest too. I need someone willing to go after them."

**On cargo:** "The cargo matters to my business. The crew matters more. Bring back what you can after you know they're safe."

**On officials:** "They want better information before they send people out. I understand that. I also know my son is still out there while they wait."

**On reward:** "Two hundred each to find the truth. Another two hundred each if you bring my people home."

**On Raf:** "He tries to make his eyes look brave before the rest of his face catches up."

**If PCs promise certainty:** "Don't promise me he's alive. Promise me you'll look properly."

## Captain Sheila Coppertree

### Voice

Casual humor until danger becomes practical. Never panics in front of crew.

### What She Knows

- Saw unnatural wave pull *Jade Lion* toward Skyhorn.
- Aryn Hest is aboard.
- Lucien is reliable and stubborn.
- Lighthouse light remains active but wrong.
- Local sea/tide behavior.
- Gaspar/Neeno have been following her.
- Fennick's *Slippery Eel* left port recently and hasn't returned.

### What She Doesn't Know

- Bowl details.
- Eelfolk occupation scale.
- Exact location of Jade Lion.
- Current status of captives.
- What the smugglers are actually doing.

### Useful Lines

**Greeting:** "You here about Skyhorn? Good. I need hands that don't freeze when the sea gets ugly."

**On fear:** "Being afraid is fine. Check your knots twice and keep listening. Panic is when people stop hearing orders."

**On Lucien:** "He talks too much, knows the coast, and has kept that light burning through weather that drove better sailors into port."

**On Aryn:** "If he's alive, he'll be looking for a way out. He's stubborn like that."

**On the sea:** "I know what wind and tide can do. The wave that took the *Jade Lion* moved against both."

**On heroics:** "Save the speech. On my deck, if I shout, move first and ask why after."

**On Fennick (if asked):** "Nervous little man. Runs cargo nobody asks about. If he's out there, he's either found something valuable or something's found him."

## Gaspar

### Voice

Loud, grinding teeth, spitting, trying to dominate public space.

### Wants

Win without looking weak. Ship if possible. Respect from Neeno even though he suspects none.

### Useful Lines

**To party:** "Stay out of it, landlubbers."

**If challenged:** "Ye think numbers make ye brave?"

**If given exit:** "Fine. Later, then."

**If Neeno exposed:** "Say that again."

**If beaten:** "This ain't finished."

## Neeno

### Voice

Soft enough that people lean in; neat; controlled; no wasted emotion.

### Wants

Leverage, survival, ship, future position over Gaspar.

### Useful Lines

**Opening:** "We can settle this without making the dock ugly."

**If bribed:** "Now we're talking about something measurable."

**If threatened:** "If you mean it, act. If you don't, save us both the time."

**If betrayal exposed:** "You're trusting a stranger over me?"

**If withdrawing:** "Fine. We leave it here—for now."

- "Benji's been asking every crew in the room. Most of them won't go near Skyhorn."
- "Sheila's at the end of the main pier. Blue tricorn. You'll hear her before you see her."

## Eirik Ropehand — Optional Dock Foreman

### Useful Lines

- "I keep them busy. Idle crews start listening to the worst storyteller in the harbor."
- "Bad weather I can plan around. Captains are refusing this route because none of them can explain what happened to the *Jade Lion*."
- "If you're sailing, secure your own gear. My people have enough to do."
- "Fennick? Aye, the *Slippery Eel*. Left two days back. Odd little ship. Odd little man."

## Tova Arnsdottirsdottir — Optional Fish Seller

### Useful Lines

- "Merchants are counting storage fees. Families are counting who hasn't come home. Both are angry, for different reasons."
- "Find the missing crews. You can argue about the monster afterward."

## Old Njal Fen — Optional Lighthouse Supply Hand

### Useful Lines

- "Salt ruins everything if you give it time. Lucien spends half his life keeping the tower one repair ahead of it."
- "Lucien can tell you which stair squeaks in winter and which window sticks in a north wind."
- "The lamp has gone out before. Weather, cracked glass, bad oil. This skipping beam is different."
