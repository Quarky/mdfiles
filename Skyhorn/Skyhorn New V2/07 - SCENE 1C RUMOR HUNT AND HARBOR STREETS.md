# 07 - SCENE 1C: RUMOR HUNT AND HARBOR STREETS

## Scene Goal

This is optional connective tissue for groups that enjoy investigation and roleplay. Use it to make the world feel larger without adding a second mystery.

## Quick Investigation Menu

Give players this naturally through descriptions rather than as a literal menu if possible.

They can:

- Inspect the lighthouse beam from shore.
- Speak to dockworkers.
- Check shipping manifests.
- Ask lighthouse suppliers.
- Visit the watch.
- Speak to stranded captains.
- Observe animals/weather.
- Buy or prepare maritime gear.
- Find Sheila.
- Ask about unusual ships or recent departures (smuggler thread).

## Lighthouse Observation

### Without a Roll

The beam remains visible but irregular.

### If Someone Studies Timing

Tark/Borrik or any attentive PC can establish:

- It's not simply obscured by fog every time it fails.
- The irregularity is real.
- The tower is still producing light, meaning total abandonment is unlikely.

### If Someone Uses Magic to Inspect From Afar

Reward creativity with detail, not spoilers. The lighthouse remains too distant for most low-level magic to reveal the caves or occupants.

## Dockworker Conversations

### Eirik Ropehand

If asked what changed first:

> "Depends who you ask. Merchants say the missing ship. Sailors say the light. Old people say the birds."

If asked whether the docks are dangerous:

> "Only in the ordinary ways. Ropes, cranes, idiots, debt collectors."

If asked about Sheila:

> "She checks her hull herself. That already puts her above half the captains in port."

### Smuggler Whispers (Optional)

If the PCs ask about strange ships or unusual cargo:

> "Fennick? Aye, the *Slippery Eel*. Little ship, nervous captain. Runs cargo nobody asks about. Left two days back, heading toward Skyhorn. I figured he was just another fool chasing a payday. Maybe he found one. Maybe he found something else."

## Shipping Records

Records can establish:

- The *Jade Lion* is genuinely overdue.
- Aryn Hest is experienced rather than reckless.
- Traffic near Skyhorn dropped sharply after fear spread.
- No mundane storm alone explains the pattern of cancellations.
- If you want to seed the smuggler thread: the *Slippery Eel* filed no cargo manifest and listed no destination.

### Nymera's Personal Thread

If present, include the doubled/altered cargo mark as a separate inconsistency. It doesn't point to the eelfolk—or it might, if you choose to link it to the smugglers. Either way, it's her thread to pull.

## Watchtower / Harbor Watch

The watch isn't hiding evidence. Their problem is uncertainty and limited resources.

A watch officer can say:

> "Send a patrol boat into a storm rumor and I'm gambling with other people's lives. Give me a target, a survivor, a place to search—something."

If the PCs say they will investigate:

> "Then bring back facts. And if you find people, bring them back first."

Cadwallon and Aelar may appreciate this more than characters expecting corruption.

## Supplies and Preparation

Let sensible preparation pay off later narratively:

- Ropes ready near rail.
- Packs secured below deck.
- Cold-weather clothing.
- Waterproofing documents.
- Simple medical supplies.
- Food for stranded survivors.
- Extra lanterns.

Don't turn every item into a bonus. Use them to reduce friction or create automatic success where reasonable.
