# 08 - SCENE 2A: THE DOCKS AND THE ANNABEL LEE

## Scene Goal

Introduce Sheila as a capable recurring ally, let players see the real cost of the harbor stoppage, and position Gaspar/Neeno as a social problem before the voyage.

## Foundry

Activate **The Docks**.

Keep the *Annabel Lee* visible as the obvious ship preparing to leave.

## READ ALOUD — The Docks

> Fog hangs low over the docks, hiding the far ends of the piers. Ships sit packed together with their sails furled, and dockworkers work around stacks of cargo that should already be gone.
>
> A watchtower bell sounds above the mist. At the end of the main pier, the dark-hulled *Annabel Lee* is being made ready to sail. Her crew is actually moving with purpose.

That person is Sheila.

## Sheila Coppertree — Fast Roleplay Card

**Presentation:** Tan, athletic half-elf; blonde braid; faded or weathered tricorn; smells of salt and rum; black tar dice constantly moving through one hand.

**Voice:** Warm, teasing, decisive under pressure.

**Habit:** Calls people shipmate or landlubber; sings or hums sea songs; spits over left shoulder for luck before sailing.

**Wants:** Reach Skyhorn; find Aryn; keep crew and ship alive.

**Won't do:** Abandon the wheel during the sea attack; join the lighthouse caves later unless circumstances radically change.

## Sheila Opening

If she knows the party is coming:

> "There you are. I was starting to think Gundbarg had talked another group into becoming sensible."
>
> She rolls two black dice over her knuckles and catches them without looking.
>
> "Captain Sheila Coppertree. That's the *Annabel Lee*. She's faster than she looks, meaner than she looks, and currently the only ship in this harbor whose captain hasn't developed an urgent theological objection to sailing."

If they haven't spoken to Benji:

> "Before we go: Frogwater's boy is on the missing ship. If you haven't spoken to him, do it now or accept that you're going out there with less information than I'd like."

## Sheila Conversation Tree

### "What Did You See Happen to the Jade Lion?"

She becomes serious.

> "A wave took her sideways."
>
> "Not rolled her. Not hit her. Took her."
>
> "Wind said one thing. Tide said another. Water ignored both."

### "How Far Is Skyhorn?"

> "About three hours in this weather if the sea behaves."

Pause.

> "I'm no longer using that as a prediction."

### "Why Are You Willing to Go?"

> "Aryn Hest was aboard. He's my friend."
>
> "Lucien Skyhorn is out there too, if the light means what I think it means."
>
> "And because eventually somebody has to be the first ship to stop being afraid."

### "What Is the Lighthouse Like?"

She can describe its isolated rocky setting and Lucien's stubbornness but not the current interior state.

> "Stone tower. Bad landing in bad weather. Worse stairs. Lucien keeps it like it's a member of his family, which in fairness it practically is."

### "What Do You Know About Creatures From the Sea?"

> "Plenty of creatures live in the sea. Most of them have better things to do than steal merchant ships."

### "Do You Believe the Monster Stories?"

> "I believe what I saw."

### "Who Is Annabel Lee?"

She smiles without humor.

> "That's a long story for a calm night and a bottle we're not opening before sailing."

Don't force the romantic/rival reveal unless the player earns intimacy.

### "What Do You Need From Us on the Ship?"

> "Stay out of the crew's way until I need you. If I shout, listen the first time. If somebody goes overboard, throw a line before you jump after them."

That last line foreshadows the attack.

## Sheila's First Impressions of the PCs

Use selectively.

- **Valemon:** Notices competence and privacy; doesn't pry.
- **Tark/Borrik:** Appreciates inspection, mildly mocks over-analysis until it proves useful.
- **Dan/Cassian:** Banter; refuses to be charmed into unsafe choices.
- **Kreslin/Elira:** Answers precise questions, complains if they interview the crew while lines are being hauled.
- **Sarduuk/Veszara:** Judges performance by action, not status.
- **Cadwallon/Aelar:** Naturally places them where protection matters.
- **Mara:** Quietly points out a crew member with a strained wrist or split knuckle.
- **Rurik:** Gives him a line to haul immediately.
- **Lethiel:** Provides practical sailor observations about birds, currents, and weather.
- **Nymera:** Answers ship-movement questions directly if they don't compromise someone's private cargo business—or mentions Fennick's odd departure if pressed about unusual traffic.

## Annabel Lee — Recurring Crew Faces

The source gives Sheila a crew of twenty loyal sailors using **bandit** statistics. Most do not need names or individual actors. Four recurring faces are enough to make the ship feel crewed without turning the voyage into an NPC roll call.

### Branna Keel — Bosun

Weathered human woman, shaved at the temples, heavy wool coat, missing the tip of one ear. Branna handles work assignments and has no patience for passengers standing in rigging lanes.

**Use:** Orders PCs out of the way, hands practical jobs to Rurik/Cadwallon/Aelar, and becomes Sheila's voice on deck while Sheila is at the helm.

**Line:** "If you want to help, take that line. If you don't know what that means, ask before the ship moves."

### Olek Vann — Deckhand

Young Northlander sailor with a broken nose and a habit of checking knots twice. He has heard every sea-monster story in Gundbarg and believes about a quarter of them.

**Use:** Good target for Mara's pre-voyage check, Dan's humor, or Kreslin/Elira questions from a normal sailor's perspective.

**Line:** "I've sailed bad water. Bad water doesn't usually come looking for you."

### Piri Underbough — Cook / Deckhand

Middle-aged lightfoot halfling with a wool cap pulled down over both ears. Piri cooks when the sea is calm and works deck when it is not.

**Use:** Keeps the crew human. Offers hot broth before departure, complains about heroes bleeding on clean planks, and helps injured sailors after the elemental attack.

**Line:** "If you're going to be sick, use the lee rail. If you're going to die, try not to do that either."

### Sael Thorne — Lookout

Lean half-elf with grey-green eyes, a long scarf, and the unnerving habit of going quiet in the middle of a sentence when something on the horizon moves.

**Use:** Can notice the wrong-way wave if no PC does. If a PC is actively keeping watch, Sael confirms what they saw instead of stealing the discovery.

**Line:** "There. Port side. That water's cutting across the chop."

All four can use the existing **Bandit** actor mechanically. Their job in Session 1 is continuity, not combat spotlight.

## Living Dock Activity

While the party speaks:

- A crewman rolls a barrel aboard and mutters a prayer under his breath.
- Someone on a neighboring vessel shouts, "Coppertree! Write your will before you go!" Sheila answers with an obscene hand gesture.
- A dockworker asks whether the party is really going to Skyhorn; their face shows hope and fear equally.
- A black gull watches from a piling.
- The water near the dock is unusually still between wind gusts.
- If you want to seed the smuggler thread: a dockhand mentions the *Slippery Eel* again. "Fennick's boat still hasn't come back. Not that anyone's mourning him. Nasty little man. But still."

Then Gaspar and Neeno arrive.
