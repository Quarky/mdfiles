# 20 - SESSION 1 RUMOR TABLE

Roll or choose when players ask random locals instead of named NPCs.

| d12 | Rumor | Truth status | GM use |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | "The lighthouse keeper died and his ghost keeps the light burning." | False | Shows superstition. |
| 2 | "The *Jade Lion* was dragged toward Skyhorn by a wave moving against the tide." | True core | Reliable clue. |
| 3 | "Something with tentacles wrapped around the ship." | False/exaggerated | Monster-story inflation. |
| 4 | "Captain Hest could sail blindfolded through worse weather than this." | Broadly true reputation | Establishes Aryn competence. |

| 8 | "Merchants invented the monster to raise prices." | False as total explanation | Cassian/Dan social texture. |
| 9 | "The water near Skyhorn glowed green." | Unconfirmed | Ominous but not solution. |
| 10 | "Lucien Skyhorn once talked a storm out of sinking a ship." | Folkloric exaggeration | Builds Lucien reputation. |
| 11 | "Pirates are using the panic to steal unattended cargo." | Possible local opportunism | Foreshadows Gaspar/Neeno theme without tying them to main plot. |

Don't make every rumor actionable. A living town produces noise.
