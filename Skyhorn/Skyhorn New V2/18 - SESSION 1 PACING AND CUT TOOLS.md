# 18 - SESSION 1 PACING AND CUT TOOLS

## If Roleplay Is Running Long

Compress without deleting agency.

### Cracked Cask Compression

After one good Benji scene and one rumor exchange, summarize the remaining consensus and send them to Sheila.

### Dock Compression

Start Gaspar/Neeno conflict score at 2 if players already have strong leverage or if you need a fast resolution.

### Voyage Compression

Use one conversation beat and one foreshadowing beat before the elemental.

## If Session Is Running Short

### Best Cliffhanger 1 — After Docks

> The *Annabel Lee* pulls away. Gundbarg disappears into Widow's Shroud.

### Best Cliffhanger 2 — Elemental Appears

> The wave rises against the wind and forms shoulders.

### Best Cliffhanger 3 — Lighthouse Arrival

Recommended.

> The gate above swings once in the wind. Something scrapes on stone beyond it.

## If Session Is Running Fast

Add depth rather than extra combat:

- More party conversation aboard ship.
- Technical lighthouse observation.
- Minor ship repair after elemental.
- Investigate dock and cliff base.
- Climb to broken gate and end before Area 1.

Only enter the lighthouse proper if everyone wants the longer session.
