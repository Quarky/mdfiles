# 13 - SCENE 3C: SKYHORN LIGHTHOUSE ARRIVAL / SESSION CLIFFHANGER

## Scene Goal

End Session 1 with certainty that the crisis is larger than the harbor rumor and a clear physical objective for Session 2.

## Foundry

Activate **Skyhorn Lighthouse — Exterior Approach**.

If session time is very short, keep the party on the dock and reveal the exterior visually without beginning exploration.

## READ ALOUD — Arrival

> The *Annabel Lee* strikes the dock hard enough to make every plank complain.
>
> Above you, the island rises in black wet rock. A narrow stone stair switches back up the cliff through wind and spray.
>
> At the top stands a broken wall and the lighthouse beyond it—sixty feet of grey stone stained dark by salt.
>
> The light is still burning.
>
> It sweeps once across the water.
>
> Flickers.
>
> Sweeps again.
>
> The gate above hangs crooked on one hinge.
>
> Nothing moves around it.

Then give them silence.

## What They Can See Immediately

- The dock is algae-slick and weathered.
- The stone stair climbs toward the lighthouse grounds.
- The summit wall is damaged.
- The gate is broken.
- The lighthouse remains active but irregular.
- No *Jade Lion* is visible from this side.
- Signs of recent violence may be visible higher up if they advance.
- The island feels abandoned from below, but the active light contradicts that impression.
- **Optional smuggler detail:** On the far side of the island, barely visible through the mist, a small dark-hulled ship sits anchored in a cove. No lights. No movement on deck. It's the *Slippery Eel*—but the party won't know that unless they've been tracking it.

## Sheila's Decision

Sheila needs to inspect and secure the *Annabel Lee*. This naturally removes her from the assault team without making her cowardly.

> "I can keep her afloat, or I can go sightseeing with you. Pick which one gets us home."

If the party insists she come:

> "Aryn needs rescuing. So do all of us when you're finished. I'm staying with the ship."

## Party Decision Point

Ask:

> "The stairs are in front of you. The ship behind you is damaged but holding. What do you do before you climb?"

Possible actions:

- Short rest.
- Inspect ship damage.
- Secure treasure.
- Scout stairs.
- Send familiar/animal.
- Inspect water.
- Search dock.
- Immediately climb.

For the recommended session boundary, let them state the plan and **end before resolving the first lighthouse encounter**.

## Strong Session-End Lines

Choose one based on tone.

### Ominous

> Somewhere above, from inside the wall, something scrapes across stone.

### Heroic

> Behind you, the *Annabel Lee* is still floating. Ahead, the Eye of the Storm is still burning.
>
> Somebody has held on long enough for you to arrive.

### Mystery

> The beam passes overhead.
>
> For the first time from this close, you see that the light itself is steady.
>
> Something else is making it fail.

Use the last line only if it fits your intended technical interpretation.

### Smuggler Thread (Optional)

> On the far side of the island, half-hidden by mist and rock, a small ship sits at anchor. No lights. No movement. Just waiting.
>
> Whoever they are, they got here before you.
