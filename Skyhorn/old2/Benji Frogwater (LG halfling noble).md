# Benji Frogwater (LG halfling noble)

## Official Description
Very concerned merchant. Offers reward. His son [Raf](#raf-frogwater-cg-halfling-commoner) is on the *Jade Lion*.

## Immersive Enhancements
*[Enhancement] Appearance: Round spectacles, red nose, constantly dabbing sweat from brow. Fingers stained with ink from ledgers. Wears a velvet coat that's slightly too large.*  
*[Enhancement Mannerisms] Twists his wedding ring while speaking. Speaks quickly, runs words together when anxious. Paces in small circles.*  
*[Enhancement Secret] He lost his wife to a fever last year. Raf is all he has left.*

## Example Dialogue & Player Interactions
- **Initial plea:** *“My boy… my cargo… I'll pay anything. 200 gold each just to investigate, and another 200 if you bring back my son and crew.”*
- **If pressed:** *“Please. I've asked everyone. The harbor master laughed at me. The guard captain said to wait. I can't wait. Raf is only eight. He's never even seen a real fight. Please.”*
- **If players hesitate:** *“I've heard of your reputation. You handle things others can't. I need people who handle things. Name your price — within reason.”*
- **If Raf is rescued (later):** Benji weeps openly, hugs his son, then shakes each PC's hand with both of his. *“Anything. Anything you need. You have my eternal gratitude.”*

## DM Notes
- Benji is not a fighter. He stays in town.
- He can provide a letter of credit or a favor instead of gold if the party prefers.
- His grief is genuine; play him as desperate but not pathetic.
