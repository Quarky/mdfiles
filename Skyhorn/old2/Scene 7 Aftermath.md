# Scene 7: Aftermath

## The Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals

### Immersive Description

The ritual chamber falls silent. The bowl's water settles, the blue glow fading to a dull glimmer. The eelfolk lie dead — their bodies crackling with residual electricity, then dissolving into brine and ash. Grigor, the terrified sailor, slumps against the bowl, sobbing.

[Lucien Skyhorn](#lucien-skyhorn-ng-human-druid) appears in the doorway, leaning on his staff. Cinder the crab scuttles at his feet. He looks at the bowl, then at the party. His eyes are wet.

**Lucien's Dialogue:** *“You've done it. Six generations of Skyhorns have protected this place, and you… you saved it. Thank you.”* He kneels beside the bowl, touching the obsidian rim. *“The bowl is safe. The sea is calm. For now.”*

### The Bowl's Rules (Official)

- The bowl is **fixed in place**. It cannot be removed without destroying its magic.
- If pried from the ground, it becomes a mundane stone bowl (no magical properties).
- It can summon a water elemental twice per day (dawn and dusk) when activated by a creature touching it and speaking a command word (known only to Lucien and his bloodline).
- The summoner must maintain concentration (as per *concentrating on a spell*) or the elemental becomes hostile to everyone.

### Player Choices & Consequences

**If the party tries to take the bowl:**

Lucien's face hardens. *“No. That stays here. It's bound to this place. Take it, and it becomes a useless rock. And you become my enemy.”* He raises his staff. His crab hisses.

- Lucien will not fight the party unless they persist. Instead, he warns them: *“Leave now. And don't come back.”*
- He does **not** award the Mariner's Coins. He gives a terse *“thank you and goodbye.”*
- Future adventures with Lucien are impossible (he considers the party oathbreakers).

**If the party leaves the bowl (expected outcome):**

Lucien relaxes, his shoulders sagging. *“You are true friends of the lighthouse. Please, accept these — a token of my gratitude.”* He reaches into a pouch and produces a handful of hammered copper coins, each stamped with a shining lighthouse.

**Reward:** Each PC receives a **Mariner's Coin**.

- *Mariner's Coin:* When presented to a non-hostile sailor, it grants advantage on one Charisma check to influence him or her.
- *[Enhancement] The coin also works once on any sailor who recognizes the Skyhorn crest (anyone who has sailed these waters for more than a year).*

## Sailing the Jade Lion

### Immersive Description

Back on the surface, the sea air is a relief after the caves. The *Jade Lion* still lists in the cove, but [Captain Aryn Hest](#captain-aryn-hest-cg-human-veteran) — now freed and bandaged — stands on its deck, barking orders. His voice is hoarse but strong.

**Aryn's Dialogue:** *“You there! Grab that line! We've got cargo to salvage and sailors to heal. And someone find me a bottle of rum — I've earned it.”*

### Mechanics (Official)

- The *Jade Lion* has a snapped mast but can sail at half speed with a crew of at least 10 sailors.
- A DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) check is required to navigate back to port (Aryn can make this with advantage).
- The ship's cargo (what remains) is worth 5,000 gp (Area 8a). The party can claim a share (negotiate with Benji).

### Aryn's Gratitude

- He gives each PC a **favor** (a future free passage on his ship, or a letter of introduction to another captain).
- He tells a story about the mermaid who saved him (his secret) if asked. *“She had eyes like green glass and a voice like the tide. I've never loved another.”*

## Returning Home

### Immersive Description

The *Annabel Lee* waits at the dock, [Sheila Coppertree](#captain-sheila-coppertree-cg-half-elf-spy) at the helm. She waves as the party approaches. *“You look like hell. Get on board — I've got fresh water and bandages.”*

The voyage back is quiet. The fog has lifted. The lighthouse beam is steady now — Lucien has fixed it.

**In Port:** Benji Frogwater waits on the dock, surrounded by guards. When he sees Raf — alive — he bursts into tears. *“My boy! My boy!”* He hugs his son, then shakes each PC's hand with both of his.

### Rewards (Official)

- **Returning Raf:** 200 gp per PC.
- **Returning surviving crew and chests of goods:** Additional 200 gp per PC.
- **Total possible:** 400 gp per PC.

**Additional Rewards:**
- Benji sends a detachment of four soldiers and one captain to repair and guard the lighthouse henceforth.
- [Sheila Coppertree](#captain-sheila-coppertree-cg-half-elf-spy) will sail the PCs to any port of their choice, as promised.
- *[Enhancement] Benji also offers a 10% discount on any future purchases from his merchant network.*

## Future Adventure Hooks (Official)

### Hook 1: Sheila's New Mission

*“You handled yourselves well out there. I've got another job — if you're interested. There's a pirate lord operating out of the Gray Isles. Name of Annabel Lee. Ever heard of her?”* (Sheila's secret love. She wants to find her — or confront her.)

### Hook 2: Lucien's Strange Visitor

Weeks later, a messenger seagull finds the party. Lucien's voice: *“A strange sailor came to the lighthouse. He asked about the bowl. He knew things he shouldn't. I'm worried. Can you come?”*

### Hook 3: The Eelfolk Clan

Rumors reach the party of a nearby eelfolk clan, led by a recently ascended **eelfolk stormcaller**. The clan is gathering strength, planning revenge for the slaughter at Skyhorn Lighthouse. The party may wish to root them out before they strike.

### Enhancement: The Stormcaller's Challenge

If the party pursues the eelfolk clan, they find a coastal cave network. The stormcaller (use [Eelfolk Stormcaller](#eelfolk-stormcaller) stats) has a lair with lightning traps, enslaved merfolk, and a magical storm that surrounds the area. Defeating her earns the party a **Cloak of the Manta Ray** and the gratitude of coastal villages.
