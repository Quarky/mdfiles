# Eelfolk – General Enhancements

*[Enhancement – all eelfolk] They have a nervous tic of licking their own eyes (like geckos). Their skin is slick with mucus that smells like low tide. When they speak Eelfolk, it sounds like wet gravel grinding. They hate fire and bright light – optional rule: disadvantage on attack rolls if within 10 ft of a lit lantern or torch.*  
*[Enhancement – tactical] Eelfolk prefer to ambush from water. They use hit-and-run tactics, retreating into deep water to heal (if possible). They fear lightning (ironic, given their immunity) — actually, they revere it. A stormcaller is treated as a demigod.*
