# DM Prep & Tools

## Quick Reference: Underwater Combat & Breathing

| Rule | Summary |
|------|---------|
| **Swimming** | Each foot costs 1 extra foot (unless swim speed). |
| **Hold Breath** | 1 + Con mod minutes (min 30 sec). |
| **Suffocation** | After breath ends, survive Con mod rounds (min 1), then drop to 0 HP. |
| **Underwater Melee** | Disadvantage unless swim speed or using dagger, javelin, shortsword, spear, trident. |
| **Underwater Ranged** | Disadvantage unless crossbow, net, or thrown weapon (dagger, javelin, spear, trident). |
| **Fire damage** | Resistance underwater (unless from a source that creates its own oxygen, e.g., *fire bolt* doesn't work, *flame strike* does). |

## Common Player Questions & Answers

**Q: Can we take the bowl?**  
A: Lucien will forbid it. The bowl loses magic if moved. You can try, but he'll become hostile and you'll lose the Mariner's Coins.

**Q: What if we befriend the eelfolk?**  
A: They're chaotic evil and hate surface-dwellers. No peaceful resolution — they want to kill Lucien and control the lighthouse.

**Q: Can we save the water elemental's Beads of Force for later?**  
A: Yes. They're magic items. Use them as described (DMG).

**Q: What if we miss the trap door in Area 3?**  
A: Lucien will tell them about it (Scene 5). If they don't go to him, have them find the trap door via a Perception check (DC 15) when searching the store room.

**Q: Can we recruit Aryn Hest or Sheila for the caves?**  
A: Aryn is too weak to fight (exhaustion). Sheila must guard the ship. The party is on their own.

## Final DM Advice

- **Tension:** The caves are the climax. Don't rush. Describe the bioluminescent fungi, the cold water, the distant shark fins.
- **Player agency:** Let them choose the order — go up to Lucien first, or down to the caves? Both work.
- **Pacing:** If the party is low on resources before Area 9, consider having Grigor lose concentration early, turning the elemental against the eelfolk (a three-way fight).
- **Heroic moments:** Encourage creative solutions — using *create water* to blast a shark, pushing a stalagmite onto the scourge, or intimidating the eelfolk after killing their leader.
- **Aftermath:** End with a sense of accomplishment. The lighthouse beam is steady. The harbor is safe. The party has earned their reward and a place in local legend.
