

## Immersive Area Description

**The Atmosphere:** The air in Gundbarg is sharp and carries the sting of sleet. Unlike the muddy High Road of Leilon, the harbor here is a maze of deep, dark "finger-fjords" carved into towering grey cliffs. The smell of pine resin and smoked fish hangs heavy. Large Northlander longships with dragon-headed prows bob in the dark water alongside merchant cogs from the south.

**The People:** Towering Northlanders in thick furs and iron-studded leather move with purpose. They speak in gruff, low tones, glancing at the darkening sky. There is a sense of communal dread; the "Eye of the Storm" (the lighthouse) is failing, and for a culture that lives and dies by the sea, this is an ill omen.

**The Meeting:** You meet **Benji Frogwater** in a warm, amber-lit tavern called the _Cracked Cask_. He stands out—a round, spectacles-wearing halfling dabbing sweat from his brow in a room full of bearded giants.

## Key Locations for the Adventure

- **The Barge Yard (The Docks):** Due to the town's shallow mud flats, Leilon lacks a true harbor. Large, sturdy barges are poled out from a barge yard to meet ships in deeper water to transfer cargo via rickety cranes. This is a perfect spot for the encounter with [Captain Sheila](#captain-sheila-coppertree-cg-half-elf-spy) and the pirates, **Gaspar** and **Neeno**, who might be trying to strong-arm the operation.

- **Taverns for Rumors:**
    - **The Knight's Goblet:** A traveler-focused inn described as "clean, boring, and overpriced." This is where the nervous, honest sailor might be found.
    - **The Orc's Tusks:** A favorite of locals, "friendly, crowded, and cheap." The braggart who lies about the monster would feel right at home here.

- **The Town Council:** The settlement is run by a council likely led by **Grizzelda Copperwraught**, with other members managing the fishery, construction, and defenses. They could serve as alternative quest-givers.

# Scene 1: Rumors in the Harbor (Leilon Adaptation)

The adventure begins in Leilon's Barge Yard. The town has the typical housing, provisions, and shipping dock, but everything is tinged with the frontier reality of a town in the midst of rebirth. Traffic in the harbor has come to a standstill amidst the rumors.

## Immersive Area Description

The characters begin in the muddy, bustling barge yard on the edge of Leilon. Unlike a proper harbor, the shore here is a shallow, silty flat. A dozen massive, battered barges are tied to a rickety wooden pier. Their crews are using long poles to navigate the shallows, offloading goods from a ship anchored further out using groaning, crane-like contraptions. The air smells of low tide, mud, and nervous sweat.

Normally, this place would be a cacophony of shouting laborers and creaking cranes. But today, it's different. Workers cluster in small groups, speaking in hushed tones. They point out to sea, where the faint glimmer of Skyhorn Lighthouse is visible on a small island a few miles out. Some cross their fingers or touch iron charms, and a few have already left for the day.

## NPCs & Dialogue

### Benji Frogwater (LG halfling noble) – *Appeal to Reward*

**Immersive Appearance:** Short, even for a halfling, wearing a merchant's coat that is slightly too large. He has round spectacles perched on a red nose and is constantly dabbing sweat from his brow. He wrings his hands as he speaks.

**Dialogue:**
> *“Please, you must help. My son, Raf, is on the Jade Lion. I don't care about the cargo. I'll pay anything. Two hundred gold each just to investigate. Another two hundred if you bring them back alive.”*
> *“I've asked the harbor master. He laughed. The guard captain said to wait. I can't wait.”*

### Captain Sheila Coppertree (CG half-elf spy) – *Appeal to Heroism*

**Immersive Appearance:** A tan, athletic woman with a long blonde braid, favoring a faded blue tricorn hat. She leans against a piling, flipping a pair of black tar dice.

**Dialogue:**
> *“You look like the sort who doesn't run from a storm. I need a crew for a short voyage. My friend, Aryn Hest, was on that ship. I saw a wave grab it—a wave moving against the tide. That's not natural. I'll take you there for free if you help.”*
> *“Old Lucien Skyhorn is stubborn as barnacles. If he's still alive, the light will be on.”*

### Sailors (Rumor Sources)

- **The Liar (at The Orc's Tusks):** *“I saw it! Tentacles as big as masts! Eyes like burning coals!”* (Avoids eye contact.)

- **The Nervous Sailor (at The Knight's Goblet):** *“I didn't see no monster. But I saw the Jade Lion get dragged toward the lighthouse by a wave that was going the wrong way. Water just… grabbed it. I'm not going out there.”*

### The Seagull – *Appeal to Discovery*

A seagull lands on a character or a nearby crate. It cocks its head and opens its beak. A man's weary voice comes out:

> *“This is a message from Lucien Skyhorn, from the lighthouse. We've been attacked by creatures from the sea! Please, send help before it's too late, I beg any who hears this…”*

It squawks once, then flies clumsily away. A few feathers drift down.

## Example Player Interactions

- **Asking for Rumors:** DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) gets a sailor to talk. Success → the nervous sailor's truthful account. Failure → the liar or someone who refuses.

- **Insight on the Liar:** DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) reveals he's lying. A PC can call him out; he slinks away embarrassed.

- **Finding Sheila:** Sailors point toward the barge yard: *“Sheila Coppertree is crazy enough to try. Look for the woman singing about mermaids.”*

- **Following the Seagull:** DC 15 Perception to track it, but it flies out to sea toward the lighthouse (reinforces the hook).

## GM Notes

- **Pacing:** 5–10 minutes. Goal: give the party a reason to go to the barge yard and meet Sheila.

- **Tone:** Anxious, not panicked. People are scared but not fleeing.

- **If players get stuck:** Have Sheila approach them directly. She's been watching, sizing them up.

## Transition

The PCs go to the barge yard to seek passage to or information about Skyhorn Lighthouse → leads to **Scene 2: Scurvy Dogfight**.
