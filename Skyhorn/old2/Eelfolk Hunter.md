# Eelfolk Hunter

- **AC 13** (hide armor), **HP 39** (6d8+12), speed 30 ft, swim 40 ft.  
- **Str 14, Dex 13, Con 14, Int 6, Wis 10, Cha 7**  
- **Skills:** Perception +2, Stealth +5  
- **Damage Immunities:** lightning  
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft, passive Perception 12  
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)

**Ambuser:** The hunter has advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has surprised.

**Multiattack:** The hunter makes two spear attacks.

**Actions:** *Spear.* Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 6 (1d8+2) piercing (two-handed melee) or 5 (1d6+2) piercing (ranged).
