# Captain Sheila Coppertree (CG half-elf spy)

## Official Description
**Appearance:** Tan, athletic, blonde braided hair, favors tricorn hats.  
**Mannerisms:** Sings bawdy sea songs about mermaids. Loves black tar dice.  
**Official Dialogue:** *“Want to lose at a round of black tar dice?”*

## Immersive Enhancements
*[Enhancement] She hums “The Mermaid’s Lament” when nervous — a slow, mournful tune. . She always smells of salt air and rum.*  
*[Enhancement Mannerisms] She rolls dice between her fingers when thinking. She calls everyone “shipmate” or “landlubber.” She spits over her left shoulder for luck before sailing.*

## Example Dialogue & Player Interactions
- **Approaching the party:** *“You look like the sort who doesn't run from a storm. Name's Sheila. I need a crew for a short voyage. Well, not a crew — I have a crew. I need muscle. And maybe some magic. You've got that look.”*
- **About the lighthouse:** *“Old Lucien Skyhorn is stubborn as barnacles. If he's still alive, the light will be on. And look there — it's still burning. That means he's fighting. That means there's hope.”*
- **If asked about Annabel Lee:** *“That's a story for a quieter night and a full bottle of rum. Let's just say the sea is wide, but not wide enough.”*
- **During the dock fight (to party):** *“These two have been trailing me for days. They're all bluster until someone bleeds.”*
- **After the water elemental attack:** *“The *Annabel* has taken worse. I'll patch her up. You go do what you do best.”*

## DM Notes
- Sheila is brave but not reckless. She won't leave the ship unless absolutely necessary.
- She knows the sea and can provide exposition about tides, currents, and the lighthouse's history.
- If the party befriends her, she becomes a recurring ally (future adventures).
