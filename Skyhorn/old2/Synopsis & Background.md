# Synopsis & Background

## Synopsis (Official)
Ships have stopped entering the harbor due to “sea monster” fears. Skyhorn Lighthouse is in distress. Captain [Sheila Coppertree](#captain-sheila-coppertree-cg-half-elf-spy) takes the party there. A water elemental attacks en route. At the lighthouse, [eelfolk](#eelfolk-standard) have it under siege. The party finds flooded caves where eelfolk force captured sailors to use a **Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals** to summon elementals.

## Background (Official)
- [Lucien Skyhorn](#lucien-skyhorn-ng-human-druid) protects the bowl, hidden in caves below the lighthouse.  
- Eelfolk entered via a hidden cove, found the bowl, captured the *Jade Lion*, and now force crew to summon elementals (elementals sometimes turn on summoners).  
- Eelfolk want to kill Lucien (barricaded in lantern room) to control the island.

## DM Prep Help
- **Read the whole module once** before running. The caves (Areas 5-9) are the climax.
- **Prepare battle maps** for the dock fight, ship deck, lighthouse main hall, and cave complex.
- **Print monster cards** for eelfolk, hunters, scourge, sharks, and water elemental.
- **Underwater rules cheat sheet:** swimming costs double movement, hold breath = 1+Con mod minutes, ranged weapons disadvantage unless crossbow/net/spear/javelin/dagger/trident.
- **Potion of Water Breathing** – Lucien gives these. Remind players to use them before entering water.
