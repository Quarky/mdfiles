# 04 - PC HOOK INTEGRATION MATRIX

## How to Use This Section

Only use the rows for PCs actually selected by players. The hooks should create **attention and choice**, not secret mandatory plot lines.

Before starting, ask each player to answer the final question from their player hook. Write the answer next to the character name. That answer is your easiest source of personalized table material.

## Valemon

**Starting concern:** preserving the sacred Fanhaen seedling; Lucien may know coastal preservation methods.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** Old Njal or Yrsa can confirm that Lucien keeps hardy coastal plants and knows how to protect living things from salt exposure. Benji’s fear for Raf should resonate without requiring Valemon to talk about his past.

**Docks spotlight:** Sheila notices the care Valemon gives the seed container and says only, “If it matters that much, keep it below deck when the spray starts.” No prying.

**Voyage spotlight:** offer a choice: protect the seed below deck during rough weather, or keep it on his person. Neither should be a mechanical punishment; it is roleplay pressure.

**Sea attack spotlight:** a crew member may reach for the same dry storage where Valemon placed the seed. Give him a chance to save both person and object rather than forcing a cruel binary.

**Do not force:** do not damage or kill the seed merely to motivate him.

## Tark Stonefire

**Starting concern:** the lighthouse failure behaves like a system failure, not an omen.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** give him three mundane observations without a roll if he asks sensible questions:

1. The beam has become irregular rather than simply dim.
2. Supply runs to the lighthouse have been disrupted by fear only recently.
3. Sailors report unnatural water movement closer to Skyhorn.

**Docks spotlight:** Eirik Ropehand appreciates precise questions. Sheila respects Tark checking lines, hull, and deck before departure.

**Voyage spotlight:** he can compare the beam’s rhythm at different distances. The closer they get, the less the problem resembles normal maintenance alone.

**Sea attack spotlight:** this is proof of an external hostile force. Tark should feel vindicated but not finished: the elemental explains attacks, not necessarily the beam’s failure.

**Do not force:** do not make every technical question require Arcana. Ordinary observation matters.

## Handsome Dan

**Starting concern:** workers and families pay first when systems fail.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** let dockworkers know him if the player claimed that connection. A worker asks Dan whether all “heroes” wait for rich merchants to offer coin before helping.

**Docks spotlight:** the Gaspar/Neeno confrontation is Dan’s natural stage. Bystanders are watching. His ability to reframe the confrontation publicly can matter as much as a formal check.

**Voyage spotlight:** Sheila is unimpressed by performance but amused by good jokes. She can challenge him to make frightened crew laugh without pretending the danger is fake.

**Sea attack spotlight:** give Dan a visible rescue opportunity: dangling line, fallen crewman, or panicking sailor who needs direction.

**Do not force:** do not turn every scene into applause. Dan cares about people more than being liked.

## Kreslin

**Starting concern:** deaths and disappearances at sea; magical force or summoning.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** two witnesses describe the same event differently. Kreslin can identify that both may be honest but frightened.

**Docks spotlight:** a sailor who survived a strange wave farther down the coast recognizes Kreslin if that connection was claimed. The sailor insists the water “moved like it had a decision.”

**Voyage spotlight:** the absence of normal animal noise near Skyhorn is relevant evidence, not just mood.

**Sea attack spotlight:** if Kreslin examines the elemental or its remains, confirm that it is magical and compelled rather than a natural sea creature. Do not reveal the bowl.

**Do not force:** locals can find him unsettling without treating necromancy as automatic villainy.

## Sarduuk Magavario

**Starting concern:** build influence, favors, and remembered successes.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** Benji immediately understands social capital. If Sarduuk speaks in terms of obligation, Benji can offer introductions and future merchant access rather than only money.

**Docks spotlight:** Neeno tries to assess whether Sarduuk is a buyer, patron, or threat. Sheila assesses whether he is useful under pressure.

**Voyage spotlight:** let Sarduuk learn which kinds of reputation matter to sailors: showing up, keeping promises, and not endangering a crew for vanity.

**Sea attack spotlight:** a dramatic use of fire or magic will absolutely be remembered by the crew, but so will protecting someone instead of chasing the biggest display.

**Do not force:** do not punish ambition. Let influence be a legitimate character motivation.

## Cadwallon ap Bliedd

**Starting concern:** quiet Harper inquiry; protect civilians and determine whether the disruption hides a larger threat.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** an old Harper contact mark can exist in an unobtrusive location if the player claimed it. It confirms only that Harpers have passed through Gundbarg before; it does not hand him the mystery solution.

**Docks spotlight:** give Cadwallon a clear view of civilian positioning before the pirate confrontation escalates. He can move people, establish a safe lane, or stand between weapons and workers.

**Voyage spotlight:** Sheila respects professional calm and may quietly ask him whether he thinks the others are ready.

**Sea attack spotlight:** an ideal protector scene—crew, helm, and overboard risks all compete for attention.

**Do not force:** avoid making his Harper status the only reason to care.

## Nymera Fanewhisper

**Starting concern:** altered manifests and a contact whose trail passed through Gundbarg.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** give her access to a public shipping slate or copied manifest list if she asks. Her personal trail does not solve Skyhorn; it simply intersects the closed harbor.

**Suggested tiny inconsistency:** one cargo mark appears twice under slightly different spellings on two manifests. This is not eelfolk evidence; it is a separate personal thread for later use.

**Docks spotlight:** Sheila can answer recent movement questions better than officials because she knows who actually sailed, not just who filed paperwork.

**Voyage spotlight:** Nymera can watch the horizon and crew behavior. She is likely to notice the unnatural wave early if the player describes active vigilance.

**Do not force:** keep her private investigation separate from the main villain unless later campaign play chooses to connect them.

## Borrik Emberquill

**Starting concern:** lighthouse engineering, wards, access, maintenance, failure points.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** Old Njal can answer mundane questions about salt, stone, supply routes, and maintenance culture.

**Docks spotlight:** Borrik can inspect the *Annabel Lee* or the harbor structures. Let practical expertise earn respect.

**Voyage spotlight:** the lighthouse looks structurally sound from a distance; the irregular beam suggests the visible problem is not simply the whole tower collapsing.

**Sea attack spotlight:** after impact, Borrik immediately has real damage to assess on the ship. Sheila may ask him whether the hull will survive the landing approach.

**Do not force:** avoid turning him into a walking skill check. Let him make diagrams and conclusions from described evidence.

## Cassian Vell

**Starting concern:** crises create opportunities for “legal” predators and respectable coercion.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** a merchant argues that stranded crews still owe full berth fees. This is a low-stakes example of the behavior Cassian watches for.

**Docks spotlight:** Gaspar and Neeno couch theft in the language of debt and cheating. This is Cassian’s strongest Session 1 hook.

**Voyage spotlight:** Sheila can discuss the difference between maritime custom and someone inventing a rule because they have more blades.

**Sea attack spotlight:** give Cassian mobility problems to solve—rigging, railings, crew trapped by shifting cargo.

**Do not force:** do not make him automatically know the law. His expertise is reading coercion, not omniscience.

## Professor Elira Morcant

**Starting concern:** record the human truth of a disaster before folklore replaces testimony.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** she can collect names: ship, captain, last reliable witnesses, families waiting, workers who saw the beam fail.

**Docks spotlight:** while others focus on pirate personalities, Elira may note who witnesses the confrontation and how the story changes immediately afterward.

**Voyage spotlight:** Sheila can give her a first-hand account of the unnatural wave that took the *Jade Lion*.

**Sea attack spotlight:** Elira now becomes a primary witness to the exact phenomenon. Tell her what she can observe precisely: direction, behavior, timing, and unnatural intentionality.

**Do not force:** her academic interest should not override empathy. The player hook already establishes she cares about names and forgotten people.

## Veszara Cinderborn

**Starting concern:** create a reputation based on her own choices rather than family bloodline.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** a few locals stare at first, then stop once she behaves like any other capable traveler. Let usefulness normalize her presence.

**Docks spotlight:** Sheila judges her by whether she helps. Gaspar may try one cheap insult; Neeno is too calculating to waste leverage on prejudice.

**Voyage spotlight:** the cold, wet environment is a natural contrast to her fire magic without mechanically suppressing her.

**Sea attack spotlight:** give her a chance to be remembered for protecting the ship, crew, or companions. Reputation grows from witnessed choices.

**Do not force:** avoid repeated tiefling prejudice scenes; the hook explicitly says Gundbarg is comparatively freeing.

## Aelar Dawnshield

**Starting concern:** a navigational light is a promise to strangers; the failed promise matters.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** an old sailor can frame the lighthouse exactly that way without knowing Aelar’s private feelings: “You see the light before you see home.”

**Docks spotlight:** Aelar is useful as quiet presence during escalation. Sheila may recognize him from a cold harbor watch if claimed.

**Voyage spotlight:** ask what Aelar watches: horizon, crew, rigging, or the light. Reward the answer with tailored detail.

**Sea attack spotlight:** someone is at risk of being left behind or overboard. Give him the chance to act on his private promise.

**Do not force:** do not mirror his past tragedy too literally.

## Sister Mara Venn

**Starting concern:** harbor closure is becoming a humanitarian problem.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** she notices hunger, exhaustion, minor injuries, and people quietly rationing coin.

**Docks spotlight:** a sailor Sheila previously sent for treatment recognizes Mara if claimed. Dockworkers trust her because she has already helped without asking who would pay.

**Voyage spotlight:** she can check crew condition and organize basic emergency readiness before the attack.

**Sea attack spotlight:** multiple small injuries are more appropriate than a forced dramatic casualty. Let Mara’s preparation matter.

**Do not force:** do not make healing her only contribution; her soldier background supports command and triage.

## Rurik Bearmantle

**Starting concern:** closed trade routes hurt families while respectable people hide behind contracts.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** a merchant loudly prioritizes cargo losses over missing crew. Let Rurik react if he wants; do not script outrage for him.

**Docks spotlight:** Gaspar’s bullying and Neeno’s calculated coercion are obvious targets for his values.

**Voyage spotlight:** Sheila gives him practical work without ceremony—hauling line, securing cargo, helping crew prepare.

**Sea attack spotlight:** the ship physically needs someone who can hold a line, move debris, or pull a person from danger.

**Do not force:** strength should create options beyond attacking.

## Lethiel Moonsong

**Starting concern:** the relationship among lighthouse, settlement, sea, animals, weather, and magic has gone wrong.

**Cracked Cask spotlight:** locals confirm animal behavior has changed near the outer harbor. This is observation, not prophecy.

**Docks spotlight:** gulls are present around town but become fewer and quieter as attention turns seaward.

**Voyage spotlight:** fish activity and bird calls diminish unnaturally near Skyhorn. The sea becomes too still before the attack.

**Sea attack spotlight:** the water elemental is not “nature taking revenge.” Its movement is purposeful and magical. Let Lethiel distinguish disturbed nature from weaponized magic.

**Do not force:** do not make every animal a magical messenger.
