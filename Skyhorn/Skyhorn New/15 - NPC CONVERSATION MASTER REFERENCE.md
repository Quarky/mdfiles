# 15 - NPC CONVERSATION MASTER REFERENCE

## Benji Frogwater

### Voice

Fast when anxious; sentences run together; becomes very clear when discussing Raf.

### What he knows

- *Jade Lion* is overdue.
- Raf is aboard.
- Aryn Hest is captain.
- Sheila is willing to investigate.
- witnesses describe unnatural water near Skyhorn.
- Lucien is lighthouse keeper.

### What he does not know

- current condition of Raf/Aryn/crew;
- eelfolk numbers;
- bowl;
- caves;
- exact nature of “sea monster.”

### Useful lines

**Opening:** “My son is aboard the *Jade Lion*. Start there. Everything else is secondary.”

**On cargo:** “Insured. Replaceable. People aren’t.”

**On officials:** “I understand why they wait. Understanding is not the same as forgiving it.”

**On reward:** “Two hundred each to find the truth. Another two hundred each if you bring my people home.”

**On Raf:** “He tries to make his eyes look brave before the rest of his face catches up.”

**If PCs promise certainty:** “Promise effort. I can believe effort.”

## Captain Sheila Coppertree

### Voice

Casual humor until danger becomes practical. Never panics in front of crew.

### What she knows

- saw unnatural wave pull *Jade Lion* toward Skyhorn;
- Aryn Hest is aboard;
- Lucien is reliable and stubborn;
- lighthouse light remains active but wrong;
- local sea/tide behavior;
- Gaspar/Neeno have been following her.

### What she does not know

- bowl details;
- eelfolk occupation scale;
- exact location of Jade Lion;
- current status of captives.

### Useful lines

**Greeting:** “You look like people who have already made at least one bad decision today. Good. I need volunteers.”

**On fear:** “Fear’s useful. It tells you to check the line twice. Panic tells you to cut the line.”

**On Lucien:** “He can lecture a storm until it gets bored.”

**On Aryn:** “If he’s breathing, he’s insulting whoever captured him.”

**On the sea:** “Water does strange things. It does not normally make choices.”

**On heroics:** “Save the speech until your feet are on something that isn’t moving.”

## Gaspar

### Voice

Loud, grinding teeth, spitting, trying to dominate public space.

### Wants

Win without looking weak. Ship if possible. Respect from Neeno even though he suspects none.

### Useful lines

**To party:** “Stay out of it, land-lovers.”

**If challenged:** “Ye think numbers make ye brave?”

**If given exit:** “Fine. Later, then.”

**If Neeno exposed:** “Say that again.”

**If beaten:** “This ain’t finished.”

## Neeno

### Voice

Soft enough that people lean in; neat; controlled; no wasted emotion.

### Wants

Leverage, survival, ship, future position over Gaspar.

### Useful lines

**Opening:** “We can settle this without making the dock ugly.”

**If bribed:** “Gold has the virtue of clarity.”

**If threatened:** “Threats are only interesting if the next part happens.”

**If betrayal exposed:** “You’re trusting a stranger over me?”

**If withdrawing:** “Debts survive weather.”

## Yrsa Veld — Optional Cracked Cask Proprietor

### Useful lines

- “Rumor costs a drink. Truth usually costs more.”
- “Benji has asked everyone. You’re simply the first group armed enough to make him hopeful.”
- “Sheila? End of the main pier. Listen for someone singing badly on purpose.”

## Eirik Ropehand — Optional Dock Foreman

### Useful lines

- “Nobody’s idle. I can invent work faster than they can finish it.”
- “The sea’s dangerous every day. This is different because captains can’t explain the danger.”
- “If you’re sailing, secure your own gear. My people have enough to do.”

## Tova Arn — Optional Fish Seller

### Useful lines

- “When merchants say the harbor is closed, they mean money. When families say it, they mean names.”
- “Bring people back. Then I’ll care what monster you killed.”

## Old Njal Fen — Optional Lighthouse Supply Hand

### Useful lines

- “Salt always wins eventually. Maintenance is just how long you make eventually wait.”
- “Lucien knows every stone in that tower.”
- “The light has failed before. Not like this.”
