# 10 - SCENE 2C: DOCK AFTERMATH AND DEPARTURE

## Scene Goal

Give everyone one last breath in Gundbarg, then make departure feel like a threshold.

## Immediate Tasks

The *Annabel Lee* crew:

- finishes securing lines;
- stows loose cargo;
- checks the helm;
- passes out oilskins or blankets if needed;
- asks PCs to keep weapons/magic clear of rigging;
- quietly watches the party as the people who may determine whether they come home.

## Last-Minute NPC Beats

### Benji arrives if he was not at the docks

He does not give another speech. He only says:

> “Raf has freckles. Big green eyes. He tries to look brave when he’s scared.”

Then:

> “Please remember that before you remember the reward.”

### Yrsa sends food

Optional living-world touch: a wrapped packet of dense bread, smoked fish, and something hot in a stoppered jug.

> “Bring the jug back.”

This implies expectation of return.

### Eirik checks one line twice

If Tark/Borrik helped inspect the ship:

> “If she breaks, it won’t be from that line.”

### Jessa Bellward / watch runner

> “If you see the *Jade Lion*, signal before you tow anything into harbor. We’ll clear berth space.”

## Departure Read-Aloud

> The last mooring line drops away.
>
> For a few moments the *Annabel Lee* moves almost silently, pushed by practiced hands and the dark pull of the fjord. Gundbarg’s docks slide past: stacked cargo, idle cranes, fur-clad workers, faces turned toward you.
>
> Someone raises a hand.
>
> Then another.
>
> The watchtower bell sounds once through the fog.
>
> Sheila spits over her left shoulder, takes the wheel, and says, “Right then. Let’s go make the sea explain itself.”

## What Gundbarg Does After They Leave

The world continues:

- Benji returns to the Cracked Cask or waits near the harbor for news.
- Rumors spread immediately that adventurers have gone to Skyhorn.
- Gaspar/Neeno consequences continue depending on outcome.
- Harbor watch prepares space for possible survivors.
- Some captains privately hope the *Annabel Lee* succeeds so they can sail again without being first.
- Dockworkers talk about which PC seemed most reliable, frightening, kind, or ridiculous.

You can use these impressions when the party returns in later sessions.
