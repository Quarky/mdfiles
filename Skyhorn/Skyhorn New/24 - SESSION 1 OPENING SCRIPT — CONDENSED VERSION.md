# 24 - SESSION 1 OPENING SCRIPT — CONDENSED VERSION

Use this if you want to begin play quickly without rereading the longer sections.

> Sleet sweeps across Gundbarg’s harbor. Longships and southern cogs sit crowded between grey cliffs, but too many sails are tied down. Dockhands keep working because stopping would mean admitting how afraid they are.
>
> Offshore, through Widow’s Shroud, Skyhorn Lighthouse still burns.
>
> Its beam is weak.
>
> Worse, it is late.
>
> In the Cracked Cask, a halfling merchant named Benji Frogwater has been asking anyone armed, capable, or desperate enough to listen for help. His ship, the *Jade Lion*, is overdue. His young son Raf is aboard.
>
> Captain Sheila Coppertree says she saw the ship taken toward Skyhorn by a wave moving against the tide. She is willing to sail there.
>
> Almost nobody else is.

Then ask each player:

> “Why did your character come into the Cracked Cask today rather than staying somewhere safer?”

Let their player hooks answer the rest.
