# 00 - SESSION 1 DASHBOARD

## Purpose

This is the **Session 1 Living World GM Guide** for the **Northern Fjords / Gundbarg** version of *The Secrets of Skyhorn Lighthouse*. It is designed for fast table use in Foundry while still giving enough depth that Gundbarg feels like a real settlement reacting to a real crisis rather than a quest lobby.

This guide assumes the party uses the **Level 5 pregenerated characters and player hooks** already prepared for this campaign. It does **not** repeat PC or NPC stat blocks, because those actors already exist in Foundry. When combat begins, open the appropriate Foundry actor and run the creature from there.

## Session 1 Intended Scope

**Recommended full-session endpoint:** the party reaches Skyhorn Lighthouse after the attack on the *Annabel Lee*, secures the immediate landing area, sees the damaged lighthouse above them, and ends on the choice to ascend.

This makes Session 1 a complete dramatic arc:

1. **Gundbarg is hurting.**
2. **The party learns why people are afraid.**
3. **The party chooses to get involved.**
4. **A local conflict tests how they deal with people before monsters.**
5. **They leave the safety of town.**
6. **The sea itself appears to attack them.**
7. **They arrive at the isolated lighthouse with proof that the danger is real.**

### Expected runtime

| Session length | Likely endpoint |
|---|---|
| 3 hours | Resolve the Cracked Cask and dock confrontation; depart Gundbarg as cliffhanger. |
| 4 hours | Reach the *Annabel Lee* attack and end after defeating/escaping the elemental. |
| 4.5–5.5 hours | Full recommended Session 1: Gundbarg, docks, voyage, elemental, lighthouse arrival. |
| 6+ hours | Optional overflow into the lighthouse exterior or Area 1. Do not force this; Session 2 benefits from starting with the lighthouse siege. |

## Foundry Scene Order

Use these existing scenes in this order:

1. **Gundbarg — Fjord Harbor**
2. **The Cracked Cask — Tavern**
3. **The Docks**
4. **The Annabel Lee**
5. **Skyhorn Lighthouse — Exterior Approach**

Keep **Skyhorn Lighthouse** and **Skyhorn Lighthouse Caves** hidden until later sessions unless the players reach them naturally.

## Session 1 Core Truths

These are the facts behind the rumors. Keep them straight even when NPCs do not know them.

- Skyhorn Lighthouse, called the **Eye of the Storm** by locals, is functioning poorly rather than being fully dark.
- The harbor is slowing toward a standstill because captains are afraid to sail near the lighthouse.
- The merchant vessel **Jade Lion** is overdue and was pulled toward the lighthouse by an unnatural wave.
- **Benji Frogwater** wants his son **Raf** and the crew back alive and is willing to pay capable people to act.
- **Captain Sheila Coppertree** is willing to sail toward Skyhorn and is personally concerned for **Captain Aryn Hest**.
- **Lucien Skyhorn** is still alive at the lighthouse when the adventure begins.
- The apparent “sea monster” is not a natural beast. Water elementals are being weaponized against ships.
- Eelfolk are responsible for the attack on the lighthouse and the use of the bowl below it.
- **Gaspar and Neeno are not connected to the eelfolk plot.** They are opportunistic local trouble on the way to the real threat.

## Northern Canon Decision

The consolidated source notes contain an older **Leilon/Barge Yard adaptation** alongside the later northern material. For this campaign:

- **Use Gundbarg.**
- Use the **dark finger-fjords, grey cliffs, sleet, pine smoke, smoked fish, longships, Cracked Cask, Widow’s Shroud, Jarl’s watchtower bell, and northern harbor culture**.
- Ignore Leilon-specific shallow mud flats, barge-yard logistics, and Leilon council references unless you deliberately reuse a detail under a northern name.

The generated Northern Fjords module and player hook handout both reinforce Gundbarg as the active version.

## Table-Use Legend

- **READ ALOUD** = written so it can be read or paraphrased directly.
- **GM TRUTH** = the factual answer behind uncertainty.
- **EXPECTED PLAYER ACTIONS** = common choices and prepared reactions.
- **LIVING WORLD** = what happens even if the PCs do nothing.
- **OPTIONAL EXPANSION** = invented session texture that supports the source but is not required canon.
- **FOUNDry** = what scene, journal, or actor to pull up.

## Session 1 Safety Valve

If the group stalls, do not add more mystery. The adventure already has enough uncertainty. Move information toward them:

- Benji approaches them.
- A dockworker tells them Sheila is the only captain still willing to sail.
- Lucien’s speaking-gull message reaches the Cracked Cask.
- A watch bell rings while the lighthouse beam visibly stutters.
- Sheila enters the tavern long enough to say, “If any of you are actually going to help, meet me at the *Annabel Lee* before the tide turns.”

The goal is **choice**, not difficulty discovering the adventure.
