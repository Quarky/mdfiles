# 21 - QUICK NPC REACTION TABLES

## If PCs Mention Money

| NPC | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Benji | Practical; money is a tool to get Raf back. |
| Sheila | Fine with pay, suspicious of anyone whose courage exists only after price is named. |
| Gaspar | Immediately interested. |
| Neeno | Treats coin as leverage. |
| Dockworkers | Mixed; they need wages too. |

## If PCs Mention “Sea Monster”

| NPC | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Benji | “Call it whatever you want after you find my son.” |
| Sheila | Corrects speculation toward what she actually saw. |
| Nervous sailor | Insists the water moved intentionally. |
| Braggart | Adds more monster details. |
| Lethiel-oriented local | Mentions animal behavior instead of monsters. |

## If PCs Mock Superstition

Most Gundbarg locals tolerate skepticism. They dislike contempt.

Good tone:

> “You don’t have to believe the Eye is watching. You still need the light.”

## If PCs Present Themselves as Heroes

- Benji does not care about titles.
- Sheila cares about competence.
- Gaspar sees a challenge.
- Neeno sees potential leverage.
- dockworkers wait to see what they actually do.
