# 16 - LIVING WORLD REACTION MATRIX

## Public Reputation During Session 1

Track broad impressions, not numerical reputation points.

### The party is compassionate and practical

Gundbarg remembers them as people who listened. On return, workers approach first and officials second.

### The party is highly mercenary but competent

Locals still respect results, though warmth is limited. Benji honors agreements.

### The party is theatrical but effective

Stories spread before facts do. Dan/Sarduuk/Veszara may enjoy or dislike the resulting reputation.

### The party is reckless around civilians

They can still save the lighthouse, but townspeople remember damaged property and endangered workers.

### The party publicly humiliates Gaspar/Neeno

Dockworkers enjoy it. The surviving pirates remember it personally.

### The party pays the pirates off

Some call it smart; others call it rewarding extortion. Sheila mostly cares that she can sail.

### The party refuses Benji’s reward

Benji is deeply moved but still insists on repaying them somehow later.

### The party negotiates harder because Benji is desperate

He pays if he must. Mara/Dan/Rurik may notice locals judging the interaction.

## Merchant Reaction

Merchants care about reopening the route and minimizing losses. They are not a single faction.

- Some praise decisive action.
- Some complain about delay or violence.
- Benji’s opinion carries weight if the party helps him.
- Sarduuk can convert success into introductions later.

## Sailor Reaction

Sailors care most about whether the party understands shipboard danger.

Things sailors respect:

- securing gear;
- obeying helm commands;
- rescuing crew;
- not casting recklessly through rigging;
- admitting when they do not know the sea.

## Dockworker Reaction

Dockworkers care about whether the party treats them as people rather than obstacles.

Things they remember:

- who protected them in the pirate confrontation;
- who asked about lost wages;
- who damaged cargo unnecessarily;
- who helped haul line before departure.
