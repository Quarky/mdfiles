# 03 - SESSION CLOCKS AND ESCALATION

## Why Use Clocks

The world should react if the PCs spend an hour interviewing everyone, shopping repeatedly, splitting up, or debating whether to help. Do **not** punish investigation. Instead, let the settlement change around them.

These clocks are GM tools, not player-facing mechanics.

## Clock A — Harbor Confidence

Start at **2: Uneasy**.

| Level | State | Visible effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Recovering | People openly prepare to sail once news improves. |
| 1 | Concerned | Crews double-check rigging and routes. |
| 2 | Uneasy | Starting state. Long-distance departures are being cancelled. |
| 3 | Fearful | More captains refuse to leave; arguments over berths and storage increase. |
| 4 | Near Standstill | Only desperate or exceptionally bold crews consider sailing near Skyhorn. |

**Increase when:** time passes without new information, another rumor spreads, visible lighthouse failure worsens, public violence erupts.

**Decrease when:** PCs publicly commit to investigating, dock conflict is resolved cleanly, credible evidence replaces wild rumors.

## Clock B — Benji’s Desperation

Start at **2: Frantic but coherent**.

| Level | Behavior |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hopeful enough to answer calmly. |
| 2 | Paces, repeats himself, offers the published reward. |
| 3 | Starts offering letters of credit, favors, or additional personal wealth. |
| 4 | Tries to hire an obviously unsuitable crew if the PCs refuse. |

If the party refuses him but later changes its mind, do not make him petty. He is relieved, not vindictive.

## Clock C — Gaspar / Neeno Opportunity

Start at **1: Watching Sheila**.

| Level | Behavior |
|---|---|
| 1 | Shadowing her near the docks. |
| 2 | Move to confront her as soon as the PCs look useful. |
| 3 | Begin the public demand for the ship. |
| 4 | If ignored completely, attempt to board or sabotage preparations, forcing the situation into the open. |

They do not attack the PCs out of nowhere. Their objective is the ship and leverage, not random murder.

## Clock D — Weather and Light

This is atmospheric, not punitive.

**Early:** sleet, intermittent fog, weak visibility of lighthouse beam.

**Later:** Widow’s Shroud thickens over the water; wind turns colder; the watchtower bell sounds more often as visibility drops.

**Departure window:** the weather is bad enough to be dramatic but still sailable for Sheila.

**At sea:** fog becomes patchy, revealing and hiding Skyhorn in turns.

## Recommended Session Timeline

This is a staging recommendation rather than fixed canon.

| Relative time | Event if PCs do nothing |
|---|---|
| Start | Benji is in the Cracked Cask; Sheila is at the docks preparing the *Annabel Lee*. |
| +30 min | Another southern captain formally cancels departure; word reaches tavern. |
| +60 min | Gaspar and Neeno move closer to the *Annabel Lee*. |
| +90 min | A watch runner reports another visible irregularity in the lighthouse beam. |
| +2 hr | Sheila sends word: “I sail with help or without it when the tide turns.” This is pressure, not a bluff she wants to carry out. |
| After departure | Gundbarg recedes; the settlement continues coping without the party. |
