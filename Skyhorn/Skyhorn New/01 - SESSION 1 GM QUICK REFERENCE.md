# 01 - SESSION 1 GM QUICK REFERENCE

## One-Minute Recap for the GM

**Gundbarg:** northern fjord settlement under sleet; harbor traffic collapsing; people nervous but still functioning.

**Cracked Cask:** Benji Frogwater is seeking help. Rumors conflict. The lighthouse beam is weak and irregular.

**Docks:** Sheila Coppertree is preparing the *Annabel Lee*. Gaspar and Neeno confront her over an alleged gambling debt and want the ship.

**Social conflict:** starts at **3**. Reach **0** = pirates back down. Reach **5** = fight. Persuasion/Deception can reduce it; failed checks worsen it; intimidation tends to make them dig in. Insight can expose Neeno’s planned betrayal of Gaspar.

**Voyage:** three-hour trip. Use it for PC conversations, Sheila exposition, weather, and foreshadowing.

**Attack:** a water elemental strikes near the lighthouse. Sheila needs three rounds to reach the dock. The party handles the threat while she pilots.

**End:** the ship reaches the lighthouse dock; damage is visible; the lighthouse still burns but flickers; silence above is wrong.

## NPC Motivation at a Glance

| NPC | Wants right now | Fears right now | What makes them trust the PCs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benji Frogwater | Raf and the *Jade Lion* crew returned alive | Waiting until rescue is impossible | Taking Raf seriously; asking practical questions; committing to action |
| Sheila Coppertree | Reach Skyhorn, find Aryn, keep her ship intact | Losing another person to the sea while everyone argues | Competence, courage without swagger, helping her crew |
| Gaspar | Humiliate Sheila and acquire leverage/ship | Looking weak in front of Neeno and witnesses | He does not truly trust PCs; he respects visible strength and exits he can call “his choice” |
| Neeno | Gain the ship, preserve himself, eventually remove Gaspar | A fight he cannot control; exposure of his plan | Deals that preserve future advantage |
| Dockworkers | Work, pay, safe harbor, no collateral damage | Being crushed between merchants, pirates, and monsters | PCs who see them as people, not scenery |
| Sailors | Proof of what is happening; someone else willing to sail first | Being the next missing ship | PCs who listen rather than mock them |
| Harbor watch | Keep violence contained | A dock brawl spreading into ships/fire | PCs who de-escalate or contain trouble |

## Session Tension Curve

1. **Unease** — Gundbarg still works, but badly.
2. **Human urgency** — Benji makes the missing ship personal.
3. **Ambiguity** — rumors disagree.
4. **Social pressure** — Gaspar and Neeno force a public choice.
5. **Release** — the party sails; town falls behind.
6. **Isolation** — fog and cold sea replace crowds.
7. **Proof** — the unnatural wave attacks.
8. **Commitment** — Skyhorn is no longer a rumor on the horizon.

## Things Not to Reveal in Session 1 Unless Players Earn Them

- Do not explain the Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals yet.
- Do not tell them where the *Jade Lion* is hidden.
- Do not reveal the full eelfolk occupation.
- Do not explain how many prisoners remain alive.
- Do not tell them the “monster” is a summoned elemental until they identify it or defeat it.
- Do not resolve Lucien’s status before arrival; the burning light only implies someone may still be resisting.
