# 14 - OPTIONAL OVERFLOW: IF THE PARTY REACHES THE LIGHTHOUSE GROUNDS

## Use Only If Session Time Remains

If you have 30–60 minutes and the group wants to continue, allow the ascent and first exterior investigation. Avoid pushing deep into the interior unless the group clearly wants a longer session.

## Exterior Tone

- slick seawater on stone stairs;
- algae;
- strong wind;
- ozone smell;
- broken gate;
- scattered debris;
- electrical scorch marks;
- a dead gull;
- one abandoned boot;
- no bodies in the open.

These details come directly from the existing scene notes and foreshadow eelfolk without explaining them.

## Expected PC Questions

### “Where are the bodies?”

No obvious bodies outside. That absence should be disturbing, not answered immediately.

### “Are the scorch marks natural lightning?”

An appropriate investigation can suggest the pattern is unusual. Do not reveal every eelfolk ability before they encounter them.

### “Can we see Lucien?”

No clear sight from outside.

### “Can we hear anything?”

Wind, surf, gate impact, and possibly distant movement if you want to end on an auditory cue.

## Recommended Hard Stop

End when the party reaches the broken gate or first opens a route into Area 1.

Session 2 then starts with **Under Siege** at full tension.
