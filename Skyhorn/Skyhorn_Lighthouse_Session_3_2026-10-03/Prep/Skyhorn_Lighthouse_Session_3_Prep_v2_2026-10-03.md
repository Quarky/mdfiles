# 00 - SESSION 3 DASHBOARD

## What This Session Covers

Session 3 begins after the party has found the damaged *Jade Lion* and learned from Raf that Captain Aryn Hest and the surviving crew were taken farther into the caves.

This is the rescue-and-resolution session. Session 1 was uncertainty. Session 2 was discovery. Session 3 is about people who are still alive, a ritual already underway, and choices that cannot all be delayed until the party feels perfectly ready.

The core sequence is:

1. Return from the *Jade Lion* toward the occupied cave interior.
2. Reach Area 8 and find the surviving crew.
3. Prevent the two guards from warning Area 9 if possible.
4. Learn that another summoning is already in progress.
5. Decide whether to free everyone first or move immediately.
6. Enter Area 9 and stop Grigor's forced ritual.
7. Defeat or scatter the remaining eelfolk.
8. Decide what to do with the Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals.
9. Get the survivors, cargo, and ships back under control.
10. Return to Gundbarg and let the living world react.

## Recommended Endpoint

For a normal 4.5–5.5 hour session, finish with the party back in **Gundbarg**.

Do not rush the return. The emotional payoff is not only that the monsters are defeated. It is Benji seeing Raf alive, Sheila seeing Aryn walk off the *Jade Lion*, exhausted sailors reaching familiar ground, and Gundbarg learning that the danger strangling its harbor was real and has been stopped.

A good final image:

> By the time the ships reach Gundbarg, the fog has lifted enough for the Eye of the Storm to be seen behind them.
>
> The beam crosses the mouth of the fjord once.
>
> This time, nobody mistakes it for a warning.

Then let the players say what their characters do next.

## Alternate Endpoints by Runtime

| Available play time | Good endpoint |
|---|---|
| 2.5–3 hours | Stop after Area 9 and the bowl decision |
| 3.5–4 hours | Finish rescue and ship evacuation; end as the convoy leaves Skyhorn |
| 4.5–5.5 hours | Return to Gundbarg and resolve Benji, Sheila, Aryn, and public aftermath |
| 6+ hours | Add downtime conversations, personal-hook follow-through, and campaign hooks |

## Session 3 Core Truths

- Twelve surviving commoner sailors are tied in groups of four in Area 8.
- Aryn Hest is alive among the prisoners.
- Two eelfolk guard Area 8 and try to run to Area 9 when the party appears.
- Aryn tries to trip any fleeing guard who passes close enough.
- Grigor is being forced to summon a water elemental in Area 9.
- Grigor is not willingly helping the eelfolk. He continues because he expects torture if he stops.
- The Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals is fixed into cracked alabaster tile.
- Grigor completes the ritual at the end of his fourth turn of unbroken concentration if nobody stops him.
- If Area 9 is warned and the party waits more than about two minutes, the ritual can finish before the party enters.
- The bowl loses all magical properties if pried from the ground.
- Lucien rewards the party with Mariner's Coins if they do not attempt to steal the bowl.
- Aryn can sail the damaged *Jade Lion* back to port once crew and cargo are recovered.
- Benji rewards the party for returning Raf and again for bringing back surviving crew and goods.

## Five Things to Keep Visible While Running

1. **The hostages are alive.**
2. **Stopping the two runners in Area 8 matters.**
3. **Grigor is a victim, not the villain of the ritual.**
4. **The final room has a four-turn summoning clock once combat begins.**
5. **The return home is part of play, not bookkeeping after it.**

# 01 - SESSION 3 ONE-MINUTE QUICK REFERENCE

## Area 8 — Hostages

Present:

- twelve human commoner sailors tied in groups of four;
- Captain Aryn Hest;
- two eelfolk guards.

The guards try to reach Area 9. Aryn is unarmed and injured, but he tries to trip a guard that runs close to him.

Aryn immediately warns the party that another sailor is being forced to summon a water elemental in the next room.

### If the Guards Reach Area 9

The final room gets time to prepare. Eelfolk take cover. If the party waits longer than about two minutes, the summoning can complete and the water elemental comes into Area 8.

If the sailor loses concentration after the elemental has been brought forth, it turns on him rather than remaining obedient.

### If the Guards Do Not Reach Area 9

The final group is focused on the ritual. The party may be able to enter with surprise if its approach beats the eelfolk scourge's passive Perception 13.

## Area 8A — Plunder

- Door from Area 8 is unlocked.
- Chests and barrels contain 5,000 gp worth of *Jade Lion* trade goods.
- This is cargo, not loose coin.

## Area 9 — Summoning Room

Features:

- clustered stalagmites;
- ruined circular chamber;
- broken tower crenellations;
- half cover from stone and stalagmites;
- Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals set into cracked alabaster tile;
- eelfolk scourge;
- eelfolk and hunters per Foundry/map placement;
- Grigor kneeling at the bowl.

Grigor uses the **Bandit** chassis with no weapons. He completes the summoning at the end of his fourth turn of unbroken concentration.

The eelfolk scourge closes on a vulnerable-looking PC. Basic eelfolk gang up on strong melee characters. Hunters use cover and throw javelins for about three rounds before joining melee if the battle still supports that plan.

## Aftermath

### Bowl

If pried out:
- it loses all magical properties.

If the party tries to take it:
- Lucien is angry;
- he gives only a terse thank-you and goodbye;
- no Mariner's Coin reward.

If left in place:
- Lucien gives each PC one Mariner's Coin.

### Mariner's Coin

Hammered copper coin stamped with a shining lighthouse.

When presented to a non-hostile sailor, it grants advantage on one Charisma check to influence that sailor.

The source does not say the coin is consumed. Do not invent charges unless you deliberately house-rule them.

### Jade Lion

Aryn can get the surviving crew and cargo aboard by rowboat and sail the ship back to port for repairs.

### Return

- *Annabel Lee* can sail home.
- Benji pays 200 gp per PC for Raf's safe return.
- He pays another 200 gp per PC for surviving crew and recovered goods.
- The original adventure has Benji send four soldiers and one captain to repair and guard Skyhorn afterward.
- In the Gundbarg version, treat that as a locally organized guard detail funded or arranged by Benji unless your campaign already gives him private soldiers.

# 03 - SESSION 3 PRESSURE CLOCKS

## Clock A — Area 9 Warning

Start at **0** if no eelfolk escaped from Session 2.

Start at **1** if the party has been loud in the caves.

Start at **2** if an eelfolk runner already escaped toward the final rooms.

### 0 — Unaware

Area 8 guards are inattentive enough to be caught off guard. Area 9 focuses on Grigor and the bowl.

### 1 — Nervous

Area 8 guards watch the routes. Area 9 still performs the ritual normally.

### 2 — Expecting Trouble

Area 8 guards run immediately when they see the party. Area 9 responds quickly if warned.

### 3 — Warned

Area 9 takes cover and prepares. Begin the two-minute pre-summoning window.

### 4 — Ritual Completed Before Entry

A water elemental has already been summoned. It can move into Area 8 under eelfolk coercion.

Do not add a second elemental unless the fiction and bowl usage clearly justify it.

## Clock B — Grigor's Ritual

Once Area 9 combat begins and Grigor is still performing the ritual:

- **Turn 1** — first stage.
- **Turn 2** — water rises and reacts.
- **Turn 3** — the bowl begins to pull sea-water unnaturally.
- **Turn 4** — ritual completes at the end of Grigor's turn.

If concentration is broken before completion, the ritual stops.

"Halted" can mean:

- physically pulled from the bowl;
- knocked unconscious;
- reassured that the eelfolk cannot punish him;
- restrained;
- convinced to stop;
- concentration broken by combat;
- magic that prevents the action.

Do not require the party to damage him.

## Clock C — Prisoner Safety

Start at **2 — exhausted but alive**.

Pressure rises if:
- combat spills directly into tied prisoner groups;
- area effects catch them;
- an elemental reaches Area 8;
- nobody cuts them loose while the room becomes unsafe.

Safety improves if:
- Mara or another healer triages;
- groups are untied;
- someone escorts them toward the cove;
- weapons are kept away from panicked captives until people can think clearly.

This is not a hidden hit-point tax. It is a reminder that twelve bound civilians occupy the battlefield.

## Clock D — Evacuation

- **0:** all still tied / trapped.
- **1:** Area 8 secured.
- **2:** first group moving toward Area 7.
- **3:** survivors aboard *Jade Lion* or moving toward *Annabel Lee*.
- **4:** all living survivors accounted for.

Aryn can manage much of this once freed.

## Clock E — Tide

Use tide for atmosphere and logistics:
- stronger pull through cove;
- rowboats knocking against dock;
- louder surf masking speech;
- harder cargo movement.

Do not change map geometry mid-combat without warning.

## Clock F — Gundbarg Response

This starts once a returning ship is visible in the fjord.

1. Lookouts notice.
2. A ship is recognized.
3. Crowd begins to form.
4. Survivors disembark.
5. Story spreads faster than facts.

# 05 - PC HOOK INTEGRATION MATRIX — SESSION 3

## Valemon

Bound sailors and a child already rescued make captivity unavoidable. Let Valemon choose what that means rather than narrating his emotions.

Useful actions:
- cut bonds;
- protect an escape route;
- identify the prisoner who is too frightened to move;
- understand quickly why Grigor continues under threat.

If someone calls Grigor a coward, give Valemon room to answer.

## Tark Stonefire

Area 9 is a battlefield where careless power can harm captives or damage something nobody understands.

Reward:
- careful line selection;
- smaller precise spells;
- positioning allies before a large effect;
- deliberate restraint.

His past makes precision meaningful without requiring a monologue about it.

## Handsome Dan

There is no crowd to impress until the return to Gundbarg.

In Area 8, Dan can simply get frightened people moving.

He does not need a Persuasion check to make sailors follow a sensible evacuation instruction once Aryn confirms he is helping.

On the return, let Dan decide whether to correct the exaggerated version of the story spreading through the harbor.

## Kreslin

Grigor's ritual creates an interesting distinction between magical power and coercion.

Kreslin may:
- assess whether the ritual is killing or exhausting Grigor;
- break concentration;
- stabilize him afterward;
- ask what happens when elemental control snaps.

Do not make the bowl secretly necromantic.

## Sarduuk Magavario

The bowl is power with consequences.

If Sarduuk argues for taking it, Lucien answers him seriously.

Lucien's strongest point:

> "You saw what one bad week of ownership did."

No moral speech is needed beyond that.

## Cadwallon ap Bliedd

Area 8 is a rescue under threat of reinforcement.

Give Cadwallon the physical facts:
- two guards;
- tied groups;
- runner route;
- final-room door.

If he assigns who blocks what, reward the plan through position.

Aryn respects anyone who puts the crew before glory.

## Nymera Fanewhisper

Nymera is naturally suited to stopping the Area 8 runners.

If the optional smuggler-cargo thread remains active, Area 8A can hold one matching cargo mark among many ordinary ones.

It proves a supply overlap, not the whole conspiracy.

## Borrik Emberquill

The bowl is architecture as much as magic.

Borrik can see:
- it is fixed;
- it draws on the surrounding sea;
- removing it means breaking the installation;
- the source gives no safe dismantling method.

Do not invent one automatically.

## Cassian Vell

Grigor is a clean example of compliance under threat rather than willing collaboration.

Cassian can recognize that without a special roll.

Aryn responds better to plain questions than to social performance.

## Professor Elira Morcant

If Elira asks for names, Aryn can give them.

If she documents the final room, Lucien permits observation after the crisis.

He becomes wary only if scholarship turns into ownership or removal.

## Veszara Cinderborn

The final room rewards controlled fire and careful placement.

Let discipline be noticed quietly.

If she helped free crew, Aryn remembers her name without caring about her bloodline.

## Aelar Dawnshield

This is the simple version of his protector theme: people were left below; he can bring them out.

Aryn's battered attempts to keep protecting crew while bound can resonate without either man explaining it.

## Sister Mara Venn

Mara has real triage this session.

Priorities:
1. immediate life threats;
2. move prisoners out of active danger;
3. food/water carefully for starved people;
4. treat burns and shock;
5. calm panic.

With Grigor, a direct calm instruction may be enough once the scourge cannot punish him:

> "Grigor. Look at me. Stop."

## Rurik Bearmantle

This session has many useful strength problems:
- move injured people;
- clear debris;
- handle rowboats;
- shift cargo;
- protect civilians;
- hold the line.

Do not turn every one into a roll.

## Lethiel Moonsong

The ritual is coercion in two directions:
- Grigor is coerced by the eelfolk.
- The elemental is coerced by the bowl and summoner.

The elemental is still dangerous. Do not turn it into a misunderstood pet.

Afterward, Lucien is willing to discuss whether the bowl should remain, but he does not know a safe method to end it.

# 42 - EXPECTED PLAYER ACTIONS AND PREPARED REACTIONS

## "We Free Every Prisoner Before Area 9"

Allow it.

Ritual advances.

Aryn organizes evacuation.

## "We Chase and Leave Them Tied"

Also valid.

Aryn understands.

He may shout:

> "Go!"

## "We Seal the Area 9 Door"

It buys time only if it can physically be secured from their side.

Use actual map geometry.

## "We Interrogate Aryn First"

He answers quickly and urges them onward.

## "We Take the 5,000 gp"

It is bulky trade cargo.

Moving it consumes labor and time.

## "We Kill Grigor to Stop the Ritual"

Mechanically possible.

Morally consequential.

Aryn and the crew know he was forced.

## "We Persuade Grigor"

Assess whether the scourge can still punish him.

If not, a single clear voice may be enough.

## "We Use Calm Emotions"

Good use.

Apply spell normally.

## "We Use Dispel Magic"

Distinguish:
- active ritual/effect;
- permanent bowl enchantment.

The source does not give a specific dispel DC for the bowl.

## "We Banish the Elemental"

Use normal rules.

If it works, it works.

## "We Use the Bowl Ourselves"

The source does not provide a complete player-use procedure.

Do not invent a reliable combat superweapon on the spot unless you intend to expand the adventure.

## "We Pry It Out"

It becomes nonmagical.

Lucien objects.

## "We Smash It"

Published result is unspecified.

Choose deliberately.

## "We Tow Jade Lion Instead"

Plausible if ship conditions allow.

Sheila and Aryn can assess.

Do not force the published solution if a good nautical alternative exists.

# 44 - QUICK GM RULINGS

## Cutting Bonds
No roll needed under no pressure.

In initiative, use an appropriate action/object interaction ruling.

## Carrying Injured Sailors
Roll only when terrain, current, or combat makes the outcome uncertain.

## Trade Goods
Bulky. Use labor and boat trips, not exact pound-by-pound accounting unless desired.

## Grigor's Concentration
Treat the ritual as concentration-like per the adventure's description.

## Elemental Hostility
If control breaks, Grigor is the immediate target. The elemental is still dangerous to everyone.

## Surprise
Use 2014 surprise rules. Scourge passive Perception is 13.

## Cover
Stalagmites and broken tower crenellations provide half cover.

## Aryn
Starts with no equipment.

This file is meant to be split and filled after play. It records the exact state that future modules and sessions must preserve.

## Required Record

- Selected PCs.
- Every surviving, dead, surrendered, captured, escaped, or allied NPC.
- Raf's condition and relationship state.
- Aryn and crew survivor count.
- Grigor's condition and treatment.
- Fennick and *Slippery Eel* status.
- *Annabel Lee* and *Jade Lion* condition.
- Cargo recovered, lost, or marked.
- Dagger of Venom and Area 5 treasure disposition.
- Bowl condition, location, secrecy, and Lucien trust.
- Beacon condition.
- Mariner's Coins awarded.
- Benji payments.
- Public Gundbarg story.
- Active future hooks.
- Any changes to personal PC hooks.

## Next-Session Opening

Write one concrete image, not a summary.
