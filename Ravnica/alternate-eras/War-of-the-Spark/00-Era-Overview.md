# War of the Spark Era

This overlay changes leadership, planar access, threat level, location state, and normal guild politics.

Nicol Bolas attacks Ravnica with Eternals through the Planar Bridge. Planeswalkers are drawn to Ravnica and trapped by the Immortal Sun.

MTGFoundry rule: while the lock is active, Planeswalkers can arrive but cannot simply planeswalk away. This is era-specific, not a permanent Ravnica rule.

The city feels like mass civilian movement, active rescue, destroyed infrastructure, guild fortification, leadership legitimacy changing in real time, and rivals forced to negotiate while grieving.

Operation Desperation requires cooperation from all ten guilds, resurrects Niv-Mizzet, and makes him the new Living Guildpact.

GM tone: not “the guilds finally become friends,” but ten incompatible institutions forced to produce one coordinated act while several are compromised, leaderless, furious, or grieving.
