# War of the Spark Leader State and Loyalties

Azorius: Isperia dead; Dovin Baan controls the Senate before fleeing; Lavinia and loyalists resist.  
Boros: Aurelia remains defender.  
Dimir: Lazav remains hidden guildmaster.  
Golgari: Vraska has taken leadership after Jarad; loyalties are volatile.  
Gruul: Domri Rade rises and dies; Borborygmos remains a major clan figure.  
Izzet: Ral Zarek leads; Niv-Mizzet dies and is resurrected.  
Orzhov: Kaya destroys the Obzedat and becomes guildmaster, but old-family interests remain.  
Rakdos: Rakdos remains parun.  
Selesnya: Trostani/Conclave politics are strained; Emmara becomes important.  
Simic: Prime Speaker Vannifar leads.

When someone says “the guild wants,” ask which leader, which internal faction, whether news of the latest death/defection has reached this precinct, and what local commanders believe.
