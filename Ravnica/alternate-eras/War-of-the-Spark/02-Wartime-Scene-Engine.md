# War of the Spark Wartime Scene Engine

Every wartime scene should include: civilian need, active Eternal threat, local guild defense, rival-guild request, leadership uncertainty, personal grief, and one reason cooperation remains possible.

Example: Boros needs a Golgari tunnel to evacuate civilians. The tunnel keeper has orders from a leader who may already be dead. A Selesnyan healer refuses to move wounded until transport is safe. A Dimir agent knows the surface route has fallen but will not explain how.
