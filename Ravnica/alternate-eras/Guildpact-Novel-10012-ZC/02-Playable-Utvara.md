# Playable Utvara

Utvara is a pressure cooker of Orzhov ownership, plague history, Selesnyan intervention, Izzet research, Gruul territorial reality, and planar/spiritual instability.

NPC positions: Orzhov administrator sees deeds and deadlines; Gruul resident sees outsiders monetizing inhabited land; Selesnyan healer sees ecological/public-health duty; Izzet researcher sees extraordinary conditions; guildless settler sees the gap between reclamation on paper and surviving there.

Scene seed: Orzhov marks occupied buildings for demolition. Gruul elder says foundations cross a burial route. Izzet says instability is connected to the Schism. Residents want to know where they sleep tonight.
