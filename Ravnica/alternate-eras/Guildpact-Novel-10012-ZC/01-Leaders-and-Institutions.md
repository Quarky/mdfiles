# Original Trilogy Leadership and Institutions

Azorius: Grand Arbiter Augustin IV is defining in the later original trilogy; old Prahv is destroyed.  
Boros: Razia is ancient guildmaster and dies; Feather becomes important; Parhelion matters directly.  
Dimir: Szadek is ancient vampire parun and hidden guildmaster.  
Golgari: Savra seizes leadership; Svogthir returns; Jarad becomes important in aftermath.  
Gruul: clan-specific politics remain primary; Borborygmos remains major.  
Izzet: Niv-Mizzet remains parun; Utvara research matters.  
Orzhov: Obzedat rules; Teysa Karlov increasingly challenges aspects of power.  
Rakdos: Rakdos remains parun.  
Selesnya: Mat’Selesnya and the Conclave are attacked/manipulated during the crisis.  
Simic: Momir Vig creates Project Kraj; Vig, Kraj, and Novijen are destroyed in the Dissension crisis.

Do not casually use New Prahv or later Zameck politics as though they existed unchanged in this period.
