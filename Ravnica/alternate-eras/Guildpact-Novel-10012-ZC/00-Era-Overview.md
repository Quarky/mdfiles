# Guildpact Novel Era - 10012 Z.C.

Use the main geography where still valid, but replace leadership and institutional state.

This overlay centers on the Guildpact novel period, about twelve years after the first novel’s Decamillennial crisis, while retaining enough original-trilogy context to understand the city.

The old magical Guildpact is broken. The city has not yet settled into the later Living-Guildpact system. Politics is post-constitutional: recent leader deaths, damaged guildhalls, unstable authority, Utvara reclamation, plague history, Agyrem and the Schism.

Utvara brings together Orzhov property, Teysa Karlov, Kuga plague history, Selesnyan Vitar Yescu, Izzet Cauldron research, Gruul inhabitants, the Schism, and Agrus Kos.

GM tone: What happens when the ten-thousand-year magical constitution is gone and nobody knows what replaces it?
