# Guild Relations

Ravnican guild relationships are **issue-specific**, not a fixed friend/enemy chart. Two guilds can cooperate on one civic problem while sabotaging each other elsewhere.

## Scene engine
For a quick multi-guild scene, choose:
1. a **public need** (water, food, safety, transport, medicine, housing);
2. a guild with formal jurisdiction;
3. a second guild whose expertise is necessary;
4. a third guild that pays, protests, spies, or exploits;
5. guildless residents who live with the result.

This creates Ravnican politics without requiring a world-ending plot.
