# Guild Relations
Relationships are issue-specific rather than a permanent ally/enemy chart.

| Pair | Cooperation | Friction |
|---|---|---|
| Azorius–Boros | public safety | procedure vs immediate justice |
| Azorius–Izzet | infrastructure | regulation vs experimentation |
| Dimir–Orzhov | information markets | secrets vs contractual power |
| Golgari–Selesnya | ecology and food | decay vs cultivated harmony |
| Golgari–Simic | biology | natural cycles vs redesign |
| Gruul–Izzet | occasional shared threats | infrastructure as encroachment |
| Izzet–Simic | research | competing methods |
| Rakdos–Orzhov | venues, labor, money | freedom vs hierarchy |
| Boros–Selesnya | civilian protection | hierarchy vs consensus |

## Scene engine
Choose a public need, the guild with formal jurisdiction, a second guild whose expertise is necessary, a third party that pays/protests/spies/exploits, and guildless residents who must live with the result.
