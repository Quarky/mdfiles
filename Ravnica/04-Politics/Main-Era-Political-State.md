# Main Era Political State - Jace as Living Guildpact

Jace is the magical embodiment of the Guildpact, not a conventional mayor. Ravnica still runs through ten institutions. His frequent absence creates constitutional latency: disputes wait, functionaries improvise, guilds create facts on the ground, and ambitious subordinates exploit uncertainty.

| Guild | Leader | Strategic concern |
|---|---|---|
| Azorius | Isperia | continuity and legal authority |
| Boros | Aurelia | protection, raids, infiltration |
| Dimir | Lazav | information advantage |
| Golgari | Jarad | undercity legitimacy and faction balance |
| Gruul | Borborygmos | strength enough to unify clans when needed |
| Izzet | Niv-Mizzet | research, infrastructure, hidden designs |
| Orzhov | Obzedat | property, debt, insulation from reform |
| Rakdos | Rakdos | relevance, labor, entertainment, parun's whims |
| Selesnya | Trostani | community growth and autonomy |
| Simic | Zegana | research autonomy and internal balance |

Standing fault lines: law vs justice; surface prosperity vs invisible labor; infrastructure vs safety; contract vs coercion; four definitions of nature; information sovereignty.

Political heat: 1 routine rivalry, 2 active contest, 3 precinct crisis, 4 interguild confrontation, 5 Guildpact crisis.
