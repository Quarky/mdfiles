# Political Pressure Cookbook

Legal: permit revoked, injunction, seizure, evidence excluded, jurisdiction transfer, emergency rule.  
Economic: debt called, supplier bought, shipment delayed, rent raised, labor replaced.  
Social: denunciation, invitation withdrawn, guild membership questioned, public satire, commendation/censure.  
Information: leak, blackmail, forged record, missing archive, memory alteration, rumor.  
Infrastructure: water shutoff, power reroute, transit closure, condemned bridge, sewer blockage, quarantine.  
Territorial: easement, rubblebelt expansion, undercity access claim, checkpoint, warehouse acquisition.  
Spiritual: debt after death, ancestor demand, Worldsoul interpretation, prophecy, angelic command, demon's favor.

A strong political problem combines one official pressure, one material pressure, and one personal relationship.
