# Daily Life, Culture & Economy
Ravnica should feel crowded, vertical, old and continuously repurposed. New structures stand on forgotten foundations; bridges become markets; abandoned routes become Golgari paths or Dimir dead drops.

Guilds are not merely adventuring factions. They are employers, civic institutions, cultures and communities. Money matters, but so do permits, favors, secrets, credit, rank and contractual obligation.

The city's survival is interdependent: Izzet infrastructure, Golgari reclamation, Selesnyan cultivation, Simic medicine, Azorius administration, Boros security and many guildless workers all intersect.

For adventure texture, start with ordinary urban needs—water, food, housing, transit, medicine, sanitation or safety—and let overlapping guild interests turn them into conflicts.
