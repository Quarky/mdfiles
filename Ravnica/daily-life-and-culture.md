# Daily Life, Culture & Economy

## Street-level Ravnica
Ravnica should feel crowded, vertical, old, and continuously repurposed. New construction sits on forgotten foundations. Bridges become markets. Abandoned transit tunnels become Golgari routes or Dimir dead drops. A Selesnyan garden can occupy the courtyard of an Azorius block while an Izzet conduit runs beneath both.

## Work
Guild identity often determines profession but not perfectly. Boros soldiers, Azorius functionaries, Izzet engineers, Simic researchers, Orzhov financiers, Rakdos performers and laborers, Golgari reclaimers, Selesnyan growers, and Dimir information workers are visible parts of the economy. Gruul communities represent people and places pushed outside the city's preferred definition of civilization.

## Money and obligation
Coins circulate, but favors, permits, guild rank, credit, secrets, and contractual obligation can matter just as much. Orzhov debt is particularly dangerous because obligation can outlast ordinary employment—and sometimes life.

## Food
Ravnica's food chain is a civic miracle nobody wants to think about too closely. Golgari reclamation turns decay into usable resources and food; Selesnya cultivates living spaces and agriculture; ordinary markets distribute products through dense neighborhoods.

## Law
Azorius law can be precise enough to become absurd. Boros enforcement is more immediate and moralistic. Orzhov contracts create a parallel world of private obligation. Local reality is therefore jurisdictional: being legally correct does not mean the Boros captain, Orzhov advokist, or angry neighborhood agrees with you.

## Faith and belonging
Religion is inseparable from faction culture for several guilds. Orzhov ritual sanctifies hierarchy and debt; Selesnya seeks communion and collective harmony; Boros service can take on sacred dimensions; Gruul shamans preserve older relationships to the world beneath the city.

## Adventure texture
Use mundane civic problems to make magic credible: a burst Izzet steam main, a zoning injunction on a Selesnyan root system, a Simic clinic treating mutation, a Rakdos show whose permit was revoked, an Orzhov foreclosure, or a Golgari tunnel suddenly cut off by new construction. Then let faction agendas turn the problem into an adventure.
