# Golgari Swarm — Black/Green

## Identity
The Golgari govern the truth polite Ravnica hides: everything dies, everything decays, and life feeds on what came before. They reclaim waste, cultivate the undercity, feed populations, and preserve communities beneath the celebrated surface.

## At the table
**Voice:** practical, patient, conscious of cycles and old grievances.  
**Wants:** resources returned to use, undercity autonomy, recognition of necessary labor.  
**Fears:** surface exploitation, sterilization, waste, being treated as disposable.  
**Offers:** food, fungi, poisons, corpse handling, hidden passage, undercity knowledge.  
**Complications:** factional resentments, undead labor, dangerous ecosystems, old political wounds.

## Scene principle
Show the indispensable civic service before the horror. A rot farm can be unsettling and still be what keeps a neighborhood alive.

## Cross-guild hooks
Selesnya disputes over nature; Simic biological research; Dimir tunnels; Orzhov death and debt; surface redevelopment.
