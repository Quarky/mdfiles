# Golgari Swarm — Black/Green

## Identity
The guild centers on reclamation, decay, food cycles and undercity survival. Its philosophy answers a real Ravnican need, which is why ordinary people can sincerely choose it.

## At the table
**Voice:** practical and patient.  
**Wants:** autonomy and recognition of necessary labor.  
**Offers:** food, fungi, corpse handling and hidden routes.  
**Failure mode:** old grievances and dangerous ecosystems.

## GM principle
Show what this guild contributes before showing its excess. That keeps faction conflict ideological and social rather than reducing the guild to a costume or monster type.
