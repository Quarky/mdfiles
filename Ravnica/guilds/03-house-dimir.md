# House Dimir — Blue/Black

## Identity
The guild centers on information, couriers, secrets and covert action. Its philosophy answers a real Ravnican need, which is why ordinary people can sincerely choose it.

## At the table
**Voice:** economical and indirect.  
**Wants:** information advantage and deniability.  
**Offers:** secrets, surveillance and hidden access.  
**Failure mode:** every helpful fact serving another agenda.

## GM principle
Show what this guild contributes before showing its excess. That keeps faction conflict ideological and social rather than reducing the guild to a costume or monster type.
