# Selesnya Conclave - Green/White

## Main-era leader
**Trostani**

## Civic function
community, cultivation, conservation, charity, spiritual communion, defense.

## How it actually works
vernadi centered on living trees led by voda; roles include evangels, hierarchs, equenauts, Ledev, votaries, sagittars.

## Roles you can immediately play
initiate, grower, healer, evangel, votary, sagittar, equenaut, Ledev guardian, hierarch, voda.

## Major places
Vitu-Ghazi, Great Concourse, vernadi, Concordance, Beast Haven.

## Why ordinary people join
mutual aid, belonging, ecological integration.

## Characteristic danger
conformity, soft coercion, missionary paternalism.

## Internal politics
local vernadi vs center; militia vs peaceful identity; evangelism vs respect; privacy vs community.

## NPC voice
Remember who is affected. / Community is not prison unless it refuses to let you leave. / Feed them tonight, solve why tomorrow.

## Immediate NPC recipe
Role + sincere belief + guild grievance + personal need + line they will not cross.

## Reaction sequence
Local worker notices; middle rank decides whether to report; internal faction asks who gains status; official response begins; opportunist moves faster than policy; ordinary residents bear the cost.

## Six ready archetypes
- loyalist who embodies the best case;
- opportunist using membership for status/security;
- reformer convinced the guild betrayed itself;
- exhausted professional finishing a shift;
- ambitious middle rank competing with rival;
- anomaly who fits philosophy but not stereotype.
