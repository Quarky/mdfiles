# Orzhov Syndicate - White/Black

## Main-era leader
**Obzedat**

## Civic function
banking, lending, contracts, property, religion, legal advocacy, organized crime.

## How it actually works
old families plus borrowers, syndics, knights, ministrants, pontiffs; advokists, priests, enforcers, spirits.

## Roles you can immediately play
borrower, syndic, advokist, enforcer, priest, knight, ministrant, pontiff, oligarch.

## Major places
Orzhova, Vizkopa, Plaza West, Irbitov, vaults.

## Why ordinary people join
continuity, capital, reliable contracts, institutional memory.

## Characteristic danger
predatory debt, inherited obligation, spiritual bondage.

## Internal politics
old families vs outsiders; charitable priests; lawyers vs crude criminality; advancement politics.

## NPC voice
Of course we can help. / Nothing requires you to be happy. / Debt is a promise with documentation.

## Immediate NPC recipe
Role + sincere belief + guild grievance + personal need + line they will not cross.

## Reaction sequence
Local worker notices; middle rank decides whether to report; internal faction asks who gains status; official response begins; opportunist moves faster than policy; ordinary residents bear the cost.

## Six ready archetypes
- loyalist who embodies the best case;
- opportunist using membership for status/security;
- reformer convinced the guild betrayed itself;
- exhausted professional finishing a shift;
- ambitious middle rank competing with rival;
- anomaly who fits philosophy but not stereotype.
