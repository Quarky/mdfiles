# Guildhall and Politics Stories

ORIGINAL FICTION - non-canon examples.

## The Empty Chair
**Guilds/themes:** Living Guildpact / all guilds

Ten delegations arrived at the Chamber. Jace was off-plane. Azorius proposed postponement. Boros asked where displaced families would sleep. Orzhov offered financing. Selesnya offered beds without interest. Golgari asked who closed the lower tunnels. The Dimir representative said nothing because officially there was no Dimir representative. By sunset they had a provisional agreement none wanted Jace to know had worked without him.

GM lesson: each participant should pursue a recognizable institutional or personal need; Ravnica emerges when those needs collide without erasing ordinary life.

## Three Definitions of Consent
**Guilds/themes:** Simic / Azorius / Selesnya

The patient wanted the graft. The biomancer had it. The Azorius reviewer could forbid it. The Selesnyan healer asked, “What happens if she says yes because every other treatment is too expensive?” The patient interrupted. “What happens if you spend so long perfecting my choices that I die before I get one?”

## Dinner at Plaza West
**Guilds/themes:** Orzhov / Azorius / Dimir

The senator knew the room was bugged. The advokist knew the senator knew. The server knew both and was being paid by somebody else. They discussed zoning, then charity. They discussed the bribe once, by agreeing that soup was unexpectedly expensive this season. The Dimir listener in the ventilation shaft wrote down the soup supplier.

## The Angel and the Clerk
**Guilds/themes:** Boros / Azorius

The angel wanted the street cleared before the Gruul arrived. The clerk wanted the evacuation order entered correctly. “People may die while you stamp paper.” “People have died because angels assumed they were exceptions to procedure.” The room froze. Then the angel leaned over the desk. “Show me the fastest legal way.” The clerk smiled. “Now you are speaking my language.”

## The Quiet Vote
**Guilds/themes:** Golgari internal

Jarad asked which faction supported the tunnel. Devkarin said it honored tradition. Kraul said it employed thousands. A medusa said it was defensible. A rot farmer said nothing until asked. “All three plan to own it. None asked who clears the fungus when it floods.” For the first time that night, Jarad looked interested.
