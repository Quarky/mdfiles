# Source Index and Canon Policy

Primary project source: Guildmasters’ Guide to Ravnica, used for main-era guild structures, leaders, Tenth District, careers, and civic texture. This vault paraphrases rather than reproduces it.

MTGFoundry sources include The Glass Run GM Guide and the living-world guide. Those establish campaign facts, not external canon.

Official public Magic material is used for era/history checks, including Return to Ravnica setting material, public Living Guildpact stories, guild flavor guides, and War of the Spark public stories.

Broad named-location indexing merges many publication dates, so locations not firmly placed in the main era are marked era-sensitive.

Conflict rule: newer explicit campaign instruction beats older campaign notes; published differences are preserved with era labels rather than flattened into one timeless present.
