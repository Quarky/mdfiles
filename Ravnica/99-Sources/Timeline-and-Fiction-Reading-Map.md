# Timeline and Fiction Reading Map

## Original Ravnica cycle
*Ravnica: City of Guilds*, *Guildpact*, *Dissension*. Use for Agrus Kos, Szadek, Savra, Utvara, Agyrem, Momir Vig, Project Kraj, and old institutional states.

## Return to Ravnica era
Return to Ravnica / Gatecrash / Dragon’s Maze setting material and *The Secretist*. Use for the restored guild system, Implicit Maze, and Jace becoming Living Guildpact.

The Unspoken Realms **Return to Ravnica Block Lore Chapters** are a useful audio-access path through this period; the underlying official lore remains the canon source.

## Living Guildpact era
Public stories involving Jace’s work on Ravnica model the problem of a legitimate magical arbiter plus frequent absence. This is the principal political baseline for the main vault.

## Guilds of Ravnica / Ravnica Allegiance
Use these as the transition out of the main Jace-era baseline and into the Bolas-infiltration state.

Audio access:
- Unspoken Realms **Guilds of Ravnica Web Stories**, Episodes 161–165.
- Unspoken Realms **Ravnica Allegiance Web Stories**, Episodes 168–172.

## The Gathering Storm
The Unspoken Realms adaptation spans **20 episodes** and is especially useful as the bridge from late pre-War politics into the active War of the Spark crisis. Use it to extract leadership changes, faction maneuvering, cross-guild interaction, and the city’s changing political assumptions.

## War of the Spark
*War of the Spark: Ravnica* by Greg Weisman is an official novel/audiobook and belongs in the active wartime overlay.

Use it for invasion-state leadership, Planeswalker arrivals, forced interguild cooperation, and the breakdown of normal civic conditions.

## Immediate post-War
*War of the Spark: Forsaken* by Greg Weisman is an official sequel/audiobook. Use it for immediate aftermath and later/post-War overlays, **not** to rewrite the default Jace-era baseline.

## Recommended audio listening path
1. Return to Ravnica Block Lore Chapters.
2. Guilds of Ravnica Web Stories.
3. Ravnica Allegiance Web Stories.
4. *The Gathering Storm*.
5. *War of the Spark: Ravnica*.
6. *War of the Spark: Forsaken*.
