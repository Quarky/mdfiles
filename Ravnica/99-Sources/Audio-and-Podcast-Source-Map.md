# Audio and Podcast Source Map

These are useful **listening sources and access formats** for the Ravnica timeline. The distinction between official publication and fan audio adaptation matters for canon use.

## Official audiobooks

### War of the Spark: Ravnica — Greg Weisman
**Source class:** official published novel / audiobook.  
**Best use:** [[../alternate-eras/War-of-the-Spark/00-Era-Overview|War of the Spark]] wartime state, Planeswalker arrivals, the invasion, guild cooperation/conflict, and major character actions during the Bolas crisis.

### War of the Spark: Forsaken — Greg Weisman
**Source class:** official published sequel / audiobook.  
**Best use:** immediate **post-War aftermath**, political consequences, relationships, and the transition out of the invasion state.

Do not use post-War leadership or conditions to rewrite the default Jace-era baseline. Add them to a later/post-War overlay when that section is expanded.

## Fan audio adaptations — Unspoken Realms Podcast

The recordings below are **fan audio adaptations**. Treat the **underlying official story/lore text** as the canon source; voice performance, editing, music, narration choices, summaries, or added connective material in the fan production are not independently canon unless supported by the original text.

### The Gathering Storm — 20 episodes
**Timeline role:** major **War of the Spark prequel**.  
**Best use:** the bridge from the default Jace/Living-Guildpact setting into the Bolas-infiltration and pre-War state; guild leadership changes, Ral Zarek, Niv-Mizzet, Vraska, Kaya, political maneuvering, and mounting interguild crisis.

### Guilds of Ravnica Web Stories — Unspoken Realms Episodes 161–165
**Timeline role:** late pre-War Ravnica.  
**Best use:** street-level guild culture, named characters, faction pressures, and changes that should be separated from the earlier Jace-era baseline.

### Ravnica Allegiance Web Stories — Unspoken Realms Episodes 168–172
**Timeline role:** late pre-War / direct approach to the War of the Spark.  
**Best use:** guild leadership and political conditions as Bolas's influence becomes overt enough to alter the city-state.

### Return to Ravnica Block Lore Chapters
**Timeline role:** Return to Ravnica / Gatecrash / Dragon's Maze period.  
**Best use:** restored guild culture, Implicit Maze context, the political environment that produces Jace as Living Guildpact, and the transition into this guide's default era.

## Recommended listening order

1. **Return to Ravnica Block Lore Chapters**
2. **Guilds of Ravnica Web Stories**
3. **Ravnica Allegiance Web Stories**
4. **The Gathering Storm**
5. **War of the Spark: Ravnica**
6. **War of the Spark: Forsaken**

## Extraction checklist for this living-world guide

When reviewing any source, capture more than plot:
- named locations and what they physically contain;
- normal civilian activity before a crisis;
- guild ranks, jobs, and chain of command;
- internal disagreements;
- cross-guild speech and negotiation;
- what ordinary residents know or misunderstand;
- transport, food, housing, policing, finance, medicine, religion, and labor details;
- era-specific political assumptions;
- damage/destruction/rebuilding state of locations;
- repeated NPC behavioral cues that improve improvisation.

That material should be pushed into the relevant location, guild, NPC, and era notes rather than remaining only in a bibliography.
