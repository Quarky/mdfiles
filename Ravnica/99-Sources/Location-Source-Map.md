# Location Source Map

High-confidence main-era Tenth District locations are grounded in Guildmasters’ Guide to Ravnica: six precincts, Transguild Promenade, Tin Street, Plaza/Chamber, New Prahv, Whitestone, Griffin Heights, Augustin Station, Vitu-Ghazi, Great Concourse, Concordance, Beast Haven, Sunhome, Nivix, Bulwark, Red Wastes, Skarrg, Zonot Seven, Zameck, Blistercoils, Prism University, Ismeri Library, Deadbridge Chasm, Wayport, Smelting Quarter, and the undercity framework.

If a location has only sparse canon, use a labeled SYNTHESIS interior rather than pretending an invented plan is official.
