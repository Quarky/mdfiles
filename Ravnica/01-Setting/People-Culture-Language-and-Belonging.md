# People, Culture, Language, and Belonging

Crowds can include humans, elves, goblins, minotaurs, vedalken, loxodons, centaurs, kraul, merfolk, giants, vampires, dryads, angels, hybrids, undead, and many other beings.

Strong associations exist but are not uniforms: Ordruun minotaurs with Boros; vedalken with Azorius/Simic; Silhana elves with Selesnya; Devkarin with Golgari; kraul with Golgari; merfolk with Simic; angels with Boros.

Guild membership is material belonging: jobs, food, housing, training, healers, colleagues, identity, ritual, status, protection. Leaving can mean losing a social world even without legal coercion.

Locals usually do not stare because a Simic hybrid enters a shop or an angel crosses the skyline. They stare because the hybrid is bleeding or the angel landed in a poor neighborhood.
