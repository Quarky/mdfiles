# Magic, Infrastructure, and Civic Systems

Magic is infrastructure.

Izzet: power, boilers, conduits, elemental containment, metallurgy, mizzium, gravity, spatial research.  
Azorius: restraint, wards, investigation, compulsion, divination, legal authority.  
Simic: public health, biomancy, terraforming, aquatic ecosystems, adaptation.  
Golgari: waste, corpses, fungi, rot farms, lower-city logistics.  
Selesnya: living civic space, gardens, beasts, community agriculture.  
Orzhov: contract, property, debt, ritual, inheritance, ghosts.  
Dimir: couriers, archives, codes, memory, covert observation.  
Rakdos: mining, labor, stages, entertainment, industrial crews.

A good infrastructure crisis cascades across several guilds instead of producing one villain.
