# Dialogue and Mannerisms

Use attention patterns, not fake accents.

Azorius notices wording and authority. Boros notices danger and exits. Dimir notices omissions and listeners. Golgari notices rot, waste, water, bodies, and who gets discarded. Gruul notices fences, weak structures, fear, and dependence. Izzet notices mechanisms and anomalies. Orzhov notices status, property, obligation, and who pays. Rakdos notices audience, embarrassment, desire, fear, hypocrisy. Selesnya notices relationship, isolation, food, exclusion. Simic notices symptoms, health, systems, environmental pressure.

Use one word with different meanings. Example: safety. Azorius means compliance, Boros means nobody gets hurt, Izzet means controlled probability, Orzhov means protected assets, Selesnya means community resilience, Gruul hears control disguised as concern.
