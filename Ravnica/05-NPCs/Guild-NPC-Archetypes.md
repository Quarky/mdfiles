# Guild NPC Archetypes

For any guild choose one: loyalist, opportunist, reformer, exhausted professional, ambitious middle-rank, outsider/anomaly.

Then add a role from the guild dossier and answer: what does this person need today, what part of the guild do they dislike, who in another guild do they trust, what would make them call for institutional help, what line will they not cross?

This is more useful than a random personality table because it produces faction-aware behavior immediately.
