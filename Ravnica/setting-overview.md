# Setting Overview

## The city-world
Ravnica is an ecumenopolis: civilization stretches across the known plane in layers of streets, towers, plazas, ruins, tunnels, sewers, buried districts, and reclaimed wilderness. The Tenth District is the best-known political and adventuring center, but it is not the whole plane.

The central social fact is the **ten-guild system**. Each guild expresses a two-color philosophy and historically performs a civic function. Their authority overlaps constantly, producing cooperation, bureaucracy, corruption, sabotage, and occasional open violence.

## The ten guilds at a glance
| Guild | Colors | Public role | Dramatic tension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Azorius Senate | WU | law, courts, regulation | order vs paralysis |
| Boros Legion | RW | military, policing, protection | justice vs zeal |
| House Dimir | UB | information, couriers, covert work | knowledge vs manipulation |
| Golgari Swarm | BG | reclamation, rot, food cycles | renewal vs resentment |
| Gruul Clans | RG | displaced wilderness tradition | freedom vs destruction |
| Izzet League | UR | infrastructure, engineering, research | invention vs safety |
| Orzhov Syndicate | WB | finance, religion, contracts | obligation vs exploitation |
| Cult of Rakdos | BR | entertainment, hard labor, spectacle | expression vs cruelty |
| Selesnya Conclave | GW | community, agriculture, nature | harmony vs conformity |

## Guilded and guildless
A large part of Ravnica's population is guildless. They live under guild-built infrastructure and guild law without necessarily sharing guild privilege. A useful street-level scene asks: **which guild claims authority, who actually does the work, who pays, and who bears the risk?**

## Magic and technology
Ravnica mixes pervasive magic with urban technology. Izzet systems move water, heat, power, and transit; biomancy can alter bodies; spirits can be bound by debt; law can be magical; architecture can be grown as well as built. Magic is ordinary enough to be infrastructural but extraordinary enough to create specialists, hazards, and inequality.

## Campaign tone
Ravnica supports procedural investigation, political thriller, urban fantasy, heists, civic disaster, faction diplomacy, street action, horror beneath the city, and high-level planar crisis. The best Ravnican conflicts rarely reduce to good guild versus evil guild: every guild has a legitimate social need it answers and a characteristic way of becoming dangerous.
