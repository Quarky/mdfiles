# Setting Overview
Ravnica is a vast city-world of streets, towers, plazas, ruins, tunnels, buried districts and reclaimed wild spaces. Its central social fact is the ten-guild system. Each guild combines a two-color philosophy with historical civic responsibilities, and their jurisdictions overlap constantly.

| Guild | Colors | Civic identity | Central tension |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[guilds/01-azorius-senate|Azorius]] | WU | law and regulation | order vs paralysis |
| [[guilds/02-boros-legion|Boros]] | RW | military and protection | justice vs zeal |
| [[guilds/03-house-dimir|Dimir]] | UB | information and covert work | knowledge vs manipulation |
| [[guilds/04-golgari-swarm|Golgari]] | BG | reclamation and food cycles | renewal vs resentment |
| [[guilds/05-gruul-clans|Gruul]] | RG | wild autonomy | freedom vs destruction |
| [[guilds/06-izzet-league|Izzet]] | UR | infrastructure and research | invention vs safety |
| [[guilds/07-orzhov-syndicate|Orzhov]] | WB | finance, faith, contracts | obligation vs exploitation |
| [[guilds/08-cult-of-rakdos|Rakdos]] | BR | entertainment and labor | expression vs cruelty |
| [[guilds/09-selesnya-conclave|Selesnya]] | GW | community and cultivation | harmony vs conformity |
| [[guilds/10-simic-combine|Simic]] | GU | medicine and adaptation | improvement vs limits |

A large part of the population is guildless. Good Ravnica scenes ask who claims jurisdiction, who actually does the work, who pays, and who bears the risk.
