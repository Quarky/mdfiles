# Precinct Five — Scholars, Laboratories and Zonot Seven

V2 treats Precinct Five as the district's **expertise economy**: universities, laboratories, archives, specialist workshops, clinics, lecture halls, and technical rivalries. Published material places the Simic guildhall **Zameck in Zonot Seven** here.

## First impression

People carry notebooks, cases, specimen jars, instruments, rolled diagrams, and coffee. Clothing shows burns, stains, chemical damage, or careful academic respectability.

A messenger who can navigate faculty offices is as valuable as a spellcaster.

## Sensory palette

**See:** glassware in windows, lecture posters, sealed doors, animated diagrams, water channels, weird equipment being transported under tarps.

**Hear:** bells marking lectures, arguments using vocabulary bystanders do not understand, bubbling tanks, magical hum, applause from a demonstration.

**Smell:** disinfectant, brine, hot metal, ink, ozone, preserved specimens, coffee.

## Izzet and Simic rivalry

The Izzet ask what happens if energy, matter, space, or magic is pushed harder.

The Simic ask what life can become if adaptation is accelerated or redirected.

Both respect expertise and experimentation, but they have different intuitions about what counts as a system and what counts as a subject.

## Zameck and the zonot

A zonot is not merely a basement lab. It descends through city layers toward ancient water, creating its own ecosystem, culture, research spaces, and vertical movement.

Visitors should feel the temperature and humidity change as they descend. Street noise fades. Water sound grows. Architecture becomes more biological. The sense of "city" does not disappear; it transforms.

## Academic politics

Research has patrons, rivals, publication disputes, safety reviews, stolen ideas, student labor, guild priorities, and ethical boundaries. A scientific scene can be political without a villain.

## Scene seed

A Simic clinic has developed a treatment that works, but the organism used to produce it is spreading into municipal water channels. Izzet engineers want to sterilize the lines. Simic researchers insist that will destroy the only viable culture. Azorius officials want the facility closed pending review. Patients are waiting outside.

The PCs arrive while the cure is still being administered.
