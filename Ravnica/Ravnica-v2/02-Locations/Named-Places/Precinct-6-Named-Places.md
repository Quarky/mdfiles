# Precinct Six — Named Places, Labor, Night Danger, and the Undercity Edge

Precinct Six is not simply "the poor industrial area." It is a logistics engine. Warehouses, docks, wide freight roads, modest worker housing, factories, organized crime, Rakdos nightlife, Orzhov ownership, Boros storage and patrols, Golgari access, and undercity monsters all overlap.

The people who live here tend to value practical reliability over respectability.

## Smelting Quarter

### Canon anchor
The manufacturing heart at the north end of the precinct: hot, smoky, active day and night. Foundry Street supports goblin communities, crime, factories, and intense Rakdos presence.

### Shift rhythm
**Dawn:** night workers leave as day crews arrive; food vendors make their best money.  
**Midday:** freight and heat peak.  
**Evening:** workers fill cheap taverns while performers assemble stages.  
**Night:** industrial work continues while Rakdos clubs intensify; Boros watches the line between revel and riot.

### Sights
Furnace glow reflected in low cloud, cranes, carts, molten metal glimpsed behind shields, soot on pale stone, workers with heavy gloves, goblin homes squeezed around industrial structures.

### Sounds
Metal striking metal, steam, warning whistles, shouted load counts, Rakdos drums, cart chains, factory gates.

### Smells
Hot iron, coal or magical combustion, oil, sweat, smoke, street food, alcohol, sulfur after experiments.

### Social rule
People respect someone who can do their job safely under pressure. Wealth alone impresses less here.

## Kamen Fortress

### Canon anchor
A Boros garrison positioned to protect Legion interests and watch Rakdos activity around the Smelting Quarter.

### Why residents feel mixed about it
Kamen means patrols arrive faster. It also means residents are routinely observed as potential trouble.

Factory owners appreciate security. Rakdos performers treat it as an audience they did not invite. Goblin gangs map patrol timing carefully.

### Scene pressure
Kamen imposes a temporary curfew after a string of killings. The curfew protects residents from a night predator and destroys income for legitimate night workers.

## Gore House and the Demon's Vestibule

### Canon anchor
Gore House is a Rakdos club in a repurposed factory; the area provides an entrance down toward Rix Maadi through the Demon's Vestibule.

### Day
Shuttered doors, cleanup crews, damaged props, deliveries, performers sleeping late, neighbors complaining about last night's noise.

### Night
Heat, music, smoke, crowds, bouncers, performance danger, people arriving because the place is notorious.

The club should feel like a business and community as well as a cult venue.

### The descent
The path toward Rix Maadi should feel like moving away from ordinary municipal Ravnica while still carrying traces of industry: old stairs, brick, chains, heat, echo, graffiti, infrastructure abandoned or repurposed.

### Scene pressure
The PCs need information from a performer below, but Kamen has closed the visible entrance. Another route exists through factory service tunnels controlled by workers who dislike both Boros and Rakdos.

## Medori Park

### Canon anchor
Despite its name, this western area was converted from parkland into warehouse use by an Orzhov figure. It is heavily tied to storage and has a strong undead presence at night.

### The name as urban memory
Residents still call it a park because city names outlive land use.

That is deeply Ravnican: history becomes vocabulary after physical reality changes.

### Day
Warehouse clerks, guards, teamsters, loaders, inventories, Boros equipment storage, Orzhov property managers.

### Night
Shutters close. Watch changes. Undead guardians and less controlled dangers emerge. The same broad warehouse lanes that feel merely utilitarian by day become exposed and echoing.

### Scene pressure
A warehouse worker disappears on the night shift. The owner calls it abandonment. Coworkers insist a spirit has been following employees who handle one particular shipment.

## Deadbridge Chasm

### Canon anchor
A vast opening into Golgari territory and routes toward Korozda. Mossy stairs and fungal growth line the descent; kraul and Devkarin traffic connect surface and undercity trade.

### First impression
The city suddenly has an edge.

Where most Ravnica hides depth under floors and grates, Deadbridge reveals it.

Bridges span darkness. Stairs cling to walls. Fungal light marks paths below. Smell rises before details become visible.

### Sound
Echoed carts, insect wings, water, voices from far below, bridge traffic overhead, occasional falling debris.

### Smell
Decomposition, damp earth, fungus, sewage, cooking from lower settlements, surface smoke settling downward.

### Social reality
Surface citizens use the chasm as a mental boundary. Golgari traders use it as a road.

What one population calls descent, another calls commute.

### Scene pressure
A body falls from a bridge. Surface law treats the chasm as crime scene boundary. Golgari below recover the corpse first and insist local custom now applies.

## Wayport

### Canon anchor
A vertically layered neighborhood rising within the chasm, connected to surrounding city by numerous bridges and handling significant warehousing and interdistrict trade.

### Why it is visually memorable
Wayport makes Ravnica's verticality impossible to ignore.

A warehouse roof on one level can be a loading yard for another bridge. Freight moves horizontally across open depth and vertically through hoists.

### Scene pressure
A bridge closure sends freight through a lower route claimed by Golgari residents. Surface merchants call the route public. Residents disagree.

---

## Benzer's Bridge

### Canon anchor
A broad cargo thoroughfare carrying interdistrict traffic, with a small market and hidden nightlife inside or beneath the bridge structure.

### Day
Freight, shops, food, carts, constant flow.

### Night
Legitimate businesses close. Hidden rooms open. The bridge's interior becomes another district layered beneath the public one.

### Scene pressure
A daytime merchant asks the PCs to retrieve a family member from a pain club hidden under the same stall where they sell lunch.

The merchant knows exactly where the entrance is and has pretended for years not to know.

---

## Labor culture

Precinct Six works because people show up despite heat, danger, theft, ownership disputes, and bad shifts.

A worker who keeps a machine running may have more local respect than a rich visitor.

Use:
- union-like informal crews;
- shift meals;
- tool lending;
- grudges over unsafe supervisors;
- people covering for neighbors;
- silence about harmless illicit activity;
- sudden collective anger when someone endangers workers for profit.

## Precinct Six GM memory line

**Freight never stops, furnaces color the night, respectable ownership sits beside practical crime, and every bridge or warehouse has another city underneath it.**
