# Precinct Three — Named Places, Green Urbanism, and Community

Precinct Three is the Greenbelt, but it should never be described as countryside dropped into the city. It is **urban nature shaped by centuries of cohabitation**: parks, stables, gardens, old stone, wooden structures strengthened by magic, elevated green roadways, animal traffic, multigenerational homes, and Selesnyan communal institutions.

## Vitu-Ghazi

### Canon anchor
Vitu-Ghazi is the Selesnya guildhall, a monumental city-tree and cathedral to Mat'Selesnya. It stands as the center of Selesnyan culture, law, pilgrimage, and identity.

### Approach
The tree becomes visible long before the entrance is obvious.

The skyline changes from towers dominating trees to one living structure dominating towers. Roots have shaped paving and foundations. Streets widen where crowds of pilgrims routinely gather.

### Sound
Leaves high above ordinary rooftops, birds, prayer, conversation, animal harness, communal songs, children, market noise from approaches.

### Smell
Bark, soil, flowers, cooking, rain caught in vegetation, animals, herbal incense.

### Social experience
Visitors without hostile intent can enter public areas, so Vitu-Ghazi should contain both devotion and tourism.

Some people come seeking spiritual communion. Some come because the markets are good. Some are guildless residents meeting relatives. Some tourists take pictures or stare while initiates try not to resent them.

### Authority
Selesnyan law is experienced as communal legitimacy rather than a wall of official paperwork. People know who the voda is, who organizes meals, who trains militia, who mediates disputes.

### Scene pressure
A citizen accused of theft seeks sanctuary among relatives at Vitu-Ghazi. Azorius has lawful authority to arrest them. The vernadi asks for time to establish restitution without removing the person from the community.

The argument is not whether theft occurred. It is what justice is for.

## The Canopy

### Canon anchor
A northern neighborhood beneath tall trees overshadowed by Vitu-Ghazi; buildings occupy ground and larger branches, connected by ladders and rope walkways.

### Verticality
This is where Ravnica's vertical city becomes organic.

The PCs can stand on stone street level, hear a conversation above, climb a ladder to a family walkway, then cross through foliage to a home whose front door is thirty feet above the old road.

### Sights
Branch dwellings, rope rails, laundry between trees, baskets raised by pulley, children moving confidently where outsiders grip every handhold.

### Sound
Wood creak, rope strain, leaves, distant hooves below, voices from multiple elevations.

### Hazard
Fire is terrifying here. So are violent aerial creatures. Residents treat water buckets, evacuation routes, and branch maintenance seriously.

### Scene pressure
An old tree has become unsafe after magical contamination. Cutting it protects several homes but destroys dwellings built into its branches. Simic offers a biological treatment with uncertain side effects.

## Great Concourse

### Canon anchor
Elevated Selesnyan roadways converge here. On holy days it fills with worshipers; on ordinary days it operates as a major market.

### Visual identity
Polished pale pathways topped with living greenery create an impossible hybrid of road, park, bridge, and shrine.

Trade carts move beside pilgrims. Animals graze in controlled strips while merchants unload goods. Shade and sun shift through cultivated canopy.

### Social rule
Market behavior and sacred behavior overlap. A merchant can haggle loudly one day and attend a rite at the same place another.

### Scene pressure
A commercial dispute erupts during preparations for a holy day. Closing stalls would make room for pilgrims but destroy the week's income for merchants who traveled across districts.

## Concordance

### Canon anchor
An eastern neighborhood associated with the old city and among the oldest parts of the precinct.

### Urban age
Do not make “old” mean ruined.

Concordance should feel **adapted**. Doorways altered for new residents. Ancient stone beneath newer wood. Old courtyards serving new purposes. Shrines with layers of repair that visibly come from different centuries.

Residents use history casually because they live inside it.

### Scene pressure
A renovation uncovers an older sealed chamber beneath a family home. Azorius wants archaeological review. Selesnya says the space is connected to local spiritual history. The family wants their kitchen floor back.

## Stables and animal economy

Precinct Three feeds and moves more than people.

Ravnica's domesticated beasts range well beyond ordinary horses. Stable work means feed logistics, waste, veterinary care, harness craft, breeding, transport, and complaints from neighbors.

A stable accident can create a city-scale traffic event if the escaped animal is large enough.

## Evening life

The precinct's strongest contrast with dense commercial areas comes after work.

Communal gatherings, storytelling, trained animal performances, shared meals, and neighborhood care should make evening feel socially open.

The GM should show why Selesnya's offer of belonging appeals to people.

## Precinct Three GM memory line

**Old homes under living shade, animals and children treated as shared responsibilities, sacred space functioning as market space, and every green refuge carrying a political question about who gets to shape nature.**
