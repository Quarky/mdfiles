# Undercity, Verticality and the Buried City

The undercity is not one sewer level. It is **all the older Ravnicas that the current city has built over, beside, through, and forgotten**.

## Layers

A descent might pass:

1. modern cellar and utility corridors;
2. active drains, heat lines, and service passages;
3. abandoned transit;
4. older streets now roofed by later construction;
5. crypts, cisterns, ruins, collapsed plazas;
6. Golgari-controlled farms and settlements;
7. natural caverns or spaces so old that city history becomes uncertain.

The deeper party travels, the less reliable modern jurisdiction becomes.

## Sensory progression

Near the surface: warm pipes, hammering overhead, sewer water, maintenance lamps, chalk marks, service workers.

Deeper: constant damp, fungal light, insects, old stone polished by feet, roots through ceilings, distant water, organic heat from compost, echoes that make distance hard to judge.

Far below: enormous spaces suddenly opening after cramped tunnels; black water; architecture at impossible angles; statues facing walls that did not exist when they were carved; entire facades buried inside newer foundations.

## Golgari presence

The Golgari are not merely monsters living under the city. They operate food, reclamation, waste, fungal cultivation, undead labor, and communities whose relationship to the surface mixes resentment and necessity.

A surface merchant may insult Golgari food in public and still depend on their deliveries.

## Dimir presence

The undercity is useful for routes, caches, surveillance, safe houses, and forgotten access points. Do not make every shadow a Dimir agent. Their power grows when the party cannot tell which ordinary courier tunnel is also an intelligence route.

## Orzhov and the dead

Old burial spaces, forgotten ownership claims, sealed family crypts, and spirit-binding arrangements create legal as well as supernatural dangers. A haunted chamber may be less dangerous than the deed proving somebody still owns it.

## Simic depths

Zonots reveal that the buried city can open into ancient water. This makes "underground" and "aquatic" part of the same vertical geography. Water pressure, flooded ruins, strange adapted life, and scientific access tunnels can intersect Golgari territory.

## The undercity social rule

Surface characters often assume "abandoned" means "unowned." Undercity residents know better.

Before the party camps, breaks a wall, opens a sealed door, or harvests fungus, decide who already uses the space and what they call ownership.

## Scene seed

The PCs descend to repair an Izzet line. The pipe crosses a Golgari growing chamber. The Azorius map says the chamber is abandoned. The Golgari family living there has receipts showing they have supplied a surface soup kitchen for thirty years. The pipe must be shut down tonight or a residential block loses heat.

That is Ravnica: infrastructure, law, class, guild history, and human need in one room.
