# Precinct Six — Industry, Deadbridge and the Smelting Quarter

Published material anchors several important identities here: **Deadbridge Chasm occupies much of Precinct Six**, and the **Smelting Quarter** is a Rakdos-heavy industrial neighborhood. V2 leans into Precinct Six as the place where the city's appetite becomes physically visible.

## First impression

Freight. Heat. soot. cranes. shift workers. deep cuts in the urban fabric. Streets that smell like metal and damp stone at the same time.

Upper walkways may pass over chasms or industrial yards. Lower routes disappear into Deadbridge and the undercity.

## Sensory palette

**See:** foundry glow, suspended loads, broken bridges, patched railings, warning paint, slag heaps, fungus on damp retaining walls.

**Hear:** hammering, chains, whistles, drums after dark, carts, shouted safety calls, the distant echo of something falling a very long way.

**Smell:** smoke, hot iron, oil, sweat, sulfur, fungal earth, cheap food, river or sewer damp.

## Rakdos labor and entertainment

Rakdos civic function historically includes manual labor and entertainment. This is where those roles can visibly overlap.

A stagehand may work a brutal industrial shift and perform at night. A foundry crew may treat a dangerous stunt as entertainment because danger is already part of their work culture. Permanent establishments thrive where respectable citizens publicly disapprove and privately attend.

## Golgari interface

Industrial waste moves downward. Reclamation moves materials and food upward. The boundary between "factory district" and "undercity" is porous.

## Orzhov interest

Industry requires capital, leases, insurance, debt, and ownership. An Orzhov office can be as consequential to a furnace crew as the furnace itself.

## Izzet presence

Published Izzet material places a metallurgy outpost in the Smelting Quarter. That makes the neighborhood a natural mix of practical industrial workers and researchers who consider molten metal an invitation.

## Scene seed

A night shift refuses to enter a foundry after three workers vanish near a sealed lower access. Management says the stoppage violates contract. The Orzhov creditor threatens foreclosure. A Rakdos ringmaster offers to turn the search into a paid spectacle. Golgari workers below insist something from the surface broke through first.

The furnace is cooling. If it shuts completely, restarting it will cost a fortune.
