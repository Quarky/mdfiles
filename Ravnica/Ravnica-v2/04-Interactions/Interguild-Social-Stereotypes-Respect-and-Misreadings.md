# Interguild Social Stereotypes, Respect, and Misreadings

These are **common attitudes**, not objective truth. Use them to make conversations layered without making every NPC identical.

## Azorius sees…
- **Boros:** necessary but impatient.
- **Dimir:** evidence contamination wearing a coat.
- **Golgari:** difficult jurisdiction below maps.
- **Gruul:** proof civilization needs enforceable boundaries.
- **Izzet:** a permit violation waiting to happen.
- **Orzhov:** people who read every loophole more carefully than the lawmakers.
- **Rakdos:** public danger defended as expression.
- **Selesnya:** well-meaning parallel authority.
- **Simic:** experts who say "medical necessity" when they want an exception.

**Respects:** documentation, predictability, testimony that survives scrutiny.  
**Misreads:** informal legitimacy as lawlessness.

## Boros sees…
- **Azorius:** people who can make justice wait until it dies.
- **Dimir:** useful intelligence from a source that cannot be trusted.
- **Golgari:** uncomfortable allies during disasters.
- **Gruul:** dangerous raiders with courage Boros can understand.
- **Izzet:** brilliant support staff who should stop testing during emergencies.
- **Orzhov:** legal predators.
- **Rakdos:** crowd-disaster generators.
- **Selesnya:** dependable defenders who debate too long.
- **Simic:** lifesaving medics whose experiments need watching.

**Respects:** courage, accountability, protection of weaker people.  
**Misreads:** caution as cowardice and process as indifference.

## Dimir sees…
Every guild as an information system.

Azorius generates records. Boros generates reports. Golgari controls forgotten routes. Gruul creates unpredictable gaps. Izzet leaks novelty. Orzhov catalogs obligations. Rakdos shapes public perception. Selesnya distributes trust. Simic produces restricted knowledge.

**Respects:** people who understand information has cost.  
**Misreads:** genuine openness as either cover or vulnerability.

## Golgari sees…
The surface as a civilization that hides consequences below street level.

Azorius sends regulations downward. Boros sends bodies after conflict. Izzet sends runoff and broken machinery. Orzhov sends debtors and the dead. Selesnya sends organic waste while preaching purity of life.

**Respects:** people who acknowledge dependence.  
**Misreads:** cleanliness as denial rather than sometimes simply good sanitation.

## Gruul sees…
Structures as claims.

A road says "we own movement here." A wall says "we decide who belongs." A permit says "our paper outranks your memory."

**Respects:** direct speech, physical courage, people who admit the cost of what they want.  
**Misreads:** maintenance of existing communities as identical to expansion.

## Izzet sees…
Problems waiting for models.

Azorius limits variables. Boros gives constraints. Simic offers alternate systems. Gruul demonstrates raw forces. Rakdos finds weird applications. Orzhov provides funding with irritating conditions.

**Respects:** competence, novel insight, willingness to test assumptions.  
**Misreads:** emotional or cultural objection as lack of technical understanding.

## Orzhov sees…
Relationships as obligations already present whether written or not.

**Respects:** people who pay debts, honor promises, and understand leverage.  
**Misreads:** gift economies and communal reciprocity as badly documented debt.

## Rakdos sees…
Every institution's dignity as a setup for a joke.

Azorius gives rules to parody. Boros gives heroic imagery to puncture. Orzhov gives hypocrisy. Selesnya gives earnestness. Izzet gives spectacular accidents.

**Respects:** people who can take a joke, own desire, perform under pressure, and refuse false dignity.  
**Misreads:** sincere boundaries as cowardice or repression.

## Selesnya sees…
People as parts of relationships.

**Respects:** reciprocity, care, patience, contribution.  
**Misreads:** strong individual boundaries as alienation.

## Simic sees…
Bodies and environments as interacting systems.

**Respects:** observation, adaptability, consent understood as informed participation, willingness to revise assumptions.  
**Misreads:** attachment to an existing form as fear of improvement.

# Respect changes scenes

NPCs should be capable of revising stereotypes.

A Gruul scout may respect an Azorius official who personally walks disputed ground before ruling.  
A Boros sergeant may trust a Dimir courier who gives actionable truth at personal risk.  
An Orzhov advokist may admire a Selesnyan community that honors promises even without contracts.  
An Izzet researcher may respect a Rakdos rigger whose practical safety knowledge catches a design flaw.

Those moments keep guild identity from becoming caricature.
