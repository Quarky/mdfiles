# Interguild Case Studies

These are models for how Ravnican institutions collide around ordinary civic needs.

## Case 1 — Apartment Fire

**Problem:** fire in a dense residential block.

**Boros:** evacuate now, enter dangerous structure.  
**Azorius:** preserve scene if arson is suspected.  
**Izzet:** isolate heat and gas lines.  
**Orzhov:** owner/insurer asks who is liable.  
**Selesnya:** nearby vernadi shelters displaced families.  
**Dimir:** courier network knows someone left before the fire.

The fire is solved in an hour. The political incident lasts weeks.

## Case 2 — Water Contamination

**Simic:** test bodies and water, identify biological agent.  
**Izzet:** isolate pumps and pressure zones.  
**Golgari:** says contamination began in lower runoff channels.  
**Azorius:** considers quarantine and mandatory inspection.  
**Boros:** worries enforcement will trigger panic.  
**Orzhov:** sells clean water at high price.

The best villain may be nobody. A maintenance shortcut six months ago caused it.

## Case 3 — New Road Through Ruins

**Izzet:** road/utility line is necessary.  
**Azorius:** approvals are valid.  
**Gruul:** territory was never legitimately surrendered.  
**Selesnya:** old trees stabilize soil.  
**Guildless residents:** commute is currently dangerous and long.  
**Orzhov:** already purchased nearby property expecting values to rise.

All parties have facts. The dispute is about which facts count as authority.

## Case 4 — Illegal Clinic

A guildless healer uses Simic methods without official Combine oversight.

The clinic is effective, cheap, and technically illegal.

Simic fears unsafe replication. Azorius wants licensing. Boros sees no immediate victims. Orzhov offers financing in exchange for ownership. The neighborhood hides the healer.

## Case 5 — Rakdos Street Show

The performance has a permit but audience size exceeds projections.

Azorius says capacity has been violated. Boros wants exits cleared. Rakdos says crowd compression began because Orzhov security closed a side street. Orzhov says the closure protects valuable property.

A safety problem becomes a jurisdiction fight in two minutes.

## Case 6 — Missing Courier

A public Dimir courier disappears.

Azorius treats it as missing-person case. Boros suspects abduction. Dimir covertly wants the courier found before authorities inspect the route. Orzhov holds the courier's debt. Golgari witnesses saw somebody descend below street level.

The courier ran away voluntarily.

Now every institution is more interested than before.

## Case 7 — Golgari Food Shortage

A fungal blight reduces deliveries upward.

Selesnya offers surplus food but cannot replace volume. Simic wants samples. Azorius considers ration controls. Orzhov buys remaining stock. Rakdos performers start mocking hoarders.

The crisis reveals how much comfortable neighborhoods depended on food they despised.

## Case 8 — Escaped Simic Organism

Boros wants capture. Simic wants minimal injury. Izzet offers containment apparatus. Rakdos wants spectacle. Selesnya argues the creature is frightened and being driven toward crowds by pursuit.

The creature itself becomes a test of each guild's instinct.

## Case 9 — Debt Protest

Residents block an Orzhov office after mass evictions.

Orzhov presents legal contracts. Azorius confirms many are valid but investigates irregularities. Boros tries to prevent violence. Rakdos amplifies the protest. Selesnya delivers food. Dimir leaks a ledger showing one minister privately waived debts for political allies.

One document changes the moral temperature of the street.

## Case 10 — Ruin Collapse

A lower street collapses into the undercity.

Surface maps call it empty. Golgari families live below. Izzet infrastructure runs through the cavity. Orzhov owns one ancient deed. Azorius records show contradictory boundaries. Gruul claims the ruin predates all current titles.

The rescue comes first. Ownership comes immediately after.
