# An Ordinary Week in the Tenth District

This file is table synthesis built from established Ravnican institutions, amenities, guild functions, and official-fiction behavior. It is deliberately mundane. The point is to show what the city feels like **when no adventure has announced itself yet**.

# Seleszeni 12 — First Day

The morning begins with rain.

Not a storm. Persistent cold drizzle that makes the upper bridges glossy and turns every open drain into a line of moving gray water.

## 06:00 — Freight before breakfast

Precinct Two is already awake.

A bakery opens its street shutters while a Golgari delivery team moves sealed food containers from a lower access ramp.

A centaur courier complains that someone parked a produce cart across the broad lane.

An Izzet maintenance crew has three paving stones lifted beside a utility conduit. One apprentice is holding an umbrella over the open access panel instead of over herself.

A Boros patrol finishing night watch buys coffee.

None of these people are thinking about guild politics.

They are thinking about:
- wet boots;
- late deliveries;
- breakfast;
- whether the repair crew will still be blocking the corner when morning traffic peaks.

## 08:30 — The queue

An Azorius inspection closes one entrance to a public lift.

The inspection is routine.

The consequences are not.

Within fifteen minutes:
- office workers are late;
- a teacher has twelve children gathered in the wrong place;
- a loxodon porter cannot fit easily through the alternate stair;
- a merchant begins shouting;
- a street vendor moves closer to the queue and triples sales.

The inspector is not malicious.

The lift's emergency brake actually failed a test.

Nobody in line cares until the inspector explains what the brake does.

## 12:15 — Lunch politics

Two Izzet researchers sit outside a café arguing about whether a malfunction counts as a successful test if it revealed a previously unknown property.

At the next table, an Orzhov advokist is reviewing a lease.

Across the street, a Selesnyan volunteer is distributing small bowls of soup to people displaced by a building repair.

A Dimir courier leaves three sealed packets at three different offices and eats standing up.

This is what interguild life usually looks like:
**shared pavement, separate purposes**.

## 18:00 — Home traffic

Rain stops.

Steam rises from warm grates.

Balconies open.
Laundry appears.
Restaurants fill.

A Rakdos crew measures a small square for an evening performance.

An Azorius local official tells them the permit allows a stage but not open flame.

The Ringmaster says the torches are "illumination."

The official says everyone involved knows exactly what open flame means.

They negotiate.

Nobody draws a weapon.

## 23:30 — The same street

The bakery is closed.

The café is louder.

A Boros patrol now walks the opposite direction.

The Rakdos stage is gone.

There is a scorch mark where the official said there would not be open flame.

A sanitation worker makes a note.

# Dhazo 3 — Second Day

## 07:00 — Household trouble

A tenement's heat fails on three upper floors.

The landlord says the Izzet utility connection is responsible.

The Izzet utility desk says the building's internal line is private property.

The tenants say the pipes are cold and they do not care whose legal problem that is.

By noon:
- Azorius receives a complaint;
- an Orzhov representative reminds the landlord that loan covenants require habitability;
- a local Izzet technician agrees to look unofficially after shift;
- a Selesnyan family lends blankets;
- a guildless plumber says everybody is overcomplicating a cracked valve.

The plumber is right.

The cracked valve was caused by vibration from a nearby Izzet test.

Everybody is also right.

## 16:00 — Children know first

Children discover that warm vapor is escaping from a maintenance grate behind the building.

They turn it into a game.

Adults turn it into evidence.

# Prahz 19 — Third Day

## 09:00 — Market morning

The market smells like:
- wet vegetables;
- coffee;
- mushroom broth;
- baking bread;
- animal sweat;
- soap;
- cheap perfume.

A Boros veteran sells used buckles and repaired leather.

A Selesnyan grower argues amiably with a Golgari mushroom seller about compost.

A Simic hybrid shops for fruit while a child stares at their gills until the child's parent apologizes.

The hybrid laughs.

A street preacher uses the moment to begin explaining that all bodies reveal spiritual debt.

The crowd collectively decides not to engage.

## 11:30 — Small crime

A purse is stolen.

The victim shouts.

A guildless teenager points toward the wrong alley because the thief is their cousin.

A Dimir courier saw the real route and does not want involvement.

A Boros patrol arrives.

An Azorius arrester happens to be nearby and immediately objects to the patrol questioning a minor without proper process.

The stolen purse contains:
- twelve zibs;
- a work key;
- a medicine note;
- no grand conspiracy.

It matters enormously to the owner.

## 14:00 — The consequences of twelve zibs

The owner misses an appointment because the work key is gone.

The employer docks pay.

The clinic cannot find the original medicine authorization.

A clerk later waives the replacement fee.

That clerk may never know they just prevented a family from missing rent.

Small institutional kindness can matter as much as heroism.

# Mokosh 8 — Fourth Day

## 05:30 — Precinct Six shift change

Foundry workers emerge into cool air already smelling of hot metal.

Some are Rakdos.
Some are Izzet.
Some are guildless.
One is Selesnyan and leaves immediately for morning communion.

An Orzhov agent waits outside for a worker who missed two loan payments.

A laborer quietly signals the debtor through a side exit.

The agent notices.

They do not confront the laborer.

They write down the name.

## 09:00 — Deadbridge freight

A Golgari crew brings reclaimed material upward.

Surface buyers inspect:
- fungal fiber;
- salvage metal;
- treated bone;
- compost substrate;
- recovered stone.

Nobody uses the word "garbage" while negotiating price.

## 15:00 — A maintenance problem becomes a political problem

An industrial drain backs up.

The surface foreman says Golgari blockage.

The Golgari crew says the drain was narrowed by a new Izzet sleeve.

Izzet says the sleeve met specification.

Azorius says the permit was conditional.

The original permit copy is in an office across the district.

The smell gets worse.

By evening, a guildless worker has removed the obstruction while four institutions argue about who is authorized to reimburse him.

# Paujal 21 — Fifth Day

## 10:00 — Vernadi morning

A Selesnyan vernadi is busy, not silent.

People:
- cook;
- mend clothes;
- train;
- teach children;
- prune growth;
- treat minor injuries;
- argue about chores;
- pray;
- plan a communal meal.

A visitor expecting mystical serenity discovers that spiritual community includes deciding who cleans the kitchen.

## 13:00 — Recruitment without pressure

A guildless worker eats there regularly.

Nobody has demanded they join.

A friend casually asks whether they have considered staying permanently.

The worker says no.

The friend says, "All right."

Three weeks later the question will feel different because the worker now knows everyone's names.

That is how belonging grows.

## 20:00 — Defense drill

Training begins.

The same courtyard that served lunch becomes a militia space.

Weapons come out of storage.
Children move to supervised rooms.
Routes are cleared.

The transition takes minutes because it has been practiced.

An outsider realizes the peaceful commune has been military infrastructure the entire time.

# Cizarm 5 — Sixth Day

## 07:30 — Precinct Five academic traffic

Students and researchers fill cafés.

A Simic technician carries a sealed case with warning markings.

An Izzet attendant carries six notebooks tied with cord because the laboratory copying device is broken.

A professor is late.

Three students immediately invent reasons involving Dimir sabotage.

The professor simply overslept.

## 10:00 — Clinic

A warehouse worker arrives at a Simic clinic with chemical burns.

The biomancer:
- asks precise questions;
- treats pain;
- explains options;
- requests permission to take a tissue sample.

The worker hears:
"You can save me."

The researcher also hears:
"This exposure pattern is scientifically valuable."

Both are true.

The scene is Ravnican when the medical miracle and research appetite exist together.

## 17:00 — Research ethics in a tavern

Two Simic scientists disagree about a proposed adaptation.

Neither is a villain.

One believes the change is too permanent to recommend casually.

The other believes refusing a proven adaptation because it looks unfamiliar is prejudice disguised as caution.

A guildless bartender listens for twenty minutes and finally asks whether the patient has been invited to the argument.

Silence.

# Tevnember 14 — Seventh Day

## 08:00 — Guildpact administration

At the Chamber, the queue already contains:

- a Boros territorial complaint;
- a Golgari delegation;
- an Izzet request for emergency variance;
- an Orzhov contract dispute;
- a Selesnyan petition;
- two guildless complainants who have been told three times their case belongs somewhere else.

Lavinia or another administrator sorts the morning into:
- matters Jace must hear;
- matters that can be mediated;
- matters that should never have reached this office;
- matters nobody wants to admit are urgent.

The grand constitutional center of Ravnica begins with triage.

## 11:00 — One case becomes a neighborhood

A property dispute concerns six feet of alley.

On paper, trivial.

In practice:
- it is the only freight access to a bakery;
- an Orzhov lender owns the adjacent warehouse;
- the old deed references a wall removed fifty years ago;
- a Golgari tunnel runs below;
- an Izzet utility line runs through the disputed foundation;
- Boros wants the alley kept open as an emergency route.

Six feet.

Five guild interests.

Forty residents.

That is Ravnica.

## 19:00 — Week's end

People go home.

Some do not.

Night shifts start.

A courier runs.
A stage assembles.
A patrol changes.
A Golgari access gate opens.
A laboratory light stays on.
A clerk takes unfinished work home.
A family eats late because the lift broke again.

Somewhere, a famous person is making a decision that could alter the balance of ten guilds.

Most people will hear about it tomorrow.

Tonight, they need sleep.
