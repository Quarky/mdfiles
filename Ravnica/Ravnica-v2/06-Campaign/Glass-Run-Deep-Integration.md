# Glass Run — Deep Integration with Ravnica V2

This note keeps published setting anchors separate from campaign inventions.

## Canon anchors used by the one-shot

The adventure fits naturally into published Ravnica because:

- the Tenth District is a dense center of guild activity;
- Izzet laboratories can specialize intensely while remaining tied to civic infrastructure;
- the **Laboratory of Orientation** studies teleportation and spatial recombination;
- the **Laboratory of Gravitational Inversion** maintains a workshop near **Augustin Station**;
- Izzet metallurgy has presence in the **Smelting Quarter**;
- Gruul clans occupy ruins, rubblebelts, and contested urban edges;
- Rakdos stages can appear in streets and plazas and are moved between venues;
- Precinct Six and the Smelting Quarter support the industrial/nightlife tone used by the chase.

## Hydrating the opening workshop

Do not describe it only as "Izzet lab under attack."

### Before the attack

**Sight:** overhead gantries, suspended instrument cages, chalk calibration marks, mizzium housings, bundled cables, cargo platform waiting by the door.

**Sound:** transformer hum, a repeating alignment chime, technicians calling measurements, freight from Augustin Station beyond the wall.

**Smell:** hot dust, machine oil, ozone, coffee gone cold.

**Ordinary work:** one technician logs data, one argues with a supplier, one worker is fixing a mundane lift.

Then the sabotage matters because it disrupts a workplace the players briefly understand.

## Hydrating the chase

Every leg should change **social context**, not just terrain.

### Near Augustin Station

Crowded, scheduled, transit-oriented. People hate being blocked.

### Through a Rakdos street performance

The chase becomes public entertainment. Bystanders choose sides. Performers improvise around danger. The party can gain or lose crowd support.

### Toward the Smelting Quarter

Heat, shift labor, freight, soot, cranes, foundry noise. Workers care whether the chase interrupts production.

### Orientation annex

Spatial instability should sit on top of mundane abandonment: old notices, broken furniture, obsolete instruments, evidence that people once worked routine shifts here.

## Tavin Rell in a living city

Tavin works better if he uses Ravnica rather than merely running across it.

He knows:

- which checkpoint can be delayed by paperwork;
- which Rakdos troupe will turn pursuit into spectacle;
- which freight route avoids Boros;
- which Dimir courier can move a message;
- which guildless mechanic will help for cash without asking questions.

That makes him a Ravnican smuggler rather than a generic thief.

## Consequence memory

After the one-shot, record:

- which guild saw the PCs act;
- what damage they caused;
- who they helped;
- which local worker remembers them;
- whether the prism returned to Izzet hands;
- whether any institution now considers the party useful, dangerous, indebted, or embarrassing.

Those are hooks for the next visit.
