# Language, Signage, and Etiquette

Ravnica is cosmopolitan enough that communication problems usually come from **institutional language, class, jargon, and etiquette** rather than basic inability to speak.

## Signage

A street can contain:

- official Azorius notices;
- commercial signs;
- guild insignia;
- transit markers;
- handwritten repair warnings;
- chalk route marks;
- Selesnyan community announcements;
- Rakdos posters;
- Orzhov legal notices;
- coded Dimir marks;
- Gruul paint deliberately defacing formal signs.

The visual density should feel urban.

## Guild jargon

**Azorius:** statutes, jurisdiction, filing, injunction, deposition, variance.  
**Boros:** squad, sector, civilian, threat, secure, hold, evacuate.  
**Izzet:** load, field, alignment, pressure, calibration, containment.  
**Simic:** adaptation, viability, host, clade, response, baseline.  
**Orzhov:** consideration, security, obligation, principal, penalty, guarantor.  
**Golgari:** substrate, cycle, reclaim, rot, harvest, upper waste.  
**Selesnya:** vernadi, community, harmony, communion, shared need.  
**Rakdos:** billing, act, audience, mark, rig, finale.  
**Dimir:** delivery, source, contact, route, verification, exposure.  
**Gruul:** clan, ground, wall, hunt, raid, scar, old place.

Jargon becomes roleplay when NPCs assume outsiders understand it.

## Queue etiquette

Ravnicans are used to bureaucracy and density. They know how queues work and become furious when someone uses status to skip one—unless status is exactly what the culture expects to permit skipping.

## Insignia etiquette

Visible insignia can function like badge, uniform, credential, warning, or invitation.

House Dimir complicates this because overt affiliation and covert membership do not align cleanly.

## Hospitality

Accepting food, entering a vernadi, drinking in a Rakdos club, taking a private Orzhov meeting, or accepting laboratory protective gear all signal different degrees of trust.

The GM should let players learn these meanings by watching locals.

## Social mistake examples

- addressing an Azorius clerk as though they can overrule a judge;
- insulting Golgari food while eating subsidized rations;
- treating a Boros sergeant as a generic city guard;
- assuming a Selesnyan communal meal is free of obligation;
- asking a Simic hybrid whether their body is "natural";
- calling a Gruul camp abandoned because it lacks permanent construction;
- accepting an Orzhov "favor" without asking what is recorded.
