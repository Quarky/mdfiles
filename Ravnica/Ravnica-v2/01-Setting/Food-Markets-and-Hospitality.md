# Food, Markets, and Hospitality

Food is one of the fastest ways to make Ravnica feel inhabited.

## Markets as cross-guild spaces

Markets are where institutional systems become personal.

A Golgari supplier delivers mushrooms. A Selesnyan grower sells herbs. An Orzhov lender financed the stall. An Azorius inspector checks weights. Boros keeps a lane open. Izzet heat plates cook food. Dimir couriers eat while waiting for deliveries. Rakdos performers pull a crowd and block customers.

No guild owns "the market." Every guild touches it.

## Food by class

Poor food should not mean rotten garbage. Cheap staples can be hot, filling, repetitive, and culturally familiar.

Wealth buys variety, freshness, imported delicacies within the plane, better wine, private dining, and time.

A laborer may have a favorite inexpensive stew shop with strong community reputation. A rich official may eat in a polished restaurant where reservations themselves signal status.

## Golgari food

Golgari sustenance is politically charged because it makes dependency visible. The upper city can mock fungal gruel while relying on it as a hunger safety net.

Some Golgari cuisine should be genuinely good. A culture centered on fungus, reclamation, and underground agriculture would develop skill, not only desperation food.

## Selesnyan hospitality

Meals reinforce belonging. Guests are fed because feeding people is part of community.

But repeated acceptance may carry a social expectation: help with work, attend a meeting, listen to a teaching, become part of the circle.

## Orzhov dining

Business hospitality creates controlled obligation. Fine food, privacy, and courtesy make the guest feel respected while also reminding them who paid for the room.

## Rakdos food and drink

Street food around performances should be loud, spicy, messy, cheap, and designed for crowds. Vendors know which shows produce good business and which end in property damage.

## GM table

For any food scene choose:

- **temperature:** hot stew / chilled fruit / baked bread / fried snack
- **texture:** crisp / chewy / creamy / fungal / smoky
- **social meaning:** charity / date / business / ritual / shift meal / celebration
- **hidden pressure:** debt / recruitment / gossip / status / permit / shortage

A meal can carry more setting than a lore speech.
