# Sights, Sounds, Smells, and Social Signals — Master Palette

Use this as a fast table-side layer. Pick one item from each column and connect it to something happening.

## By precinct

### One
**Light:** clean reflected stone, controlled illumination at night.  
**Crowd:** officials, diplomats, wealthy visitors, service workers.  
**Pressure:** being seen matters.

### Two
**Light:** domestic windows, shopfronts, New Prahv shadow.  
**Crowd:** working families, retired soldiers, clerks, small business.  
**Pressure:** everyone knows who belongs on the block.

### Three
**Light:** broken by canopy, green reflection, warm communal lamps.  
**Crowd:** families, gardeners, animals, pilgrims, traders.  
**Pressure:** shared responsibility.

### Four
**Light:** furnace flare, laboratory glare, watch fires, damaged streets.  
**Crowd:** soldiers, merchants, transients, technicians, scavengers.  
**Pressure:** people know how to react when trouble starts.

### Five
**Light:** library lamps, laboratory glow, watery reflection at the zonot.  
**Crowd:** students, academics, researchers, brokers.  
**Pressure:** knowledge equals status.

### Six
**Light:** furnace red, warehouse lamps, dark chasm below.  
**Crowd:** laborers, teamsters, goblins, performers, freight crews.  
**Pressure:** practical competence matters more than polish.

## Layered sound rule

Never use only one ambient sound. Combine:
1. **near:** shoes, argument, utensil, machine;
2. **middle:** cart, crowd, patrol, workshop;
3. **far:** bells, horns, roc cry, industrial pulse, performance music.

Example: "A clerk's pen scratches beside you; outside, freight wheels hammer the avenue; somewhere above the roofs a Boros horn answers another across the district."

## Smell as geography

Smell can tell players they changed neighborhoods before narration does.

- perfume/incense before Orzhov frontage;
- damp fungus before an undercity access;
- ozone before Izzet works;
- hot iron before Smelting Quarter;
- brine before Zonot Seven;
- wet greenery before Selesnyan space;
- smoke and hide near rubblebelt camps.

## Crowd reaction vocabulary

When tension starts, crowds do more than flee.

- people stop but pretend not to watch;
- vendors pull fragile goods inward;
- parents move children behind them;
- couriers calculate alternate routes;
- regulars identify exits;
- off-duty guild members reveal themselves by instinct;
- opportunists move closer;
- workers become angry if the incident blocks their job;
- someone starts telling a version of the story before it is over.

That last reaction is especially Ravnican: the event is becoming politics while the PCs are still inside it.
