# Izzet League — Deep Dossier

## The Izzet self-image

The Izzet believe ignorance is a solvable problem and reality is a system that can be interrogated, modeled, dismantled, rebuilt, and improved.

Their virtue is **creative competence**.

Their vice is that curiosity can become moral permission. "We need to know what happens" can overpower "we should not make this happen."

## Laboratories as culture

The League is organized around laboratories specializing in fields of research. Published examples include pyrology, storms and electricity, metallurgy, alchemy, orientation, mimeography, continuism, arcane geometry, gravitational inversion, and plasma-dermatology.

The **Izmundi** coordinate laboratories and projects. Senior figures can enter the **Izmagnus**, advising Niv-Mizzet more directly.

The important table implication is that "the Izzet" is not one coherent research department. Two labs can compete, duplicate work, hoard findings, argue methodology, and interpret the same directive differently.

## Niv-Mizzet

Niv-Mizzet is not merely a mascot genius. He defines the guild's scale of ambition. Researchers may measure themselves against a dragon whose intellect, ego, and institutional memory vastly exceed theirs.

That creates a culture of intellectual daring and insecurity.

## Sensory palette

Blue-white electrical light. red-orange furnace glow. copper, brass, glass, ceramic insulators, mizzium housings. warning signs written over older warning signs.

Ozone. hot metal. scorched dust. oil. steam. chemical sweetness. coffee.

Buzzing, humming, venting, shouted counts, sudden silence when everyone realizes a reading is wrong.

## Ordinary work

Most Izzet members do maintenance, measurement, fabrication, calibration, data copying, specimen transport, repair, or routine monitoring. The city depends on this work.

A good Izzet scene begins with someone fixing something that normally works.

## Safety culture

The Izzet are not uniformly reckless. They have people whose entire job is preventing catastrophic failure. The conflict is that the institution also rewards discovery.

A safety technician can be the most frustrated person in the room because they genuinely understand the experiment and still think it should stop.

## Interaction habits

**Azorius:** permits and review versus experimental tempo.  
**Boros:** weapons, emergency engineering, and deep anxiety about prototypes.  
**Simic:** intellectual respect, rivalry over method and ethics.  
**Rakdos:** pyrotechnics and spectacle; both enjoy dramatic energy for very different reasons.  
**Gruul:** raw elemental power is admired; infrastructure encroachment is hated.  
**Dimir:** research secrecy is valuable and leaks are inevitable.

## How to play an Izzet NPC

Give them a hypothesis and a deadline.

Good dialogue:
- "It is stable. Stable is not the same thing as safe."
- "We did not expect that result, which is why it is important."
- "If you shut it down now, we lose the only measurement that tells us whether shutting it down was necessary."

## Scene pressure

A transit lift is failing intermittently. The Izzet team can replace the core safely in twelve hours or test an experimental bypass in twenty minutes. Hundreds of people are trapped on the wrong level. Boros wants emergency use restored. Azorius has already prohibited the bypass design.

The prototype probably works.
