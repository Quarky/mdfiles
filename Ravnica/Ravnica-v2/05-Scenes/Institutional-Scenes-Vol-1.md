# Institutional Scenes — Volume 1

## 1. Sova Hearing: The Missing Stair

An apartment owner sealed an exterior stair after repeated thefts. Fire evacuation now fails code.

The owner argues the stair was never part of the leased premises. Tenants have used it for forty years. The original building plans are from a street level that no longer exists.

The judge wants evidence. The residents want the stair reopened today. The owner wants reimbursement. An Izzet inspector says the current internal stair is unsafe anyway.

**Room texture:** high ceiling, ink, benches polished by generations of waiting, whispers cut off whenever the clerk stands.

## 2. Boros Briefing: Six Streets, Four Soldiers

The local captain has four squads and six simultaneous problems. The PCs are present because they know something about one of them.

The captain's map is covered with notes: riot risk, missing child, Gruul movement, broken bridge, Orzhov security request, Simic animal escape.

The scene is about prioritization. Every decision leaves somebody waiting.

## 3. Izzet Safety Review

A prototype passed every designed test and then did something the test did not measure.

Half the team wants to shut it down. Half wants to reproduce the anomaly immediately.

The room contains scorched instruments, three versions of the same diagram, and a mug fused to a table by heat.

No one is stupid. The disagreement is about whether unknown behavior is evidence of danger or opportunity.

## 4. Orzhov Debt Conference

The room is warm, quiet, and comfortable.

The debtor is offered water and a chair.

The advokist explains five repayment plans. Every plan is survivable in isolation. All of them reshape the next decade of the debtor's life.

The cruelty is in how reasonable everything sounds.

## 5. Selesnyan Vernadi Meeting

Food is served before debate.

Residents sit in a circle beneath a living tree. The voda listens while a family asks permission to leave the vernadi and sell their home to an Orzhov-backed developer.

Legally the home is theirs. Socially, the sale would fracture the community courtyard.

Nobody raises their voice.

## 6. Simic Consent Room

A patient needs adaptation to survive.

The biomancer explains the procedure clearly. The patient understands. Their employer insists the alteration may violate workplace rules. The patient's spouse is frightened of permanent change.

The Simic doctor becomes angry—not because anyone questions the science, but because people are discussing property and employment while the patient's condition worsens.

## 7. Dimir Archive Request

The public archive clerk is helpful, ordinary, and slightly bored.

The record the PCs want exists but was moved after a water leak.

The clerk remembers who requested it last month because the requester complained about the wait.

That tiny detail is the entire clue.

## 8. Rakdos Rehearsal

The rehearsal is more disciplined than the performance.

Rigging is checked. Marks are measured. A stage manager screams about safety. The star performer keeps improvising anyway.

The party sees the truth: chaos on stage requires enormous backstage competence.

Then one prop turns out to be an actual confiscated Azorius evidence cage.
