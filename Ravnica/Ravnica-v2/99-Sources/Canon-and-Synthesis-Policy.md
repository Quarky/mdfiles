# Canon and Synthesis Policy

V2 is intentionally deeper than a sourcebook summary, so every section should distinguish **what is published** from **what is inferred for play**.

## Labels

### Canon
Directly supported by official Ravnica setting or fiction.

Examples: the ten guilds, their civic histories, the Tenth District, the Transguild Promenade, zibs and zinos, the Living Guildpact baseline, Izzet laboratory names, Zameck in Zonot Seven, Deadbridge Chasm, the Smelting Quarter.

### Campaign
Facts established by MTGFoundry / Glass Run material.

Examples: Olya Renn, Tavin Rell, the orientation prism, exact leyline anomalies, specific one-shot events.

### Synthesis
A practical inference that connects canon facts without claiming publication.

Example: describing how a broken public lift creates class pressure, or how an Azorius permit dispute would affect a neighborhood.

### Original scene
Dialogue, sensory prose, invented citizens, and scene seeds written to make the setting playable.

## Rule for deep description

Sensory and social detail can be invented **when it grows from canon structure**.

It is reasonable to describe an Izzet utility site as hot, metallic, loud, and ozone-scented because the guild uses elemental engineering and public works. It is not acceptable to invent a named canon institution and present it as published fact.

## Era discipline

The default V2 era is the Living Guildpact baseline reflected in *Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica*: Jace embodies the Guildpact and is frequently absent.

War of the Spark facts, post-war leadership, and original Ravnica-trilogy conditions must not silently replace this baseline.

## Copyright discipline

Do not reproduce full copyrighted source text.

Use:
- concise paraphrase;
- fact extraction;
- short quotations only when truly useful;
- source titles and links;
- original prose for read-aloud and scenes.

The objective is a setting bible for play, not a copied book.
