# Consequence and News Clocks

| Time | Typical response |
|---|---|
| 0-10 min | witnesses, workers, guards, rescue |
| 10-60 min | local authority, owner, employer, guild liaison |
| 1-8 hr | filings, command review, shutdown, retaliation, rumor |
| 1-3 days | leadership notices patterns and pressures allies |
| weeks | rents, routes, prices, patrols, reputations change |

News moves from witnesses to neighbors/coworkers to guild channels to brokers/reporters/Dimir to formal notice to competing narratives.

Ask who learned first, who can prove it, who can sell it, and who wants it forgotten.
