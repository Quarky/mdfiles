# Sources & Further Reading

## Primary project source
**Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica** — the project's available PDF is the main tabletop-setting source. It covers character integration, all ten guilds, the Tenth District and its six precincts, adventure construction, treasures, and guild-specific NPCs/creatures. This guide paraphrases rather than reproduces the book.

## Existing project material
**Ravnica — The Glass Run — GM Guide** — project-created adventure material. It already grounds play in the Tenth District and uses the Laboratory of Orientation, gravitational-inversion research, Augustin Station, and the Smelting Quarter. It is treated as campaign material, not external canon fiction.

## Official public Ravnica resources
- **Ravnica plane overview** — Wizards' concise overview of the city-world, ten guilds, color pairs, and Guildpact.
- **A Flavorful Guide to the Guilds of Ravnica** — official feature covering the Guilds of Ravnica-era five.
- **A Flavorful Guide to the Guilds of Ravnica Allegiance** — official feature covering the other five and era-specific leadership.
- **War of the Spark: Ravnica — Old Friends and New**
- **War of the Spark: Ravnica — The Path to Opulent**
- **War of the Spark: Ravnica — Rallying the Reluctant**
- **War of the Spark: Ravnica — Desperate Operatives**
- **War of the Spark: Ravnica — Operation Desperation**
- **War of the Spark: Ravnica — Ashes**

The War of the Spark sequence is especially useful for studying how people from incompatible guild cultures speak, bargain, mistrust one another, and still cooperate when the city is threatened.

## Other major Ravnica fiction/book lines to consult
The original Ravnica cycle and Return to Ravnica-era fiction provide additional historical characterization. Treat details such as guildmasters and political status as era-dependent rather than flattening every publication into one present day.

## Source-use rule for future additions
For every future guide addition, tag material mentally as:
- **Canon:** directly supported by an official setting/story source.
- **Campaign:** established by this MTGFoundry campaign.
- **Synthesis:** a practical inference that connects canon facts.
- **Original fiction:** illustrative scene written for this guide.

Do not paste full copyrighted stories into this repository. Store titles, citations/official links where appropriate, concise summaries, and original examples instead.
