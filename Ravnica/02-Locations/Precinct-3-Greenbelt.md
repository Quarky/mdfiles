# Precinct Three - Greenbelt

Feel: old stone reclaimed by vegetation, white pathways, cultivated gardens, beasts, tree-lined elevated roads, Vitu-Ghazi.

Vitu-Ghazi is Selesnya guildhall and spiritual center. Great Concourse is an elevated network carrying markets and pilgrims. Concordance / Old City shows ancient urban fabric reclaimed rather than abandoned. Beast Haven centers animal care, training, and trade.

Vernadi are communes centered on living trees and led by dryads called voda. Each is neighborhood, spiritual community, social network, and political unit.

If ignored: community absorbs the problem collectively. This can become extraordinary mutual aid or intense pressure to conform.
