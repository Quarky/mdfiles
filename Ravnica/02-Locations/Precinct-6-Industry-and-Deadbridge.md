# Precinct Six - Industry and Deadbridge

Feel: warehouses, docks, factories, smoke, freight, worker housing, bridges over voids, undercity smells.

Deadbridge Chasm is a major opening to Golgari undercity. Wayport is a stacked warehouse/bridge transfer node. Smelting Quarter is an industrial heart of foundries, manufacturing, Rakdos establishments, and worker neighborhoods. Kamen Fortress provides Boros industrial defense.

At the table: heat, slag, freight, shift whistles, food stalls, foremen, injuries, informal worker networks.

If ignored: management keeps production moving, creditors protect assets, Boros protects strategic sites, workers absorb danger until it becomes impossible to hide.
