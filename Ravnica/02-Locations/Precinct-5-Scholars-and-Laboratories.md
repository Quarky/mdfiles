# Precinct Five - Scholars and Laboratories

Feel: universities, libraries, dormitories, laboratories, canals, technical shops, researchers, information brokers.

Zonot Seven is a huge aquatic sinkhole and the only zonot in city proper. Zameck is Simic guildhall and Speakers' meeting place. The Blistercoils are important Izzet power machinery. Prism University is formally neutral but politically contested through recruitment and sponsorship. Ismeri Library is public-facing and associated with hidden Dimir information work.

Research culture makes unfinished work socially sensitive. Theft is both material loss and humiliation.

If ignored: information spreads unevenly. A thief may publish first, a broker may sell, or a laboratory may test before authorities even know the work exists.
