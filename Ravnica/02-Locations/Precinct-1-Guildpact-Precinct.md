# Precinct One - Guildpact Precinct

Feel: clean stone, monuments, manicured green space, diplomats, officials, wealthy visitors, expensive hospitality, discrete guards.

Tenth District Plaza is important neutral ground for recreation, commerce, diplomacy, and public visibility.

The Chamber of the Guildpact receives petitions and interguild disputes. In the Jace era, functionaries continue processing cases during his absences, creating constant uncertainty over delegated authority.

Plaza East holds government and diplomatic residences. Plaza West has luxury restaurants, clubs, and elite housing with strong Orzhov influence. Plaza South / Downside is denser, commercial, and more permanently residential.

Ready NPCs: junior diplomat; Selesnyan groundskeeper; Azorius security officer; Orzhov host; guildless delivery driver; Dimir observer posing as artist.

If ignored: problems here are delayed, bought, proceduralized, or reframed. The key danger is who controls the official record.
