# Beyond the Tenth District

The Tenth is the detailed hub, not the whole plane.

Other core districts remain city-scale and internally diverse even where published detail is sparse.

Named broader regions include rubblebelts, Irbitov mausoleum district, Jezeru lake district, Utvara reclamation zone, Agyrem / Ghost Quarter, and numerous other districts and quarters.

When inventing a new district, choose its dominant civic function, competing guilds, older buried layer, connection route to the core, three neighborhoods of different class, what it exports, and what it depends on.
