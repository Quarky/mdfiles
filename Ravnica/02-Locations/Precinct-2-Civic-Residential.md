# Precinct Two - Civic Residential

Feel: orderly streets, pale stone houses, professionals, schools, offices, airship traffic, New Prahv.

New Prahv has three towers: Sova judiciary, Jelenn legislature, Lyev enforcement. Lower spaces are monumental; upper floors become ordinary administration: records, queues, assignments, career rivalry.

Whitestone is orderly residential space with veterans and established households. Griffin Heights looks respectable but carries a reputation for corruption. Augustin Station is a major airship/freight/passenger node.

Main pressures: rackets hidden by respectability, records becoming official truth, private debt intersecting public law, security and surveillance.

If ignored: respectable capture. A racket normalizes, a record hardens into truth, or a claim proceeds because nobody objected correctly.
