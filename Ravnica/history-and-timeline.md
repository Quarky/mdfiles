# History & Timeline
More than ten thousand years before the modern setting, devastating conflict ended in the magical **Guildpact**, signed by the paruns and assigning civic functions to ten guilds.

At the Decamillennial the original magical Guildpact was broken, though the guild system endured. During the Return to Ravnica era the Implicit Maze culminated in **Jace Beleren** becoming the Living Guildpact.

In the Guilds of Ravnica / Ravnica Allegiance period, Nicol Bolas's plans destabilized several guilds. During the **War of the Spark**, Bolas drew Planeswalkers to Ravnica, the Immortal Sun prevented their escape, and the Eternals invaded through the Planar Bridge.

## MTGFoundry timeline rule
The Immortal Sun lock is timeline-dependent, not an intrinsic permanent feature of Ravnica. Leadership and political-status notes should always be tagged by era.
