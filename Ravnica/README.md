# Ravnica Living World Setting Bible

This vault is a GM operating encyclopedia, not a short lore primer. It is built so you can stop anywhere in Ravnica, describe the physical place, improvise a believable NPC from any faction, understand what the factions want, and know what happens if the player characters do nothing.

## Default playable era

The main body uses the period after Jace Beleren becomes the Living Guildpact through the Implicit Maze and before the War of the Spark becomes the active wartime state.

For day-to-day play, the leadership baseline follows Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica: Isperia, Aurelia, Lazav, Jarad, Borborygmos, Niv-Mizzet, the Obzedat, Rakdos, Trostani, and Prime Speaker Zegana.

Jace's frequent absence is a core political fact. The Chamber of the Guildpact still receives petitions, but officials and guild leaders often have to improvise while the one magical arbiter is away.

## Standard for every major location

Every detailed location answers:
1. what it looks, sounds, and smells like;
2. what is above and below;
3. what civic work happens there;
4. who claims authority;
5. who has practical control;
6. who lives and works there;
7. what changes by time of day;
8. what current pressures exist;
9. what happens if nobody intervenes;
10. what changes in alternate eras.

## Standard for every faction NPC

Every guild dossier supports immediate improvisation by giving roles, ranks, internal politics, sincere beliefs, cross-guild tensions, escalation behavior, bargaining positions, and sample dialogue.

## Canon labels

CANON: directly grounded in published Ravnica material.  
ERA CANON: valid only in a specified period.  
CAMPAIGN: established by MTGFoundry material such as The Glass Run.  
SYNTHESIS: practical GM expansion where published detail is sparse.  
ORIGINAL FICTION: illustrative scenes written for this guide.
