## Canon policy
This guide distinguishes **canon/source-grounded material** from **campaign synthesis** and **original fiction**. It does not reproduce copyrighted books or stories. Published fiction is indexed and summarized so the GM can use it as a model for voice, social behavior, faction conflict, and scene construction.

## Campaign use
Ravnica is not merely a city with ten adventuring clubs. The guilds are civic institutions, cultures, employers, ideologies, criminal networks, religions, laboratories, armies, and extended communities. Most scenes should therefore involve overlapping jurisdiction: an Izzet pipe under an Azorius street, maintained by guildless labor, financed by Orzhov credit, feeding a Simic facility while Dimir learns who signed the work order.

The default campaign lens is the Tenth District, with timeline-sensitive notes where leadership changes matter.
