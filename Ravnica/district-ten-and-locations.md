# District Ten & Adventure Geography
The **Tenth District** is the principal D&D campaign area in *Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica*. It contains six precincts, major trade ways and extensive spaces beneath the streets.

Treat precincts as differing urban textures rather than hard borders. Precinct One is strongly governmental and monumental; other precincts provide commerce, dense neighborhood life, mixed institutions, neglected infrastructure and transitions toward rubblebelts.

The undercity is not one sewer dungeon: it includes buried architecture, caverns, Golgari communities, farms, forgotten infrastructure, monsters and routes through older layers of Ravnica.

## Campaign anchor
**The Glass Run** uses an Izzet workshop near Augustin Station, spatial/teleportation research, gravitational-inversion work and the Smelting Quarter. This is a useful model: begin with a credible civic/research function, then let other factions contest it.
