# Interaction Vignettes

These are **original campaign fiction**, not canon stories. They are designed as examples of how guild philosophies can collide in ordinary scenes.

## 1. The Permit and the Fire
The Azorius arrester arrived while the laboratory roof was still burning.

"Your suppression cistern is unlicensed," she told the Izzet chemister.

The chemister stared at the orange light reflected in her spectacles. "It is also currently suppressing a fire."

"That does not make it licensed."

A Boros wojek shoved past them carrying a coughing goblin. "Argue after everyone is out."

The arrester's jaw tightened. She stepped aside, then quietly began writing a temporary emergency variance on the back of the citation.

**Use:** Nobody abandons their guild philosophy. The Azorius solves the contradiction through procedure; Boros establishes the immediate moral priority; Izzet cares that the machine works.

## 2. Bread from Below
A Selesnyan initiate inspected the gray mushroom cakes stacked in the Golgari stall.

"You could grow something beautiful here," she said.

The kraul vendor clicked his mandibles. "I grew something edible here. Beauty does not feed six blocks."

An Orzhov collector behind her marked another line in a ledger. "Fortunately, hunger is financeable."

Both turned on him at once.

For one excellent second, the Conclave and the Swarm were in perfect harmony.

**Use:** Shared values can appear through a shared enemy without erasing deep differences.

## 3. Noise Complaint
The Rakdos stage manager held up the Azorius closure order and read it to the crowd as dramatic monologue. Every prohibition earned applause.

The lawmage waited until the laughter stopped. "You understand that performing the injunction does not satisfy it."

"No," said the stage manager. "But now three hundred witnesses understand precisely what you prohibited."

From a balcony across the street, a Dimir courier folded away a copy. Information had become more valuable than the show.

**Use:** Rakdos can be socially perceptive, not merely violent; Azorius power depends on legibility; Dimir profits when conflict creates information.

## 4. The Clinic
The Simic physician offered the wounded Gruul scout a graft that would let the damaged lung tolerate smoke.

"You mean make me into one of your projects."

"I mean keep you alive."

A Selesnyan healer placed a hand on the scout's shoulder. "Survival can be a choice without becoming surrender."

The scout looked from cultivated serenity to engineered gills in a glass tank. "You city people have very complicated ways to say yes."

**Use:** Simic adaptation, Gruul bodily autonomy, and Selesnyan mediation can all be sympathetic.

## 5. A Debt That Salutes
The Boros veteran had paid the principal years ago. The Orzhov advokist agreed.

"Then why is there still a lien?"

"Because gratitude accrued separately."

The veteran's hand went to his sword.

The advokist smiled. "Threatening me would add a security surcharge."

A voice from the empty chair beside them whispered, "Page nineteen."

Neither had seen the Dimir agent enter. The veteran turned the contract to page nineteen and found the clause the Syndicate hoped he would never read.

**Use:** Dimir assistance should create a new question: why did the agent want this debt broken?

## 6. The Broken Street
An Izzet crew wanted to replace the conduit. A Gruul raiding party wanted the conduit gone. The residents wanted hot water.

The solution came from the guildless superintendent: reroute the pipe through an abandoned foundation and leave the old trench open as a planted drainage strip.

The Izzet engineer called it inefficient. The Gruul shaman called it insufficient. The residents called it Tuesday.

**Use:** Guildless Ravnicans are not passive scenery. They are often the people most practiced at living between ideologies.

## Canon models behind the tone
Public War of the Spark stories show hostile guilds negotiating and cooperating under existential pressure, while Wizards' guild guides emphasize that every guild contains recognizable civic motives as well as excesses. The vignettes above borrow those structural lessons, not story text.
