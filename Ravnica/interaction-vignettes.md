# Interaction Vignettes
> [!note]
> These are **original campaign fiction**, not canon stories.

## The Permit and the Fire
The Azorius arrester arrived while the laboratory roof was still burning.

"Your suppression cistern is unlicensed," she told the Izzet chemister.

"It is also currently suppressing a fire."

A Boros wojek shoved past carrying a coughing goblin. "Argue after everyone is out."

The arrester stepped aside, then began writing a temporary emergency variance on the back of the citation.

## Bread from Below
A Selesnyan initiate inspected the gray mushroom cakes at a Golgari stall. "You could grow something beautiful here."

The kraul vendor clicked his mandibles. "I grew something edible. Beauty does not feed six blocks."

An Orzhov collector behind her said, "Fortunately, hunger is financeable."

Both turned on him at once.

## Noise Complaint
A Rakdos stage manager read an Azorius closure order aloud as dramatic monologue. Every prohibition earned applause.

"You understand that performing the injunction does not satisfy it," the lawmage said.

"No. But now three hundred witnesses know exactly what you prohibited."

Across the street, a Dimir courier quietly folded away a copy.

## The Clinic
A Simic physician offered a wounded Gruul scout a graft that would let a damaged lung tolerate smoke.

"You mean make me one of your projects."

"I mean keep you alive."

A Selesnyan healer answered, "Survival can be a choice without becoming surrender."

## The Broken Street
An Izzet crew wanted to replace a conduit. A Gruul band wanted it gone. Residents wanted hot water.

A guildless superintendent rerouted the pipe through an abandoned foundation and left the old trench as planted drainage.

The Izzet called it inefficient. The Gruul called it insufficient. The residents called it Tuesday.
