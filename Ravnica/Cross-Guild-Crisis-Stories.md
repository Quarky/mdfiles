# Cross Guild Crisis Stories

ORIGINAL FICTION - non-canon examples.

## The Clinic During Quarantine
**Guilds/themes:** Simic / Azorius / Boros

The Azorius order closed the clinic at noon. At twelve-ten the first feverish child arrived. At twelve-twelve the Simic physician broke the seal. At twelve-fourteen Boros entered. The father said, “Arrest whoever you like after she can breathe.” The commander looked at the order, physician, and child. “Medic. Inside.” The legal problem began fifteen minutes later, which everyone considered an improvement.

GM lesson: each participant should pursue a recognizable institutional or personal need; Ravnica emerges when those needs collide without erasing ordinary life.

## The Root and the Pipe
**Guilds/themes:** Selesnya / Izzet / Azorius

The conduit had to go through the root system. The voda said no. The Izzet director said the district would lose heat. The Azorius surveyor said the easement had been granted before the root existed. The voda touched the living wall. “So your law remembers a street that is gone, but not a tree that is here?” For once, the Izzet director waited quietly.

## Raid Alarm
**Guilds/themes:** Gruul / Boros / civilians

The warning bells meant Gruul. The bakery owner locked the shutters. Her husband, raised in the rubblebelt, opened the back door. “What are you doing?” “Checking which clan.” “They are attacking!” “Maybe. Or maybe the new Boros wall cut their water route again.” She stared. He shrugged. “You married local knowledge.”

## The Stolen Formula
**Guilds/themes:** Izzet / Dimir / Prism University

The Dimir librarian knew because both students borrowed the same obscure volume within six minutes. The Izzet recruiter knew none of that. He simply wanted whichever student lied more convincingly.

## Neutral Ground
**Guilds/themes:** Civic culture

Two men drew knives in Tenth District Plaza. Three Azorius arresters, a Boros veteran, a Selesnyan groundskeeper, and an Orzhov restaurant manager all shouted at once. The men froze, not because they feared arrest, but because everybody in the plaza looked at them with the same expression: not here. Some rules survive because everybody remembers what happens without them.
