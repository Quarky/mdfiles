# Conversion Rules Reference

**Authority:** Planeswalker5e Rules GameSystem Checkpoint v9, dated 2026-10-02; newer explicit instructions or newer verified source code supersede it.

## Creature design
Use creature concept, types/subtypes, color identity, art and lore to select attack form and damage, while matching target DPR for its band. MV establishes tier, not standalone raw damage.

## Actions and tap
A creature attack uses the Attack action and normally taps unless Vigilance or specific text says otherwise. Tap-cost creature abilities normally use an Action and tap. Tapping does not eliminate movement, Bonus Action or Reaction. Mana-source tapping does not consume the Planeswalker's Action. Untapping does not grant another D&D Action.

## Timing
MTG “this turn” / “until end of turn” approximates one D&D round equivalent. Use START, DURING, END phases; anchor creature-targeted effects to that creature's next relevant phase when practical.

## Card identity
One canonical identity follows each card through Library, Hand, Stack, Battlefield, Graveyard, Exile and Adventure Exile. Native Foundry actors/items/effects implement D&D behavior without replacing that card identity.

## Recovery
Opening hand 7; no automatic per-turn draw. Two hours of long-rest meditation: recollect up to 2 graveyard cards into library, or draw 1. Leyline Communion recovers half the graveyard rounded down; Planar Renewal recovers all. Exile unaffected.
