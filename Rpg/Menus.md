# ⚠️ **Common DM Mistakes With Tavern Food**

### **1. Forgetting that food prices scale with settlement size**

- Remote village: +20–50%
    
- Big city: –10–20%
    
- Festival or crisis: +100%
    

### **2. Treating all inns as equal**

A “modest” inn in a wealthy city is still modest — not fancy.

### **3. Ignoring supply limitations**

Not every inn has:

- fish
    
- exotic spices
    
- fresh fruit
    
- wine
    

### **4. Forgetting that meals can be negotiated**

Players love:

- bartering
    
- performing for food
    
- doing chores for a free meal
    

### **5. Overcharging for ale**

Ale is **cheap** in 5e. A pint of house ale is **4 cp**, not 1 sp.

# ⚠️ **Common DM Mistakes With Drinks in D&D**

### **1. Overpricing alcohol**

RAW ale is **4 cp**, not 1 sp. Most drinks are cheap unless the inn is wealthy.

### **2. Forgetting regional availability**

- Wine is rare in cold climates
    
- Cider is common in orchard regions
    
- Rum appears near coasts
    
- Mead is common where grain is scarce
    

### **3. Ignoring alcohol strength**

Ales are weak. Spirits are strong. Mead varies wildly.

### **4. Forgetting tavern personality**

A tavern with no signature drink feels generic.

### **5. Not letting drinks matter**

Alcohol can influence:

- social checks
    
- bar fights
    
- roleplay
    
- rumors
    
- downtime scenes
