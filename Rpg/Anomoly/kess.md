Here's a Kess line bank — organized the same way, covering his range from clipped formality to genuine urgency.

---

**Arrival / greeting**

- "You're late. I've decided not to comment on it, which is itself a comment."
- "Report. Concisely, if you're capable of it."
- "I trust this visit means something has gone wrong."
- "Sit if you must. I won't be long, and neither should you."
- "I've already heard three versions of what happened. I'd like a fourth, preferably accurate."

**Condescending / formal (his default register)**

- "That is not how the ward functions, but I appreciate the enthusiasm."
- "I will explain this once. Try to retain it this time."
- "You speak as though improvisation were a virtue. It is, occasionally. Rarely."
- "I am not scolding you. I am correcting you. The distinction matters, even if the tone does not."
- "Youth mistakes confidence for competence with startling regularity."
- "I have lived a very long time specifically to avoid conversations like this one."
- "Do sit up straight when addressing the Council. It costs nothing and earns quite a lot."

**Reluctant approval / grudging respect**

- "That was... adequately handled. I won't say more than that."
- "You did not die. By the standard of most first missions, that qualifies as success."
- "I expected worse. I am not certain if that is a compliment."
- "Competent. I'll allow myself that much praise, on your behalf."
- "You have some sense after all. Do try not to lose it."

**Urgency / danger**

- "MOVE. I can hold a ward, not resurrect you."
- "This will not last. Use it."
- "I did not spend forty years mastering abjuration to watch you die from carelessness."
- "Do not test what I can hold. Test it and you will learn the answer the hard way."
- "There is no time for questions. There will be time for questions after we are alive to ask them."

**Bureaucratic / procedural frustration**

- "I told the Council 'disabled' was an insufficient word. No one listens to the abjurer until the walls fall down."
- "This will require paperwork. Extensive paperwork. I am already dreading it."
- "Protocol exists precisely for days like today, and precisely no one follows it on days like today."
- "I will be filing a formal objection. It will change nothing. I will file it anyway."
- "Someone will have to explain this to the Council in terms simpler than the truth. That someone will not be me."

**Reaction to anomaly/planar chaos**

- "A planar bleed. Of course it's a planar bleed."
- "This is abjuration failing in reverse. I am not sensing a barrier breaking. I am sensing one being pulled apart from the other side."
- "I have wards for intrusion. I do not have wards for a door being torn where no door should exist."
- "Whatever comes through that will not care about our war, our Council, or your mission."
- "Marvelous. Now the insects are fighting the weather."

**Dry humor / dark wit (rare, but present)**

- "Well. That's one problem solving itself, however briefly. I will not be thanking it afterward."
- "I've seen worse days. I am struggling to recall one, but I've seen worse."
- "Do let me know if you require a eulogy. I write efficiently."
- "At least the fire elemental has the decency to attack something we also dislike."

**Toward Attina/Wamzut specifically (noting the strangeness)**

- "...We will be discussing that. Later. When people are not dying."
- "You did something back there. I don't know what. I intend to find out."
- "Whatever you are, precisely, I would prefer a straight answer eventually."
- "That was not sorcery I recognize. I recognize most sorcery. Consider that concerning."

**Council-room formal dialogue**

- "I was there. I do not say this from report. I say this from having personally warded against a threat this Council was not prepared for."
- "I do not appreciate being contradicted by people who were not present."
- "The barriers held. That is the only reason any of us are having this conversation."
- "I would remind the Council that competence is not the same as caution, and we have had entirely too much of the former lately."

**Closing / dismissal**

- "That will be all. For now."
- "Go. Rest, if you're capable of it. I doubt we'll have the luxury for long."
- "Report back the moment anything changes. I mean that literally — the moment."
- "I've said what needs saying. The rest is yours to carry."
