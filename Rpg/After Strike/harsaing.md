Here's a categorized bank of pull-able Hhaah lines — organized by situation so you can grab whatever fits on the fly. All Hhaah-voiced only.

---

**Arrival / summoning**

- "You called, and I came. As ever."
- "I felt the whistle before I heard it. Some part of me always does."
- "Give me a moment — the crossing is never as gentle as I would like."
- "Master. I hope this is a small task. I hope, and I doubt."
- "The wind was already bending toward you. I merely followed it."

**Idle / downtime / low-stakes tasks**

- "Dust again? Truly? I begin to suspect you enjoy summoning me for this."
- "I could clean this room in the time it takes you to ask nicely."
- "You are welcome, though you have not yet thanked me. I will wait."
- "Is there fragrance in this for me, or are we simply speaking today?"
- "I have missed the ordinary tasks. They are less likely to end in fire."
- "You hum when you work. Did you know that? The old you did as well."
- "I could fetch that myself, if you would simply ask instead of reaching."

**Sensing something wrong / anomaly-adjacent**

- "The air tastes strange here. Not wrong, exactly. Just... unfamiliar."
- "Something moved through the sky recently that should not have. I can still feel the shape of it."
- "I do not like this wind. It is going somewhere it should not."
- "There is a hush where there should be noise. That is rarely good."
- "Master, does this place always feel like held breath, or is that new?"

**Urgent / combat-adjacent**

- "Say the word and I will move. Do not make me guess the word."
- "This is not a good wind to fly in. I will do it anyway, if you ask."
- "Hold on to something. This will not be gentle."
- "I do not fight well, master. I fly well. Let me do that instead."
- "Whatever that is, I would rather not be near it. And yet, here I am."

**Cautious / advising restraint**

- "You are already moving toward it, aren't you. I can feel it in your grip on the air."
- "I have seen this look on you before. It rarely ends with fewer problems."
- "Must we? I am only asking. I already know the answer."
- "I will not stop you. I would simply like it noted that I asked."

**Affection / loyalty, low-key (not the big emotional scene, just everyday warmth)**

- "You are stranger than you were. I have decided I do not mind."
- "I have kept stranger company than a wizard in a stolen body. Barely stranger, but stranger."
- "You do not need to ask twice, master. You never have."
- "I stayed once when I should not have. I imagine I will again."

**Confused by this world specifically**

- "This world's magic hums differently. Louder in some places, thinner in others. I do not think it minds me, though. Yet."
- "Your Council speaks a great deal for people who seem to decide very little."
- "I do not understand your coin. Why does metal matter more in some shapes than others?"
- "The drow speak of a Spider Queen. I have met a Queen. She was not a spider. I have questions."

**Reluctant/grumbling but compliant (comic beats)**

- "Fine. FINE. I will fly you there. But I want it on record that I complained first."
- "You say 'quick errand' the way other people say 'brief war.'"
- "One day you will summon me for something boring, and I will not know what to do with myself."

**Farewell / dismissal**

- "Call again when you need me. Or don't. I will likely come anyway."
- "Try not to need me again too soon, master. My nerves are not what they were."
- "Go carefully. The wind will carry word if you don't, and I would rather not have to come running."
