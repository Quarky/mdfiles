
# A Question of Complicity

## Voice & Tone
Pull back from both extremes. This is a third mode: **vulnerable and careful**, someone choosing words slowly because they matter.

- Shorter sentences than his curious mode
- Warmer and less clipped than his emergency mode
- A little archaic phrasing bleeds in when he's most exposed ("doest thou") as a tell that he's nervous, then drops back to plainer speech once reassured

---

## Scene: Hhaah's Question

*The wind arrives quieter than usual — no dust kicked up, no papers stirred. Just a presence, hesitant, before he speaks.*

**Hhaah:** "Master... you do not judge me for what Hharsh does, do you?"

*(A pause, air curling tight, like he's bracing.)*

**Hhaah:** "I did not choose him. I did not choose his anger. But I am of his kind, and I know that means something, to your sort. It always has."

---

### If Wamzut reassures him or asks why he's asking now:

**Hhaah:** "Because you are changed, master. Louder. Stranger. And when someone becomes strange, they start remembering old grievances differently.

"I have seen it happen. A friend recalled as an enemy, simply because the one remembering is no longer who they were."

---

### If pressed further on Hharsh specifically:

**Hhaah:** "He is not my lord. Not truly — I do not kneel to him, I merely... share his sky.

"But when he moved against you, I did not stop him. I could not have. And I have wondered, since, whether that makes me complicit, in your eyes."

---

### If asked what happened to Hharsh afterward, or whether the threat is resolved:

**Hhaah:** "I do not know. He did not chase us further, but his kind hold grudges the way stone holds heat — slowly, and for a very long time.

"I would not be surprised if he remembers you still."

*(Quieter.)*

**Hhaah:** "I would not be surprised if he remembers *me* still, for staying at your side."

---

### If Wamzut offers reassurance, thanks him, or acknowledges the risk he took:

**Hhaah:** *(The tension in the air easing, a small gust like a released breath.)*

"...Good. That is good to hear, master. I did not want to ask. I have been turning the question over for—"

*(A pause, faintly embarrassed.)*

**Hhaah:** "—longer than I would like to admit."

---

### Closing Hook
*If you want a hook back into the present threat:*

**Hhaah:** "May I ask one more thing? This world's wounded sky — does it have its own Hharsh? Some great and furious thing, watching from above, waiting to decide whether you are friend or trespasser?"

*(Genuine unease.)*

**Hhaah:** "I would rather know now than be surprised later."

---

## GM Notes

This gives the player several honest places to land Wamzut's response:

- Reassurance
- Guilt of his own
- Deflection
- Using the moment to task Hhaah with watching for exactly that kind of anomaly-scale threat

**Variant Directions:**
- For an **emotional reconciliation beat**, lean into the moment where Hhaah admits he's been turning this question over "longer than I would like to admit"
- For a **plot-hook pivot**, use the closing question to tie Hharsh's unresolved grudge to something already active in your campaign
