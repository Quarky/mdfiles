**Othira:** "Old. Deliberate. Not the clumsy grasping of some hedge-caster overreaching — this was _practiced_. Whoever shaped it has done this before, many times, to many things, and rarely needed to try twice." _(a pause, considering how much to say)_ "It did not come from anywhere close, Loremaster. Whatever reached for me crossed a very great distance to do it."

**Iriandel:** "Can you tell me the distance? Or the direction?"

**Othira:** _(slowly, as though checking her own answer before offering it)_ "Not a direction, no — planes don't lie in directions the way roads do. But I can tell you it did not come from the Shadowfell, and it did not come from any of the Inner Planes we've been dealing with since the stabilizer." _(a beat)_ "It felt... further. Older. The kind of place that doesn't often bother crossing over, because it rarely needs to."

_If pressed on whether it was a god:_

**Iriandel:** "That's rather more than 'far away,' Archivist. Are you telling me a god reached for you this morning?"

**Othira:** _(a careful pause — not evasive exactly, but weighing the word before accepting it)_ "I'm telling you something that has been worshipped as one, somewhere, by someone. I don't know if that's the same claim." _(a faint, dry note)_ "Gods and things merely powerful enough to be mistaken for gods rarely announce which they are before they attack you."

_If asked how she can tell any of this, and whether that itself is worth questioning:_

**Iriandel:** _(quiet, careful, watching her closely)_ "Most scholars couldn't have told me any of that, from a single failed attempt. Most scholars would still be shaking." _(not accusatory — genuinely curious)_ "You're very calm for someone who was just tested by something that old."

**Othira:** _(meeting her gaze evenly, giving nothing away and everything away at once)_ "I've had a very long time to get used to being tested, Loremaster." _(a small, unreadable smile)_ "You'll forgive me if I don't elaborate further today."

_Closing beat, if you want to leave the scene on unresolved tension:_

**Iriandel:** _(to herself, as much as to anyone, once Othira has moved off)_ "...She answered every question except the one I actually asked." _(a pause, veil flickering with something unreadable)_ "I don't think that was an accident."
