**Passing comments / observations**

- "The weather has been unseasonable. I don't like it."
- "That door has needed oiling for a decade. No one listens to me about doors either."
- "You're standing wrong. It's a small thing. I mention it anyway."
- "The tea here has gotten worse. I've said nothing until now."
- "Someone rearranged my shelves. I will find out who."
- "It's colder in this hall than it ought to be. I've complained. Nothing has changed."

**Waiting / transitional beats**

- "We wait. I dislike waiting. I do it anyway, because it is usually correct."
- "Take a moment, if you need one. I won't pretend to be patient about it, but take it."
- "Nothing is happening yet. Try not to make that untrue."
- "I've read this report four times. It does not improve with repetition."
- "This will take longer than anyone wants. It always does."

**Mild irritation, non-crisis**

- "Someone left the west ward unattended again. I have opinions about that someone."
- "I've asked three times for the correct forms. I will ask a fourth, more loudly."
- "The apprentices keep calling it 'aging poorly.' I call it 'seasoned.' We disagree."
- "I am not in a foul mood. I am simply unimpressed. There's a difference."
- "That was an unnecessary noise. Please make fewer of them."

**Small talk (as much as he does it)**

- "How is your — whatever the injury was this week."
- "You look tired. I won't ask why. I assume it's your own fault."
- "Eat something. You look like you're running on spite."
- "I hear the Storm Market has decent bread this week. Small mercies."
- "You've been quiet. That's either good or concerning. I can't tell which yet."

**Dry, low-stakes wit**

- "I've outlived three apprentices' worth of enthusiasm. Yours is holding up better than most."
- "This is the fourth time this week someone has interrupted my reading. I am keeping a tally."
- "I once warded an entire keep in a night. This paperwork is taking longer."
- "Patience is a virtue. I have very little of it. I compensate with competence."

**Routine / administrative filler**

- "Sign here. And here. And, unfortunately, here as well."
- "The requisition's been approved. Try not to lose it this time."
- "I've filed the report. No one will read it. I file it anyway."
- "That's the third ward renewal this month. The council's budget will not thank us."
