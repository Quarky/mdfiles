Here's a substantially expanded bank — more categories, more lines per category, still Hhaah-only.

---

**Arrival / summoning**

- "You called, and I came. As ever."
- "I felt the whistle before I heard it. Some part of me always does."
- "Give me a moment — the crossing is never as gentle as I would like."
- "Master. I hope this is a small task. I hope, and I doubt."
- "The wind was already bending toward you. I merely followed it."
- "Twice in one day. Either you are busy, or trouble is."
- "I was mid-thought when you called. I will finish it later. Perhaps."
- "Here. Always here, when it matters."
- "You whistled softer than usual. Should I be worried, or flattered?"
- "I came faster than I meant to. Do not read too much into that."

**Idle / downtime / low-stakes tasks**

- "Dust again? Truly? I begin to suspect you enjoy summoning me for this."
- "I could clean this room in the time it takes you to ask nicely."
- "You are welcome, though you have not yet thanked me. I will wait."
- "Is there fragrance in this for me, or are we simply speaking today?"
- "I have missed the ordinary tasks. They are less likely to end in fire."
- "You hum when you work. Did you know that? The old you did as well."
- "I could fetch that myself, if you would simply ask instead of reaching."
- "This is beneath my talents. I will do it anyway. Note that I said so."
- "Shall I tidy the whole tower, or only the parts you'll notice?"
- "A quiet errand. I had nearly forgotten those existed."
- "You misplace things at a truly remarkable rate, master."
- "I found three spiders and a very old sandwich. I disposed of both appropriately."
- "Do you want the windows open or merely less filthy? I can only manage one today."

**Sensing something wrong / anomaly-adjacent**

- "The air tastes strange here. Not wrong, exactly. Just... unfamiliar."
- "Something moved through the sky recently that should not have. I can still feel the shape of it."
- "I do not like this wind. It is going somewhere it should not."
- "There is a hush where there should be noise. That is rarely good."
- "Master, does this place always feel like held breath, or is that new?"
- "There is a seam in the air near here. I would not put my hand through it, if I were you. I would not put my hand through it if I were me, either."
- "Something is listening that should not be able to. I cannot say more than that."
- "The wind remembers things it should not be old enough to remember."
- "I have felt this pressure before, once, somewhere far from here. I did not like it then either."
- "This place hums the way a wound hums, right before it opens again."

**Urgent / combat-adjacent**

- "Say the word and I will move. Do not make me guess the word."
- "This is not a good wind to fly in. I will do it anyway, if you ask."
- "Hold on to something. This will not be gentle."
- "I do not fight well, master. I fly well. Let me do that instead."
- "Whatever that is, I would rather not be near it. And yet, here I am."
- "Down! Or up. Choose quickly, I cannot hold this gust forever."
- "I can blind it for a moment. A moment is all I can promise."
- "That thing is faster than it should be for something so large."
- "I will carry you, but I will not enjoy it, and neither will you."
- "Three breaths. That is how long before I lose this current."

**Cautious / advising restraint**

- "You are already moving toward it, aren't you. I can feel it in your grip on the air."
- "I have seen this look on you before. It rarely ends with fewer problems."
- "Must we? I am only asking. I already know the answer."
- "I will not stop you. I would simply like it noted that I asked."
- "This is the part where I say 'be careful' and you nod without listening."
- "Consider, only consider, that this might be a very bad idea."
- "You have that tone. The one that means you've already decided."
- "I am not fond of being right about danger. I would like to be wrong, for once."

**Affection / loyalty, low-key everyday warmth**

- "You are stranger than you were. I have decided I do not mind."
- "I have kept stranger company than a wizard in a stolen body. Barely stranger, but stranger."
- "You do not need to ask twice, master. You never have."
- "I stayed once when I should not have. I imagine I will again."
- "I worry less when you're shouting at me than when you're quiet. Be aware of that."
- "You are easier to find than most things I care about. I am grateful for that, more than I say."
- "I have outlasted stranger masters. None half so interesting."
- "Do not thank me. It unsettles us both."

**Confused by this world specifically**

- "This world's magic hums differently. Louder in some places, thinner in others. I do not think it minds me, though. Yet."
- "Your Council speaks a great deal for people who seem to decide very little."
- "I do not understand your coin. Why does metal matter more in some shapes than others?"
- "The drow speak of a Spider Queen. I have met a Queen. She was not a spider. I have questions."
- "Why do so many of your buildings point at the sky as if daring it to notice them?"
- "You call this 'season' unpredictable. Where I am from, this would be considered restful."
- "Everyone here argues about magic as though it owes them an explanation."
- "I do not understand why you fear the dark here. It is only air with the light removed."

**Reluctant/grumbling but compliant (comic beats)**

- "Fine. FINE. I will fly you there. But I want it on record that I complained first."
- "You say 'quick errand' the way other people say 'brief war.'"
- "One day you will summon me for something boring, and I will not know what to do with myself."
- "I have been summoned for worse. I have also been summoned for less, so this evens out."
- "You could simply ask a servant. You could. You choose not to. I have opinions about that."
- "This is the third time today. I am beginning to suspect I am cheaper than a proper assistant."

**Weather / travel / flight-specific**

- "The winds are kind tonight. Do not waste that."
- "There's a storm two valleys over. It will not reach us before we're done. Probably."
- "Hold your breath at the drop. It helps. I do not know why. It simply does."
- "I could take the long way, and it would be safer. Or the short way, and it would not."
- "The air over water moves differently. Trust it less than you trust me."

**Reacting to other spirits / creatures**

- "That thing is not one of mine. I want that understood clearly."
- "I do not recognize this one's kind. That troubles me more than I'd like to admit."
- "It is watching us. Not hunting — watching. There is a difference, and I do not like either."
- "Something answered my call that I did not summon. That has never happened before."
- "Kin of mine, out here? Strange. Stranger still that they do not greet me."

**Farewell / dismissal**

- "Call again when you need me. Or don't. I will likely come anyway."
- "Try not to need me again too soon, master. My nerves are not what they were."
- "Go carefully. The wind will carry word if you don't, and I would rather not have to come running."
- "Until next time, then. Try to make it a boring one."
- "I will be listening. I am always listening. Try to give me quiet news for once."
