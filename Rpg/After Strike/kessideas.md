**Kess (to the Council):** "The device was not merely disabled. Disabling implies a controlled cessation. What happened was a rupture — a planar bleed, uncontained, admitting creatures from at least four elemental domains and, I suspect, worse things we did not have time to identify." _(a pause, choosing the next words carefully)_ "I was there. I do not say this from report. I say this from having personally warded against a Primordial-tier entity that took an interest in the battlefield."
