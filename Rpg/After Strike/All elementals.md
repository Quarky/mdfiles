
# Planar Bleed-Through: Elemental Encounters

## Overview
This encounter framework provides three tiers of elemental entities that might spill through a planar rupture. They are **not** a coordinated army — they are chaotic, disoriented, and driven by their own natures. Use them as environmental hazards, wildcards, or escalating threats depending on the moment.

---

## Tier 1: Minor Spirits
*Numerous, chaotic, low threat individually. Most arrive in the first few rounds and scatter quickly.*

| Type           | AC  | HP      | Speed         | Attack          | Special Notes                                    |
|----------------|-----|---------|---------------|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| **Air Wisp**   | 12  | 9 (2d8) | fly 60 ft     | 1d4 force (gust)| Invisible unless in dusty/windy area; cannot be grappled. |
| **Ember Mote** | 11  | 7 (2d6) | fly 30 ft     | 1d6 fire        | Ignites flammable objects on hit.                |
| **Mud Sprite** | 13  | 10 (3d6)| swim 30, walk 10 | 1d4 bludgeoning | Merges into wet terrain; +5 stealth there.      |
| **Pebble Wight**| 15 | 12 (3d8)| walk 20, burrow 10 | 1d6 bludgeoning | Resistant to non‑magical piercing damage.       |

**Behavior (roll or pick per spirit, per round):**
- **40%** — Investigate the nearest large magical signature (likely the hybrid).
- **30%** — Latch onto a nearby spellcaster mid‑cast (drawn to active magic).
- **30%** — Flee/scatter, occasionally colliding with combatants incidentally.

**Voicing:** Feral, wordless — describe their behavior rather than giving them lines.

---

## Tier 2: Standard Elementals
*Real combat threats. Arrive around rounds 2–3 once the rupture "settles."*

| Type                | AC  | HP  | Speed               | Attack                 | Special Features                                    |
| ------------------- | --- | --- | ------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Air Elemental**   | 15  | 90  | fly 90 ft           | 2 × (2d8+4) slam       | Whirlwind (grapple+throw); cannot be knocked prone. |
| **Fire Elemental**  | 13  | 102 | walk/climb 50 ft    | 2 × (2d6+3) fire touch | Ignites on contact; extinguished by heavy water.    |
| **Water Elemental** | 14  | 114 | swim 90, walk 30 ft | 2 × (2d8+4) slam       | Engulf (drowning risk for Medium+ creatures).       |
| **Earth Elemental** | 17  | 126 | walk/burrow 30 ft   | 2 × (2d8+5) slam       | Moves freely through unworked earth/stone.          |

**Behavior by element:**
- **Fire** — Aggressive; drawn to the largest heat/magical signature. Likely attacks the hybrid independently — may be mistaken as a temporary ally by either side.
- **Air** — Erratic; engages flying creatures or those casting concentration spells. Disrupts concentration purely by proximity/turbulence.
- **Water** — Defensive/territorial. Anchors near any water source; otherwise flounders seeking moisture (functionally passive).
- **Earth** — Slow to engage, but relentless once it picks a target. Could wander into a swarm and grind through without tactical preference.

**Voicing:** Silent forces — describe their actions; they do not speak.

## Tier 3: Lord‑Tier Wildcard (Optional)
*One max, for a genuine "oh no" moment. Arrive only if you deliberately escalate.*

| Type                 | AC  | HP    | Speed (all 90+ ft) | Attack                  | Special Features                                    |
|----------------------|-----|-------|---------------------|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **Elemental Lord**<br>*(any element)* | 18  | 200+  | varies by element   | 3 × (3d10+6)            | Legendary actions (2/round); immune to own element; speaks Primordial. |

**Behavior:** A Lord *notices* things. It may:
- Take an interest in the hybrid (as a rival apex predator).
- Focus on Wamzut specifically (if tied to Hharsh's grudge).
- Simply try to dominate the battlefield by attacking the biggest threat.

**Voicing (archaic, grand, indifferent to mortal stakes):**
> *"Mortal quarrels. How tedious. I will finish this quickly so the sky may be quiet again."*
> *(if tied to Hharsh)* *"...You. The little witch's pet spoke true — you are still breathing. How disappointing."*
> *"Kneel, or be swept aside. I do not distinguish between the two options for long."*

## Practical Running Advice

**Arrival Flow (recommended):**
1. **Round 1–3** — Roll `1d4` minor spirits per round (use behavior table).
2. **Round 2–3** — Introduce 1–2 standard elementals.
3. **Reserve** — Keep the Lord‑tier in your pocket for a dramatic escalation.

**Live‑table Random Table** (optional) – a simple `d8` to determine which spirit/elemental arrives and what it does each round:

| d8  | Result                                              |
|-----|-----------------------------------------------------|
| 1   | Air Wisp — investigates nearest magic user         |
| 2   | Ember Mote — attacks closest flammable target      |
| 3   | Mud Sprite — merges into terrain, lies in ambush   |
| 4   | Pebble Wight — burrows toward the loudest noise    |
| 5   | Air Elemental — disrupts flyers/concentration      |
| 6   | Fire Elemental — charges the hybrid                |
| 7   | Water Elemental — claims a water source            |
| 8   | Earth Elemental — slowly grinds toward a target    |

> **GM Note:** The elementals are **not** allied. They will fight each other, the hybrid, the party, and bystanders indiscriminately. Use this to create a chaotic three‑way (or four‑way) battlefield where the party must decide whom to engage, whom to avoid, and whom to exploit.

---

## Story Integration Hooks

- **Tier 1** — These spirits are the "noise" that makes the battlefield feel alive and dangerous. Use them to interrupt spellcasting, ignite cover, or create difficult terrain.
- **Tier 2** — These are the real threats. Choose elementals that contrast with the hybrid's strengths (e.g., Fire vs. the hybrid's ice, Earth vs. its mobility) to force tactical adaptation.
- **Tier 3** — If you use a Lord, tie it to an existing thread: Hharsh's unresolved grudge, a prophecy about the wounded sky, or the hybrid's origin. This elevates the encounter from "random monster" to "plot‑relevant crisis."

---

*Use this framework flexibly — the rupture is unpredictable, and so should its inhabitants be.*
