# 🕷 Yochlol — Demon Servant of Lolth

## **Classification**
- **Type:** Fiend (Demon)  
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil  
- **Plane of Origin:** Abyss — the Demonweb Pits  
- **Primary Patron:** Lolth, the Spider Queen  
- **Typical Form:** Amorphous, shifting between mist, ooze, and humanoid female

---

## **Physical Description**
A Yochlol is a **shapeshifting demon** that serves as Lolth’s handmaid and enforcer.  
Its true form is a **viscous column of yellowish slime**, constantly rippling and exuding faint heat.  
When it chooses, it can assume:
- The form of a **beautiful Drow woman** (often eerily perfect)  
- A **giant spider**  
- A **dense mist** that seeps through cracks and armor seams  

Each form retains a subtle wrongness — a shimmer, a scent of decay, or a faint whisper of chitin scraping.

---

## **Abilities**
- **Shapechanger:** Can shift between forms at will.  
- **Charm and Domination:** Serves as Lolth’s voice, capable of mentally enslaving lesser beings.  
- **Poisonous Touch:** Its slime burns flesh and corrupts magic.  
- **Spider Climb:** Moves effortlessly across any surface.  
- **Mist Form:** Immune to most physical attacks; can infiltrate sealed spaces.  
- **Lolth’s Blessing:** Can communicate directly with the Spider Queen across planes.

---

## **Behavior and Personality**
- **Fanatically loyal** to Lolth; utterly without mercy.  
- Speaks in **whispers layered with multiple voices**, as if several entities echo each word.  
- Views mortals as disposable tools — even Drow priests.  
- Exhibits **sadistic curiosity**, often testing victims before killing them.  
- When in humanoid form, mimics empathy but never feels it.  
- In mist form, it hums softly — a sound like distant chanting.

---

## **Role in Drow Society**
- Acts as **Lolth’s emissary** to her priestesses.  
- Appears during rituals, punishments, or divine tests.  
- Enforces obedience among the Drow elite.  
- Serves as a **living conduit** for Lolth’s will — her presence marks divine favor or impending doom.  
- Drow fear them more than they fear death; a Yochlol’s arrival means judgment.

---

## **Combat Notes (D&D 5e)**
- **AC:** 15 (natural armor)  
- **HP:** ~136 (16d10 + 48)  
- **Speed:** 30 ft, climb 30 ft  
- **Resistances:** Cold, Fire, Lightning, Nonmagical Weapons  
- **Immunities:** Poison, Charm, Exhaustion  
- **Condition Immunities:** Poisoned  
- **Languages:** Abyssal, Elvish, Undercommon, telepathy 120 ft  
- **Challenge Rating:** 10  

---

## **Tactical Behavior**
- Opens combat with **Charm Person** or **Dominate Person** to isolate targets.  
- Uses **Mist Form** to avoid damage and reposition.  
- Employs **Poisonous Touch** against armored foes.  
- Retreats only if commanded by Lolth or if its mission is complete.  
- Never bargains — only obeys.

---

# Yochlol — Behavior, Culture, Relation to Lolth, and Speech Patterns

## Core Identity
Yochlol (also called “Handmaidens of Lolth”) are **shapechanging fiends** created directly by Lolth.  
They are not demons with free will — they are **extensions of Lolth’s will**, living instruments of obedience, punishment, and surveillance.

They are:
- fanatically loyal  
- emotionless except when mimicking  
- sadistically curious  
- spiritually poisonous  
- embodiments of Lolth’s authority  

They do not serve the Drow.  
They **rule** the Drow on Lolth’s behalf.

---

## Behavior Profile

### 1. **Absolute Obedience**
A Yochlol has no personal agenda.  
Its only purpose is:
- enforce Lolth’s will  
- test Drow loyalty  
- punish weakness  
- deliver divine messages  
- manipulate priestesses  

It does not negotiate.  
It does not compromise.  
It does not “ally.”  
It **commands**.

---

### 2. **Predatory Stillness**
Yochlol rarely move unless necessary.  
When still, they appear:
- statuesque  
- silently watching  
- waiting for a mistake  
- evaluating every word  

This stillness is a psychological weapon.

---

### 3. **Sadistic Curiosity**
They enjoy:
- probing emotional weaknesses  
- exposing secrets  
- forcing confessions  
- watching Drow betray each other  

Not because they feel joy —  
but because **Lolth delights in suffering**, and Yochlol reflect that.

---

### 4. **Shapechanging as Psychological Warfare**
Forms:
- **Viscous ooze** (true form)  
- **Drow woman** (perfect but uncanny)  
- **Giant spider**  
- **Dense mist**  

They shift forms mid‑conversation to:
- unsettle  
- intimidate  
- test reactions  
- assert dominance  

A Yochlol’s Drow form is always *slightly wrong*:
- eyes too still  
- smile too smooth  
- movements too fluid  
- no breathing  

---

### 5. **No Personal Fear**
They do not fear death.  
They do not fear pain.  
They do not fear failure.

They only fear **Lolth’s displeasure**, which is absolute annihilation.

---

## Cultural Role in Drow Society

### 1. **Lolth’s Enforcers**
Yochlol appear when:
- a priestess is being tested  
- a house is being judged  
- a ritual requires divine oversight  
- a Matron has displeased Lolth  
- a political conflict needs “clarification”  

Their presence means:
> “Lolth is watching.”

---

### 2. **Priestess Examiners**
They test:
- faith  
- obedience  
- ambition  
- cruelty  
- willingness to sacrifice  

A priestess who hesitates is marked for death.

---

### 3. **House Judges**
A Yochlol may appear in a Matron’s chambers to:
- demand tribute  
- demand sacrifice  
- demand confession  
- demand a rival’s destruction  

Refusal is not an option.

---

### 4. **Agents of Internal Chaos**
Lolth thrives on:
- betrayal  
- conflict  
- paranoia  
- instability  

Yochlol ensure the Drow never grow too unified or too stable.

They whisper:
- lies  
- half‑truths  
- temptations  
- accusations  

Their goal is to keep houses at each other’s throats.

---

## Relationship to Lolth

### 1. **Direct Extensions of Lolth**
They are not servants.  
They are **fragments of Lolth’s consciousness** given form.

When a Yochlol speaks, Lolth is speaking.

---

### 2. **Lolth’s Eyes and Ears**
They observe:
- rituals  
- betrayals  
- political maneuvers  
- priestess behavior  
- Matron decisions  

Everything they witness is known to Lolth instantly.

---

### 3. **Lolth’s Punishment Made Flesh**
When Lolth is displeased:
- a Yochlol appears  
- a house trembles  
- a priestess dies  
- a Matron kneels  

They are divine consequences.

---

## Relationship to the Drow

### 1. **Feared, Not Revered**
Drow do not worship Yochlol.  
They **fear** them.

A Yochlol’s arrival means:
- someone will die  
- someone will be punished  
- someone will be tested  

No Drow welcomes them.

---

### 2. **Priestesses Are Terrified of Them**
Even High Priestesses know:
- a Yochlol outranks them  
- a Yochlol can kill them  
- a Yochlol can replace them  

Priestesses obey instantly.

---

### 3. **Matron Mothers Treat Them as Divine Inspectors**
A Matron will:
- kneel  
- offer blood  
- offer slaves  
- offer daughters  
- offer secrets  

Anything to avoid Lolth’s wrath.

---

## How a Yochlol Speaks

### 1. **Layered Voices**
A Yochlol’s voice is:
- multiple tones at once  
- whispering under itself  
- echoing faintly  
- slightly out of sync  

It sounds like:
> “a chorus speaking through a single throat.”

---

### 2. **Emotionless Delivery**
They do not:
- laugh  
- shout  
- show anger  
- show joy  

Their tone is:
- flat  
- cold  
- clinical  
- inevitable  

Even when threatening.

---

### 3. **Unsettling Cadence**
Speech patterns:
- long pauses  
- sudden shifts  
- unfinished sentences  
- statements instead of questions  
- pronouns used incorrectly (“We is watching.”)  
- referring to themselves as “we” or “this form”  

---

### 4. **Truth and Lies Mixed Seamlessly**
They speak in:
- riddles  
- half‑truths  
- prophetic fragments  
- commands disguised as questions  

A Yochlol never lies outright —  
but they never tell the whole truth.

---

### 5. **Examples of Yochlol Speech**
- “We is watching your faith… and your fear.”  
- “This form finds your hesitation… disappointing.”  
- “Lolth hears the tremor in your voice.”  
- “Your house bleeds weakness. Shall we show the Spider Queen?”  
- “Kneel. Slowly. We enjoy the sound of obedience.”  

---

## Summary
Yochlol are:
- Lolth’s will made flesh  
- feared by all Drow  
- emotionless enforcers  
- masters of psychological terror  
- divine judges  
- manipulators of priestesses  
- agents of chaos and obedience  

Their speech is:
- layered  
- cold  
- unsettling  
- prophetic  
- commanding  
- never fully honest

# Actions

## **Corrupting Touch** (Drow or Hybrid form)
*Melee Spell Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target  
**Hit:** 21 (4d8 + 5) psychic damage  
**Effect:** DC 18 Wis save or the target is **Charmed** for 1 minute and obeys the Yochlol’s commands.  
Repeat save at end of each turn.

---

## **Acid Lash** (Ooze form)
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft.  
**Hit:** 27 (6d6 + 4) acid damage  
**Effect:** DC 18 Dex save or armor/weapons take a **-1 penalty** (stacking).  
Destroyed at -5.

---

## **Venomous Bite** (Spider or Bone‑Spider form)
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit  
**Hit:** 15 (2d10 + 4) piercing + 18 (4d8) poison  
**Effect:** DC 18 Con save or **Poisoned** for 1 hour.  
Fail by 5+ → **Paralyzed** instead.

---

## **Suffocating Shroud** (Mist or Blood‑Mist form)
Creatures in a 10‑ft radius must make a **DC 18 Con save** or take:
- 14 (4d6) necrotic  
- cannot speak  
- cannot cast spells with verbal components  
- begins suffocating (cannot regain breath)

Repeat save at end of each turn.

---

## **Shadow Strike** (Shadow‑Silhouette form)
*Melee Spell Attack:* +10 to hit  
**Hit:** 22 (4d10) necrotic  
**Effect:** DC 18 Wis save or target is **Blinded** for 1 minute.

# Reactions

## **Lolth’s Rebuke**
When hit by an attack, the Yochlol forces the attacker to make a **DC 18 Wis save** or take:
- 10 (3d6) psychic  
- disadvantage on next attack  
- disadvantage on next Wis save  

---

# Legendary Actions (3/round)

### **1. Shift Form**
The Yochlol changes form without using an action.

### **2. Corrupting Gaze**
One creature within 30 ft. makes a **DC 18 Wis save** or is **Frightened** until end of next turn.

### **3. Command of Lolth (Costs 2 Actions)**
The Yochlol issues a divine command:  
One creature must make a **DC 18 Wis save** or take **no actions** on its next turn except to kneel or cower.

### **4. Psychic Rupture (Costs 3 Actions)**
All creatures within 10 ft. take:
- 18 (4d8) psychic  
- DC 18 Int save or be **Stunned** until end of next turn.

---

# Lair Actions (If in a Lolth‑aligned temple)

### On initiative count 20 (losing ties):

- **Webbed Ground:** 20‑ft area becomes difficult terrain; DC 15 Str save or restrained.  
- **Whispers of Lolth:** DC 18 Wis save or take 10 psychic and have disadvantage on next save.  
- **Shadow Surge:** Shadows extinguish all nonmagical light in 30 ft.

---

- Spiders behave unnaturally and gather in large numbers.  
- Whispering voices echo in tunnels.  
- Drow feel watched; disadvantage on Insight checks against deception.
