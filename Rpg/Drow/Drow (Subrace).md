# Drow (Subrace)

Parent Race: Elf  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Additional Traits (Paraphrased):  
- +1 CHA  
- Superior Darkvision (120 ft)  
- Sunlight Sensitivity  
- Drow Magic (Dancing Lights, Faerie Fire, Darkness)

GM Notes:  
- Superior Darkvision is extremely strong.  
- Sunlight Sensitivity is a major drawback in surface campaigns.  
- Spell list is excellent for CHA casters.

Synergy Notes:  
- Ophelia: Very strong synergy with CHA and darkness themes.  
- Attina: Sensitivity is a major issue for wizards.
