

# Session: The Stranger at the Moonlit Path

## Session Premise

The party travels toward the Eilistraee colony through the forested approach route protected by allied High Elves and Wood Elves.

The colony is not openly marked on regional maps and is deliberately screened from outsiders. The surrounding forest acts as its first layer of defense.

The party is intercepted before reaching the settlement.

Before the encounter can be resolved, an extraordinarily unusual figure arrives:

A well-dressed Itich.

The creature speaks clearly and formally.

> "I humbly request the sanctuary of Lestray... and I advise you not to trust these travelers."

None of the elves understand how strange this actually is.

The party does.

---

# Campaign Context

The Eilistraee colony is a refuge for Lolth defectors and others attempting to build a new life outside Lolth's control.

It exists in alliance with neighboring High Elf and Wood Elf communities.

The elves protect the approaches rather than filling the colony itself with soldiers.

This arrangement allows the Eilistraee settlement to remain open and peaceful internally while maintaining a highly effective hidden perimeter.

The nearby Itich population lives primarily around the abandoned dwarven forge and mine complex.

Ordinary Itich are highly primitive.

They are animalistic, tribal, and dominated primarily by survival instincts.

Complex diplomacy, formal speech, elaborate clothing, and abstract political manipulation should therefore immediately appear impossible to anyone who actually understands Itich behavior.

Unfortunately, almost nobody here does.

Only the party possesses meaningful knowledge about what the Itich normally are.

The Council of Nine does not possess detailed knowledge of the strange crystal infection.

The crystal phenomenon is still largely known only to the party.

---

# Scene 1: Entering the Protected Forest

The forest gradually changes as the party approaches the hidden route to the Eilistraee colony.

There are no obvious walls.

No guards stand beside the road.

Instead, signs of observation slowly accumulate.

A snapped branch has been deliberately repositioned.

A bird call repeats from two different directions.

Tracks appear beside the party's route and then disappear.

A distant figure is visible between the trees for only a moment.

The Wood Elves have been watching the party for some time.

The High Elves are waiting farther ahead.

## Atmosphere

The forest should feel protected rather than hostile.

The party is allowed to proceed because the patrol wants to understand where they are going.

Eventually, the road opens into a small clearing.

A High Elf steps onto the path.

Several Wood Elves remain concealed among the trees.

The High Elf speaks calmly.

> "You approach lands under our protection."

> "Beyond this forest lies a settlement allied with our people."

> "State your names, your purpose, and your business with Lestray."

The elves do not immediately draw weapons.

They are courteous.

They are also completely in control of the encounter.

---

# Scene 2: The Border Interview

The High Elf patrol leader questions the party.

Important questions include:

- Who are you?
- Who told you the colony exists?
- Why are you seeking Lestray?
- Are you carrying messages for anyone inside?
- Have you been followed?
- Are you affiliated with Lolth?
- Are you carrying dangerous magical objects?
- Do you intend to remain in the colony?

The Wood Elves quietly reposition during the conversation.

Characters with strong perception may notice additional scouts.

## Possible Checks

### Perception

Notice hidden Wood Elf archers.

### Insight

Realize that the High Elf is not primarily concerned about the party attacking.

The patrol is concerned about the party leading someone else to the colony.

### Persuasion

Convince the patrol that the party has legitimate reasons to enter.

### History or Religion

Demonstrate meaningful knowledge of Eilistraee.

This should help, but it should not automatically grant entrance.

---

# Scene 3: The Impossible Visitor

During the questioning, one of the Wood Elf scouts gives a warning signal.

Something is approaching along another path.

The patrol shifts immediately.

Weapons are prepared but not fired.

A figure emerges from the trees.

At first glance it appears humanoid only because of the clothing.

It wears carefully layered garments.

A heavy cloak.

Gloves.

A high collar.

Perhaps even a hood or carefully wrapped head covering.

Everything about the outfit seems designed to conceal as much of the body as possible.

Then the face becomes visible.

It is an Itich.

The party recognizes what it is.

The elves do not understand the significance.

The Itich stops at a respectful distance.

It bows.

Then speaks in remarkably controlled Common.

> "I humbly request the sanctuary of Lestray."

A pause.

The creature looks directly toward the party.

> "And I advise you not to trust these travelers."

---

# Important GM Information

The Itich is infected with the crystal phenomenon.

The crystals are hidden beneath the clothing.

They should NOT be immediately visible.

Do not describe glowing crystals through gaps in the clothing unless the party deliberately exposes them.

Ordinary magical observation should not simply reveal what is happening.

Mage Sight is limited and should not automatically identify the infection.

The elves therefore have no obvious reason to conclude that the creature is magically altered.

From their perspective, they have encountered an intelligent unfamiliar humanoid requesting sanctuary.

The party's reaction may initially seem more suspicious than the Itich itself.

---

# Scene 4: The Patrol Reacts

The High Elf patrol leader raises one hand.

Everyone stops.

The Wood Elves cover both groups.

The patrol leader addresses the Itich.

> "You have requested sanctuary."

> "Under whose protection do you claim passage?"

The Itich answers carefully.

It knows enough terminology to sound convincing.

Then the patrol leader turns toward the party.

> "You recognize this creature."

This is where the party becomes the source of information.

If they explain that ordinary Itich do not behave this way, the elves will listen.

But they have no independent evidence.

The patrol leader may respond:

> "You tell me this creature should not be capable of speaking."

> "Yet it speaks."

> "You tell me it should not understand sanctuary."

> "Yet it has requested sanctuary according to our customs."

> "Either your information is incomplete..."

> "...or something has happened to it."

That is exactly the mystery.

---

# Scene 5: The Accusation

The Itich does not simply accuse the party of being dangerous.

It provides details.

Some should be true.

That is what makes the encounter uncomfortable.

Possible statements:

> "They interfere with powers they do not understand."

> "They were present when planar boundaries failed."

> "They carry knowledge of forces that have destroyed others."

> "They have dealings with beings who manipulate the Weave."

> "They conceal information from those who claim authority."

Some of these are technically accurate.

Others may be distorted.

The Itich is attempting to undermine the party without making accusations that can easily be disproven.

---

# Scene 6: The Question of Sanctuary

The Eilistraee alliance takes sanctuary seriously.

The patrol cannot simply kill the Itich because the party says it is dangerous.

This creates a real conflict.

The creature has:

- approached peacefully;
- surrendered tactical advantage;
- requested sanctuary;
- obeyed instructions;
- made no immediate attack.

The patrol leader therefore makes a difficult decision.

Both parties will be escorted toward a controlled meeting place outside the main colony.

Neither is permitted to approach the settlement freely.

---

# The Neutral Clearing

Before reaching Lestray, the elves maintain a protected clearing used for meetings with outsiders.

This becomes the location for the next stage of the session.

## Battlemap Features

The clearing should include:

- a circular stone meeting platform;
- two small shelters;
- a shallow stream;
- elevated Wood Elf observation platforms;
- narrow trails disappearing into dense woodland;
- hidden defensive positions;
- old standing stones;
- moon symbols marking the alliance with Eilistraee;
- no obvious fortress architecture.

The location should feel peaceful.

Its defenses only become obvious if someone attempts violence.

---

# Scene 7: First Examination

The elves want to determine what the Itich actually is.

The creature initially refuses to remove its clothing.

It claims:

> "My body has been injured."

> "I do not wish to be displayed."

This is a clever use of the elves' own sanctuary customs.

The party now has a problem.

They suspect something is hidden underneath.

But forcibly stripping a sanctuary seeker in front of the patrol would make them look extremely dangerous.

---

# Ways the Truth Can Begin to Emerge

## Heat

The Itich appears unusually cold or unusually warm.

## Movement

The clothing occasionally shifts strangely around rigid structures beneath it.

## Sound

When the Itich moves, there is occasionally a faint crystalline clicking sound.

## Injury

A minor accidental wound exposes something beneath the skin.

## Detection

A spell reveals something abnormal without identifying exactly what it is.

## Behavior

The Itich pauses strangely before answering questions, almost as though something else is helping formulate its responses.

---

# Scene 8: Crystal Reveal

Eventually the crystals are exposed.

This should be a dramatic reveal.

The garment is pulled aside.

Beneath it, crystalline growths spread through the Itich's body.

They may emerge between segments of the exoskeleton.

Some may appear embedded rather than attached.

Others may pulse faintly.

The elves have never seen anything like it.

The party has.

This immediately changes the balance of the conversation.

The patrol leader now understands that the party's concern was not simple prejudice toward an unfamiliar species.

However, the crystals still do not explain the intelligence.

That becomes the more disturbing question.

---

# Central Mystery

The session should leave several possibilities open.

The Itich may be:

- mentally altered by the crystals;
- controlled by another intelligence;
- part of an experiment;
- transformed by anomaly exposure;
- connected to Arion;
- connected to the hybrid plane;
- carrying some form of copied intelligence;
- being used as a messenger;
- developing abilities no Itich should possess.

Do not reveal the answer immediately.

The important discovery is:

**The Itich are changing.**

Or at least this one has.

---

# Scene 9: Why It Distrusts the Party

The Itich should know something it reasonably should not know.

Not everything.

Just enough to be alarming.

Possible examples:

It knows Attina's name.

It knows Arion attempted to control the dragon.

It knows the stabilizer was disabled.

It knows someone in the party can enter the hybrid Astral-Weave plane.

It knows someone can perceive the Shadow Weave.

It knows about the crystal infection.

Any one of these is enough.

Do not give it all of them.

One impossible piece of knowledge is much more effective.

---

# Optional Twist: The Itich Is Afraid

At some point, the party realizes something unexpected.

The Itich is genuinely frightened.

Not frightened of the elves.

Not even primarily frightened of the party.

It is frightened of something that may be following it.

This creates the next threat.

---

# Scene 10: Something Enters the Forest

Wood Elf scouts disappear from one section of the perimeter.

No alarm signal is heard.

A patrol fails to report.

Animals begin fleeing through the clearing.

The sanctuary hearing is suddenly interrupted.

Something has followed the Itich.

Possible threat options:

### Crystal-Infected Itich Pack

Primitive Itich infected with smaller crystalline growths.

They behave more aggressively and move with unnatural coordination.

### Crystal Predator

A creature that tracks whatever crystal energy is inside the intelligent Itich.

### Hybrid Creature Fragment

A smaller creature connected to the enormous hybrid monster currently burrowing toward the city.

### Shadow-Weave Hunter

Something that cannot easily be detected through normal Weave perception.

### Arion's Agent

Not necessarily Arion himself.

Something sent to retrieve or destroy the altered Itich.

---

# Forest Defense Encounter

The elves finally reveal why they guard the colony from the forest.

Wood Elf archers appear from concealed positions.

High Elf wardens activate prepared magical defenses.

Paths change.

Illusions conceal escape routes.

Moon-marked standing stones create barriers.

The party is asked to help defend the clearing.

The intelligent Itich remains under guard.

Whether it helps depends on the party's actions.

# High Elf Silver Warden

Medium Humanoid (Elf)

## Suggested Challenge

CR 4

## Armor Class

18

## Hit Points

Approximately 70-80

## Speed

30 ft.

## Key Abilities

DEX and INT should be strongest.

## Traits

### Fey Ancestry

Advantage against being charmed.

Magic cannot put the elf to sleep.

### Tactical Awareness

The warden cannot be surprised while conscious.

### Alliance Commander

Allied creatures within 30 feet gain advantage on their first saving throw against fear.

## Actions

### Multiattack

Two longsword or longbow attacks.

### Longbow

Strong ranged attack.

### Binding Shot

A specialized nonlethal arrow.

On a hit, target must make a Strength saving throw or have speed reduced to 0 until it removes the restraint.

## Reactions

### Arcane Parry

Increase AC against one incoming attack.

## Magic

Suggested spells:

- Misty Step
- Shield
- Hold Person
- Detect Magic
- Message
- Minor Illusion

# Living World: Lestray Colony

If the party is ultimately permitted inside, the transition from guarded forest to colony should feel dramatic.

The defensive atmosphere disappears.

There are no rows of soldiers.

Instead there are:

- children running between homes;
- communal kitchens;
- music being practiced;
- craftsmen working outside;
- refugees learning surface agriculture;
- elves teaching one another languages;
- gardens planted around moon symbols;
- unfinished buildings;
- people repairing roofs;
- hunters returning from the woods;
- communal meals being prepared;
- quiet memorials to people lost during escape from Lolth.

The colony is visibly still being built.

Nothing matches perfectly.

Architecture reflects multiple origins.

Some structures have Drow stonework.

Others use High Elf craftsmanship.

Some are Wood Elf structures integrated into living trees.

Others were clearly built quickly by refugees who were simply trying to survive their first winter.

The result is imperfect.

And alive.

---

# Evening in Lestray

At sunset, the colony changes.

Lanterns are lit.

Silver-blue magical lights appear among the trees.

Music begins.

People gather for communal meals.

The Moon Garden becomes the center of activity.

Dancing is common.

Weapons remain nearby, but they are not carried as symbols of authority.

This is deliberate.

The colony is trying to create a society where people do not constantly demonstrate dominance.

---

# Important Social Conflict

The intelligent Itich creates a major philosophical problem for Lestray.

If the colony exists because people deserve the opportunity to escape what they were born into...

what happens when an Itich asks for exactly the same thing?

The party knows that normal Itich barely possess the intelligence required to understand the request.

But this one clearly does.

Some colonists will argue:

> "If it understands sanctuary, we must hear it."

Others:

> "It is infected with something we do not understand."

Others:

> "That is exactly what people said about us when we fled Lolth."

Others:

> "And trusting the wrong stranger could kill everyone here."

There should be no immediate consensus.

---

# Possible Session Endings

## Ending A: The Itich Is Granted Temporary Sanctuary

The colony agrees to hold it under observation.

The party is invited to investigate the crystals.

## Ending B: The Itich Escapes

During the forest attack, it disappears.

Now the party must determine whether the attack was a rescue.

## Ending C: The Crystals Activate

Something happens during examination.

The Itich's personality changes.

Its speech becomes fragmented.

Someone else begins speaking through it.

## Ending D: It Names Arion

The creature looks directly at Attina and says:

> "He is still looking for you."

End session.

## Ending E: It Knows About the Hybrid Plane

The creature turns toward a character capable of entering it.

> "You have walked where only magic can be seen."

Nobody except the party should know that.

End session.

# Future Hooks Created by This Session

1. Investigate how the Itich gained advanced intelligence.
2. Determine the nature of the crystal infection.
3. Discover who sent or altered the Itich.
4. Determine how it knew information about the party.
5. Explore tensions within the Eilistraee colony over sanctuary.
6. Investigate missing Wood Elf scouts.
7. Track whatever followed the Itich.
8. Compare the crystals to the anomaly surrounding the Weave.
9. Investigate whether the crystals interact with the Shadow Weave.
10. Return to the abandoned dwarven mines to determine whether other Itich are changing.
11. Determine whether Arion is involved.
12. Discover whether the creature is genuinely seeking freedom or performing an elaborate manipulation.

# GM Principle

Do not make the intelligent Itich obviously evil or obviously trustworthy.

The mystery works because both possibilities remain credible.

It should provide useful information.

It should also lie about at least one thing.

The party should leave the session knowing significantly more than they did before while being considerably less certain about what the crystals are actually doing.
