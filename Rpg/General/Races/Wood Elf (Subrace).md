# Wood Elf (Subrace)

Parent Race: Elf  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 35 ft  

Additional Traits (Paraphrased):  
- +1 WIS  
- Mask of the Wild (hide in light natural cover)  
- Increased speed (35 ft)

GM Notes:  
- Fastest PHB race.  
- Excellent for stealth and exploration.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: WIS bonus helps cleric casting.  
- Attina: Mobility is always useful.
