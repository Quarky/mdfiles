# Warforged (Eberron)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Constructed Resilience:
Advantage vs poison  
  - Resistance to poison  
  - Immune to disease  
  - No eating, drinking, or sleeping  
- Integrated Protection (built-in armor scaling)  
- Specialized Design (tool proficiency)

GM Notes:  
- **One of the most durable races in 5e.**  
- Immunities and resistances dramatically reduce attrition.  
- Integrated Protection simplifies AC optimization.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: This is Heart’s race — perfect synergy with tank/caster hybrid roles.
