# Mountain Dwarf (Subrace)

Parent Race: Dwarf  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 25 ft  

Additional Traits (Paraphrased):  
- +2 STR  
- Armor Training (light + medium armor)

GM Notes:  
- Medium armor proficiency is a huge boon for casters or rogues.  
- STR bonus pushes toward martial builds.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: Strong for melee cleric/warlock hybrids.  
- Attina: Armor proficiency is surprisingly useful.
