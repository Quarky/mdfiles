# Shifter (Eberron)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Darkvision  
- Shifting (bonus action; temp HP = 2 × proficiency bonus + CON mod; subtype benefit)

GM Notes:  
- Subtypes define combat style (claws, fangs, agility, resilience).  
- Shifting is a strong defensive and offensive tool.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: Works well for melee cleric/warlock builds.
