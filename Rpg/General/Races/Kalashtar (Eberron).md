# Kalashtar (Eberron)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Dual Mind (advantage on WIS saves)  
- Mental Discipline (resistance to psychic damage)  
- Telepathy  
- No dreams; immune to dream-based effects

GM Notes:  
- **One of the best anti-psychic races in 5e.**  
- Telepathy is powerful for silent coordination.

Synergy Notes:  
- Attina: Strong for casters who need WIS save protection.  
- Heart: Good for resisting mind-control effects.
