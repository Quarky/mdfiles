# Gnome (Race)

Type: Player race  
Size: Small  
Speed: 25 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Darkvision  
- Gnome Cunning (advantage on INT, WIS, CHA saves vs magic)

GM Notes:  
- **One of the best anti-magic defensive traits in the game.**  
- Subraces define spellcasting or tinkering.

Synergy Notes:  
- Attina: Excellent wizard chassis.  
- Heart: Strong defensive synergy.
