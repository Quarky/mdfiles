# Dragonborn (Race)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Draconic Ancestry (choose damage type)  
- Breath Weapon (action; 2d6 → 5d6 scaling; Dex or Con save)  
- Damage Resistance (matches ancestry)

GM Notes:  
- Resistance is the long-term value; breath weapon is early-game burst.  
- Works best for melee or gish builds.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: Good for frontline caster durability.  
- Ophelia: CHA synergy but melee focus is less ideal.
