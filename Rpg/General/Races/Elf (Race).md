# Elf (Race)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Darkvision  
- Keen Senses (Perception proficiency)  
- Fey Ancestry:
- Advantage on saving throws vs **charm**  
  - Immune to magical sleep  
- Trance (4-hour rest)

GM Notes:  
- **Charm resistance is one of the strongest defensive racial traits in 5e.**  
- Perception proficiency is universally valuable.  
- Trance provides logistical and exploration advantages.

Synergy Notes:  
- Attina: Helps protect a fragile caster from charm effects.  
- Ophelia: Strong synergy with social and magical manipulation themes.  
- Heart: Protects against mind-control.
