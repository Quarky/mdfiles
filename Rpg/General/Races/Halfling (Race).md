# Halfling (Race)

Type: Player race  
Size: Small  
Speed: 25 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Lucky (reroll 1s on attacks, saves, checks)  
- Brave (advantage vs fear)  
- Halfling Nimbleness (move through larger creatures)

GM Notes:  
- **Lucky is one of the strongest racial traits in 5e.**  
- Fear resistance is valuable in many campaigns.

Synergy Notes:  
- Attina: Lucky helps with concentration checks.  
- Ophelia: CHA builds benefit from reliability.
