# Rock Gnome (Subrace)

Parent Race: Gnome  
Size: Small  
Speed: 25 ft  

Additional Traits (Paraphrased):  
- +1 CON  
- Artificer’s Lore (history checks about magic items/tech)  
- Tinker (create small clockwork devices)

GM Notes:  
- Tinker is mostly flavor but can be creatively powerful.  
- CON bonus improves survivability.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: CON bonus helps tanking.  
- Attina: Lore bonus fits wizard themes.
