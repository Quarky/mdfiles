# Stout Halfling (Subrace)

Parent Race: Halfling  
Size: Small  
Speed: 25 ft  

Additional Traits (Paraphrased):  
- +1 CON  
- Stout Resilience (advantage vs poison; resistance to poison damage)

GM Notes:  
- Essentially “Halfling + Dwarf Resilience.”  
- Very durable for a Small race.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: Poison resistance is excellent for frontline roles.  
- Attina: CON bonus helps concentration.
