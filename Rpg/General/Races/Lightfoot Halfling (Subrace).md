# Lightfoot Halfling (Subrace)

Parent Race: Halfling  
Size: Small  
Speed: 25 ft  

Additional Traits (Paraphrased):  
- +1 CHA  
- Naturally Stealthy (can hide behind creatures larger than you)

GM Notes:  
- One of the best stealth subraces in the PHB.  
- CHA bonus supports bard/warlock/sorcerer builds.

Synergy Notes:  
- Ophelia: CHA synergy is excellent.  
- Attina: Stealth utility is helpful.
