# Dwarf (Race)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 25 ft (not reduced by armor)  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Darkvision  
- Dwarven Resilience (advantage vs poison; resistance to poison damage)  
- Dwarven Combat Training  
- Tool Proficiency  
- Stonecunning (situational)

GM Notes:  
- Poison resilience is extremely strong in many campaigns.  
- Stonecunning is rarely impactful.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: Excellent for tank builds.  
- Attina: Less synergy with INT focus.
