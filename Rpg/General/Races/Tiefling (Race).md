# Tiefling (Race)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Darkvision  
- Fire Resistance  
- Infernal Legacy (Thaumaturgy, Hellish Rebuke, Darkness)

GM Notes:  
- Fire resistance is extremely valuable.  
- Spell list is strong for CHA casters.

Synergy Notes:  
- Ophelia: Very strong synergy with CHA and fiendish themes.
