# High Elf (Subrace)

Parent Race: Elf  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Additional Traits (Paraphrased):  
- +1 INT  
- Elf Weapon Training  
- Extra Cantrip (wizard cantrip of choice)

GM Notes:  
- One of the best caster subraces in the PHB.  
- Free cantrip expands early‑game versatility.

Synergy Notes:  
- Attina: Perfect synergy for wizards.  
- Ophelia: INT bonus less relevant.
