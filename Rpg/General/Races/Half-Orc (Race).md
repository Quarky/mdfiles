# Half-Orc (Race)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Darkvision  
- Relentless Endurance (drop to 1 HP instead of 0 once per long rest)  
- Savage Attacks (extra die on crits)  
- Intimidation proficiency

GM Notes:  
- Relentless Endurance is extremely strong for frontline survival.  
- Savage Attacks benefits crit-fishing builds.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: Works well for melee cleric/warlock hybrids.
