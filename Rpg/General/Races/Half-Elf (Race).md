# Half-Elf (Race)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Darkvision  
- Fey Ancestry  
- +2 CHA, +1 to two other stats  
- Two skill proficiencies
- Fey Ancestry:
- Advantage on saving throws vs **charm**  
  - Immune to magical sleep  

GM Notes:  
- One of the most flexible and powerful races for CHA casters.  
- Skill versatility is excellent for social or exploration roles.

Synergy Notes:  
- Ophelia: Perfect synergy with CHA 20.  
- Heart: Useful for hybrid builds.
