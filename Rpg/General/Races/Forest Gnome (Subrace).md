# Forest Gnome (Subrace)

Parent Race: Gnome  
Size: Small  
Speed: 25 ft  

Additional Traits (Paraphrased):  
- +1 DEX  
- Minor Illusion cantrip  
- Speak with Small Beasts (limited communication)

GM Notes:  
- Minor Illusion is one of the strongest utility cantrips.  
- Beast communication is flavorful and occasionally useful.

Synergy Notes:  
- Attina: Strong synergy with illusion magic.  
- Ophelia: Cantrip adds utility.
