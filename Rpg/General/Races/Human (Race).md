# Human (Race)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Standard: +1 to all stats  
- Variant: +1 to two stats, one skill, one feat

GM Notes:  
- Variant Human is one of the strongest races due to early feat access.  
- Standard Human is simple but less optimized.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: Variant Human works well for hybrid builds.  
- Attina: Early feats are excellent for wizards.
