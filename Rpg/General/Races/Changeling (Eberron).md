# Changeling (Eberron)

Type: Player race  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 30 ft  

Core Traits (Paraphrased):  
- Shapechanger (at-will appearance change)  
- +2 CHA  
- Two skill proficiencies  
- Social versatility

GM Notes:  
- Shapechanging is **non-magical** and cannot be dispelled.  
- One of the best infiltration races.

Synergy Notes:  
- Ophelia: Extremely strong synergy with her social/infiltration toolkit.
