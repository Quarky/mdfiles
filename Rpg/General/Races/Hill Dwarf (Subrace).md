# Hill Dwarf (Subrace)

Parent Race: Dwarf  
Size: Medium  
Speed: 25 ft  

Additional Traits (Paraphrased):  
- +1 WIS  
- Dwarven Toughness (max HP increases by +1 per level)

GM Notes:  
- One of the tankiest subraces in the PHB.  
- HP scaling is extremely strong over long campaigns.

Synergy Notes:  
- Heart: Excellent for frontline durability.  
- Attina: WIS bonus helps with saves.
