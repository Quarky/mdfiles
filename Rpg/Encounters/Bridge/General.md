*Medium undead/aberration (Fold‑tainted), Chaotic Unbound*

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |

**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

**Crystal Reassembly.**  
When the creature is first approached within 10 ft, it begins to reform.  
The observer takes **1d4 necrotic damage** as the air is drained around them.  
The creature regains **10 HP** at the start of each of its turns unless exposed to radiant damage or unless Attina succeeds on an **Arcana DC 14** check to disrupt the crystal resonance.

**Mage‑Bone Memory.**  
The crystal bracelets contain fragments of the mages’ last spells.  
Once per round, the creature can add **+1d6 force** to one attack.

**Warforged Reject Plates.**  
The creature’s plating is unstable.  
When it takes thunder or lightning damage, it becomes **staggered** (disadvantage on attacks) until the end of its next turn.

**No Human Consciousness.**  
Insight checks automatically reveal:  
*“There is nothing human left in it.”*

---

## Actions

### **Multiattack**
The horror makes **two Claw attacks**.

### **Claw**  
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft  
*Hit:* 2d6 + 3 slashing  
If both claws hit the same target in a round, the target must succeed on a **DC 13 Con save** or take **1d6 necrotic** as crystal shards embed into flesh.

### **Crystal Lash (Recharge 5–6)**  
A whip‑like tendril of crystal and sinew lashes out.  
*Ranged Spell Attack:* +4 to hit, 20 ft  
*Hit:* 2d8 force  
Target must succeed on a **DC 14 Dex save** or be **pulled 10 ft** toward the creature.

### **Reassembly Surge (1/Day)**  
The creature violently pulls scattered pieces back into itself.  
Creatures within 10 ft must make a **DC 14 Wis save** or take **2d6 psychic** and be **frightened** for 1 round.
