# Encounter 3: The Burning Pass – Narrow Push

**Scale:** 400 ft wide × 600 ft deep (80×120 grid at 5 ft per square)  
**Elevation:** Pass floor at +0 ft; ridge walls up to +50 ft  
**Fire:** Anomaly flamethrowers, explosive glyphs, burning terrain

**Terrain Zones**  
- **Pass Entrance (0–150 ft)** – Narrow canyon, 40 ft wide. Scorched rock, patches of burning oil (1d6 fire if entered). Two flamethrower emplacements (stone bunkers) flank the entrance.  
- **Mid‑Pass (150–450 ft)** – 30 ft wide, walls rise 40 ft. Explosive glyphs on floor (10, visible Arcana DC 12). A collapsing ridge on the east wall (40 ft high, unstable).  
- **Flame Corridor (300 ft)** – A chokepoint where two flamethrowers cross fire. Battle‑mages can counterspell the flames (reaction, Arcana DC 14) to create a 10‑ft safe passage for 1 round.  
- **Rear Firebase (450–600 ft)** – Enemy camp with three corrupted evocation mages. A large anomaly flamethrower (mounted) sweeps the pass. Powder barrels stacked nearby.  
- **Collapsing Ridge** – East wall, 40 ft high, 100 ft long. Unstable. Can be collapsed (demolition or heavy damage) to create a rockslide (blocks pass, deals 6d6 bludgeoning to anyone underneath).

**Interactive Elements**  
- **Flamethrowers** – 4 total. Each is a metal tank with a nozzle (AC 15, HP 20). Fires 30‑ft cone (Dex save DC 13, 4d6 fire). Can be disabled by destroying tank (explodes, 10‑ft radius, 4d6 fire). Battle‑mages can counterspell the flames (reaction).  
- **Explosive Glyphs** – 12 glyphs. Trigger: stepping on. Dex save DC 13 or 3d6 fire, and ignites adjacent glyphs (chain reaction). Can be dispelled (Dispel Magic DC 13).  
- **Corrupted Mages** – 3 enemy battle‑mages. Use evocation spells (Fire Bolt, Burning Hands). They are linked to the anomaly flamethrower – if it is destroyed, they lose concentration (disadvantage on spell attacks).  
- **Anomaly Flamethrower** – Mounted, 60‑ft cone, recharge 4–6. Control panel (Intelligence DC 14) can be hacked to fire on enemies.  
- **Powder Barrels** – 6 barrels near firebase. Chain explosion if ignited (20‑ft radius, 6d6 fire).  
- **Collapsing Ridge** – Supports can be destroyed (40 HP total, can be targeted). After destruction, ridge collapses in 2 rounds, blocking pass.

**Battle‑Mage Integration**  
- Counterspell flamethrowers (reaction, Arcana DC 14).  
- Stabilize ridge (Arcana DC 15) to prevent collapse for 3 rounds.  
- Create firebreak (action, Arcana DC 12) – clears burning terrain in 20‑ft radius.  

**Tactical Opportunities**  
- Hack flamethrowers to fire on enemy mages.  
- Trigger glyphs early to clear path.  
- Collapse ridge to block reinforcements.  
- Use powder barrels as traps.  
- Disable flamethrowers to reduce fire hazard.

**Council Tie‑Ins**  
- Selvara Thane wants stolen anomaly weapons (salvage flamethrower parts).  
- Marrek Voss senses necrotic residue in the flames (enemy using corrupted power).  
- Othira Quill identifies flamethrower tech as Fold‑derived.  
- Caldus Renn can use firebreak data to enhance his transmutation defenses.
