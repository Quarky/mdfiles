# Anomaly Soldier 
*Medium humanoid, Chaotic Neutral*

Armor Class: 14 (Runed armor)  
Hit Points: 32 (5d8+10)  
Speed: 30 ft

STR 14 (+2)  
DEX 16 (+3)  
CON 14 (+2)  
INT 8 (-1)  
WIS 12 (+1)  
CHA 6 (-2)

Saving Throws: Dex +5, Con +4  
Skills: Perception +3, Athletics +4  
Damage Resistances: psychic, necrotic  
Damage Immunities: poison  
Condition Immunities: charmed, frightened  
Senses: darkvision 60 ft, anomaly‑sense 60 ft  
Languages: broken Undercommon  
Challenge: 3 (700 XP)

---

## Traits

**Fold‑Warped Reflexes.**  
Once per round, when the soldier is targeted by an attack, it may teleport 5 ft to an unoccupied space. This does not provoke opportunity attacks.

**Anomaly Pulse (Recharge 5–6).**  
As a bonus action, the soldier emits a Fold surge.  
Creatures within 10 ft must succeed on a DC 13 Wis save or suffer disadvantage on their next attack roll.

**Crystal Infection.**  
When the soldier hits a creature with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a DC 12 Con save or take 1d4 necrotic damage at the start of its next turn (repeat save ends).

---

## Actions

**Multiattack.**  
The soldier makes two attacks: one Fold Blade and one Fold Bolt.

**Fold Blade.**  
Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft  
Hit: 1d8+3 slashing + 1d4 psychic

**Fold Bolt.**  
Ranged Spell Attack: +5 to hit, range 60 ft  
Hit: 1d6+3 force  
If the target is within 10 ft of another anomaly, it takes +1d4 psychic damage.

---

## Bonus Actions

**Phase Step.**  
The soldier teleports up to 10 ft to a space it can see.

---

## Reactions

**Warp Counter.**  
When a creature misses the soldier with a melee attack, the soldier may make one Fold Blade attack against that creature.

---

## Weaknesses

Cold Damage — slows teleportation; soldier cannot use Fold‑Warped Reflexes for 1 round.  
Radiant Damage — removes Crystal Infection effect immediately.
