### Ionization Shield Ritual (Solo Caster)

**Name:** Kess's Ionization Bastion

- **Casting Time:** 3 rounds per location; 5 locations in a pattern specified depending on location (Kess must spend his action each round)
- **Range:** Self-centered; creates a dome with radius 50 ft
- **Duration:** 6 hours normally; can be extended if a Booster Spell is used

**Ritual Execution (Kess alone):**
- **Round 1:** Kess inscribes a grounding rune at his feet (action). Provokes opportunity attacks. If damaged, must make Concentration check DC 10 or restart.
- **Round 2:** Kess channels arcane energy into the rune (action). Must maintain concentration; if damaged, Constitution save DC 10 or restart from Round 1.
- **Round 3:** Kess completes the linkage – a shimmering, semi-translucent dome of ionized energy expands to a 50-ft radius around him if it is the last location.

**Effect after completion:** Dome is 100 ft diameter (radius 50 ft), centered on Kess. Blocks ionic cloud and alerts to entry. No concentration required once active.

**Booster Spell:** Anomaly specialists and other specialists can create power lines to make the spell last indefinitely.

# Full Spell Reference – Details, Rolls, Common Mistakes

## Cantrips

### Blade Ward
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Self
- **Duration:** 1 round
- **Effect:** Resistance against bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from weapon attacks.
- **Rolls:** None.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it only works on weapon attacks, not spells. Using it when you're not expecting weapon damage.
- **Tip:** Cast before moving through a threatened area.

### Create Bonfire
- **School:** Conjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 ft
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 min
- **Effect:** 5-ft cube, Dex save or 2d8 fire. Any creature that enters first time on a turn also saves.
- **Rolls:** Dex save vs DC; damage 2d8 fire.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting concentration. Placing where allies must pass.
- **Tip:** Block narrow passages.

### Fire Bolt
- **School:** Evocation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 120 ft
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** Ranged spell attack, 2d10 fire. Ignites flammable objects.
- **Rolls:** Ranged spell attack (Int + prof). Damage 2d10 fire.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it can target objects.
- **Tip:** Ignite powder barrels from distance.

### Mage Hand
- **School:** Conjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 30 ft
- **Duration:** 1 min
- **Effect:** Spectral hand manipulates objects ≤10 lbs.
- **Rolls:** None.
- **Common Mistakes:** Trying to attack or carry too much.
- **Tip:** Trigger traps, retrieve items.

### Minor Illusion
- **School:** Illusion
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 30 ft
- **Duration:** 1 min
- **Effect:** Sound or image of an object within 5-ft cube.
- **Rolls:** Investigation vs DC to disbelieve if interacted with.
- **Common Mistakes:** Trying to create creatures (can't – only objects). Moving it (static).
- **Tip:** Create fake cover or decoy.

### Prestidigitation
- **School:** Transmutation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 10 ft
- **Duration:** Up to 1 hour
- **Effect:** Minor tricks: clean, soil, warm, chill, flavor, small trinket, mark.
- **Rolls:** None.
- **Common Mistakes:** Trying to deal damage.
- **Tip:** Dry wet clothes, light campfire, create distraction.

### Toll the Dead
- **School:** Necromancy
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 ft
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** Wis save. On fail, 1d8 necrotic (full HP) or 1d12 necrotic (missing any HP).
- **Rolls:** Wis save vs DC; damage 1d8 or 1d12 necrotic.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting damage die changes based on current HP.
- **Tip:** Use on wounded enemies for d12.

## 1st Level Spells

### Absorb Elements
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 reaction (when you take acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage)
- **Range:** Self
- **Duration:** 1 round
- **Effect:** Resistance to triggering damage type. Next melee attack deals extra 1d6 of that type.
- **Rolls:** None (resistance halves damage). Extra damage rolled on next hit.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it only lasts 1 round. Using it on non-elemental damage.
- **Tip:** Use against dragon breath or fireball.

### Alarm
- **School:** Abjuration (Ritual)
- **Casting Time:** 1 minute (or ritual)
- **Range:** 30 ft
- **Duration:** 8 hours
- **Effect:** Ward a 20-ft cube. Mental alert if entered.
- **Rolls:** None.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it's a ritual.
- **Tip:** Guard camp or ambush flanks.

### Burning Hands
- **School:** Evocation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Self (15-ft cone)
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** Dex save or 3d6 fire (half on success).
- **Rolls:** Dex save vs DC; damage 3d6 fire.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting cone starts from you – you may hit allies.
- **Tip:** Clear melee enemies swarming you.

### Chromatic Orb
- **School:** Evocation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 90 ft
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** Ranged spell attack, 3d8 damage (choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison, thunder).
- **Rolls:** Ranged spell attack; damage 3d8 chosen type.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting you need a diamond worth 50 gp. Forgetting to choose damage type.
- **Tip:** Bypass resistances by choosing different element.

### Magic Missile
- **School:** Evocation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 120 ft
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** 3 darts, each 1d4+1 force. Auto-hit. Can target same or different creatures.
- **Rolls:** Damage only (1d4+1 per dart). No attack roll.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it forces concentration checks (each dart is separate). Forgetting *shield* spell blocks it.
- **Tip:** Break concentration or finish multiple wounded enemies.

### Mage Armor
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Duration:** 8 hours
- **Effect:** Target's base AC becomes 13 + Dex (if not wearing armor).
- **Rolls:** None.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it doesn't stack with worn armor.
- **Tip:** Cast on squishy mages before combat.

### Protection from Evil and Good
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 min
- **Effect:** Target has disadvantage on attacks from aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, undead. Also immune to possession/charm/frighten from them.
- **Rolls:** None.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it only works on specific creature types.
- **Tip:** Use against anomaly creatures, demons, undead.

### Shield
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 reaction (when hit by attack or targeted by *magic missile*)
- **Range:** Self
- **Duration:** 1 round
- **Effect:** +5 AC until start of next turn. Immune to *Magic Missile*.
- **Rolls:** None.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it only lasts 1 round. Using it when hit by a save-based spell (doesn't help).
- **Tip:** Use when an attack would otherwise hit.

## 2nd Level Spells

### Aganazzar's Scorcher
- **School:** Evocation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 30 ft line (30 ft long, 5 ft wide)
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** Dex save or 3d8 fire (half on success).
- **Rolls:** Dex save vs DC; damage 3d8 fire.
- **Common Mistakes:** Misjudging line shape (hits all in its path).
- **Tip:** Use when enemies are in a straight line.

### Arcane Lock
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Duration:** Permanent until dispelled
- **Effect:** Locks a door, chest, or gate. Increases lockpicking DC by 10.
- **Rolls:** None.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it's a ritual (can be cast as ritual).
- **Tip:** Secure a door behind you during retreat.

### Mirror Image
- **School:** Illusion
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Self
- **Duration:** 1 min
- **Effect:** Three duplicates of yourself. When attacked, roll d20: 6+ hits duplicate (destroyed), 11+ (2 duplicates), 16+ (1 duplicate). Duplicate AC = 10 + Dex.
- **Rolls:** See effect (d20 to determine if attack hits you or duplicate).
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting duplicates don't move with you (they stay in place). Forgetting they don't help against AoE.
- **Tip:** Cast before combat to avoid being targeted.

### Scorching Ray
- **School:** Evocation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 120 ft
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** 3 rays, each ranged spell attack, 2d6 fire per ray. Can target same or different.
- **Rolls:** 3 ranged spell attacks; damage 2d6 fire each.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting each ray is a separate attack roll.
- **Tip:** Focus all three on one target to spike damage.

### See Invisibility
- **School:** Divination
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Self
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Effect:** See invisible creatures and objects as if visible. See into Ethereal Plane.
- **Rolls:** None.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it doesn't reveal hidden creatures (just invisible).
- **Tip:** Use when drow or fey are suspected.

## 3rd Level Spells

### Counterspell
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 reaction (when a creature casts a spell within 60 ft)
- **Range:** 60 ft
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** Cancel a spell. If spell level ≤ slot level, auto-succeed. Otherwise, ability check (Int + prof) DC = 10 + spell level.
- **Rolls:** Ability check (Int + prof) if spell level higher.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting you need to see the spell being cast. Forgetting the reaction timing.
- **Tip:** Always save a 3rd level slot for *Counterspell* against enemy fireballs.

### Dispel Magic
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 120 ft
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** End a spell on a creature, object, or magical effect. Roll ability check (Int + prof) DC = 10 + spell level if spell level > slot level.
- **Rolls:** Ability check (Int + prof) if spell level higher.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it can target magical traps or effects, not just spells.
- **Tip:** Use on crystal chests before they explode.

### Fireball
- **School:** Evocation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 150 ft
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Effect:** 20-ft radius sphere, Dex save or 8d6 fire (half on success). Ignites flammable objects.
- **Rolls:** Dex save vs DC; damage 8d6 fire.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting the radius is huge – allies may be caught. Forgetting it ignites powder barrels.
- **Tip:** Open with *Fireball* against clustered enemies.

### Wall of Sand
- **School:** Evocation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 90 ft
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 min
- **Effect:** Creates a 30-ft long, 10-ft high wall of swirling sand. Blocks vision. Difficult terrain. Creatures entering take 1d6 slashing damage.
- **Rolls:** None (damage on entry: 1d6 slashing).
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting it requires concentration. Forgetting it blocks vision for allies too.
- **Tip:** Block archer line of sight or create a smoke screen.

## 4th Level Spells

### Otiluke's Resilient Sphere
- **School:** Evocation
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 30 ft
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 min
- **Effect:** Encases a Large or smaller creature in a shimmering sphere of force. The sphere is immune to all damage, and the creature inside cannot be harmed from outside, but cannot attack or cast spells through it.
- **Rolls:** Dex save negates.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting the target gets a Dex save. Forgetting the sphere blocks both ways – you can trap an enemy OR protect an ally.
- **Tip:** Use to save a dying ally or trap a dangerous enemy for 1 minute.

## Additional Spell: Glyph of Warding (2014 version)

*Note: This appears in the PDF as a separate entry, likely for Kess's special uses.*

- **School:** Abjuration
- **Level:** 3rd
- **Casting Time:** 1 hour
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (incense and powdered diamond worth at least 200 gp, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Until dispelled or triggered
- **Classes:** Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Wizard

**Description:**
When you cast this spell, you inscribe a glyph that harms other creatures, either upon a surface (such as a table or a section of floor or wall) or within an object that can be closed (such as a book, a scroll, or a treasure chest) to conceal the glyph. If you choose a surface, the glyph can cover an area of the surface no larger than 10 feet in diameter. If you choose an object, that object must remain in its place; if the object is moved more than 10 feet from where you cast this spell, the glyph is broken and the spell ends without being triggered.

The glyph is nearly invisible and requires a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC to be found.

You can further refine the trigger so the spell activates only under certain circumstances or according to physical characteristics (such as height or weight), creature kind (for example, the ward could be set to affect aberrations or drow), or alignment. You can also set conditions for creatures that don't trigger the glyph, such as those who say a certain password.

When you inscribe the glyph, choose **explosive runes** or a **spell glyph**.

- **Explosive Runes:** When triggered, the glyph erupts with magical energy in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on the glyph. The sphere spreads around corners. Each creature in the aura must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 5d8 acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage on a failed saving throw (your choice when you create the glyph), or half as much damage on a successful one.

- **Spell Glyph:** You can store a prepared spell of 3rd level or lower in the glyph by casting it as part of creating the glyph. The spell must target a single creature or an area. The spell being stored has no immediate effect when cast in this way. When the glyph is triggered, the stored spell is cast. If the spell has a target, it targets the creature that triggered the glyph. If the spell affects an area, the area is centered on that creature.
