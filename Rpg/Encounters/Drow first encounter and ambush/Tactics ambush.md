# Tactics – The’lana Convoy Ambush

## 🛡️ General Convoy Doctrine
- The commander’s priority is protecting the crystal chests. If overwhelmed, he orders destruction of cargo rather than capture.
- Guards never leave the immediate vicinity of the wagons unless pursuing a direct threat.
- If the signal horn sounds, reinforcements arrive in 5 rounds.
- The convoy moves in single file; stopping the lead wagon halts all.

---

## 👥 The’lana Guards (12 total, in 4 squads of 3)

**Role:** Line infantry, protect cargo, suppress ambushers.

**Tactics**
- Form a perimeter around the wagons at all times.
- If ambushed from range, take cover behind wagons (three‑quarters cover) and return fire with heavy crossbows.
- If engaged in melee, two guards focus on the attacker while the third calls for help and moves to protect the nearest crystal chest.
- When a powder barrel is threatened, one guard moves to secure it (interpose body, cover action).
- If the commander gives a Command Word, one guard immediately takes an extra action (typically to fire a crossbow or throw a powder barrel).

**Retreat / Morale**
- Fight to the death if the commander is alive.
- If commander dies, attempt to flee toward the camp with any remaining crystals.

---

## 🎩 Drow Commander

**Role:** Leader, morale anchor, last‑line defender.

**Tactics**
- Stays mounted on horseback for mobility and elevation.
- Uses the horse to reposition rapidly, targeting the most dangerous PC (spellcaster or heavy damage dealer).
- Keeps one guard squad in reserve near his position.
- If the convoy is clearly lost, he:
  - Rides to the wagon with the largest crystal cache.
  - Shouts: *“For The’lana – better ash than tribute!”*
  - Uses an action to ignite a powder barrel with a lit torch or fire spell.
  - The explosion deals 4d6 fire in 15‑ft radius; he does not attempt to escape.
- Before death, he may throw his signet ring to a guard with orders to deliver it to The’lana (narrative hook).

**Mount Tactics**
- Horse can trample (single target, +4 to hit, 2d4+2 bludgeoning).
- If the horse is killed, the commander fights on foot with rapier and crossbow.

---

## 🔮 The’lana Battle‑Mages (2)

**Role:** Defensive casters, counterspelling, area denial.

**Tactics**
- Stay inside the middle wagon (canvas provides cover, can see out).
- Priority spells:
  1. **Shield** if targeted by ranged attack.
  2. **Absorb Elements** if caught in fire/lightning AoE.
  3. **Web** to block the road behind the convoy (slows pursuit) or on an ambush position.
  4. **Magic Missile** to finish a wounded PC or to force concentration checks.
- **Mirror Image** is cast before combat if they suspect an ambush (active at start).
- Protect the cargo: use **Protect Cargo** reaction when a crystal chest or powder barrel is directly targeted.
- If the commander is killed, they attempt to flee with one chest of crystals (Dimension Door? They don’t have it – they’ll run on foot).

**Spell Use Priority**
| Situation | Spell |
|-----------|-------|
| Ambush starts (if they have 1 round prep) | Mirror Image on self |
| PC archer or caster lining up a shot | Shield |
| Fireball or elemental AoE incoming | Absorb Elements |
| Need to block pursuit or pin down melee fighters | Web on road behind convoy or on ambush point |
| Need guaranteed damage to finish a threat | Magic Missile |

---

## 🚚 Wagons & Drivers

**Role:** Mobile cover, explosive hazards, blocking terrain.

**Tactics**
- Drivers (commoners, not statted) will urge horses forward if threatened, not fight.
- If a driver is killed, the wagon stops (horses may wander).
- Guards can take the reins as an action.
- Wagons can be used as mobile cover (half cover when standing behind).
- Turning a wagon sideways creates a barricade (takes 2 actions).

**Powder Barrels**
- Any fire damage within 5 ft ignites a barrel.
- A barrel can be thrown (action) 10 ft by a creature with STR 14+ (improvised weapon, range 10/20, on hit 2d6 bludgeoning + ignites on next turn).
- Chain reaction: if one barrel explodes, all barrels within 15 ft also explode (cumulative damage).

**Crystal Chests**
- If a chest is opened (Thieves’ Tools DC 15), crystals can be removed safely.
- If chest is damaged by fire or force, it explodes (20‑ft radius pulse, Con save DC 14 or stunned 1 round, 2d6 force).
- Crystals can be used as unstable grenades: as an action, smash a crystal on a hard surface – 10‑ft radius, 2d6 force, Dex DC 12 half.

---

## 🐎 Draft Horses

**Role:** Wagon propulsion, potential stampede.

**Tactics**
- Do not attack unless cornered (then kick: +3 to hit, 2d4+1 bludgeoning).
- If frightened (by fire, thunder, or a spell), they bolt.
- Bolting horses move 40 ft in a random direction (roll 1d8). The wagon follows.
- A bolting wagon crashes into anything in its path (4d6 bludgeoning to creatures/objects struck, wagon takes 2d6 damage).

---

## 🗡️ Vreldarr Scouts (Optional Allies)

**Role:** Flanking support, emergency extraction.

**Tactics (if they join the fight)**
- Stay hidden until the party is losing (e.g., one PC down or mage wounded).
- Attack from the rear of the convoy using Cunning Action to hide after each shot.
- Target the battle‑mages first.
- If a party member is down, one scout will attempt to drag them to cover (use Help action for death saves).
- They will not sacrifice themselves; if the fight turns decisively against the party, they retreat and report to Veldrin.

---

## 🧝 Veldrin & Urthak (Not present at convoy – but if engaged)

**Role:** Distraction, psychological warfare (if he appears).

**Tactics (if he chose to observe or intervene)**
- Veldrin does not fight openly. He uses Darkness on himself, then rides Urthak to cause chaos.
- Urthak’s displacement makes him hard to hit; he targets isolated guards or attempts to drag a crystal chest into the darkness.
- Veldrin uses Invisibility to escape if threatened.
- He will not risk his life for the party – this is their job.

---

## 🔥 Reinforcements from Camp (if alarm triggers)

**Arrival:** 5 rounds after horn sounds or rider escapes.

**Force:** 2 squads of anomaly soldiers (3 each), 1 anomaly caster.

**Tactics**
- Squads advance in a line, using cover.
- Target the party’s position, not the wagons.
- Anomaly caster stays behind, uses **Anomaly Bolt** on the most dangerous PC.
- If the party has already seized crystals, the reinforcements prioritize retrieving or destroying them.

---

## ⚔️ Encounter Flow Summary

| Phase            | Action                                                                                                     |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ambush Setup** | Party chooses positions (bluffs, creek bed, farmhouse).                                                    |
| **Round 1**      | Party attacks from ambush (surprise likely). Commander shouts, guards take cover, mages cast Mirror Image. |
| **Rounds 2–3**   | Guards return fire, mages cast Web/Magic Missile, wagons try to move.                                      |
| **Round 4+**     | Commander may ignite powder barrels if losing. Reinforcements arrive if alarm triggered.                   |
| **End**          | Victory: party seizes/destroys crystals. Defeat: party retreats, Vreldarr withdraws offer.                 |

---

This tactics guide assumes the party initiates the ambush. Adjust if the convoy is alerted beforehand (e.g., if the party failed Stealth checks).
