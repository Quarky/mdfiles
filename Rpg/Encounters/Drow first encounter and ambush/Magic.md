## 📖 Spell Descriptions (GM Quick Reference)

**Absorb Elements (1st level)** – When you take acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage, gain resistance to that damage. Next melee attack deals +1d6 of that type.

**Anomaly Bolt (cantrip)** – Ranged spell attack, 2d8 force damage. Scales with character level (additional 1d8 at 5th, 11th, 17th).

**Darkness (2nd level)** – 15‑ft radius sphere of magical darkness. Nonmagical light cannot illuminate. Darkvision doesn’t work.

**Fire Bolt (cantrip)** – Ranged spell attack, 1d10 fire damage. Can ignite flammable objects.

**Invisibility (2nd level)** – Target becomes invisible. Ends if target attacks or casts a spell.

**Magic Missile (1st level)** – 3 darts of force, each deals 1d4+1. Auto‑hits. Can target one creature or several.

**Mage Hand (cantrip)** – Spectral hand can manipulate objects, open doors, pour liquids, etc. Cannot attack.

**Mirror Image (2nd level)** – 3 duplicates. When targeted by an attack, roll d20; 6+ (3 duplicates) / 8+ (2) / 11+ (1) redirects to duplicate. Duplicate AC = 10 + Dex.

**Prestidigitation (cantrip)** – Minor magical effects: clean, soil, warm, chill, flavor, create small trinket or mark.

**Shield (1st level)** – Reaction to being hit. +5 AC until start of next turn. Immune to Magic Missile.

**Web (2nd level)** – 20‑ft cube of sticky webs. Creatures entering or starting in area must make Dex save or be restrained. Flammable (2d4 fire if burned).
