# NPC Descriptions – The’lana Convoy Ambush

---

## 🧝 Veldrin Vreldarr – The Sharran Shadow

**Appearance:** Veldrin is tall even by drow standards, his silver hair pulled into a tight knot. His skin is the color of bruised twilight, and his eyes hold no reflection – just two voids that drink the lantern light. He wears a high-collared coat of black leather, stitched with faint silver runes that whisper when he moves. A jagged scar runs from his left jaw to his collarbone, a souvenir from the house that tried to absorb his own.

**Demeanor:** He does not fidget. He does not blink often. When he speaks, it is soft, measured, as if each word is a coin he is reluctant to spend. He shows no fear, no anger – only cold calculation.

**Voice:** Low, unhurried, with a faint rasp. He never raises his voice.

**Quote:** *“I don’t follow the idiot Spider Queen. Nor do I bow to her dancing daughter. Their songs are pretty lies. I walk in Shar’s silence.”*

---

## 🐾 Urthak – The Displacer Beast

**Appearance:** Urthak is larger than most of its kind – six long legs, muscular haunches, and a pair of sinewy tentacles sprouting from its shoulders, each tipped with a bony barb. Its fur is a deep, shifting blue-black that seems to drink light. It does not purr or growl. It watches.

**Demeanor:** Silent, patient, and utterly still unless commanded. When Urthak moves, reality stutters – an afterimage trails half a second behind, making it impossible to track. It never breaks eye contact with whoever is speaking.

**Voice:** None. It communicates through posture and Veldrin’s cues.

**Quote (Veldrin):** *“He likes you. That’s rare.”*

---

## 🎩 Drow Commander – The’lana Noble

**Appearance:** This drow wears a finely crafted breastplate over dark silk robes, with a crimson sash denoting his House rank. His white hair is swept back, held by a silver clasp shaped like a spider. A signet ring glitters on his left hand. His face is sharp, aristocratic, with thin lips and eyes that glitter with arrogance.

**Demeanor:** He holds himself with the practiced authority of a noble who has never known real danger. His voice is clipped, impatient. He treats his guards as expendable tools. When the ambush begins, his arrogance cracks into desperate fury.

**Voice:** Sharp, commanding, with a haughty drawl. He shouts orders in a high, carrying tone.

**Quote:** *“You think you can steal from The’lana? Burn it all! Better ash than tribute!”*

---

## 🗡️ The’lana Guards – Anomaly‑Trained Soldiers

**Appearance:** These drow wear chain shirts over dark padded cloth, with steel helmets that leave only their eyes visible. Their shields bear the The’lana sigil – a stylized web encircling a crystal. Each carries a longsword at the hip and a heavy crossbow slung across the back. Their faces are identical in their blank, disciplined stillness.

**Demeanor:** They move in perfect synchronization, reacting to commands without hesitation. They do not flinch at explosions or death. They are not fanatics – they are professionals. They will follow the commander’s orders even to their own destruction.

**Voice:** Rarely speak. When they do, it is short, clipped, and deferential (*“Yes, Commander.”*).

**Quote:** *“Protect the chests!”*

---

## 🔮 The’lana Battle‑Mages

**Appearance:** These two drow wear dark robes embroidered with arcane sigils that glow faintly blue. Their heads are shaved, showing tattoos of The’lana’s web‑and‑crystal crest. They carry no weapons – only a spell component pouch and a small focus rod of polished obsidian.

**Demeanor:** Nervous, watchful, and highly alert. They sense magic and are quick to raise shields. Unlike the guards, they show fear – they know what the crystals can do if mishandled.

**Voice:** Sibilant, hurried, often speaking to each other in arcane shorthand.

**Quote:** *“Shield the wagon – they’re targeting the cargo!”*

---

## 🚚 Wagon Drivers (Commoners)

**Appearance:** Two scruffy drow commoners per heavy wagon, wearing simple tunics and leather vests. Their faces are weathered, eyes squinting against the mist. They carry no visible weapons – only a whip and a small knife.

**Demeanor:** Terrified. They are not soldiers. They will obey the commander but will flee at the first real opportunity.

**Voice:** Hoarse, stammering. They plead, curse, and pray under their breath.

**Quote:** *“Don’t kill me – I just drive the cart!”*

---

## 🐎 Draft Horses

**Appearance:** Heavy, shaggy beasts, dark grey with matted manes. Their breath steams in the cold air. They wear leather harnesses connected to the wagons by thick iron chains.

**Demeanor:** Skittish, especially near fire or loud noises. They will bolt if frightened.

**Voice:** Snorts, whinnies, and the pounding of hooves.

**Quote:** None.

---

## 🛡️ Vreldarr Scouts (Optional Allies)

**Appearance:** Two lean drow in dark grey cloaks that blend with the rocks and mist. They wear no armor – only padded leather and a hood. Each carries a shortsword and a shortbow. Their faces are half‑hidden by scarves, only their eyes visible – sharp, calculating, and amused.

**Demeanor:** Confident, almost cocky. They move like predators, never staying in one place for more than a few seconds. They speak in low, teasing tones.

**Voice:** Whispered, sardonic, often making dark jokes.

**Quote:** *“Took you long enough. Veldrin said you’d need help. He didn’t say you’d make a mess of it.”*

---

## 🔥 Reinforcement Anomaly Caster

**Appearance:** A drow in patchwork robes, their face half‑withered by prolonged exposure to anomaly crystals. One eye glows faintly blue. Their fingers are stained black. They carry a cracked crystal focus on a chain.

**Demeanor:** Unstable, muttering to themselves. They are addicted to the anomaly’s power and will risk anything to get more crystals.

**Voice:** Raspy, disjointed, sometimes speaking in two different tones.

**Quote:** *“More crystals… more… I can feel them calling…”*

---

## 🐎 Reinforcement Anomaly Soldiers (2 squads of 3)

**Appearance:** Similar to the The’lana guards, but their armor is pitted and cracked, and faint blue veins of crystal growth creep up their exposed necks. Their eyes are glassy, unfocused, as if they are not entirely present.

**Demeanor:** They move with unnatural coordination, like puppets on unseen strings. They do not speak. They do not retreat.

**Voice:** Silence, save for the soft hum of the crystals embedded in their gear.

**Quote:** None – only the hum of anomaly energy.
