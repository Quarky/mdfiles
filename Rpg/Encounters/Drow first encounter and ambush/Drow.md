# Drow Trade Control – Global Context

Surface resources are scarce across the world. The drow collectively control the underground – the Underdark tunnels, mines, fungal forests, and deep caverns that produce rare ores, crystals, reagents, and other materials that cannot be found on the surface. This gives them a global monopoly.

**The’lana** is not the only drow trade house, but they are the dominant house in **this region** (around Veyrun’s Reach and Pacthold). They manage local contracts, trade routes, and resource allocation.

**House Vreldarr** (Second Ring, mercenary) wants to injure The’lana’s local control to create an opening for their own rise.

---

## 🧝 Veldrin Vreldarr – The Sharran Shadow

Veldrin is tall even by drow standards, his silver hair pulled into a tight knot. His skin is the color of bruised twilight, and his eyes hold no reflection – just two voids that drink the lantern light. He wears a high-collared coat of black leather, stitched with faint silver runes that whisper when he moves. A jagged scar runs from his left jaw to his collarbone, a souvenir from the house that tried to absorb his own.

He does not fidget. He does not blink often. When he speaks, it is soft, measured, as if each word is a coin he is reluctant to spend.

> *“I don’t follow the idiot Spider Queen. Nor do I bow to her dancing daughter. Their songs are pretty lies. I walk in Shar’s silence.”*

> *“The’lana pretends they own this area. They’ve pulled every local resource into their web. You can’t trade here without their permission. I want you to cut a thread.”*

He pauses, stroking Urthak’s tentacle.

> *“But that is not the only reason I approached you. House Vi’shen has marked you. The bio‑engineers. They deal in living metal, flesh-shaping, crystals that infect armor. They see you as... specimens. You will need an ally who knows their methods. I offer you that.”*

> *“Help me wound The’lana. In return, I give you warning of Vi’shen’s movements, and a place to hide when they come hunting. Vreldarr does not forget its debts.”*

---

## 🐾 Urthak – The Displacer Beast

Urthak is larger than most of its kind – six long legs, muscular haunches, and a pair of sinewy tentacles sprouting from its shoulders, each tipped with a bony barb. Its fur is a deep, shifting blue-black that seems to drink light. It does not purr or growl. It watches.

When Urthak moves, reality stutters. An afterimage trails half a second behind, making it impossible to track. It never breaks eye contact with whoever is speaking. Veldrin calls it *“my shadow’s shadow.”*

The beast rests its massive head on its master’s knee. Veldrin strokes its tentacle absently.

> *“He likes you. That’s rare.”*

---

## 🤝 The Deal

**Target:** The’lana supply convoy carrying anomaly‑weave crystals.  
**Objective:** Sabotage it – destroy cargo, kill key couriers, or delay delivery.  
**Reward:**  
- Patrol map of the camp (bypass one encounter or gain advantage on Stealth checks)  
- Sigil of Shar (one‑use advantage on a Stealth or Deception check)  
- Safe passage through Vreldarr-patrolled zones  
- Warning and protection against House Vi’shen (intel on their operations, safe house if needed)

## 🗣️ Veldrin Describes the Mission

Veldrin crouches beside Urthak, one hand idly stroking the beast’s tentacle. His voice is low, unhurried – each word placed like a dagger in a sheath.

> *“Three days from now, a The’lana convoy moves through the eastern pass. They carry anomaly‑weave crystals. You’ve seen what those stones do – they anchor the storm, they feed the machine in the cave. Without them, The’lana’s contracts falter. Their local grip loosens.”*

He gestures toward the misty hills.

> *“The convoy rolls at dawn. Six wagons, heavy with powder and cargo. Twelve guards, two mages, a drow commander who thinks he is clever. They hug the sunken road – easy ground to ambush, if you know where to wait.”*

He traces a line in the dirt.

> *“Here. The bend at the old farmhouse. The road narrows, the bluff blocks their view. You strike there.”*

He looks up, eyes void-dark.

> *“I do not ask you to kill everyone. I ask you to wound. Burn the crystals. Sink the wagons. Send a message that The’lana cannot protect their own goods. The rest – the guards, the mages, the commander – they are your problem. I only care that The’lana bleeds.”*

He leans back, Urthak’s tentacle curling around his wrist.

> *“Do this, and I give you the patrol routes to the enemy camp – a way to slip past their eyes. A Sigil of Shar to cloak your passage. And when Vi’shen comes sniffing for you, Vreldarr will know. We will give you warning. A place to hide.”*

A pause.

> *“You are not fighting for me. You are fighting for your own survival. Vi’shen will hunt you. I offer a chance to strike first.”*
