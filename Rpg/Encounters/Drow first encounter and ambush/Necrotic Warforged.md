# Necrotic Warforged Hybrid (Limb‑Powered Variant)
*Large Construct (Undead), Chaotic Evil*

**Armor Class** 18 (composite plating)  
**Hit Points** 165 (22d10 + 44)  
**Speed** 30 ft

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 20 (+5) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 8 (−1) |

**Saving Throws** Con +5, Wis +4  
**Skills** Perception +4, Arcana +3  
**Damage Resistances** necrotic; cold; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** poisoned, charmed, exhaustion  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14  
**Languages** understands Common and Infernal but cannot speak  
**Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP)

---

## Limb‑Core Power Cycle
When the hybrid is below half its hit points, its unstable limb‑cores begin to overload.  
At the start of each of its turns, roll 1d4:

1. **Left Arm Surge** — 20‑ft radius necrotic pulse.  
   *Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 Con save or take 4d8 necrotic damage.*

2. **Right Arm Arc Burst** — 30‑ft line of lightning.  
   *Creatures in the line must make a DC 15 Dex save or take 4d10 lightning damage.*

3. **Left Leg Shockwave** — Ground quake.  
   *Creatures within 10 ft must succeed on a DC 14 Str save or be knocked prone.*

4. **Right Leg Overload** — Speed doubles for 1 round.  
   *The hybrid’s next attack deals an extra 2d8 force damage.*

---

## Actions

### Multiattack
The hybrid makes **two limb attacks**.

### Necrotic Claw
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage.

### Arcane Pulse
*Ranged Spell Attack:* +6 to hit, range 30 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 18 (4d8) force damage.

### Soul‑Drain (Recharge 5–6)
One creature within 10 ft must make a DC 15 Con save.  
*On a failure:* the creature takes 21 (6d6) necrotic damage, and the hybrid regains hit points equal to half the damage dealt.

---

## Reactions

### Energy Redirect
When the hybrid takes damage from a spell, it redirects half the energy into its opposite limb‑core (green ↔ blue).  
Its next attack deals that damage type.

---

## Traits

### Distributed Power System
The hybrid has **no central core**.  
Critical hits do not deal extra damage to it.

### Unstable Limb Reactors
If a limb is targeted specifically (called shot, DM discretion), damaging it by 15+ in a single hit disables that limb’s overload effect for 1d4 rounds.
