
# Encounter 4: Phase 1 – The Silent Perimeter (Camp Entry)

**Scale:** 500 ft wide × 400 ft deep (100×80 grid at 5 ft per square)  
**Elevation:** Camp ground at +0 ft; palisade 20 ft high; cave mouth at north end  
**The Silence:** Illusion‑masked undead patrol, emotion‑sensing wards, silent alarm totems

**Terrain Zones**  
- **Outer Perimeter (0–100 ft)** – Palisade wall with gate (closed). Scattered supply crates (cover). Two watchtowers (25 ft high) with archers.  
- **Camp Yard (100–300 ft)** – Tents, powder barrels, wagon. Illusion‑masked undead (appear as living soldiers) patrol in pairs. Four silent alarm totems (stone pillars, 8 ft high) at corners.  
- **Ritual Circle (250 ft)** – A 30‑ft diameter circle of black stone, etched with necrotic runes. A cave mouth (north) exhales necrotic fog (20‑ft radius, heavily obscured, 1d6 necrotic per round inside).  
- **Cave Mouth (300–400 ft)** – 30 ft wide, reinforced with struts. Necrotic fog drifts out. Two undead guards at entrance.  
- **Emotion‑Sensing Wards** – Invisible fields covering key areas. Trigger if any creature feels fear (Wisdom save DC 12 or alarm). Lysaen can suppress wards (action, Arcana DC 14).

**Interactive Elements**  
- **Silent Alarm Totems** – 4 totems. Each has AC 15, HP 15. If not disabled, they trigger reinforcements when undead are killed. Disable by: crushing the rune (melee), dispelling (Dispel Magic DC 13), or using a battle‑mage’s jam ability.  
- **Illusion‑Masked Undead** – 8 undead (use zombie stats). Appear as normal soldiers (Investigation DC 16 to see through illusion). Vaelira can unravel the illusion (action, automatically reveals all undead).  
- **Necrotic Fog** – At cave mouth. Heavily obscured. Creatures inside take 1d6 necrotic per round. Marrek Voss can temporarily clear a 20‑ft radius (action, Religion DC 12).  
- **Ritual Circle** – Black stone. Can be disrupted (Religion DC 15) to weaken undead (disadvantage on attacks for 1 minute). Lysaen can invert the circle to frighten enemies instead.  
- **Cave Mouth Guards** – 2 undead (use ghoul stats). They do not leave the fog. If the fog is cleared, they become vulnerable.

**Battle‑Mage Integration**  
- Dispel illusions (Vaelira – automatically reveals undead).  
- Jam alarm totems (action, Arcana DC 12 – disables one).  
- Create silence bubble (Lysaen – 20‑ft radius, blocks totem alarms for 1 round).  

**Tactical Opportunities**  
- Disable totems before engaging undead.  
- Unravel illusions to identify real threats.  
- Clear necrotic fog to approach cave safely.  
- Disrupt ritual circle to debuff undead.  
- Use emotion‑sensing wards as traps (trigger fear to activate).

**Council Tie‑Ins**  
- Marrek Voss recognizes undead as recently fallen city defenders.  
- Vaelira Myrr identifies enemy sigils from unraveled illusions.  
- Iriandel Vos sees a future‑echo of cave collapse (warning).  
- Lysaen Corvell notes that the wards react to fear – his enchantments could counter them.
# Encounter 5: Phase 2 – The Necrotic Siphon (Camp Interior)

**Scale:** 400 ft wide × 500 ft deep (80×100 grid at 5 ft per square)  
**Elevation:** Ground level; siphon tower 30 ft high; ritual circle at base  
**The Siphon:** Rotating beams, necrotic geysers, anomaly‑infused undead

**Terrain Zones**  
- **Siphon Tower** – 30 ft high stone tower at center, 40 ft diameter. Four rotating beams emit from top (line attacks). Stairs spiral up interior.  
- **Ritual Circle** – 50‑ft diameter around tower base, etched with runes. Four necrotic geysers at cardinal points (10‑ft diameter pools of bubbling black liquid).  
- **Undead Patrol** – Anomaly‑infused undead (8) with shifting resistances (change each round: roll 1d4 for resistance type: fire, cold, lightning, necrotic).  
- **Geyser Field** – Each geyser erupts every 2 rounds (20‑ft radius, Dex save DC 13, 3d6 necrotic). Can be blocked by battle‑mages (Arcana DC 14 to stabilize for 1 round).  
- **Siphon Beams** – Four rotating beams (each covers a 90‑degree arc). Deal 2d8 necrotic to any creature in the beam (no save). Can be disrupted by damaging the tower’s focusing crystals (AC 15, HP 15 each).

**Interactive Elements**  
- **Focusing Crystals** – 4 crystals on tower top. Destroying a crystal disables one beam. Each destroyed crystal destabilizes the siphon (increases geyser frequency).  
- **Ritual Circle Runes** – Can be disrupted (Religion DC 16) to weaken all undead (half HP). Marrek Voss can invert the circle (Arcana DC 18) to heal allies instead of harming them.  
- **Necrotic Geysers** – Can be capped (Strength DC 18) or dispelled (Dispel Magic DC 14). Battle‑mages can channel geyser energy into a single devastating blast (30‑ft cone, 6d6 necrotic, allies immune).  
- **Anomaly Undead** – Resistances shift each round. Battle‑mages can identify the current resistance (Arcana DC 12, free action).  
- **Siphon Core** – Inside tower base. Can be destroyed (deals 40 damage) or hijacked (Intelligence DC 16 to reverse polarity – heals allies instead of harming them for 1 round).

**Battle‑Mage Integration**  
- Invert siphon to weaken enemies (Marrek – action, Religion DC 15).  
- Stabilize ritual circle for PC use (Lysaen – creates safe zone 20‑ft radius).  
- Channel geyser into blast (Othira – once per encounter).  

**Tactical Opportunities**  
- Destroy focusing crystals to disable beams.  
- Invert siphon to heal party.  
- Cap geysers to remove hazards.  
- Use shifting resistances to your advantage (identify then exploit weakness).  
- Hijack siphon core for massive area heal.

**Council Tie‑Ins**  
- Marrek Voss learns siphon is not enemy‑made (it’s Fold‑adjacent tech).  
- Othira Quill identifies siphon as ancient Fold artifact.  
- Selvara Thane wants to weaponize siphon energy.  
- Iriandel Vos sees that siphon draws power from something deeper.
