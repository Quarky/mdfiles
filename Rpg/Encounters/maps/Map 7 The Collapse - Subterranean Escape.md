# Map 7: The Collapse - Subterranean Escape

**Scale:** 1,200 ft long × 40 ft wide (240x8 grid), plus side tunnels and chambers
**Elevation:** Descends -20 ft then rises +30 ft to exit; multiple levels

## Terrain Zones

**Main Tunnel (0-400 ft)** - 20 ft wide, 15 ft high. Wooden support beams every 30 ft. Floor is damp rock (normal movement). Intermittent ceiling cracks drip water.

**Collapse Zone (400-600 ft)** - Ceiling partially collapsed. Rubble piles (difficult terrain). Two large boulders block path (can be climbed over - Athletics DC 12). A narrow crawl space (3 ft high) bypasses boulders (requires Squeeze, 2 rounds).

**Side Cavern (500 ft, east)** - 40 ft × 40 ft, 30 ft high. Contains a pool of charged water (20 ft diameter) and a wounded anomaly soldier. Glowing crystals on walls (dim light).

**Lower Level (600-800 ft)** - Tunnel splits into two parallel passages, 10 ft apart, with a central pillar. Both passages have steep steps (difficult terrain). One passage has a hidden pit trap (Perception DC 14, 15 ft deep, 2d6 falling).

**Waterfall Shaft (800-1,000 ft)** - A 10 ft wide shaft with water cascading down from ceiling (creates difficult terrain, noise imposes disadvantage on Perception). A wooden ladder (slick, Acrobatics DC 12 to climb) leads up 30 ft to a side ledge.

**Final Ascent (1,000-1,200 ft)** - Narrow tunnel (10 ft wide) sloping upward. Loose gravel (difficult terrain). At the end, a metal grate exit. Beyond: starlight.

## Interactive Elements

**Support Beams** - Can be collapsed (Strength DC 18) to block tunnel behind party (takes 1 round). Collapsing a beam causes a small cave-in (10-ft radius, 2d6 bludgeoning).

**Rubble Piles** - Can be cleared (Athletics DC 14, 2 rounds) to open path. Alternatively, can be used as cover (half cover).

**Charged Pool** - In side cavern. Conducts electricity. Any lightning spell or effect in pool causes 2d6 to all creatures in pool.

**Wounded Soldier** - Hiding in side cavern. Will attack if approached. Has a grenade (can be taken on successful Sleight of Hand vs Perception).

**Pit Trap** - Covered with thin wood planks and dirt. Can be triggered by stepping on it (Perception DC 14 to avoid). Pit has spikes (3d6 piercing).

**Waterfall Ledge** - At top of ladder, a small alcove (10 ft × 10 ft) contains a supply cache: 2 glow rods, 1 healing potion, rope.

**Metal Grate** - Exit. Locked (Thieves' Tools DC 12). Lever on inside (hidden, Perception DC 14) unlocks silently. Can be forced (Strength DC 20, loud).

## Tactical Opportunities

- Collapse support beams to block pursuers.
- Use rubble as cover during ambush.
- Lure enemies into pit trap.
- Use charged pool as trap (cast lightning when enemy enters).
- Climb waterfall shaft to side alcove for supplies.
- Use grenade from soldier to clear rubble or damage pursuers.

## Hazards

- Falling rocks (random, DC 12 Dex or 1d6).
- Pit trap (3d6 piercing).
- Slick ladder (fall 30 ft, 3d6 bludgeoning).
- Charged pool (2d6 lightning).
- Grate exit may be guarded (GM discretion).
- Soldier ambush in side cavern.
