# Map 6: The Stabilizer Chamber - Reality Warp Core

**Scale:** 400 ft wide × 350 ft deep (80x70 grid)
**Ceiling height:** 100 ft with hanging crystalline formations; lower stalactites at 30-50 ft

## Terrain Zones

**Central Machine** - 50 ft × 50 ft lattice, 30 ft high. Pulsing blue-white core. Four anchoring rods at corners, each 15 ft high. Machine emits low hum (Perception disadvantage within 30 ft).

**Crystalline Vein Network** - Crisscrosses entire floor in a web pattern. Veins are 2 ft wide, raised 1 ft. Stepping on a vein deals 1d6 lightning. Touching two veins simultaneously deals 2d6.

**Charged Pools** - Four pools of water (15 ft diameter) at corners. Water shimmers. Any lightning damage to pool causes 5-ft radius burst (2d6) and electrifies pool for 1 round (1d6 to anyone inside).

**Elevated Platforms** - Two platforms at north and south, 15 ft high, 30 ft × 30 ft. Stairs and ladders. Provide elevation and half cover. Each has a control panel.

**Stalactite Field** - Ceiling covered with 5-10 ft long stalactites. Any explosion or thunder damage causes 2d6 falling bludgeoning (Dex DC 14) in a 20-ft radius.

**Shielded Alcove** - West wall, 20 ft × 20 ft, protected by energy shield (AC 15, HP 30). Inside: droid operator and backup data core.

**Crystal Clusters** - Four large crystal formations (10 ft high) at midpoints. Each can be shattered (20 HP) to release a 10-ft radius lightning burst (3d6). Shattering one destabilizes machine (increases pulse frequency).

## Interactive Elements

**Lead Technician** - At central console (25,25). Has shutdown key (device). Fanatic; will use key if persuaded (DC 18 Persuasion) or forced (grapple + Intimidate). Also has a self-destruct trigger (countdown 5 rounds).

**Anchoring Rods** - Each has a control panel. Can be used to:
- Overload rod (Arcana DC 16) - machine pulse (15-ft radius, 4d6 force + stun).
- Divert power (Intelligence DC 14) - shuts down one turret (if present) for 1 round.
- Initiate safe shutdown (requires all four) - 10-minute ritual.

**Data Core** - Behind central console. Extract (Sleight of Hand DC 15) for schematics, drow logs, and shutdown codes.

**Crystal Clusters** - Can be shattered for area damage, but each shatter increases machine instability. After two shatters, machine pulses every round. After three, overload countdown begins.

**Covering Technicians** - Two at (10,5). Will surrender and can perform shutdown ritual (10 minutes) if protected. Know machine's weak points.

**Energy Shield Generator** - In alcove. Can be disabled (Intelligence DC 16) to access droid and backup core.

**Droid Operator** - In alcove. Will fight if alcove breached. Has a data spike (can be used to hack machine). Destroying it triggers alarm.

## Tactical Opportunities

- Use control panels to safely disable machine.
- Shatter crystals to damage enemies (but risk instability).
- Lure enemies onto veins or into pools.
- Extract data core for mission intel.
- Use elevated platforms for cover and ranged advantage.
- Force technician to use shutdown key or self-destruct.

## Hazards

- Machine pulse (stun + damage).
- Vein and pool electrocution.
- Stalactite collapse.
- Crystal shatter cascade.
- Self-destruct countdown (5 rounds to escape).
