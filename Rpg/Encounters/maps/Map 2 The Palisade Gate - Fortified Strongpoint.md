# Map 2: The Palisade Gate - Fortified Strongpoint

**Scale:** 500 ft wide × 400 ft deep (100x80 grid at 5 ft per square)
**Elevation:** Approach slopes upward (+10 ft); walls 20 ft high; two internal elevation levels

## Terrain Zones

**Killing Field (0-150 ft)** - Open mudflat, scattered caltrops and fougasse pits (covered, Perception DC 14 to spot). Deep mud (difficult terrain). Wooden stakes (sharpened, 3 ft high) create obstacles (Dex DC 10 to avoid 1d4 piercing).

**Gate Approach (150-250 ft)** - A cobblestone road, 20 ft wide, raised 2 ft above mud. Provides normal movement. Flanked by two ballistae emplacements (stone half-walls) on small mounds (10 ft diameter, +5 ft elevation).

**Palisade Wall (250-300 ft)** - Double-layered wooden wall, 20 ft high, with a fighting platform behind the outer layer. Arrow slits every 10 ft (three-quarters cover). Two watchtowers (30 ft high) at corners.

**Gatehouse (center)** - 30 ft wide, 40 ft deep, two-story. Ground floor: heavy portcullis (iron, can be raised/lowered via winch inside). Upper floor: archer positions, murder holes (drop boiling oil - 10-ft cone, 3d6 fire, Dex DC 14 half).

**Internal Courtyard (300-500 ft)** - 200 ft wide × 200 ft deep. Cobblestone near gate, dirt beyond. Three elevation tiers: +0 ft (gate area), +5 ft (supply yard), +10 ft (command ridge base). Stone walls separate tiers, with stairs and ramps.

## Interactive Elements

**Ballistae** - Two at gate approach. Each can be crewed (one action to load, one to fire). Bolt: +6 to hit, 3d10 piercing, range 200 ft. Can be turned to face any direction. Ammo: 6 bolts each.

**Portcullis** - Winch inside gatehouse (Strength DC 16 to lower). Can also be lifted from outside with a grappling hook and rope (Athletics DC 18, 3 rounds).

**Oil Cauldrons** - At murder holes. Each contains 2 gallons of oil. Tipping (action) creates 10-ft cone (3d6 fire, persists for 2 rounds, 1d6 per round).

**Watchtower Winch** - In east tower. Can lower a heavy metal grate (blocks 10-ft wide passage) or raise supplies. Also has a signal lantern (can be shuttered to send Morse code).

**Arrow Slit Shutters** - Can be closed (action) to block enemy fire but also block vision.

**Internal Tier Gates** - Stone walls have iron gates. Locked (Thieves' Tools DC 15). Can be forced (Strength DC 20). Each gate controls access to higher tiers.

## Tactical Opportunities

- Capture ballistae to fire on defenders.
- Use oil cauldrons against clustered enemies.
- Lower portcullis to split enemy forces.
- Climb watchtowers for elevated fire.
- Shutter arrow slits to block archers.
- Use grappling hook to bypass gate.

## Defenders

12 anomaly soldiers (4 at gate, 4 on walls, 2 in each watch tower). 2 archers on gatehouse upper floor. 1 field commander in courtyard.
