# Map 5: The Cave Mouth - Warded Threshold

**Scale:** 300 ft wide × 250 ft deep (60x50 grid)
**Elevation:** Cave mouth at ground level; stone terrace +10 ft above ravine

## Terrain Zones

**Stone Terrace** - 100 ft × 60 ft, paved with flagstones. Slick from moisture (Acrobatics DC 10 to avoid slip). Low stone wall on three sides (half cover).

**Cave Entrance** - 30 ft wide, 40 ft high, natural arch reinforced with metal struts. Crystalline veins glow (dim light 30 ft).

**Heavy Door** - 15 ft × 15 ft, durasteel, warded with arcane sigils. AC 18, HP 60. Requires code or keycard. Has a small viewing port (can be opened, 3 in × 5 in).

**Ventilation Shafts** - Two shafts: east (3 ft diameter) and west (4 ft diameter). East shaft leads to maintenance tunnel (200 ft long, ends in cave interior). West shaft leads to a dead end with a spinning arcane fan (blades deal 2d6 force damage if active).

**Arcane Sentinel Emplacements** - Four stone alcoves, two on terrace, two flanking door. Each contains a hovering crystal orb that fires a **force beam** (60 ft line, Dex save DC 14, 3d6 force damage, recharge 4-6). The orbs are powered by glyphs on the floor.

**Supply Alcove** - North of door, 20 ft × 20 ft, stacked with crates (ammunition, rations, medical supplies). Guarded by one soldier.

## Interactive Elements

**Arcane Orbs** - Control panels at each alcove (Arcana DC 15 to disable or reprogram). Can be destroyed (AC 15, HP 20; immune to nonmagical weapons). A destroyed orb releases a 5-ft pulse of force (2d6, Dex DC 12 half).

**Door Keypad** - Requires 8-digit code (from commander's datapad). Three incorrect attempts trigger alarm (reinforcements in 2 rounds). Ward can be suppressed with Dispel Magic (DC 16) or by touching a correctly attuned focus (keycard).

**Ventilation Fans** - West shaft fan is arcane - runes glow. Can be turned off at a control stone (inside alcove). Blades can be jammed with a crowbar (Strength DC 14) or dispelled (Dispel Magic DC 13).

**Metal Struts** - Supporting cave entrance. Can be loosened (Strength DC 18) to cause partial collapse (creates rubble difficult terrain, blocks vision, deals 2d6 bludgeoning to anyone nearby).

**Oil Barrels** - Two barrels near supply alcove. Can be rolled into sentinel fire zone (explodes, 10-ft radius, 3d6 fire).

**Viewing Port** - Small window in door. Can be used to see inside (dim light) - or to shoot a crossbow bolt or spell (disadvantage due to small opening).

## Tactical Opportunities

- Disable or reprogram arcane orbs to fire on defenders.
- Use ventilation shaft for stealth entry (requires Squeeze).
- Jam fan to disable shaft hazard.
- Roll oil barrels into sentinel beams to cause explosions.
- Collapse entrance to block reinforcements.
- Use viewing port to scout interior.

## Defenders

2 heavy anomaly soldiers at door, 1 guarding supplies, 2 technicians inside door (non-combatants). Arcane orbs are automated.
