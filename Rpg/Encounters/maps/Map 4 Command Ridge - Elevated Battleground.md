# Map 4: Command Ridge - Elevated Battleground

**Scale:** 500 ft wide × 400 ft deep (100x80 grid)
**Elevation:** Ridge 40 ft above camp; ravine 80 ft deep; multiple rock ledges

## Terrain Zones

**Switchback Path** - 100 ft long, 10 ft wide, rises 20 ft. Rocky steps (difficult terrain). Two hairpin turns with rock walls (cover). A landslide at one turn (rubble, requires Athletics DC 12 to cross).

**Ridge Top** - 200 ft × 150 ft, uneven rock with natural pillars (10 ft high, provide three-quarters cover). Scattered boulders, some precariously balanced.

**Command Tent** - 30 ft × 30 ft, reinforced canvas, sandbag perimeter (half cover). Inside: radio equipment, maps, data terminal, cot. A trapdoor leads to a 10-ft basement (ammo cache).

**Rope Bridge** - 80 ft long, 5 ft wide, spans a 80-ft deep ravine. Wooden planks, rope rails. Crossing requires Acrobatics DC 12 if alone, DC 15 if two or more. Ropes are frayed - can be cut.

**Ravine Bottom** - 80 ft below, rocky with a stream (2 ft deep, difficult terrain). Can be accessed by climbing down (Athletics DC 14) or by falling (8d6 bludgeoning). Contains a hidden cave (20 ft deep) with a wounded soldier.

**Eastern Watchtower** - Stone, 40 ft high, spiral stairs. Top platform (15 ft × 15 ft) has a heavy ballista and a signal fire. Ballista can rotate 360°.

## Interactive Elements

**Field Commander** - In tent. Has datapad with access codes and a remote detonator (connected to powder barrels in cave). Can be captured.

**Radio** - In tent. Can be used to call reinforcements - or to send false orders. Requires code (known by commander).

**Precarious Boulders** - Two large boulders (10 ft diameter) on ridge edge. Can be pushed (Strength DC 18) to roll down switchback path (10-ft wide, 60-ft long, 6d6 bludgeoning, Dex DC 14 half).

**Rope Bridge** - Cut ropes to drop bridge (falling creatures take 8d6). Anchor posts can be untied (Sleight of Hand DC 12).

**Basement Trapdoor** - Under rug in tent. Leads to ammo cache (4 frag grenades, 2 smoke grenades, 1 rocket launcher with 2 rockets - 8d6 fire, 20-ft radius).

**Signal Fire** - In watchtower. Can be lit to signal allies - or to attract enemies. Also can be doused to create thick smoke (heavily obscured 50-ft radius).

**Ballista** - With 10 bolts. Can be aimed at camp or cave.

## Tactical Opportunities

- Push boulders onto switchback path.
- Cut bridge to isolate enemies.
- Capture commander for codes and detonator.
- Use rocket launcher on cave door.
- Use signal fire to confuse enemy.
- Climb down ravine for stealth approach to cave.

## Hazards

- Bridge fall (8d6).
- Boulders rolling (reflex save or crushed).
- Commander may trigger detonator (explodes powder in cave).
- Watchtower ballista (3d10 damage).
- Ravine stream (cold water, exhaustion risk).
