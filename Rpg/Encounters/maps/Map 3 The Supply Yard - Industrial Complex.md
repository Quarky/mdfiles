# Map 3: The Supply Yard - Industrial Complex

**Scale:** 600 ft wide × 500 ft deep (120x100 grid at 5 ft per square)
**Elevation:** Flat, with raised loading docks (+5 ft), a crane tower (+30 ft), and underground storage pits (-10 ft)

## Terrain Zones

**Loading Dock (north)** - 100 ft × 40 ft, wooden platform 5 ft high. Ramps on both ends. Stacked crates (light cover). A hand-cranked crane can lift up to 1,000 lbs (2 rounds to lift 20 ft).

**Central Yard (200 ft × 200 ft)** - Packed dirt with wagon tracks. Piles of powder barrels (30 barrels total, in clusters of 3-5). Wooden pallets with ammunition crates. A large canvas tent (50 ft × 30 ft) serves as a mess hall (tables, benches, cooking fire).

**Storage Sheds** - Four wooden sheds (20 ft × 20 ft) at edges. Each contains: shed A: tools and spare parts; shed B: captured equipment (weapons, armor); shed C: food and water; shed D: a hidden prisoner (scout).

**Underground Pits** - Three pits (20 ft diameter, 10 ft deep) near south edge, covered with wooden planks. Pits contain spare ammunition, fuel barrels, and one pit has a wounded beast (wild boar, will attack if disturbed).

**Crane Tower** - 30 ft high, wooden structure with a boom that can rotate 360°. Top platform (10 ft × 10 ft) has a heavy winch and a bell (signal). Can drop heavy loads (3d6 bludgeoning, 10-ft radius).

## Interactive Elements

**Crane** - Can lift a PC or heavy object. Also can swing the boom to drop a crate (5-ft square) anywhere within 40 ft radius.

**Powder Barrels** - 30 barrels. Each explosion: 10-ft radius, 3d6 fire, Dex DC 13 half. Chain reaction if within 15 ft of another barrel.

**Cooking Fire** - In mess tent. Can be kicked to spread fire (5 ft per round). Smoke fills tent (heavily obscured, Con save DC 10 or coughing disadvantage).

**Prisoner (Shed D)** - Scout tied to chair. Has intel on cave layout and machine shutdown code. Also has a hidden lockpick in boot (Perception DC 16 to find).

**Tool Shed** - Contains crowbar, sledgehammer, rope, pulleys, nails, and a portable ram (advantage on Strength checks to break doors).

**Pit Covers** - Can be removed (action) to reveal pit. Creatures in pit cannot climb out without rope or assistance (Athletics DC 15). Pit with beast: boar attacks anyone entering.

## Tactical Opportunities

- Use crane to drop crates on enemies.
- Detonate powder barrels to clear areas or create distractions.
- Free prisoner for intel and help.
- Use portable ram to breach doors quickly.
- Lure enemies into pits.
- Start fire in mess tent for smoke cover.

## Hazards

- Chain explosion from powder barrels (can wipe party).
- Pit falls (2d6 falling damage).
- Crane collapse if overloaded (20% chance).
- Wild boar in pit (CR 1/2).
- Burning shed (fire spreads, smoke inhalation).
