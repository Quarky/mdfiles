# Map 1: The Fog-Choked Foothill Approach - Wide Valley Assault

**Scale:** 800 ft wide × 600 ft deep (160x120 grid at 5 ft per square)
**Elevation:** Rolling hills, +5 ft to +30 ft; two ridgelines, a dry creek bed, and a rocky escarpment
**Visibility:** 60 ft in ion fog; static arcs every 2 rounds in random 10-ft zones

## Terrain Zones

**Southern Approach (0-200 ft)** - Open grassland with scattered boulders (half cover). Tall grass provides concealment (lightly obscured). A collapsed farmhouse (30 ft × 30 ft) offers a defensible position with shattered walls (three-quarters cover) and a basement hatch (leads to 10-ft crawl space).

**Dry Creek Bed (200-350 ft)** - 20 ft wide, 8 ft deep, winds diagonally. Provides three-quarters cover. Bottom is rocky (difficult terrain) with small pools of charged water (1d6 lightning if entered). A fallen tree spans the creek (can be used as bridge, but unstable - Acrobatics DC 12).

**Mid-Slope Ridge (350-450 ft)** - Elevation +15 ft, scattered pine trees (cover). A ruined watchtower (20 ft high, spiral stairs, collapsed roof) at (400,250). Inside: a rusty bell that can be rung (alerts camp) and a hidden cache of 2 healing potions.

**Rocky Escarpment (450-600 ft)** - Steep slope, elevation +30 ft. Large granite outcroppings (three-quarters cover). A narrow chimney (5 ft wide) allows climbing from bottom to top in 2 rounds (Athletics DC 14). A cave mouth (natural, 10 ft diameter) at base of escarpment - leads to a short tunnel that exits behind the palisade (but guarded by 2 sleeping soldiers, Perception DC 12 to sneak).

**Northern Crest (600-800 ft)** - The palisade visible 200 ft ahead. Open ground with shell craters (10 ft diameter, 5 ft deep) that provide half cover and concealment.

## Interactive Elements

**Farmhouse** - Collapsed but intact basement. Inside: a rusty lever (pulling it releases a hidden trapdoor in the floor - leads to 15-ft pit, 2d6 falling). Also a workbench with tools (crowbar, hammer, nails).

**Watchtower** - Spiral stairs are damaged (missing steps, Acrobatics DC 10 to climb). Top has a pulley system for hauling supplies - can be used to lift a character up or drop a heavy object (3d6 bludgeoning).

**Creek Pools** - Conduct electricity. Dropping a metal weapon into a pool creates a 5-ft radius shock (2d6 lightning) when any lightning spell hits the water.

**Chimneys** - Narrow vertical shaft. At top, a loose rock can be pushed to create a rockslide (10-ft wide, 30-ft long, 4d6 bludgeoning, Dex DC 14 half).

**Cave Tunnel** - Dark, 50 ft long. Two sleeping soldiers (can be subdued silently). Exit behind palisade in a storage shed.

**Shell Craters** - Some contain unexploded ordnance (Perception DC 14). Tapping or fire causes explosion (10-ft radius, 4d6 fire, Dex DC 13 half).

## Tactical Opportunities

- Use farmhouse basement as sniper nest.
- Collapse the chimney rockslide to block pursuit.
- Lure patrol into creek pools and zap with lightning.
- Sneak through cave to bypass gate.
- Use watchtower bell as diversion.
- Drop heavy objects from watchtower onto enemies below.

## Static Discharge Zones

Five zones (marked blue), 15-ft radius each. Roll 1d4 each round; on 1, discharge hits all creatures inside (Dex DC 12 or 1d6 lightning).

## Patrol Routes

Two anomaly soldier patrols (3 each) moving in opposite directions along ridgelines. Timing: 3 rounds at each end, 2 rounds to traverse.
