# Map 8: The'Lana Convoy Ambush

**Scale:** 800 ft wide × 600 ft deep (160x120 grid at 5 ft per square)
**Elevation:** Rolling hills, a sunken road, rocky bluffs, and a dry creek bed
**Lighting:** Overcast, misty; lanterns on wagons; dim light with pockets of shadow

## Terrain Zones

**Sunken Road (center)** - A dirt road worn 10 ft below the surrounding fields. 20 ft wide, 500 ft long. Wagon ruts (difficult terrain). Stone culverts every 100 ft (can be used as cover or crawlspaces).

**North Bluff (0-200 ft north)** - Rocky escarpment, 30 ft high. Dense scrub brush (concealment). Three natural overlooks provide three-quarters cover. A narrow goat trail leads down to the road (Athletics DC 10).

**South Fields (200-600 ft south)** - Open grassland with tall grass (lightly obscured). Scattered boulders (half cover). A dry creek bed (15 ft wide, 6 ft deep) runs parallel to the road, offering covered approach.

**East Bend (500-800 ft east)** - The road curves around a low hill (20 ft high). An abandoned stone farmhouse (30 ft × 30 ft, partially collapsed) provides a defensible position. A wooden watchtower (20 ft high, rotting) overlooks the bend.

**West Approach (0-200 ft west)** - Open ground with tall grass. A small pond (40 ft diameter, 5 ft deep) at the roadside. Reeds provide concealment.

## The Convoy

**Composition:**
- 3 heavy wagons (each 20 ft long, 8 ft wide, canvas cover). Each carries 6 powder barrels and 2 crates of anomaly crystals.
- 1 light scout wagon (10 ft long) at the front.
- 12 The'lana guards (anomaly-trained soldiers).
- 1 drow commander (noble, on horseback).
- 2 battle-mages (The'lana's own, using evocation and abjuration).

**Wagon defenses:** Each heavy wagon has a mounted repeating crossbow (range 120 ft, 2d8 piercing, reload 1 round). The scout wagon has a signal horn (can call reinforcements from the camp, 3-round delay).

## Tactical Elements

**Ambush Positions:** Party can set up on the bluffs (ranged advantage), in the creek bed (stealth approach), or at the farmhouse (chokepoint).

**Wagon Movement:** Convoy moves at slow pace (10 ft per round). Stopping the lead wagon blocks the others.

**Powder Barrels:** If ignited, 15-ft radius explosion (4d6 fire, Dex DC 13 half). Chain reaction can destroy multiple wagons.

**Crystal Chests:** If damaged by fire or force, they release an anomaly pulse (20-ft radius, Con save DC 14 or stunned 1 round, 2d6 force).

**Horse Stampede:** Killing or frightening the draft horses causes wagons to crash (creates difficult terrain, possibly flips wagons).

## Interactive Elements

**Farmhouse Well** - 30 ft deep, dry. Can be used as a hiding spot or to drop a powder barrel (grenade effect).

**Watchtower Rope** - A frayed rope holds a counterweight. Cutting it drops a heavy log (3d6 bludgeoning, 10-ft line).

**Pond** - Shallow, muddy. Entering creates noise (disadvantage on Stealth) but can extinguish fire.

**Goat Trail** - Steep, narrow. Can be collapsed (Strength DC 16) to block the road.

**Culverts** - Stone arches under the road. Can be used to crawl beneath wagons (Squeeze) or plant explosives.

## Victory & Consequences

**Success (cargo seized or destroyed):** Veldrin honors the deal; The'lana's local operations are delayed (camp gets fewer reinforcements later); party gains intel on Vi'shen's bio-arcane experiments.

**Failure (convoy escapes or party routed):** The'lana reinforces the camp (additional guards, early warning); Veldrin withdraws his offer; Vi'shen learns of the party's interest.

**Partial Success (some cargo lost, but not all):** Reduced reward, but Veldrin still owes a favor; The'lana is weakened but not crippled.
