# Fold‑Scarred Convergence Beast
*Large aberration (Fold‑born), Chaotic Neutral / Unstable*

**Armor Class** 16 (warped chitin + rejected warforged plating)  
**Hit Points** 210 (20d10 + 80)  
**Speed** 30 ft, climb 20 ft, swim 30 ft

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 20 (+5) | 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

**Saving Throws** Con +8, Wis +6, Int +7  
**Skills** Perception +6, Arcana +7, Athletics +9  
**Damage Resistances** necrotic, psychic, lightning  
**Damage Immunities** poison  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, poisoned  
**Senses** blindsight 60 ft, tremorsense 30 ft, darkvision 120 ft  
**Languages** understands Deep Speech, Undercommon, Drow, and Machine‑Cant but cannot speak  
**Challenge** 12 (8,400 XP)

---

## Fold‑Born Traits

**Aberrant Fusion.** The creature is a forced amalgam of drow flesh, mole musculature, aboleth tissue, elder brain matter, mind flayer neural strands, and rejected warforged components.  
Creatures that see it clearly must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom save** or be **nauseated** (disadvantage on next attack roll).

**Necromantic Overbinding.** When the beast takes 50 damage, it **evolves**, gaining resistance to the last damage type dealt.  
When it takes 100 damage, it unlocks **Necrotic Breath** (see below).  
Battle‑mages can halt this for 1 round with **Arcana DC 16**.

**Tethered to the Core.** The aboleth‑like tentacles connect it to the **ring‑within‑rings anomaly device** behind it.  
While tethered:  
- Gains **+2 AC**  
- Regains **10 HP at start of its turn**  
- Reality‑shift zones trigger **one round earlier**

Cutting a tendril (AC 12, 10 HP) deals **2d6 psychic** to the beast and **stuns it for 1 round**.

---

## Actions

### **Multiattack**
The beast makes **two tentacle attacks** or **one tentacle + one psychic lash**.

### **Tentacle Slam**  
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 15 ft  
*Hit:* 2d10 + 5 bludgeoning + 1d6 psychic  
If both tentacles hit the same target in a round, the target must succeed on a **DC 16 Strength save** or be **dragged 10 ft** toward the lair pit.

### **Psychic Lash**  
*Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 60 ft  
*Hit:* 3d8 psychic  
Target must make a **DC 15 Int save** or be **staggered** (speed halved, no reactions) until end of next turn.

### **Mind‑Rot Pulse (Recharge 5–6)**  
A burst of elder‑brain energy erupts in a **20‑ft radius**.  
Creatures must make a **DC 15 Wis save** or take **4d6 psychic** and be **confused** (as per spell) for 1 round.

### **Necrotic Breath (Unlocked at 100 damage)**  
30‑ft cone, **Dex DC 15**  
*Hit:* 4d8 necrotic + 2d6 force  
Creatures reduced to 0 HP dissolve into black ash unless stabilized.

---

## Legendary Actions (3 per round)

**Tendril Whip (1 point).** One tentacle attack.  
**Reality Flicker (2 points).** Teleport 20 ft to an unoccupied space.  
**Brain‑Echo Surge (3 points).** Forces one creature to reroll a successful save against the beast.

## Non‑Lethal Resolution Flow — Cave Heart Beast

### 1. Reduce the Beast to 0 HP (Non‑Lethal Declared)
- PCs must explicitly declare non‑lethal intent.
- The beast collapses but the anomaly core continues pulsing violently.
- Reality begins to destabilize (visual + sound cues).

### 2. Stabilize the Beast’s Mind (Skill Challenge)
**3 successes before 2 failures**  
Each attempt takes 1 round.

**Options:**
- **Arcana (DC 16):** Sync with the anomaly core’s rhythm.  
- **Animal Handling (DC 15):** Calm the mole‑instinct portion.  
- **Persuasion (DC 17):** Speak to the drow‑mind fragment.  
- **Insight (DC 15):** Identify which fused psyche is dominant.  
- **Medicine (DC 14):** Soothe necromantic overbinding.

**Failure:** The beast begins to re‑evolve (Phase‑2 traits re‑activate for 1 round).

### 3. Sever or Harmonize the Tendrils
PCs choose one path:

#### **A. Sever the Tendrils (Mercy‑Kill the Connection)**
- Requires **3 tendrils cut** (AC 12, 10 HP each).  
- Beast becomes stable but unconscious.  
- Core powers down.  
- **Outcome:** Beast survives but loses anomaly abilities.

#### **B. Harmonize the Tendrils (Save the Beast Fully)**
- Requires **Arcana DC 18** with help from battle‑mages.  
- Othira Quill provides +2 if present.  
- Success stabilizes the core and the beast simultaneously.  
- **Outcome:** Beast awakens, calm, and Fold‑aligned.

### 4. Extract or Preserve the Backup Core
- **Arcana DC 14** to extract data safely.  
- If preserved, beast retains memory fragments.

### 5. Final Outcome (Choose One)
- **Saved & Harmonized:**  
  Beast becomes a **Fold‑born guardian** of Veyrun’s Reach.  
  Grants **Anomaly Boons** (GM‑chosen: teleport nodes, storm anchors, Fold‑sight).

- **Saved but Disconnected:**  
  Beast becomes a **docile, damaged creature**.  
  City gains **biological insights** but no anomaly power.

- **Killed:**  
  Region stabilizes.  
  Factions gain political leverage.  
  Iriandel Vos sees a darker future.

### 6. Epilogue Hooks
- Beast may dream‑speak to PCs.  
- Core may reactivate later.  
- Factions may fight over the anomaly boons.
