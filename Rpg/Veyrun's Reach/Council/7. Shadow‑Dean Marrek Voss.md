## **7. Shadow‑Dean Marrek Voss**

**School:** Necromancy  
**Vibe:** Cold academic of death and Fold‑echo cadavers.

### **Personality**
- Detached, analytical, and morbidly curious  
- Views life and death as data points  
- Fascinated by the Warforged prototype’s Fold entanglement  
- Speaks with clinical precision  

### **Mannerisms**
- Fingers stained with ink and embalming reagents  
- Tilts his head sharply when something interests him  
- Rarely blinks; when he does, it’s slow  
- Voice low, monotone, and eerily steady
