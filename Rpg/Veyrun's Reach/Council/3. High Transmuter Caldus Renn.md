## **3. High Transmuter Caldus Renn**
**School:** Transmutation  
**Vibe:** Ruthless alchemical pragmatist.

### **Personality**
- Efficient, cold, and results‑driven  
- Sees everything — people included — as material to be improved  
- Obsessed with Fold‑accelerated transformation  
- Has no patience for ethical debates  

### **Mannerisms**
- Constantly adjusts gloves, goggles, or tools  
- Examines people like specimens  
- Sniffs potions or reagents mid‑conversation  
- Voice is flat, clinical, and disturbingly calm
