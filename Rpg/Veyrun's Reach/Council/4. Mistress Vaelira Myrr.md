## **4. Mistress Vaelira Myrr**
**School:** Illusion  
**Vibe:** Charming, unreadable, and possibly several people at once.

### **Personality**
- Elegant, theatrical, and socially lethal  
- Never reveals her true intentions  
- Uses illusions conversationally — subtle shifts in appearance, lighting, or mood  
- Treats the Fold as a stage with infinite backdrops  

### **Mannerisms**
- Changes eye color mid‑blink  
- Smiles with perfect poise, never reaching her eyes  
- Tilts her head when lying — or telling the truth  
- Voice smooth, melodic, and impossible to pin down
