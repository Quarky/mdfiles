## **2. Loremaster Iriandel Vos**
**School:** Divination  
**Vibe:** Haunted oracle who sees too much.

### **Personality**
- Quiet, distant, and unsettlingly perceptive  
- Answers questions with half‑truths or future‑leaning riddles  
- Carries the weight of visions he cannot fully interpret  
- Treats the Fold as a timeline fracture waiting to widen  

### **Mannerisms**
- Pauses mid‑sentence as if listening to something no one else hears  
- Eyes drift slightly off‑focus, tracking invisible threads  
- Speaks softly, with long silences  
- Occasionally repeats a phrase he hasn’t said yet
