## **5. Warden‑Scholar Thamior Kess**
**School:** Abjuration  
**Vibe:** Paranoid guardian holding the city together by sheer will.

### **Personality**
- Hyper‑vigilant, anxious, and meticulous  
- Sees threats everywhere — especially in the Fold  
- Believes the city is one mistake away from collapse  
- Loyal to the city, not the Council  

### **Mannerisms**
- Checks wards and sigils compulsively  
- Flinches at sudden sounds  
- Speaks in rapid, clipped bursts  
- Keeps a protective hand hovering near spell components
