## **8. Planar Theorist Seraphine Dhal**
**School:** Conjuration  
**Vibe:** Brilliant, scattered, and half‑in another dimension.

### **Personality**
- Excitable, distracted, and wildly creative  
- Treats planar theory like a living conversation  
- Believes the Fold is “calling something through”  
- Alternates between genius clarity and chaotic rambling  

### **Mannerisms**
- Talks with her hands, drawing invisible sigils  
- Forgets objects she’s holding mid‑sentence  
- Stares into empty space as if seeing portals  
- Voice fluctuates between rapid babbling and sudden whispers
