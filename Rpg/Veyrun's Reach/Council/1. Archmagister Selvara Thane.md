## **1. Archmagister Selvara Thane**
**School:** Evocation  
**Vibe:** Controlled destruction; a storm in a bottle.

### **Personality**
- Intense, disciplined, and fiercely intelligent  
- Believes power must be mastered, not feared  
- Treats the Fold as a potential weapon  
- Speaks bluntly; hates political games  

### **Mannerisms**
- Fingers twitch with micro‑sparks when irritated  
- Taps her thumb against her palm in a rhythmic “countdown”  
- Stares directly, unblinking, as if measuring your explosive potential  
- Voice sharp and clipped, like a crack of lightning
