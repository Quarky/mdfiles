# 16 — The Sith Palace Approach

## Scene card

| Field | At the table |
|---|---|
| Canon chapter | The Sith Palace |
| Purpose | Make the published palace reveal a full exterior scene with room for reconnaissance, pursuit, and last negotiation. |
| Start | The ridge path opens onto the ancient palace. |
| End | The party enters through the parted doors or an alternate breach with factions positioned. |

## Player-facing opening

> The palace rises from the desert on columns broad enough to be towers. Eroded guardians stand watch, and one narrow thread of smoke leaks from a high window.

If the group arrives under pursuit, add engine echoes or distant lights without revealing exact numbers. If they arrive wounded, describe the environment noticing weakness rather than simply becoming louder. If they arrive after using the dark side, give the affected character a moment of unnatural comfort before the danger becomes visible.

## Full walkthrough

1. Deliver the published monumental description.
2. Let players reconnoiter before the guide urges entry.
3. Reveal Warde’s camp smoke and at least one alternate breach.
4. Give a final chance to resolve pursuers outside.
5. Have the guide move more easily as he nears the palace.
6. Use statues and courtyards for a mobile standoff.
7. Let Force users feel the vergence concentrate inside.
8. Record entrance, faction order, and who carries the holocron.

Treat zones as tools, not mandatory stops. Movement between them should change sight lines, available cover, social privacy, or the pressure clock. Never hide the only required clue behind a single check.

## NPC and world behavior

- **Moraband wants:** certainty, isolation, and choices made from fear or anger.
- **Moraband fears:** corroboration, compassion under pressure, and characters admitting uncertainty without surrendering agency.
- **The Wanderer/Warde:** observes how the group handles weakness, enemies, and forbidden knowledge; before his reveal, he acts helpful but never casually vulnerable.
- **Pursuers:** follow their established agenda rather than teleporting into every encounter. Their condition and morale carry forward.
- **Companions:** react as living beings. Animals notice impossible scent or silence before interpreting it as supernatural.

Dialogue prompts: “What would make this risk worth it?” “Which part of what you saw are you certain was real?” “If the future were fixed, why would anyone need to threaten you?” Use answers as later temptation material, but do not invalidate player-authored history.

## Checks and failure-forward results

| Check | Stakes |
|---|---|
| Average Perception | identify occupied high window. |
| Hard Knowledge (Lore) | distinguish palace from tomb. |
| Average Stealth | flank through service court. |

- **Advantage:** reveal useful terrain, reduce the clock, preserve supplies, or let an ally notice a contradiction.
- **Threat:** strain, exposure, a lost position, rival progress, or a temptation tag; never erase the discovered route.
- **Triumph:** establish a lasting shortcut, uncover redundant evidence, or create a fact Warde cannot dismiss later.
- **Despair:** separate goals rather than players, advance an active clock, or make a moral cost immediate and visible.

## Clues and discoveries

- The structure is a palace, not a burial complex.
- The doors have been used regularly.
- Smoke and water containers prove habitation.

Clue redundancy: place one clue in terrain, one in a recoverable record or object, and one in NPC behavior. A failed check changes who controls the discovery or what it costs; it does not delete the information required to continue.

## Environment, hazard, and pressure

- Palace awakening: silent, watching, whispering, manifest. Entry advances at least one step.

At each meaningful transition use three layers: one physical change, one doubtful perception, and one personal emotional pressure. Do not turn every manifestation into damage. Quiet uncertainty is part of the threat.

## Optional encounters

- Pursuers demand Warde before entry.
- A guardian statue speaks in a PC’s voice.
- Warde offers to enter alone and “prepare the tomb.”

Use at most one optional encounter when pacing is already tight. Use two only when the players deliberately explore or when a failed route creates a meaningful new situation.

## Branches and outcomes

- Reconnaissance grants alternate starting positions.
- Violence outside shapes Warde’s certainty about the PCs.

Record changes in the consequence ledger. Preserve mercy, betrayal, accepted dark-side help, rescued rivals, companion reactions, and evidence about mutable futures for the final Warde scene.

## Player/GM boundary

Players may know what they can directly sense, what a revealed handout states, and what an NPC chooses to say. Keep Warde’s identity, manifestation triggers, clock thresholds, rival goals, and whether an apparition is “real” in GM-only notes until play reveals them.
