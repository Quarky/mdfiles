# Moraband Player and GM Information Boundaries

Player-facing: direct sensory information, revealed handouts, established campaign history, visible NPC behavior, and confirmed mechanical effects.

GM-only until revealed: the Wanderer’s identity, clock thresholds, manifestation triggers, rival private goals, Warde’s changing state, hidden page contents, and whether any particular apparition is metaphysically real.

Never use burned-in labels on player maps. Keep map keys, secrets, encounter zones, and tactical instructions in GM notes and the five-tab overlay.
