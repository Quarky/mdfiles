### 1st Level Spells

### Bless (Cleric)
- **Effect:** 3 allies add +1d4 to attack rolls and saving throws
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Roll:** None
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting it applies to saves, not just attacks
- **Example:** Cast before combat, boost party accuracy

### Healing Word (Cleric)
- **Effect:** Bonus action heal for 1d4 + WIS (2) at 60 ft range
- **Roll:** None (automatic heal)
- **Common Mistake:** Can't cast another leveled spell same turn (bonus action rule)
- **Example:** Revive downed ally without moving

#### Inflict Wounds (Cleric)
- **Effect:** 3d10 necrotic damage on touch
- **Roll:** Melee spell attack (+5)
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting it requires melee range
- **Example:** Upcast to 3rd level for 5d10 damage

#### Identify (Wizard, Ritual)
- **Effect:** Learn magic item properties, attunement requirements
- **Casting Time:** 1 minute (or 11 min as ritual)
- **Roll:** None
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting it doesn't reveal curses
- **Example:** Identify loot after combat

#### Alarm (Wizard, Ritual)
- **Effect:** Ward 20ft cube, mental or audible alert
- **Duration:** 8 hours
- **Roll:** None
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting it detects invisible creatures
- **Example:** Protect campsite during rest

#### Command (Cleric)
- **Effect:** One-word command (Flee, Drop, Grovel, Halt)
- **Save:** Wisdom DC 13
- **Common Mistake:** Using words that cause self-harm (invalid)
- **Example:** "Drop" to make enemy drop weapon

#### Shield (Wizard)
- **Effect:** +5 AC until your next turn, immune to Magic Missile
- **Casting Time:** Reaction (when hit)
- **Roll:** None
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting it lasts the whole round
- **Example:** Block a critical hit

#### Absorb Elements (Wizard)
- **Effect:** Half elemental (acid/cold/fire/lightning/thunder) damage, +1d6 on next melee attack
- **Casting Time:** Reaction
- **Roll:** None
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting the bonus damage only applies to melee
- **Example:** Survive a dragon's breath

#### Grease (Wizard)
- **Effect:** 10ft square, Dex save or prone, difficult terrain
- **Save:** Dexterity DC 16
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting creatures can fall again each turn
- **Example:** Control a choke point

#### Armor of Agathys (Warlock)
- **Effect:** Gain 5 temp HP; melee attackers take 5 cold damage while temp HP lasts
- **Duration:** 1 hour (no concentration)
- **Roll:** None
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting cold damage only triggers on melee
- **Example:** Pre-cast before battle, punish melee attackers

#### Hellish Rebuke (Warlock)
- **Effect:** 2d10 fire damage as reaction when damaged
- **Save:** Dexterity DC 13 half
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting it's a reaction, not a bonus action
- **Example:** Punish an enemy who hits you

#### Chromatic Orb (Wizard)
- **Effect:** 3d8 damage, choose type (acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison, thunder)
- **Roll:** Ranged spell attack (+8)
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting material component (50gp diamond, not consumed)
- **Example:** Bypass resistance with chosen damage type

#### Hypnotic Pattern (Wizard)
- **Effect:** 30ft cube, creatures are charmed and incapacitated (Wis save)
- **Save:** Wisdom DC 16
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Common Mistake:** Forgetting damage breaks the effect

#### Counterspell (Wizard)
- **Effect:** Cancel a spell being cast within 60 ft
- **Casting Time:** Reaction
- **Roll:** If spell level >3, INT check DC 10 + spell level (Heart: +8)

- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Roll:** None

- **Example:** Cast on yourself or a martial ally for massive mobility

## Common Mechanics

### Arcane Ward
- Starts with 15 HP (2 × wizard level 5 + INT 5)
- When you take damage, ward takes damage first
- Ward recharges when you cast Abjuration spells (recharge = 2 × spell level)

### Concentration
- You can only have one concentration spell active at a time
- When you take damage, roll Constitution save: DC = 10 or half damage taken (rounded down), whichever is higher
- Heart has CON +4 and Lucky (can reroll failed saves)

### Spellcasting from Multiple Classes
- Wizard spells use INT (attack +8, DC 16)
- Cleric spells use WIS (attack +5, DC 13)
- Warlock spells use CHA (attack +5, DC 13)
- Spell slots are shared between Wizard and Cleric (full caster: level 5 Wizard + level 1 Cleric = level 6 caster)
- Warlock Pact slot is separate, recharges on short rest
- **Common mistake:** Using wrong ability modifier for spells

### Advantage / Disadvantage
- Advantage: Roll 2 d20, take higher
- Disadvantage: Roll 2 d20, take lower
- Heart has advantage on initiative from Sentient Shield
