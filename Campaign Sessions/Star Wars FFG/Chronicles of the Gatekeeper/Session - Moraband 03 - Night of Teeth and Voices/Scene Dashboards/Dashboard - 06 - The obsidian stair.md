## GM fast scan
- When the last phantasm dissipates, a sealed passage behind the amphitheater opens onto an obsidian stair climbing out of the canyon. The stairs are too regular to be natural and too exposed to be safe. At the top sits a small storm shelter built from fallen blocks.
- As the party climbs, red lightning gathers on the horizon. Each flash shows an elderly man standing at the top. Between flashes, nobody is there.
- If the group was overwhelmed, have the old man appear during the final exchange and disperse a phantasm with a quiet gesture. If they triumphed, he waits at the shelter with water warming over a small heater. Either way, do not reveal his identity.
- His first line is simple:
- “You took longer than I expected. That is encouraging.”
- End there, or allow only the characters’ immediate reactions. Session 4 is the full encounter with the Wanderer.
