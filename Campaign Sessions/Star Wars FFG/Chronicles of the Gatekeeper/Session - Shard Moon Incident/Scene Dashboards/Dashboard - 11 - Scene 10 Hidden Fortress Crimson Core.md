## Pressure / hazards / clocks
- At Wakefulness 4+, Vesh-Null is active. At 5+, W-Prime begins waking or has already awakened. At 6, containment logic is actively changing doors and atmosphere to force resolution.
- A clever party can exploit the contradictions. Presenting W-12 as a person with agency may not persuade it morally, but can break an inventory-based rule if credentials or records have been altered. Technical sabotage can isolate its network control. Combat remains valid and dangerous.
- At high Vergence Pressure, the Whisper offers the easiest solution: certainty. It tells one PC exactly what to destroy, whom to kill, or which switch will “end this.” Some of the information may even be true. The temptation is surrendering judgment, not merely doing something violent.
- The party isolates Serrin, disables Vesh-Null, stabilizes W-Prime or leaves them contained by choice, and removes critical power. Best for preserving evidence and minimizing collapse.
- The party frees W-12/W-Prime/other viable subjects and prioritizes evacuation over records. The facility remains partly intact and may continue to be dangerous.
- The party overloads or collapses the core. Make this a timed escape with specific routes opened or closed by earlier choices. Do not erase all evidence automatically; allow the party to carry what they deliberately secured.
