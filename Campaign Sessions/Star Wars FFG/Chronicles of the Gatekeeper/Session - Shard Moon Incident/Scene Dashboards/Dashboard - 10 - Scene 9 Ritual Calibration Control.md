- If not already encountered, Serrin speaks through the system here. Decide the form based on campaign tone: preserved holographic intellect, ritual imprint, or half-living remnant sustained deeper in the facility. The important point is that Serrin is facility-bound and has incomplete control.
- Serrin's argument should be coherent:
- Warde represented an unacceptable concentration of unpredictable power.
- The Sith needed a way to reproduce and control such potential.
- Failed subjects were regrettable but necessary iterations.
- The PCs are standing in a site that could, with their help, finally provide answers the original researchers lacked.
