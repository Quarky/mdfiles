## GM fast scan
- 04 — Ritual Plaza, with 07 — Obsidian Ritual Annex as an optional side area.
- The researchers believed Warde's significance could not be copied from tissue alone. They tried to manufacture compatible bodies, expose them to carefully shaped fear and choice, and use the vergence to force a repeatable spiritual pattern.
- The result was not a line of obedient Wardes. It was broken identity, unstable foresight, conditioned violence, and entities that remembered things they had never lived.

- Technical bypass: use observatory credentials, repair a control station, and command the access mechanism.
