## GM fast scan
- He does not attack incapacitated characters. He chooses dramatic attacks over efficient executions. Every round should contain a spoken claim that players can answer:
- Round 1: “You drew weapons before I named you enemies.”
- Round 2: “Every mercy brought you closer to the power you wanted.”
- Round 3: “You repaired the fighter because you feared being stranded—not because wreckage can become whole.”
- When wounded: “There. The vision narrows. You feel how easy the ending becomes.”
- When a PC protects him: “Do not pretend restraint absolves the blow you intend later.”
