## GM fast scan
- When Warde suffers a Critical Injury, reaches half wounds, or hears two strong contradictions, he claims his death is near. This is the party’s clearest opening. If they stop attacking, add the published Boost. If they continue striking, he reads it as confirmation.
- At Resisting, Warde begins arguing with the apparitions rather than the PCs. He may add his skill or characteristic to the next social check, and the resistance becomes the published Daunting difficulty without his full Discipline opposition.
- If the final appeal succeeds, describe silence before surrender:
- Warde’s blade stops close enough to cast green light across your face. The phantasms continue shouting, but he looks past them. His hand opens. The lightsaber falls, extinguishes, and rolls across the red stone. For the first time since you met him, he looks not old, but tired.
- He admits that he made the party his executioners because death was easier to foresee than atonement. He gives them the crystal, but does not ask forgiveness.

## Checks / mechanics to have ready
- Move his state through Certain → Provoked → Doubting → Resisting → Redeemed. A successful focused appeal advances one step when supported by evidence. Triumph can advance an additional step or cause Warde to assist the next appeal. Despair can move the fight to the balcony or bring rivals into the chamber, but never deletes the possibility of redemption.
