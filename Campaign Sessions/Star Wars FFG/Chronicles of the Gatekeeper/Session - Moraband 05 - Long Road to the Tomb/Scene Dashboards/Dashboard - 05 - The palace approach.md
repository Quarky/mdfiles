## GM fast scan
- The final miles pass through toppled statues and a road exposed by shifting dunes. Warde now moves without the cane. He may still call the building a tomb, but it is visibly a palace.
- Towers and jagged spires rise from the desert on massive columns. Weathered statues stand guard around broad steps; others lie half buried with their hands reaching from the sand. No lights burn within. As you approach, doors that have stood open for centuries widen another handspan.
- Before crossing the threshold, Warde asks one final question: “If the future showed you killing me, what would you do differently?” Do not resolve his reaction. End on the answer and the sickly purple light igniting inside the palace.
