## GM fast scan
- Begin in motion. The Wanderer sets a slow pace but never checks direction. The landscape offers no trail. Ask where each character walks relative to him and who watches the rear.
- The old man’s cane leaves a line of small circles in the dust. Dunes erase your own tracks within minutes, but his remain untouched behind him. When anyone looks directly at them, they appear ordinary again.
- The purpose of this session is not to hide that the Wanderer is Warde at all costs. It is to let the party accumulate a case, decide what that truth means, and prove through action that prediction does not remove choice.
