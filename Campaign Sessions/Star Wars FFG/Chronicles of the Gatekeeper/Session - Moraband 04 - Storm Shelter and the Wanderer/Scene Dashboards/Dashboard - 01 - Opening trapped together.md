## GM fast scan
- Open with the storm making departure impossible. Red lightning walks across the canyon rim without thunder, and sheets of charged dust turn the open ground into a lethal choice. The old man has a stone shelter large enough for everyone but not large enough for privacy.
- The stranger braces his cane against the door until the last person is inside. Red light pulses through cracks in the stone. He passes a flask without asking who needs it most. “Introductions can wait,” he says. “Breathing cannot.”
- The water is real and safe. Let the party test it. He does not take offense, but notices who drinks first and who gives their share away.
