# Moraband Shared GM Engine — Canonical Reference

**Purpose:** Shared procedures used by the repaired Moraband extended sessions. This replaces repeating the same generic guidance inside every scene toolkit.

## Core principles

- Moraband pressures choices; it does not remove player agency.
- A failed check should change cost, position, information, time, injury, strain, pursuit, or temptation rather than halt the story.
- Visions can be true, false, symbolic, partial, or manipulative. Never present an involuntary vision as proof of what a PC “really wants.”
- Rivals and creatures continue acting off-screen according to what they know.
- Warde's social arc is evidence-based. Track what the party learns, what he admits, and how they treat him; do not reduce it to one final Persuasion roll.
- Each map has a current state, entrances/exits, environmental activity, and consequences. Maps are not static illustrations.

## Shared symbol-spending guidance

Use Advantage/Threat/Triumph/Despair to change fictional position: reveal useful details, improve cover, create a shortcut, expose a character, worsen weather, attract attention, damage gear, or strengthen/weaken a relationship. Avoid repeatedly spending symbols on generic strain when a scene-specific consequence is available.

## Rival motion

At the end of each scene ask:

1. Who now knows where the party is?
2. Who gained or lost time?
3. What evidence did the party leave behind?
4. Did open Force use or violence make them easier to track?
5. What would the rival logically do next?

## Handout/reveal rule

Reveal evidence when the characters can actually perceive or recover it. Keep interpretation separate from observation. If an item is player-facing once found, move it to the player-visible state at discovery rather than requiring the GM to remember a later reveal.
