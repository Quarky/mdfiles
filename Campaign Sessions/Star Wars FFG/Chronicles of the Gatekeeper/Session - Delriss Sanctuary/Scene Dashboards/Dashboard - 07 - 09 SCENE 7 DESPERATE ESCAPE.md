## GM fast scan
- Establish the first impact at every occupied location. Characters aboard ship, in the hangar, archive, and upper level perceive different consequences of the same event.
- Resolve immediate footing and danger. Use the published Average Coordination check where appropriate; do not make securely braced characters roll without cause.
- Ask for goals. Data, Werron, allies, archive works, fighter, ship, and route are competing priorities. Make players choose what each character is doing.
- Run one action interval. Resolve exposed actions, apply prepared advantages, then advance collapse once.
- Change the route physically. Flood, tilt, falling debris, jammed door, or revealed breach should follow established station state.
- Reunite or cross-cut split groups. Nobody disappears because another group took longer at the table.
- Reach the ship or an alternate exit. Pay off collar, return-route, power, scuttler, Werron, and fighter decisions.

- [MODULE] First violent shift: Average Coordination. Failure means prone and 1 strain +1 per Threat.
- [MODULE] When the station flips: Hard Coordination to remain standing.
- [MODULE] The pilot makes a Daunting Piloting (Space) check. An underwater-capable vessel reduces the difficulty by two, making the campaign ship's baseline Average.
- [MODULE] Narrative rather than standard combat, Coordination checks, station rollover/collapse, creature invulnerability to ordinary personal attacks, underwater Gunnery limits, facility loss, leviathan reveal, chase difficulty, aquatic reduction, repeated attempts, system strain, and Critical Hit consequence.

## Pressure / hazards / clocks
- Resolve immediate footing and danger. Use the published Average Coordination check where appropriate; do not make securely braced characters roll without cause.
- Run one action interval. Resolve exposed actions, apply prepared advantages, then advance collapse once.
- Run fast, but do not make choices meaningless. Announce the immediate hazard, ask each character for a goal, resolve in short intervals, update the collapse, and cut to the next exposed character. Avoid a single group Athletics roll that turns the whole finale into one number.
- Start at the higher of current Instability or 3. Advance after each full action interval, major failure, or leviathan impact.
- If the clock began below 3, the first impact sets it to 3. Do not add empty ticks just to prolong suspense.
- Advance collapse once and show the leviathan's new behavior.

## Clues / discoveries / reveals
- **Archive to ship:** The archive group faces the impossible wave first. A successful route action reaches Level One; failure washes them toward the low-side shortcut or costs gear, not instant drowning.
- **Exterior reveal:** As the ship pulls away, the sanctuary finally gives you the scale of what has been attacking it. Hundreds of meters of armored body coils around the ridge.
