## GM fast scan
- At Power 0, the sanctuary is quiet enough that the party can hear distant impacts through the frame. At Power 1+, emergency circuits wake in isolated islands. At Instability 3+, one route changes while the party explores.
- The conduit narrows until equipment must be pushed ahead by hand. Cable bundles sag into standing water. Tiny cyan lights withdraw around the next bend. Through the grating below, an empty corridor slopes into darkness, its emergency strips dead.
- The torn plates open into a flooded chamber where furniture and storage containers have collected against the low wall. Your lamps show scoring on the inside edge of the breach. Whatever struck the sanctuary did not stop at the hull.
- Add: “A fresh line of water runs from a seam that was dry when you first illuminated it.”
- Change the soundscape. Once the pressure door closes, remove ship/ocean noise and replace it with drips, transmitted groans, and breathing.
- Orient the party to the tilt. Loose objects collect downhill. Movement is possible, but hurried action has consequences.
- Let them inspect the receiving chamber. The absence of bodies and selected removal of personal effects are clues.

## Checks / mechanics to have ready
- Average Mechanics or Skulduggery. Success takes time but gains both the route and supplies. Failure still opens it; choose lost time, damaged kit, or Instability +1.
- They cannot make the sanctuary safe indefinitely before pressure and the leviathan overtake the work. A Hard Mechanics check plus significant time may reduce Instability by 1 once. Further attempts yield local advantages but also time/awareness costs.
