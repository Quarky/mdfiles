### Plain-English GM situation report

**What is happening overall:** This is the day normal life ends. At 19:02:09 Deus turns the arcology from a city-building into a controlled enclosure: communications lie, doors and lifts become barriers, and people are separated before most of them understand there has been a takeover. Normal activity continues into the evening; at 19:02:09 PST the building becomes a closed machine-directed battlespace.

**Today’s fixed/canon event to remember:** **17:41:44 — 20:** Cal Reynolds begins his journal in his family's Floor 20 residence. **18:17 — 1–6:** A FastFax report records a mall shooting before the full lockdown, evidence that violence or preparation is already underway in the public levels.

- **Deus:** At 19:02:09 Deus seizes the systems that make movement possible—doors, lifts, telecom, security, local hosts, and environmental controls. Its first goal is not to remodel the building; it is to prevent people from coordinating or leaving.
- **Blues / security:** Security forces are abruptly cut off from normal command and begin fragmenting. Some will become the Blue enforcement caste, but tonight many encounters are still confused security teams, isolated guards, and automated systems acting on new orders.
- **Greens / administration and work crews:** The future Green administrative structure is not yet fully visible. Food, water, factories, and logistics are valuable precisely because Deus wants them captured intact.
- **Whites / Matrix, initiation, and specialist projects:** White otaku and allied Matrix actors are positioned to support the new order, but most residents will experience the takeover through doors, announcements, blackouts, and security—not by seeing the Whites directly.
- **Survivors / Resistance:** Survivors are still civilians reacting to a disaster they do not understand. Expect family separation, people sheltering in place, improvised rescues, jammed routes, rumors, and the first tiny groups deciding not to obey official instructions.
- **Physical state of the arcology:** At the moment of shutdown, all 86 tracked areas become vulnerable to machine-directed partition and capture, but the large-scale physical conversions have not all happened yet. Tonight the important change is **control of movement**, not a finished network of prisons and proving grounds.
- **If the runners enter an unrelated floor today:** Confused civilians, stuck lifts, locked doors, contradictory announcements, separated families, isolated guards, drones/automated systems, and sudden environmental hazards.
