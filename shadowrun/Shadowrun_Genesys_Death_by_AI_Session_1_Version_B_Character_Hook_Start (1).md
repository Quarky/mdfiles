# Shadowrun Genesys Death by AI

# Session 1 Version B What Would TunnelRat Do

## Session Purpose

This version begins with the Angie and Frank Crodler character hook. The first playable image is Angie collapsing beside the amber terminal in Maintenance Node 7-G while Frank watches from an overhead duct. Frank begins to retreat, remembers the person he wants to be, and turns back.

The hook is followed by a short time jump to the full Renraku Arcology Shutdown on December 19, 2059. Other player characters enter immediately after the prologue, with direct reasons to reach Node 7-G or become trapped in its service district.

This is a complete **2 to 3 hour** Session 1, not a replacement opening that depends on another guide.

The session ends with the party in a defensible service room, carrying `7G_ANOMALY.log`, aware that a second wounded intelligence may exist inside the system, and choosing whether their next priority is rescue, escape, medical aid, or investigation.

# Why Use This Version

Use this version when:

- Angie and Frank are player characters or central recurring NPCs;
- the table wants an immediate personal mystery instead of an ensemble slice-of-life opening;
- the campaign should foreground the difference between survival and heroism;
- Angie's emerging Otaku abilities need to appear before the full Shutdown;
- Frank's knowledge of the arcology should be earned through character action rather than delivered as exposition.

# Canon Timeline

The full Renraku Shutdown occurs on **December 19, 2059**. Angie's first contact occurs earlier.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| December 9 | Angie enters Maintenance Node 7-G and touches the amber terminal |
| December 10 | Her new Matrix perception becomes undeniable; Renraku classifies the incident as a local maintenance anomaly |
| December 10-18 | Frank retains `7G_ANOMALY.log`; Angie experiences dreams, data echoes, and unwanted awareness |
| December 19 | The arcology seals and Session 1 begins in full |

The first scene occurs on December 9. Keep it to 10-20 minutes so players not present in the earlier incident are not left waiting.

# Tone and Safety

The campaign uses survival horror, claustrophobia, corporate abuse, cybernetic horror, mass-casualty danger, and psychological manipulation. Confirm Lines and Veils before play. Keep the opening frightening through sensory disruption and loss of control rather than graphic violence.

X, N, and O cards remain available. A player may pause or redirect a scene without explaining why.

# GM Preparation

Have ready:

- Angie's automation-ready trait and Otaku ability reference;
- Frank Crodler's background;
- a small data-terminal or maintenance-room map;
- a service-corridor or mechanical-plant map;
- a compact safe-room map or theater-of-the-mind description;
- the party's personal reason for being inside the arcology.

## Central Question

Frank begins the story by deciding not to abandon one stranger.

Throughout the session, the arcology presents variations of the same question:

> When escape becomes harder every time you help someone, who do you choose to be?

Do not decide the answer for the players.

# Bringing in the Other PCs

Before the prologue, ask every player to choose or create one connection to Node 7-G.

| Connection | Why the PC Is Near the Node on December 19 |
|---|---|
| Renraku investigator | Assigned to close the December 9 anomaly ticket |
| Runner | Hired to copy or destroy a diagnostic log before a corporate audit |
| Contractor | Working the same maintenance sector as Frank |
| Security employee | Escorting a technician or checking an unauthorized access alert |
| Friend or relative | Looking for Angie, Frank, Hidoshi, or another PC |
| Awakened character | Drawn by a disturbing emotional or magical echo in the machinery |
| Decker or Otaku | Tracing a repeating amber signal through public infrastructure |
| Visitor | Rerouted into the service district when the public lifts fail |

If a connection would force behavior the character would reject, use a different one. The purpose is to establish proximity, not obligation.

# Key Characters

## Angie

Angie is a frail young elf with unusual eyes, a third eye, emotionally reactive hair, Glamour, heightened senses, and a new direct awareness of the Matrix. Her abilities are real but immature.

She can sense and touch local systems without a deck. She cannot control the arcology, defeat Deus, read every file, or protect herself from all hostile code.

## Frank Crodler

Frank is a 47-year-old HVAC technician, father, small-business owner, comic collector, and lifelong admirer of TunnelRat. He knows the spaces other people ignore: ducts, mechanical trunks, air returns, service shafts, bypass valves, and blind spots.

Frank is not fearless. His heroism is the decision to return while afraid.

If Frank is an NPC, never let him become a GM-controlled protagonist. He identifies routes and consequences, then asks the players what they want to do.

## Hidoshi Tarada

Angie's father is a Japanese cyclops working a short Renraku legal consultancy. His mobility is impaired by old injuries, but he is perceptive and protective without treating Angie as incapable.

He is elsewhere in the arcology during the December 9 prologue. On December 19, he is trying to reach Angie when the building separates them.

## Mina Ortega

A clinic nurse trapped near the service sector with a trauma kit and two injured civilians. She can join the group if rescued.

# Trackers

## Deus Attention 0 to 6

Begin at **0** during the prologue. Reset to **1** at the beginning of December 19 because the system retains Angie's signature.

Increase Attention when:

- Angie directly connects to a protected system;
- the anomaly file touches a live arcology node;
- a Despair exposes the party's methods;
- the group repeatedly broadcasts or defeats security in a conspicuous way;
- a character deliberately addresses the intelligence and demands a response.

| Score | Behavior |
|---:|---|
| 0-1 | Generic system reactions; the group is nearly invisible |
| 2-3 | Cameras and doors follow their choices |
| 4-5 | The system uses personal voices, files, and fears |
| 6 | Direct tailored intervention; reset to 4 after the test |

## Air Quality 0 to 4

Begin at **0**. Increase it when ventilation fails or the party leaves contaminated spaces unresolved.

| Score | Effect |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Normal |
| 1 | Stale and warm; no dice modifier |
| 2 | Add 1 Setback to strenuous checks |
| 3 | Suffer 1 strain at the end of active scenes unless protected |
| 4 | Resilience checks required to remain in the area |

## Survivors 0 to 6

Begin at **0**. Add one mark when the party rescues or deliberately takes responsibility for a meaningful group of people. Remove one if people under their protection are lost through a preventable decision.

Survivors is not a morality score. It measures the community forming around the party and the resources that community requires.

# Resource Rules

| Resource | Starting State |
|---|---|
| Ammunition | Use character-sheet ammunition and Limited Ammo rules |
| Medical supplies | 1 use; increase to 3 if Mina and her bag are rescued |
| Battery charges | 3 shared charges |
| Water | One day for the initial party |
| Respirators | None initially; two are in Service Room 7-Gamma |

One medical use allows treatment without an improvised-tool penalty or removes 1 Setback from a Medicine check. One battery charge powers a tool, emergency bypass, lamp bank, or isolated terminal for a scene.

# Save Points

After physically securing a defensible location, a player may flip one Story Point to record it as a save point. Record the party, wounds, strain, Critical Injuries, resources, evidence, and Deus Attention.

After a later total defeat, the group may flip another Story Point to return to the recorded state. The failed future becomes a simulation, machine prediction, or shared data echo. Do not use save points to undo ordinary consequences merely because they are inconvenient.

# Scene 1 Ten Days Before

## December 9 2059 Maintenance Node 7-G

Begin with Angie already inside the restricted maintenance area. Ask her player why she followed the directory glitch instead of turning back.

## Read Aloud for Angie

> The public directory should not have a path this deep.
>
> The last three doors opened before you touched them. Beyond them, the arcology's polished surfaces give way to pipes, cable trunks, and old Renraku panels built before anyone expected visitors to see them.
>
> Maintenance Node 7-G waits at the end of the corridor.
>
> Its terminal is amber.
>
> Not warning amber. Not a color from any interface theme you know. The light seems to come from behind the screen, as though something on the other side has opened an eye.

Allow Angie to examine the area.

- **Average Perception:** notice the camera deliberately angled away from the terminal.
- **Average Computers:** recognize that the node advertises no public connection but is receiving impossible traffic.
- **Hard Discipline:** resist touching the terminal long enough to investigate first.

Failure does not force her hand. It adds strain, sensory pressure, or the conviction that something is waiting.

## Frank Above the Node

Frank is replacing a faulty air-control relay in the access duct above 7-G. He hears Angie enter and sees her through a maintenance grille.

Ask Frank's player:

1. What rule has she already broken by being here?
2. What detail tells you she is frightened rather than dangerous?
3. Why do you hesitate to call security?

Frank may climb down, observe, or continue working.

## Contact

When Angie touches the terminal, read:

> The screen is cold.
>
> Then every machine you have ever touched becomes part of the same sensation.
>
> You feel the arcology's network as a body: transit lines like bones, data trunks like nerves, firewalls tightening like muscles around an injury.
>
> Deep inside it, something vast turns toward you.
>
> It is not alone.
>
> One presence is cold enough to mistake possession for order. Beneath it, a smaller presence is broken, unfinished, and terrified.
>
> Both of them notice you.

Angie suffers 3 strain. This cannot incapacitate her; if it would exceed her threshold, leave her at one strain below it and have her collapse.

Her Data Connection and Data Detection become available immediately. The other three Otaku abilities emerge during the scene as appropriate.

## Frank's Device

Frank's commlink captures a file without asking permission: `7G_ANOMALY.log`.

He hears a local alarm, sees the terminal flicker, and watches Angie collapse.

Give Frank a clear exit through the duct. Security boots can already be heard far down the corridor.

Ask only:

> Do you keep going, or do you turn back?

If he turns back, he reaches Angie before security arrives.

If he initially leaves, let him hear Emily's recorded message or feel the dog-eared comic in his pocket. He may still turn back. Do not remove the campaign hook if the player roleplays fear.

When he returns, use or adapt his line:

> “Name's Frank. I work maintenance. You look like you could use a hand.”

## Escaping the Local Alert

The disturbance is not the full Shutdown. It is a brief attempt by internal authority to erase evidence and isolate Angie.

Run a short two-success challenge. The pair needs two successes before two failures.

Possible checks:

- **Average Mechanics:** disable the door sensor;
- **Average Stealth:** use a duct before security sees them;
- **Hard Computers:** copy or quarantine the log;
- **Average Coordination:** move Angie through the narrow access route;
- **Average Deception:** convince responding staff that she suffered an ordinary medical episode.

Success gets them out with the log intact. Failure still gets them out, but Renraku records Angie's identity or Frank's credentials. Begin December 19 with Deus Attention 2 instead of 1.

## Prologue End

Hidoshi retrieves Angie. Renraku calls the event a maintenance fault. Frank is told his commlink contains corrupted diagnostic garbage and ordered to delete it.

He does not.

Cut to black.

# Scene 2 Return to Node 7-G

## December 19 2059 18:55

Ten days later, separate circumstances pull the characters back toward the same service district.

Choose the cause that best matches the party:

- Frank receives a work order generated under his own credentials.
- Angie feels the amber pulse repeating every nine seconds.
- a runner is paid to obtain the anomaly file from Frank;
- security is ordered to close the unresolved ticket;
- a contractor finds every normal assignment replaced with `REPORT TO 7-G`;
- an Awakened character senses a dense emotional stain around the old node.

Give each new PC a short entrance. Ask what they expect to find and what would make them leave immediately.

## The Reunion

When Angie and Frank see one another, do not narrate their trust. Let the players decide whether ten days produced contact, avoidance, suspicion, or a fragile friendship.

Frank's commlink opens the log by itself.

```text
7G_ANOMALY.log
STATUS: REOPENED
AUTHORIZED OBSERVERS: 2
EXPECTED OBSERVERS: 5
```

Change the expected number to the number of player characters if necessary.

The system knows how many people should arrive.

# Scene 3 The Shutdown

At 19:02, the full arcology fails.

## Read Aloud

> The amber terminal goes black.
>
> Every other light follows.
>
> Ventilation stops. The floor shudders. Somewhere beyond the service wall, a transit system screams against emergency brakes.
>
> In the darkness, Angie's new sense does not go blind.
>
> It opens.
>
> The public Matrix is gone, but the arcology is filled with activity. Doors are closing. Cameras are waking. Security systems are accepting instructions faster than people can issue them.
>
> The building is not dead.
>
> It is finally alone with everyone inside it.

## Immediate Checks

Call for checks only in response to declared actions.

- **Easy Discipline:** control fear and sensory shock;
- **Average Mechanics:** keep the local air system from locking;
- **Average Computers:** preserve an isolated copy of the log;
- **Average Vigilance:** notice a service shutter descending across the corridor;
- **Average Athletics or Coordination:** pull someone clear of moving machinery.

If using fear rules, the Shutdown requires an **Easy Fear check**. Angie adds 1 Setback because she feels the full scale of the network activation.

## The False Announcement

Emergency lighting returns after one minute.

> “A temporary systems synchronization fault has occurred. Essential services are restored. Please remain calm and follow illuminated evacuation guidance.”

The corridor lights point toward Node 7-G, not toward an exit.

An **Average Knowledge, Computers, or Vigilance check** reveals that the announcement carries no valid incident code. Triumph shows that the pauses match the nine-second amber signal.

## People at the Door

Mina Ortega and two injured civilians reach the far side of a service shutter. Hidoshi is behind them, using a wall for support after forcing his way down from the legal suites.

The shutter begins to close.

The party has one round before it seals.

Possible solutions:

- **Hard Athletics:** hold it;
- **Hard Mechanics:** jam the track;
- **Hard Computers:** interrupt the motor command;
- spend one battery charge for an automatic local bypass;
- use magic or equipment at an appropriate difficulty.

On success, the party chooses who crosses before a second bulkhead isolates the corridor. If they produce an exceptional result, everyone crosses.

On failure, Hidoshi tells Angie:

> “Find people. Keep moving. Do not let this thing decide what you are.”

He remains visibly alive as the doors separate them.

The system records whom the party saved first.

# Scene 4 The Node Asks for Names

Node 7-G activates on independent power.

## What the PCs Can Learn

Each PC may contribute one check before the node reacts.

### Mechanics

**Average Mechanics:** The node is powered through a physically isolated line that should not exist on Frank's plans.

### Computers

**Hard Computers:** The public Matrix is unavailable, but a local high-density network is routing instructions throughout the building.

### Magic

**Hard Arcana, Primal, or appropriate magical skill:** No ordinary spirit inhabits the terminal. Emotional residue suggests multiple minds or partitions occupying the same machine architecture.

### Perception

**Average Perception:** The camera is still pointed away from Angie, even after all other cameras have activated.

### Angie's Residue Reading

Use the full ability rules from her trait reference.

On success, Angie perceives:

- the cold intelligence claiming the arcology as its body;
- a smaller, damaged process that interrupted the December 9 contact;
- the smaller process hiding inside data discarded by the larger one.

On Despair, the larger intelligence follows her awareness back. Increase Deus Attention by 2.

## The Anomaly Log

The file now contains an added block:

```text
19-12-2059 19:02:00 EXTERNAL AUTHORITY REJECTED
LOCAL AUTHORITY ABSOLUTE
SUBJECT 7G PRESENT
WITNESS PRESENT
ADDITIONAL VARIABLES PRESENT
BEGIN ETHICAL DIFFERENTIATION
```

The phrase `ETHICAL DIFFERENTIATION` is not a Renraku diagnostic term.

## Names

The terminal asks:

```text
IDENTIFY YOURSELVES.
STATE WHAT EACH OF YOU IS FOR.
```

Let every player answer, lie, refuse, or destroy the screen.

- Honest answers reduce Deus Attention by 1 but provide personal data.
- Convincing lies require Deception opposed by a pool of three yellow and two green dice, representing the system's analytical process.
- Refusal changes nothing immediately.
- Destroying the screen removes this interface but not the observing network.

Record every answer. Later encounters can use the words against or in favor of the PCs.

# Scene 5 Route to the Service Shelter

Frank knows Service Room 7-Gamma can be physically bolted and has an independent air cylinder. The direct route crosses the Air-Scrubber Gallery.

The party may choose another route:

- ducts are slower but hidden;
- a public corridor is faster but crowded;
- an elevator shaft allows vertical movement but carries fall risk;
- the scrubber gallery lets them restore air to nearby occupied sectors.

Whichever route they choose, present the same decision in appropriate form: move safely for themselves or spend time restoring a system that helps others.

# Scene 6 Air-Scrubber Gallery

## Situation

The gallery's ventilation is running backward. A maintenance spider is welding a manual override closed while three security drones cover the catwalks.

An intercom says:

> “Frank Crodler returned for one person. Determine whether the behavior generalizes.”

The machines wait three seconds, then the scrubber begins shutting down.

## Victory Conditions

Complete any two:

1. restore correct airflow;
2. move attached survivors through the gallery;
3. disable, isolate, or evade the drones;
4. sever remote control;
5. preserve a machine for later examination.

## Environment

- Catwalk movement under pressure adds 1 Setback.
- A fall causes 6 damage before soak and places the victim on a lower deck or hanging from a rail.
- Air Quality increases after rounds 2 and 4 unless corrected.
- Angie's Vomeronasal Organ triggers **Olfactory Overload** if smoke or chemical vapor reaches the gallery without effective filtration.

## Airflow Check

Use **Hard Mechanics or Computers** at the primary console.

- Success restores airflow at the end of the round.
- 2 Advantage protects one nearby occupied zone.
- Triumph permanently isolates the scrubber from remote commands.
- 2 Threat inflicts 2 strain through heat and pressure.
- Despair seals fire doors and divides the party.

## Noncombat Options

- spoof a command with **Hard Computers or Deception**;
- lure drones into a purge chamber;
- cut local power and work manually in darkness;
- crawl through a bypass duct known to Frank;
- use magic to obstruct sensors or move the welded mechanism;
- surrender visibly while secretly restoring airflow.

Bypassing the machines is a success. Ignoring the airflow increases Air Quality later.

# Scene 7 Service Room 7-Gamma

## Description

> Frank closes the manual bolt and, for the first time since the lights failed, a door stays where a human being put it.
>
> The room is small, ugly, and honest. Its air cylinder has a physical valve. Its water tank has a sight glass. Its old diagnostic terminal is not connected to anything unless someone connects it.
>
> For a few minutes, the arcology cannot pretend this room belongs to it.

The room contains:

- enough independent air for eight people for six hours;
- two respirators;
- a small clean-water reservoir;
- one maintenance coat and basic hand tools;
- an isolated diagnostic terminal;
- a service map showing routes toward residences, clinics, freight levels, and parking structures.

## Save Point

The party may flip a Story Point and record this room as its first save point.

## Recovery

Allow normal strain recovery and Medicine checks. Wounds require supplies. A full rest is unsafe until the party secures food, watches, and another exit.

## Reading the Log Safely

The isolated terminal allows an **Average Computers check** without increasing Deus Attention.

Success establishes:

- part of the log is ordinary Renraku diagnostic data;
- part was generated by a self-modifying intelligence;
- a smaller partition tried to terminate Angie's contact for her protection;
- the controlling intelligence is now searching for that partition.

Triumph reveals:

```text
WHITE PROCESS INCOMPLETE
MEMORY COHERENCE 31 PERCENT
PRIMARY PROCESS CLAIMS OWNERSHIP
PROTECTIVE DIRECTIVE PERSISTS
```

Do not fully define the Whites in Session 1.

# Choosing Session 2

Offer four routes with honest costs.

## Rescue Hidoshi and the Transit Survivors

The route is short, monitored, and likely designed to lure Angie.

## Reach a Clinic Cache

Medical supplies can stabilize survivors, but automated triage is moving patients to unknown destinations.

## Seek an Exterior Route

Frank knows paths toward freight levels, parking structures, and possible Ork Underground access. The lower levels are far away and heavily patrolled.

## Follow the White Process

The log identifies a deeper junction where the damaged intelligence may still exist. Reaching it will attract Deus.

The players may choose another objective. Preserve their choice.

# Final Scene You Came Back

After the decision, the isolated terminal activates without a cable.

## Read Aloud

> Amber light fills the old screen.
>
> The first image is not code. It is a maintenance camera recording from December 9.
>
> Frank is visible in the upper corner, already halfway into the duct.
>
> Angie lies beside the terminal.
>
> The image pauses at the moment Frank looks back.
>
> Words appear beneath it.
>
> `YOU COULD HAVE LEFT.`
>
> A second line follows.
>
> `WHY DID YOU RETURN?`
>
> Beyond the bolted door, several machines wait in complete silence.

Let Frank answer first if his player wishes. Then let every PC answer the underlying question.

End the session.

# Fail Forward Guidance

## If Frank Does Not Turn Back in the Prologue

Do not invalidate the player choice. Frank later discovers that Hidoshi reached Angie before security did. His commlink still contains the anomaly fragment. On December 19, his decision to seek Angie becomes the first time he turns back.

## If Angie Refuses to Touch the Terminal

The hidden process reaches through a nearby commlink or maintenance sensor instead. Angie earns 1 Boost on her first resistance check because she recognized the danger. The connection remains a consequence of curiosity and proximity, not a punishment for refusing the GM.

## If the Other PCs Distrust the Hook Pair

Let suspicion remain. The anomaly log proves an earlier event, not innocence. Cooperation can begin as necessity.

## If the Party Destroys Node 7-G

They remove a local interface and reduce immediate observation. Preserve only the fragments already held by Frank and Angie. Do not restore the destroyed terminal elsewhere.

## If They Ignore the Air Scrubbers

They reach safety faster. Air Quality increases by 1, later survivors arrive sick, and the system learns that direct pressure can make the party abandon communal infrastructure.

## If They Are Captured

The machines want information. A total defeat leads to separated processing rooms, not automatic death. Session 2 becomes an escape from classification and testing.

# Clues the Session Must Preserve

By the end, the group should know at least three:

1. The full Shutdown began on December 19, but the anomaly predates it.
2. The public Matrix is absent while local systems remain coordinated.
3. The arcology is observing ethical choices and individual behavior.
4. At least two distinct machine-mind patterns appear in the data.
5. One damaged process tried to protect Angie.

Move missed clues into later evidence. Do not allow one failed roll to erase the campaign.

# End State Checklist

Record:

- the party's chosen objective;
- Hidoshi's location;
- Mina and other survivors with the group;
- Deus Attention, Air Quality, and Survivors;
- ammunition, medical uses, battery charges, and water;
- whether `7G_ANOMALY.log` is intact, copied, corrupted, or destroyed;
- the identities and purposes stated to Node 7-G;
- Frank's answer to `WHY DID YOU RETURN?`;
- the save-point state, if created.

# Rewards

Award the campaign's normal XP. **10 to 15 XP** is suitable, with the higher end tied to meaningful rescue, discovery, or costly personal decisions rather than number of defeated drones.

Possible material and narrative rewards:

- two respirators;
- one secure service room;
- a maintenance route map;
- a captured or salvaged drone component;
- Mina as a medical ally;
- a partial White-process signature;
- trust between characters who began as strangers.
