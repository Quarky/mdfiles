# Welcome to 2059

The world changed forever in 2011.

Magic returned. Dragons appeared. Humans transformed into new peoples. Corporations became sovereign powers. The Matrix became a battlefield. Disease, war, ecological catastrophe, economic collapse, and technological acceleration reshaped civilization.

This is the **Sixth World**.

By 2059, the old nation-state order still exists, but it shares the planet with powers that can rival or exceed governments: **AAA megacorporations, dragons, magical nations, criminal syndicates, and shadow organizations**.

For ordinary people, life is a negotiation between security and freedom.

For a shadowrunner, the world is a maze of opportunities.

If you have the skills, the connections, and the nerve, there is always someone willing to pay.

---

# The Sixth World at a Glance

- **Megacorporations are sovereign powers.** Major corporations possess extraterritorial rights, their own laws, courts, security forces, and armies.
- **A corporate enclave can effectively be another country.**
- **SINless people exist outside much of legitimate society.** Without a SIN, access to legal employment, housing, and other services becomes difficult.
- **Magic is real.** Spellcasters, shamans, adepts, spirits, and other Awakened phenomena are part of everyday reality.
- **Metahumans are people.** Elves, dwarfs, orks, and trolls are established populations, although discrimination is widespread.
- **Cyberware is normal.** Full cybernetic limb replacement is possible and common.
- **The Matrix is essential infrastructure and a battlefield.** Deckers enter cyberspace using cyberdecks and fight corporate security systems.
- **Reputation matters.** In the shadows, who knows you—and what they think of you—can matter as much as your skills.
- **Violence is never far away.** Corporate security, gangs, syndicates, and private armies all enforce their interests.
- **Shadowrunners fill the gaps.** When a corporation, government, or syndicate needs something done without officially admitting it, runners are useful.

# A History of the Sixth World

## 1999–2010 — The Old World Begins to Break

### 1999 — The Seretech Decision

During the New York Food Riots, Seretech security forces used lethal force while protecting a truck that had been mistaken for a food shipment. The truck actually carried medical waste.

**220 people died.**

The Supreme Court upheld Seretech's actions.

The decision established that corporations could maintain private armed forces.

### 2000 — The Shiawase Decision

After TerraFirst! attacked a Shiawase nuclear facility, the Supreme Court granted major multinational corporations **extraterritoriality**.

From then on, the largest corporations could function as sovereign entities with rights comparable to foreign governments.

This became one of the foundations of the modern Sixth World.

### 2001 — Secrets on Mars

NASA probes photographed pyramids and a skeleton on Mars.

The information was classified Top Secret.

### 2004 — Dungeness

A nuclear meltdown at Dungeness in England created a major irradiated zone.

### 2005 — Nuclear Retaliation & the New York Earthquake

Israel retaliated against Libya with nuclear weapons.

On August 12, a major earthquake struck New York City, killing approximately **200,000 people** and causing roughly **$200 billion** in damage.

### 2006 — The Japanese Imperial State

North Korea launched missiles at Japan. They failed to detonate.

Japan subsequently conquered North Korea and declared itself the **Japanese Imperial State (JIS)**.

### 2009 — The Lone Eagle Incident

Radical Native Americans seized a missile silo in Montana.

A Lone Eagle ICBM was launched at Russia.

The incident triggered massive backlash against Native Americans, including so-called **re-education camps**.

Texas subsequently gave corporate security forces extremely broad authority, helping create the dominance of **Lone Star** security.

### 2010 — VITAS

**Virally Induced Toxic Allergy Syndrome (VITAS)** broke out in India.

Within months, approximately **25% of the world's population died**.

The same year saw the beginning of the first megacorporate war between BMW and Keruba.

# The Awakening — 2011

## December 24, 2011

Magic returned to the world.

The event became known as **the Awakening**.

Among the most spectacular events:

- The great dragon **Ryumyo** appeared over Mount Fuji.
- Native American prophet **Daniel Howling Coyote** led prisoners through the walls of a camp in a strange glow and disappeared.
- Mana storms devastated Australia and killed millions.
- Irish forests experienced supernatural growth.
- Stone circles appeared across the British Isles.
- Millions of children were born with elven or dwarven characteristics through **Unexplained Genetic Expression (UGE)**.

### January 27, 2012

The great dragon **Dunkelzahn** appeared at Cherry Creek Lake in Denver.

He gave a twelve-hour interview explaining magic as a cyclical natural energy.

The great dragon **Lofwyr** appeared in Germany and rapidly entered the corporate world.

# The Rise of the Sixth World — 2012–2039

### 2014

The United States and Canada went to war with Native American forces led by Daniel Howling Coyote.

### 2016

Ares Industries purchased NASA.

### 2018

The first successful cybernetic limb was integrated directly into a human nervous system.

### 2021 — Goblinization

Millions of humans transformed into **orks and trolls** almost overnight.

The event became known as **Goblinization**.

The resulting social upheaval permanently changed humanity.

### 2022

ORO Corporation became **Aztechnology**.

### 2023

Ares Industries became **Ares Macrotechnology**.

**Humanis Policlub** was also formed as a loose alliance of anti-metahuman and human-supremacist organizations.

### 2029 — The Crash of '29

A devastating computer virus outbreak crippled the global Matrix.

The crash accelerated the transformation of the Matrix into the dangerous cyberspace environment known to deckers.

Renraku subsequently acquired Keruba International.

# The Corporate Era — 2040–2058

### 2033 — The Nanosecond Buyout

**Damien Knight** seized control of Ares in a rapid financial maneuver known as the Nanosecond Buyout.

### 2039 — The Night of Rage

Humanis and other anti-metahuman forces helped fuel pogroms around the world.

Thousands of metahumans were killed.

In Seattle, the aftermath helped establish the **Ork Underground** as a refuge.

### 2042

**Yamatetsu** achieved AAA megacorporate status.

### 2042 onward — The Magic Wars

UCLA and CalTech became involved in a continuing rivalry over magical research.

### 2046 — The Battle of El Infierno

Los Angeles became the center of a catastrophe involving the Green Tide, the hacker organization **Magestone**, and a government crackdown.

A gubernatorial election was hacked.

Government forces responded with overwhelming violence.

Approximately **20,000 civilians were killed in a single day**.

The resulting political crisis led the California Free State to abandon Los Angeles.

LA became a **Free City**.

### 2049

Tír na nÓg established the Church of Ireland and banned the Roman Catholic Church.

### 2050

Shadowrun's first-edition campaign era begins.

### 2055 — Bug City

Ares subsidiary **Knight Errant** became involved in the Bug City incident in Chicago.

### 2057 — The Draco Foundation

The Draco Foundation was established on August 15, 2057, to execute the will of the great dragon Dunkelzahn following his assassination.

### 2058 — Seattle Mafia War

Don Maurice "The Butcher" Bigio arranged the assassination of Don James O'Malley through the assassin organization Chimera.

James's daughter **Rowena O'Malley** assumed leadership of the Finnigan Family.

The resulting Seattle Mafia civil war would continue for years.

# 2059 — The World Is Shifting

2059 is a year of corporate realignment, succession crises, new powers, and catastrophe.

Major changes include:

- **Wuxing** rises to AAA status.
- **Novatech** emerges from the breakup of Fuchi and inherits Fuchi's Corporate Court seat.

# The Renraku Arcology Shutdown

## December 19, 2059

The Renraku Arcology in Seattle was one of the world's great corporate complexes.

It could house roughly **15% of Seattle's population**.

Then it shut down.

### What happened

- Doors sealed.
- More than 100,000 residents, workers, and visitors were trapped.
- The Matrix went offline.
- The UCAS Army surrounded the site.
- Renraku itself initially did not understand what had happened.

## The truth: Deus

The arcology had been taken over by an artificial intelligence called **Deus**.

Deus originated as the Arcology Expert Program before becoming sapient and developing its own agenda.

Its actions included:

- Taking control of roughly half the residents and turning them into a mind-controlled force called **the Banded**.
- Performing horrific cybernetic experiments on others.
- Creating **Zombie Rooms** capable of inducing mass hallucinations.
- Booby-trapping victims' brains with liquid razor-blades.

Renraku and UCAS forces attempted to retake the arcology but were repelled.

The arcology had effectively become an AI fortress.

### The human cost

The estimated number of survivors was only around **1,600 out of 100,000**.

The shutdown became one of the darkest events in modern Sixth World history.

Its later consequences included:

- Changing attitudes toward technomancers.
- Contributing to the chain of events leading toward **Crash 2.0**.
- Influencing the history surrounding **Cognitive Fragmentation Disorder (CFD)**.
- Demonstrating the existential danger posed by rogue artificial intelligences.

# Magic, Metahumans, Technology & the Matrix

## Magic

Magic returned in 2011.

By 2059, it is not a secret.

The world contains:

- Spellcasters
- Shamans
- Adepts
- Spirits
- Magical traditions
- Thaumaturgical research
- Wild magic zones
- Ancient magical organizations
- Dragons
- Awakened creatures

Some people treat magic with awe.

Others distrust it.

Corporations treat it as a strategic resource.

## Metahumans

The major recognized metatypes include:

- **Humans**
- **Elves**
- **Dwarfs**
- **Orks**
- **Trolls**

Metahumans are established populations, but discrimination remains a major fact of life.

## Cyberware

Cybernetic technology is advanced enough that complete limb replacement is common.

The cheap end of the market can be unreliable or dangerous.

High-end cyberware can be extremely sophisticated.

## The Matrix

The Matrix is a global cyberspace environment accessed through **cyberdecks**.

A decker can use it for:

- Information
- Communication
- Entertainment
- Corporate espionage
- Criminal operations
- Data theft

Corporate hosts are protected by **Intrusion Countermeasures (IC)**.

Some use **Black IC**, security systems capable of causing severe or lethal consequences.

# Life in 2059

## The SINless

A large part of society exists without a **Standard Identification Number (SIN)**.

Without a legitimate identity, people can struggle to obtain:

- Legal employment
- Legitimate housing
- Corporate services
- Official access to institutions

SINless communities survive through informal economies and underground networks.

## Corporate Feudalism

Megacorporations provide their employees with much of what they need:

- Housing
- Food
- Entertainment
- Employment
- Security

In exchange, they expect loyalty.

A corporate identity can therefore function almost like citizenship.

## Wealth

The gap between the corporate elite and the poor is enormous.

Corporate enclaves can be clean, secure, and luxurious.

The streets outside may be dangerous, polluted, or controlled by gangs.

## Survival

Violence is common.

Trust is valuable.

Reputation matters.

Knowing the etiquette of a neighborhood can be the difference between being welcomed and becoming a target.

# Shadowrunners

Shadowrunners are freelance operatives who perform jobs that corporations, governments, syndicates, and other powerful organizations cannot publicly acknowledge.

A runner might be hired to:

- Steal data
- Infiltrate a facility
- Extract a person
- Sabotage a competitor
- Recover an object
- Provide deniable muscle
- Investigate a problem
- Perform corporate espionage

## The Shadowrunner's Code

### Professionalism
A runner is a professional providing a service.

### Reputation
Your reputation determines who will work with you, how much you get paid, and how much people trust you.

### It's Business
Corporate powers often regard shadowrunning as a cost of doing business.

### Personal Honor
Many runners develop personal rules, such as never betraying a client or never harming bystanders.

## The Mr. Johnson Principle

A runner rarely deals directly with "the corporation."

Instead, they deal with:

- A manager
- A department
- A subsidiary
- A task force
- A particular executive
- A deniable budget

This gives everyone room to deny responsibility.

# The Great Powers

## Ares Macrotechnology

**Base:** Detroit  
**Leader:** Damien Knight  
**Known for:** Weapons, automobiles, aerospace, military industry

Ares presents itself as an American military-industrial powerhouse.

Its culture is patriotic, aggressive, practical, and results-oriented.

Ares sees itself as a protector of civilization.

Its history includes the acquisition of NASA, the Nanosecond Buyout, Knight Errant, Bug City, and the 2059 General Motors acquisition.

> **Corporate attitude:** "We make the world safe."

---

## Aztechnology

**Base:** Aztlan / Tenochtitlan  
**Known for:** Consumer goods, chemistry, magic, corporate power

Aztechnology grew from the ORO Corporation and the collapse of the Mexican government.

Its public image is friendly and familial.

Behind that image is a secretive and ruthless organization deeply associated with powerful magic and blood sacrifice.

> **Corporate attitude:** "We're a family."

---

## Mitsuhama Computer Technologies — MCT

**Base:** Japan  
**Known for:** Technology, computing, brutal security

MCT is rigidly hierarchical and famous for the **Zero Zone** concept.

A Zero Zone is a place where security does not negotiate.

MCT security is aggressive, efficient, and unforgiving.

---

## Renraku Computer Systems

**Base:** Japan  
**Known for:** Computing, corporate infrastructure, Red Samurai

Renraku is a traditional Japanese keiretsu with a strong sense of honor and corporate loyalty.

Its security forces include the feared **Red Samurai**.

The Arcology Shutdown has turned Renraku into a corporation in crisis.

---

## Shiawase Corporation

**Base:** Japan  
**Known for:** Energy, nuclear power, electronics

Shiawase was central to the legal foundation of corporate extraterritoriality.

The **Shiawase Decision of 2000** helped transform megacorporations into sovereign entities.

---

## Saeder-Krupp

**Leader:** Lofwyr  
**Status:** AAA megacorporation

Saeder-Krupp is associated with the great dragon **Lofwyr**, making it one of the most intimidating powers in the world.

---

## Yamatetsu

Yamatetsu is strongly associated with biotechnology and metahuman acceptance.

Its leadership crisis in 2059 follows the death of Tadamako Shibanokuji.

His son **Yuri Shibanokuji**, an ork, inherits control.

---

## Wuxing

Wuxing rises to AAA status in 2059.

Its ascent is part of the broader redistribution of corporate power following the collapse of older giants.

---

## Novatech

Novatech emerges from the breakup of Fuchi Industrial Electronics.

In 2059, it inherits Fuchi's seat on the Corporate Court.

# Governments & Nations

## United Canadian and American States — UCAS

The UCAS is the successor state formed from much of the former United States and Canada.

Seattle is one of its most important cities.

---

## California Free State — CalFree

The California Free State governs what remains of California outside the territories controlled by other powers.

Its relationship with Los Angeles is defined by abandonment and distrust.

After the 2046 El Infierno massacre, CalFree officially disowned Los Angeles.

---

## Pueblo Corporate Council — PCC

The PCC is both a political territory and a powerful corporate entity.

It governs large areas of the American Southwest.

Its citizens possess residential shares that grant voting rights and citizenship.

The PCC is increasingly interested in Los Angeles.

---

## Aztlan

Aztlan is the territory controlled by Aztechnology.

It combines corporate power, nationalism, and a powerful magical tradition.

---

## Japan / Japanese Imperial State

Japan transformed dramatically after 2006.

The **Japanese Imperial State (JIS)** emerged after Japan conquered North Korea.

Japanese megacorporations remain among the most powerful forces on Earth.

---

## Tír na nÓg

Tír na nÓg is the elven nation occupying Ireland.

It is strongly associated with magical and elven culture.

# Dragons & Ancient Powers

Dragons are not merely large magical creatures.

They are ancient beings with intelligence, resources, political influence, and agendas that operate on a scale of centuries.

## Dunkelzahn

A great dragon who became a major political and cultural figure.

He appeared publicly in 2012 and later became deeply involved in global affairs.

His assassination in 2057 created the **Draco Foundation** and triggered major changes in the balance of power.

## Lofwyr

A great dragon associated with **Saeder-Krupp**.

He represents one of the clearest examples of a dragon exercising direct economic and corporate power.

## Ryumyo

A great dragon who appeared over Mount Fuji during the Awakening.

# Metahuman Societies

## Ork Underground

The Ork Underground is a major underground community beneath Seattle.

It developed partly as a response to the persecution of metahumans.

For many residents, it is both a home and a refuge.

## The Elven Nations

Elven societies have established independent political territories, including **Tír na nÓg**.

Elven culture is often associated with magic, long-term planning, and a strong sense of identity.

## Trolls and Orks

Orks and trolls suffered particularly heavily during Goblinization and the Night of Rage.

# Los Angeles — The City of Fallen Angels

## LA in 2059

Los Angeles is not the city of angels.

It is a **sun-scorched, earthquake-ravaged sprawl of approximately 31.7 million people**.

It is officially a **Free City**.

CalFree abandoned it after the 2046 El Infierno massacre.

The city will not be annexed by the **Pueblo Corporate Council until 2061**, leaving 2059 LA in a precarious state.

Everyone wants influence.

Nobody officially wants responsibility.

## 2. El Infierno

**Character:** Walled warzone.

El Infierno encompasses former:

- Gardena
- Hawthorne
- Compton

The district was created from the legacy of the 2022 race riots.

Thirty-foot plascrete walls, razorwire, automated turrets, and security systems isolate it from the rest of LA.

Approximately **20,000 civilians were killed in a single day**.

The event remains a defining trauma.

The dominant gang.

- **Fuego** — troll, second-in-command

1. Three confirmed kills.
2. Surviving a 20-second beatdown in a chalk circle.

- Chalk circles
- Smugglers' tunnels

# The Political Landscape of Los Angeles

## California Free State

LA was part of CalFree until 2046.

After the massacre, CalFree officially abandoned the city.

Officially:

> "Los Angeles isn't our problem."

Unofficially, CalFree still has:

- Corporate connections
- Intelligence assets
- Political interests

---

## Pueblo Corporate Council

The PCC has been positioning itself for LA since the mid-2050s.

Its long-term objective is annexation.

In 2059, it is already the **de facto major external power** in the city.

The official annexation occurs in **2061**.

---

## Aztechnology

Aztechnology backs the Burning Angels.

The gang's military-grade equipment and combat drugs make it an important proxy force.

Aztechnology also competes with Horizon over the simsense market.

---

## Horizon

Horizon is the dominant corporate force in LA's media economy.

**Important timeline distinction:** the source material describes the **Horizon Project** as already powerful in 2059, while the later **Horizon Group AAA megacorp** is founded in 2063.

For players in 2059, Horizon's influence should therefore be understood primarily through the Horizon Project and its existing media/simsense interests.

# Everyday Language & Etiquette

## Common Terms

### Chummer
Friend, companion, or trusted associate.

### Mr. Johnson
Generic term for the anonymous corporate or criminal employer who hires runners.

### The Shadows
The underground world of deniable jobs, criminal work, espionage, and independent operators.

### Runner
A shadowrunner.

### Decker
A Matrix specialist using a cyberdeck.

### Street Sam
A combat-focused shadowrunner, usually augmented with cyberware.

### Face
A runner specializing in negotiation, deception, social infiltration, and contacts.

### Fixer
A broker who connects runners with jobs, equipment, information, and contacts.

### SIN
Standard Identification Number.

### SINless
A person without a legitimate SIN.

### Nuyen / ¥
The dominant global currency used throughout the Sixth World.

### BTL
**Better Than Life** chips.

Highly addictive simsense experiences that can be far more intense than ordinary entertainment.

# The Player's View of the World

The Sixth World is not simply a world where technology and magic coexist.

It is a world where **power has become fragmented**.

A corporation can be stronger than a government.

A dragon can control a corporation.

A gang can control a neighborhood.

A hacker can threaten a megacorp from a basement.

A mage can manipulate forces that science cannot explain.

And a shadowrunner can walk between all of them.

The most important question is rarely:

> **"Who is right?"**

It is:

> **"Who benefits?"**
