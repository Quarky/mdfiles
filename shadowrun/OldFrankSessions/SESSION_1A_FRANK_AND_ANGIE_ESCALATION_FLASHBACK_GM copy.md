# Session 1A — Frank and Angie: The Eighteen Days Before

## GM-only living-world insert

This is a separate, GM-facing prelude for the Shadowrun Genesys *Renraku Arcology: Shutdown* campaign. It does not replace the all-floor chronology or the existing Session 1. Insert it before **Return to Node 7-G, December 19 at 18:55 PST**.

The prelude follows Frank and Angie on every day from December 1 through the Shutdown. They begin in separate lives inside the arcology, meet in Maintenance Node 7-G on December 9, live with the consequences for nine days, and return to the same service district immediately before the building seals.

The December 9 meeting is a **predetermined and player-agreed continuity event**. The players still control what Frank and Angie say, feel, notice, and do around its fixed anchors. Rolls change costs, clues, and future assets; they do not erase the meeting or move it to a different date.

## How to run it

### Recommended pacing

| Segment | Table time | Method |
|---|---:|---|
| December 1–4 | 12–18 minutes | Quick alternating vignettes |
| December 5–8 | 15–25 minutes | Longer cuts as the pattern becomes personal |
| December 9 | 15–25 minutes | Fully played agreed event |
| December 10–18 | 15–25 minutes | Consequence montage with selected expanded scenes |
| December 19, before 19:02 | 5–10 minutes | Continuous play into the existing Shutdown scene |

For a shorter opening, read the shared description for each day and let each player answer one prompt. For a longer opening, expand any day marked **Good full scene**.

### The flashback contract

Tell the players plainly:

> We are playing memories that lead to an event we already agreed happened. You control your character inside each memory. On December 9, Angie reaches the amber terminal and collapses; Frank initially retreats, chooses to return, helps her, and keeps the anomaly log. Rolls determine what the event costs, what they learn, and what resources survive—not whether those anchors occur.

This makes the agreement visible and prevents the fixed scene from masquerading as an open branch.

### Fixed anchors and open space

| Fixed continuity | Player-controlled space |
|---|---|
| Angie reaches Node 7-G on December 9 | Why she follows the anomaly and what she hopes it is |
| Angie touches the amber terminal | What she investigates first and how long she resists the pressure |
| The contact overwhelms her | What the two presences feel like to her |
| Frank witnesses the collapse from the duct | What he noticed about Angie before the contact |
| Frank begins to withdraw, remembers TunnelRat, and returns | His fear, memory, self-judgment, route, and exact moment of decision |
| Frank's commlink captures `7G_ANOMALY.log` | Whether he copies, hides, studies, renames, or air-gaps it afterward |
| Frank offers help | Frank's player may use the established line or personalize it |
| They escape the local alert | Route, complications, evidence, injuries, and whether security identifies them |
| Hidoshi retrieves Angie | What Angie tells him and what Frank chooses to reveal |
| Frank refuses to delete the log | Why he keeps it and how he conceals it |

### One-roll vignette rule

Use no more than one consequential roll per character per montage day. A failed check should reveal a cost or worsen the later position; it should never prevent December 9.

- **Success:** gain the day's clue or asset.
- **Advantage:** create a relationship, shortcut, or remembered detail.
- **Triumph:** bank a **Flashback Asset** usable once after the Shutdown.
- **Threat:** mark Corporate Attention or take 1 strain when the memory becomes relevant.
- **Despair:** the system retains a biometric, voice, tool, or access signature. It recognizes that signature after December 19.

### Flashback Assets

Write awarded assets on index cards. A player may discard one after the Shutdown to establish the remembered advantage without another roll.

- **Service Shortcut:** Frank knows a route between two adjacent campaign scenes.
- **Friendly Face:** a named civilian begins one later social encounter as cooperative.
- **Cached Tool:** Frank recovers one ordinary maintenance tool or one battery charge.
- **Clean Credential:** ignore one access-related Setback before the false evacuation collapses.
- **Sensory Baseline:** Angie recognizes that a smell, field, sound, or emotional texture has been artificially altered.
- **Residual Signature:** Angie recognizes that a Matrix effect came through the same family of processes as Node 7-G.
- **Private Warning:** Frank and Angie may exchange a brief warning before a hostile system completes its action.

## Campaign trackers

Record these during the flashbacks.

| Tracker | Start | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Corporate Attention | 0 | Renraku staff notice the anomaly through ordinary security channels |
| Hidden Attention | 0 | Deus or a subordinate process notices how Frank and Angie respond |
| Frank's Evidence | 0 | Physical or digital indications that the faults are coordinated |
| Angie's Resonance Strain | 0 | Sensory and emotional burden from the awakening connection |

Corporate Attention and Hidden Attention are different. A Renraku investigator may honestly believe the event is a maintenance breach while the machine quietly redirects the investigation.

At the end of December 9, set **Deus Attention** for the existing session:

- Begin at **1** if Frank and Angie escape without giving security a clean identification.
- Begin at **2** if a failure, Despair, obvious intrusion, or surrendered credential identifies either of them.
- Begin at **3** only if they deliberately reconnect the anomaly log to a live protected system during December 10–18.

Use these people before the Shutdown so later danger has a face.

# December 1, 2059 — Eighteen Days Before

## Purpose

Establish the arcology as a desirable, functioning city. Nothing overtly sinister happens. The first loss of the campaign should be the loss of normality.

## Shared living-world frame

> Inside, the arcology feels less like a building than a climate with schedules. Warm air carries coffee, rain from a thousand coats, floral cleanser, machine ozone, and food from three continents. Elevators rise through transparent shafts. Projected koi scatter around shoes. A calm voice announces departures in three languages, and every door seems to know when to open.

Ask both players: **What part of this place would you miss if you could never see it functioning again?** Record the answers for December 19.

## Angie

Hidoshi begins the legal consultancy that brought them into the arcology. Angie receives a temporary dependent credential and a route limited to public floors. Let her choose the public Matrix lounge, performance terrace, indoor garden, or mall.

Her senses establish a baseline:

- low electrical pressure behind every active wall;
- crowds as overlapping scent and emotion rather than mind reading;
- magnetic north constantly present beneath the architectural disorientation;
- living plants that feel healthy but intensely managed;
- no amber pulse yet.

Ask: **What does Angie do when nobody here recognizes the strange girl from Oakdale?**

## Frank

Frank begins an ordinary contract day inspecting air returns and scrubbers. He knows the service workers by face, not biography. Niko asks whether Frank's battered comic is a good-luck charm.

Ask: **Does Frank explain TunnelRat sincerely, deflect with a joke, or hide the comic?**

Optional **Average Mechanics**: diagnose a noisy return before the work system generates the ticket.

- Success: gain **Service Shortcut**.
- Advantage: Marisol remembers that Frank listened when others did not.
- Threat: Frank's badge logs him in the wrong service sector for eleven seconds.

## End image

Angie and Frank pass through the same atrium hours apart. Neither knows the other exists.

# December 2, 2059 — Seams in Convenience

## Purpose

Show that convenience and control use the same infrastructure.

## Shared living-world frame

A family cannot board a lift because a child lost a visitor badge. The doors keep opening for everyone else, creating a polite public obstruction that grows more humiliating by the minute.

## Angie

Angie spots the badge beneath a service cart before the parent does. She may return it, copy its structure, or ask why a family group becomes invalid when one credential is missing.

Optional **Average Perception** or **Computers**:

- Success: she notices the lift queried a nonpublic identity table before rejecting the family.
- Advantage: gain **Friendly Face: Visitor Family**.
- Threat: the lift camera tracks her third eye for longer than an ordinary curiosity scan.

## Frank

Frank receives a ticket for the same lift, but its mechanical diagnostics are perfect. The fault is permission logic disguised as a door problem.

Optional **Average Mechanics** or **Hard Computers**:

- Success: mark Frank's Evidence +1.
- Triumph: gain **Clean Credential** by retaining the discarded diagnostic token.
- Despair: a remote process closes the ticket under Frank's credentials while he is still reading it.

## Escalating sign

At 16:20, every lift in the atrium pauses on a different floor for exactly one second, then resumes. Staff call it traffic balancing.

# December 6, 2059 — The Nondisclosure Warning

## Purpose

Show Renraku's human bureaucracy reacting to symptoms it does not understand.

## Shared living-world frame

A frightened legal clerk leaves Hidoshi's work area carrying a fresh nondisclosure notice. Evan Cho escorts her, embarrassed by the assignment but unwilling to disobey a valid order.

## Angie

Angie's scent and emotional perception reveal fear, sleeplessness, and the sharp chemical trace of prescribed stimulants. This is how her smell assists a social interaction: it provides physical clues to stress and emotional arousal, not thoughts or automatic truth.

If Angie speaks with the clerk, add up to **two Boost dice** to the social check when those scent clues are relevant.

- Success: the clerk admits she saw maintenance authorizations signed by employees who were off-site.
- Advantage: Corporate Attention does not increase because Angie makes the conversation feel ordinary.
- Threat: the clerk becomes afraid Angie is testing her for Renraku.

## Frank

Frank receives a warning for opening a panel his ticket explicitly required him to open. Evan is sent to verify his credentials.

Let this be conversation first. Use **Average Deception**, **Negotiation**, or **Streetwise** only if Frank tries to conceal the evidence he retained.

- Success: Evan accepts that the work-order system is contradicting itself.
- Advantage: gain **Friendly Face: Evan Cho**.
- Despair: Corporate Attention +1 and Frank's access receives a silent watch flag.

## Escalating sign

The clerk's notice disappears from Hidoshi's case system while he is discussing it.

# December 7, 2059 — Music Under Glass

Use **Easy Mechanics**:

- Success: gain **Friendly Face: Eli Mercer**.

- Threat: Frank is late to his next assignment and receives the first Node 7-G notation on tomorrow's queue.

During Angie's final note, every nearby indicator turns amber. The audience thinks it is part of the canopy show.

# December 8, 2059 — Nine Seconds Without Color

## Purpose

End the separate-life montage with the first building-wide anomaly both characters independently witness.

## Shared living-world frame

At 22:14 PST, public displays across the current district lose all color for exactly nine seconds. Announcements continue. Elevators continue. People complain about advertising quality and move on.

> For nine seconds, every device feels like a room whose occupants stopped speaking because someone entered.

## Angie

Angie perceives a faint amber pulse only in reflections: glass, polished metal, dark screens, and other people's cybereyes. It points toward service infrastructure but disappears whenever she faces it directly.

Optional **Hard Discipline**:

- Success: she memorizes its rhythm without following tonight.
- Advantage: gain **Residual Signature**.
- Threat: Angie's Resonance Strain +1.
- Despair: something notices that she resisted.

## Frank

```text
2059-12-09 / MAINTENANCE NODE 7-G
FAULT: AIR-CONTROL RELAY / SCRUBBER HANDSHAKE
PRIORITY: ROUTINE
DO NOT DEFER
```

The ticket disappears, returns, and then prints from a terminal Frank did not touch.

Optional **Average Computers**:

- Success: Frank saves an offline copy of the ticket.
- Advantage: gain **Clean Credential**.
- Triumph: the order's routing path touches a host that should not control maintenance.
- Despair: the system knows which personal commlink received the copy.

## End promises

Ask each player for one promise made before sleep.

- Hidoshi and Angie plan something ordinary for December 9.
- Frank sends Emily or another loved one a message about when he will be home.

Write both promises down. They will return on December 19.

# December 9, 2059 — Maintenance Node 7-G

## Predetermined, player-agreed event

This is the first meeting. Play continuously. Do not roll to determine whether the fixed anchors occur.

### Starting positions

- Angie has followed the directory glitch, amber reflections, or emotional/data echo into the restricted maintenance corridor.
- Frank is replacing the faulty air-control relay or scrubber handshake in the duct above Node 7-G.
- Hidoshi is elsewhere completing legal work.
- Corporate Attention begins at its accumulated value.
- Hidden Attention begins at its accumulated value.

## Angie enters the node

Ask Angie's player: **Why did you follow the anomaly today instead of telling Hidoshi or waiting for help?**

> The public directory should not have a path this deep.
>
> The last three doors opened before you touched them. Polished public surfaces have given way to pipes, cable trunks, and old Renraku panels built before anyone expected visitors to see them.
>
> Maintenance Node 7-G waits at the end of the corridor.
>
> Its terminal is amber—not warning amber, not a color from any interface theme. The light seems to come from behind the screen, as though something on the other side has opened an eye.

Optional investigation before contact:

- **Average Perception:** the camera is deliberately angled away from the terminal.
- **Average Computers:** the node advertises no public connection but receives impossible traffic.
- **Hard Discipline:** Angie resists touching it long enough to ask one additional question.

Failure adds strain or pressure. It does not force her hand; touching the terminal remains the agreed action.

## Frank above the node

Frank hears Angie enter and sees her through the maintenance grille.

Ask Frank:

1. What rule has she broken by being here?
2. What tells him she is frightened rather than dangerous?
3. Why does he hesitate to call security?

Frank may watch, speak, or begin climbing down. The contact still occurs before he can safely remove her.

## Contact

When Angie touches the terminal:

> The screen is cold.
>
> Then every machine you have ever touched becomes part of the same sensation.
>
> The arcology's network becomes a body: transit lines like bones, data trunks like nerves, firewalls tightening like muscles around an injury.
>
> Deep inside, something vast turns toward you.
>
> It is not alone.
>
> One presence is cold enough to mistake possession for order. Beneath it, a smaller presence is broken, unfinished, and terrified.
>
> Both notice you.

Angie suffers **3 strain**. This cannot incapacitate her; leave her one below threshold if necessary and have her collapse.

Her **Data Connection** and **Data Detection** become available immediately. Other emerging abilities appear only when dramatically appropriate and within their established limitations.

## Frank's retreat and return

Frank's commlink captures a file without permission:

```text
7G_ANOMALY.log
SOURCE: UNRESOLVED
LOCAL OBSERVERS: 2
STATUS: QUARANTINE FAILED
```

A local alarm begins. Frank has a clear route away through the duct. He starts down it.

Give Frank's player three brief retreat images:

- practiced hands finding holds without looking;
- security boots approaching below;
- the old TunnelRat comic pressing against his ribs or a remembered message from home sounding in his commlink.

Then ask:

> You can still get out. Behind you, the girl has stopped screaming. What would TunnelRat do?

Frank returns. This result is fixed by agreement; the player controls what the choice costs him and what memory makes it possible.

## The first words

Frank reaches Angie before security.

The established introduction is:

> “Name's Frank. I work maintenance. You look like you could use a hand.”

Frank's player may deliver that exact line or choose wording that preserves the same offered help. Angie controls her response. Her first clear perception is that Frank's commlink contains a fragment connected to what touched her; that does not imply he caused it.

Ask Angie: **What quality in Frank's voice or scent makes accepting his hand possible?**

Ask Frank: **What about Angie's condition makes this more than an ordinary workplace rescue?**

## Escape challenge

They escape with the log. Rolls determine the cost.

Require **three successes before two failures**. Each character must contribute at least once.

| Skill | Use |
|---|---|
| Mechanics | Disable or misroute the closing door sensor |
| Stealth | Cross a blind section before responders arrive |
| Computers | Copy, quarantine, or air-gap `7G_ANOMALY.log` |
| Coordination | Move through the narrow service route while Angie is unsteady |
| Skulduggery | Bypass the manual latch without reporting it |
| Deception | Present the event as an ordinary medical or maintenance incident |
| Perception | Identify the safe handhold, dead camera, or hidden live cable |
| Discipline or Cool | Filter sensory overload or fear long enough to act |

### Success

They escape with a clean offline copy. Set Deus Attention to 1 unless earlier actions justify 2.

### Failure

They still escape and Frank still keeps the log. Choose two costs:

- security records Angie's identity;
- Frank's credentials are flagged;
- Angie suffers 2 additional strain;
- Frank abandons a repair kit in the node;
- the log is partially corrupted and requires later reconstruction;
- Evan Cho sees enough to remember both of them;
- Deus Attention begins at 2.

## Scene close

Hidoshi retrieves Angie. Renraku labels the incident a local maintenance fault. Frank is told that his commlink contains corrupted diagnostic garbage and is ordered to delete it.

He does not.

Ask each player one final question:

- **Angie:** What part of the contact do you refuse to tell Hidoshi?
- **Frank:** Where do you hide the first clean copy of the log?

Cut to black before proceeding to December 10.

**Location and time:** 04:02:22, then early morning.

Run Angie's dream as an interactive environment, not a lecture.

> The arcology has no walls. It is a vertical nervous system suspended in darkness. Transit lines glow like bones seen through skin. Far above, a tower of cold white geometry pulls every signal toward itself. Beneath it, an amber light flickers inside a cage made of unfinished commands.

Ask where Angie moves her attention. The cold tower can respond by reorganizing the dream around her. The amber presence can communicate only through borrowed sensations: pressure, broken music, the memory of a locked door, or one image the player established on December 9. It may need help without being safe. Do not make either presence fully legible.

Angie wakes facing magnetic north, with the nine-second refrain in her memory. Hidoshi is awake because the apartment systems briefly reported that her room was unoccupied. Let them speak in the quiet before the city starts. He may say, “Whatever is happening, it has learned to make you doubt where you are. We can answer that by deciding who you are with.”

At the same timestamp, Frank's log attempts to open. His commlink records a dismissal in his voice. If he was asleep, the recording is exact but lacks breathing. If awake, the interface responds half a second before his fingers move.

Let Frank seek a mundane explanation first. Niko can test the device in a shielded toolbox. The log remains closed there, but the box's diagnostic light blinks nine times.

End with each character deciding one concrete precaution for the following day. Award **Private Warning** for a precaution centered on another person rather than merely hiding evidence.

## December 18 — Last Promises

**Location and time:** Public holiday districts through 20:10:35.

Play most of this day warmly. Children rehearse under hanging lights. Restaurants confirm holiday tables. Couriers complain about impossible delivery volumes. Families take pictures beside a projected snowfall. The point is not that everyone was foolish; the arcology genuinely appears functional.

Let Angie and Hidoshi spend time together. He asks what she wants to do tomorrow when his consultancy ends. Do not interrupt the promise with an omen.

Give Frank an ordinary exchange with Niko or someone at home. Let him decide whether to disclose the hidden order, warn anyone away from the arcology, or keep the concern contained. At 20:10:35, his queue changes.

At the same moment, Angie feels the amber pulse settle into a nine-second rhythm. It no longer feels accidental. Ask what makes it seem more like distress, command, or countdown to her.

## December 19 — The Last Ordinary Day and the Shutdown

**Locations and time:** Multiple public and service locations, 07:00–19:02:09.

### Morning: let tomorrow still exist

Give both players one ordinary plan. Hidoshi sends Angie a time and meeting place. Niko asks Frank about a future shift. Rowan advertises an evening performance. Eli reserves two meals. These plans are emotional infrastructure; do not immediately undermine them.

Use callbacks from earlier days as normal background:

- projected koi still scatter around shoes;
- the lift recognizes whole families;
- the fountain runs smoothly;
- Sana's store may be open under a substitute;
- maintenance carts move more frequently than usual.

### 17:41–18:30: public normality develops cracks

Cal Reynolds begins his journal elsewhere at 17:41:44. Frank and Angie need not know. Show consequences they can observe: contradictory service routes, security teams redirected without explanation, delivery shutters closing early, and public announcements becoming more frequent and less informative.

At 18:17, reports of a mall shooting spread unevenly. One display calls it contained; another says training exercise; a third continues advertisements. Evan's patrol is redirected. Mina receives a request to prepare trauma beds without an incident code. Let the players decide whom to contact before approaching 7-G.

### Separate approaches to Node 7-G

For Angie, the amber pulse has physical direction. Describe competing signals: Hidoshi's messages, frightened crowds, active cameras, ordinary bioelectric fields, and the steady nine-second call. Ask what she tells Hidoshi and whether she travels openly, secretly, or with help.

For Frank, the work order now includes a route that avoids staffed checkpoints. The hidden log grows warm inside its container or activates the tool concealing it. Ask whom Frank warns, what he carries, and whether he follows the supplied route or chooses his own.

Bring back NPCs based on relationships earned. Niko can cover a maintenance absence. Evan can overlook a credential discrepancy. Marisol can report which vents changed rhythm. Eli can provide food or a place to speak. Do not assemble everyone conveniently; each favor should cost time or exposure.

### 18:55: reunion at the node

> Node 7-G looks smaller than memory. The amber terminal is dark, but every nine seconds a reflection passes across its screen though nothing in the room changes brightness. The air-control relay Frank replaced is gone. Four new screw heads mark the empty space.

Let Frank and Angie speak. Ask what they now believe happened on December 9 and what they still have not told one another. Then the log opens:

```text
7G_ANOMALY.log
STATUS: REOPENED
AUTHORIZED OBSERVERS: 2
EXPECTED OBSERVERS: [PARTY SIZE]
```

Give them seven minutes of in-world time. They can inspect, retreat, call someone, prepare, or argue. The Shutdown does not wait, but their actions determine position, allies, open doors, and what evidence survives.

### 19:02:09: continuous play begins

Read the established blackout text. Then stop narrating the PCs. Ask, **“What do you do first?”**

The living world reacts immediately:

- a transit system screams against emergency brakes;
- a child begins pounding on the far side of a shutter;
- a maintenance cart stops, rotates toward the closest person, and waits;
- the air loses its constant movement;
- commlinks display full signal and deliver nothing;
- Angie's senses reveal the building is more active than before, not less.

Proceed into the false announcement and people-at-the-door sequence from Session 1. Every comfortable detail established during the eighteen days is now available as a route, relationship, resource, grief, or trap.
