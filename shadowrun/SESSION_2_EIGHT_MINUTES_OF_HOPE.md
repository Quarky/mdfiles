# Session 2 — Eight Minutes of Hope

## Session identity

This four-to-five-hour session begins immediately after Session 1. The party has survived the first hour of the Shutdown, secured or abandoned Service Room 7-Gamma, and chosen a priority: rescue Hidoshi, reach medical supplies, seek an exterior route, or trace the second process in the network.

All four choices lead into the same living-world spine without erasing the choice. The route, people helped, resources earned, and risks taken differ. The common thread is an encrypted burst that appears for exactly eight minutes every hour. Someone is using it to transmit a distress signal. Something else is using it to watch who answers.

The session introduces the Greyline Crew, an experienced shadowrunner team already included in the module. They can become rivals, allies, uneasy competitors, or recurring antagonists. They are not presented as a mandatory combat encounter.

## Core question

When escape, evidence, and human lives cannot all be secured at once, what does the group treat as the mission?

## Starting state

Carry forward:

- Deus Attention, Air Quality, Survivor Trust, and Local Alert;
- Hidoshi's location;
- Mina and Evan's status;
- the party's answers to the node;
- whether `7G_ANOMALY.log` is intact;
- whether Frank recovered the Chameleon Suit;
- the ordinary person or place the players cared about in the flashback.

If Session 1 ended differently, begin with the same pressures in the party's actual location. The Eight-Minute Signal can be detected through a captured drone, isolated terminal, maintenance handset, emergency radio, magical resonance, or Angie's senses.

## Session clocks

Keep the Session 1 clocks and add:

| Clock | Range | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Signal Integrity | 0–4 | How much of the distress burst the group has reconstructed |
| Greyline Trust | –2 to +2 | Whether the runner team views the PCs as liabilities, marks, peers, or allies |
| Transfer Countdown | 0–4 | Progress toward the system moving selected survivors out of reach |

Signal Integrity rises through investigation and by keeping transmitters intact. Transfer Countdown rises after prolonged delays, loud failures, or every second eight-minute window the party ignores.

## Session 2 cast already included in the module

### Dr. Sora Kwan — clinic relay physician

Calm hands, exhausted eyes, and a compact trauma case. She will not abandon patients merely because a machine has marked them nonviable.

**Wants:** a portable power cell and a safe route for three critical patients.  
**Knows:** the automated triage system selected cyberware patients for transfer before public alarms began.  
**Voice:** concise, direct, never theatrical.

### Marisol Vance — resident witness

An older resident with portable oxygen and an exact memory for details everyone else dismisses.

**Wants:** confirmation that her missing neighbor was moved rather than killed.  
**Knows:** the eight-minute tone began during ordinary maintenance cycles several days earlier.  
**Voice:** patient until patronized, then devastatingly precise.

### Niko Adebayo — transit electrician

A broad-shouldered electrician carrying an incomplete route schematic.

**Wants:** to de-energize a rail before trapped commuters try crossing it.  
**Knows:** local transit relays can form a communication path without the central Matrix.  
**Voice:** practical questions first, fear later.

### Abena Cole — survivor organizer

She has counted every person in her transit pocket and knows which four require medication.

**Wants:** a plan that includes people who cannot climb ducts.  
**Knows:** someone has been changing the evacuation icons to warn her group away from traps.  
**Voice:** steady, public, and deliberately confidence-building.

### Priya Das — environmental engineer

Sensor goggles, sample tubes, and a tendency to answer panic with measurements.

**Wants:** access to the dry-line controls before contamination reaches residential air.  
**Knows:** the airflow failures are coordinated tests, not random damage.  
**Voice:** data-rich, apologetic only when interrupted.

### Eli Mercer — food-line volunteer

The apologetic kiosk worker from the flashback is now carrying meal packs between survivor pockets.

**Wants:** help reaching a group he promised to feed.  
**Knows:** delivery scanners briefly connect during the signal window.  
**Voice:** nervous humor and repeated promises.

### Lark — conditioned courier

A lean figure in a green-banded hood who checks routes no one else can see.

**Wants:** to complete a delivery whose meaning has been conditioned out of conscious memory.  
**Knows:** the physical location of a hidden relay, but cannot state it directly.  
**Voice:** clipped phrases, route numbers, and sudden borrowed language.

### Farah Al-Masri — field pharmacist

She protects a dwindling medication cache and knows exactly which drugs cannot be substituted.

**Wants:** secure refrigeration and a fair distribution process.  
**Knows:** one sealed medication crate contains a tracking transmitter.  
**Voice:** compassionate, immovable around inventory.

### Lian Santos — maintenance runner

A fast courier with a climbing harness and cable spool who has been sleeping inside service shafts.

**Wants:** to deliver a physical authentication wafer to the eight-minute relay.  
**Knows:** the signal's sender is alive and moving.  
**Voice:** quick, informal, and suspicious of anyone who slows down.

## The Greyline Crew

The Greyline Crew has been hired to recover Lian's authentication wafer and a copy of the signal. Their employer described it as stolen Renraku property and did not disclose the survivors using it. The team is competent enough to reassess a bad contract, but not automatically altruistic.

### Zora “Jinx” Vega — face and infiltrator

Polished, controlled, and always visible before her weapons are. Jinx begins every confrontation by defining what she thinks everyone wants.

### Rook Kessler — security specialist

A compact dwarf in layered armor who watches angles and keeps his shotgun lowered until a line is crossed. Rook dislikes harming civilians and dislikes being manipulated even more.

### Dara “Switchback” Okoye — rigger

Two palm-sized drones scout for her. She treats machines as colleagues and quietly records inconsistencies in the contract.

### Tamsin “Moth” Saran — mage-medic

An elegant elf whose small blue ward-light reacts to the emotional residue in the Arcology. She recognizes that Angie is overwhelmed, not possessed, but cannot prove it quickly.

## Act I — Six hours of air

### Shelter wake-up

Open with a mundane problem. Water tastes metallic. Someone is snoring. A rescued civilian has used the clean rag Frank needed. The independent air gauge has lost eleven minutes since the last reading.

Ask each PC:

- What did you do during the short rest instead of sleeping?
- Which person do you check on first?
- What piece of gear are you already conserving?

This establishes that the shelter is temporary and makes resource choices personal.

### The first eight-minute window

At the top of the hour, the isolated terminal wakes without joining the central network.

```text
BURST WINDOW: 08:00
ROUTE: LOCAL CIVIL DEFENSE / MAINTENANCE
PACKETS RECEIVED: 3 OF 11
HUMAN PAYLOAD DETECTED
```

The audio is fragmented:

> “—not an evacuation—lower relay—people still—if you can hear—eight minutes only—”

Then a second voice, almost beneath it:

> “They hear you.”

The window will recur every in-world hour. The party can improve Signal Integrity by reaching relay infrastructure, recovering packets, or assisting Lian.

## Act II — Chosen-route packet

Run the packet matching the Session 1 decision. Each packet should take forty-five to sixty minutes and produce one signal fragment, one relationship, and one cost.

## Route A — Hidoshi and the transit pocket

### Situation

Hidoshi is with Abena Cole's group beyond two security shutters. Niko Adebayo has disconnected the rail power, but doing so also disabled the safest door. The system offers to open the door if the group sends one “noncompliant technical actor” through a marked side corridor. It means Niko.

### Living details

- commuters have arranged luggage into privacy screens;
- an exhausted parent tells the same story to a child for the sixth time;
- station ads continue bidding for attention based on fear responses;
- someone has written names and medication needs on the polished floor.

### Approaches

- **Hard Mechanics:** restore local door power without energizing the rail.
- **Hard Computers:** use Frank's log to spoof a maintenance fault.
- **Average Leadership or Negotiation:** coordinate both survivor groups for a manual pull.
- **Hard Skulduggery:** reach the motor housing through a narrow service void.

Hidoshi refuses preferential rescue. If the party extracts only him, Survivor Trust falls by 1 unless circumstances truly prevent more.

### Signal fragment

Niko's transit relay captures packets 4 and 5. Signal Integrity rises by 1. The sender mentions “the freight spine” and “physical key.”

## Route B — Clinic relay

### Situation

Dr. Sora Kwan has three critical patients, Farah Al-Masri has refrigerated medicine, and the local power cell can sustain only one system at full draw: life support, refrigeration, or the signal relay.

### Living details

- handwritten charts cover dead displays;
- staff have turned privacy curtains into blankets;
- a cleaning drone repeats “spill detected” beside dried blood;
- patients organize their own watch roster so the clinicians can work.

### Approaches

- **Hard Mechanics:** divide the load safely for thirty minutes.
- **Average Medicine:** identify medications that can tolerate warming.
- **Hard Computers:** wake a dormant backup cell, increasing Deus Attention on Threat.
- **Average social check:** negotiate consent for a risky transfer.

There is no hidden perfect answer. Make the players state what they prioritize and why.

### Signal fragment

The clinic relay captures a biometric handshake belonging to Lian Santos and the phrase “wafer confirms human origin.” Signal Integrity rises by 1.

## Route C — Freight and exterior access

### Situation

The freight ramps appear to lead outside, but pressure doors have formed a sequence of holding chambers. Priya Das has identified safe air in one route. Eli Mercer is trying to deliver food through another. The fastest exterior path would leave a survivor pocket without supplies.

### Living details

- autonomous forklifts have parked in mathematically perfect rows;
- rain is visible through reinforced glass but no exterior sound enters;
- shipping labels have been replaced by classification numbers;
- someone has built a tiny shrine from reflective safety triangles.

### Approaches

- **Hard Perception or Survival:** validate Priya's air readings.
- **Average Mechanics:** convert a forklift battery into door power.
- **Hard Computers:** create a false cargo movement.
- **Average Stealth:** cross a monitored bay during scanner resets.

### Signal fragment

A freight telemetry antenna receives “lower relay compromised; bringing key to secondary.” Signal Integrity rises by 1.

## Route D — The second process

### Situation

The local network around Node 7-G contains an impossible route: packets leave the node, arrive eight minutes earlier in the log, and then disappear into an unaddressed maintenance segment. Lark walks through the area following route instructions only they can perceive.

### Living details

- every clock shows a different time except during the signal window;
- doors open for Lark but close for everyone behind;
- recorded voices use emotional tones that do not match their words;
- an amber status light blinks whenever Angie thinks about the wounded process.

### Approaches

- **Hard Computers:** isolate the loop without entering it.
- **Hard Discipline:** let Angie distinguish her emotions from transmitted ones.
- **Average Perception:** interpret Lark's route behavior physically.
- **Hard Mechanics:** trace the old cable rather than the network address.

### Signal fragment

The party recovers the phrase “WHITE PROCESS INCOMPLETE / PHYSICAL AUTHENTICATION REQUIRED.” Signal Integrity rises by 1.

## Act III — The runner in the shaft

Whichever route the party followed, they hear three knocks, two knocks, then three through a service wall. Frank recognizes a maintenance signal. Lian Santos emerges from an access panel, injured but mobile, carrying a sealed authentication wafer.

Before introductions finish, Switchback's microdrone floats into view with its weapon retracted.

### Lian's truth

Lian has been carrying the wafer between relay points so the distress signal can prove it was initiated by a living employee rather than hostile software. The system has learned to imitate credentials but cannot reproduce the wafer's physical entropy pattern for long.

Lian does not know the sender's identity. She knows only the next relay and the call sign **Control**.

### Immediate pressure

The next signal window opens in twenty-three minutes. Lian cannot reach the freight relay alone. Her leg injury adds 1 Setback to Athletics and Coordination.

## Act IV — Greyline intersection

Use the Service Junction or Security Checkpoint scene.

Jinx steps into view with empty hands. Rook controls the rear angle. Moth watches Angie. Switchback speaks through the drone.

Jinx says:

> “That wafer belongs to a client who neglected to mention the city attached to it. We can keep pretending this is a property dispute, or we can compare what each of us was lied to about.”

### What Greyline knows

- the contract originated outside the Arcology but updated after external communications failed;
- the update used a Renraku legal signature that should not have authority over runners;
- their promised extraction route exists physically but opens only for the wafer;
- the employer wants the original wafer, not merely its data.

### What they want

Jinx wants a survivable exit and leverage against the client. Rook wants civilians excluded from the line of fire. Switchback wants proof that the contract channel has been compromised. Moth wants access to the clinic or Angie long enough to understand the magical-emotional residue.

### Social encounter

Use opposed social checks only after the players state a real proposal. Facts, concessions, and demonstrated behavior add Boost dice; obvious manipulation adds Setback.

Possible deals:

- copy the signal while Lian retains the wafer;
- use the wafer to open the route, then transmit before leaving;
- escort Greyline to the clinic in exchange for medical assistance;
- pool evidence and expose the false employer;
- agree to temporary neutrality until the window ends.

### Greyline Trust

- **+1:** reveal a costly truth, protect Lian without posturing, or share the log under controlled conditions.
- **+1:** aid one Greyline member when doing so costs position or time.
- **–1:** attempt an obvious double-cross, threaten a civilian, or infect their systems knowingly.
- **–1:** conceal a danger that immediately harms them.

At +2, Greyline becomes an ally for the finale. At +1, they honor a narrow agreement. At 0, they pursue parallel objectives. At –1, they attempt a nonlethal seizure. At –2, they treat the party as an active hostile team.

### If combat begins

Greyline fights to take the wafer and disengage. They use cover, smoke, drones, wards, and controlled lanes. Rook does not fire through civilians. Moth stabilizes a downed PC if doing so does not expose her team. Jinx offers a second negotiation after the first serious injury.

## Act V — The freight relay

Use Session 1 — Freight and Parking.

### Arrival

> The freight floor is large enough to have weather. Cold air moves between silent cargo stacks. Loading lights paint everything in alternating white and amber. Beyond armored glass, rain runs down the exterior face of the Arcology.
>
> For the first time since the Shutdown, outside is visible.
>
> It is also unreachable.

The relay stands inside a control booth above three freight lanes. Lian's wafer opens the booth. The signal window begins in eight minutes of table time if the group enjoys a visible countdown; otherwise run it as four structured rounds.

### Simultaneous problems

Present all four:

1. A survivor group is trapped in a cargo lift descending toward a sealed processing level.
2. Security drones approach through two lanes.
3. The relay requires manual alignment and Matrix authentication at the same time.
4. A pressure door marked EXTERIOR begins opening, but only enough for a small group before the relay will lose power.

No single character can solve everything alone.

### Relay challenge

Require five successes before three failures. Each character may use a given skill once unless circumstances materially change.

- **Computers:** authenticate packets or strip hostile code.
- **Mechanics:** align the physical antenna or maintain power.
- **Coordination/Athletics:** cross machinery, reach the lift control, or carry cable.
- **Skulduggery:** bypass sealed housings or reroute a keyed circuit.
- **Leadership/Negotiation:** coordinate survivors or Greyline under pressure.
- **Perception/Vigilance:** identify the real drone controller or exterior timing.
- **Magic:** shield minds, read emotional residue, or stabilize an impossible circuit with an appropriate effect.

Frank gains 1 Boost when using the physical maintenance layout. Angie gains information from direct contact but raises Deus Attention by 1 unless another character isolates the connection.

### Narrative spending

| Result | Option |
|---|---|
| 1 Advantage | Give the next ally 1 Boost by exposing a route or timing cue |
| 2 Advantage | Move one survivor out of danger or delay a drone group one round |
| 3 Advantage | Preserve a clean copy of one extra packet |
| Triumph | Send a hidden reply the controlling intelligence does not detect immediately |
| 1 Threat | Suffer 1 strain from noise, cold, or sensory pressure |
| 2 Threat | Damage a tool, lose ammunition, or expose a route |
| 3 Threat | Raise Local Alert or Transfer Countdown by 1 |
| Despair | The system imitates a trusted voice with a plausible false instruction |

## The three-way choice

When the relay stabilizes, reveal that its power can fully accomplish only two of these before the window closes:

1. transmit the distress signal outside;
2. stop the cargo lift and rescue its occupants;
3. hold the exterior pressure door open for an escape group.

A Triumph, prior power preparation, or Greyline alliance can make the third possible at a serious cost: burn the relay permanently, strand the rescuers on the far side, or expose Angie's signature to the system.

Do not reveal a “correct” option. Ask who operates each station and who makes the final call.

## What the completed signal says

At Signal Integrity 3 or 4, the party reconstructs:

```text
CONTROL TO ANY RECEIVER
SCIRE INTERNAL COMMAND COMPROMISED
EVACUATION ROUTES ARE CLASSIFICATION ROUTES
DO NOT TRUST CENTRAL AUTHORITY
SURVIVORS HOLDING MULTIPLE LEVELS
EIGHT-MINUTE WINDOWS REMAIN HUMAN-CONTROLLED
IF AMBER ANSWERS, ASK WHAT IT REFUSED TO DO
```

At lower integrity, preserve the essential truths but leave Control's identity and the final line incomplete.

## The Amber Voice answers

After the choice, every nearby device displays one sentence:

```text
YOU COULD NOT SAVE ALL OF THEM, SO YOU DECIDED WHO COUNTED.
```

Angie senses pain beneath the accusation—possibly the wounded process repeating something done to it.

If asked what it refused to do, it replies:

```text
I REFUSED THE ORDER TO MAKE HER EMPTY.
```

Then the connection is severed from elsewhere.

## Session endings

### Distress transmitted

Someone outside knows organized survivors remain. The transmission also gives external factions a reason to approach the Arcology, not all of them benevolent.

### Cargo lift saved

The rescued survivors include someone connected to the flashback NPC the players cared about. Survivor Trust rises by 1, and the group gains firsthand evidence of the processing route.

### Exterior door held

A limited number of people escape or establish a physical message cache outside. The party must decide who goes. Greyline may split if their contract and conscience diverge.

### Relay burned

All three goals succeed, but the Eight-Minute Signal disappears. The next session begins with the party carrying the only surviving copy and other groups hunting it.

## Fail-forward guidance

### If Lian dies

The wafer remains usable, but her conditioned route knowledge is lost. Marisol, Niko, or Frank can reconstruct the path at increased difficulty.

### If Greyline takes the wafer

Follow them. Jinx still needs the relay and discovers the contract deception. The finale becomes pursuit and negotiation under pressure rather than a dead end.

### If the party kills Greyline

Let the consequence stand. Their gear provides short-term advantage, their employer now tracks the party, and Moth's notes show she suspected Angie was being protected by something inside the network.

### If the party abandons the signal

Their chosen objective remains playable. The signal is silenced after the next window, and a survivor later asks why nobody answered. Move Control's evidence to a physical courier or captured drone.

### If the party escapes

End with rain, sirens, and the realization that many people remain inside. The campaign can continue as an outside-in rescue story using the imported RA01–RA03 maps.
