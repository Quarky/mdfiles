# Shutdown: Living Arcology GM Pack

This GM-facing Markdown pack expands the opening of the Shadowrun Genesys campaign into a living-world sequence and preserves the player-character continuity established in Frank Crodler's original start file.

## Campaign chronology used here

- **December 9, 2059:** the players experience the Renraku Arcology during an ordinary day. Frank and Angie do not meet.
- **Morning of December 10, 2059:** Frank is replacing a faulty air scrubber in Maintenance Node 7-G. He hears Angie scream, sees her collapse beside the amber terminal, initially flees into the ducts, then turns back and finds her. This is their first meeting and the campaign's present-time start.
- **Later December 10 onward:** Sessions 1 and 2 follow the first hours of the Shutdown.

This date intentionally follows Frank's supplied character file. It supersedes the alternate December 9/December 19 chronology in earlier draft session documents.

## Autosplitting

The modular encounter file and imported-map file use one level-one heading (`#`) per encounter or map. They can therefore be divided into individual journals with an H1 autosplitter. Lower-level headings belong to the entry above them.

## Table principles

- The Arcology is a city before it becomes a dungeon.
- Ordinary people have names, routines, needs, and contradictory priorities.
- Deus studies choices; it does not need to explain itself.
- Failed checks change cost, position, time, trust, or danger. They do not erase the campaign's essential clues.
- Violence is possible, but every major encounter includes another objective besides defeating enemies.
- Existing Shutdown actors, handouts, and scenes are used whenever possible. New asset needs are collected in the manifest instead of being silently assumed.
