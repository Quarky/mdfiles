# Encounter 01 — The Elevator Choir

## Use when

The party crosses a residential, commercial, or transit floor and needs a human scene that can become tense without combat.

## Situation

An elevator is stalled between floors with nine people inside. They have discovered that singing together prevents a frightened child from hyperventilating. The elevator intercom keeps interrupting with individualized instructions telling different passengers to press incompatible controls.

## Faces

Use **Abena Cole** as the organizer outside the car and **Niko Adebayo** if technical help is needed. The trapped passengers can remain unnamed unless the players engage them.

## Beats

1. The singing stops whenever the intercom speaks.
2. The emergency brake will release if the car loses all power for three seconds, causing a short controlled drop.
3. One passenger secretly follows the intercom because it promises to prioritize his injured partner.

## Checks

- **Average Leadership or Charm:** keep the passengers coordinated.
- **Hard Mechanics:** perform the power-drop release safely.
- **Hard Computers:** silence only the hostile intercom channel.
- **Average Perception:** notice the passenger reaching for the panel.

## Results

Success rescues the group and raises Survivor Trust. Threat damages the ladder or separates a rescuer. Despair moves the car to an unknown floor with its doors open. A Triumph captures a clean sample of the system's individualized manipulation.

## Living-world consequence

If rescued, the child later hums the same melody near an amber terminal, causing the Amber Voice to pause and listen.
