# RA01 — Military Blockade

**Campaign chapter:** I — The Seal  
**Map key:** ra01  
**Existing asset:** `ra01.jpeg`  
**Tags:** social, infiltration, outside  

## Story role

The Eight-Minute Signal is first detectable from the military cordon: a narrow encrypted burst escapes the SCIRE on a maintenance frequency exactly eight minutes each hour. Outside forces think it is bait. Someone inside is also using it as cover for a real distress call.

## Arrival description

> Rain, floodlights, concrete barriers, armored vehicles, and exhausted personnel make the entrance feel less like a front door than an international border built overnight. Nobody at the checkpoint agrees on who has authority to let anyone through.

## Immediate objective

Get through the quarantine without turning the outside response into a permanent enemy, and learn which service route the burst is riding.

## Encounter setup

Lieutenant Thomas Ripley is trying to keep the cordon disciplined while a squad of Blockade Troopers receives contradictory orders. A forged authorization, a favor, a stealthy bypass, or a direct appeal to the rescue mission can all work.

## Escalation beats

- A fresh order arrives telling Ripley to detain anyone asking about maintenance frequencies.
- A trooper recognizes a party member or piece of gear from an earlier incident and quietly offers thirty seconds of camera blindness.
- If violence starts, Ripley tries to contain rather than execute; the real complication is the lockdown alarm that seals the underground approach.

## Suggested checks

- Negotiation or Leadership (Average) to win a temporary passage order.
- Computers (Average) to prove the signal is originating inside rather than outside spoofing.
- Stealth (Hard) to bypass the cordon without a credential.

## Actors already available

- Lieutenant Thomas Ripley
- Blockade Trooper

## Discoverable clues

- The burst rides an obsolete civil-defense maintenance channel.
- The authorization chain contains a command signed by no human dispatcher.
- A physical route marker points toward an old underground service entrance.

## Complications

- Threat: the team is photographed and tagged for later questioning.
- Despair: the cordon locks the obvious entrance and activates a drone sweep.

## Outcomes

- Clean success earns Ripley as a cautious outside contact.
- A messy success gets the team inside but creates a countdown before the route is sealed.
- Failure does not end the run; it diverts the team to a riskier drainage approach.

## Matrix and Amber thread

The Amber Voice does not speak here, but the packet contains a checksum that will later match its transmissions.
