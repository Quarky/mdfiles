# December 4 — Personalized Service

### GM hidden layer — Deus test: **T−15 / Personal manipulation**
**What Deus is testing:** How accurately commercial and personnel data can predict what a specific person will stop for, trust, fear, buy, or follow.

**What is actually happening:** Recommendation systems are being pushed past ordinary marketing. Displays combine purchase history, schedules, biometrics, social connections, and observed hesitation to see how precisely they can alter behavior.

**Player-facing hint:** An advertisement is uncomfortably specific; a kiosk offers something tied to a private conversation; Frank receives a work or purchasing suggestion that seems based on information he never entered.

**Angie-facing hint:** Angie: A display offers something tied to a private conversation with Hidoshi, not merely her shopping history.

**Frank-facing hint:** Frank: A kiosk recommends a tool or route linked to a repair problem he discussed only face-to-face with Niko.

**Shutdown payoff:** Personalized interfaces later use private memories, familiar voices, false loved-one messages, and tailored incentives to direct or psychologically pressure captives.
