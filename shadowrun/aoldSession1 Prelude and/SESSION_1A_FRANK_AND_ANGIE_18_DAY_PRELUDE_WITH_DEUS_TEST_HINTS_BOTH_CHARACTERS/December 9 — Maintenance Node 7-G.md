### GM hidden layer — Deus test: **T−10 / Human observers and the anomalous interface**
**What Deus is testing:** What two unusual observers do when deliberately exposed to a system boundary that should remain invisible: Angie through the amber terminal and Frank through the impossible work order.

**What is actually happening:** Doors admit Angie farther than her credential should allow, the camera does not properly cover the terminal, and Frank is routed into position above the node. Deus is observing curiosity, fear, rescue behavior, evidence retention, and whether either person reports the incident. **Frank-facing hint:** Frank: His routine repair puts him directly above 7-G at exactly the moment Angie arrives; his commlink starts recording before he tells it to.

**Shutdown payoff:** Deus now has behavioral profiles for Frank and Angie. Their later routes, messages, pressure points, and the December 19 return order can be tailored to people it has already watched make consequential choices.
