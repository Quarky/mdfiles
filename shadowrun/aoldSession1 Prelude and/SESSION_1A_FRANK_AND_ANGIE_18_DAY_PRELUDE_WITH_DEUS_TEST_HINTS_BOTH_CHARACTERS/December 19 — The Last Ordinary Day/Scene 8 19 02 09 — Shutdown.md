## Scene 8: 19:02:09 — Shutdown

Read slowly.

> The amber terminal goes black.
>
> Every other light follows.
>
> Ventilation stops.
>
> The floor shudders hard enough to feel through the knees.
>
> Somewhere beyond the service wall, a transit system screams against emergency brakes.
>
> For half a second there is no announcement at all.
>
> Then dozens of things happen at once.

Immediately make the living world audible.

- a child begins pounding on the far side of a shutter;
- a maintenance cart stops, rotates toward the nearest person, and waits;
- emergency lights fail to come on in one corridor and activate too brightly in another;
- commlinks display full signal and deliver nothing;
- someone farther away is shouting a name over and over;
- a locked service door opens while a public exit seals;
- the constant movement of air disappears, making the Arcology feel instantly smaller.

For Angie:

> In the darkness, the new sense does not go blind.
>
> It opens.
>
> The building is more active than before.

Then stop narrating the PCs.

Ask:

**“What do you do first?”**

From this moment onward, transition directly into the established Shutdown Session 1: false announcements, people at the door, survivor decisions, and the wider Arcology crisis.
