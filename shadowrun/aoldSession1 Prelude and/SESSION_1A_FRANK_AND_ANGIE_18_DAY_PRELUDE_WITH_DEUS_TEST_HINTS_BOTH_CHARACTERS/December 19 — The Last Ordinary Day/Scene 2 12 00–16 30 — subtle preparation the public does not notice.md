## Scene 2: 12:00–16:30 — subtle preparation the public does not notice

Across the afternoon, insert two or three observations depending on where the players travel.

### Maintenance movement

Sealed carts move toward service lifts in unusually high numbers. Their manifests say “holiday infrastructure.” Frank can see that some are too heavy for decorations.

### Security movement

Evan's patrol route changes repeatedly. He complains by message if Frank knows him.

> Evan: “Every time we arrive somewhere, the reason for being there changes.”

### Clinic preparation

Mina receives a request to ready trauma beds without an incident code.

> Mina: “You do not prep trauma capacity for a software update.”

### Public information remains cheerful

Holiday announcements continue. Stores run promotions. Families take photographs. The contradiction should be unsettling because both realities coexist.
