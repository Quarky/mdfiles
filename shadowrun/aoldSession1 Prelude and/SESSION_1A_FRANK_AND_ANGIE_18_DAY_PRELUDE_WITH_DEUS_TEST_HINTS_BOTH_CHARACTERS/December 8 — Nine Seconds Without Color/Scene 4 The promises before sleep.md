## Scene 4: The promises before sleep

Do not end on the technical anomaly. End on people.

Hidoshi and Angie make one ordinary plan for December 9. It can be breakfast, dinner, the garden, Rowan's next set, or leaving the Arcology for a few hours.

Frank sends Emily or another established loved one a message about when he expects to be home after tomorrow's shift.

Write both promises down.

These are not foreshadowing props. Let them feel real before they become painful.
