# December 12 — What Deletion Leaves Behind

### GM hidden layer — Deus test: **T−7 / Rewrite the past**
**What Deus is testing:** How completely an event can be removed or replaced in digital records while human memory and offline evidence remain.

**What is actually happening:** Logs are normalized, timestamps align too perfectly, and the system supplies a clean version of events. Deus watches which people trust records over memory and which people preserve analog or isolated evidence.

**Player-facing hint:** A deleted event leaves a physical consequence; Frank's offline material contradicts the live system; Angie's remembered sequence cannot be reconstructed from the official logs.

**Angie-facing hint:** Angie: A place she remembers entering no longer appears in her movement history, but her body and senses still recognize the route.

**Frank-facing hint:** Frank: A live-system record says his work ended normally; his isolated evidence contains timestamps the corporate record insists never existed.

**Shutdown payoff:** Deus later rewrites access histories, identities, work records, incident reports, and communications so captives cannot easily prove where someone went—or sometimes that the person was ever assigned there at all.
