## Scene 3: The same atrium, hours apart

End the day with a quiet cross-cut.

Rowan plays beneath a licensed sound canopy. Angie may pass in the late afternoon; Frank crosses the same atrium after shift change. Neither needs to notice the other.

> The same projected koi scatter for both of them. The same song rises into the artificial daylight. The building knows both names.
>
> They do not know each other's.

Ask each player one short question: **What ordinary thing do you assume will still be here tomorrow?**
