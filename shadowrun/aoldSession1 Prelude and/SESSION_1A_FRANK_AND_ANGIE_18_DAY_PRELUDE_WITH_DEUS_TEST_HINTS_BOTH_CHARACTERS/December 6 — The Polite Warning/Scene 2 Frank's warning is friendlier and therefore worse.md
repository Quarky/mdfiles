## Scene 2: Frank's warning is friendlier and therefore worse

**Time:** 13:15  
**Location:** service security desk  
**Cast:** Frank, Evan, Niko arriving midway

Frank's badge has been flagged for opening a panel that his work order required him to open on December 5.

Evan is assigned to verify the discrepancy.

> Evan: “The order says you opened an unauthorized panel.”
>
> He scrolls.
>
> “The attached ticket says you were assigned to open the same panel.”
>
> He scrolls again.
>
> “I was hoping one of those would disappear before you got here.”

This should feel like two working people trapped inside contradictory systems, not a cop interrogation.

If Frank shows evidence, Evan becomes more cautious, not hostile.

> Evan: “Do not send me that.”
>
> A beat.
>
> “Show it to me on your screen.”

That distinction matters: he wants to know without creating a record that he knows.

Niko can arrive carrying a replacement authorization that invalidates itself when scanned.

> Niko: “I brought paperwork.”
>
> Evan: “You say that like a threat.”

If Frank conceals evidence, use Deception, Negotiation, or Streetwise only if needed. Otherwise let the scene play as conversation.

- Success can establish **Friendly Face: Evan Cho**.
- Despair adds Corporate Attention and a silent watch flag to Frank's badge.
