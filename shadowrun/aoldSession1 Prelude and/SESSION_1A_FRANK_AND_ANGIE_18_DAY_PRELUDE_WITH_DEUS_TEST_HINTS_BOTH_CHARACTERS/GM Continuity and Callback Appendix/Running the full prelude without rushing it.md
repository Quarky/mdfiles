## Running the full prelude without rushing it

This version can support multiple sessions if desired. If it must fit into one long prelude session, do not compress every day into narration. Instead, fully play the strongest scene from each date and use short transitions only between scenes. The critical difference is that even a shortened day should still contain one **actual conversation or decision**, not merely “Angie notices X” or “Frank discovers Y.”

A useful pacing split is:

- **December 1–4:** normal life with one anomaly intrusion per day.
- **December 5–8:** recurring NPC relationships plus escalating evidence.
- **December 9:** continuous play; do not montage.
- **December 10–13:** consequences, medical/technical validation, human memory.
- **December 14–16:** hidden network gains direction and begins moving people.
- **December 17–18:** personal warning, precautions, promises.
- **December 19:** continuous play from late afternoon through 19:02:09.

The prelude succeeds if, when the lights go out, the players can name people they care about, places they remember functioning, ordinary routes they understand, and promises that were supposed to happen tomorrow.
