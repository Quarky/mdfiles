## Scene 2: Eli knows something he should not

**Time:** 18:10  
**Cast:** Frank, Eli, customers

Eli waves Frank over and offers the promised meal voucher redemption.

> Eli: “No ginger unless requested. We have restored civilization.”

Then, casually:

> “How is the scrubber kid?”

Frank never told Eli about Angie.

If Frank asks what he means, Eli explains that a dispenser maintenance notice appeared the morning after the incident:

```text
ASSOCIATED MEDICAL EVENT RESOLVED
NO ACTION REQUIRED
```

Eli assumed Frank had helped a younger employee because Frank's name was attached to the service chain.

**Average Perception:** the message came from the dispenser's service channel, not from a person.

Eli becomes uncomfortable when he realizes he has been given personal information he did not request.

> “Tell me what to delete.”

If Frank asks him to preserve it instead, Eli will print or hand-copy the wording. This creates a low-tech evidence trail.
