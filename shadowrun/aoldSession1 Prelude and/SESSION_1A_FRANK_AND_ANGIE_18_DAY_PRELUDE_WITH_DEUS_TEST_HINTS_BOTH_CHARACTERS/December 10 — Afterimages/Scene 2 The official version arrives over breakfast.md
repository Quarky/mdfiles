## Scene 2: The official version arrives over breakfast

Hidoshi receives a Renraku incident summary before he requests one.

```text
INCIDENT 7G-0912
MINOR ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL FAULT
ONE VISITOR EXPERIENCED TRANSIENT SYNCOPE
NO DATA LOSS
NO SECURITY BREACH
NODE POWER STATE: INACTIVE
```

The terminal was supposedly inactive.

If Angie points out the impossibility, Hidoshi says, “Then we keep our own chronology.” He opens a paper notebook or a disconnected device and asks Angie to tell him only what she wants recorded.

This creates a human countermeasure: not hacking, not magic, just refusing to let a system be the only keeper of memory.
