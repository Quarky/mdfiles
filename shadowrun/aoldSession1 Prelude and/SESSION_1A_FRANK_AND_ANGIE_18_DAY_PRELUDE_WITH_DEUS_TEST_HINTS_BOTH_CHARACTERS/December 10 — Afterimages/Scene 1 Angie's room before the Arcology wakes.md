## Scene 1: Angie's room before the Arcology wakes

**Time:** 06:40  
**Cast:** Angie, Hidoshi

> The room is quiet except for the ventilation and the soft electrical tick of devices that never seemed worth noticing yesterday.
>
> Today every active object has direction.
>
> The bedside display is a pressure to the left. The wall outlet is a small steady field. A cleaning drone moving in the corridor passes through Angie's awareness before its wheels become audible. Somewhere beyond the apartment wall, a sleeping neighbor's bioelectric presence is faint and warm in a way no machine is.

Do not describe this as perfect new sight. It is confusing, overlapping, and local.

Hidoshi has been awake. He has water, painkillers if appropriate, and no rehearsed speech.

> Hidoshi: “I have several theories. None of them are useful yet.”
>
> “Are you in pain?”
>
> “Do you know where you are?”
>
> “Do you want questions, or breakfast?”

Let Angie decide how much of the two presences she tells him. If she mentions the smaller frightened one, Hidoshi does not immediately label it good.

> “Afraid things can still be dangerous.”

If she mentions the cold one, he asks, “Did it understand that you were a person?” That question is more unsettling than any exposition.

**Average Discipline:** separate machines, people, and ambient infrastructure without overload.

- Success: establish **Sensory Baseline**.
- Advantage: Angie learns that closing her eyes reduces visual distraction but not the new data sense.
- Threat: Resonance Strain +1.
