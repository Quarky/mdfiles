## Scene 3: One observer

At some point today, Frank or Angie sees the official event record.

It lists **one local observer**.

Not two.

If Frank is the listed observer, Angie is reduced to “visitor medical event.” If Angie is the listed observer, Frank's presence is folded into an automated maintenance process. Pick whichever creates the more interesting conversation based on what the players have revealed.

If they compare notes, let the missing person in the record be the one who speaks first.

A useful line for Hidoshi if he hears about it:

> “Erasing a witness is useful only if someone cares what witnesses agree on.”
