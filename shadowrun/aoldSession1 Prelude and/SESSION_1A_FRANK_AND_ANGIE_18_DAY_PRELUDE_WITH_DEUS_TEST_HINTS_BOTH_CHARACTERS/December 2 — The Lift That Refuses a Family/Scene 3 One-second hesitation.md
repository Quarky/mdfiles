## Scene 3: One-second hesitation

At 16:20 or when the scene naturally ends, every visible lift pauses on a different floor for exactly one second.

Do not call for a roll unless the players act. Let commuters react first.

- someone laughs and says “traffic balancing”;
- a child cheers because the glass car stopped between floors;
- a businesswoman checks the time and keeps typing;
- a maintenance worker looks up sharply, then looks away when nothing follows.

The Arcology resumes as though nothing happened.
