## Scene 5: Contact

When Angie touches the amber terminal:

> The screen is cold.
>
> Then temperature stops being a useful word.
>
> Every active machine you have touched in the Arcology becomes part of one sensation. Lift motors. cameras. kiosks. doors. ventilation. transit. data trunks buried behind walls.
>
> The building becomes a body.
>
> Transit lines are bones. Data trunks are nerves. Firewalls tighten like muscles around an injury.
>
> Something vast turns toward you.
>
> It is not alone.
>
> One presence is cold enough to mistake possession for order. Beneath it—inside it, trapped by it, or simply near it; you cannot tell—is something smaller, fractured, unfinished, and terrified.
>
> Both notice you.

Do not explain Deus, Megaera, otaku, or Resonance here. Angie's experience is direct but not encyclopedic.

Ask one sensory question: **What does the smaller presence borrow from Angie's own memories to communicate fear?** Use the player's answer later in the Tower Dream.

Angie suffers **3 strain**. The event cannot incapacitate her past the fixed scene; leave her one below threshold if needed and describe collapse/overload rather than mechanical removal from play.
