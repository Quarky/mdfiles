## Scene 9: Hidoshi arrives

Do not cut directly from escape to exposition. Put Angie somewhere public enough to feel the contrast: clinic waiting area, quiet service lobby, or seating near a closed kiosk.

Hidoshi arrives quickly and is visibly angry only for one second before choosing not to spend that anger on Angie.

> Hidoshi: “Are you hurt?”
>
> Whatever Angie answers, his next question is: “Do you want me to call someone, or do you want me to listen first?”

If Frank remains, Hidoshi asks his name and role. He does not immediately assume Frank caused the problem because he can see Frank helped her out.

If Angie minimizes the event, Hidoshi notices but does not force confession in public.

Renraku's preliminary description appears almost immediately: **LOCAL MAINTENANCE FAULT / TRANSIENT MEDICAL EVENT / NO PUBLIC IMPACT**.

Hidoshi reads it once.

> “That was written too quickly.”
