## Scene 4: Frank above the grille

Frank can see portions of the node below through the maintenance grille.

Ask three questions rather than narrating his interpretation:

1. What rule has Angie obviously broken by being here?
2. What tells Frank she is frightened, curious, or overwhelmed rather than part of maintenance?
3. Why does he hesitate to immediately call security?

If Frank speaks, Angie can hear a voice from above before she sees him. If he starts climbing down, the contact occurs before he can safely cross the distance. If he watches, let him see the exact moment the terminal changes.
