## Scene 1: Angie's day before the corridor

**Time:** late morning through early afternoon  
**Location:** public levels

Start with the ordinary plan made last night. Let the player experience enough of it that it feels like a day, not a cutscene waiting for destiny.

Possible beats:

- breakfast with Hidoshi while he reviews legal notes;
- Rowan greeting Angie from across the atrium;
- Eli remembering what she ordered earlier in the week;
- the family from December 2 passing safely through a lift;
- Marisol complaining about a new vibration no one else hears.

The amber pulse intrudes gradually.

First it appears in a polished spoon.

Later in a lift door.

Then in a blank information panel that should be showing an advertisement.

It does not behave like a marker telling Angie where to go. It behaves like a signal that becomes easier to sense whenever she happens to move in one particular direction.

Hidoshi may notice distraction.

> Hidoshi: “Do you need me to stop asking whether something is wrong, or start asking more seriously?”

Do not decide whether Angie tells him. If she does, Hidoshi offers to come, call security, or help her test it from a public area. The fixed continuity is that Angie eventually reaches 7-G, not that she must behave recklessly.

If she tries to bring someone directly to the anomaly, let the route change. A lift reroutes. A directory refreshes. A door suddenly requires a credential it accepted seconds earlier. The signal becomes clear again only when Angie is physically separated enough to continue alone. Do not state that the system “wants her alone”; let the behavior imply it.
