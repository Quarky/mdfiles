## Scene 7: First words

Frank gets down before security arrives.

The established introduction can be used exactly:

> “Name's Frank. I work maintenance. You look like you could use a hand.”

Or Frank's player can phrase the same offer differently.

Angie controls whether she accepts the hand, challenges him, asks what happened, tries to stand alone, or focuses on his commlink.

Her new perception tells her that Frank's commlink contains a fragment connected to what touched her. It does **not** tell her Frank caused it.

Ask Angie: **What quality in Frank—voice, smell, posture, electrical rhythm, or something else—makes him distinct from the huge presence she just felt?**

Ask Frank: **What makes Angie's condition look different from an ordinary fainting spell or electrical accident?**
