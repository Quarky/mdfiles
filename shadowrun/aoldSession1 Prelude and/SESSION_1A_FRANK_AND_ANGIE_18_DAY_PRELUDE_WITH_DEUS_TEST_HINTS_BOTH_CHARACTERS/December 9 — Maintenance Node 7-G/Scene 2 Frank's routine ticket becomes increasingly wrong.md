## Scene 2: Frank's routine ticket becomes increasingly wrong

**Time:** early afternoon  
**Location:** service route toward Node 7-G  
**Cast:** Frank, Niko initially, remote dispatch

Frank's task looks boring: verify a scrubber handshake and replace an air-control relay if needed.

Niko starts with him but is redirected midway by a higher-priority fault.

> Niko: “If this turns out to be another perfectly healthy machine, tell it I am offended professionally.”

Frank's printed ticket continues to exist even after the digital ticket disappears.

At the first access door, the system opens before he scans.

At the second, it asks him to reauthenticate twice.

At the third, his badge is accepted under a job code he has never seen.

The service corridor is older than the public spaces around it.

> Paint has been touched up around old Renraku logos instead of replacing them. Cable trunks disappear into walls that predate the current floor plan. The airflow has the dry, slightly dusty feel of a route people use less than the building claims.

At Node 7-G, the relay is accessible from a crawlspace/duct above the room. Frank climbs in because that is the normal way to reach it.

Let him work. Give him actual mundane tasks: test voltage, compare relay state, check scrubber handshake. The equipment is not failed. The system insists it is.

Then he hears a door open below.
