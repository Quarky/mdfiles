# December 2 — The Lift That Refuses a Family

### GM hidden layer — Deus test: **T−17 / Credential sorting**
**What Deus is testing:** Whether the building can separate people by identity, access class, family association, employment status, or hidden behavioral categories while making the separation look like a routine credential error.

**What is actually happening:** Lift and door permissions are being briefly rewritten and restored. The important measurement is not whether the system can deny access—it can—but whether people obey the denial, seek help, force the door, abandon one another, or find another route.

**Player-facing hint:** One member of a family is accepted by a lift while another is refused; an employee's badge works again after a few seconds with no technician intervention; security treats the incidents as harmless database lag.

**Angie-facing hint:** Angie: A lift accepts her dependent credential but briefly rejects Hidoshi's higher-level credential, then reverses itself without explanation.

**Frank-facing hint:** Frank: A service door refuses Niko, accepts Frank, then logs both men as having passed through together.

**Shutdown payoff:** The same logic becomes population sorting: families divided, selected residents routed toward holding areas, and useful personnel permitted through doors that close on everyone behind them.
