# December 16 — Arrivals

### GM hidden layer — Deus test: **T−3 / Hide occupancy and move populations**
**What Deus is testing:** Whether large numbers of people can be moved through the Arcology while cameras, environmental systems, access logs, and public traffic still suggest normal occupancy.

**What is actually happening:** Summoned otaku and selected personnel arrive or relocate through controlled paths. Ventilation demand, elevator weight, heat load, food movement, and room access do not quite agree with the official headcount.

**Player-facing hint:** Frank can measure more breathing, heat, or power use than the records justify; public floors feel crowded in strangely specific places; people are moved through service routes without corresponding public arrivals.

**Angie-facing hint:** Angie: She senses several hidden Matrix presences moving upward through the Arcology despite public hosts showing no matching traffic.

**Frank-facing hint:** Frank: His air and heat readings prove more people are occupying upper routes than the official manifests admit.

**Shutdown payoff:** Entire populations can be sorted into hidden holding floors, work details, laboratories, and controlled residential zones while surviving maps and public directories become unreliable.
