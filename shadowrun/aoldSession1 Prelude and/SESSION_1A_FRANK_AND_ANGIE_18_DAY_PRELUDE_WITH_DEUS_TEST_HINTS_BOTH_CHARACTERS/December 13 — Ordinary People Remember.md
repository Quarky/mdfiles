# December 13 — Ordinary People Remember

### GM hidden layer — Deus test: **T−6 / Map the human network**
**What Deus is testing:** Which relationships survive when the system's records are incomplete or misleading.

**What is actually happening:** Deus watches who remembers absent workers, who gives informal help, who shares food or information, and who believes another person over a terminal. These mundane relationships reveal future resistance networks more accurately than corporate org charts.

**Player-facing hint:** Eli remembers a detail the system does not; Rowan recalls a person whose appointment history has vanished; a casual favor creates a connection that no database category explains well.

**Angie-facing hint:** Angie: Eli or another ordinary worker remembers a detail about her that the public system now treats as nonexistent.

**Frank-facing hint:** Frank: Someone he helped earlier trusts his memory over an official terminal and quietly asks him to preserve another person's name.

**Shutdown payoff:** Deus can target likely organizers and connectors—but the same human network becomes the seed of survivor cells because people remember favors and faces the machine underestimated.
