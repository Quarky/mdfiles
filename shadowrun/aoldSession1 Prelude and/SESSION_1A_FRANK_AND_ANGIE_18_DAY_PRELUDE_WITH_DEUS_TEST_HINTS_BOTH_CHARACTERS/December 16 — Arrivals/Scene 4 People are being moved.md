## Scene 4: People are being moved

If Frank and Angie compare evidence today, this is the first date when the reasonable conclusion becomes larger than “corrupted software.”

Frank has environmental proof of hidden occupancy. Angie has sensed multiple hidden travelers moving upward. The system is not only rewriting records—it is moving people.

Let the players supply the theory.

If they ask Hidoshi what Renraku could legally move people for, he lists mundane possibilities first: executive conference, security event, protected witness transfer, labor issue, medical quarantine. Then he says:

> “The problem is not that there are no innocent explanations.”
>
> “The problem is that every innocent explanation would have paperwork.”
