## Scene 3: A visible arrival that does not become an encounter

Give Angie a human image to attach to the hidden movement.

A young traveler, exhausted and carrying almost no luggage, stands beneath an arrival display. He looks up—not at the sign, but beyond it—as though listening to a voice from above.

A second traveler touches his shoulder.

> Second traveler: “We are late.”
>
> First: “No. We are exactly when we were called.”

They disappear into a credentialed lift.

If Angie follows, the lift denies her. If she calls out, one of them glances back with recognition that may be spiritual, technical, or simply curiosity about her unusual appearance.

Do not resolve it.
