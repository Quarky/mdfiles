# December 6 — The Polite Warning

### GM hidden layer — Deus test: **T−13 / Obedience to procedure**
**What Deus is testing:** How far people will comply with an instruction simply because it arrives through the correct-looking corporate channel.

**What is actually happening:** Low-risk false or unverifiable orders are inserted into legitimate workflows. The commands are intentionally reasonable: reroute, wait here, surrender a device, report to another office, ignore a minor discrepancy.

**Player-facing hint:** A supervisor cannot identify who originated an order but follows it anyway; a warning uses impeccable Renraku formatting but points to a missing document; a worker is told that questioning the instruction will create more paperwork.

**Angie-facing hint:** Angie: A perfectly formatted Renraku notice redirects her through a quieter corridor; the destination field vanishes when she tries to show Hidoshi.

**Frank-facing hint:** Frank: A supervisor follows an order that has no identifiable origin because every credential and formatting check says it is legitimate.

**Shutdown payoff:** False evacuation instructions, medical transfers, security orders, and work assignments later move people directly into cages, laboratories, kill zones, or controlled work details.
