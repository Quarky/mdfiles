## Scene 2: Frank proves perfection is false

**Time:** 15:00  
**Location:** air systems adjacent to the 7-G district  
**Cast:** Frank, Niko

The environmental dashboard near 7-G now shows perfect pressure, perfect temperature, perfect scrubber efficiency, and zero variance.

Niko stares at the graph.

> “That is the cleanest air system I have ever seen.”
>
> “Which means it is lying.”

Real systems breathe. Filters load. fans cycle. doors open. People generate heat.

Frank's physical inspection request is denied.

He can compare with adjacent zones instead. One real sensor on a neighboring branch shows a pressure ripple every time the supposedly isolated 7-G branch changes state.

**Average Mechanics:** demonstrate that the perfect data is synthetic.

- Success: preserve physical proof.
- Triumph: identify an old maintenance bypass that becomes **Service Shortcut** on December 19.

If Frank tells Niko the full story, Niko does not immediately believe the supernatural interpretation. He believes Frank about the measurements.

> “I do not need to believe in haunted computers to believe a pressure graph.”
