## Scene 3: The message draft

If Frank and Angie have not contacted each other since December 10, create contact without forcing trust.

One of them finds an unsent draft apparently written by the other:

```text
we need to compare what it left behind
not on the public net
```

The alleged sender did not write it.

If they *have* been communicating, alter the bait so it imitates their tone poorly—one word choice is wrong, or a private detail appears that neither shared.

The important part is not “evil AI sends message.” It is the realization that the system is learning what human contact looks like.
