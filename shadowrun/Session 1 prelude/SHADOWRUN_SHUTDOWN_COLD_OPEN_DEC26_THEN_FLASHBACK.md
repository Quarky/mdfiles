# Shadowrun: Arcology Shutdown — Cold Open in the Converted Arcology

## GM purpose

Open **after** the December 19 Shutdown, let Frank and Angie experience a short, violent glimpse of what the Arcology becomes, then cut backward.

**Play order:**

1. **December 26, 2059 — Shutdown +7:** converted-Arcology cold open.
2. Angie intercepts fragments of internal traffic from December 21–26.
3. Frank is caught in a Blue-controlled transfer/clinic crisis.
4. The Resistance attack on Floors 14/13 detonates the scene.
5. At the moment both characters are about to learn too much, **hard cut to December 10**.
6. Play the December 10 immediate aftermath of the original Node 7-G contact.
7. Then cut to **December 1 / T−18** and run the existing eighteen-day prelude forward.

This cold open is a **flash-forward**, not a declaration that these exact events must occur unchanged once normal play catches up to December 26. If later player choices make the precise geography impossible, preserve the images and emotional truth rather than forcing continuity.

# Why December 26

By Shutdown +7, the takeover is no longer just locked doors and false announcements. The lower Arcology has become visibly organized around captivity and conversion:

- Floor 14 is being used as a mass holding area feeding the Floor 13 hospital.
- Floor 13 is functioning as a major implant/control facility.
- Blues conduct sweeps and transfers.
- Banded medical personnel work the clinic.
- nearby residences are being emptied.
- Resistance cells are active enough to strike the conversion complex.

That lets the players glimpse the *destination* of the eighteen-day prelude before seeing how quietly Deus built toward it.

# Scene 1 — December 26, 2059, 20:38

## Floor 15 service access, descending toward 14/13

Do not begin with exposition.

> The Arcology smells different.
>
> Not abandoned. Used.
>
> The corridor Frank remembers as apartments has been stripped of doors. Furniture is stacked behind a mesh barrier as if an entire neighborhood was cleared in a hurry and then inventoried. White arrows have been painted over the old resident wayfinding. They all point down.
>
> Somewhere below, people are chanting numbers.
>
> Not together.
>
> One voice at a time.

Frank and Angie are not initially together. Alternate quickly between them.

# Angie — The building is talking through itself

Angie's data sense catches traffic that ordinary commlinks cannot resolve. It is not one clean channel. It feels like listening to several rooms through the same wall.

Give her the first fragment from **December 21**.

The key is that she does not merely “play an audio file.” She has to **hold onto the transmission while the Arcology notices someone is listening**.

### First capture

A calm investigator's voice. Household questions. Missing child. Loyalty.

Then metadata flashes past:

```text
SOURCE AGE: 5 DAYS
ORIGIN: RESIDENTIAL 20
ROUTE: INVALID
ARCHIVE STATUS: NONE
```

Ask Angie what she does to keep the fragment.

Possible checks:

- **Average Computers:** stabilize the traffic long enough to hear the next fragment.
- **Average Discipline:** separate old emotional residue from a current signal.
- **Hard Computers:** identify that a supposedly deleted event is being replayed internally.
- **Triumph:** detect that another listener is present somewhere in the system.
- **Threat:** the cold presence turns a fraction of its attention toward her.

Do not stop the scene for failure. Failure means she gets less context and the system identifies her sooner.

# Frank — Transfer corridor

Frank is in the service passage beside a forced civilian transfer.

A Blue-controlled detail is moving residents toward Floor 14.

Do not make Frank start hidden and safe. Put him somewhere a maintenance worker plausibly reached and then discovered the job had changed around him.

> The service lift opens.
>
> Frank expects filters.
>
> People come out.
>
> Twenty of them, maybe more. No luggage. No coats. Plastic identity tabs clipped to their clothes.
>
> Two armed figures walk behind them.
>
> A third walks alongside the line reading names from a display.
>
> The display recognizes Frank.
>
> “Maintenance,” the Blue says. “Good. Door fourteen-C is sticking. Fix it.”

The cruel part is that the request is real. The door *is* sticking.

If Frank fixes it, the transfer proceeds faster.

If he sabotages it, he can create time but immediately becomes suspicious.

If he refuses, the Blue tries to requisition him through the building.

Possible action:

- fix the door while secretly creating a second fault;
- jam the transfer gate;
- hide a civilian in a maintenance void;
- spoof the number of people who crossed;
- provoke a mechanical emergency;
- physically fight only if the player chooses to escalate.

Give him immediate tactile evidence of conversion infrastructure: medical crates, eye/ear implant inventory, restraint fittings, scrubbed gurneys, or wiring bundles going down toward Floor 13.

# Angie — second and third fragments

While Frank's situation tightens, Angie catches **December 22**.

First: Brady's return.

Then: the doll.

Use the audio tracks already prepared for these beats if available.

The tonal change should be obvious: domestic conversation, a cheerful manufactured voice, and underneath it internal routing metadata.

Then hit the **December 23 telecom loop**.

Angie hears a call trying to leave the building.

For a second she hears the outside world.

Then the response repeats from *inside* the Arcology.

This is the clue that makes her understand that the network is not merely blocking communications. It can imitate successful communication.

# Cross-cut — Frank gets made

The Blue supervising the transfer receives an update.

Frank's work order changes while the Blue is reading it.

```text
CRODLER, FRANK
STATUS: VERIFIED
TASK: DOOR 14-C

CRODLER, FRANK
STATUS: HOLD

CRODLER, FRANK
STATUS: OBSERVER
```

The Blue looks up.

This is the start of the action sequence.

Use the environment:

- descending transfer cages;
- half-removed apartment walls;
- maintenance shafts;
- terrified civilians;
- a clinic gurney convoy;
- doors that can lock independently;
- a service cart carrying restraint hardware;
- Blues arriving from opposite directions.

Frank should have choices rather than a mandatory fight, but every option should move.

# Angie — live December 26 traffic

At **20:40:21**, the old fragments stop.

The live channel opens.

A doll voice is moving through the network.

It is giving directions.

Floor 20.

Then 14.

Then 13.

Angie catches pieces of:

- access acknowledgements;
- holding-pen counts;
- clinic preparation;
- surgical equipment requests;
- Blue transfer commands;
- a patient identifier changing status;
- the doll's signal moving with a human being.

This is where you play the **most engaging doll/clinic audio segment**.

Let Angie realize that she is not hearing a recording anymore.

This is happening now.

# Scene 2 — Floor 14 breaks open

Frank's problem and Angie's signal converge around Floor 14.

The holding area should immediately communicate how much the Arcology has changed.

> Someone removed the walls.
>
> Bedrooms, kitchens, laundry rooms—gone.
>
> The floor is one enormous interrupted space divided by temporary fencing and strips of light on the ground.
>
> Hundreds of people are arranged by color, number, or destination.
>
> Every few minutes a gate opens and another group disappears toward the hospital below.

A child asks Frank whether he works here.

Before he can answer, the lights go red.

# The Resistance attack

At roughly the same part of the evening as the canon Floor 13 surgery incident, a Resistance operation hits the conversion complex.

Do not make the Resistance look omnipotent. Make it violent, desperate, and partially successful.

Possible beats:

- lights blow out on one side of the pen;
- a shaped charge or sabotaged power relay opens a service boundary;
- civilians surge toward the wrong exit;
- Blues begin trying to restore ordered lines;
- a clinic team attempts to evacuate selected patients rather than everyone;
- automated doors repeatedly separate people;
- Frank can exploit infrastructure he understands;
- Angie can feel the building trying to re-establish its categories.

This provides the action climax without revealing every secret of the Arcology.

# The cut-off

Angie keeps the live channel for several more seconds.

Then the cold presence notices the path she is using.

Every transmission stops at once.

Not static.

Silence.

One final packet reaches her:

```text
OBSERVER RECOGNIZED
HISTORICAL INDEX: 2
ORIGIN EVENT: 2059-12-09
```

Frank sees a nearby screen change at the same moment:

```text
OBSERVER 2 LOCATED
```

Do not resolve the chase.

# Hard cut — December 10

Cut in the middle of motion.

Frank reaches for a tool, weapon, hatch, civilian, or Angie—

**CUT.**

> Morning sunlight through expensive Arcology glass.
>
> December 10.
>
> The building still works.

Angie wakes after the Node 7-G incident with her new perception.

Frank still has `7G_ANOMALY.log`.

Renraku is insisting yesterday was a maintenance anomaly.

Run the December 10 material from the existing prelude.

# Second hard cut — December 1 / T−18

After the immediate December 10 aftermath establishes that the December 9 encounter happened, cut farther back.

> Eighteen days before the Shutdown.
>
> Before the pens.
>
> Before the clinic.
>
> Before Frank's name became “OBSERVER.”
>
> The projected koi move out of Angie's way.
>
> A maintenance ticket appears on Frank's queue.
>
> The Arcology is beautiful.

Now begin the existing December 1 prelude and play forward.

# What the players should understand from the cold open

Do **not** explain Deus, Blues, Greens, Whites, the Banded hierarchy, or the exact conversion program.

The players should leave the cold open knowing only:

- the Shutdown becomes much worse than locked doors;
- people are being sorted and changed;
- the building can falsify communications and history;
- the doll is connected to something deeply wrong;
- Angie's strange perception can hear material the system tried to bury;
- Frank eventually becomes a specifically tracked observer;
- whatever began at Node 7-G matters to the future.

The eighteen-day flashback then answers **how the building got there** without removing the mystery of **why**.
