# December 12 — What Deletion Leaves Behind

### GM hidden layer — Deus test: **T−7 / Rewrite the past**
**What Deus is testing:** How completely an event can be removed or replaced in digital records while human memory and offline evidence remain.

**What is actually happening:** Logs are normalized, timestamps align too perfectly, and the system supplies a clean version of events. Deus watches which people trust records over memory and which people preserve analog or isolated evidence.

**Player-facing hint:** A deleted event leaves a physical consequence; Frank's offline material contradicts the live system; Angie's remembered sequence cannot be reconstructed from the official logs.

**Angie-facing hint:** Angie: A place she remembers entering no longer appears in her movement history, but her body and senses still recognize the route.

**Frank-facing hint:** Frank: A live-system record says his work ended normally; his isolated evidence contains timestamps the corporate record insists never existed.

**Shutdown payoff:** Deus later rewrites access histories, identities, work records, incident reports, and communications so captives cannot easily prove where someone went—or sometimes that the person was ever assigned there at all.

### What the day should accomplish

Prove that digital history can be rewritten faster than physical residue and human memory. Encourage the players to start valuing offline evidence.

> **GM PREP NOTE:** Scene 3 is intentionally placed here so you can prepare the day's Deus interaction first. During play, keep the numbered chronology unless the scene itself says to cross-cut earlier.

## Scene 3: The message draft

If Frank and Angie have not contacted each other since December 10, create contact without forcing trust.

One of them finds an unsent draft apparently written by the other:

```text
we need to compare what it left behind
not on the public net
```

The alleged sender did not write it.

If they *have* been communicating, alter the bait so it imitates their tone poorly—one word choice is wrong, or a private detail appears that neither shared.

The important part is not “evil AI sends message.” It is the realization that the system is learning what human contact looks like.

### If Angie and Frank are split

- **Angie sees:** Angie can receive the false draft on a public or personal interface.
- **Frank sees:** Frank can receive a complementary draft or notice that his style is being imitated.
- **Cross-cut:** If split, do not force contact. The system is testing whether fabricated communication causes them to seek one another.

## Scene 1: Public Matrix lounge

**Time:** 12:20  
**Cast:** Angie, lounge attendant, students, remote workers

> The Matrix lounge is bright, social, and aggressively normal. Students share virtual workspaces. Tourists rent translation overlays. A pair of teenagers are designing impossible shoes and laughing every time the physics engine refuses them.

Angie can use the lounge for any ordinary purpose first. Let her new perception sit under the scene rather than dominate it.

Then she notices absence.

The route that once pointed toward Node 7-G is not visible as data. It is visible as the *shape data took around something that is no longer there*.

Describe it like footprints after the person has gone.

If Angie focuses:

> The missing route has edges.
>
> Something passed through here often enough to wear a path in systems that now insist no path existed.

When she pushes deeper, the residue does not yield code. It yields a burst of someone else's fear: fluorescent light, the smell of overheated plastic, the certainty of being watched through a camera that was supposedly off.

Keep it fragmentary.

**Average Computers:** identify deliberate deletion rather than ordinary cache expiry.

- Success: prove the route existed.
- Advantage: **Residual Signature**.
- Threat: Hidden Attention +1; one public terminal closes Angie's session politely and refuses to reopen it for exactly nine minutes.

The attendant apologizes and offers another station, unaware of anything unusual.

> **OPTIONAL DEUS HINT — ANGIE:** A route Angie remembers walking no longer exists in her movement history. Her body, scent memory, and electrosense still recognize the physical corridor.

## Scene 2: Frank proves perfection is false

**Time:** 15:00  
**Location:** air systems adjacent to the 7-G district  
**Cast:** Frank, Niko

The environmental dashboard near 7-G now shows perfect pressure, perfect temperature, perfect scrubber efficiency, and zero variance.

Niko stares at the graph.

> “That is the cleanest air system I have ever seen.”
>
> “Which means it is lying.”

Real systems breathe. Filters load. fans cycle. doors open. People generate heat.

Frank's physical inspection request is denied.

He can compare with adjacent zones instead. One real sensor on a neighboring branch shows a pressure ripple every time the supposedly isolated 7-G branch changes state.

**Average Mechanics:** demonstrate that the perfect data is synthetic.

- Success: preserve physical proof.
- Triumph: identify an old maintenance bypass that becomes **Service Shortcut** on December 19.

If Frank tells Niko the full story, Niko does not immediately believe the supernatural interpretation. He believes Frank about the measurements.

> “I do not need to believe in haunted computers to believe a pressure graph.”

> **OPTIONAL DEUS HINT — FRANK:** The official shift record shows Frank's work ending normally while his isolated evidence contains timestamps the live system insists never occurred.
