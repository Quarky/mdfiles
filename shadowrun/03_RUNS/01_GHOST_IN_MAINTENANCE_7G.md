# Network Run — Ghost in Maintenance 7-G

## Brief

Frank finds Angie at the amber terminal on the date established in the Frank start files. The run begins in physical space, crosses into AR/VR only when a player chooses, and always returns to the arcology's failing maintenance infrastructure.

## Objective

Recover `7G_ANOMALY.log`, restore one safe evacuation route, and leave before Renraku's spider identifies the team. Optional objectives: preserve Angie’s partial memory, rescue a clinic patient, or copy the amber host's mark pattern.

## Opening — physical scene

The service corridor smells of wet insulation and hot dust. A ventilation fan turns one blade at a time. Angie is on one knee beside the amber terminal; Frank sees a maintenance diagnostic that should not exist. A Perception or Computers check notices the terminal is simultaneously reporting “offline” and accepting a live command.

## Matrix encounter

1. **Access System:** Hard local hub unless Frank has a legitimate maintenance credential; then Average.
2. **Matrix Perception:** reveal the amber host, Sentry 2 and a mark that resembles neither Renraku nor the runner.
3. **Disable Security Program:** the team must choose Firewall or Sentry first. Disabling Sentry prevents a trace but leaves Firewall blocking restricted files.
4. **Edit File:** copy the anomaly log. A threat result duplicates a corrupted timestamp and points responders to the physical node.
5. **Enact Command:** restore one evacuation door with an Easy check, or open all doors with Hard and a risk of crowd surge.

## Opposition

The system defender is a Renraku spider using Computers and a Firewall rating appropriate to the host. The host itself does not attack unless the GM chooses a program from the Haakonsen adversary/gear material. If a physical response is needed, use the existing Renraku Security Drone or Maintenance Spider actor from the module.

## Outcomes

- **Clean success:** log recovered; one route opens; Trace remains below 3.
- **Messy success:** log recovered, but a resident registry or clinic feed is exposed; add one Trace.
- **Failure:** the team is expelled, suffers the normal Matrix consequence, and must choose a physical bypass or a second access attempt.
- **Despair:** the amber host places a recognizable mark on a runner or sends a false “all clear” through the public announcement system.
