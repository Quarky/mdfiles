# Renraku Arcology: Day-by-Day State of Every Floor

**Coverage:** December 1, 2059 through February 10, 2060  
**Primary time zone:** PST for events inside Seattle; source transmissions retain EST when the book labels them that way.  
**Format:** Each H1 is one autosplit-ready day. Each H2 is a linkable floor or canonical floor range. The source floor index groups many levels; every numbered floor from B21 through 320, plus the rooftop and campaign Node 7-G, is covered by one and only one entry each day.

## Evidence key

- **[Canon]** — explicitly dated or stated in *Renraku Arcology: Shutdown*.
- **[Campaign-established]** — established in the Angie/Frank campaign material, especially the December 9 Node 7-G incident.
- **[Derived]** — a GM-facing extrapolation that connects dated canon to the book's February 10 floor snapshot.
- **[Optional pressure event]** — safe to move, omit, or replace if player action changes the timeline.

## How to use the chronology

The book's Floor Index is an intelligence snapshot assembled on February 10, not a minute-by-minute construction log. This document therefore preserves every explicit date and treats the evolution of each floor as derived until the snapshot catches up. A floor entry's **state** is strategic; its **activity** is the specific scene occurring there that day. If the runners intervene, keep the strategic state only when it still follows from play.

The chronology begins on December 1 because the coup requires a quiet subversion period even though the first precisely dated White-otaku record is December 14. The pre-December-14 material is intentionally marked derived. The December 9 Angie/Frank contact is campaign-established. Shutdown occurs on December 19 at 19:02:09 PST.
