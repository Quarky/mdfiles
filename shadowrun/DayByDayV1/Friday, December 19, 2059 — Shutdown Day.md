**Phase:** Shutdown Day  
**Building-wide control:** Deus seizing control  
**Daily state:** Normal activity continues into the evening; at 19:02:09 PST the building becomes a closed machine-directed battlespace.

- **17:41:44 — 20:** [Canon] Cal Reynolds begins his journal in his family's Floor 20 residence.
