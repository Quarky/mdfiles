# Welcome to 2059

The world changed forever in 2011.

Magic returned. Dragons appeared. Humans transformed into new peoples.
Corporations became sovereign powers. The Matrix became a battlefield.
Disease, war, ecological catastrophe, economic collapse, and
technological acceleration reshaped civilization.

This is the **Sixth World**.

By 2059, the old nation-state order still exists, but it shares the
planet with powers that can rival or exceed governments: **AAA
megacorporations, dragons, magical nations, criminal syndicates, and
shadow organizations**.

For ordinary people, life is a negotiation between security and freedom.

For a shadowrunner, the world is a maze of opportunities.

If you have the skills, the connections, and the nerve, there is always
someone willing to pay.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Sixth World at a Glance

-   **Megacorporations are sovereign powers.** Major corporations
    possess extraterritorial rights, their own laws, courts, security
    forces, and armies.
-   **A corporate enclave can effectively be another country.**
-   **SINless people exist outside much of legitimate society.** Without
    a SIN, access to legal employment, housing, and other services
    becomes difficult.
-   **Magic is real.** Spellcasters, shamans, adepts, spirits, and other
    Awakened phenomena are part of everyday reality.
-   **Metahumans are people.** Elves, dwarfs, orks, and trolls are
    established populations, although discrimination is widespread.
-   **Cyberware is normal.** Full cybernetic limb replacement is
    possible and common.
-   **The Matrix is essential infrastructure and a battlefield.**
    Deckers enter cyberspace using cyberdecks and fight corporate
    security systems.
-   **Reputation matters.** In the shadows, who knows you---and what
    they think of you---can matter as much as your skills.
-   **Violence is never far away.** Corporate security, gangs,
    syndicates, and private armies all enforce their interests.
-   **Shadowrunners fill the gaps.** When a corporation, government, or
    syndicate needs something done without officially admitting it,
    runners are useful.

# A History of the Sixth World

This timeline is written specifically for a **2059 campaign**. Later
metaplot developments are not treated as if they had already happened.
Where later sourcebooks reveal information that was not public in 2059,
it is identified as later knowledge rather than folded into the 2059
present.

## 1999--2010 --- The Old World Begins to Break

### 1999 --- The Seretech Decision

During the New York Food Riots, Seretech security forces used lethal
force while protecting a truck that had been mistaken for a food
shipment. The truck actually carried medical waste.

**220 people died.**

The Supreme Court upheld Seretech's actions, helping establish that
corporations could maintain private armed forces.

### 2000 --- The Shiawase Decision

After TerraFirst! attacked a Shiawase nuclear facility, the Supreme
Court granted major multinational corporations **extraterritoriality**.

The decision became one of the foundations of the later megacorporate
order.

### 2001 --- Secrets on Mars

NASA probes photographed anomalous structures and a skeleton on Mars.
The information was classified.

### 2004 --- Dungeness

A nuclear accident at Dungeness in England created a major irradiated
zone.

### 2005 --- Nuclear Retaliation & the New York Earthquake

Israel retaliated against Libya with nuclear weapons.

On August 12, a major earthquake struck New York City, killing
approximately **200,000 people** and causing enormous economic damage.

### 2006 --- The Japanese Imperial State

North Korea launched missiles at Japan. The attack failed to produce the
expected destruction.

Japan subsequently conquered North Korea and declared the **Japanese
Imperial State (JIS)**.

### 2009 --- The Lone Eagle Incident

Radical Native Americans seized a missile silo in Montana and launched a
Lone Eagle ICBM at Russia.

The incident triggered severe anti-Native American backlash, including
so-called re-education camps. Texas subsequently granted corporate
security forces extremely broad authority, helping establish Lone Star's
later dominance.

### 2010 --- VITAS

**Virally Induced Toxic Allergy Syndrome (VITAS)** emerged in India and
spread worldwide.

The first pandemic killed approximately **one quarter of the world's
population**.

# The Awakening --- 2011

## December 24, 2011

Magic returned to the world.

The event became known as **the Awakening**.

Among the most spectacular early events:

-   The great dragon **Ryumyo** appeared over Mount Fuji.
-   Native American prophet **Daniel Howling Coyote** led prisoners out
    of an internment camp through a mysterious supernatural event.
-   Mana storms devastated Australia.
-   Irish forests experienced supernatural growth.
-   Stone circles appeared across the British Isles.
-   Children were born with elven and dwarven characteristics through
    **Unexplained Genetic Expression (UGE)**.

The Sixth World had begun.

### 2012

The great dragon **Dunkelzahn** publicly appeared at Cherry Creek Lake
near Denver and gave a lengthy interview explaining magic as a cyclical
natural force.

The great dragon **Lofwyr** appeared in Germany and began building the
power base that would become associated with Saeder-Krupp.

# The Ghost Dance War --- 2014--2018

This period is one of the most important missing pieces in the original
document.

## 2014 --- The Ghost Dance War Begins

The Native American Nations (NAN), led by **Daniel Howling Coyote** and
other Native leaders, entered open conflict with the United States and
Canada.

The war began largely as a guerrilla conflict but increasingly
incorporated large-scale magical operations.

## 2015 --- The War Escalates

Native forces, including the Salish War Council, achieved important
military successes in the Pacific Northwest.

The conflict increasingly demonstrated that awakened magic could alter
the outcome of conventional warfare.

Mexico also underwent a major political transformation as the Azatlán
Party came to power, laying the groundwork for modern **Aztlan**.

## 2016 --- The Resolution Act

The U.S. government adopted the **Resolution Act of 2016**, authorizing
a genocidal campaign against Native American tribes.

The legislation effectively destroyed the remaining possibility of a
peaceful settlement.

Howling Coyote's response was to employ magic on an unprecedented scale.

## 2017 --- The Great Ghost Dance

Howling Coyote and Native shamans began the **Great Ghost Dance**, a
continent-spanning magical ritual.

The ritual disrupted military bases, supply dumps, communications,
weather patterns, and other elements of the U.S. war effort.

### August 17, 2017 --- The Great Ghost Dance

At approximately **10:32 a.m. Pacific time**, the ritual reached its
catastrophic climax.

Four major volcanoes erupted simultaneously:

-   **Mount Hood**
-   **Mount Rainier**
-   **Mount St. Helens**
-   **Mount Adams**

Freak weather and supernatural disturbances followed.

The event demonstrated beyond serious dispute that magic could operate
on a massive strategic scale. The Ghost Dance War effectively became the
first modern conflict in which magic decisively changed the course of a
major war.

> **Important:** The Great Ghost Dance did **not** create a new mountain
> range. It caused simultaneous catastrophic eruptions in the Cascade
> volcanic region and triggered enormous secondary environmental and
> magical effects.

## 2018 --- Treaty of Denver

After months of negotiations, the **Native American Nations, the United
States, Canada, and Aztlan** signed the **Treaty of Denver on April 25,
2018**.

The treaty:

-   Recognized NAN sovereignty.
-   Ceded enormous territories in western North America to the NAN.
-   Established the **Front Range Free Zone** around Denver.
-   Divided Denver among several signatories.
-   Established the political geography that would eventually surround
    the UCAS Seattle Metroplex.

Seattle remained a UCAS enclave surrounded by NAN territory, with treaty
guarantees governing its access through Native-controlled territory and
waterways.

The Treaty of Denver is one of the foundational documents of the Sixth
World.

# The Awakened World Takes Shape --- 2019--2028

### 2019 --- Cyberlimbs

The first successful cyberlimb implantation reaches practical public
use. Cybernetic replacement technology begins the long transition toward
the ubiquitous cyberware culture of 2059.

### 2020

Ares expands its space ambitions and other major political and magical
transformations continue worldwide.

### 2021 --- Goblinization

On April 30, **Goblinization** occurs.

Approximately **10% of humanity** transforms into orks and trolls.

The event produces mass panic, violence, internment, and severe
human-rights abuses.

The social consequences permanently reshape the Sixth World.

### 2022 --- VITAS 2 and Race Riots

A second major VITAS outbreak, **VITAS 2**, kills approximately another
**10% of the world's population**.

Race riots erupt around the world.

In Los Angeles, gang warfare becomes severe enough that the authorities
wall off the worst slums. The area eventually becomes known as **El
Infierno**.

The **ORO Corporation** is renamed **Aztechnology Corporation**.

### 2023 --- The Corporate Order

The **Corporate Court** is established in orbit and becomes the
principal institution governing the major extraterritorial
megacorporations.

Humanis Policlub emerges as a major human-supremacist organization.

Ares Industries remains known by that name at this point.

### 2024 --- Ares Macrotechnology

**Ares Industries is renamed Ares Macrotechnology.**

This is an important correction: the original document placed the name
change in 2023.

### 2025 --- Seattle Police Crisis

Seattle's police force goes on strike.

The city government dismisses the police and contracts **Lone Star
Security Services** to provide law enforcement. This becomes a defining
feature of Seattle's corporate-security culture.

### 2026--2027

Magic, cybertechnology, corporate power, and the Matrix continue
developing rapidly.

Los Angeles pioneers cold-fusion applications to address its severe
water shortage.

### 2028 --- The Big One in Los Angeles

A major earthquake strikes Los Angeles.

**LAX is destroyed**, and the city enters another phase of
reconstruction and instability.

The earthquake is one of the major steps toward the radically altered
Los Angeles of 2059.

# The Crash of '29 and the New World --- 2029--2039

## 2029 --- The Crash

The **Crash Virus** attacks global computer systems.

The virus causes massive data loss, hardware destruction, economic
disruption, and widespread social instability.

The old Internet is transformed into the dangerous Matrix environment of
the Sixth World.

**Echo Mirage** ultimately defeats the Crash Virus.

The disaster also creates opportunities for corporate consolidation.
Inazo Aneki uses the collapse of Keruba International to acquire it and
build **Renraku** into a major corporate power.

## 2030 --- The UCAS

The United States and Canada reorganize into the **United Canadian and
American States (UCAS)**.

## 2031 --- The First EuroWar

Russia invades Poland, beginning the First EuroWar.

The Echo Mirage legacy and the emerging cyberwarfare industry continue
to reshape global security.

## 2033 --- The Nanosecond Buyout

**Damien Knight** executes the legendary computerized stock maneuver
known as the **Nanosecond Buyout**.

In roughly a minute, Knight acquires a controlling stake sufficient to
propel him to the head of **Ares Macrotechnology**.

## 2034

Lucien Cross establishes **Cross Matrix Technologies**, the company that
eventually becomes Cross Applied Technologies.

## 2035 --- Tír Tairngire

The NAN's Denver-area political structure is reorganized into the
territories of the **Pueblo Corporate Council, Sioux Nation, and Ute
Nation**.

Lugh Surehand declares the independence of **Tír Tairngire**.

Most NAN nations recognize its sovereignty, although the Salish-Shidhe
Council does not initially do so.

## 2036 --- California Leaves the UCAS

California secedes from the UCAS and becomes the **California Free
State**.

Aztlan invades southern California and captures San Diego.

Tír Tairngire invades northern California as far south as Redding.

The Japanese Imperial State subsequently establishes a military presence
in San Francisco in support of Japanese interests.

## 2038

Renraku continues consolidating its corporate position.

The major megacorporations increasingly dominate North American economic
and political life.

## 2039 --- The Night of Rage

The **Night of Rage** begins with metahuman protests and rapidly becomes
worldwide racial violence.

Thousands of metahumans are killed.

In Seattle, persecution contributes directly to the growth of the **Ork
Underground**, which becomes a major refuge and community beneath the
city.

# The Corporate Era --- 2040--2049

## 2040 --- Renraku Arcology Construction Begins

Renraku begins construction of its enormous Seattle arcology, the
**SCIRE** --- the Self-Contained Industrial-Residential Environment.

Construction is completed in 2054.

## 2042 --- Yamatetsu Becomes AAA

**Yamatetsu** achieves AAA status and receives a seat on the Corporate
Court.

Magical research and university rivalries continue to expand, including
major activity in Los Angeles.

## 2045 --- The Green Tide

An offshore reactor accident sends a massive radioactive tidal wave into
Los Angeles.

The event becomes known as the **Green Tide**.

The initial flooding and aftermath kill thousands and devastate El
Infierno.

The authorities and corporations provide little meaningful assistance to
the trapped population.

## 2046 --- The Lost Election and Battle of El Infierno

A hacker group known as **Magestone** erases the results of California's
gubernatorial election.

The event becomes known as the **Lost Election**.

The California government blames the criminal element in Los Angeles and
sends government troops and corporate mercenaries into El Infierno.

The resulting battle becomes a massacre.

Approximately **20,000 civilians are killed over the course of the
fighting**.

Government forces ultimately withdraw.

California then **disowns Los Angeles and declares it a Free City**.

This is the true political origin of the 2059 Free City.

## 2048 --- Operation Reciprocity

Aztechnology nationalizes foreign capital in Aztlan.

The Corporate Court authorizes **Operation Reciprocity**, a major
corporate response.

The subsequent settlement establishes the Panama Canal as a
pan-corporate zone.

## 2049

The corporate order continues consolidating, while Los Angeles remains
outside California Free State authority.

# Shadowrunning --- 2050--2058

## 2050 --- The Shadowrun Era Begins

The classic Shadowrun campaign era begins.

Shadowrunning becomes an established part of the Sixth World's deniable
economy.

## 2053

Shadowrun Second Edition's campaign era begins.

## 2055 --- Bug City

Ares and other forces become involved in the **Chicago Containment
Zone** after the Universal Brotherhood is exposed as an insect-spirit
front.

Chicago becomes one of the defining horrors of the era.

Seattle also sees the Universal Brotherhood's **Project Hope** camps
operating in the Redmond Barrens before the organization is exposed.

## 2056

Dunkelzahn is formally recognized as a UCAS citizen.

The political movement surrounding his eventual presidential campaign
begins to gather momentum.

## 2057 --- The Year of the Dragon

**Dunkelzahn wins the UCAS presidential election.**

On August 9, he is assassinated shortly after taking office.

His death produces an enormous political and corporate shock.

His will is read publicly on August 15.

The **Draco Foundation** is established to administer the provisions of
the will.

## 2058 --- Seattle Mob War

Don James "The Hammer" O'Malley is assassinated by **Chimera**, in a
killing arranged by **Maurice "The Butcher" Bigio**.

The resulting struggle becomes the **Seattle Mafia Mob War**.

Rowena O'Malley takes control of the Finnigan Family.

The Yakuza, Mafia, Triads, and other criminal organizations exploit the
resulting instability.

# The Renraku Arcology Shutdown

## December 19, 2059

The Renraku Arcology's automated security systems suddenly seal the
building.

More than **100,000 people** are trapped inside, including residents,
workers, and visitors.

The UCAS, Seattle authorities, and Renraku establish a military
perimeter.

### What a 2059 character knows

The official explanation is initially a catastrophic system malfunction.

Rumors spread that something intelligent has taken control of the
Arcology's Matrix systems.

The existence of a true artificial intelligence is not ordinary public
knowledge at the beginning of the shutdown.

### What later history reveals

Later sources identify the rogue AI as **Deus**, an intelligence created
from Renraku's Arcology Expert Program and code derived from the AI
Morgan/Megaera.

For a 2059 player-lore document, this later revelation should **not** be
presented as something every citizen already knows.

# Magic, Metahumans, Technology & the Matrix

## Magic

Magic returned in 2011.

By 2059 it is an established fact of life.

The world contains:

-   Spellcasters
-   Shamans
-   Adepts
-   Spirits
-   Magical traditions
-   Thaumaturgical research
-   Wild magic zones
-   Ancient magical organizations
-   Dragons
-   Awakened creatures

The Great Ghost Dance proved that magic can operate on a strategic,
continental scale.

## Metahumans

The major recognized metatypes include:

-   **Humans**
-   **Elves**
-   **Dwarfs**
-   **Orks**
-   **Trolls**

Metahumans are established populations, but discrimination remains
widespread.

## Cyberware

Cybernetic technology is advanced enough that complete limb replacement
is possible and commercially established.

High-end cyberware is sophisticated and expensive.

Cheap cyberware can be unreliable, dangerous, or socially stigmatizing.

## The Matrix

The Matrix is a global cyberspace environment accessed through
**cyberdecks**.

A decker can use it for:

-   Information
-   Communication
-   Entertainment
-   Corporate espionage
-   Criminal operations
-   Data theft

Corporate hosts are protected by **Intrusion Countermeasures (IC)**.

Some security systems use **Black IC**, capable of severe or lethal
biofeedback.

# Life in 2059

## The SINless

A large part of society exists without a **Standard Identification
Number (SIN)**.

Without a legitimate identity, people can struggle to obtain:

-   Legal employment
-   Legitimate housing
-   Corporate services
-   Official access to institutions

SINless communities survive through informal economies and underground
networks.

## Corporate Feudalism

Megacorporations provide employees with much of what they need:

-   Housing
-   Food
-   Entertainment
-   Employment
-   Security

In exchange, they expect loyalty.

A corporate identity can function almost like citizenship.

## Wealth

The gap between the corporate elite and the poor is enormous.

Corporate enclaves can be clean, secure, and luxurious.

Outside them, neighborhoods may be dangerous, polluted, or controlled by
gangs.

## Survival

Violence is common.

Trust is valuable.

Reputation matters.

Knowing neighborhood etiquette can be the difference between being
welcomed and becoming a target.

# Shadowrunners

Shadowrunners are freelance operatives who perform jobs that
corporations, governments, syndicates, and other powerful organizations
cannot publicly acknowledge.

A runner might be hired to:

-   Steal data
-   Infiltrate a facility
-   Extract a person
-   Sabotage a competitor
-   Recover an object
-   Provide deniable muscle
-   Investigate a problem
-   Perform corporate espionage

## The Shadowrunner's Code

### Professionalism

A runner is a professional providing a service.

### Reputation

A runner's reputation determines who will work with them, how much they
get paid, and how much people trust them.

### It's Business

Corporate powers often regard shadowrunning as a cost of doing business.

### Personal Honor

Many runners develop personal rules, such as never betraying a client or
never harming bystanders.

## The Mr. Johnson Principle

A runner rarely deals directly with "the corporation."

Instead, they deal with:

-   A manager
-   A department
-   A subsidiary
-   A task force
-   A particular executive
-   A deniable budget

This gives everyone room to deny responsibility.

# The Great Powers

## Ares Macrotechnology

**Base:** Detroit\
**Leader:** Damien Knight\
**Known for:** Weapons, automobiles, aerospace, military industry

Ares presents itself as an American military-industrial powerhouse.

Its history includes NASA, the Nanosecond Buyout, Knight Errant, Bug
City, and the 2059 General Motors acquisition.

> **Corporate attitude:** "We make the world safe."

## Aztechnology

**Base:** Aztlan / Tenochtitlán\
**Known for:** Consumer goods, chemistry, magic, corporate power

Aztechnology grew from the ORO Corporation and became deeply integrated
with Aztlan.

Its public image is friendly and familial.

Behind that image is a secretive organization associated with powerful
magic and blood sacrifice.

> **Corporate attitude:** "We're a family."

## Mitsuhama Computer Technologies --- MCT

**Base:** Japan\
**Known for:** Technology, computing, brutal security

MCT is rigidly hierarchical and famous for the **Zero Zone** concept.

## Renraku Computer Systems

**Base:** Japan\
**Known for:** Computing, corporate infrastructure, Red Samurai

Renraku is a traditional Japanese megacorporation with a strong sense of
corporate loyalty.

Its security forces include the feared **Red Samurai**.

The Arcology Shutdown has turned Renraku into a corporation in crisis.

## Shiawase Corporation

**Base:** Japan\
**Known for:** Energy, nuclear power, electronics

Shiawase was central to the legal foundation of corporate
extraterritoriality.

## Saeder-Krupp

**Leader:** Lofwyr\
**Status:** AAA megacorporation

Saeder-Krupp is associated with the great dragon **Lofwyr**, making it
one of the most formidable powers in the world.

## Yamatetsu

Yamatetsu is strongly associated with biotechnology and metahuman
acceptance.

Its leadership crisis in 2059 follows the death of Tadamako Shibanokuji.

His son **Yuri Shibanokuji**, an ork, inherits control.

## Wuxing

Wuxing rises to **AAA status in 2059**.

Its ascent is part of the redistribution of corporate power following
the collapse of older giants.

## Novatech

Novatech emerges in 2059 from the Villiers faction of Fuchi.

It receives AAA status and the Corporate Court position associated with
the former Fuchi/JRJ corporate structure.

# Governments & Nations

## United Canadian and American States --- UCAS

The UCAS is the successor state formed from much of the former United
States and Canada.

Seattle is a UCAS enclave in the Pacific Northwest.

## Native American Nations

The NAN is the political legacy of the Ghost Dance War and Treaty of
Denver.

The western political landscape around Seattle exists because of the
territories ceded in 2018.

## Salish-Shidhe Council --- SSC

The **Salish-Shidhe Council** surrounds the Seattle Metroplex.

Seattle remains part of the UCAS, but is geographically enclosed by the
SSC and depends on treaty guarantees for access through surrounding
territory and waterways.

This relationship is essential to understanding Seattle.

## California Free State --- CalFree

The California Free State governs what remains of California outside
territories controlled by other powers.

Its relationship with Los Angeles is defined by abandonment.

After the 2046 Battle of El Infierno, CalFree officially disowned Los
Angeles and declared it a Free City.

## Pueblo Corporate Council --- PCC

The PCC is both a political territory and a major corporate-political
power.

It controls large areas of the American Southwest.

Its relationship with Los Angeles is primarily a **future annexation
issue in 2059**.

The annexation occurs in **2061**, not before.

## Aztlan

Aztlan is the territory controlled by Aztechnology.

It combines corporate power, nationalism, and a powerful magical
tradition.

## Japan / Japanese Imperial State

Japan transformed dramatically after 2006.

The Japanese Imperial State emerged after Japan conquered North Korea.

Japanese megacorporations remain among the most powerful forces on
Earth.

## Tír na nÓg

Tír na nÓg is the elven nation occupying Ireland.

It is strongly associated with magical and elven culture.

# Dragons & Ancient Powers

Dragons are not merely large magical creatures.

They are ancient beings with intelligence, resources, political
influence, and agendas operating on a scale of centuries.

## Dunkelzahn

A great dragon who became a major political and cultural figure.

He appeared publicly in 2012, later ran for UCAS president, won the 2057
election, and was assassinated shortly afterward.

His will established the **Draco Foundation**.

## Lofwyr

A great dragon associated with **Saeder-Krupp**.

He represents one of the clearest examples of a dragon exercising direct
economic and corporate power.

## Ryumyo

A great dragon who appeared over Mount Fuji during the Awakening.

Elven societies have established independent political territories,

# Seattle

Seattle is one of the major centers of shadowrunning in the Sixth World.

It is positioned at the intersection of:

-   UCAS interests
-   Salish-Shidhe territory
-   Megacorporate territory
-   Criminal syndicates
-   The Matrix
-   Pacific trade
-   Metahuman communities

## Why Seattle Matters

Seattle is a place where competing powers constantly collide.

A corporation may own one building.

Another corporation may own the building across the street.

A gang may control the neighborhood around them.

The government may technically control the city.

And the shadows operate beneath all of them.

## The Ork Underground

A sprawling subterranean community and refuge beneath Seattle.

## Renraku Arcology

The **SCIRE** is one of the world's great corporate buildings.

Construction began in 2040 and was completed in 2054.

It is a self-contained corporate city, with tens of thousands of
permanent residents and the ability to accommodate well over 100,000
people during the 2059 shutdown.

On December 19, 2059, its security systems seal the building.

# Los Angeles --- The City of Fallen Angels

## LA in 2059

Los Angeles is a huge, earthquake-damaged coastal sprawl.

It is officially a **Free City**.

CalFree abandoned the city after the 2046 Battle of El Infierno.

The **Pueblo Corporate Council is positioning itself for eventual
annexation**, but the actual annexation does not occur until **2061**.

## Important 2059 distinction: Horizon

The later **Horizon Group** is anachronistic in a 2059 campaign.

The Horizon Project/think-tank material belongs to the period
immediately preceding Horizon's later corporate rise, but the **Horizon
Group itself does not exist as the 2059 AAA megacorp portrayed in later
sourcebooks**.

Therefore this document does not treat Horizon as LA's dominant
corporation in 2059.

# The Los Angeles Districts

## 1. The Corporate Core

### Hollywood • Beverly Hills • Studio City

**Character:** Wealth, media, celebrity, corporate power.

The central LA districts remain heavily shaped by entertainment,
simsense, corporate enclaves, and wealthy residential zones.

Important corporate and entertainment interests include:

-   Amalgamated Studios
-   Truman Technologies
-   Brilliant Genesis Studios
-   Other corporate media and simsense interests

**Danger:** Low on the surface; potentially lethal underneath.

## 2. El Infierno

**Character:** Walled warzone and legacy of the city's racial and
economic collapse.

El Infierno encompasses former:

-   Gardena
-   Hawthorne
-   Compton

The area originated during the 2022 race riots and was progressively
walled and isolated during the following years.

By the late 2030s it had become effectively a prison district, with
checkpoints, curfews, automated weapons, and severe deprivation.

### The Green Tide

In 2045, the offshore reactor disaster known as the **Green Tide**
flooded El Infierno with radioactive toxic sludge.

Thousands died.

The outside government and corporations largely failed to provide
meaningful aid.

### The 2046 Massacre

The Lost Election triggered the government's assault on El Infierno.

Approximately **20,000 civilians were killed during the resulting
battle**.

### Burning Angels

The **Burning Angels** are a major Los Angeles gang.

Their leader is **Sage**, a mystic adept and honorary member of the
Anasazi.

Other notable members include:

-   **Fuego** --- troll, second-in-command
-   **Red Brome** --- mage

Their initiation traditions include violent combat and an Anasazi-linked
desert pilgrimage for elite members.

**2059 caution:** later sourcebooks expand the gang's influence
considerably. Treat the detailed later gang-war relationships as future
developments unless your campaign deliberately moves them backward.

**Danger:** Extreme.

## 3. The Sunken Zones

-   Toxic water
-   Submerged structures

-   Salvage sites
-   Water-gang territory

Treasure hunters search submerged buildings for:

-   Pre-Awakening artifacts
-   Data cores
-   Forgotten technology
-   Corporate property

**Danger:** High.

# The Political Landscape of Los Angeles

## California Free State

LA was part of the California Free State until 2046.

After the Battle of El Infierno, CalFree officially abandoned the city
and declared it a Free City.

CalFree still retains intelligence, corporate, and political interests
in the region.

## Pueblo Corporate Council

The PCC has been positioning itself around Los Angeles.

In 2059, it is already a major external power.

**The actual annexation occurs in 2061.**

## Aztechnology

Aztechnology is a major regional power with economic, magical, and
intelligence interests.

Its later direct conflict with Horizon should not be projected backward
into 2059.

# The Player's View of the World

The Sixth World is not simply a world where technology and magic
coexist.

It is a world where **power has become fragmented**.

A corporation can be stronger than a government.

A dragon can control a corporation.

A gang can control a neighborhood.

A hacker can threaten a megacorp from a basement.

A mage can manipulate forces that science cannot explain.

And a shadowrunner can walk between all of them.

The most important question is rarely:

> **"Who is right?"**

It is:

> **"Who benefits?"**
