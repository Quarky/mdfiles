# Street Scenes — Volume 2

## 11. The Coffee Argument

A crowded cafe has one empty table.

An Azorius advocate and Boros sergeant are already sharing it because the rest are full. They are arguing over a case from last week.

Neither is on duty. Both are regular customers. The server keeps refilling their cups because this argument has been happening for years.

The PCs can learn more about the city's law from ten minutes of overheard disagreement than from a lecture.

## 12. The Rent Lift

The building's lift only stops at floors whose rent is current.

The landlord calls this an automated access-control feature. Tenants call it extortion because elderly residents cannot climb fifteen flights.

The enchantment is Izzet-made, the lease is Orzhov-written, and the Azorius complaint hearing is next month.

Someone has begun sabotaging the lift.

## 13. The Temporary Bridge

An Izzet crew installs a metal footbridge after a collapse.

Residents love it because it cuts twenty minutes off the walk.

Three days later, Selesnyan gardeners discover the bridge blocks sunlight to a courtyard greenhouse.

Nobody wants the old route back.

## 14. The Veteran's Fine

A Boros veteran has accumulated Azorius fines for feeding stray rocs near a restricted landing zone.

The veteran insists the animals recognize old military calls and are harmless.

They are not harmless.

One of them has stolen an Orzhov messenger case.

## 15. The Mushroom Festival

A guildless neighborhood holds an annual street festival celebrating cheap Golgari food that kept residents alive during an old shortage.

This year an Orzhov sponsor offers to pay for decorations if its emblem appears over the main gate.

Half the organizers want the money. The other half say the festival exists because institutions failed them.

## 16. The Quiet Rakdos Performer

A famous pain artist comes into a clinic with a routine injury and is terrified of needles.

The Simic medic recognizes them and tries not to laugh.

A group of fans gathers outside after someone spots the performer's costume bag.

The patient's public persona becomes a medical privacy problem.

## 17. The Gruul Mason

A huge Gruul woman is repairing an old retaining wall.

She says her clan built the original dry-stone foundation generations ago.

The current property owner wants her arrested for trespass.

An Izzet engineer quietly admits her repair technique is better suited to the old structure than modern mortar.

## 18. The Wrong Funeral

Two processions meet at an intersection.

Orzhov attendants insist theirs has contractual right of passage. Selesnyan mourners refuse to split their group. A Golgari corpse cart is trapped behind both and the driver becomes increasingly sarcastic.

The Boros patrol assigned to traffic control is one rookie with a whistle.

## 19. The Student Demonstration

Simic and Izzet students are holding competing demonstrations across the same plaza.

One has built a self-cooling plant wall. The other has built a heat-exchange device that makes the wall unnecessary.

The projects begin interfering magically.

Their professors are pretending not to enjoy this.

## 20. The Empty Shop

A beloved neighborhood shop closes overnight.

No crime. No disappearance.

The owner accepted an excellent Orzhov buyout and moved to a better district.

Residents feel betrayed anyway.

A Dimir rumor claims coercion because people prefer a villain to the idea that someone simply chose to leave.
