# Night and Undercity Scenes

## 1. Shift Change Below

At midnight, a maintenance hatch opens and Golgari workers climb up carrying empty food crates while guildless sanitation workers descend with full waste carts.

For ten minutes, the alley becomes a border crossing between two versions of the city.

A drunk insults the Golgari workers. One sanitation worker tells him to shut up because these are the people who keep his block from drowning in refuse.

## 2. Rain in the Sunken Street

Soft rain falls through an opening three stories above.

The street used to be open sky before newer construction surrounded it. Dim lamps reflect in standing water. A courier waits beneath an arch with no visible door.

The party hears footsteps on an upper level but cannot tell whether they are overhead or across the buried street.

## 3. The Fungal Wake

A Golgari family holds a wake beside luminous fungus beds.

Food is served. Stories are funny. The body is present and will later be returned to the local cycle according to custom.

An Orzhov representative arrives with documents claiming funerary rights under an old debt.

The family does not dispute the signature. They dispute whether the dead can still owe.

## 4. The Last Lift

The final public lift of the night fails between levels.

Inside: a Boros soldier off duty, a Rakdos performer carrying props, an Orzhov clerk, a Selesnyan grandmother, two students, and a cage containing something small from a Simic lab.

The failure itself is ordinary.

The cage is not.

## 5. The Illegal Garden

A patch of pale flowers grows beneath a bridge where no direct sunlight reaches.

Selesnyan gardeners tend it quietly at night because the property owner has ordered the space cleared.

The flowers absorb runoff from an Izzet conduit and are the reason nearby residents stopped getting sick.

The owner has already leased the space for storage.

## 6. The Closed Tunnel

Azorius seal. Boros warning ribbon. Golgari chalk marks. Gruul paint. Dimir symbol that may be fake.

Everyone has marked the same tunnel for a different reason.

The tunnel leads to an older transit platform recently uncovered by a collapse.

Nothing inside is immediately hostile. That is what makes everyone nervous.

## 7. The Smelting Quarter After Show

The performance is over. The real city begins.

Stagehands coil chains. Injured performers drink water. Factory workers arrive for night shift. Vendors count coins. Boros watches from the edge without intervening.

A star performer privately asks the PCs to find a missing stagehand because calling Boros would damage the troupe's reputation.

## 8. The Water Below Zonot Seven

A Simic guide takes the party beneath public areas of Zameck.

Street noise fades. Air becomes damp. Pumps thrum. The water below is black until bioluminescent organisms move and reveal impossible depth.

The guide stops talking like a scientist for the first time and simply says, "This was here before the streets."
