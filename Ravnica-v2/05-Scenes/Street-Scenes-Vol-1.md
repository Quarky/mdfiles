# Street Scenes — Volume 1

These are not encounters waiting for combat. They are **living slices of Ravnica** with sensory detail, competing needs, and consequences.

## 1. The Heat Line

**Opening:** Warm vapor rolls from a trench cut across the street. Copper piping glows faintly blue beneath wet cobbles. Thirty residents in coats crowd behind an Azorius barrier while an Izzet technician argues with an arrester.

**Sound:** hissing steam, angry residents, metal tools, a child coughing.

**Situation:** The line feeding three apartment blocks is failing. Izzet can restore heat quickly by bypassing a safety regulator. Azorius has prohibited that model after a previous explosion.

**People:**  
- Senna Vur, Izzet technician: exhausted, competent, wants twenty uninterrupted minutes.  
- Officer Parel, Azorius: has seen the earlier explosion report and refuses to be the official who ignores it.  
- Old Mikk, tenant: his spouse is ill upstairs.

**If PCs help:** choose speed, legality, evacuation, or a clever alternative. Any success makes one institution remember them.

## 2. The Bread Queue

**Opening:** Rain has turned the market awning into a drum. The line for Golgari ration bread bends around a shuttered bakery.

**Smell:** wet wool, mushroom broth, sour yeast.

**Situation:** A surface warehouse fire destroyed normal supplies. Golgari sellers doubled deliveries, but an Orzhov creditor has seized the bakery ovens over unpaid debt.

A Boros patrol is present because tempers are rising. The seizure is legal.

**Truth:** the bakery owner gave away food during the fire instead of making loan payments.

**Pressure:** feed people now, respect property, or challenge the contract.

## 3. The Moving Stage

A giant carries half a Rakdos stage down an avenue while stagehands run beside it tightening cables.

The troupe has a permit for the next square, but a damaged bridge forces a detour through a Selesnyan neighborhood during evening meal.

The performers are rude but not initially violent. Residents are furious because the last unscheduled show damaged a community garden.

The interesting solution is logistical and social: alternate route, compensation, temporary dismantling, or turning the move itself into a show.

## 4. The Wrong Door

A Dimir courier knocks on the PCs' door, realizes immediately it is the wrong apartment, apologizes, and leaves.

Ten minutes later, an Azorius investigator asks whether anyone delivered a sealed packet.

The courier genuinely made a mistake. The packet was intended for the identical apartment number on the old lower street beneath the building.

Now the PCs know a lower street exists.

## 5. The Fallen Centaur

A centaur slips when a freight lift jerks unexpectedly. The injury blocks a narrow market lane.

A Simic medic begins treatment. Boros clears space. Merchants complain because the blockage is destroying the day's trade.

The lift operator insists the machine was serviced yesterday.

An Izzet tag proves that is true.

The cause is not sabotage but a building owner who overloaded the lift to avoid paying for a second freight run.

## 6. The Eviction Choir

Orzhov enforcers arrive to remove a family from a tenement. Selesnyan neighbors stand in the stairwell singing quietly.

Nobody attacks.

The song makes the enforcers hesitate because forcing passage will be ugly and public.

Azorius confirms the eviction order is valid. A Rakdos performer has begun attracting a crowd outside.

The party can solve the family, the debt, the crowd, or the law—but probably not all four at once.

## 7. The Garden Under the Road

Survey marks appear through a community garden. Izzet plans show a new utility route. Selesnya says the mature trees stabilize the block's old foundation.

Both are correct: the road needs the line and the roots are structurally important.

Gruul graffiti appears overnight: **LET IT FALL.**

A resident admits they invited the Gruul to scare the planners.

## 8. The Unclaimed Body

A dead man is found beneath a bridge.

Boros believes he was murdered. Orzhov claims the body under a funeral contract. Golgari says the body was left in undercity territory and has already begun reclamation rites. Azorius seals the scene.

The dead man carried three guild insignia, none registered to his name.

Dimir has not arrived, which is itself suspicious.

## 9. The Beautiful Monster

A Simic transport tank cracks in a plaza. A juvenile krasis escapes.

It is frightened, not hostile.

Children love it. Merchants do not. Boros wants the square cleared. Simic wants the creature alive and uninjured. A Rakdos scout offers the handlers a fortune to rent it for a show later.

The creature keeps returning to a fountain because the water calms it.

## 10. The Permit Nobody Read

A street festival has been operating under the same Azorius permit for twenty-three years.

A new clerk notices that the original text accidentally authorizes "unrestricted public assembly until sunset on the final day of Ravnica."

Nobody knows what that sentence legally means.

Three political groups immediately decide it means exactly what they need.
