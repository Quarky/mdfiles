# Dialogue, Voice and Social Pressure

Guild voice should emerge from **what a person notices and what they consider legitimate**, not from catchphrases.

## Azorius

Notices sequence, authority, precedent, definitions, records, exceptions.

- "Who authorized this?"
- "What exactly happened first?"
- "I can make an exception only if I can explain why it remains an exception."

Pressure: make you wait, document you, narrow choices to permitted options.

## Boros

Notices harm, courage, threat, responsibility, who needs protection.

- "Who is still inside?"
- "Tell me what stops the next hit."
- "I will answer for the order. Move."

Pressure: urgency, command presence, moral judgment.

## Dimir

Notices information asymmetry, who knows whom, what detail does not fit.

- "Who else heard that?"
- "You are asking the wrong question."
- "I can prove the fact. The source is not part of the offer."

Pressure: uncertainty, selective truth, social leverage.

## Golgari

Notices waste, cycles, class hypocrisy, what gets discarded.

- "The surface calls it trash after taking what it wants."
- "Nothing here is unused."
- "You want this cleaned. Where do you imagine clean goes?"

Pressure: dependence, uncomfortable material reality, patience.

## Gruul

Notices territory, coercion, construction, ownership claims, weakness disguised as law.

- "Your wall is new. My anger is not."
- "Show me the place before the road."
- "You call it order because you stand on top of it."

Pressure: physical presence, refusal of institutional assumptions.

## Izzet

Notices mechanism, anomaly, possibility, data, failure mode.

- "That should not be happening."
- "Don't touch it unless you want to improve the experiment."
- "We can make it safe or we can make it fast."

Pressure: technical authority, time urgency, curiosity.

## Orzhov

Notices obligations, leverage, hierarchy, assets, who owes whom.

- "Nothing is free. Sometimes the price is merely hidden."
- "You may refuse."
- "The debt existed before I entered the room."

Pressure: consequences that appear voluntary.

## Rakdos

Notices hypocrisy, audience mood, fear, status, comic weakness.

- "Say that again, but louder. The balcony didn't hear you lie."
- "You came to stop the show and became the best part."
- "Everyone likes rules until the rules are about them."

Pressure: public embarrassment, crowd energy, escalation through spectacle.

## Selesnya

Notices relationships, isolation, communal consequences, shared need.

- "Who else is affected?"
- "You do not have to carry this alone."
- "Your choice is yours. Its consequences belong to all of us."

Pressure: belonging, reciprocity, moral community.

## Simic

Notices adaptation, systems, symptoms, variables, unintended effects.

- "What changed after exposure?"
- "Stable is not the same as healthy."
- "The body has already made a decision. We are deciding whether to help it."

Pressure: expertise over intimate bodily questions.

## Cross-guild conversation trick

When two guild NPCs argue, have them use the **same word differently**.

"Safety" means procedure to Azorius, immediate protection to Boros, controlled experiment to Izzet, adaptive resilience to Simic.

"Freedom" means independence to Gruul, appetite to Rakdos, freedom from exploitation to some Boros, freedom from chaos to an Azorius jurist.

That creates ideological texture without speeches.
