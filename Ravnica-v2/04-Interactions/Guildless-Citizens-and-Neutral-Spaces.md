# Guildless Citizens and Neutral Spaces

The guildless are not the absence of a faction. They are the majority of ordinary social life that remains after ten institutions stop pretending they own everything.

## Guildless competence

A guildless building superintendent may understand Azorius notices, Izzet plumbing, Orzhov leases, Boros patrol patterns, Golgari food delivery, and which Selesnyan healer will make a house call.

That is a kind of expertise guild specialists often underestimate.

## Why remain guildless?

- family identity;
- distrust of institutional obligation;
- independent profession;
- previous bad experience with a guild;
- mixed-guild family;
- local community stronger than guild identity;
- desire to work across guild boundaries;
- inability to qualify or gain sponsorship;
- refusal to accept ideological expectations.

## Neutral spaces

Neutrality is produced socially.

A cafe used by clerks from three guilds stays neutral because the owner ejects anyone conducting official business at the tables.

A market square stays neutral because all sides need freight to move.

A building can become neutral because the residents collectively refuse uniforms inside.

A bathhouse may ban weapons and insignia not from idealism but because fights ruin business.

## What guildless people think of guilds

Avoid a single "anti-guild" attitude.

A resident can hate Azorius bureaucracy and still want an Azorius judge when the landlord cheats them. They can distrust Golgari food and eat it every week. They can condemn Rakdos violence and know the best show in town. They can fear Simic mutation and bring a sick child to a Simic clinic.

That contradiction is normal urban dependence.

## Neighborhood power

Local influence can come from:

- building owners;
- tenant associations;
- market elders;
- gangs;
- unions;
- religious groups;
- healers;
- schools;
- veterans;
- neighborhood kitchens;
- couriers;
- families who have lived there longer than current maps.

## Running a guildless NPC

Give them a **multi-guild relationship map**.

Example: Mara Venn, bakery owner.

- Boros buys morning bread for the local garrison.
- Her sister is Selesnyan.
- Her lease is Orzhov-financed.
- Izzet heat lines keep the ovens reliable.
- A Dimir courier rents the room upstairs.
- She hates the Azorius inspector who keeps citing her back stair.
- She quietly feeds Golgari children who come up on delivery routes.

Mara is not "neutral." She is Ravnican.
