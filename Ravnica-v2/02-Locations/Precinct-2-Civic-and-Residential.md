# Precinct Two — Civic and Residential Pressure

V2 treats Precinct Two as a place where **the city is lived rather than displayed**: apartments, schools, shops, workshops, cafes, clinics, small offices, local shrines, public services, and endless arguments over shared space.

## First impression

Balconies. Signs. Laundry. Children. delivery carts. neighbors leaning out windows. Repairs happening while everyone steps around them.

The architecture is still Ravnican—old stone, later additions, bridges, towers—but the scale of attention is domestic. A blocked doorway matters more than a guildmaster's speech.

## Sensory palette

**See:** window boxes, drying clothes, patched plaster, shop awnings, legal notices, pipes disappearing through walls, communal courtyards.

**Hear:** vendors, household arguments, lessons through open windows, smithing, a baby crying, a lift bell, music practice, street repairs.

**Smell:** cooking, soap, bakery heat, damp stairwells, animal pens, cheap incense, laundry steam.

## Social fabric

Everyone knows something about everyone nearby, but not necessarily correctly. Landlords, shopkeepers, block captains, healers, building superintendents, local gang members, and retired guild employees have influence that formal institutions underestimate.

## Common disputes

- rent and repairs;
- a business expanding into shared space;
- noise from a Rakdos rehearsal;
- an Izzet service shutdown;
- a Selesnyan garden blocking planned construction;
- Boros patrol frequency;
- Azorius inspection notices;
- Orzhov debt collectors;
- rumors that a courier tenant is Dimir;
- Simic clinic waste;
- Golgari deliveries nobody wants to see unloaded.

## What the PCs can accidentally break

A heroic chase across rooftops may destroy water tanks. A fireball can ruin three families' laundry and the wooden additions to an old masonry wall. Summoned creatures block stairs. A fight in a shop changes whether that shop survives the month.

## Scene seed

A child is missing. The family blames a Rakdos troupe because a performance passed the block. A Boros patrol believes the child ran away. A Dimir courier quietly knows the child boarded a freight lift. The lift operator will talk only if someone clears his brother's Azorius fine.

The neighborhood solves problems through relationships before institutions.
