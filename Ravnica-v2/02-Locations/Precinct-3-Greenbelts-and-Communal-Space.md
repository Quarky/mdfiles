# Precinct Three — Greenbelts and Communal Space

V2 uses Precinct Three to show that Ravnica's "nature" is not untouched wilderness. It is **nature surviving, being cultivated, being defended, and sometimes reclaiming the city**.

## First impression

Stone does not vanish; it becomes entangled. Roots lift paving. Trees grow through courtyards. Old walls become terraces. Vernadi and community gardens create pockets where sound changes and shade matters.

## Sensory palette

**See:** vines on masonry, green roofs, centaurs moving comfortably through broader paths, birds and insects, white-green banners, water channels, carved benches swallowed by roots.

**Hear:** leaves, conversation, training calls, children, devotional song, hooves, distant city noise softened by vegetation.

**Smell:** wet soil, flowers, compost, cooking herbs, bark, rain, animal fur.

## Selesnyan presence

Selesnyan community spaces can be genuinely welcoming: food, healing, childcare, companionship, training, worship, and safety. The same community can apply intense social pressure toward harmony and common purpose.

Do not reveal the tension by making everyone creepy. Reveal it when the PCs ask someone to place personal desire above the group.

## Gruul friction

Green does not automatically mean Gruul approval. The Gruul can see managed gardens as domesticated nature and another claim that civilization gets to decide where wilderness belongs.

## Simic interest

The Simic see ecological systems, adaptation, and useful biological relationships. Their solutions can help preserve urban green space—or turn it into a research site residents no longer recognize.

## Development conflict

Precinct Three scenes become strong when a practical civic need is real.

A new transit line would shorten commutes for thousands but remove a mature grove. A Selesnyan vernadi refuses. Izzet engineers insist there is no safe alternate path. Azorius planners have approved the project. Gruul saboteurs threaten to destroy the whole works. Local guildless residents are split because they need both shade and transport.

Nobody needs to be secretly evil.

## Linger clock

After ten minutes: someone offers food or asks why the PCs are there.  
After an hour: they are invited to help with something communal.  
After a day: people expect reciprocity.
