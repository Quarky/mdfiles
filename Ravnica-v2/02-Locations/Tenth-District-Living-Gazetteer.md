# Tenth District Living Gazetteer

The Tenth District is not a theme park divided into ten guild zones. All ten guilds operate here, often on the same street, and the district's importance comes from **density of institutions, headquarters, trade, transit, politics, and population**.

## How to think about the six precincts

The published precincts are broad GM canvases rather than sealed boroughs. V2 uses each precinct as a strong scene lens, not a hard border. A single walk can move from polished civic stone to crowded housing, then into a green courtyard, then across a freight viaduct without the city stopping to announce a precinct change.

| Precinct | V2 scene identity | Immediate pressure |
|---|---|---|
| One | Guildpact, ceremony, administration, visitors | access, precedent, public visibility |
| Two | Dense civic/residential life | housing, commerce, services, policing |
| Three | Greenbelts, communal spaces, living city | preservation, growth, territorial stewardship |
| Four | hard edges, garrisons, rebuilding, contested space | security, displacement, militarization |
| Five | universities, laboratories, expertise, specialized institutions | research, ethics, permits, intellectual rivalry |
| Six | industry, Deadbridge, smelting, freight, undercity interface | labor, pollution, crime, decay, infrastructure |

## Crossing precincts

Do not transition with exposition. Transition through **street evidence**.

From One into Two, official stone gives way to signs, balconies, laundry, market awnings, delivery traffic, and people who cannot pause for a delegation.

From Two into Three, the sound softens before the city actually becomes green. Roots lift paving. Courtyards widen. Small shrines and community gardens appear. Birds and insects become audible over wheels.

From Five into Six, academic glass and polished apparatus give way to soot, freight cranes, hot metal, night shifts, broken masonry, and routes that descend rather than rise.

## Neutrality is negotiated

Major trade ways and shared civic spaces function because too many people rely on them for any guild to own them completely. "Neutral" means everyone has reasons not to destroy the space—not that nobody spies, recruits, arrests, bargains, or intimidates there.

## Tenth District background motion

Whenever a scene feels empty, add one of these:

- an Izzet maintenance crew arguing over a conduit while traffic flows around them;
- a Boros patrol escorting an injured prisoner to a healer;
- Azorius clerks attaching legal notices to a condemned balcony;
- Golgari porters delivering cheap food uphill;
- a Selesnyan group carrying saplings toward a new courtyard;
- an Orzhov agent inspecting collateral;
- Rakdos stagehands measuring a plaza for that night's rig;
- Simic technicians transporting a sealed tank that moves from inside;
- Gruul graffiti painted over a redevelopment notice;
- a Dimir courier who looks completely ordinary and is probably doing completely ordinary courier work.

The district feels alive when **guild business overlaps ordinary business in the same frame**.
