# Canonical Named Locations — Deep Atlas

This atlas prioritizes **officially named locations** from *Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica* and the official Planeswalker's Guides. Any extension beyond explicit canon is marked **table synthesis**.

# City-spanning geography

## Transguild Promenade

**Canon:** a major avenue running through the core districts, lined with markets, parks, and colonnades, used for freight and public processions. It has special neutral importance.

**At the table:** use it as a cross-section of the plane. One block can hold guildless vendors, Orzhov offices, Boros traffic control, Azorius notices, Izzet access panels, Selesnyan plantings, Dimir couriers, and Rakdos advertising.

**Table synthesis:** sheltering colonnades collect food stalls and copyists; freight pull-offs create constant conflict between carts and pedestrians; political posters accumulate over old legal notices.

## Tenth District

**Canon:** one of the city's core districts and a dense center of guild activity with six precincts and major guild headquarters.

**At the table:** never treat it as one guild's territory. Its identity is **collision**.

# Azorius and civic sites

## New Prahv

**Canon:** rebuilt Azorius guildhall after the destruction of old Prahv, monumental in scale, supported by magical construction.

**Functions:** lawmaking, courts, administration, enforcement coordination, archives, diplomacy.

**Table synthesis:** the district around it supports legal copyists, food vendors, temporary lodging, advocates, messengers, evidence couriers, and people selling help to citizens who do not understand procedure.

## Augustin Station

**Canon:** Tenth District station near an Izzet Laboratory of Gravitational Inversion workshop.

**Campaign use:** central to *The Glass Run*.

**Table synthesis:** a place where commuters, freight, and experimental movement infrastructure meet. Magical failures here inconvenience ordinary people immediately.

## Prism University

**Canon:** the Izzet Laboratory of Arcane Geometry has a small presence here.

**Use:** lectures, academic politics, copied research, visiting experts, student rivalries, permit disputes.

# Boros sites

## Sunhome

**Canon:** Boros guildhall, fortress, barracks, and symbolic religious/military center.

**Living feel:** drill courts, armor maintenance, mess halls, command rooms, memorial spaces, aerial access, supply movement, healers, messengers.

## Parhelion II

**Canon:** immense flying Boros garrison.

**Use:** strategic mobility and civic spectacle.

**Table synthesis:** its passage changes street light; residents look up; children point; criminals recalculate.

## Kamen Fortress

**Canon:** major Boros command site, associated in Return-era material with anti-Rakdos operations.

**Use:** hard military atmosphere, intelligence, staging, detention, rapid-response planning.

# Dimir sites

## Duskmantle

**Canon:** restored guildhall hidden in the undercity behind memory wards.

**Play principle:** its security is epistemic. Visitors can leave knowing they were somewhere without being able to reconstruct how they arrived.

## Ismeri Library

**Canon:** officially guildless public library operated by Dimir personnel and used as a communications node.

**Why it matters:** it is genuinely useful. Citizens research history, property, magic, literature, and law while covert messages pass through the same information system.

## Dinrova Heights

**Canon:** rooftop meeting place for high-ranking Dimir mages, protected against observation.

**Use:** espionage with the entire city visible below.

## Bane Alley

**Canon:** point of access to Dimir extortion, intimidation, murder, and clandestine problem-solving.

**Play principle:** knowing how to reach it is itself part of the price.

## Nightveil

**Canon:** area of Dimir elites, caverns, tunnels, crypts, walled mansions, and spectral security.

**Use:** affluent secrecy and evidence that Dimir power is not confined to poor alleys.

# Golgari sites

## Korozda, Maze of Decay

**Canon:** Golgari guildhall, an arched cathedral enclosed by an immense circular maze of overgrown ruins.

**Use:** governance embedded in reclamation. The approach should communicate that decomposition here is managed.

## Stonefare

**Canon:** old brick tunnel network in Golgari territory with slime, fungi, webs, and petrified remains incorporated into the environment.

**Use:** transport, neighborhood boundary, intimidation, ancient city infrastructure.

## Deadbridge

**Canon:** major Golgari area built around a vast sinkhole and the bridge/platform spanning it.

**Use:** catastrophe converted into neighborhood identity and resource economy.

## Zanikev, the Great Rot Farm

**Canon:** enormous reclamation zone created from ruined neighborhoods.

**Use:** the Golgari as urban ecologists and resource processors.

## Corpsefloat Valves

**Canon:** fungal membranes regulating waterways and corpse traffic.

**Implication:** Golgari territory includes schedules, maintenance workers, transport knowledge, and practical infrastructure—not only monsters.

# Gruul sites

## Rubblebelt

**Canon:** discontinuous wasteland of gutted buildings, shantytowns, ruins, and roaming elementals near more developed areas.

**Core interpretation:** this is contested post-urban land, not untouched wilderness.

## Skarrg

**Canon:** ruined palace used as a cross-clan gathering ground, marked by a great fire and no guaranteed truce.

**Use:** feasting, boasts, challenges, temporary alliance, political violence without paperwork.

## The Husk

**Canon:** Slizt territory characterized by tall ancient structures and excellent ambush positions.

**Use:** vertical ruin warfare.

# Izzet sites

## Nivix, Aerie of the Firemind

**Canon:** giant Izzet tower and guildhall, with level after level of laboratories and Niv-Mizzet's aerie at the top.

**Use:** research bureaucracy at skyscraper scale.

A single floor can have:
- experiment bays;
- storage;
- instrument repair;
- attendants;
- safety officers;
- specimen traffic;
- competing research teams.

## Blistercoils

**Canon:** huge wheel-like structures whose mizzium coils generate dangerous quantities of mana.

**Use:** mysterious infrastructure visible at civic scale.

## Boilerpits

**Canon:** labyrinth of high-pressure pipelines, especially navigable by heat-hardy steam goblins.

**Use:** industrial undercity rather than ancient dungeon.

## Mizzium Foundry

**Canon:** sole mizzium smelting location.

**Use:** supply-chain politics, industrial danger, sabotage, labor, guild security.

## Laboratory network

Published examples include Pyrology, Storms and Electricity, Metallurgy, Alchemy, Orientation, Mimeography, Continuism, Arcane Geometry, Gravitational Inversion, and Plasma-Dermatology.

Treat these as **institutional cultures**, not tiny workshops.

# Orzhov sites

## Orzhova, Cathedral Opulent

**Canon:** overwhelming Orzhov guildhall combining religious grandeur with economic power.

**Use:** a place where architecture teaches hierarchy before anyone speaks.

## Vizkopa Bank

**Canon:** major Orzhov financial center and minting/storage location with supernatural defenses.

**Use:** certification, asset freezes, inheritance, transfer, and debt are usually more interesting than robbery.

## Irbitov

**Canon:** mausoleum district filled with tombs, memorials, vaults, priests, thrulls, and necromantic presence.

**Use:** death as property and status.

## Coiner's Row

**Canon:** high-status Orzhov street known for concentrated wealth, clergy, administration, luxury shops, and decorum.

**Use:** visible respectable power.

# Rakdos sites

## Rix Maadi, the Dungeon Palace

**Canon:** Rakdos guildhall built around an ancient undercity lava pit and reached through a monumental descent.

Official descriptions emphasize:
- increasing heat;
- old volcanic geology;
- banners;
- blood;
- basalt;
- lava glow;
- cavernous scale.

**Use:** a journey from civic city into demonic court.

## Festival Grounds

**Canon:** battered courtyard before Rix Maadi filled with ruined performance structures and evidence of violent festivals.

**Use:** spectacle leaves physical aftermath.

## Diversion and debauchery clubs

**Canon:** Rakdos Rings operate shifting venue networks from relatively public entertainment clubs to concealed, more dangerous establishments.

**Living-world implication:** nightlife geography changes faster than legal maps.

# Selesnya sites

## Vitu-Ghazi

**Canon:** world-tree, spiritual focus, guildhall, and home of Trostani.

**Use:** cathedral, organism, government, pilgrimage site, home, and defensive asset at once.

## Ivory Oaks

**Canon:** sacred grove guarded by loxodon warrior-priests and used for treaties, marriages, oaths, rites, and contracts.

**Use:** ceremonial authority that can include guilded and guildless citizens.

## Sumala, the Topiary Grove

**Canon:** highly ordered living garden/forest and famous work of Selesnyan landscape craft.

**Use:** order expressed through life instead of bureaucracy.

## Great Concourse

**Canon:** elevated road system above forest canopy, serving both gatherings and everyday traffic/market functions.

**Use:** sacred space that is also infrastructure.

# Simic sites

## Zonots

**Canon:** immense sinkholes descending through city layers to ancient oceans, each a habitat/community with its own Speaker and culture.

**Critical implication:** beneath the city-world remains planetary geography older than the city.

## Zonot One

**Canon:** first opened, remote, smaller, associated with Zegana and a comparatively somber culture.

## Zonot Four

**Canon:** the largest, with a physically larger and more direct/brutal local culture.

## Zonot Five

**Canon:** more visitor-facing; home of the Helical Stair.

## Zonot Seven

**Canon:** only zonot in the Tenth District and location of Zameck.

## Zameck

**Canon:** Simic guildhall just below the surface of Zonot Seven, with opalescent plax skylights, kelp supports, algae, and a spiral meeting table.

**Use:** alien biological beauty serving ordinary political function.

## Helical Stair

**Canon:** magically grown marine plant path spiraling from surface toward ancient ocean level.

**Use:** public outreach, tourism, scientific wonder, class contact.

## Jezeru, the Lake District

**Canon:** shallow flooded district developed as a major Simic aboveground presence, with adapted movement systems.

**Use:** city deliberately redesigned for different bodies rather than merely repaired.

# Industrial and mixed anchors

## Smelting Quarter

**Canon:** industrial area in Precinct Six, strongly associated with Rakdos establishments; Izzet metallurgy also maintains a presence.

**Use:** heat, freight, shift work, entertainment, debt, industrial accident, mixed jurisdiction.

## Deadbridge Chasm

**Canon:** major Precinct Six feature tied to Deadbridge and undercity descent.

# Canon-first generation rule

Named canon sites are anchors, not limits.

When creating an unnamed café, school, alley, apartment block, clinic, workshop, or local shrine:

1. anchor it near a published place;
2. determine what real civic need it serves;
3. connect at least two guilds to that need;
4. give it one older physical layer;
5. label the invented proper name as table synthesis.

Example:

Nivix canonically needs workers, freight, records, food, waste removal, housing, medicine, and transport. Therefore an invented all-night café for Izzet attendants is plausible urban infill. It is not a published named location and should not be presented as one.
