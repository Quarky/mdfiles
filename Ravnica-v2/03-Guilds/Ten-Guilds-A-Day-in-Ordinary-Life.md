# Ten Guilds — A Day in Ordinary Life

These are baseline rhythms for ordinary guild members. They are not scripts; they are anchors for improvisation.

## Azorius junior scribe
**06:30:** breakfast while reviewing today's filing list.  
**08:00:** queues already exist; corrects forms before the office officially opens.  
**10:30:** supervisor sends an urgent precedent request.  
**Lunch:** twenty minutes, mostly spent arguing with a Boros friend about last night's arrest.  
**Afternoon:** copies witness statements, verifies seals, tells six people their cases belong in another office.  
**Evening:** studies for promotion and complains that nobody understands how much chaos the paperwork prevents.

**Fear:** making a mistake that becomes precedent.  
**Want:** a competent supervisor and one uninterrupted afternoon.

## Boros garrison soldier
**Dawn:** formation, equipment check, patrol assignment.  
**Morning:** neighborhood patrol, mostly mundane disputes.  
**Midday:** quick meal, equipment repair.  
**Afternoon:** emergency call becomes hours of crowd control.  
**Evening:** writes the incident report they hate writing.  
**Night:** tries to sleep while another unit drills outside.

**Fear:** being too late.  
**Want:** enough people to cover the sector properly.

## Dimir public courier
**Morning:** collects packets and memorizes route.  
**Day:** offices, shops, residences, station platforms, archives; learns who changed jobs, who is angry, which door is newly guarded.  
**Afternoon:** one delivery contains a coded instruction recognized only because of a tiny mark.  
**Evening:** an ordinary delivery is actually the covert task.  
**Night:** goes home and behaves exactly like someone with nothing to hide.

**Fear:** patterns becoming visible.  
**Want:** to know just enough to stay useful.

## Golgari grower
**Early:** checks humidity, fungal beds, insect activity, contamination.  
**Morning:** receives waste and substrate from upper routes.  
**Midday:** prepares food shipment upward.  
**Afternoon:** faction argument interrupts work; the crop does not care about politics.  
**Evening:** repairs chamber supports and eats with family.  
**Night:** surface traders arrive because they prefer not to be seen buying.

**Fear:** upper-city decisions damaging systems nobody above understands.  
**Want:** respect for work already keeping people alive.

## Gruul clan scout
**Morning:** checks territory, water, tracks, new construction.  
**Midday:** reports survey markers near a clan-used ruin.  
**Afternoon:** argues whether to destroy them, observe, or confront workers.  
**Evening:** hunt, repair, stories, challenge.  
**Night:** watches city lights spread into places elders remember as dark.

**Fear:** another piece of ground becoming permanently inaccessible.  
**Want:** the city to stop treating existence as permission to expand.

## Izzet technician
**Start:** calibration.  
**One hour later:** recalibration because another lab changed the load.  
**Midday:** fixes a public system while a researcher complains maintenance interrupts data collection.  
**Afternoon:** discovers anomaly and becomes excited despite knowing it means overtime.  
**Evening:** writes notes nobody will read until something fails.  
**Night:** called back because something failed.

**Fear:** preventable failure blamed on "Izzet chaos."  
**Want:** enough time and power budget to solve the actual problem.

## Orzhov syndic
**Morning:** checks ledgers and appointments.  
**Late morning:** assists a priest with tithes and borrower records.  
**Afternoon:** delivers a polite notice to a client who cannot pay.  
**Later:** a superior asks for a favor outside formal duty; refusal would be remembered.  
**Evening:** reviews personal debts upward in hierarchy.

**Fear:** owing more powerful people than owe them.  
**Want:** leverage and security.

## Rakdos stagehand
**Late morning:** wakes after last night's teardown.  
**Afternoon:** repairs rigging, scavenges materials, argues with a performer about safety.  
**Evening:** hauls stage components into a square.  
**Night:** show begins; everything appears spontaneous because six people worked all day.  
**After midnight:** teardown, injuries, drinks, gossip about who got Rakdos's attention.

**Fear:** dying in a boring way.  
**Want:** a show people remember and a crew competent enough to survive it.

## Selesnyan vernadi member
**Morning:** shared work—garden, kitchen, animal care, craft, teaching.  
**Midday:** communal meal and news.  
**Afternoon:** personal work plus training or spiritual study.  
**Evening:** gathering, mediation, storytelling, worship, shared care.  
**Night:** takes watch because the neighborhood received threats.

**Fear:** isolation and fracture.  
**Want:** everyone contributing enough that nobody is abandoned.

## Simic lab assistant
**Morning:** sample checks, feeding, environmental measurements.  
**Midday:** clinic duty or research support.  
**Afternoon:** senior researcher changes protocol after a new result.  
**Evening:** records biological response and notices an effect the senior missed.  
**Night:** goes home damp, smelling faintly of disinfectant and brine, wondering whether to report the anomaly immediately.

**Fear:** uncontrolled consequences and being ignored by senior researchers.  
**Want:** meaningful work and recognition without becoming responsible for disaster.

## GM use
When PCs meet a guild member, assume the NPC was already in the middle of one of these rhythms. Adventurers are an interruption. That single choice makes exposition feel like life.
