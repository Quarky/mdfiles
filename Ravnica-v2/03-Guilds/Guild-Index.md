## Use rule

When a guild enters a scene, identify:

- the **public service or need** that makes the guild legitimate;
- the **institutional habit** that makes it dangerous;
- the **individual member's practical problem**;
- the **other guild whose cooperation is necessary**;
- the **other guild whose values make that cooperation painful**.

A guild becomes believable when its vice grows from its virtue.
