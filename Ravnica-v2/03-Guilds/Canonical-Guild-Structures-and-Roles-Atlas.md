# Canonical Guild Structures and Roles — Source-First Atlas

This is the **check-the-source-before-you-improvise** companion to the interpretive guild dossiers.

It draws primarily from *Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica* and the official Planeswalker's Guides. It deliberately preserves the distinct organizational logic of each guild rather than reducing every guild to "leader + officers + members."

---

# Azorius Senate

## Institutional frame

The Senate is built around three Columns.

### Sova Column

Judicial work:

- judges;
- advocates;
- prosecutors;
- legal aides;
- pages;
- librarians;
- precedent specialists;
- experts;
- arbitration and mediation staff.

### Jelenn Column

Legislative work:

- legislators;
- policy researchers;
- legal writers;
- lobbyists;
- aides;
- clerks;
- analysts.

### Lyev Column

Enforcement work:

- arresters;
- investigators;
- officers;
- lawmages;
- hussars;
- infantry;
- surveillance and precognitive functions.

The three Columns are not three isolated buildings. They are different ways the same institution turns law into lived reality.

## Canonical specialist roles

**Konstructors:** magical architects and builders.  
**Elocutors:** messengers, mediators, negotiators, and communication specialists.  
**Hieromancers:** magic users who sanctify or protect places and things.  
**Lawmages:** coercive or restraining magic used to preserve legal order.  
**Hussars:** mounted Azorius troops.  
**Infantry:** unmounted formations, including tower-shield crowd control.

## Everyday institutional ecology — canon-supported inference

A structure this large necessarily includes:

- records clerks;
- evidence custodians;
- public intake desks;
- court schedulers;
- archive staff;
- custodial workers;
- building maintenance;
- food service;
- messengers;
- translators;
- stable and griffin handlers where relevant.

These jobs are not a new invented Azorius faction. They are the ordinary labor required by the source's own institutional scale.

## Internal conflicts to play

- Sova complains Lyev creates weak cases.
- Lyev complains Sova releases obvious threats.
- Jelenn writes law neither group thinks is practical.
- Elocutors believe everybody would cooperate if people would stop shouting.
- Konstructors are irritated that new legal requirements arrived after construction began.
- a justiciar wants precedent;
- an arrester wants action;
- a legislator wants a problem that can be described cleanly enough to regulate.

# Boros Legion

## Institutional frame

The Boros combine:

- standing army;
- military police;
- air force;
- angelic strategic leadership;
- religious/moral identity;
- local garrisons;
- intelligence functions.

Their hierarchy is explicit and culturally important.

## Angelic roles

**Warleaders:** high-level strategists.  
**Firemanes:** elite angelic champions, often focused on individual combat.  
**Battleforce angels:** angelic warriors who take a more direct role in formations and field operations.

Under Aurelia, official Return-era material emphasizes a shift toward more practical angelic involvement.

## Mortal and mixed roles

**Guildmages:** combine martial and magical authority.  
**Swiftblades:** elite assault soldiers.  
**Flamekin:** elemental soldiers whose presence can function as living symbols of valor.  
**Skyknights:** aerial military forces riding rocs.  
**Wojeks:** law-enforcement and investigative arm.  
**Skyjeks:** aerial Wojek patrol.  
**Sergeants, brigadiers, captains, commanders:** regular command progression.  
**Sunhome Guard:** defensive elite.

## The Wojek distinction

Wojeks are particularly important for living-world play because they sit at the seam between military institution and city policing.

They:

- investigate crimes;
- protect property and citizens;
- build cases;
- manage civil disorder;
- watch for interguild threats;
- can investigate misconduct inside Boros structures.

This makes a Wojek game feel different from a standard "soldier mission."

## Operational ecology — canon-supported inference

A permanent garrison needs:

- armorers;
- cooks;
- healers;
- quartermasters;
- animal handlers;
- trainers;
- clerks;
- dispatch staff;
- repair crews;
- mess staff;
- supply officers;
- laundry;
- stable workers;
- people moving wounded and prisoners.

Boros daily life should include far more than marching.

# House Dimir

## Institutional frame

The Dimir explicitly operate in layers.

### Overt layer

Public-facing work:

- couriers;
- investigators;
- reporters;
- archivists;
- librarians;
- information specialists.

This layer is genuinely useful and is part of why Dimir can exist inside ordinary city life.

### Covert layer

- spies;
- assassins;
- infiltrators;
- mind mages;
- handlers;
- coded communication;
- secret drop points.

### Clandestine layer

The deepest network around Lazav, insulated from ordinary agents and sometimes from other Dimir leadership.

## Canonical operating methods

- hidden symbols;
- codes;
- telepathic instructions;
- thought-based messages;
- memory erasure;
- sleeper agents;
- compartmentalized contacts;
- agents embedded in other guilds;
- ordinary cover lives.

## Correct low-level Dimir play

A junior operative should usually know:

- their cover identity;
- the immediate task;
- the delivery or contact method;
- what to do if compromised.

They should **not** know the entire conspiracy.

That is much closer to published Dimir structure than giving every agent encyclopedic secret knowledge.

## Ordinary overt jobs matter

A courier still needs:

- route knowledge;
- door staff relationships;
- schedule discipline;
- weather gear;
- fast food;
- repair for bags and boots;
- information about closures.

Those mundane skills are exactly what make the courier useful to an intelligence network.

# Golgari Swarm

## Institutional frame

The Swarm is internally plural.

Important factions and communities include:

- Devkarin elves;
- medusas/gorgons;
- kraul;
- undead populations;
- Street Swarm labor structures;
- shamans;
- rot farmers;
- corpse scavengers;
- Ochran assassins.

Leadership can change violently without the guild ceasing to function.

## Devkarin

The Devkarin have deep spiritual and political importance. Their matka provides religious authority even when temporal leadership shifts.

## Street Swarm

Official material emphasizes neighborhood-level Golgari labor chapters led by local swarm-bosses.

This is excellent living-world structure.

It means an ordinary district can have a Golgari presence that is not personally directed by Jarad.

## Canonical work

- agriculture;
- waste processing;
- fungal cultivation;
- corpse handling;
- reclamation;
- undercity transport;
- scavenging;
- tunnel labor;
- necromancy;
- assassination.

## Internal tensions to play

- old Devkarin prestige versus other factions;
- surface-facing pragmatists versus isolationists;
- local swarm-bosses versus higher political interests;
- workers who need stability versus leaders who treat assassination as normal political change;
- kraul resentment;
- gorgon influence;
- arguments about who benefits from reclaimed territory.

# Gruul Clans

## Institutional frame

The Gruul are **not a centralized guild** in the way Azorius, Boros, or Orzhov are.

They are a loose collection of clans, each with different traditions, leaders, and survival patterns.

## Canonical major clans

### Burning Tree

Largest and most feared, associated with Borborygmos.

Use for:
- broad clan identity;
- large gatherings;
- political gravity;
- strength-based authority.

### Ghor

Associated with Ruric Thar and frequent, brutal attacks close to populated areas.

Use for:
- aggressive raiding;
- intimidation;
- direct challenge to city control.

### Scab

Known for scars and body modification as expressions of authentic rage and identity.

Use for:
- anti-pretension;
- body-as-history;
- painful ritual belonging.

### Bolrac

Heavy brutes focused on destroying large structures.

Use for:
- siege;
- demolition;
- symbolic destruction of civic architecture.

### Slizt

Stealth-oriented, high-ground ambushers, strongly associated with The Husk.

Use for:
- scouts;
- ranged attacks;
- survival through cunning;
- clan stigma from more direct warriors.

### Zhur-Taa

Closest continuity with the old druidic ways, beasts, deep-earth religious traditions, and expectation of Ilharg.

Use for:
- spiritual Gruul;
- shamanic politics;
- old world memory;
- anti-city cosmology rather than simple rage.

## Living-world correction

"Gruul" is not enough to tell you how an NPC behaves.

Always choose the clan or decide the character is unaffiliated.

# Izzet League

## Institutional frame

The Izzet are organized around laboratories, projects, attendants, researchers, and high-level coordination beneath Niv-Mizzet.

## Senior structures

**Izmundi:** court/board involved in carrying out broad directives.  
**Izmagnus:** elite advisors with unusually close access to Niv-Mizzet.

Niv-Mizzet's habit of compartmentalizing research means projects may appear disconnected to the people performing them.

## Canonical laboratories from *Guildmasters' Guide*

- Laboratory of Pyrology;
- Laboratory of Storms and Electricity;
- Laboratory of Metallurgy;
- Laboratory of Alchemy;
- Laboratory of Orientation;
- Laboratory of Mimeography;
- Laboratory of Continuism;
- Laboratory of Arcane Geometry;
- Laboratory of Gravitational Inversion;
- Laboratory of Plasma-Dermatology.

## Canonical roles

**Chemisters:** design and fabricate magical/alchemical tools and materials.  
**Blastseekers:** field or operational mages who wield experimental systems.  
**Attendants:** record, acquire materials, assist, organize, and survive dangerous work.  
**Researchers:** conduct experiments.  
**Independent researchers:** senior members with latitude.  
**Directors:** run laboratories.  
**Advisors:** senior figures near Niv-Mizzet's strategic level.

## Everyday Izzet labor

A functioning lab also requires:

- glassware cleaning;
- calibration;
- parts inventory;
- specimen transport;
- copying notes;
- hazard marking;
- ventilation maintenance;
- power isolation;
- repair;
- emergency response;
- food delivered at unreasonable hours.

That mundane labor should be visible.

# Orzhov Syndicate

## Institutional frame

The Orzhov fuse:

- finance;
- religion;
- law;
- organized crime;
- property;
- status;
- contract;
- undeath.

Their hierarchy is not decorative. It is the guild's worldview made social.

## Senior structure

**Obzedat:** undead oligarchic ruling council in the baseline era.  
**Grand Envoy:** public diplomatic interface associated with Teysa Karlov.  
**Cartels:** semi-autonomous operating structures controlling markets and territories.  
**Cardinals/kingpins:** cartel leadership.

## Canonical role vocabulary

**Pontiffs and priests:** religious authority.  
**Ministrants:** lower-ranking clergy/administrators.  
**Sinstrikers:** punish or shame doctrinal deviation.  
**Absolvers:** offer release from guilt or sin in exchange for payment/obligation.  
**Syndics:** administrators, accountants, counselors, lawyers, couriers.  
**Advokists:** legal specialists and lawmages.  
**Coiners:** finance and debt accounting.  
**Traffic merchants:** resource and transaction coordinators.  
**Fences:** move or hide illicit goods.  
**Knights:** titled enforcers/paragons.  
**Executioners/assassins/soldiers:** coercive force.  
**Thrulls:** constructed servants.  
**Indebted spirits:** dead who remain obligated.

## Correct Orzhov conflict

Do not reduce every scene to "pay us money."

Orzhov power works through:

- obligation;
- inheritance;
- property;
- reputation;
- service;
- fear of default;
- patronage;
- access.

Money is the measurement system, not always the immediate demand.

# Cult of Rakdos

## Institutional frame

Rakdos himself is supreme. Below him, official Return-era material describes **Rings** led by Ringmasters.

Rings are fluid, competitive, venue-centered, and shaped by public demand as much as formal territory.

## Canonical role vocabulary

**Ringmaster:** local organizer/leader.  
**Bloodwitch:** powerful specialist and political operator.  
**Riot mage:** leads festivals and public chaos.  
**Roustabout:** worker/stage support.  
**Spiker:** muscle.  
**Madcap:** entertainer.  
**Performer:** broad category.  
**Laborer/miner:** rooted in the guild's civic role.

## Critical correction

The Cult's historical civic functions include:

- entertainment;
- mining;
- manual labor.

That means Rakdos culture must appear in:

- foundries;
- quarries;
- moving stage crews;
- freight;
- rigging;
- night labor;
- physical work;
- clubs.

A guild made only of murder clowns loses one of its most important contradictions.

## Internal variation

Different Rings can emphasize:

- satire;
- nightlife;
- pain;
- music;
- pit fighting;
- circus;
- labor solidarity;
- predation;
- criminal enterprise.

They remain Rakdos without being identical.

# Selesnya Conclave

## Institutional frame

The Conclave is distributed across local **vernadi**.

Each vernadi:

- centers around a great tree;
- is led by a dryad called a voda;
- functions as community, religious center, training site, and local support network.

Vitu-Ghazi and Trostani are spiritually central, but daily Selesnyan life is local.

## Canonical roles

**Voda:** local dryad leader.  
**Initiate:** ordinary starting member.  
**Religious teacher:** spiritual instruction.  
**Military instructor:** daily readiness.  
**Hierarch:** important ritual/spiritual role.  
**Evangel:** outreach.  
**Votary:** guardian role.  
**Sagittar:** elite archery.  
**Equenaut:** mounted/air role.  
**Ledev guardian:** road and community champion.  
**Dignitary:** high-level emissary.  
**Wurmcaller:** works with giant subterranean wurms.  
**Healer/grower:** natural extension of published social functions.

## Structural principle

The same vernadi may simultaneously be:

- home;
- temple;
- garden;
- school;
- militia assembly point;
- food system;
- social safety net.

That overlap is why leaving the guild can be so costly.

# Simic Combine

## Institutional frame

The Combine is organized by both **place** and **research purpose**.

### Zonots

Each zonot is:

- habitat;
- community;
- political unit;
- laboratory ecosystem;
- access point to ancient ocean.

Each has a Speaker.

### Speakers' Chamber

Speakers collectively select or recognize the Prime Speaker.

### Clades and projects

Cross-disciplinary research groups focused on functional goals rather than species or caste.

## Canonical clades

**Hull:** defense and durability.  
**Fin:** movement.  
**Crypsis:** concealment and information protection.  
**Gyre:** cycles, metamagic, redirection.

Other programs include Guardian-related work and specialized adaptation.

## Canonical roles

**Technician:** operational laboratory worker.  
**Researcher:** conducts experiments.  
**Project leader:** directs focused research.  
**Clade leader:** senior intellectual/administrative role.  
**Speaker:** zonot political leader.  
**Prime Speaker:** guildmaster.  
**Biomancer:** living-body manipulation.  
**Terraformer:** environmental adaptation.  
**Guardian:** modified operative.  
**Deepsage:** contemplative philosophical role.  
**Krasis handler/researcher:** manages experimental organisms.

## Philosophical frame

**Holdfast:** remain anchored to natural systems; avoid becoming detached from living reality.

**Upwelling:** renewal, replacement, discovery, transformation.

These are better played as competing scientific philosophies than as "good side versus reckless side."

# Cross-guild ordinary-systems map

Ravnica becomes real when one mundane system touches several guilds.

## Hospital

- Simic: medicine, adaptation, research.
- Selesnya: healing, food, family support.
- Azorius: licensing, quarantine, rights.
- Orzhov: finance, debt, ownership.
- Golgari: disposal, fungal medicine, cheap supply.
- Boros: emergency security.
- Izzet: equipment, heat, power, sterilization.
- Dimir: records and information.
- Rakdos: injured laborers and performers.
- Gruul: patients who distrust formal authority.

## Bridge

- Izzet: engineering.
- Azorius: closure orders and liability.
- Boros: emergency access.
- Orzhov: financing/property.
- Selesnya: environmental impact.
- Golgari: old foundations below.
- Gruul: encroachment.
- Dimir: alternate routes.
- Rakdos: performance opportunity.
- Simic: living reinforcement or biological deterioration.

## Funeral

- Orzhov: inheritance, contract, debt, spirit.
- Golgari: decay and return to cycle.
- Selesnya: communal spiritual meaning.
- Boros: honor and service.
- Azorius: official record.
- Dimir: secrets and unfinished information.
- Simic: cause of death and biology.
- Rakdos: mortality as spectacle or philosophy.
- Gruul: clan tradition.
- Izzet: unfinished work, dangerous devices, research ownership.

# Core V2 rule

A guild becomes deep when its **institutional structure touches ordinary life**.

Do not ask only, "What does this guild believe?"

Ask:

- who gets paid?
- who gets fed?
- who signs?
- who repairs?
- who waits?
- who has access?
- who cleans up afterward?
- who benefits from delay?
- who knows the shortcut?
