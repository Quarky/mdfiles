# Calendar, Festivals, and Civic Time

Ravnica uses a 365-day year divided into twelve months. The year begins on **1 Seleszeni**, corresponding to March 1, and the annual Guildpact celebration begins on **28 Zuun** and carries into the new year.

The exact calendar is useful not because players need to memorize month names, but because **city behavior changes around shared dates**.

## The months

1. Seleszeni — March  
2. Dhazo — April  
3. Prahz — May  
4. Mokosh — June  
5. Paujal — July  
6. Cizarm — August  
7. Tevnember — September  
8. Golgar — October  
9. Quaegar — November  
10. Xivaskir — December  
11. Griev — January  
12. Zuun — February

## Guildpact celebration

This is part civic holiday, part political theater, part neighborhood festival, part security nightmare.

Every guild wants visibility without appearing to threaten the others. Procession order, banners, who speaks, who stands beside whom, and which streets close become political signals.

Rakdos performers lampoon the ceremony. Azorius overplans it. Boros worries about crowd safety. Orzhov sponsors expensive events. Selesnya turns communal meals into recruitment. Dimir uses the volume of movement to hide quieter exchanges.

## Ordinary civic time

Ravnica also runs on smaller clocks:

- shift changes;
- court filing deadlines;
- market opening;
- lift schedules;
- patrol rotations;
- lab observation windows;
- Selesnyan communal meals;
- debt payment dates;
- performance nights;
- sanitation collection;
- freight windows on major roads.

A city scene feels real when characters can be **late** for something.

## Seasonal texture

Even in a plane-wide city, weather and light affect life. Rain changes traffic. Heat makes industrial quarters harsher. Cold reveals which buildings have reliable Izzet heating.

Seasonal change is experienced through infrastructure more than agriculture in many neighborhoods.

## GM time pressure

Use civic deadlines instead of arbitrary countdowns.

- The courthouse closes in forty minutes.
- The night freight window begins at dusk.
- The Selesnyan meeting starts when the evening meal is served.
- The Orzhov payment comes due tomorrow.
- The Izzet test can only run during a low-demand power period.
- The Rakdos show begins once the crowd is large enough.

This makes urgency feel like city life rather than adventure scripting.
