# News, Rumor, and Information

Ravnica is too large for anyone to know what is happening everywhere. Information travels through **couriers, guild reports, market gossip, official notices, performers, sermons, taverns, neighborhood networks, and deliberate misinformation**.

## Speed of news

Public spectacle travels fast.

A fight on the Transguild Promenade can become district gossip within hours because thousands saw it, vendors repeat it, couriers carry versions outward, and Rakdos performers may parody it by night.

Private institutional news travels differently. A legal filing may reach three offices before the person named in it hears anything.

## Dimir information ecology

House Dimir thrives because the city already needs information specialists.

A useful Dimir plot does not invent a separate spy world. It hides inside normal information flow:

- courier routes;
- archive requests;
- print shops;
- messenger services;
- private investigators;
- damaged records;
- memory magic;
- paid witnesses;
- rumor brokers.

## Official notices

Azorius notices create public truth even when everyone knows the underlying reality is more complicated.

A building is "condemned." A street is "closed." A demonstration is "unlawful." Those words change what institutions are allowed to do next.

## Rakdos as media

Rakdos performance can become political media. A successful satire turns a complicated scandal into one humiliating image everyone remembers.

That makes Rakdos dangerous to institutions even when the show reveals nothing technically secret.

## Selesnyan networks

Vernadi spread news through dense trust. The information may move more slowly than courier systems but arrive with social interpretation already attached: who needs help, who can be trusted, what the community should do.

## Rumor table

When news spreads, distort one element:

1. casualty count doubles  
2. wrong guild gets blamed  
3. location shifts one precinct  
4. motive becomes political  
5. one eyewitness is elevated to hero  
6. one official becomes villain  
7. magical detail is exaggerated  
8. true secret is dismissed as absurd  
9. unrelated old scandal gets attached  
10. rumor becomes mostly correct

## PC reputation

Track not only "fame" but **which version of the story each network heard**.

Boros may know the PCs saved people. Azorius may know they violated an order. Rakdos may know they looked ridiculous doing it. Orzhov may know who paid for the damage.

All are true.
