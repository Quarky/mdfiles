# Sensory Palette and Daily Rhythm

## Morning

Morning is not quiet. Freight has often been moving all night. Bakers open shutters into streets already occupied by delivery carts, Golgari food sellers, sanitation crews, sleepy Rakdos performers, clerks, students, and soldiers finishing night watch.

**See:** mist between towers, damp cobbles, banners hanging heavy with moisture, lift cages beginning their first full runs, winged patrols crossing pale sky.

**Hear:** shutters thrown open, wheels on stone, temple bells, vendors calling breakfasts, courier whistles, boots changing shift.

**Smell:** yeast, coffee, wet stone, horse and beast sweat, hot pipes, fungal broth, cheap soap.

**Social tone:** purposeful, impatient, practical. A delay feels personal because thousands of people are trying to reach work through the same bottleneck.

## Midday

The city becomes bright, crowded, and transactional. Bureaucracies are fully awake. Courts, labs, workshops, schools, markets, and guild offices operate at maximum pressure.

**See:** document runners, public notices pasted over older notices, street repairs, uniformed escorts, packed cafes, queues under awnings.

**Hear:** argument, construction, official proclamations, street music, machinery, classroom recitation, distant magical detonations that nobody nearby finds surprising.

**Smell:** hot metal, cooking oil, fruit, incense, ozone, dust, warm masonry.

**Social tone:** visible hierarchy. Who gets waved through? Who waits? Who is served first? Who has an appointment? Who can interrupt the line?

## Evening

Shift changes make streets briefly chaotic. Office workers, laborers, students, soldiers, and performers all move at once. Restaurants and taverns fill. Selesnyan courtyards become communal spaces; Orzhov establishments become negotiation spaces; Rakdos crews start assembling mobile stages.

**See:** lanterns and magical lights appearing floor by floor; reflections in rainwater; guild colors becoming more dramatic after dark.

**Hear:** music, bells, drinking songs, sermon, machinery winding down in one block and starting up in another.

**Smell:** grilled meat, mushroom stew, perfume, lamp oil, smoke, wet vegetation, sewage from stressed drains.

## Night

Ravnica does not sleep uniformly. It changes ownership.

Couriers still run. Watch patrols change routes. Dimir activity becomes harder to distinguish from ordinary delivery work. Golgari workers emerge from lower passages. Rakdos stages can transform a square in minutes. Izzet researchers keep impossible hours. Orzhov collectors prefer private visits. Hospitals, barracks, freight depots, and major markets remain lit.

Night should feel **less crowded, not empty**.

## Weather

Rain is useful because it reveals verticality: water runs off roofs, through gargoyles, across bridges, down chained gutters, into grates, and eventually into systems maintained or contested below. Fog hides upper bridges before it hides street level. Wind behaves strangely among towers. Heat leaks from infrastructure in winter and makes some alleys steam.
