# Childhood, Education, and Family

A city feels real when the GM knows what happens before adulthood.

## Family structures

Ravnican families can be guild-homogeneous, mixed-guild, guildless, adoptive, communal, or institutionally raised.

A household with a Boros parent and Azorius parent can argue over law at dinner without becoming a political metaphor. A Selesnyan child may grow up in a vernadi where dozens of adults share responsibility. A guildless family may have relatives in four guilds and call all of them irritating.

## Education

Education is uneven and institutionally shaped.

Azorius values legal literacy and formal study. Izzet and Simic train technical specialists. Selesnya teaches spiritual and communal practice alongside military readiness. Boros trains discipline and service. Orzhov teaches hierarchy, finance, ritual, and legal leverage to those inside its system.

Guildless schools, tutors, apprenticeships, family trades, and neighborhood institutions fill gaps.

## Apprenticeship

Many professions are learned through work. A teenage courier learns city geography by doing it. A market child learns prices and social reading. An Izzet apprentice learns which hum means "normal."

This creates NPCs who are competent young without feeling like miniature adults in every domain.

## Recruitment

Guild recruitment can offer real advantages.

- housing;
- food;
- education;
- career;
- community;
- status;
- protection;
- access to magic.

The ethical tension is strongest when the offer is genuinely life-changing.

## Mixed-guild families

Use them often.

A family can love one another and still interpret the world differently.

Example: a Boros daughter thinks her Orzhov uncle is predatory. He paid for her mother's surgery. Both facts shape every holiday.

## Children in scenes

Children should know the city differently.

They know shortcuts, rooftops, which guards tolerate play, which maintenance crews give them scrap, which Rakdos posters are too dangerous to touch, and where food can be found cheaply.

They also repeat guild stereotypes with brutal simplicity.

## Scene seed

A school trip is trapped behind an emergency cordon. The teacher is guildless, the escort is Azorius, the nearest safe shelter is a Selesnyan vernadi, and one child's parent has explicitly forbidden Selesnyan religious influence.

The emergency is simple. The social decision is not.
