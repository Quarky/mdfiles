# Crime, Black Markets, and Informal Power

Crime in Ravnica is not one guild's department.

## Why black markets exist

The city has regulation, scarcity, guild monopolies, debt, dangerous magic, restricted research, confiscated goods, and people who need things quickly.

That guarantees unofficial markets.

## Common illicit trades

- forged permits;
- stolen lab access;
- restricted magical components;
- information;
- counterfeit insignia;
- smuggled creatures;
- debt records;
- unlicensed medicine;
- weapons;
- hidden transit;
- property deeds;
- body modifications;
- performance materials banned for public safety.

## Guild involvement

**Dimir:** information, routes, identity, secrecy.  
**Orzhov:** financing, laundering, protection, coercion.  
**Rakdos:** venues, muscle, crowd cover, illicit entertainment.  
**Golgari:** undercity routes, reclamation, hidden spaces.  
**Izzet:** stolen prototypes and parts.  
**Simic:** illegal modifications or escaped specimens.  
**Azorius/Boros:** corruption is possible, but more often their records and enforcement create the pressure black markets route around.

## Gangs and neighborhood power

A local gang may be more important to one block than any guild.

Their power comes from:

- knowing residents;
- controlling stairwells;
- managing illegal utilities;
- collecting protection;
- settling disputes;
- organizing smuggling;
- maintaining a relationship with one guild contact.

## Crime as service

The most interesting criminals solve real problems badly.

A smuggler delivers medicine around an Azorius quarantine. A gang keeps a violent landlord away. A Dimir broker finds missing people. An Orzhov collector enforces contracts no court can process quickly.

If the players remove the criminal, ask what service disappears with them.

## Scene seed

A neighborhood pays protection to a gang. Boros finally has enough evidence to arrest the leaders.

Residents quietly ask the PCs to delay the raid until they can arrange another night watch because the gang is also the only reason a predatory Rakdos crew stopped targeting the street.

Lawful success can create a security vacuum.
