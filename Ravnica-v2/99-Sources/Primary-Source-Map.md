# Primary Source Map

## Project source

### Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica
Primary tabletop-setting source available in project files.

Most relevant material for V2:

- opening overview: city-world, guild system, history, Living Guildpact;
- life in the city: currency, amenities, languages, calendar;
- Chapter 2: internal guild structures, goals, roles, interguild attitudes;
- Chapter 3: Tenth District, precincts, trade ways, undercity;
- later NPC and creature material for guild-specific table texture.

### Ravnica — The Glass Run — GM Guide
Campaign source for the one-shot.

Use as campaign canon only. It establishes the specific orientation-prism plot, named scenario NPCs, chase structure, and MTGFoundry test context.

## Official public web sources

### Ravnica plane overview
https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/ravnica-plane

Useful for concise official plane identity and guild framing.

### A Flavorful Guide to the Guilds of Ravnica
https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/flavorful-guide-guilds-ravnica-2018-10-03

Useful for Guilds of Ravnica-era public-facing guild personality and cultural tone.

### A Flavorful Guide to the Guilds of Ravnica Allegiance
https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/flavorful-guide-guilds-ravnica-allegiance-2019-02-07

Useful for the other five guilds and era-specific political context.

### The Gorgon and the Guildpact
https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/gorgon-and-guildpact-2014-03-12

Especially useful for the lived politics of Jace as Living Guildpact: delegations, interguild grievances, Lavinia, and the difference between constitutional power and daily administration.

### Project Lightning Bug
https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/project-lightning-bug-2015-05-27

Useful for Ravnican atmosphere, undercity travel, Ral Zarek, and the awkward political reality of Jace's position.

## Source hierarchy

When sources conflict:

1. explicit current campaign instruction;
2. chosen campaign era;
3. official source specific to that era;
4. broader official setting source;
5. V2 synthesis;
6. original scene flavor.

Do not flatten contradictory eras into one timeless present.
